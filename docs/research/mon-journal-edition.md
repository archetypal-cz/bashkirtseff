# *Mon Journal* — the printed edition we transcribe

Marie Bashkirtseff, *Mon Journal*, texte intégral, transcribed from the manuscript notebooks (BnF) by Ginette Apostolescu; Montesson, Cercle des Amis de Marie Bashkirtseff, 16 volumes, 1995–2005. `content/_original` follows this edition; our scans are `content/_raw/scans/Tome1..16.pdf` (Tomes 1–5 are low-resolution page images, ~75 dpi for 3–5), the OCR text `content/_raw/tome01..16.docx`.

ISBNs and legal-deposit dates are read from the copyright page of each volume in our scans (ISBN-10 check digits verified; ISBN-13 derived). Note that tome 8 and tome 9 carry each other's sequence numbers (8 → …-8-8, 9 → …-7-X), as printed. Carnet ranges per tome: `content/_raw/CARNET-REFERENCE.md`.
