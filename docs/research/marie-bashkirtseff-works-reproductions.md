# Marie Bashkirtseff: published reproductions of her works

Compiled 2026-10-02 (works-reproductions pass). This companion to `marie-bashkirtseff-works-catalogue.md` uses that file's work numbers (#1–#62). It covers the works that have no image, only a small image (under 800 px) or only an 1885-catalogue plate, and records where each has been reproduced in print.

**Sweeps.** Four parallel sweeps covered: French PD sources; English and German PD sources; Russian, Ukrainian and Serbian sources; and modern books and auction catalogues. Every row marked *seen* was checked against the page image. Rows marked *reported* rest on a search snippet, a review or a library record.

**Not reached in this pass.** Gallica throttled us (403) partway through the French sweep. HathiTrust, Google Books, НЭБ, goskatalog.ru, WorldCat and Dorotheum were blocked or unreachable. The "Still open" list at the end records what these gaps leave undone.

**PD rule used:** published before 1930 and the engraver or photographer unnamed or dead for more than 70 years.

## 3. Unidentified works found in print (candidates for new entries)

- **«La vie enfantine»**: a dark interior with several small children, dolls and a fan, signed lower edge. It is printed in the *Nouveau journal inédit*, 1901, as a halftone ([n152](https://archive.org/details/nouveaujournalin00bash_0/page/n152)). The same image appears in *The Last Confessions* (Stokes, NY 1901, facing p. 102) captioned "Marie Bashkirtseff's last picture". No 1885 title matches it with certainty.
- ***Paysage***: an avenue of trees by water, line block, *Nouveau journal inédit*, 1901 ([n108](https://archive.org/details/nouveaujournalin00bash_0/page/n108)).
- **Trois études de têtes** (a child, a girl in profile, a woman in a hat), line blocks, *Nouveau journal inédit*, 1901 ([n104](https://archive.org/details/nouveaujournalin00bash_0/page/n104)). These may be the studies for *Les trois Rires*.
- **«Soirée à Gawronzy près Poltawa»**, a drawing, *Nouveau journal inédit*, 1901 ([n164](https://archive.org/details/nouveaujournalin00bash_0/page/n164)).
- **"Statuette by Marie Bashkirtseff"**, a reclining nude male figure (not the Nausicaa), *The Woman's World* 1888, p. 356 ([n399](https://archive.org/details/womans-world-ny_1888_1/page/n399)). It might be 1885 sculpture no. 4 *Petit Garçon*, but that is not established.
- **Studio photographs** with works hung on the walls: *Nouveau journal inédit* 1901 (n36, n204), and *The Last Confessions* 1901 (facing pp. 40, 78, 136). They show L'Atelier Julian, the *Trois Rires* frame and several unidentified canvases. A matching pass against the 1885 list is still to do.
- **Sale records without plates** (INHA, Jacques Doucet): vente 2 April 1927, lot 38 «Femme russe», 0,55 × 0,46 ([INHA 65470](https://bibliotheque-numerique.inha.fr/idurl/1/65470)); vente 11–12 March 1929, lot 63 «Portrait de femme», 0,54 × 0,45 (INHA 68009).
- Drawings in *Lettres de Marie Bashkirtseff* (1891, Gallica bpt6k29545w: f95, f159, f252) are caricatures and an atelier plan, not catalogue works.

## 4. Negative results worth keeping

- **Catalogue illustré du Salon (Dumas), 1880–1884:** none of these volumes has a drawing after Marie. The artist index runs Barrias → Bastien-Lepage in every year (Gallica bpt6k213779f, bpt6k3045423m, bpt6k2135725, bpt6k202494s, bpt6k213573j). Caveats: the second 1880 series was not checked, and the 1881 index was seen only in part. Her 1884 Salon numbers were 116 (*Un meeting*) and 117 (*Paysage*).
- **English editions with only a portrait of Marie:** Cassell NY 1889 ("Illustrated"), Serrano's *Letters* 1891 and Dutton 1919.
- **Нива 1883–1893 and Всемирная иллюстрация 1883–1889 (IA OCR):** no work reproduced except the 1883 *Jean et Jacques* cover. The 1884 obituary portrait (VI no. 828) and Нива 1890 no. 50 show Marie herself.
- **Russian Museum catalogues of 1902, 1903, 1912 and 1917:** none has a plate of her work.

## 5. Books to buy or borrow (ranked by target works likely covered)

Plate lists could not be opened for any modern book, so the coverage below is inferred from reviews and records.

1. **Т. Швец (ed.), *Избранница судьбы. Мария Башкирцева / L'Élue du destin*, Moscow: Особая книга, 2018**, 320 pp., colour, bilingual, ISBN 978-5-9797-0117-2. The publisher says it is the first collection of colour reproductions from museums and private collections, with a catalogue of works in private hands. That makes it the best single source for #28–31, #34, #35 and #45–52 and for the Russian Museum works. Not in Czech catalogues; [urss.ru](https://urss.ru/cgi-bin/db.pl?lang=Ru&blang=ru&page=Book&id=248430), moscowbooks.ru. The 2008 Вече edition (248 pp., ISBN 978-5-9533-2780-0) is smaller.
2. ***Marie Bashkirtseff: peintre et sculpteur, écrivain et témoin de son temps***, exhibition catalogue, Nice, 1 July–29 October 1995, Régie autonome des musées de la Ville de Nice, ISBN 2-901412-70-X, 160 pp. Probably covers the Nice works (#7, #8, #9, #12–15) and the Nausicaa. It is out of print. Borrow from the BMVR Nice (Louis Nucéra) or the museum's documentation service; not in Czech catalogues.
3. **Becker & Weisberg (eds.), *Overcoming All Obstacles: The Women of the Académie Julian*, Dahesh Museum / Rutgers UP, 1999**, pb ISBN 0-8135-2756-2, OCLC 41096183, 31 colour + 61 b/w ill. The exhibition included #7, #53, L'Atelier Julian, Femme orientale and La Parisienne. It costs about USD 30 used and is not in Czech libraries.
4. **Colette Cosnier, *Marie Bashkirtseff: un portrait sans retouches*, Paris: Horay, 1985**, ISBN 2-7058-0161-8; reissued by PUR in 2022, ISBN 978-2-7535-8245-3 (€22, in print). Its plates are not known. It is **available in Prague**: NK ČR holds it under sigla ABA004 and ABA007, so check it first.
5. **Laurence Madeline (ed.), *Women Artists in Paris 1850–1900*, Yale UP / AFA, 2017**, ISBN 978-0-300-22393-4. Covers #26 and *Un meeting*. **NK ČR ABA001** (reading room).

Also in Prague: NK ČR (ABA007) holds the 1885 *Catalogue des œuvres de Mlle Bashkirtseff* (Baschet), the source of the existing plates.

## 6. Still open

- **Gallica**, once the 403 lifts. Throttle to about one request every 2 s. Check at issue level: Figaro-Salon (1881–84), Le Livre d'or du Salon, L'Art, Chronique des arts and Gazette des Beaux-Arts (Dec 1884 to the Feb 1885 exhibition), Le Monde illustré (Nov 1884, Mar 1885), and L'Illustration vol. 84 p. 283 (the 1884 death notice). Also look at the plates of *Cahiers intimes* (1925, bpt6k65726142) and the second 1885-catalogue copy, bpt6k3778093.
- **Wolf edition of the Russian diary**, «Дневник Марии Башкирцевой», 2nd ed. [1910], with 7 phototype plates (contents unknown). No scan was found; try RSL, РНБ, or an antiquarian dealer.
- **HathiTrust and Google Books full view**, and the German periodicals (Die Kunst für Alle, Zeitschrift für bildende Kunst, Über Land und Meer, the 1897 Tagebuch).
- The remaining volumes of The Magazine of Art and the Art Amateur, and The Review of Reviews vol. 3 (two hits not opened).
- *Further Memoirs* (1901): 18 "illustration" OCR hits, not inspected.
