# Marie Bashkirtseff: catalogue of surviving works

Compiled 2026-10-02 (works-catalogue pass). Glossary entries live in `content/_original/_glossary/culture/art/`; the gallery is the hub entry MARIE_BASHKIRTSEFF_WORKS. Every holding claim has a URL or a bibliographic citation. **Bold** = verified by this pass at the holder's own record, POP/Joconde notice or museum catalogue PDF. Everything else is flagged with its source (Kernberger caption, Wikidata, Commons, auction listing, search snippet).

**Baseline:** *Catalogue des œuvres de Mlle Bashkirtseff* (Union des Femmes Peintres & Sculpteurs, Paris: Baschet, 9 Feb 1885), Gallica ark:/12148/bpt6k131932r. The numbered list was transcribed in this pass from IIIF scans: paintings 1–100 (views 23–32), pastels 101–106 and drawings 107–224 (views 35–36), sculptures 1–5 (view 40). Sizes are printed H × L in metres. Plates (captions read from scans): Le Meeting (v.21), Parisienne (v.33), Nausicaa (v.37), Femme appuyée (v.41), La Cigarette, Endormie and La Lecture (v.45–48), Jean et Jacques (v.61), Georgette (v.73), Portrait de Mme P.B. (v.77), Le Parapluie (v.81), Portrait de Mlle Bashkirtseff d'après son tableau (v.85), Portrait de Mlle de Canrobert (v.93).

**Kernberger** = Kernberger (trans.), *I Am the Most Interesting Book of All* / *Lust for Glory* (Fonthill Press, 2013), illustrated EPUB. The EPUB has no page numbers, so plates are cited by image number. Captions are in `content/_raw/reports/kernberger_images.md`.

**Image status:**
- **got it**: a PD or CC0 image of at least about 800 px is on the site.
- **low-res PD**: a PD image exists but is under 800 px, so it is not used.
- **museum page only**: an image exists only under restricted rights.
- **book only**: the work is reproduced only in a book or the 1885 plates.
- **none**: no image found.

**Glossary:** an ID means the work has an entry in `culture/art/`. **NEW** means the entry was created in this pass.

**Count:** 40 works in public collections, of which 29 have a verified or credibly cited holder record and 11 rest on a Kernberger caption or Wikidata alone. Add 12 works in private hands or on the market, a total of 52 surviving, plus about 10 recorded works whose whereabouts are unknown or doubtful. That fits "at least 60". The Russian Museum's own total of 8 paintings and 13 drawings means several more unidentified RM works exist.

**Not found anywhere:** no Bashkirtseff in Poltava, Odesa, Kyiv or the Hermitage, and no lots at Christie's or Bonhams. lostart.de (Anubis wall) and goskatalog.ru (timeouts) were unreachable in this pass.

## Image-permission requests worth sending (in priority order)

1. **Musée des Beaux-Arts Jules Chéret, Nice**: musee.beaux-arts@ville-nice.fr. Ask for HD files of the *Autoportrait à la palette* (N.Mba 1982, the iconic image; only 600 px PD online), *Une larme* (N.Mba 1801), *Ombres de carnaval* (N.Mba 1977.1–2), Paul, Femme orientale, and the Kernberger-listed Nice works (Partie de cartes, Still life 1878, Autoportrait en pied 1879, "A Lady"), plus their inventory numbers.
2. **Musée d'Orsay / RMN-Grand Palais (photo agency)**: Dina pastel (RF 39091, only 600 px PD), *Douleur de Nausicaa* (RF 3159, no PD image at all), drawings RF 39089 and RF 39090. Orsay's download terms allow private use only (musee-orsay.fr/en/legal-information); ask for a non-commercial educational licence.
3. **State Russian Museum**: rusmuseumvrm.ru/contacts/ (terms: /terms/). Ask for *Automne*, *Printemps* (both only ≤500 px PD), and a list and images of its 8 paintings and 13 drawings.
4. **National Museum of Serbia**: narodnimuzej.rs. Ask for the Bojidar portrait (NMS 34_912).
5. **Musée du château, Nérac / CNAP**: chateau.musee@ville-nerac.fr. Ask for *Georgette* (POP 1071×1200 © J. Jacob).
6. **Musée Ziem, Martigues**: musee@ville-martigues.fr. Ask for the 2024×2808 POP file of ZP 147 and its licence.
7. **National Gallery Athens**: nationalgallery.gr/en/epikoinonia/. The image is CC BY-NC-ND; ask for permission to resize.
8. **Krasnoyarsk Surikov Museum**: info@art-museum24.ru (the site has a "Заказать изображение" order form). Ask for Grenade and Madame Po.

11. **Saratov Radishchev Museum**: the Saintes Femmes sketch. Confirm the holding and the inventory number.
