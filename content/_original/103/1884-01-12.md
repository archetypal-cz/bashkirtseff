[^5]: The verses are a mock chanson in the style of a Montmartre street-song, with refrains ending in Montmartre and then rue Legendre (Marie writes "Legeindre", "Lengendre"), where the Bastien-Lepage brothers lived; "geindre" (to whine) is the pun. The song mocks the painter Jules Bastien-Lepage; "son frère était architèque" is his brother Emile, the architect, and "architèque" is a comic rhyme on "astèque" (Aztec, slang for an odd-looking little man). "Adolphe" is the name the song gives Jules; Marie later uses it as a nickname for him. The signature "Manet" (the painter had died in April 1883) is a mock attribution.
