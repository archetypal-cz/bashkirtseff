# Lundi 18 décembre 1882

%% 097.0265 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% 2026-02-02T15:00:00 LAN: "rue de Seze" - exhibition venue; Cercle des arts libéraux %%

[^1]: The exhibition in the rue de Sèze, where the dealer Georges Petit had opened his gallery in 1882.
