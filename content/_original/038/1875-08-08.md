%% 2026-10-21T12:58:37 RSR: Croesus and the Delphic oracle, see the footnote. "J'ai dit que les cartes avaient menti" (0201, struck through) refers back to her card readings of the night before (038/1875-08-07: "depuis dix heures et demie je fais les cartes"). %%
J'étais aussi injuste de faire des reproches aux cartes, que Crésus à l'oracle. Crésus et moi nous avons mal compris.[^038.0202.1]

[^038.0202.1]: Croesus, king of Lydia in the 6th century BC, asked the oracle at Delphi whether he should make war on the Persians and was told that if he did, he would destroy a great empire. He attacked, and the empire he destroyed was his own (Herodotus, Histories I.53 and I.91). Marie applies the story to her own misreading of the cards.
