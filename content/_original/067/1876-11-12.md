Nadine's children arrive from Tchernakovka estate. Marie creates spectacle in gray traveling dress and black velvet bonnet, drawing crowds to windows. She inducts Lola and Nadine into the "Coeur d'Argent" society. The expensive journey to Vienna costs 500 rubles (paid by herself). Kousma, the lunatic valet who wept in the garden after his wedding, escorts the horses with Chocolat. Marie's final night with Pacha lasts until 3 AM, reading him touching passages from her journal and complaining about "l'homme que vous savez" (Antonelli). The dramatic departure scene includes missing luggage, Marie's theatrical Italian eloquence for the amused public, and the poignant final goodbye - Pacha's "Baiser de chien, fidèle et respectueux" (Dog's kiss, faithful and respectful). Her reflection shows complex emotions: pride in inspiring devotion, recognition of deliberate cruelty, yet genuine respect for his "cœur d'or, caractère loyal, esprit droit" (heart of gold, loyal character, honest spirit). The departure marks the end of her Russian period and the beginning of her return to European society. )
