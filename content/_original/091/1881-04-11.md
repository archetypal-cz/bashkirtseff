# Lundi 11 avril 1881

Encore à la Chamre. Question Andrieux et conseil municipal[^2]. Bescherelles nous place dans la loge diplomatique, grande séance mais ennuyeuse. Le soir aux Variétés[^3]. Judic et Dupuis sont des comédiens si fins, si parfaits que la pièce insignifiante amuse tout le temps.

%% 2026-10-02T15:33:44 RSR: CORRECTION of the source footnote below: Andrieux is the biological father of Louis Aragon, not of Georges Sorel (standard Aragon biographies; not verified against the print), and he did not leave the prefecture in early 1881 (the Chamber debate Marie attends is dated April 1881; exact departure date not verified). %%
[^2]: Louis Andrieux (1840–1931): radical politician and prefect of police of Paris (1879–1881). The Andrieux question referred to controversies surrounding his tenure as prefect, raised in the Chamber and the municipal council; the exact date of his departure from the prefecture later in 1881 is not verified here. He is known from standard Louis Aragon biographies as Aragon's biological father (not verified against the print); the earlier statement that he was the secret father of the anarchist Georges Sorel was wrong. The municipal council debate was a perennial parliamentary battle between republican factions.

[^3]: The Théâtre des Variétés on the Boulevard Montmartre: one of Paris's most popular boulevard theatres, home to operetta and light comedy. Anna Judic (1849–1911) and José Dupuis (1833–1900) were its stars.
