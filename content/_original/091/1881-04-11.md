# Lundi 11 avril 1881

%% 091.0465 %%
%% [#Chambre_des_Deputes](../_glossary/places/social/CHAMBRE_DES_DEPUTES.md) [#Andrieux](../_glossary/people/mentioned/ANDRIEUX.md) [#Bescherelle](../_glossary/people/mentioned/BESCHERELLE.md) [#Theatre_des_Varietes](../_glossary/places/theaters/THEATRE_DES_VARIETES.md) [#Judic](../_glossary/people/mentioned/JUDIC.md) [#Dupuis](../_glossary/people/mentioned/DUPUIS.md) %%
%% [#Theater_theme](../_glossary/culture/themes/THEATER_THEME.md) %%
Encore à la Chamre. Question Andrieux et conseil municipal[^2]. Bescherelles nous place dans la loge diplomatique, grande séance mais ennuyeuse. Le soir aux Variétés[^3]. Judic et Dupuis sont des comédiens si fins, si parfaits que la pièce insignifiante amuse tout le temps.

[^2]: Louis Andrieux (1840–1931): radical politician and prefect of police (1879–1881), later revealed to have been the secret father of the anarchist Georges Sorel. The Andrieux question referred to controversies surrounding his tenure; he was removed from his prefecture in early 1881. The municipal council debate was a perennial parliamentary battle between republican factions.

[^3]: The Théâtre des Variétés on the Boulevard Montmartre: one of Paris's most popular boulevard theatres, home to operetta and light comedy. Anna Judic (1849–1911) and José Dupuis (1833–1900) were its stars.
