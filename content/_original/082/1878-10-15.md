# Mardi, 15 octobre 1878

%% 082.0630 %%
%% 2026-02-03T10:00:00 LAN: "fichue existence" vs "existence fichue" - damn existence vs damned/finished existence - wordplay on adjective position changing meaning %%
%% 2026-02-09T15:55:00 RSR: Brilliant wordplay on French adjective position: "fichue existence" (damn existence - adjective before noun = intensifier) vs. "existence fichue" (finished/ruined existence - adjective after noun = literal meaning). Marie is grateful her situation is merely cursed rather than destroyed - a mature philosophical distinction between difficult circumstances and complete despair. %%
%% 2026-10-21T15:48:32 RSR: footnote 82.630.1 revised in the 071–106 footnote review — rewritten: dropped the reading of her relief. %%
C'est une fichue existence[^82.630.1], bienheureuse encore de pouvoir ne pas dire: une existence fichue.

[^82.630.1]: Marie plays on French adjective placement: *fichue existence* (adjective before the noun) = «a damned existence»; *existence fichue* (adjective after) = «a ruined existence».
