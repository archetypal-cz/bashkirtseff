## Overview

%% GLO_RUE_DE_BOULOGNE.0002 %%
%% 2026-10-01T14:00:00 RSR: From the diary only: the address 10 bis rue de Boulogne is where Marie sends her letters and watercolour to Paul de Cassagnac (075.0075, the address in his own letter; 078.0087, 1878-02-08; 081.0131, 1878-06-29). No secondary source consulted. %%
**Rue de Boulogne**, Paris: street of Paul de Cassagnac's residence, **10 bis rue de Boulogne**, to which Marie addresses her mail to him.

%% GLO_RUE_DE_BOULOGNE.0003 %%
## In Marie's diary

%% GLO_RUE_DE_BOULOGNE.0004 %%
- 075.0075 (1877-09-29 entry): the address "10^bis^ rue de Boulogne" closes Cassagnac's letter of 19 September 1877.
- 078.0087 (1878-02-08): Marie sends the comic watercolour at midnight "rue de Boulogne 10 bis".
- 081.0131 (1878-06-29): she addresses a letter to the editors of "Le Pays", thinking it would not reach "le Défunt" (evidently Cassagnac) at rue de Boulogne.
