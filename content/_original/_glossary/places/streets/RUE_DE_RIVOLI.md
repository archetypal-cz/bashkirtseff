# Rue de Rivoli

## Overview
The rue de Rivoli in Paris, along the Tuileries and the Louvre. Marie drives out of it onto the place de la Concorde when she sees Cassagnac in May 1879 (085.0054), and watches a procession in January 1883 from M. Marinovitch's windows, «rue de Rivoli 240» (097.0362). Not the duc de Rivoli (Masséna), whom Marie names often in 1879–1880.
