# Avenue Marbeuf (Paris)

## Overview

The Paris avenue where Marie, in September 1874, looks for a horse. The street is named only as the address of horse dealers.

## In Marie's diary

- 1874-09-25 (024.0112, Paris): after saying goodbye to Ferry she goes «chez Fourmi, dans l'avenue Marbeuf, un [Rayé: loueur] marchand de chevaux. Il n'a rien de bon, mais cela me fait du bien d'aller dans les écuries.» Then to Maurice's, where she falls for a chocolate-coloured eight-year-old for twelve thousand francs.
- 1874-09-29 (024.0162): Moreno «me conseille un certain Hulot, avenue Marbeuf pour les chevaux».
