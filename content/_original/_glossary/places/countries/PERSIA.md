# Persia

## Overview

The country (now Iran), under the Qajars in Marie's day. The only tagged mention is in a dinner guest's biography: on 6 November 1874 in Nice «M. Lubimoff et fils, un vieux sénateur de Pétersbourg et ami d'Anitchkoff avant que ce dernier fût ambassadeur en Perse» (025.0148). The Shah's visit to Vienna in 1873 is in the separate entries for the Shah of Persia.
