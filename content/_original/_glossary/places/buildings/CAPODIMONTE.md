## Overview

%% GLO_CAPODIMONTE.0002 %%
Capodimonte is the Bourbon royal palace, with its large wooded park, on the hill above Naples. Per Wikipedia, «Museo di Capodimonte», it was begun in 1738 for Charles of Bourbon, King of Naples, to house the Farnese art collection and as a hunting lodge; after 1860 it passed to the House of Savoy and is today a museum. The porcelain made in the royal factory there (Capodimonte ware) is a separate matter; see below.

## Relevance to Marie

%% GLO_CAPODIMONTE.0004 %%
On 16 April 1876 Marie, in Naples with her mother, Walitsky, Altamura and Chocolat, drives out to the palace: «Nous avons visité le palais de Capo di Monte. Le parc est quelque chose d'admirable, j'en suis charmée» (058.0088). Her verdict on the building, in the comparison with Rome: «grand et magnifique, mais assez barbare… un peu caserne» (058.0089).
