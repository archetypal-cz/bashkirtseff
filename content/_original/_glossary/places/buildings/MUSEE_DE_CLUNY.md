# Musée de Cluny

%% 2026-11-10T08:08:56 RSR: User report fix. Was filed as a person (people/mentioned); it is a museum, moved to places/buildings. The generic alias «Musee» was dropped. %%

## Overview

The Paris museum of medieval and Renaissance art in the Latin Quarter (5th arrondissement), housed in the Hôtel de Cluny, the late-fifteenth-century town house of the abbots of Cluny, built beside the ruins of the Gallo-Roman baths (thermes de Cluny). The collector Alexandre Du Sommerard installed his collection of medieval objects there in 1832; after his death in 1842 the State bought the house and the collection, and the museum opened in 1844 under his son Edmond Du Sommerard, who directed it until 1885. Today it is the Musée de Cluny – musée national du Moyen Âge (en.wikipedia «Musée de Cluny»; fr.wikipedia «Musée de Cluny – Musée national du Moyen Âge»).
