# Consulate (Russian)

## Overview
The Russian consulate, mostly the consulate general in Paris, where Marie has papers legalised and signed in the inheritance affairs of 1883 (099.0348–0349, 100.0288, 101.0427, 101.0430, 101.0440, 101.0457). The Russian Orthodox Easter service of 1877 in Naples, held in «la chancellerie du Consulat» (070.0264), is the Russian consulate there. Not the Consulat of French history (1799–1804), which Marie names for a dress style (087.0853, 091.0213, untagged).
