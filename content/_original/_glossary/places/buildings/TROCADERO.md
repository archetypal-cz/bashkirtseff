# Trocadéro

## Overview
The Trocadéro hill in Paris and, from 1878, the Palais du Trocadéro built for the Exposition universelle of 1878, with its great concert hall («la salle des fêtes du Trocadéro», 082.0431). In 1873 Marie looks over Paris from the hill (008.0114); in 1877–78 she follows the building works and the Exposition (076.0389, 079.0121, 081.0144); later she goes to concerts and charity matinées there (099.0232, 100.0041).
