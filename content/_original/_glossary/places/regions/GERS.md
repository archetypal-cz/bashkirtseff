# Gers

## Overview

The département of the Gers in Gascony (chief town Auch), Paul de Cassagnac's political fief: he sat for it in the Chamber (for Condom, then from 1881 for Mirande; Assemblée nationale, Sycomore «Granier de Cassagnac»), and his family's house at Couloumé is there ([CASSAGNAC](../../people/recurring/CASSAGNAC.md)).

## References in Diary

- 083.0019 (23 October 1878): «les populations du Gers ont demandé à grands cris de voir Mme Paul» (Cassagnac's new wife).
