# Splendide Hôtel (Paris, 1874)

## Overview

A Paris hotel with a lift (*ascenseur*), where the Bashkirtseffs were on their arrival in Paris in May 1874 (probably lodging there). On 19 May Paul comes to the Russian restaurant dinner «avec Striker (nous les avons rencontré le jour où nous arrivâmes, dans l'ascenseur du Splendide Hôtel)» (019.0288). Probably the same house as [Hôtel Splendide](HOTEL_SPLENDIDE.md), the Paris hotel Émile d'Audiffret uses in 1875-76 (same city, same name; not stated by Marie). Merge proposed (confidence 0.7).
