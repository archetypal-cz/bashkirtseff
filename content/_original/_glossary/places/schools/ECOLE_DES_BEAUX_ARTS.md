# École des Beaux-Arts (Paris)

## Overview
The École nationale des Beaux-Arts, rue Bonaparte, Paris, the state art school (closed to women until 1897) and a venue for exhibitions: the Prix de Rome entries (075.0375, 085.0578), the portrait exhibitions of 1883 (099.0191, 100.0070), the Manet retrospective of January 1884 (102.0649) and the Gambetta competition of June 1884 (104.0502). Not the Nice drawing school of 1874 (culture/history/BEAUX_ARTS) or the Madrid academy Marie visits in 1881 (093.0175, untagged).
