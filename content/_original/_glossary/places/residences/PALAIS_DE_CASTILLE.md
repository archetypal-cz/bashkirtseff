## Overview

%% GLO_PALAIS_DE_CASTILLE.0002 %%
%% [#La_Reine](../hotels/LA_REINE.md) [#Paris](../cities/PARIS.md) %%
%% 2026-09-07T12:00:00 RSR: Per existing entry LA_REINE and the footnotes on 091/1881-03-27 and 095/1882-05-01. Marie names the salon by its hostess ("la Reine"), not by the building. %%
The Palais de Castille, on the Avenue Kléber in [Paris](../cities/PARIS.md), was the residence in exile of Queen Isabel II of Spain, whom Marie calls simply [la Reine](../hotels/LA_REINE.md). Formerly the Hôtel Basilewski, it housed the exiled Spanish Bourbon court after 1868 and served as a royalist and Bonapartist salon; the building is today the Peninsula Paris hotel.

## In Marie's Diary

%% GLO_PALAIS_DE_CASTILLE.0004 %%
Marie and her mother went there as part of their campaign to enter the most aristocratic Parisian circles. On 1 May 1882 she notes in the margin that she went to the Queen's in the hope of meeting Cassagnac. See [La Reine](../hotels/LA_REINE.md) for the visits and the Queen herself.
