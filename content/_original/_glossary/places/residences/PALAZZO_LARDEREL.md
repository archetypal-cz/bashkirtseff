# Palazzo Larderel (Florence)

## Overview
The Larderel family palace at via de' Tornabuoni 19, Florence, formerly Palazzo Giacomini, designed by Giovanni Antonio Dosio and completed in 1580; the Larderel family bought it in 1839 (en.wikipedia «Palazzo Larderel»; RIBApix record «Palazzo Larderel, also known as Palazzo Giacomini»). In 1877 it is the home of Count Alexandre de Larderel's mother.

## Relevance to Marie
- On arriving in Florence: «je fais passer devant le n°19 via Tornabuoni, palazzo Larderel. Trois fenêtres de façade mais massif et beau» (071.0066).
