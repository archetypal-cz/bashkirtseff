# Camden Place

## Overview
Camden Place, the house at Chislehurst, Kent, where Napoleon III lived in exile and died (1873) and where the Empress Eugénie lived until 1880. At the Prince Imperial's funeral in July 1879 Marie says she was «dedans à Camden» in spirit while Cassagnac stood outside (085.0570). See [#Chislehurst](../cities/CHISLEHURST.md).
