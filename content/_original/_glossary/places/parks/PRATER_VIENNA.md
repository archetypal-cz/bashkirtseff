## The Prater, Vienna (1873)

%% GLO_PRATER_VIENNA.0002 %%
The great park and promenade of Vienna, which Marie drives through during her stay for the Vienna Exposition in August 1873. Not Marie's dog Prater ([#Prater](../../people/mentioned/PRATER.md)).
