# Notre-Dame de Paris

## Overview
The cathedral of Notre-Dame in Paris. Marie climbs to the top in October 1878 (083.0007), compares a face to one of its roof monsters (084.0489) and goes there with the Gavini in June 1879 (085.0296). Victor Hugo's novel of the same name (086.0659) is not tagged here.
