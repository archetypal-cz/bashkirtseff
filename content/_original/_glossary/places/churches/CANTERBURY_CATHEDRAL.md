# Canterbury Cathedral

## Overview

The cathedral of the archbishop of Canterbury, seat of the leader of the Church of England and of the Anglican Communion; formally the Cathedral and Metropolitical Church of Christ, Canterbury, on the site of one of the oldest Christian structures in England, part of a World Heritage Site (Wikipedia, «Canterbury Cathedral», https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canterbury_Cathedral). Not a person.

## Relevance to Marie

On 16 September 1874, staying with the Howards at Canterbury, Marie writes only: «Canterbury, je vais à la cathédrale avec Lise et Hélène» (023.0661). No impression of the building is recorded.
