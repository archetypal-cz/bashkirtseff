# Vichy

## Overview

The Bourbonnais spa town. It appears three times, always as a cure someone else takes or is proposed.

## In Marie's diary

- 1874-06-22 (020.0379, Spa): her mother, restless about where to go that summer, «lorsqu'on lui proposait Vichy elle répondait par une négation furieuse», and now that they are at Spa says it is a hole at the end of the world.
- 1876-06-22 (062.0558, Nice): «Bibi part demain pour Vichy» ([Bihovetz](../../people/mentioned/BIHOVETZ.md); Marie is waiting for a letter foretold by the cards).
- 1879-09-17 (086.0291, Paris): about Cassagnac: «Mme Paul est à Vichy (foie et estomac) et M. Paul au château de la Reine» (Mme Paul is Julia Cassagnac, per the RSR note).
