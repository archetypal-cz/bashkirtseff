# Le Havre

## Overview

The Channel port. The only tagged mention is a relay in Bojidar's travels: on 21 August 1883 «Bojidar retour d'Aix via Havre. Il a l'air mélancolique, c'est l'amour pour Sarah qui l'aura repris» (101.0221). Bojidar Karageorgevitch is on his way back from Aix-les-Bains; Marie attributes his melancholy to his love for Sarah (Bernhardt).
