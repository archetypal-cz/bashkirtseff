# Philadelphia

## Overview
The city in Pennsylvania that held the United States Centennial Exposition from 10 May to 10 November 1876 (en.wikipedia «Centennial Exposition»).

## Relevance to Marie
After a noisy dinner and a play, *L'Oncle Sam*, at the Théâtre-Français in Nice on 8 May 1875, she writes that it makes her want to go to America: «et j'irai en Amérique, l'été prochain à l'Exposition de Philadelphie» (032.0352). She never went; in the summer of 1876 she was in Russia and Germany (carnets 062–066).
