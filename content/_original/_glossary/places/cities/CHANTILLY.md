## Overview

%% GLO_CHANTILLY.0002 %%
%% 2026-10-01T15:00:00 RSR: Identification from the diary text only: the town north of Paris with the Condé château and the racecourse. No secondary source consulted. %%
**Chantilly** is the town north of Paris, known for the château of the princes de Condé, its stables and its racecourse (and, as a trade name, for Chantilly lace). It is tagged here as a place; in the diary the word is also once an adjective for lace (016/1874-02-04, "fichu de Chantilly", untagged).
