## Description
**A place, not a person:** Cancello, a railway station north-east of Naples on the line to Caserta and Rome. The first section of the Rome–Cassino–Naples railway, Naples–Cancello–Caserta, opened on 20 December 1843, and a branch ran from Cancello to Nola from 1846 (Wikipedia, «Rome–Cassino–Naples railway»). On 12 February 1877 Marie and her mother board Larderel's train at Naples on the pretext of fetching her aunt, supposedly stranded at Cancello (068.1378); the express does not stop there (068.1402) and they get off further on to take a train back to Naples (068.1436). «Cancello» becomes her shorthand for the episode: «s'il n'y avait pas eu de voyage à Cancello» (068.1499), «A Cancello, à Monaco, au bal masqué» (075.0497), «l'aventure de Cancello» (074.0179).
