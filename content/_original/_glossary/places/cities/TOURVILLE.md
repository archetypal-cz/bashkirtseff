# Avenue de Tourville (Paris)

## Overview

Streets in Paris, on the way to the Trocadéro, where Marie goes for a drive: on 18 August 1881, after Julian's studio, «ma tante, Cerny et moi allons nous promener en fiacre sur les rives de la Seine, du côté du Trocadéro, les avenues de Tourville, etc. Ravissant quartier qu'on ne connaît pas assez» (092.0429). She admires the sky and the fine tones of the distance, which she thinks Breslau used to do.

Filing: a street (the entry sits in places/cities); a move to places/streets, with the name «Avenue de Tourville», is proposed.
