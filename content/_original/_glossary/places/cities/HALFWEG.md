## Overview

%% GLO_HALFWEG.0002 %%
%% 2026-10-01T15:00:00 RSR: Identification from the diary text only (letter 080.0171); geography from general knowledge. No secondary source consulted. %%
**Halfweg** is the Dutch village between Haarlem and Amsterdam, in North Holland. In the diary it is the place from which Jeanne Suermondt writes (letter of 14 May 1878).
