# Mont-de-Marsan

## Overview
Mont-de-Marsan, préfecture of the Landes, south-western France, from where Multedo writes his letters to Marie in July–August 1878 (081.0480, 081.0507, 081.0516, 081.0532–0533, 081.0648, 082.0161).
