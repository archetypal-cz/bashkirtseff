## Schwalbach, German spa (1875, 1877)

%% GLO_SCHWALBACH.0002 %%
The spa town near Schlangenbad and Wiesbaden where Marie spends the summer of 1875; she finds it a charm after the dull Schlangenbad ("A Schwalbach on voit des êtres vivants").
