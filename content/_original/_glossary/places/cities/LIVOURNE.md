## Overview

%% GLO_LIVOURNE.0002 %%
%% 2026-10-01T14:00:00 RSR: Livourne is Marie's French form of Livorno (Leghorn), the Tuscan port. Identified from the diary text only: 071.0409 (1877-05-22) ties it to the Larderel family; 075.0085 (1877-09-29) places Melissano and Larderel there. No secondary source consulted. %%
**Livourne** is the French name of Livorno, the Tuscan port city. In the diary it appears only as a place linked to the Larderel family (Marie's Alexandre).

%% GLO_LIVOURNE.0003 %%
## In Marie's diary

%% GLO_LIVOURNE.0004 %%
- 071.0409 (1877-05-22): Galula, speaking of Livourne, names the Larderels.
- 075.0085 (1877-09-29): Schininà tells Marie that Melissano is at Livourne with Larderel, where there is a rich American woman.
