# Saint-Jean (Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat)

## Overview
Fishing village and small harbour on the Cap Ferrat peninsula, east of Nice, beside the roadstead of Villefranche-sur-Mer. In 1875 it was a hamlet of the commune of Villefranche; it became a commune of its own only in 1904 (Wikipedia, "Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat"). Not to be confused with the Quai Saint-Jean in Paris.

## Relevance to Marie
Destination of the excursion planned by Audiffret on 12 June 1875 ("il nous mène mardi... à Saint-Jean") and made on Tuesday 15 June 1875: dinner at an inn, a boat trip round the gulf, a moonlit terrace and the "philippine" game with Audiffret (034.0292-0298).
