# Konotop

## Overview
Konotop, a town in the Chernigov province of the Russian Empire (now Sumy oblast, Ukraine), on the Kiev–Kursk railway; the station nearest to the Bashkirtseff estates in 1876 (064.0457, «station de chemin de fer voisine du bien de mon père»). Étienne telegraphs from there in August 1876 (064.0388); in 1878 «Mme Tarnowsky, une aristocrate de Konotop» comes to lunch (083.0058).
