# Gulf of Finland

## Overview

The arm of the Baltic on which St Petersburg lies (Wikipedia, «Gulf of Finland»). Marie sees it on a single excursion to the [Islands](THE_ISLANDS.md) on 7 August 1876 (064.0447, Petersburg): «Après dîner, nous allâmes aux Iles et à la pointe de laquelle on admire le coucher du soleil sur le golfe de Finlande.» She adds that they arrived after sunset and that it was cold for July.
