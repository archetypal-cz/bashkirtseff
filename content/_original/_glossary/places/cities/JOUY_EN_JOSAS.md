# Jouy-en-Josas

## Overview

The village near Versailles where the Canroberts have their country house, and where Marie spends many days from July 1883 to September 1884. She first sees it with her aunt on 8 July 1883: «Ma tante me mène chez les Canrobert, près de Versailles dans le village de Jouy en Josas. Charmante propriété. Un ancien pavillon de chasse approprié avec le génie anglais du confort, quoiqu'assez simple» (100.0294). The paragraph footnote at 102.0225 gives the location (south-west of Paris, in the Bièvre valley) and notes that the Mallets also had a country house there. Marie writes «Jouy». See [the Canrobert family](../../people/mentioned/CANROBERT.md), [Claire Canrobert](../../people/recurring/CLAIRE_CANROBERT.md).

## In Marie's diary

- 1883-08-30 (101.0281): she stays on for some days «comme à une pénitence»; 09-06 (101.0311): rides to Paris and back (Meudon woods, see [Meudon](MEUDON.md)); 09-17 (101.0379): «le tableau rêvé à Jouy»; 10-14 (101.0604): retouches the portrait of Louis, croquet.
- 1883-11-11 (102.0225): dines there, «je crois que j'aime vraiment ces gens-là»; 11-16, 11-17 (102.0251-0260): landscape painting in the rain; «Jouy, la campagne, fait très vivement sentir la beauté des tableaux de Bastien».
- 1884-03-18 (103.0553): a day at Jouy looking for her blossoming orchard; 1884-07-12, 07-25, 07-28, 07-30 (105.0145-0359): the summer visits and the painting there; 1884-09-21 (106.0099): «Corvée suprême. Déjeuner à Jouy. La petite a copié le tableau, mal.»
