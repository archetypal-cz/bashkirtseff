# Nijni-Novgorod

## Overview

The Volga city known for its great annual trade fair. It is mentioned twice, both times in connection with Russia.

## In Marie's diary

- 1875-06-19 (034.0451, Nice): talking of Russia, Marie says that Émile d'[Audiffret](../../people/recurring/EMILE_D_AUDIFFRET.md) «veut aller voir la foire de Nijni-Novgorod».
- 1876-08-12 (064.0550, Moscow): musing on what the Emperor could do, she says that if he spoke to his people «à Pétersbourg, à Moscou, à Nijni-Novgorod et à Kiev» all Russia would swear loyalty, and that the merchants «(et ils ne sont pas la minorité en Russie)» must be addressed through their interest.
