# Trieste

## Overview

The Adriatic port, then Austrian. The only mention is a piece of Russian gossip in Nice: on 1 December 1874 «Nous avons parlé de Schestakoff qui est agent militaire à Trieste comme Wittgenstein l'est à Paris» (027.0107), i.e. a Russian military attaché, like Prince Wittgenstein in Paris.
