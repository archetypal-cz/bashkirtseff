# Vesuve

Mount Vesuvius (French *le Vésuve*), the volcano above the Bay of Naples. Marie sees it during her stay in Naples in February 1877 (carnets 068–069; «le Vésuve est là», 12 February 1877) and names it again among the sights of Italy in June 1877 (carnet 072).
