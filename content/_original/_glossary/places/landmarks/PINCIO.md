## Overview

%% GLO_PINCIO.0002 %%
%% 2026-10-01T15:00:00 RSR: Identification from the diary text only (051.0421, 052.0153, 052.0321); the Pincian Hill and its promenade are well known. No secondary source consulted. %%
The **Pincio** is the Pincian Hill in Rome, the fashionable promenade and public garden above the Piazza del Popolo, where society drove and walked in the afternoon. It is a hill/garden, not a city (the entry was misfiled under places/cities).

## In Marie's diary

%% GLO_PINCIO.0004 %%
- 051.0421 (1876-01-06), 052.0153 (1876-01-14), 052.0321 (1876-01-20), Rome winter 1876: Marie drives to the Pincio; "cette promenade me raccommode un peu avec Rome", with a crowd of carriages in which she sees the King. It is a regular stop on her Roman rounds.
