# Théâtre-Français (Nice)

## Overview

The French-language theatre of Nice, which Marie calls «le Théâtre Français» (or «théâtre français»). It is not the Paris Comédie-Française (see [Comédie-Française](COMEDIE_FRANCAISE.md)); in Nice it stood beside the Italian opera house (see [Italian Opera, Nice](ITALIAN_OPERA_NICE.md)). Its address and history are not yet researched.

## What the diary shows

- Marie names it as a Nice theatre: «une loge pareille à celle que nous avions au Théâtre Français à Nice» (054.0325, Rome, 20 February 1876).
- It kept a season: «Ce soir ouverture du Théâtre Français» (009.0162, 13 September 1873).
- Its bill mixed comedy, vaudeville and opera sung in French: «La joie de la maison, Le chapeau de paille», with the actor Ravel (001.0008, 12 January 1873); «Héloïse et Abélard» (002.0169, 3 March 1873); «Robin des bois», Weber (004.0325, 10 May 1873); «La Traviata en français», in a ground-floor box Barnola left them (061.0218, 27 May 1876).
- Visiting Paris actors played there: «au Théâtre français, … où Agar, de la Comédie française, dans une représentation» (068.0915, 1 February 1877), which shows the two houses are distinct.
