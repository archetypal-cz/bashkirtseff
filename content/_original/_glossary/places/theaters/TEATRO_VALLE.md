# Teatro Valle (Rome)

## Overview

The Teatro Valle, near Sant'Andrea della Valle in the centre of Rome, built for the Capranica family to designs by Tommaso Morelli and opened on 7 January 1727; rebuilt in masonry by Giuseppe Valadier and Gaspare Salvi around 1818-1822. Rossini's *La Cenerentola* had its premiere there in 1817; in the second half of the nineteenth century it turned increasingly to spoken drama and lighter fare (en.wikipedia «Teatro Valle»; FAI, «Teatro Valle»).

## Relevance to Marie

In the spring of 1876 the Valle is the family's other Roman theatre after the Apollo, with French operetta on the bill and the Caccia-Club in its *barcaccia*:

- 10 March 1876: «nous allons au Théâtre Valle»; Pandola and Zucchini in the club's barcaccia (055/1876-03-10).
- 11 March: first night of *La Vie parisienne* (055/1876-03-11); 23 March: *La Fille de Mme Angot*, an evening of tension with Pietro Antonelli (055/1876-03-23), afterwards recalled as the evening of «cette impertinence au Valle» (055/1876-03-25, 056/1876-03-27).
- 10 April: *Madame l'Archiduc* «au bénéfice de la première chanteuse Preziosi» (057/1876-04-10).
- 27 April: Pietro still resents «la soirée du Valle où vous avez fait cette supposition si humiliante» (059.0500).
