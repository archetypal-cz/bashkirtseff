# Comédie-Française

## Overview

France's national theatre in Paris, in the Salle Richelieu on the rue de Richelieu, also called the Théâtre-Français. Marie mostly writes «le Français» or «le théâtre Français», sometimes «la Comédie Française». Its general administrator through all the diary's Paris years was Émile Perrin (1814–1885), in office from 8 July 1871 to 8 October 1885 (en.wikipedia «List of administrators of the Comédie-Française»; «Émile Perrin»).

Not to be confused with the Théâtre-Français of Nice, which Marie also calls «le Théâtre Français» (see [Théâtre-Français (Nice)](THEATRE_FRANCAIS.md)).

## Relevance to Marie

From 1878 on, an evening «au Français» is part of the family's Paris social round, often in the box of the Gavinis or other friends. The Tuesday subscription nights matter to her: Mme Randouin «nous parlait de ses démarches auprès de M. Perrin pour avoir une loge au Français le mardi» (086.0303, 19 September 1879); «nous menons [le père Gavini] à la Comédie Française pour parler d'un abonnement aux mardis» (085.0636, 6 August 1879).
