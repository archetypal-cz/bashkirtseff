# Teatro Pagliano (Florence)

## Overview
Florentine theatre built for Girolamo Pagliano on the site of the old Stinche prison, opened in 1854 and renamed Teatro Verdi in 1901 (en.wikipedia «Teatro Verdi (Florence)»).

## Relevance to Marie
- September 1875, during the Michelangelo centenary: the family is given a box («la clef de la loge et deux cartes d'entrée», 043.0198) and goes «à huit heures … au théâtre Pagliano» (043.0206).
- 1 May 1877: with the Pergola closed, she hears Wagner's *Rienzi* there with Marcuard and understands nothing of the music; «Ce théâtre n'est pas à la mode» (071.0083–0084).
