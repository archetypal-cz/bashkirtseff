# Théâtre du Gymnase

## Overview

The Théâtre du Gymnase (Gymnase-Dramatique), on the boulevard de Bonne-Nouvelle in Paris, opened in 1820. Under its director Adolphe Montigny it became the house of the modern society comedy and drama (Scribe, Augier, Dumas fils, Sardou); Meilhac and Halévy's *Frou-Frou* was first given there in 1869 (Wikipedia, "Théâtre du Gymnase Marie Bell"; "Frou-Frou (comédie)").

## Relevance to Marie

One of the Paris theatres she goes to for contemporary plays, from her stay at the Grand Hôtel in 1875 to the 1880s.
