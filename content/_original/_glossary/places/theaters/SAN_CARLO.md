# San Carlo

The Teatro di San Carlo in Naples, the city's great opera house (opened 1737). In February 1877 Marie goes to its charity *veglione* (Carnival masked ball, 10 February; see [Veglione](../../culture/social_customs/VEGLIONE.md)) and hears «Les Guaranis» (Gomes, *Il Guarany*) there on 11 February (carnet 068); she returns for *La Forza del destino* on 25 February (carnet 069).
