# Villa Savelieff

**Research Status**: Stub
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-31
**Diary Coverage**: Carnet 005 (June 1873, Nice)

## Overview

The Villa Savelieff was the Nice residence of M. and Mme Savelieff, a Russian family in the Bashkirtseff social circle. Mme Savelieff died there in May 1873 after a prolonged illness. After her death, the Patton family temporarily moved into the villa (June 1873) while their own house was being renovated, despite the painful memories the place held for them.

## Diary References

- **May 11, 1873** (004.0330): The Bashkirtseffs visit Mme Savelieff who is dying there.
- **June 9, 1873** (005.0224): The Patton family is staying at the Villa Savelieff while their house is being repaired.

## Research Notes

Savelieff (Савельєв) is a common Russian surname. The villa's exact location in Nice is unknown; it appears to have been in the vicinity of the Russian church and the Bashkirtseff residences of the period.
