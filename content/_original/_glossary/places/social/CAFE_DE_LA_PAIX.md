## Overview

The large café-restaurant on the ground floor of the Grand Hôtel in Paris, at the corner of the boulevard des Capucines and the place de l'Opéra (9th arrondissement). It opened in 1862 together with the hotel and became one of the best-known cafés of the grands boulevards, frequented by journalists, politicians and the boulevard public (en.wikipedia «Café de la Paix»; fr.wikipedia «Café de la Paix»).
