# Grand-16

## Overview
Not a person: the «Grand Seize», the most famous private dining room (cabinet particulier no. 16) of the Café Anglais on the boulevard des Italiens, Paris. en.wikipedia «Café Anglais» calls it «Le Grand 16»; the Tour d'Argent's history of the Café Anglais cellar calls it the «Grand Seize» salon. The entry is filed under people/ by an old auto-generated stub; see OPEN-ITEMS (glossary) for the move to places/social.

## Relevance to Marie
On 17 February 1878 Marie and her friends sent Kolémine and his companion ahead to the Café Anglais, promising to follow in ten minutes, ordered her favourite dishes and went home: «Kolémine mené comme un chien toute la soirée en sera pour un souper dans le Grand-16 et un affront devant les garçons» (078.0209). Three days later «agir comme pour le Grand-16 du Café Anglais» is her shorthand for making a rendezvous and not keeping it (078.0263, about Marcuard).
