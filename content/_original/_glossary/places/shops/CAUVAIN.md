# Cauvain (shop, Paris)

## Overview

A Paris shop where table services were ordered in September 1874 (a porcelain or glass merchant by implication). The diary gives only the name; it is not a person Marie knew. No entry for Cauvain was found in the Mon Journal indexes consulted.

## Relevance to Marie

On 29 September 1874, among the day's shopping errands (a cup at Baccarat, a horse): «Nous étions aussi chez Cauvain, là on a commandé des services de table etc.» (024.0160).
