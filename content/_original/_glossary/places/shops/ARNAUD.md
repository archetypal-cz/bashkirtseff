# Arnaud (bric-à-brac shop, Paris)

## What it is
An antique and bric-à-brac dealer in Paris where Marie and her mother buy two mantelpiece sets (*garnitures de cheminée*) on 29 September 1874, while furnishing for the winter: «Avec lui nous allons chez Arnaud, bric-à-brac, on y achète deux garnitures de cheminées» (024.0157; «lui» is the cab-driver they hire by the day, 024.0156). The Mon Journal t.4 index: «Arnaud, magasin d'antiquités, 161». The address is not given.

## Not to be confused with
[Arnaud de l'Ariège](../../people/recurring/ARNAUD_DE_L_ARIEGE.md) («Arnaud», «Joseph», 1879-1882), Marie's later admirer.
