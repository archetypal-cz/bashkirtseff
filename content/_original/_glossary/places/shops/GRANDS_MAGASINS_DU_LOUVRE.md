## Overview

%% GLO_GRANDS_MAGASINS_DU_LOUVRE.0002 %%
The Grands Magasins du Louvre were the large Paris department store on the rue de Rivoli, beside the Palais-Royal square and the Grand Hôtel du Louvre (not the museum, which is [#Louvre](../buildings/LOUVRE.md)). Per Wikipedia, «Louvre Saint-Honoré», the store opened in 1855 on the ground floor of the Pereire brothers' hotel and took the name Grands Magasins du Louvre from 1863; French sources name Alfred Chauchard and Auguste Hériot among its founders. It was one of the great fixed-price stores of the Second Empire and Third Republic, like [#Le Bon Marché](../theaters/LE_BON_MARCHE.md). (Dates as given by the sources consulted; they differ by a few years between them.)

## Relevance to Marie

%% GLO_GRANDS_MAGASINS_DU_LOUVRE.0004 %%
Marie uses «le Louvre» (or «le magasin du Louvre») for the store in contrast with the museum, which she also calls «le Louvre» (see the museum entry; the context decides). In 1874 it is the «paradis de Dina», the cousin's love of shops; in 1879 and 1884 she or her mother and the household buy fabric, a skirt and other plain goods there, while her dresses come from the couturiers.

## References in Diary

- 1874-05-16 (019.0229): shop-crawl in Paris with Dina: «où nous avons été au Louvre, au Bon Marché, au Petit Saint-Thomas, au Grand Saint-Paul…» (a list of department stores).
- 1874-05-23 (019.0318): «Nous rôdions, hélas ! autour du Louvre; le paradis de Dina».
- 1875-09-14 (043.0173): in Florence she says she would weep after three quarters of an hour at the «Louvre ou du Bon Marché, ou même chez Worth» (a store beside Worth).
- 1879-12-31 (087.0103): «Nous sommes allées au magasin du Louvre en sortant de l'atelier. Il faudrait Zola pour décrire cette foule agaçante, affairée, dégoûtante».
- 1884-07-07 (105.0074): «Ces dames vont au magasin du Louvre, je les prie de m'en rapporter trente mètres de mousseline blanche.»
- 1884-07-14 (105.0167): «que maman aille s'acheter une jupe de soixante francs au Louvre».
- Not this entry: 043.0115 («Pas le Louvre, le Louvre est incritiquable»: reads as the palace/museum), 003.0072 and 094.0389 (the Hôtel du Louvre, Naples).
