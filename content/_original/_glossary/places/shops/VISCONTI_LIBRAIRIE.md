# Visconti (librairie, Nice)

## Overview
Visconti's bookshop and reading room (librairie, «salon de lecture») in Nice, on the carnival route; it also sells carnival tickets and has a terrace overlooking the parade. Mon Journal indexes: «Visconti, salon de lecture» (t.1 p. 39, 43; t.2 pp. 74, 104, 240; t.4 p. 173; t.6 p. 141), «Visconti, librairie» (t.3 pp. 75-76, 78), «Visconti, libraire» (t.8 p. 232). Not to be confused with the old Roman [#Visconti](../../people/mentioned/VISCONTI.md) (baron Visconti), nor with the novel *Marco Visconti* (Tommaso Grossi) mentioned in 014/1873-12-15.
