# Au Bon Goût (Paris shop)

## Overview

A Paris shop named once by Marie, on 10 June 1874, in a list with the department store Bon Marché: «je passai une heure et demie au Bon Marché, au Bon Goût etc.», on a day when she also failed to order her riding habit (020.0200). It is a retail shop not otherwise identified; no address, owner or goods are given.

## Identity uncertain

We are not sure which shop this is. The diary names it only as one of the places of a shopping afternoon with the Bon Marché; the Mon Journal indices we checked have no entry for it. If you can identify it, we would be glad to hear from you.

## In Marie's diary

- 1874-06-10 (020.0200, Paris): the only mention.
