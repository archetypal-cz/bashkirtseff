## Lambert

%% GLO_LAMBERT.0002 %%
A shop of curiosities and fine goods. Two mentions, possibly two shops: in June 1873 Marie arranges her dressing room with a mirror «que j'ai achetée chez Lambert en quittant Acqua Viva» (006.0090, Nice, 26 June 1873); on 6 November 1874 she waits outside «chez Lambert, un magasin de curiosités et de belles choses» while her mother buys oysters, when a boy rushes up (025.0139). No further details.
