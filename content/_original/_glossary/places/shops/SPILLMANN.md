# Spillmann (Rome)

## Overview
Confectioner's and restaurant in Rome frequented by the foreign colony in the 1870s. Kernberger (2013, glossary): "Spillman's: restaurant in Rome".

## Relevance to Marie
A meeting point during her Roman stays: on 26 December 1876 «Chez Spillman ce fut un presque attendrissement» (068.0301); on 7 February 1877 she learns there that a great veglione is being given at the Apollo that night, and stays in Rome for it (068.0991). From Naples on 10 April 1877 she imagines Larderel making the round of Rome, «de l'hôtel de Rome … du Corso, chez Spillmann, chez Mortes, à l'Opéra, au Pincio, au Champ de Courses» (070.0490).
