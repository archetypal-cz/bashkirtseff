# Spillmann (Rome)

## Overview
Confectioner's and restaurant in Rome frequented by the foreign colony in the 1870s. Kernberger (2013, glossary): "Spillman's: restaurant in Rome".

## Relevance to Marie
A meeting point during her Roman stays: on 26 December 1876 «Chez Spillman ce fut un presque attendrissement» (068.0170); on 7 February 1877 she learns there that a great veglione is being given at the Apollo that night, and stays in Rome for it (068.0648).

- 1876-12-26 (068.0170), 1877-02-08 (068.0648)
