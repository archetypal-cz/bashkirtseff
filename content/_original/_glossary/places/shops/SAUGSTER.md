# Saugster (London shop)

## Overview
A London umbrella and parasol maker whom Marie patronises in September 1874; the Mon Journal t.4 index has «Saugster, magasin, 137».

## Relevance to Marie
On 8 September 1874, in London, she lists what she has bought or ordered: «costume - Wolmershausen : 11 £; amazone - Paole : 10 £; waterproof - Scott : 3 £; ombrelle - Saugster : 4 £; chapeau Brown : 2 £; chapeau Harman : 18 s; cravache - Ellam : 2 £ 4 s» (023.0598). So Saugster supplied a parasol (*ombrelle*) for £4. See [SCOTT](../../people/mentioned/SCOTT.md) for the waterproof maker and [THE_CITY](../neighborhoods/THE_CITY.md) for the day's shopping in London.
