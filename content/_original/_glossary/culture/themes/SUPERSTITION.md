## Thematic Tag: Omens & Superstition

%% GLO_SUPERSTITION.0002 %%


%% GLO_SUPERSTITION.0003 %%
This thematic tag collects diary paragraphs where Marie reads a sign: a broken mirror, a dog that follows her, a fortune told by a card-reader or an acquaintance, bibliomancy with an encyclopaedia, the numerology of a lover's name. Marie half-believes and half-mocks her own credulity, and the signs she notes tend to cluster around love and death.

%% GLO_SUPERSTITION.0004 %%
## Tagging Criteria

%% GLO_SUPERSTITION.0005 %%
- **Tag** an omen read or feared (a broken glass, a number, an animal, a day of the week).
- **Tag** a fortune told, cards laid, a fortune-teller or a medium consulted.
- **Tag** a superstition admitted or practised: bibliomancy, counting letters of a name, a charm.
- **Tag** a prophecy that she quotes and then watches for fulfilment.
- **Do not tag** church ritual, prayer and vows ([Faith & Prayer](RELIGION.md), [Church Attendance](CHURCH.md)).
- **Do not tag** a dream with no interpretation ([Dreams](DREAMS.md)).
- **Do not tag** figurative "bonne étoile" or "mauvais œil" with no belief behind it.
- **Do not tag** a ghost story or a play she is watching.
- The bar is that the paragraph is substantively *about* the theme; a passing word never qualifies. A paragraph takes at most three themes.

%% GLO_SUPERSTITION.0006 %%
## Common French Cues

%% GLO_SUPERSTITION.0007 %%
- **Nouns**: un signe, un présage, un mauvais signe, la superstition, les cartes, la tireuse de cartes, la bonne aventure, le sort
- **Phrases**: "cela veut dire", "c'est bon signe", "signe de mort", "j'ai fait les cartes", "j'ai ouvert au hasard"
- **False friends**: *sort* as fate in general; *glace* broken (a mirror) versus a figurative "glace"

%% GLO_SUPERSTITION.0008 %%
## Notable Patterns

%% GLO_SUPERSTITION.0009 %%
- Many of Marie's signs concern her future husband: the number of letters in his name, a card, a game at a party.
- Superstition and [Faith & Prayer](RELIGION.md) shade into each other when she bargains with God; tag Religion only if faith or prayer is actually present.
