## Thematic Qualifier Tag: Property: family

%% GLO_PROPERTY_FAMILY.0002 %%
This tag marks Property paragraphs about Marie's own property or her family's: the Babanine and Bashkirtseff estates, grandfather's and papa's wills, Marie's dowry and capital, the family's lawsuits over land. It is a qualifier of [Property](PROPERTY.md): every paragraph that carries it also carries the Property tag, and the tagging criteria live there.
