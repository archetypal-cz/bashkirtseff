## Thematic Tag: Dreams

%% GLO_DREAMS.0002 %%


%% GLO_DREAMS.0003 %%
This thematic tag collects diary paragraphs where Marie records or interprets a night dream: Hamilton or Cassagnac in her sleep, the dead returning, a dream read as an omen. She notes dreams quickly and often reads them as signs, so this tag sits close to [Omens & Superstition](SUPERSTITION.md) but is judged separately.

%% GLO_DREAMS.0004 %%
## Tagging Criteria

%% GLO_DREAMS.0005 %%
- **Tag** a dream of the night before recorded ("j'ai rêvé que…", "cette nuit j'ai vu en rêve").
- **Tag** the interpretation Marie or others give to a dream.
- **Tag** a dream told in a margin note or a letter.
- **Tag** a nightmare or sleep-vision that Marie describes in some detail.
- **Do not tag** daydreams and ambitions ("je rêve de gloire", "mon rêve").
- **Do not tag** a person who is "un rêve" (lovely as a dream).
- **Do not tag** sleeplessness with no dream ([Health & Illness](HEALTH.md) if it is a symptom).
- **Do not tag** dreams in books or plays she discusses.
- The bar is that the paragraph is substantively *about* the theme; a passing word never qualifies. A paragraph takes at most three themes.

%% GLO_DREAMS.0006 %%
## Common French Cues

%% GLO_DREAMS.0007 %%
- **Verbs**: rêver, voir en rêve, faire un rêve, songer (in sleep)
- **Nouns**: un rêve, un songe, un cauchemar
- **Phrases**: "j'ai rêvé que", "cette nuit", "en dormant", "ce rêve veut dire"
- **False friends**: *rêver de* + noun in the sense of "to long for"; *rêverie* is daydreaming

%% GLO_DREAMS.0008 %%
## Notable Patterns

%% GLO_DREAMS.0009 %%
- Marie often dreams of the men she loves and reads the dream as a sign.
- A dream recorded only in a margin note still counts; the kind marker does not change the criterion.
