## Thematic Tag: Nature & Landscape

%% GLO_NATURE.0002 %%


%% GLO_NATURE.0003 %%
This thematic tag collects diary paragraphs where Marie looks at nature for itself: a view, the sea, a garden, a moonlit lake, the Russian steppe or the Riviera hills, described or felt as landscape. It is about what she sees and feels in front of nature, not about the day's weather.

%% GLO_NATURE.0004 %%
## Tagging Criteria

%% GLO_NATURE.0005 %%
- **Tag** a landscape, view or garden described.
- **Tag** the sea, mountains, a river or a forest as a presence.
- **Tag** nature felt: the effect of a place or season on her mood.
- **Tag** a comparison between landscapes (the green of the South against the North).
- **Do not tag** weather as the day's conditions ([Weather](WEATHER.md)).
- **Do not tag** a landscape as a painting subject or a study for a picture ([Her Art](ART_PRACTICE.md)).
- **Do not tag** a journey ([Travel](TRAVEL.md)) with no landscape in view.
- **Do not tag** a place named only as a location.
- The bar is that the paragraph is substantively *about* the theme; a passing word never qualifies. A paragraph takes at most three themes.

%% GLO_NATURE.0006 %%
## Common French Cues

%% GLO_NATURE.0007 %%
- **Nouns**: la campagne, la mer, la montagne, les arbres, la verdure, le jardin, la forêt, le lac, le paysage, la vue, la lune, le coucher du soleil
- **Phrases**: "que c'est beau", "un paysage", "à perte de vue", "les champs"
- **False friends**: *nature* in the sense of temperament; *la nature* as a model in art

%% GLO_NATURE.0008 %%
## Notable Patterns

%% GLO_NATURE.0009 %%
- Landscape writing often arises on journeys (Switzerland, Germany, Italy), where it also takes [Travel](TRAVEL.md).
- A landscape often triggers a mood passage: melancholy in the North, exaltation on the Riviera.
