## Thematic Tag: Studies & Lessons

%% GLO_EDUCATION.0002 %%


%% GLO_EDUCATION.0003 %%
This thematic tag collects diary paragraphs about Marie's own education outside the studio: lessons and teachers, governesses, the plan of study she draws up for herself, Latin and Greek, history and languages, examinations. Marie is a fierce autodidact who educates herself because no one else will.

%% GLO_EDUCATION.0004 %%
## Tagging Criteria

%% GLO_EDUCATION.0005 %%
- **Tag** a lesson, a teacher or a governess, in a specific scene or judgment.
- **Tag** a plan of study, an hour table, a decision to learn or give up a subject.
- **Tag** Latin, Greek, mathematics, history and languages studied.
- **Tag** Marie's self-assessment of what she knows.
- **Do not tag** art training in the atelier or lessons with painters ([Her Art](ART_PRACTICE.md)).
- **Do not tag** singing and piano lessons ([Music](MUSIC_THEME.md)).
- **Do not tag** reading for pleasure ([Reading](READING.md)) unless framed as study.
- **Do not tag** children's education in general with no bearing on her.
- The bar is that the paragraph is substantively *about* the theme; a passing word never qualifies. A paragraph takes at most three themes.

%% GLO_EDUCATION.0006 %%
## Common French Cues

%% GLO_EDUCATION.0007 %%
- **Nouns**: la leçon, le cours, le professeur, l'institutrice, la gouvernante, les études, le plan d'études, le latin, le grec, l'examen
- **Verbs**: étudier, apprendre, répéter, repasser, travailler
- **False friends**: *travailler* often = to paint or to practise, not to study; *cachet* = the fee for a lesson
