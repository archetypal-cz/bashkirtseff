## Thematic Tag: Weather

%% GLO_WEATHER.0002 %%
This thematic tag collects diary paragraphs where Marie notes the actual weather: sun, rain, wind, cold, heat, snow, fog, the state of the sea or the sky on a given day. Like many diarists she often opens an entry with the weather ("Il fait un temps superbe"), and the note matters to her because it decides whether there is a promenade, a drive, a ride on the Promenade des Anglais, or a day indoors.

%% GLO_WEATHER.0003 %%
## Tagging Criteria

%% GLO_WEATHER.0004 %%
- **Tag** a paragraph when it records the weather as it is (or was, that day or that night): "il pleut", "il fait froid", "un soleil splendide", "la mer est houleuse", "il neige à Paris".
- **Tag** when the weather changes what the family does: a promenade cancelled for rain, a drive taken because the day is fine, a crossing delayed by storm.
- **Do not tag** figurative weather: an "orage" of feelings, a "temps" meaning time, "un froid" between two people, "le soleil de ma vie".
- **Do not tag** general remarks on a climate ("le climat de Nice est doux") unless they describe the weather she is actually experiencing.
- Light, moonlight and sunset described purely as scenery are borderline: tag them only when the paragraph is also telling us what kind of day or night it was.

%% GLO_WEATHER.0005 %%
## Common French Cues

%% GLO_WEATHER.0006 %%
- **Weather verbs**: il fait beau / mauvais / chaud / froid / doux, il pleut, il neige, il gèle, il vente
- **Nouns**: le temps, la pluie, l'averse, l'orage, le vent, le mistral, la neige, le brouillard, la brume, le soleil, les nuages, la chaleur, le froid
- **Sea and sky**: la mer calme / houleuse / agitée, un ciel pur / gris / couvert
- **Typical phrasing**: "Il fait un temps superbe", "un temps affreux", "un temps de chien", "Quel temps !"

%% GLO_WEATHER.0007 %%
## Notable Patterns

%% GLO_WEATHER.0008 %%
- In the Nice years (1873–1876) weather notes cluster at the start of entries and frame the day's social round of promenades and visits.
- Weather is often a mood barometer: grey days are paired with boredom and melancholy, radiant ones with hope; tag only when the weather itself is noted, not the mood alone.
- Travel entries note weather for crossings, trains and arrivals, so WEATHER often co-occurs with [Travel](TRAVEL.md).
