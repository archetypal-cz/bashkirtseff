## Thematic Tag: Money

%% GLO_MONEY.0002 %%
This thematic tag collects diary paragraphs about money: prices and costs, sums spent, lent, borrowed, owed, won or lost, allowances and pocket money, the family's income from its Poltava estates, bills and debts, gambling at Monaco, dowries counted in roubles or francs, and Marie's own worries or boasts about being rich or poor. The Bashkirtseff household lived well but not always solvently, and quarrels about money run through the family's history.

%% GLO_MONEY.0003 %%
## Tagging Criteria

%% GLO_MONEY.0004 %%
- **Tag** a stated price, cost or sum ("cent francs", "trois mille roubles"), a payment, a debt, a loan, a bill, an allowance, money sent or awaited, gambling stakes and winnings.
- **Tag** remarks about wealth or poverty as money ("nous sommes ruinés", "il est très riche", "un parti de cent mille francs de rente").
- **Do not tag** figurative value ("cela n'a pas de prix", "je donnerais tout au monde"), or a purchase with no money in view (that is [Shopping](SHOPPING.md) only).
- Possessions and property as things owned belong to [Ownership](OWNERSHIP.md); a paragraph about selling an estate for a sum takes both.

%% GLO_MONEY.0005 %%
## Common French Cues

%% GLO_MONEY.0006 %%
- **Currencies**: francs, sous, louis, napoléons, roubles, kopecks, lire, livres sterling
- **Nouns**: l'argent, la somme, le prix, les frais, la dépense, la dette, la rente, les revenus, la dot, la pension, la facture, le jeu, la roulette, le trente-et-quarante
- **Verbs**: coûter, payer, dépenser, prêter, emprunter, devoir, gagner, perdre (at play), envoyer de l'argent

%% GLO_MONEY.0007 %%
## Notable Patterns

%% GLO_MONEY.0008 %%
- Gambling at the Monaco casino recurs in the Nice years; the family's losses and winnings are recorded with sums.
- Money ties into [Marriage](MARRIAGE.md) through dowries and a suitor's "rente", and into the long family disputes over the Babanine and Bashkirtseff estates.
- Marie's outfit and shopping notes sometimes give the price, so Money co-occurs with [Fashion](FASHION.md) and [Shopping](SHOPPING.md).
