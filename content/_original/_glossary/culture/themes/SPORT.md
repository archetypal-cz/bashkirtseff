## Thematic Tag: Sport & Riding

%% GLO_SPORT.0002 %%


%% GLO_SPORT.0003 %%
This thematic tag collects diary paragraphs about physical recreation and sport: riding, skating (the rink in Paris), croquet, sea-bathing, fencing and the races as spectacle. Marie rides and skates with pride, and notes what her body can still do.

%% GLO_SPORT.0004 %%
## Tagging Criteria

%% GLO_SPORT.0005 %%
- **Tag** she or someone else rides, skates, swims, plays croquet or another game of skill.
- **Tag** a race meeting or a competition watched, with attention to the sport.
- **Tag** plans or desires for exercise ("je voulais aller à cheval") that are the point of the paragraph.
- **Tag** pride or frustration at her physical prowess.
- **Do not tag** a horse or dog loved as a creature ([Animals](ANIMALS.md)) when no riding or sport is in view.
- **Do not tag** "amazone" as a garment ([Clothing & Fashion](FASHION.md)).
- **Do not tag** a drive in a carriage or a walk.
- **Do not tag** a place of amusement named only as a venue.
- The bar is that the paragraph is substantively *about* the theme; a passing word never qualifies. A paragraph takes at most three themes.

%% GLO_SPORT.0006 %%
## Common French Cues

%% GLO_SPORT.0007 %%
- **Activities**: monter à cheval, la promenade à cheval, patiner, le patinage, le rink, le croquet, nager, se baigner, l'escrime
- **Nouns**: les courses (races), l'amazone, le cheval, la selle, le manège
- **False friends**: *courses* = races or errands ([Shopping & Errands](SHOPPING.md)); *se baigner* in a thermal cure belongs to Health

%% GLO_SPORT.0008 %%
## Notable Patterns

%% GLO_SPORT.0009 %%
- Winter riding on the Promenade des Anglais and the Paris skating rink are the two high points of her sporting life.
- Riding and skating are among the first things she gives up when illness takes her strength.
