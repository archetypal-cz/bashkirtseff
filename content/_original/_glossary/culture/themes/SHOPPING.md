## Thematic Tag: Shopping & Errands

%% GLO_SHOPPING.0002 %%
This thematic tag collects diary paragraphs where Marie or her family buy things, go to shops, order goods, or run errands: visits to the dressmaker, milliner and glover, trips to bookshops and art suppliers, buying presents, ordering furniture for a new villa, "faire des courses" in town. Shopping in the diary is both a social outing (with her aunt, her mother, Dina) and a record of the family's spending and taste.

%% GLO_SHOPPING.0003 %%
## Tagging Criteria

%% GLO_SHOPPING.0004 %%
- **Tag** an actual or planned purchase or order: "j'ai acheté", "nous avons commandé", "maman m'a acheté", a dress ordered at Worth, books bought, a present chosen.
- **Tag** going to shops or running errands, even when nothing is bought: "courir les magasins", "faire des courses", a morning spent at the milliner's.
- **Tag** a fitting or an order at a dressmaker's (this also takes [Fashion](FASHION.md)).
- **Do not tag** a dress merely worn or described (that is Fashion only), prices mentioned without any buying (that is [Money](MONEY.md) only), or a gift received with no purchase in view.
- **Do not tag** "acheter" used figuratively ("acheter le bonheur").

## Common French Cues

%% GLO_SHOPPING.0006 %%
- **Verbs**: acheter, commander, choisir, marchander, essayer (at the dressmaker's), faire des courses, faire des emplettes, courir les magasins
- **Places**: le magasin, la boutique, la couturière, la modiste, le gantier, le bijoutier, le libraire, le marchand de couleurs, le Louvre and the Bon Marché (department stores)
- **Things**: les emplettes, les achats, la commande, la facture, le cadeau (when bought)

## Notable Patterns

%% GLO_SHOPPING.0008 %%
- Most shopping in the early carnets is for clothes; see [Fashion](FASHION.md) and the fashion houses under culture/fashion (Laferrière, Virot).
- Shops with their own glossary entries live under places/shops (for example [Spillmann](../../places/shops/SPILLMANN.md)); tag the place entry as well when the shop is named.
- Shopping often co-occurs with [Money](MONEY.md) when Marie records what things cost.
