## Thematic Tag: Ownership & Property

%% GLO_OWNERSHIP.0002 %%
This thematic tag collects diary paragraphs about who owns what: the family estates in Poltava province, villas bought, rented or furnished, inheritance and wills, jewels, furniture, horses, carriages, pictures and other possessions, and quarrels over property. It is about things as belongings, and about the relations (between father and mother, grandfather and uncles, owner and tenant) that ownership creates.

%% GLO_OWNERSHIP.0003 %%
## Tagging Criteria

%% GLO_OWNERSHIP.0004 %%
- **Tag** land, houses and estates as property: buying, selling, renting, leasing or managing them; the family's lands and income-bearing property.
- **Tag** inheritance, wills, legacies, dowry as property, family claims and lawsuits over belongings.
- **Tag** a possession that is significant in the paragraph because it is owned, given or lost: a jewel, a horse, a carriage, a dog bought, a painting she keeps, "mes robes" counted as a wardrobe.
- **Do not tag** every passing object ("ma chambre", "mon piano") when ownership is not the point.
- Sums of money belong to [Money](MONEY.md); a paragraph about the price of a villa takes both.

%% GLO_OWNERSHIP.0005 %%
## Common French Cues

%% GLO_OWNERSHIP.0006 %%
- **Property**: la propriété, la terre, les terres, le domaine, la villa, la maison, le bail, le loyer, le locataire, le propriétaire
- **Inheritance**: l'héritage, le testament, hériter, léguer, la succession, la dot
- **Possessions**: les bijoux, les diamants, les chevaux, la voiture, les meubles, l'argenterie, mes affaires

%% GLO_OWNERSHIP.0007 %%
## Notable Patterns

%% GLO_OWNERSHIP.0008 %%
- The Babanine and Bashkirtseff estates (Gavronzi, Tcherniakovka) and the family's quarrels over them surface whenever Marie is in Russia or her father is discussed.
- In Nice and Paris the household moves between rented villas and apartments; Marie notes the house, the lease and the furnishings.
- Grandfather Babanine's will and the uncles' claims recur in the early carnets (see [Money](MONEY.md) for the sums involved).
