## Thematic Qualifier Tag: Property: others

%% GLO_PROPERTY_OTHERS.0002 %%
This tag marks Property paragraphs about somebody else's property, or property in general: a suitor's estates, an heiress's fortune, a friend's villa, the sale of another person's land or stable. It is a qualifier of [Property](PROPERTY.md): every paragraph that carries it also carries the Property tag, and the tagging criteria live there.
