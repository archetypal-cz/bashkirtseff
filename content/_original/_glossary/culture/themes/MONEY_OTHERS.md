## Thematic Qualifier Tag: Money: others

%% GLO_MONEY_OTHERS.0002 %%
This tag marks Money paragraphs about somebody else's money, or money in general: other people's fortunes, debts and losses, what a rich match is worth, prices and money as a subject. It is a qualifier of [Money](MONEY.md): every paragraph that carries it also carries the Money tag, and the tagging criteria live there.
