## Thematic Tag: Property & Investments

%% GLO_PROPERTY.0002 %%
This thematic tag collects diary paragraphs about property and capital: the family estates in Poltava province, land sold or bought, houses and villas owned, rented or leased, inheritance and wills, dowries, shares, bonds, capital placed to yield a *rente*, and quarrels or lawsuits over any of these. It is about wealth that is held, as distinct from [Money](MONEY.md), which is about money that is spent, owed, lost or counted.

%% GLO_PROPERTY.0003 %%
## Tagging Criteria

%% GLO_PROPERTY.0004 %%
- **Tag** estates, land and houses as property: buying, selling, renting, leasing or managing them; the income they yield.
- **Tag** inheritance, wills, legacies, dowries, marriage settlements, and family claims or lawsuits over property.
- **Tag** investments: capital, shares, bonds, a *rente* seen as capital producing income.
- **Do not tag** everyday belongings ("ma chambre", "mon piano", a new hat), a rented hotel room or villa mentioned only as an address, or wealth mentioned only as a sum (that is [Money](MONEY.md) only).
- A paragraph about the price of an estate or the income from a sale takes both Property and Money.

%% GLO_PROPERTY.0005 %%
## Whose Property: the Qualifier Tags

%% GLO_PROPERTY.0006 %%
Every paragraph tagged Property also takes one or both qualifier tags, which say whose property it is:
- [Property: family](PROPERTY_FAMILY.md) (`#Property_family`) — Marie's own, or her family's: the Babanine and Bashkirtseff estates, grandfather's will, her dowry, her capital.
- [Property: others](PROPERTY_OTHERS.md) (`#Property_others`) — somebody else's, or property in general: a suitor's lands, an heiress's fortune, a duke selling his stable, the law of inheritance.

%% GLO_PROPERTY.0007 %%
In the site's filter, selecting the qualifier narrows Property to one side; selecting Property alone shows both.

## Common French Cues

%% GLO_PROPERTY.0009 %%
- **Property**: la propriété, le bien, la terre, les terres, le domaine, la villa, la maison, le bail, le loyer, le locataire, le propriétaire
- **Inheritance**: l'héritage, l'héritière, le testament, hériter, léguer, la succession, la dot, le contrat de mariage
- **Capital**: le capital, les actions, les obligations, la rente (as capital), placer, la fortune

## Notable Patterns

%% GLO_PROPERTY.0011 %%
- The Babanine and Bashkirtseff estates ([Gavronzi](../../places/residences/GAVRONZI.md), [Tcherniakovka](../../places/residences/TCHERNIAKOVKA.md)) and the family's quarrels over them surface whenever Marie is in Russia or her father is discussed.
- Grandfather Babanine's will and the uncles' claims recur in the early carnets; the sale of a Russian estate in 1881 is expected to give Marie her own capital.
- Dowry and settlements tie Property to [Marriage](MARRIAGE.md).
