## Thematic Tag: Art World

%% GLO_ART_WORLD.0002 %%


%% GLO_ART_WORLD.0003 %%
This thematic tag collects diary paragraphs about other people's art and the art world as Marie meets it: museums and galleries, exhibitions, the Salon seen as a public show, painters, sculptors and critics. It is the spectator's counterpart of [Her Art](ART_PRACTICE.md), which covers her own work and studio.

%% GLO_ART_WORLD.0004 %%
## Tagging Criteria

%% GLO_ART_WORLD.0005 %%
- **Tag** a museum, gallery or exhibition visited, with a judgment on what she sees.
- **Tag** a painter, sculptor or critic discussed; a particular picture praised or criticised.
- **Tag** the Salon or another show as an event, with its works and crowd.
- **Tag** the state of art and the art market in her time.
- **Do not tag** her own work, studio, models or Salon entries ([Her Art](ART_PRACTICE.md)).
- **Do not tag** a museum named only as a place she passed or an outing.
- **Do not tag** other artists' work discussed purely in terms of her own practice, which goes to Her Art.
- **Do not tag** art as decoration in a house or a purchase ([Shopping & Errands](SHOPPING.md)).
- The bar is that the paragraph is substantively *about* the theme; a passing word never qualifies. A paragraph takes at most three themes.

%% GLO_ART_WORLD.0006 %%
## Common French Cues

%% GLO_ART_WORLD.0007 %%
- **Places**: le musée, le Louvre, le Luxembourg, la galerie, l'exposition, le Salon, le Prado, l'Académie
- **Nouns**: un tableau, une toile, un maître, un chef-d'œuvre, la critique, le vernissage
- **Names**: Manet, Raphaël, Rubens, the Impressionists, Bastien-Lepage (as an artist, not a lover)
- **False friends**: *exposition* = exhibition (also a world's fair); *salle* = a room in a museum or a theatre

%% GLO_ART_WORLD.0008 %%
## Notable Patterns

%% GLO_ART_WORLD.0009 %%
- Travel entries are full of museum visits (Berlin, Rome, Madrid); the Salon opens a May calendar of verdicts on other people's pictures.
- The Salon and the great exhibitions of Paris give her a running commentary on contemporary painting.
