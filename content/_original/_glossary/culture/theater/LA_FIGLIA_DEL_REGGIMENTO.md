# La figlia del reggimento (Donizetti)

%% GLO_LA_FIGLIA_DEL_REGGIMENTO.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_LA_FIGLIA_DEL_REGGIMENTO.0003 %%
*La fille du régiment* (The Daughter of the Regiment) is an opéra comique in two acts by Gaetano Donizetti, to a French libretto by Jules-Henri Vernoy de Saint-Georges and Jean-François Bayard, first performed by the Opéra-Comique, Paris, on 11 February 1840 (Wikipedia, «La fille du régiment»). The heroine Marie, a foundling raised by a regiment of Napoleon's army, loves the Tyrolean Tonio and is claimed by a noble family. Marie hears it under its Italian title, *La figlia del reggimento*.

%% GLO_LA_FIGLIA_DEL_REGGIMENTO.0004 %%
## Marie and La figlia del reggimento

%% GLO_LA_FIGLIA_DEL_REGGIMENTO.0005 %%
**16 December 1875, Nice.** "Ce soir la première de "La figlia del regimento"" (050.0817). The footnote in the diary identifies it as Donizetti's opera.

%% GLO_LA_FIGLIA_DEL_REGGIMENTO.0006 %%
**18 December 1875.** After an afternoon of turmoil Marie decides to go: "j'ai l'idée d'aller à l'Opéra, c'est la lettre A une lettre élégante, et on donne "La figlia del Regimento", c'est presque une première, l'autre soir le bal de Francia a empêché beaucoup de venir" (050.0929). "La lettre A" is the opera subscription series, the most prestigious one at Nice, per the diary's footnote. She expects to see Prodgers there. See the [Nice Opéra](../../places/theaters/OPERA_NICE.md).
