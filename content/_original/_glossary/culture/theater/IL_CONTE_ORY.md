# Le comte Ory (Rossini)

%% GLO_IL_CONTE_ORY.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_IL_CONTE_ORY.0003 %%
*Le comte Ory* (Count Ory) is a comic opera by Gioachino Rossini, first performed at the Paris Opéra on 20 August 1828. The French libretto is by Eugène Scribe and Charles-Gaspard Delestre-Poirson, from a comedy they wrote in 1817; part of the music was reused from Rossini's *Il viaggio a Reims* (1825) (Wikipedia, «Le comte Ory»). A medieval nobleman and his companions try to enter a castle whose men are away at the crusade, first disguised as a hermit and then as pilgrim nuns.
