## Les Saltimbanques

%% GLO_LES_SALTIMBANQUES.0002 %%
Play or operetta given at Nice on 14 April 1875 together with *La Périchole*: «Walitsky et Olga viennent. On donne "La Périchole" et "Les Saltimbanques"» (031.0231). Not identified: the well-known operetta of that name by Louis Ganne dates from 1899 and cannot be the work. See LA_PERICHOLE_OFFENBACH.
