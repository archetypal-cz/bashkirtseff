## Les Brigands (Offenbach)

%% GLO_LES_BRIGANDS.0002 %%
Opéra-bouffe by Jacques Offenbach, first performed at the Théâtre des Variétés in 1869 (Wikipedia, «Les Brigands»). Marie dresses to go on 21 November 1874 at Nice, but the evening is changed to Offenbach's *Barbe-bleue* (026.0023; see BARBE_BLEUE_OFFENBACH); on 23 December 1875 she sees it in her old box with «une nouvelle actrice, Mlle Aimée» (050.1162).
