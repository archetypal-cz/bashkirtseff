# La forza del destino (Verdi)

%% GLO_LA_FORZA_DEL_DESTINO_VERDI.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_LA_FORZA_DEL_DESTINO_VERDI.0003 %%
*La forza del destino* (The Force of Destiny) is an opera in four acts by Giuseppe Verdi, to an Italian libretto by Francesco Maria Piave after the Spanish drama *Don Álvaro o la fuerza del sino* (1835) by the Duke of Rivas, with a scene taken from Schiller's *Wallensteins Lager*. It was first performed at the Bolshoi Kamenny Theatre in Saint Petersburg on 10 November 1862 (new style); Verdi revised it for La Scala in 1869 (Wikipedia, «La forza del destino»). The plot follows two lovers and the brother who pursues them to avenge his family's honour.

## Marie and La forza del destino

%% GLO_LA_FORZA_DEL_DESTINO_VERDI.0006 %%
**8 March 1873, Nice.** At the Théâtre Italien, in a violet dress: "On donnait pour la première fois "La Forza del destino" de Verdi; on dit qu'il s'y trouvait" (002.0235). Marie notes that there was a crowd, but "la *société* n'y était pas". "On dit qu'il s'y trouvait" is hearsay; she does not say who "il" is, perhaps Verdi, and she does not say she saw him. See [Théâtre Italien, Nice](../../people/mentioned/THEATRE_ITALIEN_NICE.md).

%% GLO_LA_FORZA_DEL_DESTINO_VERDI.0007 %%
**25 February 1877, Naples.** After a long walk on the Chiaja Marie goes to the [San Carlo](../../places/theaters/SAN_CARLO.md): ""La Forza del destino". Grand succès de Merisano qui abandonne le ballet au plus bel endroit pour nous voir sortir. J'étais vraiment très intéressante ce soir" (069.0014). Merisano, whom Marie names without describing, leaves the ballet to watch the family go out; the paragraph says nothing of the singing. Marie is in Naples at this date.
