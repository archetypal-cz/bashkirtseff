# La Favorite (Donizetti)

## Overview

*La Favorite* is a grand opera in four acts by Gaetano Donizetti, to a French libretto by Alphonse Royer and Gustave Vaëz (with Eugène Scribe), after Baculard d'Arnaud's play *Le Comte de Comminges*. It was first performed at the Paris Opéra on 2 December 1840. The Italian translation, *La favorita*, became the form in which the work was most often sung in Italian houses (Wikipedia, "La favorite"). The story concerns Fernand, a novice, who loves Léonor, the king's mistress, and learns too late of her position.

## Marie and this work

The title is in the repertoire of the Nice opera seasons Marie attends, and she hears it in both languages.

- **20 and 27 April 1873, Nice.** She wishes to go to the Italiens for "La Favorite" with Mme Galletti (004.0099); on the 27th she sees "La Favorita di Donizetti" at the opera, "la première fois que je vois La Favorite", and is content with Galletti (004.0195).
- **1 January 1874, Nice.** "Hier on donnait La Favorite": the prima donna is "au-dessous de toute critique" (015.0003).
- **1 December 1875, Nice.** "On donne La Favorite, Pasqua chante comme un coeur" (050.0364); the singer is probably the mezzo Giuseppina Pasqua, which is inferred and not confirmed in the text.
- **27 January 1877, Nice.** She goes to the Opéra to hear Faure in "La Favorite" (068.0840). The source footnote identifies Jean-Baptiste Faure (1830-1914), the leading French baritone of the day; Kernberger (2013) also identifies him as Jean-Baptiste Faure.
- **17 May 1879.** An allusion only: Marie teaches her maid Rosalie to answer "comme dans la Favorite" (085.0087). The quoted line is not identified.
