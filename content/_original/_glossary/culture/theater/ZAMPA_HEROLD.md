# Zampa (Hérold)

%% GLO_ZAMPA_HEROLD.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_ZAMPA_HEROLD.0003 %%
*Zampa, ou La Fiancée de marbre* is an opéra comique in three acts by Ferdinand Hérold (1791-1833) to a libretto by Mélesville, first performed at the Opéra-Comique, Paris, on 3 May 1831 (Wikipedia, «Zampa»). A pirate chief, Zampa, who has wronged a young woman, mocks a marble statue of her by putting his ring on its finger, and the statue claims him.

%% GLO_ZAMPA_HEROLD.0004 %%
## Marie and Zampa

%% GLO_ZAMPA_HEROLD.0005 %%
**21 August 1873, Paris.** Marie writes that she has subscribed to *Le Derby* and dined at the Russian restaurant, where Dina ordered food Marie dislikes; "Comme nous sommes vis-à-vis l'Opéra-Comique nous y allâmes: "Zampa" pour la 475e représentation" (008.0104). In the next paragraph she adds: "Je vois "Zampa" pour la première fois" (008.0105). The house is the [Opéra-Comique](../../places/theaters/OPERA_COMIQUE.md). The count of 475 performances is Marie's figure.
