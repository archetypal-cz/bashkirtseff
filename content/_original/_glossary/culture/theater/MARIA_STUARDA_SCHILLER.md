# Maria Stuart (Schiller), as played by Ristori

%% GLO_MARIA_STUARDA_SCHILLER.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_MARIA_STUARDA_SCHILLER.0003 %%
*Maria Stuart* is a five-act tragedy by Friedrich Schiller (1759-1805), first performed in Weimar in 1800 (Wikipedia, "Mary Stuart (play)"). It dramatises the last days of Mary, Queen of Scots, imprisoned by Elizabeth I, including the invented confrontation between the two queens. Adelaide Ristori played Mary in an Italian version that she describes as "translated into splendid Italian verses by Andrea Maffei", and which she kept among the signature roles of her career (Ristori, *Studies and Memoirs*, 1888). She was already playing the part at eighteen (Wikipedia, "Adelaide Ristori") and took it to Paris, London, the United States and Australia. See [Adelaide Ristori](../../people/artists/ADELAIDE_RISTORI.md).

## Marie and Maria Stuart

%% GLO_MARIA_STUARDA_SCHILLER.0005 %%
%% [#Adelaide_Ristori](../../people/artists/ADELAIDE_RISTORI.md) [#Apollo](../../places/theaters/APOLLO.md) %%
- **24 April 1876, Rome.** The day has gone in meetings with Antonelli on the Corso. Marie ends on the evening: "Ce soir à l'Apollo la Ristori joue "Marie Stuart" et il n'y a pas une loge" (059.0244). Whether she found another seat is not said in that entry.

%% GLO_MARIA_STUARDA_SCHILLER.0006 %%
- **27 September 1879, Paris.** At an evening "au profit d'incendiés alsaciens" there is "un acte de "Marie Stuart", joué par la grande, l'admirable, l'incomparable Ristori. On lui fait une ovation"; Marie is dressed to perfection (086.0321).

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_MARIA_STUARDA_SCHILLER.0008 %%
Marie's other "Marie Stuart" mentions are not the play. They are a fashion: the "bonnet Marie Stuart" she orders from a milliner in Rome (052.0321), the "coiffures Marie Stuart" (052.0377), a "corsage Marie Stuart" (049.0428), "en Marie Stuart" at the Opéra (050.0551), the "bonnet Marie Stuart" of 5 January 1877 (068.0408) and a "robe Marie Stuart" (053.0125). In 1882 she also names the queen as a subject Bastien-Lepage might paint (096.0100). The bare name is therefore not used as an alias.
