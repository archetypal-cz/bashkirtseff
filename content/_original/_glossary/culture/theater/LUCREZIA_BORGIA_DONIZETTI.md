# Lucrezia Borgia (Donizetti)

%% GLO_LUCREZIA_BORGIA_DONIZETTI.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_LUCREZIA_BORGIA_DONIZETTI.0003 %%
*Lucrezia Borgia* is a melodramma in a prologue and two acts by Gaetano Donizetti (1797-1848), with an Italian libretto by Felice Romani after Victor Hugo's play *Lucrezia Borgia* (1833). It was first performed at La Scala, Milan, on 26 December 1833 (Wikipedia, «Lucrezia Borgia (opera)»). The title role, a poisoner whose own son, unrecognised by her, dies of her poison, is a showpiece for dramatic sopranos.

%% GLO_LUCREZIA_BORGIA_DONIZETTI.0004 %%
The opera dramatises the legend of Lucrezia Borgia (1480-1519) as Hugo had shaped it, not the historical woman.

## Marie and Lucrezia Borgia

%% GLO_LUCREZIA_BORGIA_DONIZETTI.0006 %%
**5 April 1873, Nice.** At the Opéra Marie sees *Lucrezia Borgia*: "les acteurs ont bien chanté, la prima donna Scarati s'est surpassée, elle chantait si mal et désagréablement, aujourd'hui c'est le contraire" (003.0203). The theatre is full, she wears a pink dress and puts her hair up, and she regrets that "il n'y avait personne pour me regarder"; the rest of the paragraph is about the Duke of Hamilton, whom she calls "une divinité insaisissable". The singer is [Scarati](../../people/mentioned/SCARATI.md); the house is the [Nice Opéra](../../places/theaters/OPERA_NICE.md).

%% GLO_LUCREZIA_BORGIA_DONIZETTI.0007 %%
**9 August 1873, Vienna.** Marie and her family find Gutman's card and a box for the opera and arrive at the second act: "la chanteuse *Wilt,* est admirable. Il y a longtemps que je n'ai passé une si belle soirée. Elle a une voix superbe, trop grave (elle est un peu grasse). Vraiment je suis charmée" (007.0385). The singer is Marie Wilt (1833-1891), an Austrian dramatic coloratura soprano who sang at the Vienna Hofoper from 1867 (Wikipedia, «Marie Wilt»).
