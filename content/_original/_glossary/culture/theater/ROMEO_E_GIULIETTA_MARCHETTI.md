# Romeo e Giulietta (Filippo Marchetti)

%% GLO_ROMEO_E_GIULIETTA_MARCHETTI.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_ROMEO_E_GIULIETTA_MARCHETTI.0003 %%
*Romeo e Giulietta* is a *dramma lirico* in four acts by Filippo Marchetti (1831-1902), to a libretto by Marco Marcello after Shakespeare. The printed libretto (Milan, Francesco Lucca) states that the work was "da rappresentarsi al Teatro Apollo di Roma, quaresima 1876" (Internet Archive, "romeoegiuliettad00marc"), that is, for the Lent season of 1876 at the [Teatro Apollo](../../places/theaters/APOLLO.md) in Rome. Marchetti's other opera in the diary is [Ruy Blas](RUY_BLAS_MARCHETTI.md). Gounod's *Roméo et Juliette* (1867) is a different work, and the Apollo performance is not his.

%% GLO_ROMEO_E_GIULIETTA_MARCHETTI.0004 %%
## Marie and Marchetti's Romeo e Giulietta

%% GLO_ROMEO_E_GIULIETTA_MARCHETTI.0005 %%
**23 April 1876, Rome.** Marie, writing from the Corso in Rome (the windows "donnent sur le Corso", 059.0194) and back from Naples, is waiting for the explanation of Pietro Antonelli's conduct. It rains: "nous ne sortirons pas mais le soir il faudra aller au théâtre, d'autant plus qu'on donne 'Roméo et Juliette' et que ce sera la dernière soirée musicale de l'Apollo" (059.0193). Marie does not name the composer. The identification with Marchetti's opera rests on the libretto printed for that season at the Apollo (confidence given in the Researcher's note: 0.85); the Apollo entry records the same conclusion.

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_ROMEO_E_GIULIETTA_MARCHETTI.0007 %%
- Other candidates are Gounod's *Roméo et Juliette* (1867) and Bellini's *I Capuleti e i Montecchi*. In January 1875 Marie hears Bellini's *I Capuleti e i Montecchi* under the same French title "Roméo et Juliette" at Nice (028.0119; see [Dory](../../people/mentioned/DORY.md)); that is a separate performance.
- A second passage, 064.0141 (24 July 1876), is Cassagnac's remark "Je comprends le mariage comme Roméo et Juliette", which refers to Shakespeare's lovers and not to the opera.
- Because "Roméo et Juliette" also names Shakespeare's play and other operas, the entry does not use the bare title as an alias.
