# Macbeth (Shakespeare, translated by Jean Richepin)

%% GLO_MACBETH_SHAKESPEARE.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_MACBETH_SHAKESPEARE.0003 %%
*Macbeth* is a tragedy by William Shakespeare, estimated to have been first performed in 1606 and first printed in the Folio of 1623 (Wikipedia, "Macbeth"). The Paris production of 1884 used a French verse translation by the poet Jean Richepin and was staged at the Théâtre de la Porte-Saint-Martin for [Sarah Bernhardt](../../people/artists/SARAH_BERNHARDT.md) as Lady Macbeth; it opened on 21 May 1884 (Wikipedia, "Roles played by Sarah Bernhardt"; "Jean Richepin"). The actor playing Macbeth was Marais.
