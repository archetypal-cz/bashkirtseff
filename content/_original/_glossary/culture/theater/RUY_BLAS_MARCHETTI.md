# Ruy Blas (Filippo Marchetti)

## Overview

*Ruy Blas* is a dramma lirico in four acts by Filippo Marchetti to a libretto by Carlo d'Ormeville, after Victor Hugo's play *Ruy Blas* (1838). It was first given at La Scala, Milan, on 3 April 1869 (Wikipédia fr, "Ruy Blas (Marchetti)"). Hugo's play has its own entry.

## Marie and this work

For Marie, *Ruy Blas* is "l'opéra d'Antonelli": the music she associates with Pietro Antonelli in Rome (054.0324, 062.0728, 064.0027).

- **1 March 1873, Nice.** At the Opéra, "Ruy Bias" and "Nelly" with no one of society to look at her (002.0135).
- **11 February 1875, Nice.** At the opera with Nadinka: they sing badly, and she is amused only by the beautiful Gioia opposite (029.0271).
- **31 January - 8 February 1876, Rome.** The opera is given nightly with the King present, the house packed, applauded frantically (053.0271); she goes again on 2, 6 and 8 February and on 20 February, hoping each time to see or be seen by Soroka (053.0344-0447, 054.0324).
- **26 June 1876, Nice.** Her mothers bring her the score and she reads the big Act II aria: "Ruy Blas, c'est l'opéra d'Antonelli, chantons-le pendant que je l'aime, car demain peut-être j'aimerai Audiffret et je voudrai chanter Mignon" (062.0715-0728; footnote 062.0715).
- **20 July 1876, Paris.** In the Bois she hums "des airs de Faust et de Ruy Blas. Et avec Ruy Blas j'ai pensé à Antonelli" (064.0027).
- **11 April 1879, Paris.** Her mother, refusing to let her go to Hugo's play at the Comédie-Française because "une reine est amoureuse d'un valet", says she knows it: "j'ai vu plusieurs fois l'opéra de Ruy Blas et c'est la même chose" (084.0650). The RSR note there suggests Marchetti's opera.

## Not this work

087.0893, 095.0318 and 103.0416 are Hugo's play (recitations, the lackey's love for the Queen); 096.0257 is the title on her studio shelf of favourite books; the play is also the subject of 084.0650 (the Comédie-Française), apart from the sentence noted above. These belong to the Hugo entry.
