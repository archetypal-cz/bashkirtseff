# Alice de Nevers

## Overview

Opéra-bouffe (also called "opéra fantaisiste") in three acts and five tableaux, with words and music by Hervé (Florimond Ronger, 1825-1892). First performed at the Théâtre des Folies-Dramatiques in Paris on 22 April 1875, with Hervé himself among the cast as the Prince de Ferrare (IMSLP catalogue entry; list of notable Folies-Dramatiques premieres). It was a conspicuous failure in Paris: Pascal Blanchet (Palazzetto Bru Zane) calls it a complete fiasco at its premiere and notes that Hervé abandoned writing his own libretti after it. A *Courbaril-polka* "sur des motifs d'Alice de Nevers" was published by E. Gérard the same year (Europeana record).

## Marie and this work

The Nice premiere comes six months after Paris and is the social centrepiece of carnet 047.

- **20 October 1875, Nice.** "Ce soir on donne pour la première fois Alice de Nevers, opéra-bouffe d'Hervé", in the first-tier stage box on the right, "la loge de Gioia" (047.0282). Audiffret visits her box during the evening, their first private meeting since his return; the audience applauds Hervé and also boos, and Marie says she understood nothing of the piece.
- **26-27 October.** At the second hearing she finds it "charmante" and expects it to become her "favorite" like *Giroflé-Girofla* (048.0059), then goes about repeating fragments: the command "Partez, ar... quebuses!" and the "Oui papa!" of the eight daughters (048.0120).
- **4 November.** It is given again; all the young men come to her box (048.0523).
- **7 November.** At a reception, Saëtone and Gautier sing airs from it on the sofa and the others join in (049.0037).
