# Ferréol (Sardou)

%% GLO_FERREOL_SARDOU.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_FERREOL_SARDOU.0003 %%
*Ferréol* is a play in four acts by Victorien Sardou ([Sardou](../../people/mentioned/SARDOU.md)), first given at the Théâtre du Gymnase, Paris, on 17 November 1875 (Sardou, *Théâtre complet*, vol. XII, 1951, as cited in the project's notes on 050.0087; Wikipedia, "Victorien Sardou", lists it as a drame of 1875). It was later filmed in the United States as *A Night of Mystery* (1928, Lothar Mendes), "d'après Ferréol" (Wikipedia, "Victorien Sardou"). The Gymnase had a few years earlier made a hit of Meilhac and Halévy's *Frou-Frou* ([Frou-Frou](FROU_FROU.md)), to which Marie compares it in advance.

## Marie and Ferréol

%% GLO_FERREOL_SARDOU.0005 %%
- **20 November 1875, Paris (050.0087).** "Nous allons aller au Gymnase voir Ferréol. Cela va sans doute me donner des idées amoureuses comme «Frou-Frou»."

%% GLO_FERREOL_SARDOU.0006 %%
- **20 November 1875 (050.0090).** "Ferréol ne m'a pas donné des idées amoureuses. C'est admirablement joué." Only a stage box (avant-scène) on the stage itself was available, so she could not see the audience between the acts, but she could watch the actors: "les marquises et les femmes de chambre s'asseoir sur le même canapé, raconter toutes sortes de choses. C'était très interessant."

%% GLO_FERREOL_SARDOU.0007 %%
- **21 November 1875 (050.0093).** "hier en rentrant du théâtre j'ai causé avec Winslow": the young American she had known at Spa (see his entry).

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_FERREOL_SARDOU.0009 %%
No public full text of the play was located. The 1875 first night date comes from the printed *Théâtre complet* as reported in the research note on 050.0087 and was not checked against a second source.
