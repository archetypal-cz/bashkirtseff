# Othello (Shakespeare)

%% GLO_OTHELLO_SHAKESPEARE.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_OTHELLO_SHAKESPEARE.0003 %%
*Othello, the Moor of Venice* is a tragedy by William Shakespeare written around 1603. The Moorish general Othello, manipulated by his ensign Iago, comes to believe that his wife Desdemona is unfaithful and kills her (Wikipedia, "Othello"). Ernesto Rossi (1827-1896) and Tommaso Salvini (1829-1915) were two leading Italian tragedians of the day, named with Ristori among the actors who made Giacometti's plays successes (Wikipedia, "Paolo Giacometti"); the diary's own footnote calls Salvini "famous throughout Europe for his Othello" (076.0311). See [Ernesto Rossi](../../people/artists/ERNESTO_ROSSI.md) and [Salvini](../../people/mentioned/SALVINI.md).

## Marie and Othello

%% GLO_OTHELLO_SHAKESPEARE.0005 %%
%% [#Ernesto_Rossi](../../people/artists/ERNESTO_ROSSI.md) [#Collignon](../../people/mentioned/COLLIGNON.md) %%
- **19 April 1875, Nice.** "Le soir je vais avec Collignon voir "Othello" joué par ce célèbre comédien Rossi, au Théâtre italien (robe rose, bien). La salle est pleine de jeunes filles. Oh les mamans ! Qu'elles ont bien choisi le spectacle" (031.0297). She is ironic about a play of jealousy and strangling chosen for a house of young girls. The performance was in Italian, as the next paragraph (031.0298) says she understands everything.

%% GLO_OTHELLO_SHAKESPEARE.0006 %%
%% [#Salvini](../../people/mentioned/SALVINI.md) %%
- **3 December 1877, Paris.** "Salvini le célèbre tragédien italien est ici avec sa troupe, nous allons ce soir voir "Othello"." She enjoys sitting opposite the box of the Italian embassy among a half-Italian public and listening to "cette belle langue si caressante et si terrible". "Je suis pour les hommes qui étranglent", she writes, anticipating the reply that Italians do not strangle but borrow money from women, and adds that she does not mean only Italians (076.0311).

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_OTHELLO_SHAKESPEARE.0008 %%
The Rossi performance of 1875 and the Salvini performance of 1877 are two separate evenings of the same play. The image shows Salvini, not Rossi.
