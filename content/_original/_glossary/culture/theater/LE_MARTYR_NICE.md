# Le Martyr / Le Martyre (play seen at Nice)

%% GLO_LE_MARTYR_NICE.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_LE_MARTYR_NICE.0003 %%
Marie refers twice to a performance of "Le Martyre" / "Le Martyr" at Nice, at which the Duke of Hamilton was present. She did not see it herself; she knows it only from the account of her friend Bête. The author and the genre are not named in the diary, and no source has been found that fixes the work, so the identification is open.

%% GLO_LE_MARTYR_NICE.0004 %%
## Marie and "Le Martyr"

%% GLO_LE_MARTYR_NICE.0005 %%
- **28 September 1873, Nice (010.0101).** Bête, hearing Marie describe the Duke, replies "Alors c'est le duc que j'ai vu l'autre jour au théâtre quand on donnait «Le Martyre». Il était dans la première loge à gauche et moi à droite. Il se moquait de *Joanita* tout le temps." The italicised *Joanita* is probably a character or a role in the piece; the diary does not say.

%% GLO_LE_MARTYR_NICE.0006 %%
- **30 October 1873, Nice (011.0368).** "Bête m'a dit l'autre jour qu'elle l'a vu au théâtre quand on donnait «Le Martyr», eh bien il me semble comme si je l'ai vu moi-même": Marie recalls the Duke's pose in detail, and how he spoke with Mlle White, as though she had been there herself.

%% GLO_LE_MARTYR_NICE.0007 %%
## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_LE_MARTYR_NICE.0008 %%
The date of the performance is not given; it was some time before September 1873, probably in the winter season when the Duke was at Nice. Titles that might be meant (a drama *Le Martyr* by Adolphe d'Ennery and Jules Brésil; Donizetti's *Les Martyrs*; an opera by Gilbert Duprez named *Joanita*, 1852) were looked at and none is shown to match, so none is claimed. Other occurrences of "le martyr" and "martyre" in the diary are ordinary words.
