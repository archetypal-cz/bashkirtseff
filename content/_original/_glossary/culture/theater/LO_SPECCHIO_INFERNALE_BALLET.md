# Lo Specchio Infernale (ballet)

%% GLO_LO_SPECCHIO_INFERNALE_BALLET.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_LO_SPECCHIO_INFERNALE_BALLET.0003 %%
"The Infernal Mirror", an Italian-titled ballet given at a theatre in Nice in the winter of 1873-74. Marie gives only the title. The author, composer and company are not identified, and no printed source for the title has been found, so nothing more is known about the work than the diary says. The title suggests a fantastic or diabolical subject; that is an inference from the words, not from any source.

%% GLO_LO_SPECCHIO_INFERNALE_BALLET.0004 %%
## Marie and "Lo Specchio Infernale"

%% GLO_LO_SPECCHIO_INFERNALE_BALLET.0005 %%
- **23 December 1873, Nice (014.0185).** "Ce soir je vais au théâtre, «Le legs», comédie en un acte de Marivaux et le ballet: «Lospecchio infernale»." It is her first visit to the theatre in almost three months; she wears the white dress and is pleased with her hair. The party is "trois et demie": herself, her aunt, Dina and Nadinka. They catch only the end of *Le Legs*, "ennuyeux et long". The Howards sit next to them. Marie says nothing about the ballet itself in this entry.

%% GLO_LO_SPECCHIO_INFERNALE_BALLET.0006 %%
- **17 January 1874, Nice (015.0224).** "Je voulais voir «Le Réveillon» par Ravel. Je pensais qu'on commencerait par cette pièce mais pas du tout, on nous a maltraité par «Lo Specchio Infernale»." The ballet opens the evening, to her annoyance; the comedy she came for follows (see [Le Réveillon](LE_REVEILLON_MEILHAC_HALEVY.md)).

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_LO_SPECCHIO_INFERNALE_BALLET.0008 %%
The 1873 spelling "Lospecchio" is Marie's slip for "Lo specchio". Both dates are at Nice, with a visiting company presenting an Italian ballet before or after French plays. The existing stub under places/theaters filed the title as a play; Marie calls it a ballet in December 1873.
