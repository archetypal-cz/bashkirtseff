# Les Noces de Jeannette (Victor Massé)

%% GLO_LES_NOCES_DE_JEANNETTE_MASSE.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_LES_NOCES_DE_JEANNETTE_MASSE.0003 %%
*Les Noces de Jeannette* (Jeannette's Wedding) is a one-act opéra comique by Victor Massé (1822-1884) to a libretto by Jules Barbier and Michel Carré. It was first performed at the Opéra-Comique (Salle Favart) in Paris on 4 February 1853 (Wikipedia, "Les noces de Jeannette"). Its title page in the Internet Archive copies and the vocal score by the composer confirm the authors. It is a short piece for a handful of singers.

%% GLO_LES_NOCES_DE_JEANNETTE_MASSE.0004 %%
## Marie and this work

%% GLO_LES_NOCES_DE_JEANNETTE_MASSE.0005 %%
**13 March 1877, Naples.** At an evening of Neapolitan society, in a hall full of titled people, Marie is humiliated by her "robe Virginie", which looks stale among the elegant dresses. "On jouait 'Les noces de Jeannette' en italien, les dilettanti se distinguaient" (069.0286): the opera was sung in Italian translation by amateurs, the *dilettanti*; plausibly an evening of the Neapolitan Philharmonic society (see [Società Filarmonica](../../places/social/SOCIETA_FILARMONICA.md)), which the paragraph itself does not state. The count of Balzorano, fiancé of Giselle, settles opposite her.

%% GLO_LES_NOCES_DE_JEANNETTE_MASSE.0006 %%
**25 March 1877, Naples.** At the Philharmonic's sacred-music concert (see [Concert of the Filarmonica, Naples 1877](../music/CONCERT_FILARMONICA_NAPLES_1877.md) and [Stabat Mater, Pergolesi](../music/STABAT_MATER_PERGOLESI.md)), the duke of Melito says that he "préférait 'Les noces de Jeannette' à 'cette musique de Pergolèse et Mozart'" (069.0527).
