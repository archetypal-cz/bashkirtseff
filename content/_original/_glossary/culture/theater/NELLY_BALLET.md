# Nelly (ballet, Nice, 1873)

%% GLO_NELLY_BALLET.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_NELLY_BALLET.0003 %%
A ballet given at the Opéra of Nice in the 1872-73 winter season as an afterpiece to the opera of the evening. Marie gives only the title, in quotation marks. The author, composer and choreographer are not identified, and no ballet of this title has been found for this season, so the identification is open. Whether the title is *Nelly* or a variant spelling is also unverified.

%% GLO_NELLY_BALLET.0004 %%
## Marie and "Nelly"

%% GLO_NELLY_BALLET.0005 %%
- **1 March 1873, Nice (002.0135).** "Le soir à l'Opéra ... «Ruy Blas» et «Nelly»." Marie wears a black silk low-cut dress and a high hairstyle with a gold circlet, but regrets that nobody from society is in the house, "le théâtre était assez plein, mais vide, personne pour me regarder". The opera is Marchetti's [Ruy Blas](RUY_BLAS_MARCHETTI.md); "Nelly" follows it.

%% GLO_NELLY_BALLET.0006 %%
- **12 March 1873, Nice (002.0303).** After an opera that she thinks "très bien à cause de Mlle Franchino", the family does not wait for "le ballet, «Nelly»" and goes on to the Français, to join her aunt and Mme Walkevitch for "Un bal du grand monde".

%% GLO_NELLY_BALLET.0007 %%
## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_NELLY_BALLET.0008 %%
"Nelly" here is a ballet title and not a person. A search of the usual online sources found no ballet *Nelly* at Nice in 1873; the closest title seen was *Nella* (Pilati, 1850s), which is not shown to be the same work.
