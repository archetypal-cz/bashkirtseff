# Rienzi (Wagner)

%% GLO_RIENZI_WAGNER.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_RIENZI_WAGNER.0003 %%
*Rienzi, der letzte der Tribunen* ("Rienzi, the last of the tribunes", WWV 49) is an opera in five acts by Richard Wagner (1813-1883), who also wrote the libretto after Edward Bulwer-Lytton's novel *Rienzi* (1835). It was composed between July 1838 and November 1840 and first performed at the Königliches Hoftheater in Dresden on 20 October 1842, Wagner's first success. It is set in Rome and based on the life of Cola di Rienzo (1313-1354), the populist who defeats the nobles and is finally abandoned by the people and the Church, who burn the Capitol around him (Wikipedia, "Rienzi"). The novel Marie knew has its own entry: [Rienzi (Bulwer-Lytton)](../literature/RIENZI_BULWER_LYTTON.md).

%% GLO_RIENZI_WAGNER.0004 %%
## Marie and Wagner's Rienzi

%% GLO_RIENZI_WAGNER.0005 %%
**1 May 1877, Florence.** Marcuard has spent an hour at the Bashkirtseff apartment and in the evening they meet him again "au théâtre Pagliano, la Pergola est fermée ! On donne 'Rienzi' de Wagner. Je n'ai rien compris à cette musique." (071.0083). The Teatro Pagliano is described in [Teatro Pagliano](../../places/theaters/TEATRO_PAGLIANO.md); the Pergola, Florence's opera house, was closed. Marie is not impressed by the theatre either: "Ce théâtre n'est pas à la mode" (071.0084). The Italian text of the opera in Arrigo Boito's translation was in use in Italy in the 1870s (Internet Archive, libretto printed for La Fenice, Venice, Lent 1874, and a further Italian libretto edition of 1876). That the Florence performance was in Italian is therefore likely, but she does not say.

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_RIENZI_WAGNER.0008 %%
On 17 July 1876 (063.0443) Marie compares the "conspirateur" Cardinal Antonelli to "Rienzi"; that is the tribune seen through Bulwer-Lytton's novel, which she had copied from two days earlier (063.0332), not the opera.
