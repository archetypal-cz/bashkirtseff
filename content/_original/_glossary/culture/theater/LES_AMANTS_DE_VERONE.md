# Les Amants de Vérone

## Overview

*Les Amants de Vérone* is a lyric drama in five acts and six tableaux, music and text by Paul de Richard d'Ivry (1829-1903), the "marquis d'Ivry", imitated from Shakespeare's *Romeo and Juliet*. D'Ivry, a keen Shakespearean, began the adaptation but was overtaken in 1867 by Gounod's *Roméo et Juliette*; he published his version under the title *Les Amants de Vérone* and the pseudonym Richard Yrvid. It was first staged on 12 October 1878 at the Théâtre-Italien, salle Ventadour, with Victor Capoul and Marie Heilbron in the leading roles, and revived on 15 February 1879 at the Gaîté-Lyrique (Wikipédia fr, "Paul de Richard d'Ivry"). Heilbron (1851-1886) had already sung Juliette in d'Ivry's piece in 1872 at the Cercle des Mirlitons (Wikipédia fr, "Marie Heilbron").

## Marie and this work

Marie goes to the first run in Paris in November 1878 and records two evenings.

- **1878-11-02 (083.0089):** she dresses carefully "pour entendre «Les amants de Vérone» du marquis d'Ivry aux Italiens, à Vantadour enfin"; but the box of the Marquis de Casa-Riera turns out to be two joined baignoires, a wasted effort. She explains for posterity that Casa-Riera is a rich, blind and slightly deaf Spaniard with subscriptions to all the theatres.
- **1878-11-03 (083.0098):** "Il y a beaucoup de sentiment dans la musique du marquis d'Ivry. Il s'est pénétré de «Roméo et Juliette» de Shakespeare ... sa musique est délicieuse quoique simple et presque incolore à la première audition." She praises the staging and costumes: the real Middle Ages rather than the conventional one.
- **1878-11-23 (083.0255):** she goes again with Nadine and Paul and invites Filippini. Capoul and Mlle Heilbronn "ont chanté et joué d'une façon adorable"; she is reconciled with Capoul. The score "s'ouvre comme une fleur à la seconde audition", needing a third hearing to give its full perfume. Her "Heilbronn" is Marie Heilbron.
