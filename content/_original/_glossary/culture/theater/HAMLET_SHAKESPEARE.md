# Hamlet (Shakespeare)

%% GLO_HAMLET_SHAKESPEARE.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_HAMLET_SHAKESPEARE.0003 %%
*The Tragedy of Hamlet, Prince of Denmark* is a tragedy by William Shakespeare, written between about 1599 and 1601 and his longest play. Prince Hamlet, told by his father's ghost that Claudius murdered him to seize the Danish throne, struggles to avenge him (Wikipedia, "Hamlet"). The illustration is Delacroix's painting of the graveyard scene of act V (1839, Louvre); Marie does not mention it. For the opera that Marie heard much more often, see [Hamlet (Ambroise Thomas)](HAMLET_THOMAS.md); for the author, [Shakespeare](../../people/mentioned/SHAKESPEARE.md).

## Marie and Hamlet

%% GLO_HAMLET_SHAKESPEARE.0005 %%
- **7 December 1879, Paris.** At dinner the Mouzays and Blanc, "au lieu de faire la partie de ma tante, récite des fragments d'Hamlet, couché devant ma cheminée", then take up mandolin and guitar and sing and mime serenades (086.0629). The play is recited, presumably in French; which translation is not said.

%% GLO_HAMLET_SHAKESPEARE.0006 %%
- **22 February 1883, Paris.** "Il y a des jouissances en dehors de tout, je suis très heureuse ce soir, je viens de lire Hamlet en anglais et je suis bercée par la musique d'Ambroise Thomas" (098.0386). This is the one place where she reads the play itself, in English. The music in her head is Thomas's opera, which she had seen at the Opéra six weeks earlier (097.0419). The paragraph is currently tagged only to the opera.

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_HAMLET_SHAKESPEARE.0008 %%
Many of Marie's other uses of "Hamlet" are not performances of the play. She jokes about the Hamlet costume that Audiffret wore at a masked ball (054.0298, 054.0303), calls a young friend "plus Hamlet que jamais" (066.0731), says something "d'un air de Hamlet" (073.0010) and quotes "That is the question" in English twice (057.0212, 063.0327). The opera is separate: 007.0391, 097.0419 and 103.0249.
