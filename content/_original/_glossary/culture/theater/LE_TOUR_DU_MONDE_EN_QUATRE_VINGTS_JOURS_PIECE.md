# Le Tour du monde en quatre-vingts jours (stage adaptation, Verne and d'Ennery)

%% GLO_LE_TOUR_DU_MONDE_EN_QUATRE_VINGTS_JOURS_PIECE.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_LE_TOUR_DU_MONDE_EN_QUATRE_VINGTS_JOURS_PIECE.0003 %%
Jules Verne's novel *Le Tour du monde en quatre-vingts jours* (1872-73) was turned into a stage spectacle by Verne himself and the playwright Adolphe d'Ennery, produced in Paris in 1874 (Wikipedia, "Around the World in Eighty Days"). The Paris production was at the Théâtre de la Porte-Saint-Martin and was a spectacular success with elaborate sets (as stated in the project's notes on 028.0238; Marie also calls it a "grand succès"). The play was later translated into English and taken to the United States by the Kiralfy brothers (Wikipedia, "Around the World in Eighty Days").

## Marie and the play

%% GLO_LE_TOUR_DU_MONDE_EN_QUATRE_VINGTS_JOURS_PIECE.0005 %%
- **14 January 1875, Paris (028.0238).** "Mêmes courses, et le soir nous allons voir «Le tour du monde en quatre-vingts jours» à grand succès de la Porte Saint-Martin." She says nothing more about the evening.

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_LE_TOUR_DU_MONDE_EN_QUATRE_VINGTS_JOURS_PIECE.0007 %%
- **26 September 1875, Nice (045.0045).** When "Pâris" (Yourkoff) proposes that they go to Paris together, to the hôtel Splendide and the Procès Veauradieux, Marie answers "Le tour du monde", laughing, and he says "Justement". A link with the play or Verne's novel is possible but not confirmed; it may simply be her joking reply to his programme. It is not counted as a mention of the work.
