# Bagatelle (Offenbach)

## Overview

*Bagatelle* is a one-act opéra-comique (often called an operetta) by Jacques Offenbach with a libretto by Hector Crémieux and Ernest Blum, first produced at the Théâtre des Bouffes-Parisiens on 21 May 1874 under the composer's direction (Wikipedia, "Bagatelle (opera)"; BnF catalogue record of the libretto, Choudens and Tresse, 1874). Anna Judic created the title role of Bagatelle, a café-concert star; the maid Finette, her clarinettist lover Pistache and an eighteen-year-old admirer, Georges de Planteville, complicate her boudoir until she returns the young man's love (Wikipedia).

## Marie and this work

- **20-21 May 1874, Paris.** "On donne demain aux Bouffes Bagatelle avec Judic, nouvelle œuvre d'Offenbach. Pas une seule place, hélas !" (019.0297). On the evening of the 21st (the first night) a box is found after all (019.0304), after a day of family grievances.
- **20 June 1874.** She is taken to see it again, in a second-tier box, and disguises herself in a plain black dress and veil (020.0369).
- **21 February 1875, Nice.** At the Nice theatre: "Les Pauvres de Paris et Bagatelle. Pauvre Bagatelle défigurée et méconnaissable" with Laffite and Alain; the house is empty (030.0051). Back home, with the vocal score on the piano that she remembers "voir naître aux Bouffes", she sings and thinks of the Duke (030.0052).

## Not this work
