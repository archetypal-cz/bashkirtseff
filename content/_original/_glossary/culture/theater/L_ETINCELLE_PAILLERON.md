# L'Étincelle (Édouard Pailleron)

%% GLO_L_ETINCELLE_PAILLERON.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_L_ETINCELLE_PAILLERON.0003 %%
*L'Étincelle* is a one-act comedy by [Édouard Pailleron](../../people/mentioned/PAILLERON.md), first performed at the Comédie-Française on 13 May 1879 and published the same year by Calmann-Lévy (Wikipédia, "Édouard Pailleron"). It is a light salon piece, revived at the Comédie-Française in 1907. The Parisian columnist who wrote as "Étincelle" in *Le Figaro* is unrelated (see [Étincelle](../../people/writers/ETINCELLE.md)).

## Marie and L'Étincelle

%% GLO_L_ETINCELLE_PAILLERON.0005 %%
%% [#Samary](../../people/artists/SAMARY.md) [#Theatre_Francais](../../places/theaters/THEATRE_FRANCAIS.md) %%
- **27 November 1879, Paris.** After a day of disappointments she goes in the evening to the Théâtre-Français with Alexis and Bojidar, but "par indisposition de Mlle Samary on ne donne pas "l'Etincelle" que je voulais voir" (086.0591). A new one-act drame set in the Vendée under the Terror is given instead.

%% GLO_L_ETINCELLE_PAILLERON.0006 %%
- **12 January 1880, Paris.** After her evening party, "les Krishaber voulaient absolument que je joue "l'Etincelle" chez eux. Vous comprenez cela me flatte. Donc je disais oui jusqu'à trois heures" (087.0198). She is flattered at being asked to take a part in an amateur performance, which follows her own success as the baronne de Gontran in Gozlan's comedy (see [La Pluie et le beau temps](LA_PLUIE_ET_LE_BEAU_TEMPS.md)). There is no record that the Krishaber performance took place.

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_L_ETINCELLE_PAILLERON.0008 %%
"Etincelle" without an article in later volumes of the diary is the *Figaro* columnist (for example 094.0177, "Chez les Gavini et chez Etincelle"), not the play.
