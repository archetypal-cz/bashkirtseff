## La Fleur de thé

%% GLO_LA_FLEUR_DE_THE.0002 %%
Opérette by Charles Lecocq, first performed in Paris in 1868 (Wikipedia, «Fleur-de-Thé»). Marie names it on 13 May 1874 as an example of what is not «la vraie dépravation»: she finds Sardou's *Les Ganaches* more immoral than «La Fleur de thé», «La Belle Hélène» etc. (019.0198). She does not say she saw it.
