# L'Impromptu de Versailles (Molière)

%% GLO_IMPROMPTU_DE_VERSAILLES_MOLIERE.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_IMPROMPTU_DE_VERSAILLES_MOLIERE.0003 %%
*L'Impromptu de Versailles* is a one-act prose comedy by [Molière](MOLIERE.md), created at Versailles on 14 October 1663 and then at the Théâtre du Palais-Royal on 4 November 1663. It is a play within a play: Molière appears as himself, rehearsing his actors for a command performance before the king. It answers the attacks on *L'École des femmes* and in particular Edme Boursault's *Portrait du peintre* (Wikipédia, "L'Impromptu de Versailles"). The Comédie-Française traces its origin to the royal decree of 21 October 1680, so 1880 was its bicentenary (Wikipedia, "Comédie-Française").

## Marie and L'Impromptu de Versailles

%% GLO_IMPROMPTU_DE_VERSAILLES_MOLIERE.0005 %%
%% [#Coquelin](COQUELIN.md) [#Gambetta](../../people/politicians/GAMBETTA.md) [#Comedie_Francaise](../../places/theaters/COMEDIE_FRANCAISE.md) %%
- **27 October 1880, Paris.** "Ce soir Gavini nous mène au Français, loge Casa Riera qui est une baignoire tendue de damas rouge", with Père Géry. Gambetta sits in a similar box opposite with Arnaud and another man, "venu pour voir jouer son ami Coquelin le rôle de Molière dans "L'impromptu de Versailles"". A few days earlier the Comédie had celebrated its bicentenary, with "L'impromptu" and "Le bourgeois gentilhomme" "avec les musiques du temps" (090.0174).

%% GLO_IMPROMPTU_DE_VERSAILLES_MOLIERE.0006 %%
- **Same entry (090.0175).** "Cet "Impromptu"... une actualité d'il y a deux siècles." She gives up any idea of getting to know Gambetta and painting his portrait, with Hecht and Arnaud around him, and adds that her ears are her only concern; she says she is sorely tried by them (and in 090.0174 that she would have enjoyed the evening more if she heard better).

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_IMPROMPTU_DE_VERSAILLES_MOLIERE.0008 %%
The older entry for this play, filed as a place under the theatres, says Marie saw it at the bicentenary on 27 October 1880. Her own words only say that the Comédie's bicentenary was celebrated "il y a quelques jours" and that she saw the *Impromptu* that evening.
