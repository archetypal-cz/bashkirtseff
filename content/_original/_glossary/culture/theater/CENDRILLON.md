# Cendrillon (stage versions)

## Overview

"Cendrillon" (Cinderella) is Charles Perrault's fairy tale, *Cendrillon ou la petite pantoufle de verre* (1697, Wikipedia, "Cendrillon"). Marie names it twice, in two different stage versions that are not the same work. Neither has been identified with a printed title.

## Marie and this work

- **28 March 1873, Nice.** At the Théâtre-Français, after Molière's *Tartuffe* ("beau... mais fatigant"), she "se ranime" at the second piece, "Cendrillon, très bien jouée" (003.0122). She had her blue silk dress; the theatre was full of "monde élégant" and the White family sat beside them (003.0123). The piece was probably a one-act comedy, vaudeville or féerie played by the local company; nothing further could be found.
- **14 August 1873, Vienna.** At Renz's circus (the Vienna Exposition), "une pantomime, Cendrillon", performed by children, in which historical figures appear: Bismarck, Moltke, Napoléon, Thiers, Mac-Mahon, the Shah and Tsar Alexander, which angers Marie "pour la Russie" (008.0025). A circus pantomime with political parade: this is plainly not the Nice piece.
