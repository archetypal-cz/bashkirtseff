# Gentil Bernard

Not a person in Marie's life: *Gentil-Bernard ou L'Art d'aimer*, a vaudeville by Dumanoir and Clairville (Variétés, 1846), named after the court poet Pierre-Joseph Bernard, «Gentil-Bernard» (1708–1775) (Wikipedia «Dumanoir», «Clairville», «Pierre-Joseph Bernard», as cited in the footnote on the paragraph). Marie sees it on 1874-10-23 (024.0392) at the Nice theatre: «On donne "Gentil Bernard ou l'art d'aimer", une pièce dont j'entendis fort peu de choses, nous parlions tout le temps.» In the same paragraph Saëtone is in the Durand box with M. de Cessole. Belongs among the theatre works.
