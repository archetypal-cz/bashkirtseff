# Le Roi de Lahore (Massenet)

%% GLO_LE_ROI_DE_LAHORE_MASSENET.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_LE_ROI_DE_LAHORE_MASSENET.0003 %%
*Le Roi de Lahore* ("The King of Lahore") is an opera in five acts (six tableaux) by Jules Massenet to a French libretto by Louis Gallet. It was first performed at the Palais Garnier in Paris on 27 April 1877. It was Massenet's third opera produced in Paris and his first major success there, and it went on to be played across Europe (Wikipedia, "Le roi de Lahore"; libretto on the Internet Archive, "leroidelahoreopr1877mass"). Composer: [Massenet](../../people/artists/MASSENET.md).

%% GLO_LE_ROI_DE_LAHORE_MASSENET.0004 %%
The third act, after which Marie says Massenet was acclaimed, is the scene of Indra's paradise, a ballet of dancers on a lawn under palms; a contemporary drawing of that scene by Daniel Vierge (1877) illustrates this entry (title as given on the Wikimedia Commons file).

## Marie and Le Roi de Lahore

%% GLO_LE_ROI_DE_LAHORE_MASSENET.0006 %%
**9 January 1879, Paris.** Marie, who was in the company of Mme Gavini, says she cannot comment on the music because she had to talk and listen to nonsense: "Je voudrais me payer une bonne soirée d'Opéra, mais seule ou avec de Daillens afin d'écouter. Massenet a dû passer un bon moment quand on lui a fait une ovation après son troisième acte du 'Roi de Latore' (fort peu apprécié et mal compris à l'Opéra)" (083.0576). She goes on: "Je l'ai envié à ce moment et j'ai envié sa femme ou sa maîtresse" (083.0577). Her "Latore" is printed so in Mon Journal (t.12 p.193) and is a slip for Lahore (Researcher's note); that the opera meant is Massenet's *Le Roi de Lahore* is probable and is not stated in the print.

%% GLO_LE_ROI_DE_LAHORE_MASSENET.0007 %%
**10 January 1879, Paris.** "…comme hier pour Massenet quand on a bissé son Incantation et qu'à la seconde fois cela parut moins beau" (083.0580). Marie writes of an encore of Massenet's "Incantation" at the same performance, the day before; she does not say in which opera the number occurs, and this entry does not identify it with a number in *Le Roi de Lahore*. Her remark that the work was "fort peu apprécié et mal compris" is her own judgement of the audience at the Opéra.

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_LE_ROI_DE_LAHORE_MASSENET.0009 %%
The performance that Marie attended or heard of is not dated more closely than the two entries of 9 and 10 January 1879.
