# Si j'étais roi (Adolphe Adam)

## Overview

*Si j'étais roi* («If I were king») is an opéra-comique in three acts by Adolphe Adam (1803–1856), to a libretto by Adolphe d'Ennery and Jules-Henri Brésil. It was first performed at the Théâtre-Lyrique in Paris on 4 September 1852 and became one of Adam's most popular stage works; the overture in particular stayed in the repertoire. The plot is set in a fairy-tale Goa: a poor fisherman who has saved a princess is made king for a day (en.wikipedia «Si j'étais roi»; fr.wikipedia «Si j'étais roi (opéra)»).
