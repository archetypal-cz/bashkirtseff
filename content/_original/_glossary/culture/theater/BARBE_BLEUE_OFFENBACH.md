# Barbe-bleue (Offenbach)

## Overview

*Barbe-bleue* is an opéra bouffe in three acts and four tableaux by Jacques Offenbach, to a libretto by Henri Meilhac and Ludovic Halévy, first performed at the Théâtre des Variétés, Paris, on 5 February 1866 (Wikipédia fr, "Barbe-Bleue (opéra bouffe)"). It retells Perrault's fairy tale as a farce: the prince's five earlier wives have in fact only been given sleeping draughts by his alchemist and are found alive in a luxurious store-room, and the peasant girl Boulotte leads a "feminist" procession on the palace (same source). It belongs to the Second Empire golden age of Offenbach's collaborations with Meilhac and Halévy, with *Orphée aux enfers*, *La Belle Hélène* and *La Vie parisienne*.

## Marie and this work

Marie never describes the piece; she mentions it three times, always in passing.

- **1875-11-05 (048.0592):** "On donne «Barbe bleue», il est au théâtre." Audiffret goes to the theatre; Marie stays at home, absorbed in card-reading and prayer. The work is not named beyond the title; Offenbach's operetta is the obvious candidate (identification by the researcher, not stated by Marie).
- **1876-11-08 (067.0894):** her father comes to sing "des airs de Barbe-Bleue" while she plays the piano; she turns to the "Lettre de Vénus" from *La Belle Hélène* instead and remarks that *La Belle Hélène* is delightful, from the time when Offenbach "commençait" and had not yet cheapened himself with "opérettes à deux sous".
- **1881-05-05 (091.0564):** "je n'aime pas l'opérette ... tout ce qu'on a fait depuis «Barbe Bleue, la Belle Hélène, Orphée, Giroflé-Girofla» est insipide." Here the title heads her list of the operettas she accepts as classics of her childhood (see the footnote in 091: *Giroflé-Girofla* is by Lecocq, not Offenbach, and dates from 1874).

## Identification notes

Entries 048 and 091 do not name the composer. Given the titles listed alongside it in 091 and the airs sung at the piano in 067, Offenbach's 1866 *Barbe-bleue* is certain for 067 and 091 and very probable for 048. The diary does not say which production Marie saw in 048, or whether Audiffret's evening was a Nice performance.
