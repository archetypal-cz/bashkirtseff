# Les Mirabeau (Jules Claretie)

%% GLO_LES_MIRABEAU_CLARETIE.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_LES_MIRABEAU_CLARETIE.0003 %%
*Les Mirabeau* is a historical drama in five acts and seven tableaux by the critic and novelist Jules Claretie, first performed in Paris at the Théâtre des Nations (the former Théâtre-Historique) on 31 October 1879 and published the same year by Tresse. The cast lists Paul Deshayes as the great Mirabeau, with Beaumarchais, the marquis de Mirabeau and the bailli de Mirabeau among the characters. The printed edition is dedicated "à la mémoire de Louis de Loménie, de l'Académie française, historien des Mirabeau", which ties the play to the book Marie had read in June (see [Les Mirabeau (Loménie)](../literature/LES_MIRABEAU_LOMENIE.md)). Claretie says in his preface that the drama "est chassé de presque partout" and that a historical play that touches politics risks pleasing neither friends nor opponents (Claretie, *Les Mirabeau*, Tresse, 1879, title page, dedication and preface).

## Marie and Les Mirabeau

%% GLO_LES_MIRABEAU_CLARETIE.0005 %%
%% [#Gavini](../../people/mentioned/GAVINI.md) %%
- **4 November 1879, Paris.** "Nous allons voir "Les Mirabeau" pièce nouvelle, en deux loges avec M. et Mme Gavini, Chaudordy, de Morgan, Géry et puis Gabriel" (086.0473). The theatre is not named; the identification with Claretie's drama rests on the title, "pièce nouvelle" and the opening four days earlier.

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_LES_MIRABEAU_CLARETIE.0007 %%
This is the only play of that title found for the autumn of 1879, but no review or playbill confirms that Marie's party saw it at the Théâtre des Nations. The earlier "Mirabeau" of June 1879 is Loménie's book, and "Mirabeau" as a nickname for Cassagnac is neither.
