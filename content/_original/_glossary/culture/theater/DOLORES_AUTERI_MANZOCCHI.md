# Dolores (Auteri-Manzocchi)

%% GLO_DOLORES_AUTERI_MANZOCCHI.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_DOLORES_AUTERI_MANZOCCHI.0003 %%
*Dolores* is a dramma lirico in four parts by Salvatore Auteri-Manzocchi (1845-1924), with a libretto by his uncle Michele Auteri Pomàr. The composer wrote it for the soprano Isabella Galletti-Gianoli, a Bologna-born singer (1835-1901). Its first performance was at the Teatro della Pergola, Florence, on 23 February 1875, with Galletti-Gianoli in the title role; it reached Milan (Teatro Dal Verme) in June 1875 and then Rome's Teatro Apollo, where the libretto printed for the Apollo is dedicated "Ad Isabella Galletti Gianoli" and names the season as the carnival and Lent of 1875-76 (Italian Wikipedia, «Dolores (opera)» and «Isabella Galletti Gianoli»; libretto record in the Biblioteca Fondazione Cini via librettodopera.it).

%% GLO_DOLORES_AUTERI_MANZOCCHI.0004 %%
The story is that of a Spanish noblewoman who saves a prince, Manfredi, from bandits, falls in love with him, and finds at his castle the husband she believed dead; she takes poison (Italian Wikipedia, «Dolores (opera)»).

## Marie and Dolores

%% GLO_DOLORES_AUTERI_MANZOCCHI.0006 %%
**27 January 1876, Rome.** "Ce soir la première représentation de "Dolorès", nouvel opéra par un jeune maestro protégé et créé par la célèbre Gaietti" (053.0124). Marie goes to the opera at eight with Lola and Dina; the house is full, and the "Soroka de Rome" stares at her from his place.

%% GLO_DOLORES_AUTERI_MANZOCCHI.0007 %%
**30 January 1876.** A chevalier tells her she was "l'autre soir" the admiration of the whole house; she answers, deflecting the compliment: "vous parlez de la représentation de "Dolorès" ? c'était très joli" (053.0214).

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_DOLORES_AUTERI_MANZOCCHI.0009 %%
The diary names neither the composer nor the title in full. The match with Auteri-Manzocchi's *Dolores* is an inference, but a strong one: a new opera called *Dolores*, by a young composer (he was thirty), promoted by a celebrated soprano, given in Rome in January 1876 in the 1875-76 Apollo season. "Gaietti" is taken to be Marie's rendering of Galletti, the celebrated soprano for whom the opera was written. Marie had heard a prima donna Galletti at Nice in April 1873 (see the Mme Galletti entry); that she is the same singer is not established. The precise date of the Rome première is not confirmed by the sources consulted; Marie's "première représentation" is 27 January 1876.

%% GLO_DOLORES_AUTERI_MANZOCCHI.0010 %%
The name *Dolorès* is also the first name of one of Audiffret's step-sisters (087.0833); that is a different person.
