# Le Panache (Gondinet)

%% GLO_LE_PANACHE_GONDINET.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_LE_PANACHE_GONDINET.0003 %%
*Le Panache* is a comedy in three acts by Edmond Gondinet, first performed at the Théâtre du Palais-Royal, Paris, on 12 October 1875. Gondinet drew on his own experience as a civil servant, and the piece is described as a satire of the official (Wikipedia, "Edmond Gondinet"). It was printed by Calmann-Lévy in 1875 (HathiTrust and Online Books Page catalogue records). It counts among the successes that disarmed the last prejudices against Gondinet's talent (Wikipedia, "Edmond Gondinet").

%% GLO_LE_PANACHE_GONDINET.0004 %%
## Marie and Le Panache

%% GLO_LE_PANACHE_GONDINET.0005 %%
- **13 November 1875, Nice (049.0370).** "Pendant que les miens s'habillent pour le théâtre je m'enferme chez moi et pleure. On donne «Le Panache», pour la première fois, le grand succès du Palais Royal, à Paris." The performance is therefore at Nice, a month after the Paris first night; "pour la première fois" refers to its first showing there. Marie stays at home.

%% GLO_LE_PANACHE_GONDINET.0006 %%
- **10 March 1876, Rome (055.0503).** "Pas de Torlonia. Je nomme Prater Torlonia, je ne parle que de Torlonia. Et le soir nous allons au Théâtre Valle, on donne «Le Panache», une troupe française." This is the [Teatro Valle](../../places/theaters/TEATRO_VALLE.md); the French company is not named.

%% GLO_LE_PANACHE_GONDINET.0007 %%
Marie says nothing about the play itself. The "panache rouge d'Odette" of 1 February 1883 (098.0259) is a plume, not the play.

%% GLO_LE_PANACHE_GONDINET.0008 %%
## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_LE_PANACHE_GONDINET.0009 %%
Marie never names the author. The attribution to Gondinet rests on the title, the Palais-Royal and the 1875 date, which match the printed comedy and the Paris first night of October 1875.
