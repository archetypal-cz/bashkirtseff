# Le Monde où l'on s'ennuie (Édouard Pailleron)

%% GLO_LE_MONDE_OU_L_ON_S_ENNUIE.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_LE_MONDE_OU_L_ON_S_ENNUIE.0003 %%
*Le Monde où l'on s'ennuie* is a comedy in three acts by Édouard Pailleron (1834-1899), first performed at the Comédie-Française on 25 April 1881. It satirises Parisian literary and scientific salons and their affectations. It was a prodigious success, with more than a thousand performances at the Comédie-Française (Wikipédia, "Édouard Pailleron"). Pailleron, a lawyer and co-director of the *Revue des Deux Mondes*, was elected to the Académie française in 1882 (same article). For the author see [Pailleron](../../people/mentioned/PAILLERON.md); the title plays on his earlier one-act *Le Monde où l'on s'amuse* (Gymnase, 1868).

## Marie and Le Monde où l'on s'ennuie

%% GLO_LE_MONDE_OU_L_ON_S_ENNUIE.0005 %%
%% [#Pailleron](../../people/mentioned/PAILLERON.md) [#Theatre_Francais](../../places/theaters/THEATRE_FRANCAIS.md) %%
- **2 May 1881, Paris.** Barely a week after the première: "Ce soir "Le monde où l'on [s'ennuie]" "Pailleron" au Français. Délicieuse comédie et je fais un effet de cou." She wears black, with a square neckline open just enough to show the "attaches", so that her neck looks very white and is stared at through opera glasses (091.0553). The first words of the title are partly blotted out in the manuscript.

%% GLO_LE_MONDE_OU_L_ON_S_ENNUIE.0006 %%
%% [#Gabriel_Gery](../../people/mentioned/GABRIEL_GERY.md) %%
- **13 January 1882, Paris.** In the studio, after Gabriel has taken her bare hand and kissed it, Marie compares her "trouble juvénile" to a moment in the play, when a young girl in the comedy is seen as "folle", a "petite fille", after the words of Royer and Arthur (094.0169). The surrounding words are blackened in the manuscript, so the exact scene she means is not recoverable.
