## Jarevna (Tsarevna)

%% GLO_JAREVNA.0002 %%
A vessel, not a person. On 1 December 1874, watching the crowd at the Nice station hoping to see the Empress, Marie notes that Walitsky saw her with «le consul Pelikan et Rangouli, capitaine de la *Jarevna*» (027.0106). *Tsarevna* is the Russian word for a tsar's daughter or princess; the ship is not identified further and no source on disk gives details.
