# Veglione

Italian: a large Carnival masked ball, usually held at night in a theatre. Marie hears of a «grand Veglione à l'Apollo» in Rome (8 February 1877) and goes, masked, to the charity veglione at the [San Carlo](../../places/theaters/SAN_CARLO.md) in Naples on the night of 10 February 1877, where she flirts with Larderel (carnet 068). «Nos cavaliers du Veglione» recur through carnets 069–071.
