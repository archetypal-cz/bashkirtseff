# L'Estafette

## Overview
*L'Estafette*, a Paris daily of the late 1870s, republican-leaning («un mélange de républicanisme», 083.0127). Marie reads it for Gambetta and for the death of the Prince Imperial in 1879 (085.0085, 085.0385, 085.0479, 085.0514; L. Detroyat's article); 1878 reports 082.0278, 083.0127.
