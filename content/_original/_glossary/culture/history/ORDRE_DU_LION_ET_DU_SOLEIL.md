# Order of the Lion and the Sun

## Overview
The Order of the Lion and the Sun, a Persian order of chivalry founded in 1808 and widely given to foreigners in the nineteenth century. It was created by Fath-Ali Shah Qajar and intended first to reward foreigners' services; detailed statutes followed under Mohammad Shah in 1834, and it was replaced by the Order of Homayoun under the Pahlavis. (Sources: Wikipedia, «Order of the Lion and the Sun», https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Lion_and_the_Sun; Encyclopaedia Iranica, «Decorations», https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/decorations; the later grade structure varies between sources.)

The Shah was Naser al-Din (footnote 080.0710.1).
