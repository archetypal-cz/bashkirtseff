# Battle of Gravelotte

## Overview
The battle of Gravelotte (Gravelotte–Saint-Privat), 18 August 1870, in the Franco-Prussian War. Named in a 1878 wedding report (081.0094) and in May 1883, when Marie goes to see Édouard Detaille's panorama of the evening of the battle (099.0516).
