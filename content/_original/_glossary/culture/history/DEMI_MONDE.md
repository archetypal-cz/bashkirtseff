# Demi-monde

## Overview
*Demi-monde* ("half-world") is the society of kept women and courtesans and the men who frequented them, on the margin of respectable society. The word was coined by Alexandre Dumas fils in his comedy *Le Demi-Monde* (1855) and was in everyday use by the 1870s (Wikipedia, "Demi-monde"). Marie uses it as the opposite of *le monde*, the society she wants to belong to.
