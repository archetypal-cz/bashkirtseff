## La Grotte de Fingal

%% GLO_GROTTE_DE_FINGAL.0002 %%
Piece performed at a concert of pupils of Mme Damoreau in Nice on 17 April 1875: «deux autres paires de demoiselles jouèrent "La Grotte de Fingal"» (031.0261). Marie is unimpressed by the evening (the pupils «ont chanté vilainement»).

%% GLO_GROTTE_DE_FINGAL.0003 %%
The title is the French name of Felix Mendelssohn's concert overture *Die Hebriden* (Op. 26, «Fingal's Cave»), inspired by his 1829 visit to Staffa; first performed in London on 14 May 1832, published 1833, and known under the title *Fingalshöhle* in the Breitkopf & Härtel edition of 1834 (Wikipedia, «The Hebrides (overture)»). That the girls played it in pairs points to a four-hand piano arrangement; Marie does not name the composer or the arrangement, so this is an inference from the title.
