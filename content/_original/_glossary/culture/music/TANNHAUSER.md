# Tannhäuser

## Overview
Richard Wagner's opera *Tannhäuser und der Sängerkrieg auf Wartburg* (first given in Dresden in 1845; revised for the Paris Opéra in 1861) (en.wikipedia «Tannhäuser (opera)»).

## Relevance to Marie
At a pupils' concert in Nice on 17 April 1875 she writes that after the singing «Laussel et Damoreau ont joué à deux pianos l'ouverture de "Tannhaüser" je crois, puis la dame a chanté» (031.0261); the pupils of Mme Damoreau sang badly but with good method. She is not sure of the piece (je crois).
