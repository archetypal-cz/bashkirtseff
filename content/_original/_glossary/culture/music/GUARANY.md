# Il Guarany

## Overview
*Il Guarany*, opera in four acts by the Brazilian composer Antônio Carlos Gomes, libretto by Antonio Scalvini and Carlo D'Ormeville after José de Alencar's novel *O Guarani*; first performed at La Scala, Milan, in 1870 (Wikipedia, "Il Guarany"). Its hero is a Guaraní chief in sixteenth-century Brazil. Marie calls it «Les Guaranis».
