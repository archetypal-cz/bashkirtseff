# "Connais-tu le pays" (Mignon, Ambroise Thomas)

## Overview

Mignon's romance in Act I of Ambroise Thomas's opéra-comique *Mignon* (libretto by Jules Barbier and Michel Carré after Goethe's *Wilhelm Meisters Lehrjahre*), first given at the Opéra-Comique, Paris, on 17 November 1866. Mignon, asked where she comes from, remembers a sunny land, perhaps Italy, and sings "Connais-tu le pays où fleurit l'oranger ?", the French counterpart of Goethe's poem "Kennst du das Land". (Source: Wikipedia fr, "Mignon (opéra)", https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mignon_(op%C3%A9ra).) Thomas's score is public domain.

## Marie and this work

It is Marie's "romance of the year" 1875–76 and the song of her homesickness for Italy.

- 049.0441 (15 Nov 1875): "Chaque année a sa romance. L'année dernière c'était *On revient toujours à ses premières amours*, cette année c'est l'air de *Mignon*. Toutes les deux ont fini tristement pour moi" (see [On revient toujours](ON_REVIENT_TOUJOURS_A_SES_PREMIERES_AMOURS.md)). Audiffret identifies the aria from across the street at 048.0383.
- 055.1016 (22 Mar 1876, Rome): after a confused day over Torlonia she sits at the piano: "l'air de *Connais-tu le pays* est la consolation des âmes affligées et l'accompagnement de tous les chagrins". The footnote identifies it as Thomas's aria after Goethe.
- 056.0471 (6 Apr 1876): she sings "la prière de la madone, de *Mignon*" (a different number of the opera; not identified here).
- 062.0506 (20 Jun 1876): sings for General Bihovetz, who has never properly heard her, and holds the sustained note "de Mignon".
- 062.0547 (22 Jun 1876): praising Italy she cries with Mignon «Italia ! Reggio del ciel ! sol beato…», the Italian words (footnote suggests an Italian-language version of the opera; unverified). 062.0319 carries a footnote on "Ah! son rapita" from the same opera.
- 065.0002 and 066.0235 (Aug-Sep 1876): she quotes (065.0002, struck out in the manuscript) and recalls the first line, "Pas Nice, mais l'Italie."

Marie's "Mignon" is thus always this aria except where she names the prayer; see also [Il était un roi de Thulé](IL_ETAIT_UN_ROI_DE_THULE.md).
