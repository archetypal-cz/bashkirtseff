# "On revient toujours à ses premières amours" (romance)

## Overview

A French romance, and also a proverb ("one always returns to one's first loves"). I could not identify the composer or the date of the song; searches of IMSLP, BnF and the popular-music catalogues returned nothing under this title. Treat it as an unidentified sentimental romance of the early 1870s.

## Marie and this work

It is "la romance" of her 1874-75 season in Nice, as *Mignon* is that of 1875-76.

- 030.0058 (22 Feb 1875, Nice): the song is mentioned the evening of the opera performance of *Il barbiere* (the LAN note says "likely a popular song of the era").
- 049.0441 (15 Nov 1875): "Chaque année a sa romance. L'année dernière c'était *On revient toujours à ses premières amours*, cette année c'est l'air de *Mignon*. Toutes les deux ont fini tristement pour moi" (see [Connais-tu le pays](CONNAIS_TU_LE_PAYS.md)).
- 051.0216 (31 Dec 1875): used as a proverb, not necessarily the song: Audiffret's one real passion was Miss Robenson, "on revient toujours à ses premières amours".
