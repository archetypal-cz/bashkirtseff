# Chansons niçoises

%% GLO_CHANSONS_NICOISES.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_CHANSONS_NICOISES.0003 %%
The folk songs of Nice and its county, sung in the local Niçois dialect (*nissart*), a variety of Occitan. Marie learns them in the summer of 1875, sings them with delight and later with bitterness, because she associates them with Nice and with Audiffret. Her only named example is [Le rossignol che vola](LE_ROSSIGNOL_CHE_VOLA.md).

%% GLO_CHANSONS_NICOISES.0004 %%
## Marie and the songs

%% GLO_CHANSONS_NICOISES.0005 %%
**24 June 1875, Nice.** "En honneur de Girofla ... J'ai appris des chansons niçoises. Elles me rappelleront toujours ce temps bienheureux" (034.0557). Girofla is her nickname for [Émile d'Audiffret](../../people/recurring/EMILE_D_AUDIFFRET.md).

%% GLO_CHANSONS_NICOISES.0006 %%
**9 September 1875.** Returning to Nice by train: "Depuis Antibes je m'engosille [sic] à chanter des chansons niçoises au grand ébahissement des employés des gares" (042.0043). She adds that nothing "de profond", nothing "giroflien sérieux", should be read into her mood.

%% GLO_CHANSONS_NICOISES.0007 %%
**17 June 1876, Nice.** After the break: "Audiffret m'a fait chanter les exécrables chansons niçoises" (062.0430), the songs she now describes as execrable, in a passage that goes on to her new taste for Italian language and poetry.

%% GLO_CHANSONS_NICOISES.0008 %%
Marie also speaks Niçois (047.0064) and, in Rome on 15 February 1876, has a letter written in it for her (054.0113: "je fais écrire par Fortuné une lettre en niçois").
