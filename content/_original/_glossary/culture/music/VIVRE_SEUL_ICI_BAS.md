# "Vivre seul ici-bas" (romance)

%% GLO_VIVRE_SEUL_ICI_BAS.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_VIVRE_SEUL_ICI_BAS.0003 %%
A sentimental salon romance, of which only the first line and a paraphrase of the verses survive in the diary: "Vivre seul ici bas sans amour, sans mystère". Neither composer nor poet has been identified, and the song is known only from Marie's account; no score or text can be linked.

%% GLO_VIVRE_SEUL_ICI_BAS.0004 %%
## Marie and "Vivre seul ici-bas"

%% GLO_VIVRE_SEUL_ICI_BAS.0005 %%
**5 February 1878, Paris.** Marie hears [Mme de Fayet](../../people/aristocracy/MME_DE_FAYET.md) sing it, and summarises the song with her usual mockery: "C'est une charmante romance de célibataire sans doute où l'auteur raconte comment l'oiseau suit dans les bois sa compagne qui fuit, la brise doucement agite le feuillage, les flots en murmurant caressent le rivage, le jour cherche la nuit et la nuit cherche le jour, bref tout est poursuivi excepté l'auteur qui de guerre lasse prie le bon Dieu de reprendre sa vie." Mme de Fayet has "une façon de murmurer le mot amour" that makes Marie say that, if she were a man left alone with her singing it, she would heap all the furniture in front of her as a barricade (078.0061).

%% GLO_VIVRE_SEUL_ICI_BAS.0006 %%
**8 February 1878, Paris.** She paints a comic watercolour, "une horrible pochade", of Mme de Fayet, with her opulent figure, yellow hair and blue dress, singing "Vivre seule ici-bas, etc." with her arms stretched toward a cupid with the face of "Popaul" (Paul de [Cassagnac](../../people/recurring/CASSAGNAC.md)), who flees in terror, while two birds chase each other in the sky (078.0084).

%% GLO_VIVRE_SEUL_ICI_BAS.0007 %%
## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_VIVRE_SEUL_ICI_BAS.0008 %%
The wording of the title varies: "seul" in 078.0061 (as quoted) and "seule" in 078.0084 and its Researcher's note. Both are given as aliases. Whether the song is a printed romance or a manuscript piece of Mme de Fayet's repertoire is unknown.
