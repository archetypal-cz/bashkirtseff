# Je ne suis qu'une herbagère

%% GLO_JE_NE_SUIS_QU_UNE_HERBAGERE.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_JE_NE_SUIS_QU_UNE_HERBAGERE.0003 %%
A song, or romance, with the title *Je ne suis qu'une herbagère*, which Marie sings as a party piece in 1873. The composer and the song have not been identified: searches of the usual French song and repertoire sources found no song of this title. *Herbagère* is a woman who grazes or fattens cattle on pasture (TLFi, «herbager, -ère»), so the title means roughly "I am only a grazier's girl" or "a herdswoman"; it may be a variant of a *bergère* (shepherdess) song, but this is a guess and no such song has been confirmed.

%% GLO_JE_NE_SUIS_QU_UNE_HERBAGERE.0004 %%
## Marie and « Je ne suis qu'une herbagère »

%% GLO_JE_NE_SUIS_QU_UNE_HERBAGERE.0005 %%
**9 March 1873, Nice.** At the Howards' house, after dinner in the dark drawing-room, the girls beg her to sing, kneeling: "J'ai chanté "Santa Lucia" "Le soleil s'est levé", "Je ne suis qu'une herbagère" et quelques roulades. Ils étaient tous tellement extasiés qu'ils se sont mis à m'embrasser affreusement" (002.0240). See [Santa Lucia](../../people/mentioned/SANTA_LUCIA.md).

%% GLO_JE_NE_SUIS_QU_UNE_HERBAGERE.0006 %%
**9 November 1873, Nice.** The Howard girls, [Hélène](../../people/recurring/HELENE_HOWARD.md) above all, want to take her as a model. After a thousand refusals she agrees to sing "dans une chambre sombre", "je suis comme les rossignols je ne chante que dans l'obscurité", and sings "Je ne suis qu'une herbagère"; they are "delighted, extasié, charmé" (012.0140).

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_JE_NE_SUIS_QU_UNE_HERBAGERE.0008 %%
The earlier gloss of *herbagère* as "herb-seller" is wrong; see the TLFi sense above. No score or text could be found, so there is no full-text link.
