# Caricature canvas of the Neapolitan young men (1877)

%% GLO_CARICATURES_NAPLES_1877.0001 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_CARICATURES_NAPLES_1877.0002 %%
%% [#Naples](../../places/cities/NAPLES.md) [#Zunica](../../people/mentioned/ZUNICA.md) [#Porcinari](../../people/mentioned/PORCINARI.md) [#Caracciolo](../../people/mentioned/CARACCIOLO.md) [#Santasiglia](../../people/mentioned/SANTASIGLIA.md) %%
On **23 April 1877**, at Naples, with her mother ill and the doctor and Doenhoff coming and going, Marie notes: "je fais les caricatures de Zunica, Porcinari, Caracciolo et Santasiglia ! La toile est assez grande et elle contient déjà douze personnes, faites en deux ou trois fois..." (070.0866). It is a single large canvas on which she adds caricature heads of the Neapolitan young men of her circle, a dozen by that evening, in several short sittings. These were the "jeune Naples" who came to the family's rooms in the evenings (070.0541, 070.0817). **Whereabouts unknown; no image.** She had made caricatures of her Nice circle in 1875 ([#Grande_caricature_1875](./GRANDE_CARICATURE_1875.md)).

## Marie and this work

%% GLO_CARICATURES_NAPLES_1877.0004 %%
Marie's note shows how she worked at Naples: whenever she could not go out, she painted the visitors. The same week she also drew Melissano life-size in two minutes ([#Portraits_Melissano](./PORTRAITS_MELISSANO.md)). The picture is mentioned once; the inventory titles it with the first name "Zunica" only because of Marie's phrase.
