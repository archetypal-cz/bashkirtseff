## Overview

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_DE_L_ARTISTE_FUSAIN.0002 %%
%% [#Portrait](./PORTRAIT.md) [#Dessin](../literature/DESSIN.md) %%
A drawing in charcoal and white chalk on paper, catalogued by the **Petit Palais, Musée des Beaux-Arts de la Ville de Paris** as *Portrait de l'artiste*, inventory **PPD1014**. The museum's detailed record gives it as 61 × 47 cm (framed 80 × 65 cm) and says it was given by hand by Marie Stepanovna Babanine, the artist's mother, on 11 July 1916. It is the third Bashkirtseff in the Petit Palais, beside *[La Parisienne](./LA_PARISIENNE.md)* (given 1902) and the drawing *[Femme lisant à l'angle d'un piano](./FEMME_LISANT_PIANO.md)* (given 1907).

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_DE_L_ARTISTE_FUSAIN.0003 %%
## Notes

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_DE_L_ARTISTE_FUSAIN.0004 %%
The online record carries **no image**, so the drawing could not be examined for this entry, and it is undated. Kernberger reproduces a "Self-portrait, Marie Bashkirtseff, Musée du Petit Palais, Paris" without date or medium. That is probably this sheet, since the museum's other two works by her are not self-portraits, but the identification has not been checked against the original.
