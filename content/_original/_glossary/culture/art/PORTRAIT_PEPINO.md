# Portrait of Pepino on a blue ground (1875)

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_PEPINO.0001 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_PEPINO.0002 %%
%% [#Albert_Gautier](../../people/mentioned/ALBERT_GAUTIER.md) [#Art_practice](../themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
On **29 October 1875** Marie stays in because "il n'y aura personne, Lachaud plaide" (the trial of the lawyer [#Lachaud](../../people/mentioned/LACHAUD.md) keeps the Promenade empty) and writes: "je suis en train de mettre au monde un Pépino immense, sur fond bleu" (048.0166). It is a large picture of **Pepino** (Albert Gautier of Nice, see [#Albert_Gautier](../../people/mentioned/ALBERT_GAUTIER.md)) on a blue ground. Marie does not say whether it is a painting, a pastel or a caricature, nor whether "immense" refers to the size of the picture or to the sitter, whom she calls a few days later "cet immense bâton de télégraphe" (6 November, 048.0652). **Whereabouts unknown; no image.**

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_PEPINO.0003 %%
## Marie and this work

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_PEPINO.0004 %%
Pepino was then paying court to her in the loge and the salon (048.0080-0112, 049.0055), and Marie was making oil caricatures of her Nice circle that autumn (see [#Grande_caricature_1875](./GRANDE_CARICATURE_1875.md)); the picture may belong to that group, but the diary does not say so. This is the only mention of it.
