## Overview

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_DAME_ATHENES.0002 %%
%% [#Portrait](./PORTRAIT.md) %%
*Portrait of a Lady*, about 1881, oil on canvas, 63 × 59 cm, is in the **National Gallery – Alexandros Soutsos Museum** in Athens, inventory **Π.267**. The museum's record names the donor as "Maria Stepanovna Baskirtzeff-Babanina", the artist's mother, so Greece is one more of the countries where she placed her daughter's work after 1884. The record gives no date of the gift. A copy of the image published on Europeana under CC BY-NC-ND shows a young woman in a black hat with a white collar.

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_DAME_ATHENES.0003 %%
## Notes

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_DAME_ATHENES.0004 %%
The sitter is not named. No diary passage has been matched to the picture, and its 1885 catalogue number, if it has one, is not known: the catalogue has many portraits and heads near this size.
