## Overview

%% GLO_MARTINEZ_MONTANES_VELAZQUEZ.0002 %%
The *Portrait of the sculptor Juan Martínez Montañés* (c. 1635-36) by Diego Velázquez shows the Seville sculptor (1568-1649) in black, his sculpting hand held out in front of the half-finished clay head of Philip IV he was modelling at the time (Velázquez painted it while the sculptor was in Madrid for that commission). It is in the **Museo del Prado, Madrid**. (Sources: Museo del Prado; Commons description of the Prado file; the sitter and subject are well established, the date varies slightly between catalogues.)

## Marie and this painting

%% GLO_MARTINEZ_MONTANES_VELAZQUEZ.0004 %%
**Identification.** Marie does not name the sitter. On **4 October 1881**, in the Prado, she writes of « le portrait d'un sculpteur inconnu » in which « il y a une main qui est la clef de toute l'exécution de Carolus-Duran qui comme on sait veut rééditer Vélasquez » (093.0179). On **6 October 1881** she writes that she has « copié la main de Vélasquez » (093.0189), working « bien modestement en noir et en mantille comme toutes les femmes ici ». The « portrait d'un sculpteur inconnu » is identified here with the Prado's Montañés portrait, whose most conspicuous feature is the sculptor's hand; this is an inference by the editors, not stated in the diary.

%% GLO_MARTINEZ_MONTANES_VELAZQUEZ.0005 %%
On **18 September 1882** she describes the stairway and anteroom of the studio of her master [Tony Robert-Fleury](../../people/artists/ROBERT_FLEURY.md) (the context is the portrait he is painting of her), hung with photographs of old masters: on the landing one sees the *Gioconda* and « le portrait du sculpteur de Vélasquez » (096.0257). So a photograph of this painting hung in Tony's studio, a few months after her own copying in Madrid.
