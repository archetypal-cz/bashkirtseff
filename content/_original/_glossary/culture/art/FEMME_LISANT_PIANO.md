## Overview

%% GLO_FEMME_LISANT_PIANO.0002 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) [#Reading](../themes/READING.md) %%
*Femme lisant à l'angle d'un piano* is a drawing — crayon, charcoal, *frotté* and *gratté*, 24,6 × 19,2 cm on a mount of 53 × 40 cm — in the **Petit Palais, Musée des Beaux-Arts de la Ville de Paris**, inv. **PPD929**. It was given by the artist's mother in **1907**, five years after she gave the same museum *La Parisienne*.

%% GLO_FEMME_LISANT_PIANO.0003 %%
It is undated. Marie left far more drawings than paintings — the 1885 posthumous catalogue lists 118 of them against 100 paintings, plus another eighty-odd academic studies from the model — but almost none of that graphic work survives in public collections. This sheet is one of the few, and Paris Musées has released it under **CC0** at 4317 × 5531 px, so it is also one of the best-reproduced things she made.

%% GLO_FEMME_LISANT_PIANO.0004 %%
## Description

%% GLO_FEMME_LISANT_PIANO.0005 %%
A woman reading, seated at the corner of a piano. The technique is worth noticing: alongside crayon and charcoal the museum records *frotté* (rubbed) and *gratté* (scratched back) — the drawing is worked additively and subtractively, lights lifted out of the black rather than left as blank paper. That is studio-trained handling, not sketchbook shorthand.

%% GLO_FEMME_LISANT_PIANO.0006 %%
The subject joins the two occupations that fill Marie's own days more than any others besides painting: reading and music. She read voraciously and played the piano, the harp and the guitar; a woman reading at the corner of a piano is very close to a picture of her own drawing-room life.

%% GLO_FEMME_LISANT_PIANO.0007 %%
**A possible catalogue match, offered as a guess only.** The 1885 exhibition catalogue includes among its drawings a *La Lecture* (no. 116), and reproduced four drawings after nature, one of them also called *La lecture*. Whether either is this sheet is unknown — "La Lecture" is a generic title and she drew people reading more than once.

%% GLO_FEMME_LISANT_PIANO.0008 %%
## Making of the Work

%% GLO_FEMME_LISANT_PIANO.0009 %%
Nothing is documented. The drawing is undated by the museum, unmatched in the diary, and unattached to any recorded episode.

%% GLO_FEMME_LISANT_PIANO.0010 %%
%% [#Music_theme](../themes/MUSIC_THEME.md) %%
One related but distinct idea does appear in the diary: on 27 December 1882 Marie sketches out, on going to bed, a picture she wants to make — "un homme de profil et dans l'ombre joue du piano, deux autres l'écoutent" (¶ 097.0289), one listener with his head against the base of a statue, another astride a chair with a cigarette. That is a lamp-lit interior with a man at the keyboard and is **not** this drawing; it is recorded here only because it shows the piano-corner interior was a composition she thought in. She never mentions it again.
