## Overview

%% GLO_FEMME_A_LA_CIGARETTE.0002 %%
%% [#Dessin](../literature/DESSIN.md) [#Art_Practice](../themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
A small black-chalk drawing, 24,5 × 18,5 cm, of a woman in a dark dress and hat sitting square to the viewer in a studio, with a cigarette in her right hand. It is in the French national collections, catalogued by the **Musée d'Orsay** as *Portrait de femme* under **RF 39089**. The Joconde notice gives the fuller descriptive title *Portrait de femme assise, de face, une cigarette dans la main droite*. It is undated.

%% GLO_FEMME_A_LA_CIGARETTE.0003 %%
## The record

%% GLO_FEMME_A_LA_CIGARETTE.0004 %%
According to the Joconde notice the sheet is black chalk (*pierre noire*), pasted onto another sheet, earlier inventory number JdeP 516. It came to the State in **1908** as a gift from the artist's mother, together with *[Intimité](./INTIMITE.md)* (RF 39090), and passed through the Jeu de Paume and the Musée d'Art moderne before reaching the Orsay collections.

%% GLO_FEMME_A_LA_CIGARETTE.0005 %%
**It is the drawing the 1885 catalogue calls *La Cigarette*.** The catalogue lists *La Cigarette* as drawing no. 117, and among its "Quatre dessins d'après nature" it reproduces a plate with that caption. The plate shows the same composition as the Orsay sheet: the seated woman in a hat, the canvases stacked behind her, the cigarette held low in the right hand (Gallica views 45–48). The title used here is therefore Marie's own exhibition title.
