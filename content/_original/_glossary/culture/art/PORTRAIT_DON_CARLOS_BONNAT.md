## Overview

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_DON_CARLOS_BONNAT.0002 %%
A portrait of the Carlist pretender [Don Carlos](../../people/mentioned/DON_CARLOS.md) (Carlos de Borbón, Duke of Madrid, 1848-1909) by [Léon Bonnat](../../people/mentioned/BONNAT.md), seen by Marie at the Exposition Universelle of 1878 in Paris. Marie is the only source found for it: no catalogue entry, image or present location could be traced in the sources searched (Wikimedia Commons, museum and auction databases, the Bonnat inventory). It is therefore known only from her one paragraph.

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_DON_CARLOS_BONNAT.0003 %%
## Marie and this painting

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_DON_CARLOS_BONNAT.0004 %%
On **5 May 1878** Marie goes back to the Exposition with Anna Nordgren and passes over everything «excepté les tableaux qui nous intéressaient seuls». The first picture she names is this one: «J'ai été très surprise du portrait de don Carlos par Bonnat. Mal dessiné, faux de ton et peu de ressemblance.» (080.0011). The next paragraph turns to Bonnat's famous portrait of Thiers, and she concludes: «C'est Carolus-Duran que j'aime le mieux comme vie et Bonnat comme savoir.» (080.0012). She knows the pretender's looks well from the meeting of March 1878 and from his portrait she says she is «amoureuse» of (068.0357), so her verdict on the likeness comes from knowing the face.

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_DON_CARLOS_BONNAT.0008 %%
The identification of the sitter with Carlos de Borbón follows the Don Carlos entry (Mon Journal indexes); the painting itself is untraced. It may have been shown in the French or the Spanish section of the 1878 fine-arts display, or among the portraits of princes, but this is not established.
