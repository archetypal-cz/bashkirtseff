## Overview

%% GLO_UNE_LARME.0002 %%
%% [#Portrait](./PORTRAIT.md) [#Art_Practice](../themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
*Une larme* ("A Tear") is a small oil on canvas, 40 × 32 cm, monogrammed **M.B.** at upper left, in the **Musée des Beaux-Arts Jules Chéret** in Nice under inventory **N.Mba 1801**. The museum's Joconde notice indexes its subject as a self-portrait: Marie in profile, in sorrow. The notice gives no date beyond the last quarter of the nineteenth century.

%% GLO_UNE_LARME.0003 %%
## History

%% GLO_UNE_LARME.0004 %%
The notice records it as entering the museum in **1909**, a gift by hand (*don manuel*), with "Collection privée, Bashkirtseff Marie" as its previous owner. The family's gifts to Nice ran from 1909 to 1920; the *[Autoportrait à la palette](./AUTOPORTRAIT_A_LA_PALETTE.md)* came in 1920.

%% GLO_UNE_LARME.0005 %%
**A possible catalogue match.** The 1885 memorial catalogue lists a *Profil* (no. 10) measuring 0,40 × 0,32 m, exactly the size of this canvas. A head in profile of the right size is suggestive, but a title like that could fit several studies, so the identification is not established.
