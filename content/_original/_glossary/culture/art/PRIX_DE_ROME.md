# Prix de Rome

## Overview
The Prix de Rome, the state prize of the Académie des Beaux-Arts that sent the winners to the Villa Medici in Rome; closed to women until 1903. From October 1877 Marie dreams of being «prix de Rome» (075.0131, 075.0172, 075.0295, 076.0238); she visits the competition entries at the École des Beaux-Arts (075.0375, 085.0578); Bastien-Lepage's second prize of 1875 is listed in 103.0702.
