## Overview

%% GLO_LE_PERE_JACQUES.0002 %%
*Le Père Jacques* (*The Wood Gatherer*) is a life-size picture by [Jules Bastien-Lepage](../../people/recurring/BASTIEN_LEPAGE.md): an old man bent under a great load of firewood trudges through an autumn wood, while a little girl in front of him stops to pick small flowers. Dated 1881, it was shown at the [Salon](./SALON.md) of 1882 and is in the **Milwaukee Art Museum** (Layton Art Collection, accession L102; about 197 × 182 cm). (Sources: Wikipedia, « Le Père Jacques (The Wood Gatherer) »; Milwaukee Art Museum, collection record and blog.)

%% GLO_LE_PERE_JACQUES.0003 %%
## Marie and this painting

%% GLO_LE_PERE_JACQUES.0004 %%
Marie mentions it from September 1882. On **14 September 1882** Rodolphe Julian, looking at her unfinished *Pêcheur*, says that Bastien-Lepage's *Père Jacques* « était admirablement fait mais que ça ne signifiait pas grand chose » whereas her fisherman is a true type (096.0239). On 27 April 1883 she writes that it is « la symphonie merveilleuse de la forêt en automne » and that the little girl picking flowers is a masterpiece (099.0229); on 3 May 1883 she lists it with the earlier pictures that lacked the heaviness of *L'Amour au village* (099.0335) and on 15 June it appears among his works (100.0165). On **28 October 1883**, planning her own forest picture with flaming autumn leaves, she recalls that in the *Père Jacques* « la forêt était à ce qui m'en souvient trop avancée, c'était dépouillé et un peu gris » (102.0077).

%% GLO_LE_PERE_JACQUES.0005 %%
Beware of the name: Jacques is also the boy of Marie's own [Jean et Jacques](./JEAN_ET_JACQUES.md).
