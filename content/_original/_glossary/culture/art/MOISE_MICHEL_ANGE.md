## Overview

%% GLO_MOISE_MICHEL_ANGE.0002 %%
The marble *Moses* (c. 1513-1515) by [Michelangelo](../../people/mentioned/MICHEL_ANGE.md), the central figure of the tomb of Pope Julius II in San Pietro in Vincoli, Rome (dating as in the [Michelangelo](../../people/mentioned/MICHEL_ANGE.md) entry): a seated, bearded Moses holding the tablets of the Law. It stands in San Pietro in Vincoli, as the reader's note to 105.0199 records.

%% GLO_MOISE_MICHEL_ANGE.0003 %%
## Marie and this statue

%% GLO_MOISE_MICHEL_ANGE.0004 %%
On 15 July 1884 she cites it as the example of a figure that does not move but lives: «Voyez le Moïse de Michel-Ange. Il est immobile mais il est vivant» (105.0199). It is paired in the same paragraph with the *Penseur* of the Medici Chapel ([Il Pensieroso](./IL_PENSIEROSO_MICHEL_ANGE.md)).
