## Overview

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_DE_JEUNE_FILLE_SARGENT.0002 %%
A portrait of a young woman by [John Singer Sargent](../../people/artists/JOHN_SINGER_SARGENT.md) (1856-1925) that Marie saw at the [Salon](./SALON.md) of 1882. She names only the type, «le portrait de jeune fille par Sargent». The most likely picture is *Lady with the Rose (Charlotte Louise Burckhardt)*, 1882, oil on canvas, 213.4 × 113.7 cm, now in the Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York (accession 32.154), which Sargent showed at the 1882 Salon, where it was much admired (Metropolitan Museum of Art; Wikipedia, «Lady with the Rose (Charlotte Louise Burckhardt)»).

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_DE_JEUNE_FILLE_SARGENT.0003 %%
## Marie and this painting

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_DE_JEUNE_FILLE_SARGENT.0004 %%
On **9 May 1882**, after the Salon, one picture haunts her: «C'est le portrait de jeune fille par Sargent, qui me hante, il est ravissant, c'est une œuvre exquise qu'on placerait volontiers dans un musée avec des van Dyck et des Vélasquez.» (095.0036). She gives no title, no sitter, and no description beyond «jeune fille». In May 1883 she dismisses the painter: «Sargent est médiocre» (099.0308, see the Sargent entry).

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_DE_JEUNE_FILLE_SARGENT.0008 %%
The identification with *Lady with the Rose* is probable, not certain: Marie does not title the picture, and the Salon livret of 1882 should be checked for Sargent's entries. Paragraph 103.0551 (March 1884) is not about it: there Marie lists beauties of the day, including Mme Gautreau (the sitter of Sargent's *Madame X*, painted 1883-84 and shown at the Salon of 1884), and the footnote there mentions Sargent's portrait only to identify the beauty. Marie's own comments on *Madame X* are at 104.0070 and 104.0542 (see the Sargent entry).
