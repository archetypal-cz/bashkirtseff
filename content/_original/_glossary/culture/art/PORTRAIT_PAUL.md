## Overview

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_PAUL.0002 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) [#Paul](../../people/mentioned/PAUL.md) %%
*Portrait de son frère Paul* is Marie's portrait of her brother **Paul Bashkirtseff**, dated **1876** and held by the **Musée des Beaux-Arts Jules Chéret in Nice**. If the 1876 date is right, it is one of the earliest surviving works by her hand — painted when she was seventeen or eighteen, more than a year before she entered the Académie Julian in October 1877.

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_PAUL.0003 %%
The 1885 posthumous exhibition catalogue lists a *Portrait de son frère*, 0,65 × 0,54 m, as no. 53. Whether that is this picture is likely but not established.

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_PAUL.0004 %%
## Description

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_PAUL.0005 %%
Oil; dimensions not published by the museum. The best available reproduction is a **821 × 1000 px** file on Wikimedia Commons — adequate but not museum-grade. As with the *Autoportrait à la palette*, the limiting factor is that the Musée des Beaux-Arts de Nice, which holds the largest surviving group of Bashkirtseff's work, has digitised almost none of it.

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_PAUL.0006 %%
## Making of the Work

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_PAUL.0007 %%
**The diary does not corroborate the 1876 date, and this should be said plainly.** A full sweep of carnets 060–075 — which covers May 1876 to 31 October 1877, exactly the period in question — found **no mention anywhere of Marie painting or drawing her brother**. Paul appears constantly in carnet 066 (Poltava, September 1876), but the material there concerns a quarrel over a photograph album (¶¶ 066.0109–066.0118), not painting.

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_PAUL.0008 %%
That silence is worth weighing. In these years Marie records her drawing and painting in obsessive detail — the Collignon portrait, the caricatures of Melissano, the portrait of her grandmother, the card players, the *Mirage* cartoon — so a finished oil portrait of her own brother going unmentioned is unexpected. Either the museum's date is wrong, or the picture belongs to a stretch of days the diary passes over, or it was made somewhere the diary does not follow her.

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_PAUL.0009 %%
The one direct diary reference to a portrait of a Paul comes three years later: **17 February 1879**, "L'après-midi je fais le portrait de Paul" (¶ 084.0168) — a bald single line with no follow-up anywhere in carnet 084. **This is not certainly the same sitter**: the paragraph is tagged in the source to `PAUL_FRERE`, and the researcher's own note on it hedges between the brother and a cousin, Paul Babanine. It documents a second, 1879 sitting, not the 1876 canvas.

## References in Diary

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_PAUL.0011 %%
- **No mention of this painting has been found.** Carnets 060–075 (May 1876 – October 1877), the range in which an 1876 portrait would fall, contain nothing about painting Paul.
- **1879-02-17, ¶ 084.0168** — "L'après-midi je fais le portrait de Paul." A separate, later sitting; the sitter's identity is not fully secure.
