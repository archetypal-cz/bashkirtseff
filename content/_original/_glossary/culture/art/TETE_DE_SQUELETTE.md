# Head of the skeleton (planned still life, 1878)

%% GLO_TETE_DE_SQUELETTE.0001 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_TETE_DE_SQUELETTE.0002 %%
%% [#Robert_Fleury](../../people/artists/ROBERT_FLEURY.md) [#Art_practice](../themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
On **23 March 1878**, in Paris, Marie writes that [#Robert_Fleury](../../people/artists/ROBERT_FLEURY.md), under whose "direction toute spéciale" she has placed herself, has ordered her "de faire de temps en temps des natures mortes quelconques, fort simples, pour m'habituer à manier les couleurs". Then: "Je lui ferai la semaine prochaine ou l'autre semaine sur une toile de 3, la tête de mon squelette, avec un livre ou n'importe quoi bien arrangé." (079.0050-0051). The "squelette" is the anatomical skeleton in her room (she bought "des squelettes" in November 1877, 076.0167; later she calls it Amélie, 085.0268). The diary does not say that she did paint it; her first still life that she records as finished is the violets of 12 May ([#Nature_morte_violettes_1878](./NATURE_MORTE_VIOLETTES_1878.md)). **It is a plan; whether it was executed is unknown; no image.**
