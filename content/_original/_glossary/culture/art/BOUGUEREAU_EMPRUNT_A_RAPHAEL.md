## Overview

%% GLO_BOUGUEREAU_EMPRUNT_A_RAPHAEL.0002 %%
An **unidentified picture** by [William Bouguereau](../../people/artists/BOUGUEREAU.md) (1825-1905) which Marie accuses of lifting a composition from [Raphael](../../people/artists/RAPHAEL.md). The diary names neither the picture nor the Raphael: no title, subject or place of exhibition is given, and it has not been possible to find which work she means. No image is given.

%% GLO_BOUGUEREAU_EMPRUNT_A_RAPHAEL.0003 %%
## Marie and the «vol»

%% GLO_BOUGUEREAU_EMPRUNT_A_RAPHAEL.0004 %%
On **18 January 1884**, after a day of tears over her own painting, she writes: «Et ma sainte et profonde indignation en constatant un vol audacieux fait par Bouguereau, au préjudice de Raphaël, fait rire» (103.0067). Her indignation, she says, makes people laugh. She widens the charge into a general indictment of the academic painters: «ce qu'on retrouve d'emprunts, de vols infâmes dans tous nos peintres académiques» (103.0068). They «passent leur vie à transposer les compositions des maîtres. A celui-là un personnage, à tel autre un groupe entier, le tout tourné à droite au lieu d'à gauche et de baptiser et le tour est joué» (103.0069). Her complaint belongs with her contempt for the success of the *Aurore* of Bouguereau and the *Phèdre* of Cabanel two days earlier (103.0046; see [Phèdre](./PHEDRE_CABANEL.md)).

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_BOUGUEREAU_EMPRUNT_A_RAPHAEL.0006 %%
The picture is not identified. It follows a paragraph about her own painting (103.0066), and nothing in the entry shows what she had been looking at. The charge is her opinion; the diary supplies no evidence for it beyond her own indignation.
