## Overview

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_JEUNE_FEMME_1878.0002 %%
%% [#Portrait](./PORTRAIT.md) [#Academie_Julian](../../places/schools/JULIAN.md) %%
A head-and-shoulders study of a dark-haired young woman with a red bow in her hair, oil on canvas (relined), 55 × 46,5 cm. It is monogrammed **M. B.** lower right and dated **"X 78"** upper left, scratched into the wet paint; the "X" is most naturally read as the Roman numeral for October. It was sold at **Villa Grisebach**, Berlin, on 2 December 2022 (auction 347, lot 300), with an estimate of €25,000–35,000, from a private collection in southern Germany.

## Context

%% GLO_PORTRAIT_JEUNE_FEMME_1878.0004 %%
October 1878 is the start of Marie's second year at the [Académie Julian](../../places/schools/JULIAN.md), which she entered on 3 October 1877. Other surviving works of that year include the *[Tête de jeune femme à la plume bleue](./TETE_JEUNE_FEMME_PLUME_BLEUE.md)* in Martigues, 55 × 45 cm, and the *[Caricature de Rodolphe Julian](./CARICATURE_RODOLPHE_JULIAN.md)*. The sitter is not identified, and no diary passage has been tied to the picture.
