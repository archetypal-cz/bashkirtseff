## Overview

%% GLO_SAISON_D_OCTOBRE.0002 %%
*Saison d'octobre* (*October*; also called *Les Ramasseurs de pommes de terre*, the potato harvest) is a large life-size picture by [Jules Bastien-Lepage](../../people/recurring/BASTIEN_LEPAGE.md), painted at Damvillers in 1878 and shown at the [Salon](./SALON.md) of 1879. It is oil on canvas, 180.7 × 196.0 cm, in the **National Gallery of Victoria, Melbourne** (Felton Bequest, 1928). (Sources: NGV collection record, work 3768; the date of the Salon is that given by the project's reader footnote at 102.0310.)

%% GLO_SAISON_D_OCTOBRE.0003 %%
## Marie and this painting

%% GLO_SAISON_D_OCTOBRE.0004 %%
Marie knows it as *les Pommes de terre* or *les Ramasseurs de pommes de terre*. On 15 August 1882, in a moment of bitterness about her own prospects, she writes that she wants something « tellement supérieur aux bûcherons et aux ramasseurs de pommes de terre dont on nous persécute en l'honneur de Bastien » (096.0082). On 27 April 1883, listing the exquisite sentimentality of everything Bastien-Lepage has painted, she allows an exception only for « les pommes de terre et le mendiant... Et encore... » (099.0228). On 3 May 1883 the potatoes are among the earlier canvases whose background did not show the heaviness of *L'Amour au village* (099.0335). On 15 September 1883 she names it with *Les Foins* as the two life-size pictures (101.0363).

%% GLO_SAISON_D_OCTOBRE.0005 %%
On **22 November 1883** she gives her verdict to a correspondent: « Je vous abandonne *Les Foins* si vous voulez mais *Saisons d'octobre*, vulgo les Pommes de terre, est un chef d'œuvre. Pourtant je suis moins empoignée, serait-ce un signe que je deviens plus forte ?... » (102.0310; reader footnotes on both pictures).
