## Overview

%% GLO_DIE_LESENDE.0002 %%
%% [#Dina](../../people/core/DINA.md) [#Reading](../themes/READING.md) [#Portrait](./PORTRAIT.md) %%
An oil on canvas laid on board, 64 × 53 cm, dated **1880**, that came onto the market at the **Dorotheum** in Vienna in 2021 (object ID 7201837) under the title *Die Lesende* ("The Reader"). The auction house identified the sitter as **[Dina Babanina](../../people/core/DINA.md)**, the artist's cousin. A young woman in black sits at a table with a journal open in front of her, one hand pressed to her forehead and a glass of water by her elbow. It is in a private collection.

%% GLO_DIE_LESENDE.0003 %%
## Relation to *At a Book*

%% GLO_DIE_LESENDE.0004 %%
The composition is almost the same as that of *[За книгою (At a Book)](./AT_A_BOOK.md)* in Kharkiv, 63 × 60,5 cm. In both the same fair-haired woman leans over the page with her hand in her hair. They are two distinct canvases: the Vienna picture has a warm olive background and the glass, while the Kharkiv one has a black ground, a bare orange table and no glass. Wikimedia Commons links the two files as versions of each other. The Dorotheum's naming of the sitter as Dina bears on the Ukrainian wall-text tradition that *At a Book* is a self-portrait. Both identifications are on record and neither is documented by the artist.

%% GLO_DIE_LESENDE.0005 %%
The 1885 memorial catalogue lists a *Jeune femme lisant* (no. 4) at 0,60 × 0,67 m, which is near the Kharkiv canvas in size. Which of the two versions it was, if either, cannot be settled from the catalogue.
