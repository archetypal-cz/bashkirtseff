## Legrand

%% GLO_LEGRAND.0002 %%
A shop in Nice where Marie orders hats. On 16 March 1874: «Je vais chez Legrand, je prends le chapeau marron et je m'en fais venir un noir de Paris» (017.0328); the same day she keeps a promise not to look at her hair (017.0330). Mon Journal has no separate index entry found for this name. No first name.
