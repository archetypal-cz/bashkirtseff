# Mellerio dits Meller

## Overview
Paris jewellery house founded in 1613 and still run by the family, at 9 rue de la Paix since the early nineteenth century (Wikipedia, "Mellerio dits Meller", gives 1815 for the move; other sources give 1832). It supplied the court and the aristocracy and won prizes at the Paris Expositions of 1867 and 1889.

## Relevance to Marie
In Paris in July 1876 Marie gives her diamonds to Mellerio to be reset: «J'ai donné mes diamants à Mellerio qui me fera des bijoux à la mode, non, à ma mode» (063.0136, 10 July). On 13 July she notes that a young man followed them «hier depuis Mellerio où nous avons été donner des diamants à monter» (063.0205). The edition's index lists «Mellerio (bijoutier)» (Mon Journal t.8, index, pp.264, 273).
