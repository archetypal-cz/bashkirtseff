## Overview

%% GLO_MRS_HALLIBURTONS_TROUBLES_WOOD.0002 %%
%% [#Mrs_Henry_Wood](../../people/mentioned/MRS_HENRY_WOOD.md) %%
*Mrs. Halliburton's Troubles* is a three-volume novel by the English writer Ellen Wood, who published as Mrs Henry Wood (1814–1887) and was best known for *East Lynne* (1861). It appeared in London in 1862 from Richard Bentley (the Salamanca Corpus transcribes this first edition; Project Gutenberg's copy is a 1904 printing). Marie spells the name with one *l* («Haliburton»); the author's spelling is *Halliburton*.

## Marie and this book

%% GLO_MRS_HALLIBURTONS_TROUBLES_WOOD.0004 %%
%% [#Reading](../themes/READING.md) %%
**20 April 1873, Nice (004.0100).** At fifteen: «Je me suis mise à lire "Mrs Haliburton's troubles" by Mrs Henry Wood. Et j'ai lu les lignes suivantes», after which she copies out a passage of English in her diary, an exhortation to "work on" for an object in life, whether one is "the heir of a Dukedom" or lives by daily toil. She writes the title and author in English inside the French text.

%% GLO_MRS_HALLIBURTONS_TROUBLES_WOOD.0005 %%
**12 July 1873 (006.0245).** The title appears again in her list of English books still to read, between Bulwer's *Last Days of Pompeii* and [Mabel Vaughan](./MABEL_VAUGHAN.md): «"Mrs Haliburton's troubles" by Mrs Henry Wood.» She therefore had started it in April and wanted to carry on or return to it in July.
