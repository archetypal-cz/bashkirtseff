## Overview

%% GLO_BIBLE.0002 %%
The Bible, the sacred scriptures of Christianity (Old and New Testaments) and, in its first part, of Judaism. French Wikisource gives several nineteenth-century French translations: Catholic ones (Lemaistre de Sacy, Glaire, Crampon, Giguet), Protestant ones (Segond, Ostervald, Darby, Martin) and the Bible du Rabbinat (Wikisource, «La Bible»). Marie does not say which version she read in French, and in 1876 she reads it in Italian (064.0597).

%% GLO_BIBLE.0003 %%
## Marie and the Bible

%% GLO_BIBLE.0004 %%
- **April and June 1875.** «Je lis cinq livres à la fois, la Sainte Bible, Napoléon, l'Histoire de Monaco et un roman de Dumas» (031.0240, 15 April 1875). On 10 June, tired and low: «Je vais lire la Bible et puis dormir» (034.0144).
- **27 July 1875.** In the evening they read the Song of Songs of Solomon: «Quel assemblage de mots ! quelle indécence !» (037.0469).
- **May to August 1876.** Wanting the Bible, she has «le Nouveau Testament» at hand but ends up reading Dumas: «Ne trouvant pas le livre saint j'ai lu Dumas, ce n'est pas la même chose» (060.0592, 19 May). On 4 June she and friends turn the pages of the Bible to trace the beginnings of the church (062.0165). On 2 July she says she began her study of history with the Bible, then Herodotus and Livy (062.0842). On 14 August: «Selon mon habitude je vais lire un chapitre de la Bible en italien et puis dormir» (064.0597).
- **23 February 1884.** After a long argument with herself about faith: «Ce n'est pas la lecture de la Bible qui peut faire croire... Ce n'est qu'un document historique ou tout ce qui touche à Dieu est enfantin» (103.0388). And: «Mais le Christ reconnaissait la Bible pourtant ?!» (103.0391).
- **24 February 1884.** «En attendant je me pénètre des écrits de M. Renan, de la Bible, figurez-vous que je ne l'avais jamais lue... C'est un monde...» (103.0399). See [Renan](../../people/mentioned/RENAN.md).

%% GLO_BIBLE.0005 %%
## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_BIBLE.0006 %%
- Her cry of 24 February 1884 that she had «never read» the Bible sits oddly with the readings of 1875 and 1876. The likeliest sense is that she had never read it through, as a whole and critically, as she now does beside Renan.
- Two further tagged passages do not concern reading the book. In 062.0128 (2 June 1876) she tries on a long robe «à la façon des femmes de la Bible», and in 103.0269 (3 February 1884) an opera about John the Baptist evokes «Le désert, le Prophète, la Bible».
