## Overview

%% GLO_MABEL_VAUGHAN.0002 %%
%% [#The_Lamplighter](./THE_LAMPLIGHTER.md) %%
*Mabel Vaughan* (1857) is the second novel of the American writer Maria Susanna Cummins (1827–1866), whose first, [The Lamplighter](./THE_LAMPLIGHTER.md) (1854), had been a best-seller (Wikipedia, «The Lamplighter»; Internet Archive catalogue records for *Mabel Vaughan*, 1857). It was sold as «by the author of "The Lamplighter"», the form in which Marie names it, and a German translation appeared in 1858 (Internet Archive record).

## Marie and this book

%% GLO_MABEL_VAUGHAN.0004 %%
%% [#Reading](../themes/READING.md) %%
**22 May 1873, on the way back from Geneva (005.0023).** After the Americans with whom she travelled leave the train, she writes in English that the book she had brought was useless as far as Lyon, and that she "looked for it, and found that it was lost, but I must buy one to finish it: «Mabel Waughan» by the author of «The Lamplighter»". She therefore had a copy of the novel and was part-way through it. A footnote to the paragraph identifies it as Cummins's *Mabel Vaughan*.

%% GLO_MABEL_VAUGHAN.0005 %%
**12 July 1873, Nice (006.0245).** In the closing pages of carnet 006 she lists English books to read: *The Last Days of Pompeii* by Bulwer, [Mrs Halliburton's Troubles](./MRS_HALLIBURTONS_TROUBLES_WOOD.md), «"Mabel Vaughan" by the author of "the Lamplighter"», and [The Select Works of Oliver Goldsmith](./SELECT_WORKS_OF_GOLDSMITH.md). This time she spells the title correctly.
