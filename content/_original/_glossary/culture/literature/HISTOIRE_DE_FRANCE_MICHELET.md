## Overview

%% GLO_HISTOIRE_DE_FRANCE_MICHELET.0002 %%
%% [#Michelet](../../people/mentioned/MICHELET.md) %%
*Histoire de France* is the vast national history written by the historian Jules Michelet (1798–1874), issued in volumes between 1833 and 1867 (dates as given in the project's works inventory; see Sources). It is one of the foundational texts of the French republican and romantic "national story" and treats the nation almost as a living person. Michelet was a liberal and anticlerical thinker (Wikipedia FR, "Jules Michelet"), which explains the tone Marie objects to below.

## Marie and this work

%% GLO_HISTOIRE_DE_FRANCE_MICHELET.0004 %%
%% [#Michelet](../../people/mentioned/MICHELET.md) [#Reading](../themes/READING.md) %%
On 3 October 1877 (075.0129) Marie has just read "un chapitre de l'Histoire de France de Michelet" and complains that when he speaks of God "il me semble qu'il plaisante, qu'il se moque de ceux qui n'y croient pas"; the remark comes while she is still occupied with her superstitious "sachet" (amulet).

%% GLO_HISTOIRE_DE_FRANCE_MICHELET.0005 %%
%% [#Michelet](../../people/mentioned/MICHELET.md) [#Tite_Live](../../people/mentioned/TITE_LIVE.md) [#Reading](../themes/READING.md) %%
On 23 September 1878 (082.0442) it is part of her self-imposed reading programme: "Quand j'aurai fini Tite-Live je lirai l'Histoire de France de Michelet", and then the Greeks she knows only by hearsay. Her books are still packed in crates. The programme is discussed in the RSR note at that paragraph.

%% GLO_HISTOIRE_DE_FRANCE_MICHELET.0006 %%
Her later reading of Michelet on the Revolution is a different work: see [Histoire de la Révolution française (Michelet)](HISTOIRE_DE_LA_REVOLUTION_MICHELET.md).
