# Don Quichotte

Cervantes's novel, which Marie buys in Nice on 1874-10-09 (024.0274): «J'ai acheté "Les Femmes de la Bible", et les œuvres de Byron et de Shakespeare, et "Don Quichotte" que je n'ai pas encore lu.» She lists it with the other books bought in one round of the shops (with a subscription to the Morning Post). She says she has not yet read it; the diary does not record her reading it in this passage. Not a person.
