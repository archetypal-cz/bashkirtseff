# Les Mirabeau (Louis de Loménie)

%% GLO_LES_MIRABEAU_LOMENIE.0002 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_LES_MIRABEAU_LOMENIE.0003 %%
*Les Mirabeau: nouvelles études sur la société française au XVIIIe siècle* is a family history of the Riqueti de Mirabeau, from the old marquis, the "ami des hommes", to his son the revolutionary orator. It is by Louis de Loménie (1815-1878), scholar and biographer of Beaumarchais, who died before it was finished. Two volumes appeared in 1879 (Paris, E. Dentu); his son Charles de Loménie continued it in three further volumes, 1889-1891 (Wikipedia, "Louis de Loménie"; Internet Archive catalogue records). Jules Claretie dedicated his drama *Les Mirabeau* to Loménie's memory (see [Les Mirabeau (Claretie)](../theater/LES_MIRABEAU_CLARETIE.md)).

## Marie and Les Mirabeau

%% GLO_LES_MIRABEAU_LOMENIE.0005 %%
%% [#Anna_Nordgren](../../people/mentioned/ANNA_NORDGREN.md) %%
- **15 June 1879, Paris.** "J'ai lu quatre cents pages et fini le premier volume de M. de Loménie sur les Mirabeau, tout en posant pour Anna Nordgren" (085.0310). She is amused, and when people speak of the "hôtel Mirabeau" she smiles "comme si je venais de faire la connaissance d'un Mirabeau qui serait quelque chose de moderne et d'intéressant" (085.0311). A few lines on she remarks that the vices of Mirabeau excuse the "vilenies de Popaul" (085.0315), Popaul being her nickname for Cassagnac.

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_LES_MIRABEAU_LOMENIE.0007 %%
After this reading Marie uses "Mirabeau" as a private nickname for [Cassagnac](../../people/recurring/CASSAGNAC.md), the parliamentary orator, from 16 June onwards, a comparison she had announced on 27 May (085.0185). The nickname is not the book. Which of her uses mean the historical Mirabeau and which mean Cassagnac must be decided occurrence by occurrence.
