## Overview

%% GLO_FLAMARANDE.0002 %%
*Flamarande* is a late novel by [George Sand](./GEORGE_SAND.md), published by Michel Lévy frères in 1875 (title page on French Wikisource). It is told in the first person by Charles, who enters the service of the comte de Flamarande as valet de chambre and is drawn into the count's revenge on his young wife and the fate of her son. Its continuation is *Les Deux Frères*, whose narrator is again Charles; Wikisource lists it as (1875) in the Calmann Lévy edition of 1878. The Wikisource page for *Flamarande* opens with the author's note that the story is the work of a half-educated man whose old-fashioned manner of speech she has left unrevised.

## Marie and Flamarande

%% GLO_FLAMARANDE.0004 %%
Marie reads both books in two days, **2 and 3 January 1881** (carnet 090).

%% GLO_FLAMARANDE.0005 %%
- **First reaction (090.0519).** «J'ouvre "Flamarande" de G. Sand et c'est un laquais qui raconte tout le roman. C'est dégoûtant. Les vingt premières lignes me suffisent.» She is a republican, she says, and for that very reason cannot treat valets as equals; it is odious to be always «tripoter avec des domestiques comme cette G. Sand».
- **Reversal (090.0524).** Despite her indignation she keeps reading and calls it «ce que l'auteur a fait de mieux. Les domestiques sont à leur place et le livre est exquis.» The scenes with children make her understand, for the first time, the happiness of having a child, and she thinks of [Cassagnac](../../people/recurring/CASSAGNAC.md). «Mais tout cela n'a duré qu'un instant.»
- **The sequel (090.0526-0527).** Reading fast, she finishes *Flamarande* and goes on to «"Les deux frères" qui terminent ce roman». Next day, 3 January: «J'ai fini hier "Les deux frères" à minuit et demi. C'est gentil mais il ne m'en reste rien. Ô Balzac !»

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_FLAMARANDE.0007 %%
- The entry covers both novels; Marie treats them as one story in two parts.
- The title of the sequel is a very common phrase in French. Matching should rely on the quoted form or the full title and not on bare «les deux frères» (compare 099.0329, which is unrelated).
- Marie's verdict on the 3rd («il ne m'en reste rien») echoes what she says of *Valentine* three months earlier ([Valentine](./VALENTINE_SAND.md), 090.0082).
