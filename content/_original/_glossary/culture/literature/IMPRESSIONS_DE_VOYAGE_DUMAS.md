## Overview

%% GLO_IMPRESSIONS_DE_VOYAGE_DUMAS.0002 %%
%% [#Alexandre_Dumas](./ALEXANDRE_DUMAS.md) %%
*Impressions de voyage* is the general title under which [Alexandre Dumas père](./ALEXANDRE_DUMAS.md) published his travel writing. The series began in the *Revue des Deux Mondes* in 1833 with Swiss pieces (fr.wikisource, *Impressions de voyage*) and grew into a set of books, among them the Italian volumes *Une année à Florence*, *Le Corricolo* (Naples) and *La Villa Palmieri* (1843; Florence), and the Russian journey of 1858-59, *En Russie* (diary footnote at 008.0224; catalogue records for the editions linked above).

## Marie and Dumas's travel books

%% GLO_IMPRESSIONS_DE_VOYAGE_DUMAS.0004 %%
Marie reads Dumas's travel books at three moments, in three different volumes.

%% GLO_IMPRESSIONS_DE_VOYAGE_DUMAS.0005 %%
- **27 August 1873, on the journey from Paris to Nice** (008.0224): "Je lis, n'ayant rien d'autre, *Impression de voyage en Russie* par Dumas. Il y a des absurdités ! Il décrit Pierre le Grand comme un demi-dieu. Voilà un homme dont j'ai été amoureuse !" This is *Impressions de voyage. En Russie*, the only book she has with her.
- **24 September 1876, Gavrontsi** (066.0422): "Je vais retrouver Rome par Dumas", a book she had not read before writing the pages on Rome. The book is not named. Dumas wrote about Naples and Pompeii in *Le Corricolo*, which fits her next sentence ("Pompeï et Naples"), but Rome is not specific to that volume; the identification is not settled.
- **29 May 1877, Nice** (071.0486): "en lisant les voyages d'Alexandre Dumas j'ai trouvé que cet homme d'esprit était du même avis que moi" about the Medici Venus, "deux ans avant de lire la « Villa Palmieri »". The reference is to chapter VII of *La Villa Palmieri*, on the Uffizi, where Dumas says that anyone who does not admire the Medici Venus "jusqu'à l'idolâtrie" is regarded as an atheist or a heretic, and prefers the Apollino to her (fr.wikisource, *La Villa Palmieri*, ch. VII). Marie's own verdict was written in Florence on 15 September 1875 (043.0200) and is quoted in the research note at 071.0486.

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_IMPRESSIONS_DE_VOYAGE_DUMAS.0007 %%
- On 8 May 1877 (071.0181) she spends the day "à causer avec Dumas" but does not name the book, adding the remark that many books are "only signed Alex. Dumas". Her complaint concerns Dumas's collaborators, not these travel books.
- The year of *La Villa Palmieri*, 1843, is given in the project's research note at 071.0486; the editions linked above are later.
