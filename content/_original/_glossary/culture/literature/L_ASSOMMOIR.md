## Overview

%% GLO_L_ASSOMMOIR.0002 %%
*L'Assommoir* is the seventh novel in [Émile Zola](../../people/writers/ZOLA.md)'s twenty-volume *Les Rougon-Macquart*. It was serialised in 1876 and published as a book in 1877. It is a study of alcoholism and poverty in the working-class districts of Paris and was a huge commercial success that established Zola's fame (Wikipedia, «L'Assommoir»). The story follows the laundress Gervaise Macquart, abandoned in Paris by her lover Lantier (same article).

%% GLO_L_ASSOMMOIR.0003 %%
A stage version, a drama in five acts and nine tableaux by William Busnach and Octave Gastineau, opened at the Théâtre de l'Ambigu-Comique on 18 January 1879 (Wikipedia, «William Bertrand Busnach»; BnF data record of the 1879 production). This entry covers both the novel and the play.

## Marie and L'Assommoir

%% GLO_L_ASSOMMOIR.0005 %%
- **The play, 2 February 1879.** «Hier, je suis allée voir "L'Assommoir" et je trouve que c'est très beau» (084.0165, entry of 3 February 1879), a fortnight after the premiere.
- **The novel, 14 December 1879.** With [Berthe](../../people/recurring/BERTHE.md) she leaves «l'Assommoir» and «les Mémoires de Casanova» at a bookbinder's, giving the address of Joseph ([Arnaud de l'Ariège](../../people/recurring/ARNAUD_DE_L_ARIEGE.md)) for the binding, «une folie de gaieté» (086.0660).
- **The novel, February 1881.** «J'ai fini "l'Assommoir" avant-hier, j'en ai été presque malade, tellement saisie par la vérité du livre qu'il me semblait vivre et converser avec ces gens-là.» (091.0056, entry of 3 February 1881, so she finished it on the 1st). Then, in 091.0057: «J'étais indignée de vivre et de manger pendant que ces horreurs se passent autour de moi plus bas... Tout le monde devrait le lire, on serait meilleur... Mais je suis calmée surtout parce que mon action isolée serait imperceptible.»

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_L_ASSOMMOIR.0007 %%
- The book was already in her hands in December 1879 (the binding), so the reading finished in February 1881 may be a second reading or the first completed one. She does not say.
