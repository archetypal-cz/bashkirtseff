## Overview

%% GLO_LOUIS_XIV_ET_SON_SIECLE.0002 %%
%% [#Alexandre_Dumas](./ALEXANDRE_DUMAS.md) [#Louis_XIV](../../people/mentioned/LOUIS_XIV.md) %%
*Louis XIV et son siècle* is a historical narrative by [Alexandre Dumas](./ALEXANDRE_DUMAS.md), published in 1844, shortly after *Les Trois Mousquetaires*. It portrays Louis XIV and his reign from 1643 to 1715, returns to the Fronde, and describes the court, the arts and the literary and social life of the century (French Wikipedia, «Louis XIV et son siècle»). It was reprinted many times in the nineteenth century; the Internet Archive holds scans dated 1844, 1856 and 1874.

## Marie and this book

%% GLO_LOUIS_XIV_ET_SON_SIECLE.0004 %%
%% [#Duke_of_Hamilton](../../people/core/DUKE_OF_HAMILTON.md) [#Buckingham](../history/BUCKINGHAM.md) [#Reading](../themes/READING.md) %%
**2 February 1874, Nice (016.0087).** "Maman m'a acheté encore quelques livres, ce que je lis tout de suite, c'est « Louis XIV et son siècle »." The book lies open before her and she looks at Buckingham, "il est si beau": "Il n'y a rien comme un duc anglais, parisien." She devours his adventures and sees in him a model of her ideal, as stout as the Duke of Hamilton: "Hamilton est un Buckingham moderne !" She is stopped mid-name ("comme... Ham... comme Buckingham") and crosses out *j'adore*. She adds that she still adores Nero.

%% GLO_LOUIS_XIV_ET_SON_SIECLE.0005 %%
%% [#Reading](../themes/READING.md) %%
**16 January 1874 (015.0209).** Two weeks earlier she writes: «Depuis hier j'ai dans la tête "Louis XIV et ses femmes". Je vais le lire.» The same paragraph asks her mother for Houssaye's [Les Courtisanes du monde](./COURTISANES_DU_MONDE.md), and the next lines confess her taste for Houssaye's style and that she "blushes at the name of Louis XIV".

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_LOUIS_XIV_ET_SON_SIECLE.0007 %%
The identification of 016.0087 is secure: Marie gives the title and Dumas published a book of exactly that name. The title in 015.0209, *Louis XIV et ses femmes*, is less certain. A search of the Internet Archive and of French Wikisource found no nineteenth-century book of that title. It may be Marie's own shorthand for the same book, which she received on 2 February as one of the "quelques livres" her mother bought. It may also be a Houssaye volume on Louis XIV's mistresses, since Houssaye was her reading in these weeks (see [Mademoiselle de La Vallière](./MADEMOISELLE_DE_LA_VALLIERE_HOUSSAYE.md), finished 1 February 1874). The earlier project note that attributes it to Houssaye was not backed by a source.
