# Revue de Nice (1875)

%% GLO_REVUE_DE_NICE.0001 %%
## Overview

%% GLO_REVUE_DE_NICE.0002 %%
%% [#Nice](../../places/cities/NICE.md) [#Schlangenbad](../../places/cities/SCHLANGENBAD.md) %%
*Revue de Nice* is a satirical verse sketch of the young men of the Nice promenade, written by Marie Bashkirtseff in August 1875 at [Schlangenbad](../../places/cities/SCHLANGENBAD.md) and copied into the diary on **21 August 1875** (039.0165-0185). It takes its title from the *revue de fin d'année*, the topical, mocking stage review of the period, and goes through the men of her Nice circle one after another in short rhyming stanzas, each with a physical caricature: the plump, red-faced, slightly lame patron of beginners who makes the round of the boxes showing his "main d'albâtre" ([#Andriot_Saetone](../../people/recurring/SAETONE.md)); the thin Arthur Danis with spotless collars ([#Danis](../../people/mentioned/DANIS.md)); the powdered "faquin" Maurice Gros ([#Maurice_Gros](../../people/mentioned/MAURICE_GROS.md)); the count d'Aspremont, tall "comme un mât de cocagne", with a mountain range for a nose ([#Aspremont](../../people/mentioned/ASPREMONT.md)); the badly dressed M. Gautier ([#Albert_Gautier](../../people/mentioned/ALBERT_GAUTIER.md)); the future notary Galula-Déchiar, the royalist count de Laurenti, Maurice Godard-Décrais who "brait comme un âne", and last the "châtelain illustre", whose "doux nom est Emile" ([#Emile_d_Audiffret](../../people/recurring/EMILE_D_AUDIFFRET.md)). One line of the manuscript is struck out ("Une ligne cancellée"). The piece is signed with the mock-classical pen name **"phil. Tryphodore"** (039.0185).

%% GLO_REVUE_DE_NICE.0003 %%
## Marie and this work

%% GLO_REVUE_DE_NICE.0004 %%
The verses are the only place in the diary where Marie writes down a whole satire of her Nice acquaintances, and they belong with the caricature drawings and paintings she made of the same circle that autumn (see [#Grande_caricature_1875](../art/GRANDE_CARICATURE_1875.md)). On **21 September 1875** (043.0366) she copies out new couplets about a ladies' man and notes: "Ceci est destiné à remplacer ce qu'il y a dans la revue de Nice, fait à Schlangenbad": the new lines were meant to replace part of the existing text, so the *Revue* was a piece she kept revising. A *Complainte* about a lost château follows at once (043.0368; see [#Complainte_de_Girofla](./COMPLAINTE_DE_GIROFLA.md)). The inventory note "re-made 043.0366" refers to this replacement.

%% GLO_REVUE_DE_NICE.0005 %%
Whether the *Revue* was ever circulated, sung or read aloud in Nice is not stated in these paragraphs. It has not been published separately; the only text is in the diary.
