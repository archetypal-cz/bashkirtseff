## Overview

%% GLO_VOYAGE_EN_ESPAGNE_GAUTIER.0002 %%
%% [#Theophile_Gautier](../../people/writers/THEOPHILE_GAUTIER.md) %%
*Voyage en Espagne* is the account of a journey through Spain by [Théophile Gautier](../../people/writers/THEOPHILE_GAUTIER.md) (1811-1872). It was first published in 1843 as *Tra los montes* ("across the mountains", as fr.wikipedia glosses the Spanish phrase) and later reissued as *Voyage en Espagne* (the diary's footnote at 096.0378; fr.wikisource lists the Charpentier edition among books of 1843). It follows the traveller through the Spanish cities, among them Burgos, Cordoba, Seville and Granada, and is the model of French Romantic travel writing about Spain.

## Marie and the book

%% GLO_VOYAGE_EN_ESPAGNE_GAUTIER.0004 %%
Marie herself travelled through Spain in the autumn of 1881 (Madrid in October, Granada on 30 October, in carnet 093), so she reads Gautier twice with her own memories.

%% GLO_VOYAGE_EN_ESPAGNE_GAUTIER.0005 %%
- **20 June 1882, Paris** (095.0192): "Ah ! l'Espagne, c'est un volume de Th. Gautier qui est cause de cela... Est-ce possible ! Comment j'ai passé à Tolède, Burgos, Cordoue, Séville, Grenade ! Grenade !... Eh bien c'est de la fièvre. Y retourner !!!" The volume has made her homesick for the places she saw. She does not name the book here; the diary's footnote reads it as *Voyage en Espagne*.
- **14 October 1882, on the train to Russia** (096.0378): her aunt has left her at the frontier and she travels with her brother Paul. "Je fais des croquis aux stations et en chemin je lis *Tra las Montes* de cette façon je revois l'Espagne car c'est une photographie coloriée que le voyage de Gautier. Qu'est-ce que c'est donc qui m'arrête pour aimer tout à fait Th. Gautier ?"

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_VOYAGE_EN_ESPAGNE_GAUTIER.0007 %%
- Marie's "Tra las Montes" is a misspelling of the Spanish *Tras los montes* / *Tra los montes*; both the title and the transliteration vary in the sources.
- Gautier is also the author of *Mademoiselle de Maupin*, which Marie knows; see [#Mademoiselle_de_Maupin_Gautier](./MADEMOISELLE_DE_MAUPIN_GAUTIER.md).
