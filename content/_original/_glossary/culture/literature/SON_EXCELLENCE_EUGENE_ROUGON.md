## Overview

%% GLO_SON_EXCELLENCE_EUGENE_ROUGON.0002 %%
*Son Excellence Eugène Rougon* is the sixth volume of Zola's *Les Rougon-Macquart*, published by G. Charpentier in 1876. In it Zola, in his own words, enters the «coulisses politiques» of the Second Empire; its characters are people close to power, ministers, deputies and senior officials, and the action runs from 1856 to 1861 (Wikipédia, «Son Excellence Eugène Rougon»). Eugène is the eldest son of Pierre and Félicité Rougon. A deputy under the Second Republic, he helps the coup d'état of 2 December 1851 and then enters the Senate; the novel follows his political career and his shift from authoritarian partisan to defender of the liberal policy of Napoleon III (same article). The earlier novels of the cycle had shown his ascent only from a distance.

## Marie and Eugène Rougon

%% GLO_SON_EXCELLENCE_EUGENE_ROUGON.0004 %%
Both mentions come from the weeks in November 1879 when Marie is trying to find a footing in the republican society around Gambetta.

%% GLO_SON_EXCELLENCE_EUGENE_ROUGON.0005 %%
- **15 November 1879 (086.0534).** At a dinner with [Gavini](../../people/mentioned/GAVINI.md), Chaudordy, [Géry](../../people/mentioned/CHARLES_GERY.md) and others she flirts with the older men, talks of Émile de Girardin and politics, «je m'insinue, j'intrigue», and notes: «Gavini et Géry me font comprendre bien des choses d'"Eugène Rougon" de Zola. Une femme qui n'a rien à perdre est une créature puissante et on peut faire bien des choses avec des hommes mûrs...»
- **21 November 1879 (086.0559).** «La société polie et élégante de la République est à faire en ce moment, aussi cela m'intéresse-t-il au dernier degré. Toutes mes facultés sont tendues vers ce point», and goes on: «Cela me fait penser à Oelsnitz sans laquelle j'aurais fait la connaissance de Joseph et de Coquelin et par conséquent de [Gambetta](../../people/politicians/GAMBETTA.md).» Then: «J'ai lu "l'Eugène Rougon" de Zola et... même sans cela.» The sentence breaks off and is left open here.

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_SON_EXCELLENCE_EUGENE_ROUGON.0007 %%
- The title is also the name of the novel's hero. The older glossary entry EUGENE_ROUGON (people/mentioned) is an empty placeholder for the character and holds no information.
- Whether she read the book before or after the dinner of the 15th is not clear; on the 15th she says Gavini and Géry help her understand it, on the 21st that she has read it.
