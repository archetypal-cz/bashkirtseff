## Overview

%% GLO_SELECT_WORKS_OF_GOLDSMITH.0002 %%
Oliver Goldsmith (1728–1774) was an Anglo-Irish writer, author of the novel *The Vicar of Wakefield*, the poems *The Traveller* and *The Deserted Village*, and the comedies *The Good-Natur'd Man* (1768) and *She Stoops to Conquer* (1773). Several publishers issued a *Select Works* of Goldsmith in the nineteenth century, usually with the novel, the poems and the plays; the Internet Archive lists an edition "with the portrait of the author" from 1842 and a later one with the same contents. The diary does not say which edition Marie had, and the entry does not claim any one of them.

## Marie and this book

%% GLO_SELECT_WORKS_OF_GOLDSMITH.0004 %%
%% [#Reading](../themes/READING.md) [#Dina](../../people/core/DINA.md) %%
**22 June 1873, Nice (006.0070).** The family's books are packed at the other villa, so she cannot study properly: «je lis de l'anglais, les œuvres de Oliver Goldsmith, je l'aime beaucoup, ses comédies sont tellement spirituelles !» Her enthusiasm is for the comedies, which is consistent with a volume that contains the plays, but she does not name which of them she read.

%% GLO_SELECT_WORKS_OF_GOLDSMITH.0005 %%
**12 July 1873 (006.0245).** At the end of carnet 006 she lists four English books to read, the last being «"The select works of Oliver Goldsmith"», which is the title of the volume itself (see also [Mabel Vaughan](./MABEL_VAUGHAN.md) and [Mrs. Halliburton's Troubles](./MRS_HALLIBURTONS_TROUBLES_WOOD.md) in the same list). She had been reading it three weeks earlier.
