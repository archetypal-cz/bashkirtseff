# Sapho (Daudet)

## Overview

*Sapho*, novel by Alphonse Daudet ([ALPHONSE_DAUDET](ALPHONSE_DAUDET.md)), published in 1884, the story of a young man's long liaison with a former artists' model, Fanny Legrand (en.wikipedia «Sapho (novel)»). Not Gounod's opera *Sapho* ([SAPHO_OPERA](../theater/SAPHO_OPERA.md)).
