## Overview

%% GLO_VALENTINE_SAND.0002 %%
*Valentine* is a novel by [George Sand](./GEORGE_SAND.md), published in Paris by H. Dupuy in 1832. It is counted among her "feminist" novels and is a country love story set in the Vallée noire, the Berry around Sand's own estate of Nohant (Wikipédia, «Valentine (roman)»). The orphaned peasant Bénédict, raised by his uncle, the rich farmer Lhéry, has been given an education above his station. Three young women love him: his cousin Athénaïs, Lhéry's daughter, and the two daughters of the local lord, the comte de Raimbault, Louise and Valentine (same article). In the text itself Valentine is married to the comte de Lansac (Project Gutenberg #17251).

%% GLO_VALENTINE_SAND.0003 %%
Victor Hugo, in a letter to Sand of 19 June 1875, called her "la première de toutes les femmes" as a creator of masterpieces and wrote of *Valentine* with emotion (Wikipédia, «Valentine (roman)», quoting the letter).

## Marie and Valentine

%% GLO_VALENTINE_SAND.0005 %%
Marie names the novel twice on **9 October 1880** (carnet 090), in a long passage where she argues with herself about George Sand, whom she writes «Georges Sand».

%% GLO_VALENTINE_SAND.0006 %%
- **090.0079.** Balzac, the two Dumas, Zola, Daudet and Musset never bore her, but Sand does: «Comment peut-on lire trois cents pages de "Valentine" et de "Bénédict" accompagnés d'un oncle, d'un jardinier, que sais-je !»
- **090.0082.** «"Valentine", je la lis en ce moment et cela m'agace parce que le livre m'intéresse assez pour que je le finisse et en même temps je sais qu'il ne m'en restera rien que peut-être une vague impression désagréable, il me semble que cette lecture me rabaisse». It would take a book as dreary as Sand's *Le Dernier Amour* to make her give up, and she concludes: «Pourtant "Valentine" est ce que j'ai lu de mieux de Georges Sand.» She adds that [*Le Marquis de Villemer*](../theater/LE_MARQUIS_DE_VILLEMER.md), which she knows only from the stage, must be good too.

%% GLO_VALENTINE_SAND.0007 %%
The two paragraphs in between (090.0080-0081) go on about Sand's plots («Toujours le nivellement social par l'amour, ce qui est ignoble», «La comtesse amoureuse de son valet») but name no title, so they are not tied to *Valentine* here.

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_VALENTINE_SAND.0009 %%
- **Bénédict.** In the novel Bénédict is the hero (the name runs through the whole text on Project Gutenberg), and the uncle (Lhéry) and a gardener appear in it. Marie's «"Valentine" et de "Bénédict"» may therefore describe one book by its two protagonists. A separate Sand novel called *Bénédict* has not been found.
- **Edition.** Marie does not say which edition she read.
- **Other title in the passage.** *Le Dernier Amour* (090.0082) is a second Sand novel and has no entry yet.
- **Name clash.** The older person entry VALENTINE (people/mentioned) is retired and is unrelated to this novel.
