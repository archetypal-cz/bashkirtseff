## Overview

%% GLO_NAPOLEON_BY_DUMAS.0002 %%
%% [#Alexandre_Dumas](./ALEXANDRE_DUMAS.md) %%
*Napoléon* is a biography of Napoleon Bonaparte by [Alexandre Dumas](./ALEXANDRE_DUMAS.md), published in 1840 (Internet Archive records; fr.wikisource page «Napoléon (Dumas)»). The 1840 printing was illustrated with portraits after Horace Vernet and others, and the text was reprinted in the following decades (the Internet Archive holds printings of 1841, 1859 and 1866). It is a history written for a wide public, not one of Dumas's novels.

## Marie and this book

%% GLO_NAPOLEON_BY_DUMAS.0004 %%
%% [#Reading](../themes/READING.md) [#Grand_papa](../../people/family/GRAND_PAPA.md) %%
**8 April 1875, Nice (031.0131).** «Le soir je lis Napoléon de Dumas à papa.» The paragraph says only this: she reads the book aloud to her father in the evening. The same entry's RSR note on 031.0140 records that in this period she weeps over Alexander, Napoleon, Nero and Alcibiades.

%% GLO_NAPOLEON_BY_DUMAS.0005 %%
**15 April 1875 (031.0240).** «Je lis cinq livres à la fois, la Sainte Bible, Napoléon, l'Histoire de Monaco et un roman de Dumas.» Here *Napoléon* is probably the same Dumas biography, which she was still reading a week later; the last item, an unnamed Dumas novel, is a separate book. She names only four of the five books.

## Notes and doubts

%% GLO_NAPOLEON_BY_DUMAS.0007 %%
The paragraph 031.0131 carries the tag for "Grand-papa" although Marie writes "papa", her father; this looks like a mis-tag. The title in 031.0240 is bare («Napoléon»), so it is an inference, not a certainty, that it is the Dumas volume. The diary does not say which printing she read.
