# Ministère des Affaires étrangères

## Overview
The French Ministry of Foreign Affairs, quai d'Orsay, Paris: its official receptions and balls of 1878–1880 (080.0193, 080.0413–0434, 085.0179 «On reçoit tous les lundis, aux Affaires Etrangères», 085.0264, 087.0540), Gambetta as foreign minister (093.0368), and the young diplomats attached to it (091.0342, 099.0171).
