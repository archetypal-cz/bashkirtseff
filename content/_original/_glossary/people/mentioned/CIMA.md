# Cima

Marie Sapogenikoff's young suitor from Geneva, 1875 (see Identity below).

## Identity

M. Cima (Mon Journal tome 4 index: "Cima (M.)"), "celui de Genève": a young man the Sapogenikoffs knew at Geneva, in love with Marie Sapogenikoff and talked of as her future husband in 1875 (029.0014, 22 January 1875: "elle est amoureuse de Cima ... et on va les marier"; 030.0179, 11 March 1875, "le mariage Cendrillon"). Kernberger (2013, glossary) has "Cima: boy who lives with the Sapogenikoffs". First name not found.
