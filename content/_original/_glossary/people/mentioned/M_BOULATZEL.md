# M Boulatzel

## Overview

M. Boulatzel, a very small man whom the Sapogenikoffs introduce to Marie in St Petersburg in August 1876 (Mon Journal t.8 index: «Boulatzel (M.) : 360»).

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Spelling Boulatzel as in the index; nothing further found.

## References in Diary

On 10 August 1876 she is proud of her height among the tiny Sapogenikoffs «et d'un M. Boulatzel, qu'elles m'ont présenté et qui est un tout petit homme» (064.0516); at supper he declares he knows no happier character than hers (064.0518).
