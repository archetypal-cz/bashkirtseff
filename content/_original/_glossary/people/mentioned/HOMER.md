## Homer

%% GLO_HOMER.0002 %%
The Greek epic poet. Marie writes of the Sapogenikoff household that its life is «une Iliade plus terrible et sanglante que celle d'Homère» (032.0203, 30 April 1875). Only mention; the *Iliad* is invoked as a figure of speech for a bloody quarrel.
