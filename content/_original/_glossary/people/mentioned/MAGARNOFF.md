## Magarnoff

%% GLO_MAGARNOFF.0002 %%
Family of the Nice wedding of 10 May 1874: «la noce de Mlle Magarnoff, la sœur du fiddler» (019.0169). Marie, in travel dress as a protest, attends because she is taken by force. The bride's brother is a violinist, «the fiddler». No other source found.
