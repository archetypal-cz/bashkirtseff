## Macainne family

%% GLO_MACAINNE_FAMILY.0002 %%
The English (Scottish) family Macainne at Spa in 1874: «cette famille anglaise Macainne, le frère fait l'admiration de Mme Basilévitch» (020.0419, 28 June). The brother is the officer (MACAINNE); Mon Journal indexes his sisters separately, t.4 pp. 79, 241 and t.6 pp. 159, 339. Only the brother and one tall fair sister are described. A candidate for merging into MACAINNE.
