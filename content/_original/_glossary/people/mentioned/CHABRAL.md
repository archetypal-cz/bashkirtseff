# Chabral

## Overview

An architect, «Chabral, architecte du théâtre Français» (093.0043); Mon Journal t.14 index: «Chabral (M.) architecte : 198». Marie's spelling is probably of Chabrol.

## Relevance to Marie

On 1 September 1881 she tells that Saint Amand dined with them two days before, having left Dieppe that morning: «Il va écrire à Chabral, architecte du théâtre Français. Car je suis décidée à bâtir... C'est un désir ardent d'être enfin chez moi.» The lawsuit will not end for three or four months and nothing can be committed or sold until then (093.0043).

## Identity uncertain

We are not sure who this is. Pierre Prosper Chabrol (born Limoges 1 February 1812, died at the Palais-Royal 9 March 1875) was the architect of the Palais-Royal and rebuilt the Théâtre-Français hall in 1860, but he died six years before this paragraph; his son Wilbrod Chabrol (Paris, 1835 – Le Vésinet, 2 August 1919), Prix de Rome 1862, is the likelier candidate in 1881, though nothing found confirms that he held the Théâtre-Français post (French Wikipedia, «Pierre Prosper Chabrol», «Wilbrod Chabrol»; English Wikipedia, «Salle Richelieu»). If you can identify him, we would be glad to hear from you.
