# Mme Hamsley

**Research Status**: Basic
**Last Updated**: 2025-07-06
**Diary Coverage**: Up to 1874-03-21

## Identity
Woman in Marie's social circle who went on carriage ride with Machenka and a violin-playing gentleman.

## Diary References

### 1874
- **March 21**: Machenka tells Marie about going in open carriage with Mme Hamsley and a gentleman who played violin throughout 4-hour journey.

## Uncle Étienne's companion (1880–1882)

The Mon Journal t.14 index identifies her as «Hamsley (Mme), maîtresse de Babanine Etienne : 73, 102, 297, 306-307», i.e. the companion of Marie's maternal uncle Étienne (Stepan) Babanine. In May 1880 Marie writes: «Après quinze ans de mariage il a lâché sa femme pour Mme Hamsley avec laquelle il voyage» (088.0386). In January 1881 the uncle arrives in Paris «de Nice où il s'est installé avec Mme Hamsley» (091.0020). On 21 March 1881 his wife Marie (Machenka) reaches Paris «après avoir passé un mois à Nice où son volage époux se cachait d'elle avec la Hamsley» (091.0333). In Nice in March 1882 Mme Hamsley is present, «très pâle», when the family discusses uncle Georges's death (094.0319), and in April «maman et ma tante se voient avec la Hamsley»; she and Étienne board the Bashkirtseffs' compartment (094.0400). Whether she is the Mme Hamsley of Machenka's carriage ride in Nice in March 1874 (above) is not stated, but Machenka's acquaintance with her then makes it likely (0.7).
