# Mme Muliner

## Overview

Mme Muliner, wife of M. Muliner, «un homme des tropiques», and friend of Mme Moreno (Mon Journal t.3 index: «Muliner (M.), 233-234», «Muliner (Mme), 234»). Not to be confused with Moliner, a Spaniard met at Longchamp in 1880 ([MOLINER](MOLINER.md)).

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Duplicate of the husband's entry MULINER only in the sense of one family; kept separate, both from the t.3 index.

## References in Diary

At Spa on 14 June 1874 Marie notes: «Ce Muliner est son grand ami, et Mme Muliner est une grande amie à Mme Moreno. Encore une famille de singes» (020.0265).
