# Enoteas

Marie's nickname for Alphonse Andriot, called Saëtone: «Enoteas» is «Saëtone» spelled backwards. The Mon Journal t.5 index gives «Saëtone (Andriot Alphonse), [Enoteas] [Oncle] [Mercure]» and, under the nickname, «Enoteas: Saëtone Andriot (Alphonse)»; the diary says it itself: «j'appelle Saëtone Enoteas» (034.0182). It belongs with her backwards-spelling code names («Enoteas et Terffidua» = Saëtone and Audiffret, RSR note on 027/1874-12-18).

The tagged paragraph here is 1875-12-01 (050.0371, Paris): «A la sortie je ne vois que Désiré et Enoteas.» (Désiré = Maurice Godard, per the t.5 index.) The full account of the man is in [#Saëtone](../recurring/SAETONE.md); this entry should be merged into it (see the merge list).
