# Mme Ketly

## Overview

Mme Ketly, wife of a M. Tchernichoff, said to have poisoned his first wife; mentioned by Marie as the lady he married. Not in the Mon Journal t.4 index under this spelling.

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Spelling Ketly unconfirmed (t.4 index not matching); the rumour is hearsay. Do not read it as fact.

## Identity uncertain

We are not sure who this is. Marie mentions Mme Ketly only as the woman whom a Tchernichoff (not the doctor of that name) married, in a sentence repeating a rumour of poisoning (024.0122). We could not find the name in the Mon Journal indexes. If you can identify her, we would be glad to hear from you.

## References in Diary

On 27 September 1874, going to church, Marie meets «M. Tchernichoff, celui qui était dernièrement à Nice; celui qui a épousé Mme Ketly, qui dit-on, a empoisonné sa première femme, pas le docteur Tchernichoff enfin» (024.0122). The sentence is ambiguous: the rumour of poisoning attaches either to Mme Ketly or to Tchernichoff's late first wife's death. Marie finds him agreeable but of «si mauvaises manières».
