# The Hungarian

## Overview
A man known to Marie only as «le Hongrois», whom she meets on her walks at Nice in October 1874; she does not name him. He is not in the Mon Journal indexes.

## Relevance to Marie
On 7 October 1874 at Nice, after a walk with Gioia the monkey and Walitsky: «Je n'ai rencontré que le Hongrois que j'ai vu hier, Walitsky le voit à la roulette. D'ailleurs ici il n'y a encore personne» (024.0255). So he is a man Walitsky has seen at the roulette table ([WALITSKY](../recurring/WALITSKY.md)). Her monkey is called [GIOIA](../recurring/GIOIA.md) after the Duke of Hamilton's mistress.

## Identity uncertain
He is anonymous; the line is a passing note of the early season. If you can identify him, we would be glad to hear from you.
