# Miss Hungerford

## Overview

A racehorse, not a woman: Miss Hungerford ran in the big steeplechase at Auteuil on 26 May 1874, in the rain, with President MacMahon and the duchesse de Magenta in their stands. Twenty-one horses were to take three rounds, with twenty-one obstacles and four rivers.
