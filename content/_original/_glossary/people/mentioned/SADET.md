# Sadet

## Overview
A dressmaker in Kharkov, autumn 1876. The Mon Journal t.9 index has «Sadet (couturière)». Nothing more is known of her.

## Relevance to Marie
On 26 October 1876, straight from the station in Kharkov, Marie goes «chez Sadet»; when the dressmaker makes difficulties, Marie turns the shop upside down and gives her a Laferrière dress to copy, wanting something warm for the journey (067.0322). On 1 November she walks in Poltava «en robe grise de Sadet» (067.0485).
