# The Tower of London

## Overview
The fortress and former royal palace on the north bank of the Thames, founded by William the Conqueror after 1066, with the White Tower dated to about 1078 (Historic Royal Palaces; en.wikipedia «Tower of London»). The entry is filed among the people but names a building.

## Relevance to Marie
On 7 September 1874, her first day in London: «On nous mène à la Tour de Londres que nous visitons et dont il est inutile que je parle, je dirai pourtant que j'ai trouvé tout presque mieux que je ne pensais, généralement je trouve moins bien» (023.0578). Later the same day: «visité la Tour, passé par the City» (023.0593; see [THE_CITY](THE_CITY.md)). See [ST_PAULS_CATHEDRAL](ST_PAULS_CATHEDRAL.md) for the next day's sights.
