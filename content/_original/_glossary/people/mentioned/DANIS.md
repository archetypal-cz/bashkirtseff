# Danis

## Description
A young man of Nice in Audiffret's set. Kernberger (2013, glossary) gives "Danis, Arthur: associate of Audiffret, acquaintance in Nice". On 2 January 1875 Galula tells Marie's aunt who "ce monsieur" is: "C'est M. Danis, un jeune homme de Nice", and Marie comments "Ce n'est que M. Danis, mais il n'est pas marié" (028/1875-01-02, 028.0122-0123). On 3 February 1875 he is among the men at the Carnival committee rooms where Marie buys tickets (029/1875-02-03). In July 1875 an invitation to the bal champêtre for the flood victims comes "signée président Danis" (035.0167), and Saëtone, Galula and Audiffret promise to present him to her (035.0168).
