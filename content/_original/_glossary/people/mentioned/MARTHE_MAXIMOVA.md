# Marthe Maximova

## Overview

Marie's nurse (*bonne*) in childhood, who taught her Russian nursery card games. The Mon Journal t.3 index has «Maximova (Marthe), 197».

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Surname form as printed in Mon Journal t.3 index p.197; nothing further found.

## References in Diary

On 19 May 1874, playing at *rois*, *idiots* and *mes atouts*, Marie calls them «jeux que m'enseigna *Marthe Maximova*, ma bonne, et qui est mon favori» (019.0289). This is her only appearance.
