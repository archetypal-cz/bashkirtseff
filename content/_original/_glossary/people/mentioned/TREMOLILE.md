# Princesse de la Trémoille (Tremolile)

## Overview

The mother of the baron de Wykerslooth, husband of Jeanne de Ballore (see [JEANNE_DE_BALLORE](JEANNE_DE_BALLORE.md)); Marie names her «la princesse de la Tremoille» (067/1876-12-07, 067.1454: «Jeanne est pour le moment avec son effrayant mari et sa belle-mère la princesse de la Tremoille»), «la princesse de la Tremolile» (085/1879-05-18, 085.0094) and «la princesse de la Tremoille» (094/1882-02-14, 094.0262). Mon Journal t.9 index: «Tremolile (princesse de la) : 214» and t.14: «Tremoille (princesse, née Wykerslooth) : 288». The family name is La Trémoille, one of the oldest French noble houses; which princess is meant, and how she is the mother of a baron Wykerslooth (the t.14 index calls her «née Wykerslooth»), is not established by the diary or the indexes (a search found no confirmation of a La Trémoille princess born Wykerslooth). Treat the identification as open.
