# Nadaud

## Overview

Gustave Nadaud (1820–1893), French chansonnier, author-composer of sentimental and comic songs (*Les Deux Gendarmes*, *La Maison blanche*); Légion d'honneur 1861 (footnote 020.0480.1, citing Wikipedia «Gustave Nadaud»). The Mon Journal indexes list him at t.3 pp.254–255 and t.15 p.22 («Nadaud (M.); chanteur»).

## References in Diary

On 1 July 1874 at Spa, the Bashkirtseffs go to Gambart's musical soirée where Nadaud sings (020.0480, 020.0487: «Nadaud a raconté, une Anglaise a hurlé»). In April 1879 Marie, alone, sings «La Maison blanche» by Nadaud (084.0671). On 8 June 1882, at Marie's own soirée in the studio, Nadaud performs three or four songs at the beginning and the baritone Maurel at the end (095.0142).
