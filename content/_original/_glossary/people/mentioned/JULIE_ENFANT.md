## Julie, a little girl of Marie's Nice circle (1875)

%% GLO_JULIE_ENFANT.0002 %%
A girl of six in February 1875, sister of the boy Stiopa ([#Stiopa](STIOPA_ENFANT.md)), taken out by Marie with Nadia and Nadinka. Not Julie Howard (1874-12-18) nor Julie Cornelius.
