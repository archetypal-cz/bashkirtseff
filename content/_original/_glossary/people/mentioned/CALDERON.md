# Calderon (Claro Calderon)

## Overview

A young Spaniard of Marie's Nice circle in 1875, "le petit Espagnol noir" (039.0139). Mon Journal t.5 index: «Calderon (Claro) [Colorado Claro, marquis], Espagnol, 17, 54, 253», so his given name may be Claro; the bracket «[Colorado Claro, marquis]» is the editors' note and is not explained. No later trace.

## Relevance to Marie

- **7 May 1875.** In the fancy-dress pageant Marie plans for herself and her friends: «Calderon marmiton» (kitchen boy), with Audiffret and Gericke as Dina's guards, Collignon governess, Barnola governor of the Dauphin (032.0331).
- **19 August 1875.** At Schlangenbad she sees «la demoiselle que je nommais à Nice Madame *Obeziana* [singe] et son frère, un petit qui était souvent avec Calderon, le petit Espagnol noir» (039.0139).
