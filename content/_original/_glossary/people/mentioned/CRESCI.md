# Cresci

## Identification (2026-10-21)
Italian impresario and singing teacher in Nice. Marie calls him «Cresci le directeur de l'Opéra» (044, 1875-09-22), i.e. the Italian opera of Nice, in the season 1875–76; Audiffret introduces them and wants him to give her lessons. In June 1876 he is her singing teacher: «Ce matin j'ai pris la première leçon avec Cresci» (062, 1876-06-17). The edition's index calls him «Cresci (professeur de chant)» (Mon Journal t.8, index). First name not found.
