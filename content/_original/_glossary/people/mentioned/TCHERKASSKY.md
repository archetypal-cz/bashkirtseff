# Tcherkassky

The Tcherkasskys' departure from Schlangenbad on 26 August 1875: «Les Tcherkassky sont partis, d'abord pour Wiesbaden puis pour Pétersbourg. Ils sont partis de chez nous» (039.0260). This is the same couple as [PRINCE_TCHERKASSKY](../aristocracy/PRINCE_TCHERKASSKY.md) and [PRINCESSE_TCHERKASSKY](../aristocracy/PRINCESSE_TCHERKASSKY.md), who spent the preceding two weeks with Marie's mother (the same days, the same place, and the Mon Journal t.5 index lists p.261 under both «Tcherkassy (prince)» and «Tcherkassy (princesse)»). The earlier owner ruling (3 October 2026) kept this entry separate from the other spellings; the evidence here suggests a merge (see the list).
