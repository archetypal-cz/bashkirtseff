# The Randouins (M. Randouin-Berthier and family)

## Overview
The household of M. Randouin (Alexandre Randouin-Berthier in the Mon Journal indexes), his wife Odette and their children: a fixture of Nice society under the last imperial prefect, Gavini («Mme Gavini, dernière préfète de l'Empire, à Nice. C'est sous son règne que régnèrent les Randouin, Souvoroff, Doubelt, Korsakoff», 075.0465), still there in Marie's first diary years («Belletti et Randouin étaient à la tête de l'élégance préfectorale de Nice il y a trois ans», 083.0027, October 1878), and from 1878 part of the Gavinis' Bonapartist circle in Paris. This file is for the husband, the couple and the children; Mme Randouin herself has her own entry, [Odette](ODETTE.md).

## Relevance to Marie
- Nice 1873: «le bataclan Randouin» at the pigeon-shooting (003.0179); M. Randouin, met with Lambertye, greets the family and Marie hopes he will introduce them (004.0292); he is at the Tir (012.0015). The «Mme Randouin» with whom the Duke of Hamilton danced a round on a grey, rainy day (015.0242, 18 January 1874) is Odette (0.8): she «nous connaît si bien de Nice» (082.0551).
- Paris 1878–84: M. Randouin in the Gavini set (084.0491 «Randouin et sa femme»; 085.0251, 085.0308, 086.0570, 087.0436); «Ils sont très gênés les Randouin quoiqu'entourés de luxe et d'élégance» (089.0011); he is a member of the Mirlitons (103.0116).
- January–February 1884: Marie paints «le petit Randouin», nine years old (102.0638, 102.0643, 102.0668, 103.0115), and the portraits «des petits Randouin» (103.0224), which she shows to Julian (103.0300).

## Identity
The Randouins have not been traced outside the diary and the Mon Journal indexes; Mme Randouin was «sous-préfète du temps où les Gavini faisaient si grandement les honneurs» in Nice (082.0548), so M. Randouin was presumably a sub-prefect of the Alpes-Maritimes under the Empire (not confirmed in a source).
