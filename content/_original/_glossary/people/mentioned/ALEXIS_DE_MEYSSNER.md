# Alexis de Meyssner

## Overview
M. Alexis de Meyssner (Meissner), a young Russian who arrives in Nice from St Petersburg on 23 January 1877 with a letter from Nina Sapogenikoff, whose nephew he is (068.0451). Kernberger (2013, glossary) lists "Meissner, Alexis de: nephew of Nina Sapogenikoff" and, separately, "Meissner, Baron de: fiancé of Olga, brother of Alexis".

Distinct from his brother, who marries Olga Sapogenikoff at the end of 1877 (077/1877-12-27; see [MEISSNER](MEISSNER.md)).

## Relevance to Marie
Within days he is «plus ou moins de la famille» (068.0460): he escorts the family to the races, the opera and the station, and is the butt of Marie's play-acting. She asks him whether he is inclined to fall in love with her (068.0555-0558: «un petit jeune homme doux et timide et naïf»), and during Faust she «courts» him for a quarter of an hour in the box until he stammers (068.0591-0596). He sees her off at the station on 6 February 1877 with sweets and flowers (068.0643).
