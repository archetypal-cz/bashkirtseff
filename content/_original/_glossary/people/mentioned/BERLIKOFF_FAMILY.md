# Berlikoff Family

**Research Status**: Stub

## Overview

A Russian family in the Nice social circle of spring 1873, departing for Russia on June 15, 1873 together with their physician Dr. Tchernikoff. Solominka Markevitch travels with them because the Bashkirtseffs know and trust Dr. Tchernikoff to care for her during the journey if needed.

## Diary References

- **June 15, 1873** (005.0278): "Arrivés à la gare, nous y trouvâmes la famille Berlikoff qui partait pour la Russie avec leur médecin Tchernikoff."
