# Wolkowitsky

## Overview

M. Wolkowitsky, a Russian of Poltava (Mon Journal t.14 index: «Wolkowitsky (M) russe de Poltava : 142, 144»; «(Mme) : 269»), one of the neighbours who call at the Bashkirtseff estate of Gavronzi when Marie arrives for her 1881 stay. No first name is given.

## In Marie's diary

- 1881-05-29 (092.0071, Gavronzi): «Ce matin il est venu Kapitanenko, Wolkowitsky etc.», the first callers after five years' absence.
- 1881-06-02 (092.0088): among those who come although, at her wish, nobody had been invited («On n'a invité personne selon mon désir mais il est venu du monde tout de même»): «MM. Staritsky, Trégouboff, Wolkowitsky, Michel Orloff et Lihopoy qui est allé me chercher un charmant chevalet à Karkoff.»

A Mme Wolkowitsky is indexed in t.14 (p. 269) but not tagged here.
