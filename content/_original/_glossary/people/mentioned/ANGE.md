# Ange (cab driver, Nice)

## Overview

Ange, a Nice coachman who drove the Bashkirtseffs for «plusieurs mois» before May 1875 (033.0169). The Mon Journal t.5 index has «Ange (cocher), 83», and separately «Ange : Angel (Mme)», where Marie shortens the name of Mme Angel (a different person, t.4).

## Relevance to Marie

On 24 May 1875 Marie, Nina and the others slip out of the house in disguise and take a cab, «mais ô surprise le cocher est Ange, le même Ange qui nous a servi plusieurs mois» (033.0169). Despite her «accent britannique et [sa] voix changée le maudit Ange se doute et sourit», recognising chiefly Nina and so guessing the rest (033.0172). Nothing else is said of him.
