# Miss Richards

## Overview
An English-speaking student («Miss») in the women's atelier of the [Académie Julian](../../places/schools/JULIAN.md), 1880–82. The Mon Journal t.14 index has «Richards (Miss), élève à l'atelier Julian : 60». Her first name and nationality are not given in the diary.

## Relevance to Marie
- 16 July 1880: Marie paints her at the atelier, while Amélie paints Mlle de Villevieille (089.0166).
- 9 January 1881: one of the dozen fellow students at Marie's studio-warming at «le 37» (090.0556).
- 17 December 1882: she has lent Marie her album for a drawing, and Bastien-Lepage makes a sketch in it; Marie: «Je ne sais pas pourquoi je fais le bonheur de Richards» (097.0261, 097.0263).

## References in Diary
- First mentioned: 1880-07-16 (089.0166)
- 1881-01-09 (090.0556); 1882-12-17 (097.0261, 097.0263)
