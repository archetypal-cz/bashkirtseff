## Kitty (horse)

%% GLO_KITTY.0002 %%
One of the horses Marie rides in Nice from the riding school. On 17 November 1873, after an accident with another horse, «je prends Kitty pour retourner et en bon trot commode je rentre» (012.0269); on 3 January 1874 she changes horse at the route Saint-Pons: «je pris Kitty, puis ... j'ai encore repris Rob Roy que je sais faire trotter» (015.0020).
