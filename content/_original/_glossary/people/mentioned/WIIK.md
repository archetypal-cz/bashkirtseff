# Maria Wiik

## Overview

Maria Catharina Wiik (1853–1928), Finnish portrait and genre painter, born in Helsinki. After the Helsinki drawing school she studied in Paris at the Académie Julian under Tony Robert-Fleury from 1875 (Wikipedia, "Maria Wiik"; the years she stayed are given differently, up to 1880). The Mon Journal t.12 index has «Wiik (Maria-Katarina), élève à l'atelier Julian : 198».
