# Mlle Falkenstein

## Overview

Mlle Falkenstein, an acquaintance at Spa in July 1874 (Mon Journal t.3 index: «Falkenstein (Mlle), 69, 254»). Almost certainly the same woman as [La Falkenstein](../aristocracy/FALKENSTEIN.md) of Nice in February 1874, since p.69 of t.3 is the Nice chapter and p.254 the Spa one; the name is not confirmed as the same person in the diary.

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Page numbers from the t.3 index. Listed as a merge candidate with FALKENSTEIN.

## References in Diary

On 1 July 1874 at Spa, Paul is sulking and Marie, walking alone, sits between Mmes Eristoff and Basilévitch, and finds herself like Galve, between «lady Howard et Mlle Falkenstein» (020.0480). That evening they go to a musical soirée at Gambart's, with Nadaud.
