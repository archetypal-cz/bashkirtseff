## Hulot (horse dealer)

%% GLO_HULOT.0002 %%
Dealer in horses on the avenue Marbeuf, Paris. On 29 September 1874 Moreno recommends «un certain Hulot, avenue Marbeuf pour les chevaux» (024.0162), when Marie is looking for a mount in Paris. Only mention.
