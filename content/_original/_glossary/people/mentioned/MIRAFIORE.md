# Mirafiore

## Overview

Emanuele Alberto Guerrieri, conte di Mirafiore e Fontanafredda (1851-1894), son of King Victor Emmanuel II and Rosa Vercellana, «la Bela Rosin» (en.wikipedia «Rosa Vercellana»). In 1873 he married Bianca (Blanche) de Larderel, sister of Alexandre de Larderel (thepeerage.com p4516). Kernberger (2013, glossary): «Mirafiore, Count: brother-in-law of Larderel».

## Relevance to Marie

Marie hears of his entanglement with Fanny Lear, whom the King had expelled from Italy, and of bills bearing a forged royal signature (056/1876-03-31, 056/1876-04-01; see [Fanny Lear](FANNY_LEAR.md)). On 21 April 1876, drawing Larderel, she notes: «Sa soeur est mariée au comte Mirafiori, le fils du Roi, celui qui a eu l'histoire avec Fanny Lear» (059.0110). In February 1877 Larderel shows her «sa sœur la comtesse de Mirafiore, bru du Roi» (068/1877-02-10).
