# Flores

M. Florès, an avoué (solicitor of the Nice courts) engaged by the family's circle (Mon Journal t.4 index: «Flores (M.), avoué, 176»). On 1874-10-12 (024.0295), a day Marie waits for the carriage that Georges keeps till half past six, Georges «au milieu du dîner nous amena l'avoué Florès qui eut le bon esprit de s'en aller, et avec lequel il was drinking toute la journée.» The first name and the matter he was handling for the family are not stated.
