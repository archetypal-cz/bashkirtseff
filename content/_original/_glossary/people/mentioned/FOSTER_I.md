# Foster I

«Foster I», the elder son of the English Foster family of Ostend, so named by Marie to tell him from his brother «Foster 2» (Mon Journal t.4 index «Foster I (fils)», «Foster II (fils)»). By the t.5 index entry «Foster A (fils)» and the later «Foster (Arthur)» he is Arthur Foster, who is also «celui du chien» in 1878 (see [#Foster](FOSTER.md)); this is an inference from the indexes, not stated in the diary.

- Ostend, 1874-08-17 (023.0183): «On nous a présenté un Anglais, M. Foster, et sa sœur ... M. Foster est très laid.» She dances with him; on 08-25 (023.0334) he walks her through the rooms; 08-26 (023.0344) on the jetty (estacade) Rosen attacks them; 08-31 (023.0414) he is on the jetty with a Belgian who offers to escort Marie and her aunt to London.
- Spa balls, 1874-09-05 (023.0510): «Pour cavaliers j'ai, Foster, *Chtcherbinine* et Merjeewsky.»
- London, September 1874 (023.0606, 023.0635, 023.0638): he calls on the family, stays late talking of dogs and of the duke and duchess of Edinburgh, takes them to Bill George's to choose a mastiff and promises a bigger one; Marie will write from Paris and send the money (024.0164, 09-29; 024.0246, 10-06).
