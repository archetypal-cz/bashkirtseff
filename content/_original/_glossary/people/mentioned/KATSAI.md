## Katsaï

%% GLO_KATSAI.0002 %%
On 9 January 1874, after reading the *Echos* account of the Duke of Hamilton's marriage, Marie writes: «On parla de Bade, (Katsaï) et Hamilton» (015.0099), while her father calls Hamilton «un roux sale ... un monstre». The word in brackets is the whole evidence: it may be a person linked with the Baden gambling resort, or a place or a nickname; the diary does not say.
