# Nadinka

«Nadinka» (a Russian pet form of Nadejda/Nadine) who writes from Russia in April 1875 that Paul has been received at their father's with cannon salutes and champagne (031.0307). Not identified; she is not the Nice Nadinka of 1873 (012.0075, 012.0207), who is Mlle Nadia Galitzine ([#Nadia](NADIA.md)).
