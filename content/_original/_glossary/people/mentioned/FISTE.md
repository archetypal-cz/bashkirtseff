# Fiste

Fiste, a singer of the opera company at Nice in the 1874–75 season (Mon Journal t.4 index: «Fiste (M.), chanteur, 187»). On 1874-10-27 (025.0005), at the season's opening: «La nouvelle Laffite a été hissed à sa première chanson. Il n'y a que Fiste, mais Fiste est seul. Les choristes et les figurantes sont hideuses ...» So Marie rates him the only good performer of the evening. No first name, voice type or the work performed was found. See [#Nice Opera](../../places/theaters/OPERA_NICE.md).
