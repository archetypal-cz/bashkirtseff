# Beni Yang-Yang (Arab acrobat troupe)

## Overview

An Arab troupe of acrobats that played at the theatre of Nice in October 1874. Marie writes «les Beni Yang-Yang, troupe arabe» (024.0358). The name is probably her form of the *Beni-Zoug-Zoug*, the Kabyle acrobatic company that toured Europe and America from the early 1860s (playbills of Leipzig 1863, New York 1867, Brighton 1868, found by web search; the V&A holds the Brighton bill, https://collections.vam.ac.uk/item/O1241440), but no source places a troupe of that name in Nice in 1874.

## Relevance to Marie

On Sunday 18 October 1874 Marie and her mother go in the evening to the theatre «voir les Beni Yang-Yang, troupe arabe», in the first box on the first tier lent by Lewin; the audience is «un public de dimanche» (024.0358). «Les Yang-Yang sont assez amusants et font des choses fort extraordinaires. Fortuné est au théâtre, maman a voulu lui faire voir ses ressemblants» (024.0359): Maman had taken the young Black servant Fortuné (called Chocolat) to see people she thought resembled him, a remark that is prejudiced by the standards of today.

## Identity uncertain

We are not sure which troupe this was. The Beni-Zoug-Zoug identification is the editors' guess from the name; troupes of «Arabes» with similar names toured for decades. If you can identify this troupe, we would be glad to hear from you.
