# Mme Gaupillat

## Overview

A social acquaintance of Marie's in Paris, spring 1880. Visited Marie on 27 May 1880 ("Madame Gaupillat a passé"). Marie made a sketch (*pochade*) of her, completed by 12 June 1880 ("J'ai fini la pochade de Mme Gaupillat"). She appears as a minor figure in the Parisian bourgeois-aristocratic social circuit that Marie frequented.
