## Comte Noël de Leiser

%% GLO_LEISER.0002 %%
A Pole naturalised English whom Marie meets at Spa: introduced to her mother on 15 August 1874, with Count Doenhoff (of the court of the Prussian prince), Count Dounine-Barkovsky and the two young Karageorgevitch princes: «le comte Noël de Leiser, un Polonais naturalisé Anglais» (023.0128). He returns as a dancing partner on 28 August and 5 September (023.0383, 023.0510). No other source found.
