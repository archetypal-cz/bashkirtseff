%% GLO_RAVEL.0001 %%
**Retired.** The «Ravel» of the theatre in Nice (1874-75) is the comic actor [Pierre-Alfred Ravel](../artists/PIERRE_ALFRED_RAVEL.md) (`people/artists/PIERRE_ALFRED_RAVEL.md`); use that entry instead. The «Ravel qu'on raccommode» of 7 March 1874 (017.0196) is not a person and not a carriage. It is apparently the household's word for Marie's alarm clock (*réveil*): the printed *Mon Journal* t.3 reads «Ravel» where the transcription has «réveil» at 015.0027, 015.0263 and 015.0275 (footnote 017.0196.1). It has no glossary entry.
