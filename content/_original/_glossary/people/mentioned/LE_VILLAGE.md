## Le Village

%% GLO_LE_VILLAGE.0002 %%
One-act comedy by Octave Feuillet (1856), given at the Théâtre-Français on 18 June 1874 with his *Le Sphinx*, in which Sophie Croizette was admirable: «On donnait "Le village", "Le sphinx"» (020.0360). Marie saw it from a box. *Le Sphinx* premiered on 23 March 1874 (ABAA note on Feuillet's *Le Sphinx*).
