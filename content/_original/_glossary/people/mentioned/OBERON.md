# Oberon

## Overview

Weber's romantic opera *Oberon* (1826), or its overture, which Marie plays and hears everywhere.

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Weber's work from the LAN note at 007.0372. WRONG CATEGORY: a musical work in people/mentioned.

## References in Diary

On 9 August 1873 at the Vienna Exhibition, bored and alone, she hears «fantaisie sur Obéron que je déteste. Je l'ai joué, et par-dessus cela, comme exprès, on le joue dans tous les concerts où je vais» (007.0372); then Johann Strauss appears. On 17 April 1875, at Laussel's pupils' recital in Nice, the solemnity begins with «notre ouverture d'Obéron», played on eight hands; Marie does not make a mistake and is not afraid (031.0258).
