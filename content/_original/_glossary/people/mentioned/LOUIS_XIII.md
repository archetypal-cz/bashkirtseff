## Louis XIII

%% GLO_LOUIS_XIII.0002 %%
King of France (1601-1643). Marie, describing herself on 25 September 1874, says her hair is cut «comme chez Louis XIV enfant, à Versailles, sur le tableau qui représente la mort de Louis XIII, sur l'escalier» (024.0106): Louis XIII appears only as the subject of a painting at Versailles that she compares her hairstyle to, and the picture is not identified (a web search by RSR found none).
