# Adolphe de Rothschild

## Overview
Adolphe Carl von Rothschild (1823–1900), banker and art collector of the Naples branch of the family, who moved to Paris in 1868 and lived in the rue de Monceau; he married his cousin Julie (1830–1907) in 1850 and they had no children (Rothschild Archive, «Adolphe Carl von Rothschild (1823–1900)»; en.wikipedia «Adolph Carl von Rothschild»). The Mon Journal t.5 index has «Rothschild (Adolphe, baron de), 237»; t.13 «Rothschild (Adolphe, baron de) : 8, 297; baronne de : 8, 13, 172, 297, 317».

## Relevance to Marie
- **10 August 1875** (Paris, recalling Nice): «Rothschild, Adolphe était là, celui qui, il y a deux ans à Nice, s'arrêtait tout court et me regardait en plein, alors que j'étais petite fille encore» (038.0254), in a paragraph about the glances she attracted as a girl.
- **26 June 1880:** at a reception that begins with the wedding of Lord Paget at the embassy, Mme Gavini says that for «Mme de Rothschild (Adolphe) se dérange, et viennent» the party must be a great one (089.0037). This is his wife Julie, who has no file of her own and is tagged on her husband's (research note of 22 October 2026).
