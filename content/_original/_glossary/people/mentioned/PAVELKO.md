# Pavelko

## Overview
A lawyer of Poltava in the circle of Marie's father, autumn 1876. The Mon Journal t.9 index has "Pavelko (M.), avocat, [Cicéron]". Marie: "Pavelko est un avocat et le lion d'ici" (066/1876-09-06); "Pavelko un affreux avocat" among the hunters (066/1876-09-24), where she also calls him "notre Cicéron".

## Relevance to Marie
One of the Poltava men who call at Gavronzi and at the hotel in Poltava (067.0018). He plays the secretary in the mock trial staged at Gavronzi (066/1876-10-08) and pays her compliments she finds horrible (066/1876-10-08). On 23 October 1876 uncle Alexandre tells her that "Pavelko répandait le bruit que je lui faisais des avances" (067.0265). She meets him again at the Poltava club concert (067/1876-11-02).
