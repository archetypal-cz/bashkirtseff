# Vicomte Walsh

## Overview

«Le vicomte Walsh», an elderly man of the Nice winter colony whom Marie describes in one line as «ce petit vieux qui par ses petites misères sème des petites querelles parmi sa petite société» (027.0201, 8 December 1874). He calls on the Bashkirtseffs in the afternoon after Galula and the comte de Barrême, at the time of the Promenade concerts (they stayed «tout le temps à la musique»). Mon Journal t.4 index: «Walsh (vicomte), 239».

No further mention in the diary; his family and given name are not stated, and no source we have identifies him beyond the title.
