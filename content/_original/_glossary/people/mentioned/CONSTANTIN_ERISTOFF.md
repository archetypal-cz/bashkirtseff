# Constantin Eristoff

## Identity
Prince Constantin Eristoff, lover of Marie's paternal aunt Mme Tutcheff: the Mon Journal t.9 index has "Eristoff (prince Constantin), (fils du prince Emile Eristoff), amant de la tante Tutcheff". In August 1876 he is in Monaco with Marie's father and Mme Tutcheff (065/1876-08-30), where Marie also blames him for wrecking a marriage planned for Pacha's sister ("Constantin Eristoff complota tant et dit tant de vilenies que le mariage s'est dérangé", same entry). At Gavronzi in October 1876 he arrives after the Tutcheffs (067.0167), forbidden to come near Marie (067.0174); "Son prince est à Poltava" (067.0204) refers to him.
