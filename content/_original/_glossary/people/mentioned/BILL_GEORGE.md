# Bill George (dog dealer)

## Overview

Mr Bill (Georges) George, a dog dealer; the Mon Journal t.4 index lists him as «Bill (M. Georges), marchand de chiens, 140». Marie visits his kennels with Arthur Foster in the autumn of 1874. The diary gives no address; nothing further was found (a web search for the London dealer of that name gave no usable source).

## Relevance to Marie

On 11 September 1874 Foster «nous conduit chez Bill George. J'ai choisi un mastiff, mais Foster me promet un plus grand demain à dix heures. Je ne peux acheter un chien maintenant, mais de Paris j'écrirai à Foster, lui enverrai la somme et il m'enverra le c[hien]» (023.0638). The dog question runs on through the autumn in her letters to Foster (025.0198, 043).
