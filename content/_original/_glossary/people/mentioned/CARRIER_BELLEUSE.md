# Carrier-Belleuse (Pierre, the son)

## Overview

Marie's «Carrier-Belleuse» is «le fils du statuaire», the painter **Pierre Carrier-Belleuse** (28 January 1851 – 29 January 1932), son of the sculptor Albert-Ernest Carrier-Belleuse (1824–1887); he exhibited at the Salon from 1875, painted dancers and pastels, and made the small *Panorama de Notre-Dame de Lourdes* of 1881 (Wikipedia, «Pierre Carrier-Belleuse»; a web search dated the Lourdes panorama to 1881). The Mon Journal t.15 index calls him «Carrier-Belleuse (Albert-Louis)», a name not found in the sources; t.14 has «Carrier-Belleuse (M.) : 254, 262, 272, 275». The 1881 panorama fits 093.0385, where Marie wants to buy the studies for the «panorama de Lourdes par Carrier-Belleuse, le fils du statuaire», exhibited in the cercle at 36 rue Vivienne. Confidence 0.8; the father the sculptor is a different person.

## Relevance to Marie

- **21 November 1881.** The exhibition of the studies and the project for the Lourdes panorama at 36 rue Vivienne: she asks to buy, he is «très aimable et gentil» but cannot sell (093.0385). He had called on them the day before.
- **9 December 1881 – 13 January 1882.** He calls (094.0051); the family and Brisbane visit him on 6 January («il s'occupe tout le temps de *mes tons*», 094.0142, where she judges: «n'a pas un talent monstre... mais il a une qualité»); on 13 January, Carrier-Belleuse «fondait sous mes yeux» and, «tout ramolli et sentimental», insisted on the cruelty of her motto *Gloriae Cupiditas* (094.0164), and she judges him «un garçon capable de devenir très amoureux» (094.0165).
- **30 April – 26 May 1882.** At a gathering with Bojidar, the Gaillards, Julian and others (095.0003); her mother thinks he is in love with her (095.0004); at the Salon with Gavini on 26 May (095.0106).
- **19 July 1882.** She hears she is on the mind of «un autre grand seigneur, comme qui dirait Carrier-Belleuse» (095.0317).
