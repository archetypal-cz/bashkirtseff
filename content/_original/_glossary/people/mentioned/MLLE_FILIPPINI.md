# Filippini (student of the Atelier Julian)

## In the diary

- **29 November 1882** (097.0166): in the women's studio, «Il n'y a pas une élève intéressante sauf Filippini et encore».
- **31 May 1883** (100.0045): the concours result: «c'est Amélie qui est jugée première puis cinq hommes, puis moi, puis Filippini».

She is a woman student of Julian's ladies' studio («une élève»), not Charles-Louis «Kiki» Filippini, the Corsican Bonapartist of the Gavini circle ([FILIPPINI](FILIPPINI.md)), although the Mon Journal t.15 index files both pages under him («Filipini (Charles, Louis) [Kiki] : … 125, 273»).

## Identity uncertain

We are not sure who this is. The diary gives only the surname of a fellow student at the Atelier Julian in 1882–83, placed just after Marie in the concours of May 1883; searches for a woman painter Filippini exhibiting in the 1880s or listed among Julian's students found nothing. If you can identify her, we would be glad to hear from you.
