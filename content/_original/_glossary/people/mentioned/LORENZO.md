## Don Lorenzo (Madrid)
%% 2026-10-22T22:16:12 RSR: The entry was split; the other referent is tagged elsewhere or carries an RSR note. %%
Don Lorenzo, a Madrid dealer in old Spanish fabrics whose shop Marie paints in October–November 1881: «Hier j'ai commencé une étude dans la boutique de l'illustre Lorenzo» (093.0258); she buys three hundred francs' worth of old embroideries and takes tender leave of «al senior Don Lorenzo» (093.0264, 20 October); she fears she has let herself be robbed by «maître Lorenzo» (093.0293), plans a picture from the study (093.0321), and returns on 2 November to redo the sketch (093.0326, 093.0330). Mon Journal t.14 index: «Lorenzo, Marchand de tissus espagnols».

«Lorenzo Sporagna», the model who posed for her modelling in June 1880 (089.0027), is a different man; that paragraph carries an RSR note and no tag.
