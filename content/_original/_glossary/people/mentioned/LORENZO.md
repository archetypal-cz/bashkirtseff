## Lorenzo

%% GLO_LORENZO.0002 %%
Two different people are tagged.

%% GLO_LORENZO.0003 %%
1. Don Lorenzo, a Madrid dealer in old Spanish fabrics whose shop Marie paints in October-November 1881: «Hier j'ai commencé une étude dans la boutique de l'illustre Lorenzo» (093.0258); she buys three hundred francs' worth of old embroideries and takes tender leave of «al senior Don Lorenzo» (093.0264, 20 Oct); she returns on 2 November to redo the sketch (093.0326, 093.0330) and plans a bric-à-brac picture (093.0321). Mon Journal t.14 index: «Lorenzo, Marchand de tissus espagnols», pp. 233-234, 240, 245.
