## Identification (RSR, 2026-10-21)
The Mon Journal t.10 index has «Torlonia (Clemente, duc), [Torniole]» and «Torniole, Clemente Torlonia»; the t.11 index has «Torlonia (don Clemente) [Torgnole]» and «Torgnole : Torlonia don Clemente». So the Torlonia of 1877 is don Clemente Torlonia, of the Roman banking family. His exact place in the family is not checked here. The t.10 index lists a separate «Torlonia (Alexandre, prince)» (p.302), not this man.

Marie and Rosalie call him «Torniole» (072.0190 «Torniole i.e. Torlonia»; 072.0269), and Larderel calls him «Clement» (072.0158).

## Relevance to Marie (1877)
- Nice and Monaco, June 1877: Larderel's companion and, in Marie's eyes, her «inexplicable ennemi». Drunk, he presents Larderel to her mother (072.0045); Larderel complains of «la tyrannie de Torlonia» (072.0140); Rosalie calls him «un diable comme ce Torniole» (072.0269). He travels with the family as far as their train for Paris on his way to Rome (072.0279).
- Paris, autumn 1877: met on the steps («sur le perron»), asks to call (075.0096); his journeys with the Souvoroff (075.0413).
