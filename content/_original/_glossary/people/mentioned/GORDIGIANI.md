# Michele Gordigiani

## Overview
Michele Gordigiani (1835–1909), Florentine portrait painter (en.wikipedia «Michele Gordigiani»). The Mon Journal t.10 index has «Gordigiani (Michel), (peintre), 158, 163, 170, 252»; he signs his letter of November 1877 «Michel Gordigiani» (076.0052).

## Relevance to Marie
- Florence, May 1877: Marie wants him to paint her portrait (071.0165); on 9 May she meets «le plus grand peintre de Florence», who admires her complexion and hair but does not fire her enthusiasm (071.0214); he visits, encourages her and praises her sketches (071.0269, with footnote).
- She writes to him on Maman's advice in July 1877 (072.0478) and receives his letter from Florence in November (076.0046–0052); a further letter in February 1878 (078.0220).
