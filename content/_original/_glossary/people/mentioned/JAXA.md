## Jaxa

%% GLO_JAXA.0002 %%
A Paris acquaintance of the Bashkirtseffs in May 1874, called by surname only. He accompanies the family to the Auteuil races with the deacon (019.0353, 26 May), dines with them (019.0365: «Jaxa dîne avec nous, après tout, il ne fait de mal à personne») and on 29 May «se conduit si bêtement, sottement. Il est insupportable et inconvenant» (019.0391). The Mon Journal t.3 index has him only as «Jaxa». He is the Paris Bikowsky who calls himself «prince Jacqua de Bikowsky» (JACQUA_DE_BIKOWSKY, JAXA_BIKOWSKY): the same weeks of May 1874, the same deacon. See BIKOWSKY.
