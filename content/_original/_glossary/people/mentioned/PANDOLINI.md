# Pandolini

## Overview

Pandolini, an Italian singer whom Marie hears in *Guillaume Tell* in October 1881. The footnote at 093.0211 gives «probably the Italian baritone Francesco Pandolfini (1836–1916)» (Troubetzkoy archive, «Portrait of Francesco Pandolfini»), spelling the name differently from Marie.

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Identification with Pandolfini is the footnote's, hedged ('probably'); not independently checked.

## References in Diary

On 11 October 1881 the family attend the opera, in a box on the stage, with Pollack's son and father; when «Pandolini s'est approché» Marie tells him she had heard him «à Paris où il a admirablement chanté aux Italiens»; he is «un être superbe, un Titien, la désinvolture du portrait de François 1er et une tête à la Don Carlos moderne», and she thinks the little artist is in love with her (093.0211).
