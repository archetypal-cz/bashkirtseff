# Plevasko

## Overview
Variant spelling («Plevasko») of the name of the lawyer [Fyodor Plevako](PLEVAKO.md), used in Marie's pages of August 1875 and in the Mon Journal t.5 index («Plevasko (M.), avocat»; «Plevasko (Mme)»).

## Relevance to Marie
All five paragraphs tagged here (039.0266, 040.0005, 040.0144, 040.0161, 040.0163, 26–30 August 1875) show the same man: the famous Russian lawyer called to Paris by Marie's aunt, who sees the family daily and is driven to the Bois with his wife. In May 1876 Marie writes that the family has taken Plevako «celui que j'ai vu à Paris après Schlangenbad» (059.0654), which fixes the identity (confidence 0.9). Details are in the main entry.
