# Fitz-James (family)

## Overview

The ducal family of Fitz-James, as a group or by birth name. In the diary the most important member is the dowager duchess, Marie's patroness of 1880-84: see [Duchesse de Fitz-James](DUCHESSE_DE_FITZ_JAMES.md). No duc de Fitz-James appears in person. This file is for the passages that name the family rather than the duchess:

- «les Fitz-James» as a household: «le seul vrai bal, celui des Fitz-James» (100.0076, 103.0379), i.e. the ball of 28 May 1882 at the younger duchess's (095.0109). Mon Journal t.15 index «Fitz-James (les) : 159, 163, 183, 275».
- A born Fitz-James: the wife of «Gaston» (de Larderel) speaks French like a Frenchwoman because «sa mère est française (Fitz James) et son père est le duc Salviati» (073.0342, 1877).

## References in Diary

073.0342; 100.0076; 103.0379. (088.0761 and 088.0839, «la princesse de Fitz-James» and «cette charmante vieille femme», are the dowager duchess; retag queued.)
