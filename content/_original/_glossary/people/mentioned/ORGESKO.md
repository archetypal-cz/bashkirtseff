# Orgesko

## Overview

M. Orgesko, a Russian of the Nice colony (Mon Journal indexes: «Orgesko (M.)» t.4 p.172, t.12 p.107). One of the five witnesses of Marie's grandfather's will, made at Nice (RSR at 082.0269).

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Forename not given. The witnesses list is from the RSR note at 082.0269.

## References in Diary

On 8 October 1874 Marie and Dina meet him coming back from the villa, and the mothers turn back to show him the house (024.0266). On 2 September 1878 Marie defends the will of her grandfather against her uncle Nicolas: it was made three years before in Nice, with five reputable witnesses, among them Patton, Orgesko and Bihovetz (082.0269).
