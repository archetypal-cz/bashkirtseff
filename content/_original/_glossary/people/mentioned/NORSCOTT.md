# Norscott

**Norscott** is a pseudonym, not a real family. "Alexandrine Norscott" is the alias under which Mme Marie de Daillens, née de Mouzay, attended the feminist society Le Droit des femmes in December 1880; "M. Norscott" is the invented late English husband of that persona. See [Mme de Daillens](DE_DAILLENS.md) for the full identification and sources.

- 090.0293 (1880-12-01): "De Daillens est française, veuve d'un Anglais M. Norscott."
- 090.0319 (1880-12-08): "les citoyennes Alexandrine Norscott et Pauline Orell" (Marie herself is Pauline Daria Orell).
- Mon Journal t.14 index: "Norscott Alexandrine / pseudonyme de Mme de Daillens".
