## Identification (RSR, 2026-10-21)
Henri Hecht. The Mon Journal t.13 index lists «Hecht (Henri), négociant : 70-71, 75, 84-87, 90-93, 95-97, 100-101, 103, 168-171, 180, 182, 185, 187-188, 211, 221, 304». Marie introduces him on 7 August 1879 as «Hecht, financier juif et ami intime de Gambetta» (086.0009). On 13 September 1879 she dines at his flat «rue du Château d'eau», with «de très beaux tableaux, des Rousseau, des Corot, des Troyon, Delacroix et même Raphaël» (086.0262). Probably the Henri Hecht who, with his brother Albert Hecht (1842–1889), banker and collector, built a large collection of Impressionist pictures (Wikipedia, «Albert Hecht») (0.6). Marie uses him as her entry into republican society: «la combinaison Hecht» (086.0254), «je ne le prends que comme commencement» (086.0279).

«Joseph» in this carnet is Joseph Arnaud de l'Ariège, not Hecht (except Hecht's own telegram signed «Joseph»; tag-audit ruling 2026-10-02).
