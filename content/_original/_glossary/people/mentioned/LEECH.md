## Identity
Woman in Nice social circle, possibly departed. Associated with Spang.

## Diary References

- **March 27**: "Les Leech et les Spang ne vont plus dans leur [illisible] je crois même que la Leech n'est plus ici" (The Leech and Spang no longer go to their [illegible] I even believe that Leech is no longer here).

### 1875 (added 2026-10-21)
