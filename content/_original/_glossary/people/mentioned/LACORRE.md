## Citoyenne Lacorre

%% GLO_LACORRE.0002 %%
Member of the feminist society *Le Droit des femmes*, at whose home the meeting of 17 August 1881 was held: «La réunion a lieu chez la Citoyenne Lacorre» (092.0421, in the entry of 18 August 1881). *Citoyenne* is the republican and feminist form of address. Only mention; no first name. See HUBERTINE_AUCLERT for the society.
