## Identification and the January 1881 affair (RSR 2026-10-21)
Marie writes the name «Brimont» («la Brimont», «Mme de Brimont», «la mère Brimont»); the Mon Journal t.14 index has «Brimont (Mme de) : 20, 25, 30, 32-34, 41-43, 51, 54, …» and «Brimont (comte de) parent de la précédente : 54, 188». She is a vicomtesse: «le comte de Brimont, parent de la vicomtesse» (090.0512). Her first name is not given.

On 1 January 1881 Marie reports a scandal around her and [Émile de Girardin](../writers/EMILE_DE_GIRARDIN.md): Mme Gros, widow of Duvergier de Hauranne (index «Gros (Mme) veuve Duvergier de Hauranne»), accuses her of Prussian espionage; Girardin obtains satisfaction in the Chamber; her nephew M. d'Infreville is to fight Mme Gros's husband after a jury d'honneur of Jockey Club members rules that he may act for her, with the comte de Brimont and Paul d'Abzac as seconds (090.0510–0512). No outside account was found (web search 2026-10-21).
