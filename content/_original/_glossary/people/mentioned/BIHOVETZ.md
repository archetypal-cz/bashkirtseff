# General Bihovetz

## Overview
A Polish general in the Bashkirtseffs' Nice circle. The Mon Journal t.10 index has «Bihovetz (Général Polonais), 14, 125, 173, 177, 188, 199-200, 203, 205, 207, 214, 220, 237». Not further identified; the spelling is Marie's.

## Relevance to Marie
He appears in the diary from November 1874 (025/1874-11-15, lunching with the family together with Mme Daniloff) to about 1880, mostly in Nice: he dines with the family, walks them along the Promenade, takes them to the music (050/1875-12-01). He also reads cards for Marie, and she half believes him: «O Bihovetz ! faut-il donc croire les cartes !» and «Voilà la lettre froissante annoncée par Bihovetz arrivée» (062/1876-06-24). In Naples on 19 April 1877 she copies «le résumé des cartes tirées par Bihovetz» into the diary (070.0701–0721) and notes that he has guessed a plan known only to Rosalie and herself.
