# Pauline

## Overview

Pauline, a Nice *cocotte* (Mon Journal t.2 index: «Pauline, demi-mondaine, 133, 184, 191»), kept in autumn 1873 by an Englishman and the model of the rich courtesan whom Marie says she does not wish to resemble.

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Surname not given. Her Englishman is not named. [Gioia] is another cocotte with her own entry.

## References in Diary

On 22 October 1873 Marie describes «une seconde Gioia… Pauline, qui depuis un mois est sous la protection d'un Anglais»: in a month she has an apartment on the Promenade des Anglais, a coupé, a victoria, two horses, a fashionable maid and nine thousand francs of lace bought for cash (011.0174). On 18 November, driving all the time, Bête says she looks like «la Pauline», which Marie hates (012.0276); on 22 November they discuss cocottes and husbands (013.0022).
