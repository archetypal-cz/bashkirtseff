# Nemours

## Overview

Louis d'Orléans, duc de Nemours (1814–1896), second son of King Louis-Philippe (footnote 091.0355.3; Mon Journal t.12 index «Nemours (Louis Charles, duc de) : 33»; t.14 p.104). His son Gaston, comte d'Eu, married Isabel, heiress to the Brazilian throne, hence Marie's «future impératrice».

Marie's two mentions are social lists.
