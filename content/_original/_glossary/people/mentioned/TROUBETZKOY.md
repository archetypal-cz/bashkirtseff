# La petite Troubetzkoy (Alexandra?) and Princess Lise

## Overview
In March 1882 the Troubetzkoy women are in Nice: Princess Lise (Elisabeth) Troubetzkoy, whom Marie calls «l'ineffable Lise», and her daughter, «la petite Troubetzkoy». The Mon Journal indexes list «Troubetskoy (princesse Lise)» and «Troubetskoy (Alexandra), fille» (t.9 p.205), «Troubetzkoy (Elisabeth, princesse)» (t.11 pp.150, 213, 248, 276), «Troubeskoy (princesse Elisabeth)» (t.13 p.73) and «Troubeskoy (Mme Lise)» (t.14 pp.299, 301). The «petite» of 1882 is probably the daughter Alexandra (0.7).

## Relevance to Marie
- 24 March 1882: «La scie de Pomar est finie, on dit qu'il épouse la petite Troubetzkoy, fille de l'innéfable Lise. On y travaille depuis trois mois» (094.0340). Pomar is, in Marie's words, «fils de lady Caithness» (see [POMAR](POMAR.md), [LADY_CAITHNESS](LADY_CAITHNESS.md)).
- 26 March: «Etincelle me cite dans la matinée du "Colbert" ... mais la grande tirade est pour la petite Troubetzkoy dont le mariage avec Pomar est raté. Les Troubetzkoy sont parties»; gossip says Princess Lise had her dresses seized (094.0350). The mother told Pomar that her daughter adores him and he will have her unhappiness on his conscience; Marie finds him «bien dur», one of the many girls who break their teeth on him (094.0351).
