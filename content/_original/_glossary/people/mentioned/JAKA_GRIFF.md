## Jaka Griff (Jaxa Gryf)

%% GLO_JAKA_GRIFF.0002 %%
«Au restaurant russe Renard, nous rencontrons Mme Sapogenikoff, la demoiselle russe qui est avec elle, et le diacre puis Jaka Griff» (024.0057, 22 Sept 1874). The name looks like a garbling of «Jaxa Gryf», the Bikowsky of Paris who appears with the same deacon at the same restaurant six days later (024.0151, «Jaxa Bikowsky, ce misérable bouffon»); see JAXA_BIKOWSKY and BIKOWSKY. This identification is probable, not documented: the spelling is Marie's.
