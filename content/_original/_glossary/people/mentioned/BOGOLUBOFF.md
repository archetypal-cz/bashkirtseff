# Alexey Bogolyubov (Bogoluboff)

## Overview

Alexey Petrovich Bogolyubov (16 March 1824 – 1896), Russian landscape and seascape painter, long resident in Paris (Wikipedia, «Alexey Bogolyubov», https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexey_Bogolyubov, which gives two different death dates in infobox and text; the diary's Mon Journal t.16 index has «Bogoluboff (M.), peintre : 43, 200»). He is the leading figure of the Russian art colony in Paris in 1883–1884.

## Relevance to Marie

- **1 October 1883.** At the farewell ceremony for Turgenev's body at the Paris station, «[le peintre] Bogoluboff a aussi prononcé un discours» after Renan and About; the word *peintre* was struck out in the transcription (101.0471).
- **18 May 1884.** Bagnitsky tells her that at the painter Bogoluboff's they spoke of the Salon and that an unnamed artist had said something about Bastien-Lepage (104.0222; the quoted remark is in the paragraph).
