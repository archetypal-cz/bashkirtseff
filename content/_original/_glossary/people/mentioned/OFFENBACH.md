# Offenbach

## Overview

Jacques Offenbach (1819–1880), German-born French composer of operettas (*Orphée aux enfers*, *La Belle Hélène*, *La Vie parisienne*), whom the Mon Journal indexes list as «Offenbach (Jacques), compositeur» in t.3–t.16 (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_Offenbach).

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Life dates from Wikipedia as cited; the operetta details from the existing RSR notes at 019.0297 and 028.0240. All 16 tagged paragraphs were skimmed. Marie also saw *La Belle Hélène* in German in Vienna in August 1873 (see MENELAUS).

## References in Diary

For Marie he is the music of the era's frivolity. In May 1874 she cannot get a seat for the Bouffes-Parisiens' new Offenbach, *Bagatelle*, with Judic (019.0297); in January 1875 she goes to *Madame l'Archiduc* at the Bouffes (028.0240) and walks «chantant un air d'Offenbach» (028.0257); the Nice carnival of 1875 burns Papa Carnaval to Offenbach's music (029.0261); in April 1875 she compares an admirer's letter to «l'archiduc Ernest d'Offenbach» (031.0018). Offenbach is also her shorthand for a catchphrase («comme on dit chez Offenbach», 043.0084; «ça y était comme on dit dans les bêtises d'Offenbach», 032.0434): she sees *La Créole* (050.0012, 18 November 1875) and *Le Voyage dans la lune* (050.0180) in Nice, sings his repertoire for Russian friends in 1876 (065.0340), plays the letter of Venus from *La Belle Hélène* (067.0894), and in 1884 hears the ‘pillaging’ of Offenbach in a new operetta (103.0321, 103.0323).
