## Description
An Italian nobleman bearing the marquisate of Campomarino, a town in Molise (Province of Campobasso), southern Italy, historically part of the Kingdom of Naples. He left his visiting card at Marie's lodgings in Naples in March 1877 but was not received in person.

The marquisate was a feudal grant from the Bourbon Kingdom of the Two Sicilies over the Campomarino territory. After Italian Unification (1860–61), noble titles were recognized but feudal privileges abolished.
