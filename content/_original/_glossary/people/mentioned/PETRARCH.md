# Petrarch

## Overview
Francesco Petrarca (1304–1374), Italian poet and scholar, author of the *Canzoniere*, whose lyrics celebrate his unattainable love Laura (en.wikipedia «Petrarch»).

## Relevance to Marie
She compares her own hopeless devotion to the Duke of Hamilton to Petrarch's: «Le duc, l'ombre, m'est ce que Laure était à Pétrarque...» (064.0381, 1 August 1876). The duke, who has never answered her, is an «ombre» (a shade), as Laura was for the poet. For the duke see [DUKE_OF_HAMILTON](../core/DUKE_OF_HAMILTON.md).
