## Létorière (Mme de Peyronney)

%% GLO_LETORIERE.0002 %%
The vicomtesse de Létarière, whom Marie calls «Mme de Peyronney» (091.0409, 2 April 1881): the Mon Journal t.14 index has «Peyronney (Mme), vicomtesse de Létarière», pp. 100, 109, 117 ..., and, separately, «Peyronney (Mlle de)». She is the society columnist Étincelle of *Le Figaro*; see ETINCELLE. The family name is spelled Létarière, Létorière and Létorières in different passages.
