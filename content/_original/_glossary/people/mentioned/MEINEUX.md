# Meineux

## Overview

M. Meineux, brother-in-law of Marie's father (*beau-frère de papa*), uncle of [Savine](SAVINE.md). His first name is not given, nor which side of the family the tie runs through.

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Not in the Mon Journal t.14 index (checked); no outside source. The exact kinship is not stated by Marie.

## References in Diary

On 11 March 1882, after Paul and his wife leave for Monaco, Marie notes that Savine «est devenu de la famille, du reste il est le neveu de M. Meineux beau-frère de papa» (094.0304).
