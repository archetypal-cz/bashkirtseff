## Kondratieff

%% GLO_KONDRATIEFF.0002 %%
A Russian family in the Nice winter colony of 1873-74. On 15 October 1873 Marie lists a group on the Promenade: «Mme Vigier, Mlle de Comar, Richaud, Kondratieffs cousin» (011.0013, the cousin of a Kondratieff); on 9 January 1874 a Mme Kondratieff is among the mamans watching the children's cotillon with Mme Apletcheïeff (015.0095). No first names; the family is not indexed in Mon Journal under this spelling.
