# Robert Lancaster-Johnstone

## Overview

Robert Lancaster-Johnstone (Marie writes «Lancaster», «Robert», «Lancaster Johnstone», once «Jonhstone»), an Englishman, the husband of [Berthe Boyd](../recurring/BERTHE.md), whom he married in 1878 after her flight with him. The Mon Journal indexes list him as «Lancaster-Johnstone (Robert)» (t.11 pp. 105, 144, 176, 187–188, 197–198, 208, 259; t.12 pp. 172, 176, 180, 241, 248; t.13 pp. 22, 31, 62, 66–67, 70, 72, 83, 108, 112, 118, 121, 163; t.14 pp. 110, 115, 264). No other biographical data are known; he is not the baronet [Sir Frederic Johnstone](SIR_FREDERIC_JOHNSTONE.md) of 1875.

## In Marie's diary

- 16 January and 17 February 1878 (077.0228, 078.0219): a correspondent asks for «tout le mal qu'on dit d'un certain M. L[ancaster]» and another writes of «M. Lancastre [sic]»; Marie's circle is warned about him before the affair.
- March–April 1878 (079.0030, 079.0179, 079.0187): Berthe is «incurablement folle de Lancaster».
- 10–11 April 1878 (079.0303–0311): Berthe slips away from a walk with her sister and Dina and «s'est enfuie avec Lancaster». An anonymous letter had told Mme Boyd that Berthe was «la maîtresse de ce Lancaster Johnstone» and that at a supper young men had congratulated him; Marie calls him «un vilain homme», whom she had seen once.
- 4 December 1878 (083.0350–0351): first dinner of «Berthe et son mari» at the Bashkirtseffs': «châtain, taille moyenne, favoris et moustache», grey or green eyes; Marie thinks him «un excellent homme» and does not recognise «ce héros et surtout ce viveur».
- 15 March 1879 (084.0478): he writes that Berthe has «heureusement accouché d'un joli petit garçon».
- 10 August 1879 (086.0047): he runs with MM. de Boishébert and de Rohan, which makes Gavini say «il est clair que c'est un drôle»; Marie thinks Berthe still loves him.
- 12–14 January 1880 (087.0191–0224): Berthe's rudeness to Blanc at the Russian New Year ball brings Blanc to send his seconds to Lancaster; the «affaire Blanc-Lancaster» is settled by Marie's letter.
- 1 October 1880 (089.0659): «c'est le cher Robert qui a fait le coup» in a question of money.
- 15 April 1881 (091.0470–0472): the *Gil Blas* article on Berthe, with «le chèque de cent mille francs que Robert est allé toucher en retenant dix mille francs pour lui»; Marie thinks he had perhaps already «songé à en trafiquer» before marrying her: «ce sale personnage».
