# Mme Gauthreau

## Overview

Mme Gauthereau (Marie's spelling at 103.0551: «Gautereau»), a society beauty in Paris, hostess of a Wednesday salon; the Mon Journal t.14 index has «Gauthereau (Mme) : 278». Probably the same woman as the «Mme Gautereau» of 103.0551 who has a nose «à l'antique» (an inference from the name and the 'beauty' context).

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Spelling variants (Gauthreau/Gauthereau/Gautereau). The diary does not say that the 1882 and 1884 women are the same, though both are beauties. No outside biography found.

## References in Diary

At the end of the soirée of 19 January 1882 (the evening at the comtesse de Kessler's) Marie meets her: «nous sommes invités à ses mercredis»; sitting near her she looks into her bodice «et j'ai vu que la forme est vraiment divine. Des pieds courts et gros à ce qu'il m'a semblé, ce serait dommage» (094.0190, 094.0191). In March 1884 Marie lists her among the beauties with the Empress Eugénie and Mme de Pourtalès (103.0551).
