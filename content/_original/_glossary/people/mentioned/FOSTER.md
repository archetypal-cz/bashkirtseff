# Foster family

## Overview

An English family Marie meets at Ostend in August 1874 (carnets 023–028: «les gentils Foster», several sons including «Foster 2» who dances with her, a daughter, and «le grand chien des Foster»). The daughter Florence becomes Marie's correspondent and, from 1878, Mme Lacon ([FLORENCE_LACON](FLORENCE_LACON.md)); her brother Arthur Foster («celui du chien», 080.0305; t.11 index «Foster (Arthur) : 259, 260, 263-265») is in Paris with the Lacons in May–June 1878 (080.0322, 080.0360, 080.0373, 080.0383).

In the letters of 1877 (072.0407, 072.0412, 072.0422) «Foster» is Florence herself; those tags are better placed on FLORENCE_LACON (not changed here: outside this sweep's range).
