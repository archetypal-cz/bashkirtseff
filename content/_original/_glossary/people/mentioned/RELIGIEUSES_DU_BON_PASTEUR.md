# Les religieuses du Bon Pasteur (Marie's signature)

## Overview
The signature Marie (with Collignon) put on prank cards and a letter to the Audiffrets in Nice, December 1875 – January 1876. On 24 December 1875 sœur Thérèse of the Bon Pasteur convent left them two real visiting cards of «les religieuses du Bon Pasteur» (050.1185, the real convent: COUVENT_DU_BON_PASTEUR); the next day Emile d'Audiffret told her that he had received «une lettre, une carte de visite: Les religieuses du Bon Pasteur. P.P.C.» (050.1252). In January 1876 she wrote to old Léon d'Audiffret in the nuns' name that his son's heart had been brought to them, stuffed, and kept in their alms-box (053.0004, 053.0006).

The real nuns and their convent (050.1185, 056.0090, 059.0960, 060.0578, 063.0009–0010) belong to COUVENT_DU_BON_PASTEUR, not here.
