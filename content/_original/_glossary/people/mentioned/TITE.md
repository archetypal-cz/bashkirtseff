# Tite

## Overview
A name Marie quotes once in September 1875. It is probably a spelling variant of [Tiste](TISTE.md), the comic actor of the Théâtre français at Nice (0.5), whose catch-phrases she often repeats in this period; the Mon Journal t.5–6 indexes have no «Tite» (the t.5–t.13 «Tite-Live» entries concern the historian Livy). The entry is distinct from Livy.

## Relevance to Marie
24 September 1875 (Nice): on realising that a man now knows they have taken an interest in him («à présent, c'est une certitude»), she says: «Je suis flambée, comme dit Tite. Tout est découvert» (044.0144). *Flambé* is slang for ruined, done for. If Tite is Tiste, the phrase would be one of the actor's lines in the repertoire of that season.
