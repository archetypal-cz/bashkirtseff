## Honoré (horse dealer)

%% GLO_HONORE.0002 %%
A horse dealer on the avenue Montaigne, Paris. On 26 September 1874 Marie and her mother visit two dealers there, «Bertellet et Honoré, je crois» (the names are unsure), and find the horses unworthy of her, especially at Honoré's, where one has something odd about its hind legs (024.0119). Only mention.
