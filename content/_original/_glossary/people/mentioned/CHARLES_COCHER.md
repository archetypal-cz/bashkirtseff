## Charles, Larderel's coachman

%% GLO_CHARLES_COCHER.0002 %%
Coachman and confidant of the Florentine Count Alexandre de Larderel ([#Larderei](LARDEREI.md)) in Naples, winter 1877. Marie, who is infatuated with the count, questions him through her maid Rosalie; the coachman is "très libre avec" his master and tells him everything, while Marie jokes that the count obeys him: she calls him "Monsieur le cocher", set against "Monsieur le comte".
