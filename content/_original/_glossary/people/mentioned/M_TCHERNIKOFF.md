# M. Tchernikoff

**Research Status**: Stub
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-31
**Diary Coverage**: Carnet 005 (June 1873, Nice)

## Overview

A Russian man referred to as "M. Tchernikoff" in the Nice social circle of June 1873. He appears in two distinct roles: as a social visitor to the Bashkirtseff household, and as the physician traveling with the Berlikoff family when they depart for Russia on June 15, 1873. Marie notes he and the family are known to the Bashkirtseffs, which is why Solominka Markevitch (whose health was uncertain) is placed under his care for the journey.

## Diary References

- **June 8, 1873** (005.0213): "M. Tchernikoff est venu nous voir" — visits the Bashkirtseff home; he and Walitsky plan to go to the Folies Niçoises theatre.
- **June 10, 1873** (005): Mentioned alongside Mme Teplakoff and Mme Daniloff.
- **June 15, 1873** (005.0278): The Berlikoff family departs Nice for Russia "avec leur médecin Tchernikoff"; Solominka travels with them because "il y aura quelqu'un pour la soigner."

## Research Notes

Spelling "Tchernikoff" (as opposed to "Tchernichoff") used consistently across multiple entries. May be the same person as M. Tchernichoff (see that entry), or a different individual. Doctor by profession; part of the Russian expatriate community in Nice.
