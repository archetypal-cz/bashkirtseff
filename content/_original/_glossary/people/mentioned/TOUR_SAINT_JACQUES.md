# À la Tour Saint-Jacques

## Overview
In the list in which Marie uses it, a Paris shop: «au Louvre, au Bon Marché, au Petit Saint-Thomas, au Grand Saint-Paul, à la Tour Saint-Jacques, au Nez rose, à la Cheville tournée !!!» (019.0229): the first four are department stores and drapers, so the Tour Saint-Jacques is, in this context, probably a shop of the same kind, named after the tower, not the Gothic tower itself (the surviving tower of the church of Saint-Jacques-de-la-Boucherie, restored under the Second Empire by Théodore Ballu; Paris Unlocked, «Tour Saint-Jacques»). We searched for a *nouveautés* shop of this name and found none, so the reading is not confirmed.

## Relevance to Marie
Marie complains on 16 May 1874 that Dina is «insupportable avec son amour des *boutiques*» and that they have been through half of Paris's shops from one to four o'clock. The entry is filed among the people; see also [PETIT_SAINT_THOMAS](PETIT_SAINT_THOMAS.md), [GRAND_SAINT_PAUL](GRAND_SAINT_PAUL.md) and [LOUVRE](../../places/buildings/LOUVRE.md) (the museum entry; Marie means the department store of the same name, see the footnote).
