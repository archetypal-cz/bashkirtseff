# Tiste (the actor)

## Overview
Tiste, a comic actor of the Théâtre français at Nice in 1875–77: Marie calls him «Tiste du Théâtre français de Nice» (068.0797), and the Mon Journal indexes list «Tiste (M.), acteur» (t.6 pp. 44, 77, 110; t.9 p. 277) and, for 1882, «Tiste (boutique) : 292» (t.14). The name is the usual stage short form of Baptiste; the actor's full name is not given. See [THEATRE_FRANCAIS_NICE](THEATRE_FRANCAIS_NICE.md).

## Relevance to Marie
- **Nice, 1875.** In *La Boule* he has a refrain that Marie quotes to answer her family's «palinodies» (034.0399; see [LA_BOULE](../../culture/theater/LA_BOULE.md)). In September–October 1875 Marie sits in a stage box at the Théâtre français and Tiste «dit toutes ses bêtises à notre loge» (045.0320); she imitates the "Si !" that makes the house laugh and enjoys exchanging looks with the actors (046.0101). In Lecocq's *Giroflé-Girofla* he plays «le père Boléro-Tiste» (047.0032), and she calls the operetta «Giro» for short because Tiste does (047.0166, marginal note). On 27 October she recites from *Alice de Nevers* (see [ALICE_DE_NEVERS](../../culture/theater/ALICE_DE_NEVERS.md)): «Oui papa, que répondent les huit filles de Tiste» (048.0120). See [GIROFLE_GIROFLA](../../culture/theater/GIROFLE_GIROFLA.md).
- **1877.** She compares a young man to Audiffret or «Tiste du Théâtre français de Nice» for his teasing (068.0797).
- **1882.** At the Nice carnival (entry of 21 February 1882) «nous nous rendons chez Tiste qui nous loue des dominos de calicots à pois rouges» (094.0284), a costume shop (the Mon Journal t.14 index has «Tiste (boutique)»). Whether the shopkeeper is the actor is not stated (the same name, the same town); the tag is kept here pending that question.
