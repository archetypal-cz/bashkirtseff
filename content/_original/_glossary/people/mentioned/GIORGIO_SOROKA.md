## Giorgio, the Soroka of Rome (1876)

%% GLO_GIORGIO_SOROKA.0002 %%
The young man who followed Marie and Dina in Rome in early 1876 and whom she nicknames "le Soroka de Rome", after Soroka of Nice ([#Soroka](SOROKA.md)); she learns he is "un certain Giorgio, un horticulteur je crois".
