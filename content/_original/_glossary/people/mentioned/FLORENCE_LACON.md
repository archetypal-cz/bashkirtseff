# Florence Lacon (née Foster)

## Overview

Florence Foster, an English girl Marie has known since the summer of 1874 at Ostend (where the Foster family appears often, carnets 023–028; [FOSTER](FOSTER.md)) and with whom she corresponds in 1877 («Voici une lettre de Foster», 072.0407). In early 1878 she married M. Lacon, «futur lord Lacon je crois» (080.0305, 27 May 1878: «Miss Foster, mariée depuis trois mois à M. Lacon»). The Mon Journal indexes: t.11 «Lacon (Mme Florence), née Foster : 259-261, 268»; t.12 «Lacon (Mme Florence), née Foster [Flo]»; t.13 «Lacon (Mme Florence, née Foster) : 18, 22, 24»; her brother «Foster (Arthur)» (t.11, t.12).

## Relevance to Marie

- May–June 1878: in Paris for the Exposition with the Lacons and her brother Arthur; she takes Marie to dine at Ledoyen and to the circus (080.0321–0322), and they meet at the Châtelet (080.0360) and dine together (080.0373).
- July–October 1878: letters from London and invitations to visit (081.0491; 082.0299, 082.0379, 082.0433, 082.0514).
- May 1879: Florence and her husband dine with the Bashkirtseffs in Paris (085.0139, 085.0180, 085.0184, 085.0198).

## Identity

Which Lacon she married (the family of the Lacon baronets of Great Yarmouth is the obvious candidate, given «Lady Lacon» at 080.0322) was not established (0.5).
