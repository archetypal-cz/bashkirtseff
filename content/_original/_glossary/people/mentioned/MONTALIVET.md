# Comte de Montalivet

## Overview

The Montalivet who appears in Marie's diary in May 1880 is most likely a descendant of Marthe Camille Bachasson, 3rd Comte de Montalivet (24 April 1801 – 4 January 1880), the French statesman who served as Minister of the Interior under Louis-Philippe and later as Intendant General of the Civil List. The 3rd Comte had recently died (January 1880); the person at the Salon/social circuit in May 1880 could be the heir (4th Comte) or another member of the family. The context — briefly encountered as a social acquaintance alongside Gavini — does not allow fuller identification.

## Historical Context

The Bachasson de Montalivet family was one of the prominent Orléanist noble families. Their eminence under Louis-Philippe gave them enduring social prestige in Third Republic Paris.

## References in Diary

- 1880-05-28: "Nous sortons avec Gavini que nous laissons avec Montalivet et allons essayer des robes." (We go out with Gavini, whom we leave with Montalivet, and go to try on dresses.)
