# Victor (French manservant, 1876)

## Overview
A French manservant in the household at Gavronzi in the autumn of 1876. Marie: «Le domestique français Victor est tombé malade, on dit qu'il est rouge et enflé» (066.0524, 066/1876-09-28). The Mon Journal t.9 index lists «Victor (domestique français)» separately from «Victor, chien». His surname is not given.

Not the same as [Victor](VICTOR.md), Marie's big black dog of 1874 onward, who is in Nice with the other dogs in December 1876 (067.1394). In Russia in 1876 a bare «Victor» needs checking in context.

## Relevance to Marie
- 28 September 1876: his illness frightens her into feeling feverish herself (066.0524).
- 7 November 1876: she sends him to fetch Paul Apollonovitch from the red house (067.0844).
- 15 November 1876: she means to take him west with the horses, but gives way to her father's valet Kousma (067.0983: «J'allais prendre Victor mais Kousma dévoré du désir de voyages vint supplier»).
