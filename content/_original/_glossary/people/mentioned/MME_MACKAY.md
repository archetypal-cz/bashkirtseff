## Mme Mackay

%% GLO_MME_MACKAY.0002 %%
A lady of the Paris scene of 1883, in the circle of Jules Bastien-Lepage, of whom Marie is jealous and whom she mocks ("la mère Mackay", "la Mackay"). Marie reports the gossip that Mme Mackay is Bastien's mistress and jealous of Marie.
