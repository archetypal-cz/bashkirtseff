# Starzinski

## Overview
A Polish or Polish-sounding family in Nice in 1874. The Mon Journal t.4 index has «Starzinsky (comte), 179, 235» and «Starzinsky (comtesse), 250»; t.2 lists a «Starzinska (Mme), 252». No more is known.

## Relevance to Marie
On 5 December 1874 at the Rumpelmayer café of the Cercle, the ladies' café: «il n'y avait que nous et les Starzinski» (027.0174). On 19 December, among the audience in her verses: «La Starsinsky est en rose» (027.0313).

- 1874-12-05 (027.0174); 1874-12-19 (027.0313)
