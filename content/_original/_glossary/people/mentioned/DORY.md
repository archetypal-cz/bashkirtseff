# Dory

## Overview
A female singer of the Italian opera company at the Nice Opéra in the seasons 1874-75 and 1875-76, in mezzo/contralto roles. Marie calls her "la bonne chanteuse Dory" (028.0113). First name not given.

## Relevance to Marie
- 1875-01-02 (028.0119): Bellini's *I Capuleti e i Montecchi* ("Roméo et Juliette") given "au bénéfice de Dory"; Romeo is a trouser role for mezzo.
- 1875-02-22 (030.0058): *Il barbiere*, "et Dory chante 'La Rosina'".
- 1875-08-27/28 (040.0140): Marie recalls Dory as Azucena (*Il trovatore*): "Jamais Dory en jouant 'Azucena' n'a dit avec plus de haine ... mi vendicar !"
- 1875-12-14 (050.0756): again Rosina in *Il barbiere*; in the lesson scene she sings "Il segreto" (probably the brindisi from Donizetti's *Lucrezia Borgia*), after which "le ténor" sings Marie's romance *Dormi pure*.

## Notes
The Mon Journal t.6 index lists "Dory (ténor), 230". The diary shows a woman ("la bonne chanteuse") singing Rosina, Azucena and Romeo, and at 050.0756 Dory and "le ténor" are two different singers; the index is wrong.
