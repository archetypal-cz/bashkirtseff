# Jeanne Daniloff

## Overview
Jeanne, the little daughter of Mme Daniloff of the Russian colony in Nice: «Jeanne la petite fille de Daniloff, une diablesse âgée de huit ans» (049.0410, 14 Nov 1875); Mon Journal t.6 index «Daniloff (Jeanne), 167». She goes to the Tour Audiffret with her boarding-school mistress and entertains Marie and the Sapogenikoff girls with gossip about the Audiffrets (049.0410–0419). Born about 1867.
