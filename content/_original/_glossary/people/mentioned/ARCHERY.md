# Archery (not a person)

## Overview

«Archery» is the sport, not a person or a place. On 10 November 1874 Marie writes: «J'ai écrit à Foster pour Aunt Sally et Archery, j'ai répondu aux Tamancheff. Voilà deux choses qui me pesaient sur la conscience !» (025.0198): she has written to Arthur Foster (see FOSTER) about two English outdoor games, Aunt Sally (explained in footnote 025.0198.1) and archery. Nothing more is said of the games.

Archery is a common noun and, under the owner ruling of 2026-10-22, common nouns are not tagged; the entry has no function beyond that one tag.
