# Princes Victor and Basile Kotchoubey (Dikanka, 1882)

## Who they are
The two young princes Kotchoubey of [Dikanka](../../places/residences/DIKANKA.md), neighbours of Gavrontsi, whom Marie's family hopes to marry her to in the autumn of 1882: «les princes Kotchoubey, ils sont deux; jeunes, orphelins, chevaliers-gardes et immensément riches» (096.0014, 4 August 1882). The elder is **Victor**, the younger **Basile** (Vassily): «L'aîné Victor est mince, brun… Le cadet Basile est aussi grand, beaucoup plus gros, très blond» (096.0407); «le cadet a vingt-et-un ans à peine» (096.0462). Their «quasi-sœur» (097.0046) is Mme Volkovitsky, the natural daughter of Prince Serge Kotchoubey (066.0244), who comes «avec ses frères de Dikanka» (097.0045): they are thus the two sons of [Prince Serge Kotchoubey](PRINCE_SERGE_KOTCHOUBEY.md), the widower Marie met at Dikanka in 1876. The Mon Journal t.15 index lists «Kotchoubey (prince Basile)», «Kotchoubey (prince Victor)» and «Kotchoubey (les)»; t.16 «Kotchoubey (prince Basile) : 128» and his wife «née Serran».

They are very probably Prince Viktor Sergeyevich Kochubey (1860-1923) and Prince Vasily Sergeyevich Kochubey (1862-1911), Chevalier-Guards officers (genealogical sources cited in [Pomar](POMAR.md)); Marie's first names, ages, regiment and Dikanka all agree (0.8).

## «Les Pomar»
Marie uses «Pomar», the name of the Duke de Pomar, the rich catch of Nice in the spring of 1882, as a joke name for them: «moi et Basile Kotchoubey… Ils ont bien du voir que je suis jolie ces deux Pomar de malheur» (096.0470); «les deux Kotchoubey… même Pomar cadet est assez amusant» (097.0045); «Les Pomar partent demain pour Pétersbourg» (097.0046). The t.15 index has a separate heading «Pomar (les) : 95-96, 103, 105, 107, 111-113» on the same pages as the Kotchoubey entries. They are not the Duke de Pomar.

## In the diary
- **August-September 1882** (096.0014, 096.0184, 096.0199): the plan; Basile «est celui qui avait volé mon portrait à Michka» (096.0199).
- **October 1882**, Gavrontsi and Dikanka (096.0328-0473): the long wait (096.0384-0400), the visit of 19 October with [Michka Eristoff](MICHKA_ERISTOFF.md) (096.0406-0413), the hunt cancelled (096.0422-0427), the baptism of Paul's son on 29 October (096.0459-0473), Victor's long talk «sur les Tartares» (096.0472).
- **November 1882**, Poltava (097.0006-0089): dinner at Dikanka (097.0009), the evenings at the hotel (097.0044-0046), their departure for St Petersburg; Marie's bitter «on ne trouve jamais en cherchant des Pomar» (097.0053); jokes about Basile on the journey (097.0066-0076).
- **1884-01-23** (103.0100-0101): «C'est aujourd'hui que s'est marié Basile Kotchoubey, il a épousé Melle Serran, la fille du maréchal ambassadeur» — Marshal Francisco Serrano, Duke de la Torre, Spanish ambassador in Paris in 1883-84.
