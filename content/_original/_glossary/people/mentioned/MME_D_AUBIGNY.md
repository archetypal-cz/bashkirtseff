# Mme d Aubigny

## Overview

Mme d'Aubigny, wife of the secretary of the French embassy in Berlin in spring 1881; the Mon Journal t.14 index has «Aubigny d'M., secrétaire Ambassade de France à Berlin : 141» and «Aubigny d'Mme : 141».

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Forenames not found; the date and place from the diary.

## References in Diary

On 26 May 1881 at Berlin, Gabriel brought an invitation from Mme d'Aubigny; the Bashkirtseffs did not go, so he arranged a meeting with her in the «Bois de Berlin» where Marie also met a Madame of the Belgian embassy and M. de Savigny of the French embassy (092.0061).
