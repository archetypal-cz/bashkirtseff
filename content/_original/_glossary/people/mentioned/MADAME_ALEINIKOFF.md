## Mme Aleinikoff

%% GLO_MADAME_ALEINIKOFF.0002 %%
A Russian lady met at the hotel at Wiesbaden in August 1875. Marie takes her daughters to Rauenthal on 22 August: «Je mène les filles de Mme Aleinikoff, une Russe qu'on a connue à l'hôtel, à Rauenthal, hauteur d'où l'on admire le Rhin» (039.0193); her balcony touches that of Prince Mechtchersky, which is the reason Marie goes to see her (039.0217); she spends her last evening with the Bashkirtseffs (039.0224, 23 Aug). Mon Journal t.5 index: «Aleinikoff (Mme)», pp. 257, 259.
