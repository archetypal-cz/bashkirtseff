# Poloubotok

## Overview
Spelling of the name of Pavlo Polubotok (c. 1660–1724), acting hetman of Left-Bank Ukraine from 1722, whom Marie's family counted among its ancestors. The full entry, with the legend of his gold, is [Hetman Poloubotok](HETMAN_POLOUBOTOK.md).

## Relevance to Marie
On 24 October 1874 she begins to write the family history: «Mon grand-père russe du côté paternel et petit-russien du côté maternel est descendant par les femmes de Poloubotok» (024.0396). It is too long for an evening, so she starts a separate notebook (we know of no such notebook in the edition). The Mon Journal t.4 index glosses the name: «Poloubotok, grand hetman, ancêtre de la famille Babanine, 141, 183». Note that she says here that the grandfather is Russian on the paternal side and *petit-russien* (Ukrainian) on the maternal side and descends from Polubotok through women; the index places the ancestor on the Babanine line.
