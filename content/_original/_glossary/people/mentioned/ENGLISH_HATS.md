# English Hats

*English Hats*, a hatter's shop in Paris where Marie buys a riding cap on the day of departure from Paris (entry of 1874-06-22, 020.0376): «Le lendemain jour de départ, nous allâmes chez English Hats, j'ai acheté une calotte de jockey en velours ...». The same paragraph records the visit to Caroline (Laferrière) and Dusautoy's failure to fix her riding habit. No address was found.
