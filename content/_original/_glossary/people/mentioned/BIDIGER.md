# Bidiger

## Overview

M. Bidiger (Mon Journal t.3 index: «Bidiger (M.), 199»), who left Paris with Mme Angel in May 1874. On 23 May 1874 Marie writes: «J'étais avec maman chez Mme Angel, elle part demain et nous prenons un appartement dans cet hôtel, elle part avec Bidiger, et nous prenons l'appartement de cette vieille» (019.0323). Whether he is her husband, a relative or a companion is not stated; Mme Angel's husband M. Angel arrives separately in Nice in November 1874 (see ANGEL, MME_ANGEL).

## Identity uncertain

We are not sure who this man was. The name is given once in the diary and once in the index. If you can identify him, we would be glad to hear from you.
