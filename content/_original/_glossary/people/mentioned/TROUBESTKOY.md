# Troubetskoy (Easter 1881)

## Overview
Members of the Troubetskoy (Troubetzkoy) princely family in Paris in 1881; the diary names only the surname. They are with Mme de Peyronney on Orthodox Easter night, 23 April 1881. The separate entries [TROUBETZKOY](TROUBETZKOY.md) and [PRINCESSE_TROUBETZKOY](PRINCESSE_TROUBETZKOY.md) cover other spellings; whether the same people are meant is not established.

## Relevance to Marie
After the Easter service at the Russian church, with the ambassador present: «Mme de Peyronney était là avec les Troubestkoy, mon père s'est embrassé avec l'ambassadeur et m'a fait faire sa connaissance» (091.0520). The society column «Le carnet d'un mondain» signed «Étincelle» in the Figaro will describe these splendours (see [ETINCELLE](../writers/ETINCELLE.md)).

## Identity uncertain
No first names are given. A family of Troubetskoy lived in Paris in the period (the Mon Journal t.9–t.14 indexes list «Troubetskoy (princesse Lise)» and her daughter Alexandra); the Troubetskoys of Easter 1881 may be Princess Lise and her daughters, but this is a guess. If you can identify them, we would be glad to hear from you.
