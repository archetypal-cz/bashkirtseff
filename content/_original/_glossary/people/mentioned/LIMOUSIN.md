## Limousin (journalist)

%% GLO_LIMOUSIN.0002 %%
Nice society journalist. The Mon Journal t.6 index has «Limousin (M.), rédacteur», t.7 «Limousin (M.), journaliste». On 7 November 1875 he is noticed, «rédacteur du Journal des Décavés» (049.0075; see LES_DECAVES), and watches Marie's party play (049.0083). Marie jokes about «le journal du grand Limousin», whose paragraph asks for an article on the copying of a certain white toilette (054.0303, 19 Feb 1876). On 16 Feb 1882 «Limousin de la "Vie Mondaine" (le Figaro de Nice)» enters during dinner and praises her carnival float; Marie shakes his hand as a colleague (094.0271).
