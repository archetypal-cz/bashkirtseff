# Dom Fabrice

## Overview
An invented monk's name that Marie signed to an anonymous letter of 21 January 1876, sent under cover to be passed on «au fichu Emile» d'Audiffret; the letter mocks Audiffret as the «vil coq» ridden by a departed Don Quixote. One of the masks of the Audiffret campaign (see MOINES_DE_CIMIEZ).
