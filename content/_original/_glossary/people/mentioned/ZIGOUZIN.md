# Zigouzin (nickname of Rosalie Léon)

## Overview

«La bella Zigouzin» is, on the evidence of the Mon Journal indices, Marie's nickname for **Rosalie Léon**, the Nice demi-mondaine who was the mistress of Prince Wittgenstein and whom Marie also calls «la Wittgenstein». The t.3 index reads «Léon (Mme Rosalie) [Zigouzin] [La Wittgenstein] demi-mondaine, 16-18, 36-37, 44, ...» (the t.1 index: «Wittgenstein (la), (Mme Rosalie Léon), maîtresse du prince, 30»). The single paragraph that uses the nickname fits: she is driving in a bath-chair on the Promenade, and Marie supposes the husband has beaten her.

## In Marie's diary

- 1874-01-12 (015.0129, Nice): on the Promenade, «la bella Zigouzin se promenait en voiture de malade. Son époux l'a battue, nous supposons.» In the same sentence Marie has just seen Wittgenstein with Lambertye.
