# Marechale

%% GLO_MARECHALE.0001 %%
**To be retired.** "La maréchale" has no identity of its own. Of the 17 files that carry this tag, 16 (carnets 102 and 103, December 1883 to April 1884) mean [Maréchale Canrobert](MARECHALE_CANROBERT.md) and should be retargeted there. The two paragraphs of 1 July 1877 (072.0362 and 072.0363, the military review, "la maréchale dans la tribune du centre faisant l'impératrice") mean the wife of President Mac-Mahon, Élisabeth de Beaumont-Castries, Maréchale de Mac-Mahon, duchesse de Magenta; they belong to a separate entry or to [#Mac Mahon](MAC_MAHON.md), not to the Canrobert family.
