# Schablikine (Jean Chablikine)

## Overview
A young Russian (Mon Journal t.4 index: «Schablikine (Jean), 230, 250»; the t.10 index has «Chablikine (M.), 182, 212»), among the Russian colony of Nice in 1874. The glossary has a second stub, [CHABLIKINE](CHABLIKINE.md), for the same name. Marie spells it Schablikine and Shablikine.

## Relevance to Marie
On 2 December 1874 Mme Markevitch brings to Marie's mother what «Schablikine qui sort de chez elle» has said: that the foreign colony, tired of Georges Babanine's scandals and above all his affair with Prodgers, wants to ask the new prefect, M. Decrais, to expel him from the Alpes-Maritimes (027.0121; see [DECRAIS](DECRAIS.md), [PRODGERS](PRODGERS.md)). On 19 December he is at the theatre among the young men in the stalls, in Marie's verses: «Polandoff, d'Olivier, Shablikine Jean» (027.0325).
