## Jacques (the sitter of 1884)
%% 2026-10-22T22:16:12 RSR: The entry was split; the other referent is tagged elsewhere or carries an RSR note. %%
A little boy who sat to Marie in Paris in January 1884: «Il est mauvais ce portrait de Jacques... Je fais trop de fonds dessus» (103.0154, 27 January); «Fini Jacques ! Et comme son frère est venu le chercher, un bébé de trois ans, j'en fais une ébauche» (103.0187, 31 January). The picture is the *Portrait de Jacques* (see PORTRAIT_JACQUES); the boy is not otherwise identified.

The chimpanzee «Jacques» of 12 June 1874 (020.0217, «Ces fameux chimpanzés Jacques et Louise») is not this person; that paragraph carries an RSR note and no tag.
