# Aglaé (Marie Sapogenikoff)

## Overview

«Aglaé» is the name of one of the Three Graces that Marie Bashkirtseff and the two Sapogenikoff sisters took for their secret society in Nice (A.T.E., Aglaé–Thalie–Euphrosine, founded Sunday 9 May 1875). Marie writes: «Moi - Thalie, Marie - Aglaé et Olga Euphrosine. Voyez-vous cela, les trois Grâces» (032/1875-05-15). So Aglaé is **Marie Sapogenikoff**, the elder sister, and Euphrosine her sister Olga. The nickname is therefore an alias of [Marie Sapogenikoff](MARIE_SAPOGENIKOFF.md), not a separate person.

The Mon Journal indexes are not consistent: t.5 gives «Aglaé : Sapogenikoff (Marie)», which agrees with the text, but t.7 has «Aglaé: Sapogenikoff Olga». The diary text (032, 059 reader footnote and regulations) is followed here.

## Relevance to Marie

- **30 May 1875.** On the day of a great procession from the church of Saint-Pierre she goes «à la musique avec ma tante, Aglaé et Euphrosine» (033.0358; the printed text reads «Agiaé et Eupnrosine», an OCR slip).
- The society's own papers use the names: the initials T.A.E. and A.T.E. (053/1876-02-04, 056/1876-04-03) and the 1876 regulations copied on 3 May 1876 («les deux membres fondatrices Aglaé et Euphrosine», 059.0690; «Aglaé, Euphrosine, membres fondatrices», signature block).
