# Nillsson

## Overview

A woman called «la Nillsson» in two paragraphs: a crossed-out remark in 1880 that Mlle de Cerny «ressemble beaucoup à la Nillsson» (087.0394; LAN: probably the Swedish soprano Christina Nilsson, 1843–1921), and, in 1883, a woman at the Mont-Dore with whom Saint-Amand warned the family not to talk, because «elle se croit un homme» (101.0218). The Mon Journal t.16 index has «Nillsson (Mme) : 22».

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. The two Nillssons are not shown to be the same woman. Candidate for 087.0394: the singer Christina Nilsson (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christina_Nilsson), common knowledge, unchecked against the manuscript.

## Identity uncertain

We are not sure who this is. In 1880 Marie crosses out a comparison with «la Nillsson», most likely the singer Christina Nilsson; in 1883 «la Nillsson» is a woman at the Mont-Dore of whom Saint-Amand said she believed herself to be a man. We cannot say whether the 1883 woman is the singer (the Mon Journal index says «Mme»). If you can identify her, we would be glad to hear from you.

## References in Diary

In 1880 (087.0394) the resemblance is to the singer's look, the only surviving fragment being a deletion. On 20 August 1883 Marie writes that she regrets knowing the rumour, which she finds shameful and ridiculous, and recalls hearing the like about the princess Souvoroff (101.0218).
