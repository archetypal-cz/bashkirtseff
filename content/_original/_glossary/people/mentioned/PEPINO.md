# Pepino

«Pepino» (also «Pépino») is the nickname of Albert-Joseph Gautier of Nice, a member of Audiffret's circle (Kernberger 2013, glossary: "Gautier, Albert-Joseph: ... nickname: Pepino"). In carnet 068 Marie uses both names for the same man in one evening (068.0481-0490: «le frère de Gautier *Michka*» / «mon frère, dit Pepino»).

See the main entry: [ALBERT_GAUTIER](ALBERT_GAUTIER.md).
