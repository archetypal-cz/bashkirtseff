## Domenico Antonelli, "le Sauvage"

%% GLO_DOMINIQUE_ANTONELLI.0002 %%
A brother of Pietro Antonelli; in Marie's private zoo of Roman suitors he is "le Sauvage", next to Paolo "le Lion", Torlonia "le Tigre" and Pietro "le Moine".
