# A. Foster (Arthur Foster)

## Overview

«A. Foster» is Arthur Foster, a son of the English Foster family that Marie met at Ostend in August 1874; he is the brother of Florence Foster (later Mme Lacon), «celui du chien» (see FOSTER and FLORENCE_LACON). The Mon Journal t.5 index has «Foster A (fils), 309–310» and t.11 «Foster (Arthur) : 259, 260, 263–265». The entry FOSTER already says that «A. Foster» in the 1874 paragraphs is her brother Arthur; the stub ARTHUR_FOSTER is a second entry for him.

## Relevance to Marie

On 14 October 1874 (Nice) Marie writes of the art dealer Gambart, who has just spent an hour with the family: «Ah ! si Dina devenait Mme Gambart. Pour moi c'est trop faquin et vieux, mais pour elle ce serait quelque chose de magnifique, comme dit A. Foster» (024.0343). Foster, an Englishman whom she knows well enough to quote on a marriage question, appears elsewhere as correspondent (letters about the dog, Aunt Sally and archery, 025.0198).
