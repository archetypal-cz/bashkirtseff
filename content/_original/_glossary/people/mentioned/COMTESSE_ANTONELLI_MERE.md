## The comtesse Antonelli, Pietro's mother

%% GLO_COMTESSE_ANTONELLI_MERE.0002 %%
Mother of Pietro Antonelli and his brothers, in Marie's account a once elegant and much talked-about woman, dominated by her husband, who at the Roman courtship of 1876 pronounces a speech about her son and later conveys, through intermediaries, her wish for the match.
