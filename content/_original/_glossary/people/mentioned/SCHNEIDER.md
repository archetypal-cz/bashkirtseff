# Hortense Schneider

## Overview
Hortense Schneider (1833–1920), the most celebrated operetta soprano of the Second Empire, creator of the title roles in Offenbach's *La Belle Hélène*, *La Grande-Duchesse de Gérolstein* and *La Périchole* (1868) (en.wikipedia «Hortense Schneider»). The Mon Journal t.3 index has «Schneider (Hortense), cantatrice, 195».

## Relevance to Marie
On 17 May 1874 in Paris: «Le soir aux Variétés "La Périchole" avec Schneider. Elle est adorable» (019.0266). The only mention.
