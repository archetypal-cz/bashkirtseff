# Durocher

Stub entry — needs research.

## Identity
Mme Durocher, mistress of Marie's father Constantin Bashkirtseff in 1876: the Mon Journal t.9 index has "Durocher (Mme), maîtresse de Constantin Bashkirtseff". In October 1876 Marie guesses that her father's bad mood comes from "une querelle avec sa maîtresse" (067.0106), and learns that Hélène Gorpintchenko has driven out to "la Durocher" (067.0109).
