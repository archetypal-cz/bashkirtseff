# Mme Daniloff

## Overview

Mme Daniloff, a Russian lady of the Nice and Paris colony, mother of a «petite fille» (see [Jeanne Daniloff](JEANNE_DANILOFF.md)), a friend of the Bashkirtseff household from 1873 to the late 1870s. The Mon Journal indexes list «Daniloff (Mme)» in t.1–t.10 (e.g. t.4 pp.168, 175, 190, 192, 196, 209).

## References in Diary

Marie's view is mixed. On 17 May 1873 she is among the callers at the Bashkirtseffs' (004.0375). On 4 October 1874 she lunches with her little girl, «une excellente femme» (024.0229); on 11 October she is present at the quarrel about Aunt's money (024.0293). On 4 November 1874, however, Marie writes that her presence at a dinner of ten «compromet en société» and that «sans parler de son passé, dans son présent elle ne se conduit pas comme il convient» (025.0105), while thanking her for her kindness; Mme Angel has taken lodgings near her (025.0108).
