## Zucchini, a young Roman of the Caccia-Club (1876)

%% GLO_ZUCCHINI.0002 %%
Marquis or count Zucchini, one of the young Roman nobles of the Caccia-Club "barcaccia" (with Pandola, Belmonte, Cesaro), a figure of fun for Marie and for Pietro Antonelli, who shares the joke. She first takes him for the man she had called "Cesaro".
