# Turcan and Don Fortunato (Marie's signatures)

## Overview
On 22 January 1877, in Nice, Marie wrote two anonymous letters to herself in printed capitals and posted them to «Mlle Baschkirtseff», an idea Dina gave her (068.0782, 068.0785); she later showed them about as if from an unknown correspondent. The first, «Avec tes robes blanches tu as l'air d'une réclame de candeur ! Quitte le monde !», is signed «Don Fortunato» (068.0783); the second, «Arrivé depuis peu aux Enfers j'y ai rencontré ton âme», is signed «Turcan» (068.0784). Turcan was a murderer executed in Nice in December 1876; she had already threatened Audiffret that «l'âme de Turcan, celui qu'on a exécuté hier pour cause d'assassinat, est allée aux enfers et... qu'elle t'écrira» (068.0065, 15 December 1876). The executed man himself has no separate entry; tag the signature and the «âme de Turcan» here.

Not FORTUNATA or FORTUNE (other people).
