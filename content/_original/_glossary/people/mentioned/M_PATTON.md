# M Patton

## Identity
The Mon Journal t.6 index lists "Patton (Oscar), consul de Russie" and "Patton (Mme), née Savelieff". Marie calls him "M. le consul Patton" and "le beau consul" at the Nice Opéra on 11 December 1875 (050.0674, 050.0675), next to "ses laides filles". His wife is the daughter of Mme Savelieff, whose death in May 1873 Marie records (see PATTON_FAMILY).

## Notes
- The Pattons are the Russian consul's household at Nice (Mon Journal t.2 and t.6 indexes); no diary line calls them American. His wife has her own entry, [Mme Patton](../aristocracy/MME_PATTON.md). Owner ruling A-40 (2026-11-10): four Patton entries are kept (consul, wife, Nathalie, family).
