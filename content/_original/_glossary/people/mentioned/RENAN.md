# Ernest Renan

## Overview
Ernest Renan (1823–1892), French philologist, historian of religion and philosopher, author of the *Vie de Jésus* (1863) and the *Histoire des origines du christianisme*; professor of Hebrew at the Collège de France from 1862 (dismissed 1864, reinstated 1870) and its administrator from 1883 (Britannica, «Ernest Renan»; Collège de France biography). He spoke at the departure ceremony for Turgenev's body in October 1883.

## Relevance to Marie
Renan comes into Marie's pages in 1883–84, when she is reading and seeking a belief.
- **The bust.** In June 1883 she sees at Saint-Marceaux's studio the bust of Renan on which he is working and, the day before, Renan himself in a cab: «Voilà de la ressemblance au moins ! Renan qui a étudié la Judée et le judaïsme... son buste ?» (100.0170–100.0172). The bust and the *Arlequin* are the subject of her letter to Saint-Marceaux in September (101.0346, 101.0407). See [SAINT_MARCEAUX](../artists/SAINT_MARCEAUX.md).
- **Turgenev.** At the farewell ceremony at the station on 1 October 1883 she hears speeches by «M. Renan, de M. About et de Virouboff»; Renan, seen through Saint-Marceaux's bust, «a été très bien et le dernier "adieu" a vibré» (101.0470, 101.0471). See [TOURGUENIEFF](TOURGUENIEFF.md), [VIROUBOFF](VIROUBOFF.md).
- **Belief.** «Monsieur Renan ne croit pas aux miracles, ni moi non plus, et j'en suis bien fâchée» (101.0465, 28 September 1883). On 17 December 1883 she quotes him: «Le monde est si faible d'esprit que la plus pure chose a besoin pour réussir de la coopération d'un agent impur» (102.0502). In February 1884 she thinks about God in terms of «questions scientifico-spiritualistes à la Renan» (103.0389) and writes that she is steeping herself in Renan's writings and in the Bible (103.0399); in May a complete Renan arrives with Zola and Taine (104.0116).

The Mon Journal t.15 index has «Renan (Ernest), écrivain : 286–287»; t.16 «33, 38, 43, 101, 149–150, 192».
