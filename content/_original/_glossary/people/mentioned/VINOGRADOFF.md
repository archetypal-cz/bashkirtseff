# Vinogradoff

## Identity
A merchant and leaseholder of the Poltava country, enriched in the service of the late Thadée Romanoff, ma tante's husband: "Vinogradoff un assez gros marchand enrichi au service de M. Romanoff" (067.0005, 067/1876-10-13); "un des plus importants fermiers" (066.0783, 066/1876-10-11). The Mon Journal t.9 index has "Vinogradoff (M.), fermier". First name not known.

## Relevance to Marie
In October 1876 Marie, at Poltava, sells him timber for 25,000 roubles ("je veux vendre à Vinogradoff du bois pour... vingt cinq mille roubles", 067.0014), against uncle Alexandre's reluctance to sign the contract; Alexandre brings him with the money the next day (067/1876-10-14). On 4 November she signs a power of attorney for him at a notary's in Poltava, "pour continuer... on verra après" (067.0673).
