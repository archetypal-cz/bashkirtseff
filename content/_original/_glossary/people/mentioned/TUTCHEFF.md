# Tutcheff

The Tutcheffs of Nice: Sophie Grigorievna Tutcheff, née Bashkirtseff, and her husband. Marie calls Mme Tutcheff «la sœur de mon père» (068.0117), so she is Marie's **paternal** aunt; Kernberger (2013, glossary) calls her maternal, but this glossary follows Marie. Full entry: [Sophie Tutcheff](SOPHIE.md).
