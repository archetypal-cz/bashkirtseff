## Mme Jacob, a fortune-teller (1880)

%% GLO_MME_JACOB.0002 %%
A fortune-teller Marie consults in Paris in March 1880. Her prophecy ("Vous serez riche, millionnaire"; a man whom Marie reads as Joseph Arnaud) gives Marie a running joke.
