## Identity

Voldemar Potechine, «une très ancienne connaissance de Russie» (059.0434, Rome, April 1876); the Mon Journal t.8 index lists him as «Potechine (M.)».

## References in Diary

- Rome, April–May 1876: escorts Marie and ma tante almost daily (059.0434, 060.0167 the races, 060.0536 and 060.0551 the evening of 18 May, 060/1876-05-20 at the station for their departure).
- 061.0212 (Nice, 27 May 1876): Marie asks him by letter to post four notes to her Roman admirers.
- 066.0432 (Russia, Sept. 1876): a speaker claims to have seen «ce même Potechine» kiss «la tante, Mme Romanoff, mais il est vrai qu'il était son fiancé à cette époque» — probably ma tante Nadine Romanoff (0.7), which would make him her former fiancé.
- 068.0392 (Jan. 1877): Marie remembers «la cordiale figure de Potechine» in Rome.
- 083.0123 (Paris, Nov. 1878): «Potechine et Mme Tarnowsky dînent».
