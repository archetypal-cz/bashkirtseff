# Hippolyte de Villemessant (1810-1879)

## Overview
Hippolyte de Villemessant (1810-1879), French journalist and press magnate, founder and director of the daily *Le Figaro* (relaunched 1854, daily from 1866). He spent his winters on the Riviera and died at Monte Carlo in April 1879 (Wikipedia, "Hippolyte de Villemessant"). Kernberger (2013, glossary): "Villemessant, M. de: man from the newspaper".

## Relevance to Marie
After Robert Milton's insinuating report on the Nice races in *Le Sport* (27 January 1877), the family's lawyer Malausséna advises a quiet word with M. de Villemessant rather than a lawsuit (068.0506). He is in Monaco that evening; Marie's mother sees him the next day, «et on va arranger cela» (068.0508, 068.0516). A week later Marie hears that the *Figaro* itself has printed something about the family (068.0619).
