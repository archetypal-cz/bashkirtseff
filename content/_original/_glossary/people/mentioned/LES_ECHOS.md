## Les Échos de Nice

%% GLO_LES_ECHOS.0002 %%
A Nice newspaper of the 1870s: Marie names it in full as «Les Echos de Nice» on 11 December 1874 (027.0230). On 27 December 1873 it prints an article on the winter sun (014.0243); on 9 January 1874 she finds in it «Le mariage du duc de Hamilton», much like the account in *Galignani* (015.0099). In December 1874 it has been mentioning the tall Miss Macainne, launched by Gambart and Prodgers (027.0230). No outside source consulted for the paper itself.
