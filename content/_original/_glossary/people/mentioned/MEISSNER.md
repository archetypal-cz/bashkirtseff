# Meissner

## Overview
«Un des Meissner»: the brother of Alexis de Meyssner (Meissner), who marries Olga Sapogenikoff in January 1878 (077/1877-12-27: «Olga se marie avec un des Meissner, le frère de celui que j'ai connu à Nice»). Kernberger (2013, glossary) lists him as "Meissner, Baron de: fiancé of Olga, brother of Alexis".

For the brother Marie knew in Nice in January-February 1877, see [ALEXIS_DE_MEYSSNER](ALEXIS_DE_MEYSSNER.md). The two are kept as separate entries.
