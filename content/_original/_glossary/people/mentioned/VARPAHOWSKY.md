# Varpahowsky

## Overview

A man of the family's Nice circle in 1875-76; not identified. The Mon Journal t.7 index has only «Varpahowky (M.) : 319, 326, 329» (spelled without the s).

## Relevance to Marie

- Among the guests of the Christmas supper of 1875: «tout cela pour Bihovetz, Ricardo, Varpahowsky, Pépino et Galula !» (051/1875-12-27).
- Dines with the family on their return from Rome, 28 April 1876 (059.0574), and is still calling with Olga Sapogenikoff on 2 May (059/1876-05-02).
