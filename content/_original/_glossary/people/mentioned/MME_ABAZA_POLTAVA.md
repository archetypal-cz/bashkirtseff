# Mme Abaza (née Dietrich), of Poltava

## Overview
A Poltava lady whom Marie first knew as Mlle Dietrich: «J'ai été chez Mlle Dietrich qui, devenue Mme Abaza, ne m'a pas rendue ma visite» (067.0619, 067/1876-11-02). The Mon Journal t.9 index has «Abaza (Mme) (née Dietrich) : 122, 164-165», separately from «Abaza (M.) : 76, 164, 165». Her first name and her husband's are not given.

## Relevance to Marie
At the concert of the Poltava nobility on 2 November 1876 Marie, offended by the unreturned visit, passes her without a greeting and sits beside her in the front row (067.0618, 067.0619); her father keeps asking «As-tu salué Mme Abaza ?» (067.0623), and M. Abaza (see [Abaza](../aristocracy/ABAZA.md)) insists he returned the call the next day «avec sa nièce» (067.0626).

In November 1882, back in Poltava, Marie calls «chez M. et Mme Abaza, Abaza est un ci-devant millionnaire et dont la maison à Poltava ressemble fort à un palais» (097.0054) and meets Mme Abaza at the amateur theatre; the t.15 index has «Abaza (M.)» and «Abaza (Mme)» on p.113. That this is the same Poltava couple is probable (0.7), since the «exmillionnaire» Abaza is already a Poltava figure in 1876 (066.0244).

## Not the Mme Abaza of Soden
This woman is kept apart from [Mme Abaza](../aristocracy/MME_ABAZA.md), the singer Marie meets at Soden in July 1878 and sees again on the Riviera in 1879 (081–084), for three reasons (0.8):
- in 1876 the Poltava lady has only lately married (Marie knew her as Mlle Dietrich), while the Soden Mme Abaza has a daughter nearly sixteen in August 1878 («finira par le marier avec sa fille dès que celle-ci aura seize ans», 082.0075);
- Marie introduces the Soden woman as a stranger, «D'une Mme Abaza» (081.0390), and never recalls the 1876 snub;
- the Soden woman «obtient de lui [her husband] de rester la plupart du temps à l'étranger» (081.0420), the Poltava one lives in Poltava.
