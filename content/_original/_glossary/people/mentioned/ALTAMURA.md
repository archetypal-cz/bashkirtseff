# Altamura

## Overview

Chevalier Vincenzo Altamura, a Neapolitan banker (Mon Journal t.7 index: «Altamura (Chevalier Vincenzo)»; Kernberger 2013, glossary: «Altamura, Vincenzo: Neapolitan banker, acquaintance in Rome and Naples»). Marie meets him in Rome: «On a fait la connaissance du banquier napolitain, chevalier Altamura» (054/1876-02-27).

## Relevance to Marie

In Naples in April 1876 he is the family's tireless host: he places them by the weighing enclosure at the races, as an officer of the racing society who «préside au pesage» (059.0009), introduces the baron de Saint-Joseph (059.0010), gives a dinner at the hôtel d'Angleterre (059.0063) and sees them off at the station (059.0175). His unexplained generosity puzzles Marie: he pays the cab, brings the race tickets and lost bets in the form of glove boxes, «Que je crève si je devine ce que cet homme veut» (059.0106-0108).
