# Violette

## Overview
Not a separate person: «Violette» is the name Marie signed in her letters to Alexandre de Larderel in Naples in March–April 1877, and the name she uses for herself in them, in the third person. Larderel signed his side «Violet» («Il signe Violet et moi Violette», 072.0538); «Violet» is Larderel and is tagged to LARDEREL, not here. The name plays on the violets she sent and wore that season (the bouquet of violets sent to Larderel with an anonymous card, 068.1307, 11 February 1877; «Je mets quelques violettes dans la lettre», 16 March 1877).
