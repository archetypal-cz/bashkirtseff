# Chilovski

## Overview

A Russian of the Nice winter of 1873–74 (Mon Journal t.3 index: «Chilovski, 19, 84, 91, 256», no first name), who is the type to which Marie compares others by looks and manner: Prince Tchetvertinski «est Chilovski tout à fait, la même manière de parler» (017.0009); another prince «regarde tout à fait comme Chilovski, mais il est grand et porte une barbe courte» (017.0088); Count Merjeewsky «ressemble à Chilovski, a le teint pâle, les cheveux d'un blond cendré» (020.0490).

## Relevance to Marie

On 12 January 1874, in Nice, he shows a photograph of Gioia, the Duke of Hamilton's mistress, the one «où elle est debout»: Marie compares the bent head of the countess de Galve to Gioia's on «la phot. que Chilovski a montrée» (015.0135, with a note that the word is written in Russian in the manuscript).

## Identity uncertain

We are not sure who this is. Chilovski may be the same man as the «petit Chilowsky» of the next year (CHILOWSKY); the two spellings differ only in transliteration, but no source links them. If you can identify him, we would be glad to hear from you.
