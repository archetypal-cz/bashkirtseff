# Mme de Peyronney (Étincelle) and her family

## Overview

In the diary «Peyronney» (also spelled «Peronney») is the family name of the Figaro society columnist who signs «Étincelle», and the tag is used for the woman herself, for her husband and for her daughter. The full biography (Marie Biard, daughter of the painter François-Auguste Biard and of Léonie d'Aunet; later the baronne Double) is in [#Etincelle](../writers/ETINCELLE.md); this entry only records the name forms and which paragraphs concern which member of the household.

**Sure (from the diary itself).** Marie names her in one breath: Saint-Amand prepares a launch in the «"Carnet d'un mondain" signé Etincelle, du "Figaro", que fait Mme de Peronney (le vicomte de Létorières)» (091/1881-03-19, 091.0321); and again «Mme de Peyronney (vicomtesse de Létarière)» (091/1881-04-02, 091.0409). On 11 January 1882 «Mme de Peronney» has called, and in the same entry «Etincelle fait un mot dans son carnet du "Figaro"» (094/1882-01-11, 094.0150). So Mme de Peyronney and Étincelle are one woman, and her husband is a vicomte de Peyronney («de Peyronney ne veut pas en entendre parler», 098/1883-01-24, 098.0149, on the divorce Dauble and Étincelle had tried to obtain).

**Name forms.**
- *Peyronney* / *Peronney* / *Peyronny*: Marie writes the first two; «Peyronny» is the form of the editorial footnotes (source: the Agutte sale catalogue, interencheres.com lot 87767814, as cited in the footnotes of 091/1881-03-19) and of fr.wikipedia, «Marie Biard» (via the ETINCELLE entry), which gives her husband as Vicomte Jules-Louis-Antoine de Peyronny. The contemporary directory *Les Pseudonymes du jour* (Wikisource, «Dominos féminins») has «Étincelle. — Vicomte de Létorières. — Mme de Peyronni». Treat the spelling as variable; no form is authoritative.
- *vicomte de Létorières / vicomtesse de Létarière*: pen-name forms (cf. Georges de Létorière, her name for plays and books per fr.wikipedia); Marie's «Létarière» is her misspelling (091.0409). See [#Letoriere](LETORIERE.md).
- *baronne Double / «Dauble»*: her later liaison and marriage with Baron Lucien Double; in the diary only «le baron Dauble… le quasi-mari d'Etincelle» (098.0149) and «Double» in 1884 (see ETINCELLE). Not a name she bore in 1881–83.
- Not «Gyp»: an earlier footnote at 097.0339 identified her with the comtesse de Martel («Gyp»); it was corrected on 2026-10-21 (OPEN-ITEMS D-43).

## The family in the tagged paragraphs

*Mme de Peyronney / Étincelle herself:* 091.0321 (the «Carnet d'un mondain», Saint-Amand's orchestration); 091.0409 (due to come to the Italiens); 091.0486 («Maman a été chez Mme de Peyronney qui paraît-il est adorable»); 091.0520 (present at the Russian Easter midnight service with the Troubetzkoys); 091.0579 («Voilà deux samedis que Mme de Peyronney aurait dû venir»); 091.0621 (a «lettre adorable» thanking Maman for flowers); 092.0056 (in Marie's list of Paris pressures: «les Gavini et puis Mme de Peyronney, Mme Adam, les Krishaber»); 092.0098 (Maman has also «embêté» Tony Robert-Fleury «sans parler des fleurs de Mme de Peronney»); 093.0186 and 093.0192 (the Biarritz chronicle: «"Le carnet d'n moindain" dont certains passages entiers sont de votre servante… les fioritures sont de la vicomtesse»); 094.0150 (the evening of 12 January 1882 announced in her column); 094.0218 (she visits: «cette femme supérieure»).

*Her husband:* 098.0149 (the vicomte refuses the divorce).

*Her daughter* («la petite Peyronney», «la petite Etincelle, Mlle de Peyronney»): 098.0092 (portrait planned), 098.0349–0351 (she lunches with the family and Marie sketches her: «Elle est gentille au possible»), 098.0405–0406 («Je voudrais avoir le temps de faire la petite Peyronney !!!»). Étincelle's daughter is Camille according to fr.wikipedia; the diary calls her «Mimie» at 097.0339 (nickname; the identification is plausible but not proved).
