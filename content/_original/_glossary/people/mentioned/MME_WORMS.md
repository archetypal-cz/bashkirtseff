# Mme Worms

## Overview

Mme Worms, the victim of a studio joke at Julian's in March 1879. The Mon Journal t.12 index has «Worms (Gustave), acteur : 184» and «Worms (Mme), épouse du précédent ; 242»; whether this is the actor's wife is not shown by the diary, where she is a woman who comes to the studio.

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Link to Gustave Worms the actor is the index's, not the diary's.

## Identity uncertain

We are not sure who this is. The diary gives only the surname, in a studio prank (084.0498). The Mon Journal t.12 index has a «Worms (Mme)», the wife of the actor Gustave Worms, but the prank's setting (Julian's studio) does not confirm it. If you can identify her, we would be glad to hear from you.

## References in Diary

On 18 March 1879 Amélie «a improvisé une mystification qui nous a empêché de travailler»: Mme Worms was assured that Mlle Jeanne had been shut up in the maison Dubois, while Jeanne hid behind an easel; discovering the trick «la victime a pleuré comme un veau» (084.0498).
