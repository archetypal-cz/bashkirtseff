# Chilowsky

## Overview

The name of at least two people in Marie's Nice circle: M. Chilowsky, «le petit Chilowsky, d'indifférente mémoire» (043.0285), and Mme Chilowsky, who died with the princess Galitzine in December 1875 (052.0089). Mon Journal indexes: t.5 «Chilowsky (M.), 330»; t.6 «Chilowsky (Mme), 214, 316». No first names.

## Relevance to Marie

- **20 September 1875.** The Bashkirtseffs' theatre box in Nice at the opera season «qu'avait jadis le petit Chilowsky, d'indifférente mémoire» (043.0285): a young man who held the box in an earlier season.
- **11 January 1876.** Looking forward to the English visitors and the spring season, the gentlemen of Nice «leur forment une espèce de cour comme il y a trois ans à la princesse Galitzine et à Mme Chilowsky, toutes deux mortes il y a un mois» (052.0089). The «princesse Galitzine» may be Marie's friend Nathalie Galitzine, but the identification is not made by the text.

## Identity uncertain

We are not sure who they were, nor whether Mme Chilowsky is the mother or wife of the «petit Chilowsky»; nor whether they are connected to the Chilovski of 1874 (CHILOVSKI). If you can identify them, we would be glad to hear from you.
