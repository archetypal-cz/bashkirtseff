# Thérèse

## Overview
The name covers at least two different referents in 1873–74:
1. **Thérèse (et Mantel)**, a Paris milliner: «J'ai écrit à Mantel et Thérèse qu'elles envoient un chapeau foulard à maman» (008.0266), and «un chapeau fille Angot de chez Thérèse et Mantel, en velours, cheveux rouges» worn by the woman Marie watches at the Nice station (014.0173). See [MANTEL](MANTEL.md). The Mon Journal t.2 index has «Thérèse, 34, 250».
2. **Mlle Thérèse Witoslowsky**, the young woman of the Spa season of 1874, whom the Mon Journal t.4 index lists as «Witoslowsky (Mlle Thérèse ?), 25–26, 42, 45, 62, 71–72» and «Thérèse, 71–72, 241». Count Merjeewsky has brought «la deuxième Thérèse» to Spa with him, to the distress of his wife; her father «souffre cela, est très content» (022.0255); there is also a sister and a father («le père Witoslowsky», 022.0255). «Les Thérèse que je ne peux supporter me détestent» (022.0263, 022.0264). At Nice on 11 December 1874 she dreams of «Thérèse, la mater dolorosa comme la nomme le comte Merjeewsky» (027.0231). See [MERJEEWSKY](MERJEEWSKY.md), [COUNT_MERJEEWSKY](COUNT_MERJEEWSKY.md).

## Identity uncertain
The index query mark («Thérèse ?») shows that even the editors were not sure of the surname. We keep the two apart; the milliner's paragraphs are 008.0266 and 014.0173, the others concern the Spa Thérèse. The later index lines «Thérèse (sœur)» (t.5 p.161, t.6 p.257) belong to yet another person (a «sœur»: a nun, or someone's sister). If you can help identify any of these, we would be glad to hear from you.
