# Comte de Jahal

**Last Updated**: 2026-10-21

## Identity

A gentleman of the Spa season of 1874, introduced to the family at the Gambarts' evening of 18 July: «On nous a présenté un comte de Jahal, qui s'est fixé à Pétersbourg» (021.0394); Marie finds that he looks like Brunet grown old. The Tome 4 name index of *Mon Journal* lists him as «Jahal (comte de)» (OCR «Jahai»), pp. 38–83. First name and origin not known; not identified further.

## Diary References (Spa, July–August 1874)

- **1874-07-18** (021.0394): introduced at Gambart's.
- **1874-07-19** (021.0419, 021.0424): met at the Pouhon («un charmant homme»), at the evening music.
- **1874-07-25** (022.0070): Marie wins fifteen kopecks from him on a bet that she would not go to the ball.
- **1874-07-26 – 08-05** (022.0119, 022.0177, 022.0243, 022.0287, 022.0378): walks, music, dances; one of the family's «cavaliers» at the ball.
- **1874-08-06** (022.0415): «M. de Jahal est parti.»
