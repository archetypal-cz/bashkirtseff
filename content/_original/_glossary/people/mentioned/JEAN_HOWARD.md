## Jean Howard

%% GLO_JEAN_HOWARD.0002 %%
Brother of Hélène and Aggie Howard, the English family close to the Bashkirtseffs in Nice (see HELENE_HOWARD). He is present at the sofa gathering of 8 November 1873, «Jean sur un autre canapé vis-à-vis» (012.0119). In September 1874, on Marie's visit to the Howards in England, he rides with Hélène and Marie to see the family factory: «ils nous firent voir leur fabrique, Hélène, Jean et moi à cheval» (023.0669, 16 Sept 1874). In May 1873 the sisters tell Marie that their mother always holds her up as an example (005.0004).
