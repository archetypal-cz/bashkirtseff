# Oller

## Overview

Joseph Oller (1839–1922), Catalan-born Paris entrepreneur who introduced the pari-mutuel betting system on horse races (1867) and later co-founded the Moulin Rouge (1889) (existing RSR at 020.0547; Mon Journal t.3 index «Oller, 259»).

Stub replaced. Details from the RSR at 020.0547; the Moulin Rouge founding is shared (with Charles Zidler) - not re-sourced here. Wikipedia: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Oller.
