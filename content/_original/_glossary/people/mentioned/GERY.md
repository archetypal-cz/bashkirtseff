# Géry

%% GLO_GERY.0002 %%
"Géry" in the diary is the surname of two men, a father and his son, whom Marie distinguishes only as "le père Géry" and "le fils" or "Gabriel" (085.0572). The father is an older friend of the Gavini household and of Prince Napoléon; the son, Gabriel Géry, is a young diplomat whom Marie considered, and rejected, as a husband. Both are described in one place: see [Gabriel Géry](./GABRIEL_GERY.md).

%% GLO_GERY.0003 %%
This entry is tagged on paragraphs where the surname stands alone (about 85 entries, 1879 to 1884). Where the text says only "Géry" without "père" or "Gabriel", the reference is usually to the father in 1879 to 1881 and in 1884, and to either man in 1882 to 1883.
