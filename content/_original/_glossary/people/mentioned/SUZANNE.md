# Suzanne

## Overview
Not a separate person: «Suzanne» is the name Paul de Cassagnac gave Marie during the masked visits to his flat in the winter of 1877–78, when the five masked women were supposed to keep their names and masks («Donne-moi un nom Paul. — Celui que j'aime le mieux ? — Oui. — Suzanne», 077.0514–0517, 31 January 1878). Dina was «la colonelle» and Berthe Boyd «Georges» in the same game.

## References in Diary
- 1878-01-31: the name is given (077.0517)
- 1878-02-12: Marie and Berthe sign a message «Suzanne, Georges» (078.0124)
- 1878-03-12: «il a dit cinquante choses de Suzanne, de la colonelle et d'autres» (078.0494)
- 1878-06-22: she signs her anonymous letter to Cassagnac «SUZANNE AU MASQUE» (080.0716)

Do not confuse with other Suzannes in the diary («Suzanne peut-être !», a guess at a name, 080.0160; «la chaste Suzanne», the biblical Susanna, 071.0460); tag only the mask name with this entry.
