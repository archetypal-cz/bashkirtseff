# Pierre-Auguste Cot

## Overview
Pierre-Auguste Cot (Bédarieux, 17 February 1837 – Paris, 1883), French academic painter, pupil of Léon Cogniet, Alexandre Cabanel and William Bouguereau; Salon debut 1863, chevalier of the Légion d'honneur 1874, a fashionable portraitist, best known for *Le Printemps* (1873) and *L'Orage* (1880), both in the Metropolitan Museum of Art (en.wikipedia "Pierre Auguste Cot"; Metropolitan Museum Journal 14, 1979). His death date is given as 2 August 1883 in most sources, 19 June 1883 by the BnF record (not resolved).

## Relevance to Marie
- **January 1881, Julian's atelier.** «Entrée en fonctions de M. Cot, qui va alterner avec Tony» (090.0593, 15 January 1881): he becomes the second visiting professor of the women's atelier, taking alternate months with [Tony Robert-Fleury](../artists/ROBERT_FLEURY.md) («le mois de Cot», 090.0620). Marie is reluctant to take advice from «ce faiseur de tableaux à mettre en gravures coloriées» with his «genre gracieux» (090.0595), puts him at «bien quarante-sept ans» (he was 43) and finds him «bon enfant» (090.0597). On 22 January his first corrections of *[L'Atelier Julian](../../culture/art/L_ATELIER_JULIAN.md)* are «des choses insignifiantes» and she calls Tony in instead: «Cot pour moi, ça n'existe pas» (090.0642–0644).
- **Salon juror.** In May 1881 Julian tells her that only Cot was willing to support her picture for an award (091.0628); in May 1883 she finds him «le plus aimable des hommes, son talent un instant à la mode n'a aucune importance mais il est membre du jury et très bon» (099.0600).
