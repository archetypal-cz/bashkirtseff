## Löbbecke, a German admirer of the family (1876)

%% GLO_LOBBECKE.0002 %%
An officer-like acquaintance of Rome who corresponds with Marie's mother, follows the family around, and in summer 1876 becomes assiduous around Dina, to Marie's relief. Spelled Löbbecke and Loëbecke.
