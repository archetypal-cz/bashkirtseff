# M. Volkovitsky

## Overview
A Poltava gentleman, husband of [Mme Volkovitsky](MME_VOLKOVITSKY.md). Marie: "Volkovitsky est marié à une fille bâtarde du Prince Serge Kotchoubey, par conséquent bien posé à Poltava" (066/1876-09-06). The Mon Journal t.9 index lists "Volkovitsky (M.)" and "Volkovitsky (Mme)" separately.

## Relevance to Marie
A regular of her father's Poltava circle in September-October 1876: he spends a day at Gavronzi with Pavelko (066/1876-09-06), rides with the wolf hunt ("une furie de L'Iphigénie en Tauride", 066/1876-09-24) and tells Jewish anecdotes at the hunt dinner (same entry), and calls on her in Poltava (067.0018). With his wife and Gedanoff he invites her into their carriage on the train at Iskrowka (066/1876-10-06).
