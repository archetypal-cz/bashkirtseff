# Swiss Times

**Research Status**: Basic
**Last Updated**: 2026-10-22
**Diary Coverage**: March 1873 – November 1874 (Nice, Cannes)

## Identity
«Les Swiss Times» is Marie's name for an English family of Nice, after the newspaper *The Swiss Times*, an English-language paper of Switzerland that printed lists of foreign visitors (an 1871 «Swiss Times Strangers List» sheet is known from the antiques trade). Marie reads the paper itself: «Le matin je lisais le Swiss Times, je voyais les listes des étrangers, pas à Nice mais partout … j'ai trouvé "Hamilton, the duke of" à Naples» (003.0071, 22 March 1873). The family is presumably that of the paper's man: «A Cannes nous rencontrons M. Howard du Swiss Times» (024.0180, 1 Oct 1874). Mon Journal t.1 index: «Swiss Times (les)»; t.3 index: «Swiss Times».

The family: Marie and her party stop to talk «aux Swiss Times qui étaient en fiacre» (002.0204, 5 March 1873); she and Dina visit them and speak English («Elle était ravie de me voir», 005.0279, 14 June 1873); «nous vîmes tous les Swiss Times. M. Howard est arrivé de Vienne» (006.0231, 11 July 1873); at a wedding party she sits «entre Mme Anitchkoff, Howard, Swiss Times et Bête» (019.0169, 10 May 1874); Edy «a fait connaissance par les Galignanis (au Swiss Times)» (025.0165, 7 Nov 1874).
