## Bagatelle, one of Marie's dogs (1874)

%% GLO_BAGATELLE_DOG.0002 %%
A female dog of Marie's household in 1874, kept alongside the beloved Prater ([#Prater](PRATER.md)). The dog continues in the later years (carnets 042, 072, 084, 088: the bulldog, "écrasée à Spa"). There is no park Bagatelle in the diary. Not the theatre piece "Bagatelle" of 019/1874-05-21, and not the common noun "bagatelle(s)" (003, 004, 007, 018).
