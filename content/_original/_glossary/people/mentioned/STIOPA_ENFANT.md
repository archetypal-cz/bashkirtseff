## Stiopa, the little boy of the Nice household (1875)

%% GLO_STIOPA_ENFANT.0002 %%
Not Uncle Stiopa ([#Stiopa](STIOPA.md), Stepa Babanine). In February-April 1875 "Stiopa, l'aîné" is a boy of seven in the household of Marie's uncle in Nice (with his sister Julie, six, and the small Liouba), whom Marie takes out to the opera and to Rumpelmayer's.
