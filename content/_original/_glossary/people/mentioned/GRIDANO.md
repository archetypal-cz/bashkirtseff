## Gridano

%% GLO_GRIDANO.0002 %%
A young Romanian, introduced to the Bashkirtseffs the day before 2 July 1874 (Spa, July 1874) and mentioned once. Marie calls him «le petit laideron Gridano, un Roumain présenté à nous hier» and finds him tiresome for staying with the party while they wait for Mme Basilévitch before an event with a platform («l'estrade») (020.0504, 1874-07-02).
