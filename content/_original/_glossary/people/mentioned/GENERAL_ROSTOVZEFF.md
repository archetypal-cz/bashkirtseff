# General Rostovzeff

## Overview
«Le général Rostovzeff, chef de la cour du grand-duc Nicolas», a relative of the Sapogenikoffs, whom Marie, Maman and ma tante visit at Peterhof on 9 August 1876 with Nina Sapogenikoff; he and his wife had known Maman and ma tante «il y a quatre ans à Nice» (064.0476); Marie names him again among «les personnes du grand monde qu'a connues maman il y a huit ans» (064.0499). Mon Journal t.8 index: «Rostovzeff (général) : 355, 358».

## Identification
Very probably Alexander Alexandrovich Rostovtsov (died 1888 in St Petersburg): adjutant to Grand Duke Nikolai Nikolaevich the Elder, promoted major-general on 1 April 1876, then Hofmeister of the Imperial Court, «и управлял Двором Великого Князя Николая Николаевича Старшего» (*Russkij biografičeskij slovar'*, vol. 17, «Ростовцов, Александр Александрович», azbyka.ru). The office and the date fit Marie's «chef de la cour du grand-duc Nicolas» (about 0.85). The grand duke is therefore Nikolai Nikolaevich the Elder (1831–1891), brother of Alexander II. Not Yakov Ivanovich Rostovtsev (1803–1860), the general of the peasant reform.

## Translation
Keep Marie's spellings «Rostovzeff», «Rostovtzeff».
