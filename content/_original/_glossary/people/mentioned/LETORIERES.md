## Létorières (Mme de Peyronney)

%% GLO_LETORIERES.0002 %%
A spelling of the same name as LETORIERE. On 19 March 1881 Marie writes that Saint-Amand prepares a launch in *Le Sport* and in the «Carnet d'un mondain», signed Étincelle, «que fait Mme de Peronney (le vicomte de Létorières)» (091.0321). The «Carnet d'un mondain» was the society column of *Le Figaro* signed «Étincelle», the pen name of Marie Biard de Peyronny (Agutte sale catalogue, interencheres.com lot 87767814); see ETINCELLE.
