# Marquis d'Anglesey (Henry Paget)

## Overview

Henry Paget, 4th Marquess of Anglesey (25 December 1835 – 13 October 1898), styled Lord Paget until he succeeded on 30 January 1880. He married three times: Elizabeth Norman; Blanche Mary Boyd (the Boyd sisters of the Baden-Baden and Paris set), who died in 1877; and from 1880 the American widow Mary «Minna» Livingston King. (Source: Wikipedia, «Henry Paget, 4th Marquess of Anglesey», https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Paget,_4th_Marquess_of_Anglesey; Mon Journal t.13 index: «Anglesey d' (marquis, lord Paget) : 297», t.14: «Anglesey (d', marquis) : 264».) He is the person in the entry LORD_HENRY_PAGET.

## Relevance to Marie

- **22 July 1876.** Of the Boyd family: «Ils ont une maison à Londres, depuis le splendide mariage de Blanche Boyd, une fille de vingt-six ans, avec lord Paget qui sera marquis d'Anglesey» (064.0049); Marie, at the circus, sits between Bertha (Boyd) and Mlle de Toulé.
- **14 December 1881.** Of another Boyd daughter: «l'ex-mari de sa sœur, le marquis d'Anglesey et sa nouvelle femme la présentant partout elle [est] acceptée dans le monde» (094.0068). The sister's husband had succeeded in 1880 and remarried that year, which fits Wikipedia; Marie calls him «ex-mari» of the sister although Blanche had died in 1877.
