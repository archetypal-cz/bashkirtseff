# Gatowsky

## Overview

A family (M., Mme and Mlle Gatowsky) in Naples in the spring of 1877, relatives of the prince Gryf-Jaxa-Bikowsky ([GRYF_JAXA_BIKOWSKY](GRYF_JAXA_BIKOWSKY.md)). The *Mon Journal* t.10 index has «Gatowsky (M.), 37, 40, 42, 138, 140», «Gatowsky (Mme), 37, 40, 42, 120, 138», «Gatowsky (Mlle), 37, 40, 42, 120, 138».

## Spelling

Marie first writes «Mme et Mlle Hotowsky, parents de Bikowsky» (19 March 1877, 069.0451; also 069.0489), as printed in *Mon Journal* t.10 p.37 (scan checked 2026-10-21). On 25 March she corrects herself: «Les Gatowsky (et non Holowsky) et Olga Hamontoff étant chez nous» (069.0515). The HOTOWSKY stub is the same family; its two tags in 069 were moved here on 2026-10-21.
