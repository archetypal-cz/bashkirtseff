## Identification (RSR, 2026-10-21)
Two 1879 mentions: «M. Koechlin, le beau-frère d'Andrieux» at a lunch with the Karageorgevitch (084/1879-04-13), and Alexis's «ami Koechlin qui est à la mort», whom he goes to see at Mulhouse (086.0304, 086/1879-09-19). The Mon Journal t.13 index identifies the second as «Koechlin-Schwartz (Alfred) : 100». That is uncertain (0.5): Alfred Koechlin-Schwartz (1829–1895), a Mulhouse mill director expelled from Alsace in 1871, became mayor of the Paris 8th arrondissement in 1879 (BnF authority record) and did not die in 1879. The Koechlins were a large Mulhouse family (Wikipedia, «Koechlin family»). Whether the two mentions are one man is not known.
