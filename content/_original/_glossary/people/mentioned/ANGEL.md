# Angel (M. and Mme Angel)

## Overview

«Angel» is the surname of the Russian couple Marie met in Nice in 1874: Mme Angel, whom Marie nicknames «Ange», and her husband M. Angel. The same woman is the subject of the fuller entry MME_ANGEL ([Mme Angel](MME_ANGEL.md)), which already records the proposed merge ANGEL → MME_ANGEL. Mon Journal t.4 index: «Angel (Mme): [Ange], 180–185, 187–189…» and «Angel (M.), 202, 204, 209»; t.3 «Angel (Mme), 183, 199»; t.5 «Angel (Mme): [Ange]».

## Relevance to Marie

- **6 May 1874.** At the wedding of Mme Warvatzi's niece «la princesse me présenta à Mme Angel» (019.0134), the first meeting.
- **23 May 1874.** Marie and her mother call on Mme Angel, who leaves next day «avec Bidiger» and whose apartment in the hotel they take (019.0323).
- **28 October 1874.** «Angel» is with the crowd that cheers Thiers at the hotel (025.0021; the tag of 025.0019–0021 is on Thiers).
- **8 November 1874.** «Le mari d'Ange est arrivé et ils nous firent une visite» (025.0171); on 11 November «M. Angel» is at the small tea party (025.0201); on 16 November «Ange avec son mari» lunch with the family (025.0252).

The nickname explanation is in 025.0108 (see MME_ANGEL): «Ange a la voix et le caractère plutôt d'un hussard que d'un ange».
