# Le Bec

**Research Status**: Basic
**Last Updated**: 2025-07-06
**Diary Coverage**: Up to 1874-03-07

## Identity
Marie's mocking nickname ("le Bec amoureux", the lovesick beak) for the daughter of Princess Labanoff, a young woman of Nice's Russian society; "la maman" is Princess Labanoff. Not a young man (corrected 2026-10-01). Same person as [Bec](BEC.md).

## Diary References

### 1874
- **March 7**: "Le Bec après une longue absence a paru de nouveau à la Promenade, mais seul avec la maman" - The Beak reappeared at the Promenade after a long absence, but alone with her mother.

## Context
- The nickname mocks her mouth and nose (016/1874-02-02, 02-05)
- Regular attendee at fashionable Promenade des Anglais
- Her "long absence" was noted by Marie
- Appears with her mother, Princess Labanoff
- Part of Marie's observational catalog of Nice society
