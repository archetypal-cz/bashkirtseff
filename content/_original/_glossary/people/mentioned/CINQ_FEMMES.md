# Les cinq femmes

## Overview
Not a person: «les cinq femmes (du monde)» is the collective signature under which Marie and four friends wrote anonymously to Paul de Cassagnac in January 1878, and the name of the masked visits to his house that followed. The first letter asks, «se confiant à votre honneur de gentilhomme cinq dames du monde s'aventurent jusqu'à vous demander de leur donner une tasse de thé chez vous. Elles seront masquées» (077.0161); he answered through the *Figaro* and by letter («Mes chères cinq femmes», 077.0331; «monsieur le secrétaire des cinq femmes», 077.0412). The five were Marie, Dina («la colonelle»), Berthe Boyd («Georges»), Mme Yorke («la novice») and Mme de Daillens («Colette») (077.0446); Cassagnac gave Marie the name «Suzanne» (077.0517, see SUZANNE). On 23 January four of them, in white with white masks, called on him and ended by unmasking (077.0331–0356); the masked tea itself was on Sunday 27 January 1878 (Marie's title-page notes, «Dimanche 27 janvier 1878 / les cinq femmes», 084.0002; «dimanche 27 janvier 1878 : 5 femmes», 085.0002), written up on 28 January (077.0446). Berthe's part still angers Marie that September («Le masque de Berthe qui me met encore en colère», 082.0283; 082.0350).

Marie came back to it for years: she rereads «la correspondance avec les cinq femmes» (085.0205), and in November–December 1879 she and Mme de Daillens try «une reprise des cinq femmes» on Gambetta (086.0606, 086.0620).
