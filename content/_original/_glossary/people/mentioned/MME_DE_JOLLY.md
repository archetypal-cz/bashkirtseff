# Mme de Jolly

## Overview

Mme de Jolly, one of the «ravissant trio» of beauties (Mmes de Reverseaux, Thouvenel and de Jolly) in Paris society in January 1882; the Mon Journal t.14 index has «Jolly (M. de) : 274» and «Jolly (Mme) : 280».
