# Arcangiolo (Arcangelo, model)

## Overview

Arcangiolo, an Italian-named artist's model, called in the Mon Journal t.14 index «Arcangiolo (M.), modèle à l'atelier Julian : 194». Marie calls him «notre ancien modèle le premier que j'ai dessiné» (093.0005). That fits the model «Arcangelo» whose head Marie drew in her first weeks at Julian's and showed Robert-Fleury (075, 13 October 1877: «la tête d'Arcangelo que je vous avais fait recommencer»; entry ARCANGELO). Confidence 0.85 that Arcangiolo and Arcangelo are the same man.

## Relevance to Marie

On 20 August 1881 she calls on the sculptor Falguière to ask for lessons, pretending to be an American; he sends her to a studio where he gives advice. She would prefer the studio of Saint-Marceaux «que j'adore» and would be satisfied with «l'atelier où Arcangiolo notre ancien modèle... est domestique» (093.0005): that is, her former model now works as the studio servant (*domestique*, the attendant) at the sculpture studio to which Falguière sent her.
