# Roy (ironworks, Nice)

## Overview
M. Roy, proprietor of an ironwork shop at Nice (the Mon Journal t.3 index has «Roy, atelier de ferronnerie, 239»). The entry is filed under people but names a tradesman and his workshop.

## Relevance to Marie
On 17 June 1874 the family is building and furnishing in Nice: «Nous étions chez Roy pour la grille, il nous soumettra des dessins et des projets. Puisque l'argent arrive, tout le reste marche; aujourd'hui ou demain arrivent les plans des chambres qu'a demandés Duval; la grille est presque commandée» (020.0324). Roy is therefore the craftsman who is to design and make the iron gate (*grille*); Duval has asked for the room plans (his role is not stated). The property is not named in this paragraph.
