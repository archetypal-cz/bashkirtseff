# Sofia Scalchi

## Overview
Sofia Scalchi (1850–1922), Italian contralto, a long-time member of the Covent Garden company; she sang Siébel in the Metropolitan Opera's first production, Gounod's *Faust*, on 22 October 1883 (en.wikipedia «Sofia Scalchi»; Opera Scotland). The diary does not give her first name.

## Relevance to Marie
On 25 February 1882 the family goes to Monte Carlo for a *Faust* «avec Faure, Maurel, Gayarre, Alban et Scalchi. Cinq artistes réunis, dont un seul peut faire le bonheur d'une soirée...» (094.0289). So Scalchi was in the cast; the role is not stated. The sources found do not confirm the Monte Carlo evening itself.
