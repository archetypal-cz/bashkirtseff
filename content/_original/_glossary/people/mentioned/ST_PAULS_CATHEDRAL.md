# St Paul's Cathedral

## Overview
The cathedral of the Diocese of London on Ludgate Hill, rebuilt by Christopher Wren after the Great Fire (completed 1710) (en.wikipedia «St Paul's Cathedral»). The entry is filed among the people but names a building.

## Relevance to Marie
On 9 September 1874, her second day of sightseeing in London after Ostend: «Il pleut deux ou trois fois. Nous visitons l'abbaye de Westminster et St Paul's. Depuis que je visite toutes sortes de monuments je trouve ceux-là seulement dignes d'être vus. Quelle antiquité, quelle grandeur !» (023.0614). See [WESTMINSTER_ABBEY](WESTMINSTER_ABBEY.md), [TOWER_OF_LONDON](TOWER_OF_LONDON.md).
