# Micha

## Overview

Micha, the young prince Michel Eristoff of the Poltava circle, called Micha in the summer of 1881 (Mon Journal t.14 index: «Eristoff (Michel, Micha, prince) : 89, 143, 148-149, 153, 155, 161, 163-165, 167-168»). Marie calls him «Micha» in 1881 and «Michka» elsewhere; see [Michka Eristoff](MICHKA_ERISTOFF.md), where the full identification is made. Marie says he is twenty-three in July 1881 (092.0241), which agrees with the birth year ~1858 given there.

## References in Diary
