# Moujik

**Research Status**: Basic
**Last Updated**: 2025-07-05
**Diary Coverage**: Up to 1874-02-11

## Identity
Russian term for peasant, used by Marie to describe Allard's assistant.

## Diary References

### 1874
- **February 11**: Marie calls Allard's substitute "ce moujik" but admits "ce moujik coiffe très bien" (this peasant styles hair very well).

## Context
- Derogatory class term
- Shows Marie's social prejudices
- Admits skill despite class disdain
- Russian word showing family's cultural background
