## Louise

%% GLO_LOUISE.0002 %%
Two different Louises are tagged.

%% GLO_LOUISE.0003 %%
1. One of «ces fameux chimpanzés Jacques et Louise», sick and miserable-looking on 12 June 1874 (020.0217; see JACQUES).

%% GLO_LOUISE.0004 %%
2. Queen Louise of Prussia (1776-1810), whose mausoleum Marie's party visits near Berlin on 29 July 1876: «le mausolée du roi Frédéric et de la reine Louise» (064.0307). The mausoleum of King Frederick William III and Queen Louise stands in the park of Charlottenburg Palace, so Marie confused it with Schloss Bellevue (diary footnote 1 at 064.0307, citing Wikipedia, «Mausoleum (Charlottenburg Palace Park)»).
