# Mme Helene

## Overview

Mme Hélène, a travelling companion of the Bashkirtseff family in spring 1882 who knows Antonelli and the circle at Nice. Not Hélène Howard (see [HELENE_HOWARD](../recurring/HELENE_HOWARD.md)); the Mon Journal t.14 index was checked and has no «Hélène» line.

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Not identified; family relationship unclear (first name only, 'Mme'). Possible duplicate: HELENE (stub).

## Identity uncertain

We are not sure who this is. Marie names her only as «Mme Hélène» in company of the family at Nice and Monaco in March–April 1882 (094.0339, 094.0383); her surname, and whether she is a relation, are not given. If you can identify her, we would be glad to hear from you.

## References in Diary

On 24 March 1882 she goes with the family and friends to Nice after Marie lunches with Barnola, «la princesse, Mme Hélène» (094.0339). On 11 April, on a journey, Mme Hélène asks for news of Antonelli; Marie cries that he never pleased her and then tells her the whole Larderel affair, which takes more than an hour (094.0383).
