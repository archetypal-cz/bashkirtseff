## Overview

A generic entry for the name "Jeanne" where no surname is given. Several different women are called Jeanne in the diary and each should have her own file; the former tags here mostly belonged to **Jeanne de Ballore**, baronne de Wykerslooth, now [JEANNE_DE_BALLORE](JEANNE_DE_BALLORE.md).
