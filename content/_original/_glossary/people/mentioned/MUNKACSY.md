## Mihály Munkácsy (1844-1900)

%% GLO_MUNKACSY.0002 %%
Hungarian painter, one of the most celebrated artists of Paris in Marie's years (the LAN note in 087/1880-02-14 cites "Milton"; his "Christ before Pilate" of 1881 made him a sensation). Marie spells his name Munkacsy, Munkaczy and, once, Munkassy.
