# Mme Bianchi

## Overview

Mme Bianchi, whose box at the Comédie-Française on the Tuesday subscription nights Mme Gavini has inherited («le mardi des abonnés»). Nothing else is said of her; she is not in the Mon Journal t.12 index lines checked.

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Whether Mme Bianchi died, left Paris or gave up the box is not stated; she is not otherwise identified.

## Identity uncertain

We are not sure who this is. Marie mentions her only as the former holder of a Comédie-Française subscription box, now Mme Gavini's (084.0548). We found no other reference in the diary or the Mon Journal indexes. If you can identify her, we would be glad to hear from you.

## References in Diary

On 25 March 1879 Mme Gavini arrives «comme une bombe» to take Marie to the theatre, having come into Mme Bianchi's box; Gambetta also has his regular Tuesday box (084.0548).
