# Les cireurs de bottes de Nice

## Overview
Not real boot-blacks: «les cireurs de bottes de la ville de Nice» is the signature of two prank letters Marie wrote on 13 February 1876, one to Emile d'Audiffret («Monseigneur !») and one to his friend Saëtone, in which the boot-blacks of Nice beg him to give up his supposed plan for «un établissement de cirage de bottes à vapeur dans sa grande salle à manger». Part of the same campaign as MOINES_DE_CIMIEZ.
