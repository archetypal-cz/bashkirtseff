# Winslow

## Overview
A young American man whom Marie meets at Spa in July 1874 and again, with his mother Mrs Winslow, at her Paris hotel in November 1875. First name not given.

## Relevance to Marie
- Spa, July-August 1874 (carnets 021-022): one of the American set around the Row sisters and Ladd; he dances and flirts with Marie: "Winslow danse beaucoup avec moi, et reste auprès de moi" (021.0172); "ce sont Winslow et Ladd qui me font la cour, beaucoup" (022.0409); a steward of the Spa fête (022.0281).
- Paris, November 1875 (carnet 050): "A la table d'hôte je rencontre Winslow et sa mère" (050.0007); "C'est à présent seulement que je me souviens qu'il m'a fait la cour à Spa" (050.0093); "une brute absurde, Winslow ... ça m'a fait parler anglais" (050.0181). He sends Marie the address through which her anonymous letters to Audiffret are to be posted from America by Ida Row (050.0201). Mrs Winslow calls on Marie before the family leaves for Nice (050.0205, 050.0207, 050.0211).
- He recommended the Hôtel New York to the family (043.0098) and introduced an American whom Marie meets in Nice (051.0085).

## Notes
The Mon Journal t.6 index lists "Winslow (Mlle), Américaine". The diary shows a man ("il m'a fait la cour", 050.0093; dancing partner at Spa) and his mother; the index entry is wrong on the sex.
