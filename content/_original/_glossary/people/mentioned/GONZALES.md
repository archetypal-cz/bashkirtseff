# Gonzales

## Identification (2026-10-21)
In the 1874–76 carnets this file is used for **M. de Gonzalès père**, the father of Rémy de Gonzalès (REMY_DE_GONZALES), whom Marie knew from Baden-Baden in 1870 (000.0023; "Le père Gonzalès", 000-06). Marie also calls him **Moreno**: "*Voici le père Moreno et avec lui Rémy*" (024.0050); "Nous avons rencontré les Gonzalès, ou Moreno comme on veut" (063.0113). The family name appears in full as "Rémy de Gonzalès Moreno" (004, 1873-04-17) and "Rémy Moreno de Gonzalès" (015, 1874-01-08). In Paris, September 1875, he and Rémy call on Marie at the Grand Hôtel (041.0051), and a visiting card "Moreno" (041.0105) turns out to be Rémy's visit. Duplicate files for the same family: MORENO, MORENO_DE_GONZALES, GONZALES_FAMILY (see the ledger).
