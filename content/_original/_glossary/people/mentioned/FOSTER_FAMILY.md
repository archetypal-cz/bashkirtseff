# Foster Family

The English Foster family of Ostend and Nice: the parents, Florence and two or more sons (see [#Foster](FOSTER.md) for the family account and [#Florence Lacon](FLORENCE_LACON.md) for Florence). This tag collects the "les Foster" paragraphs of the Ostend weeks of August–September 1874: the family invites Marie to Bruges (023.0382, 08-28), goes bathing with them (023.0470), sits opposite them at the theatre («vis-à-vis sont les gentils Foster», 023.0215), dines with them (023.0347) and dances with «Foster 2, qui est tellement laid» (023.0440); and one paragraph of 1876-01-16 (052.0216) where the Fosters know Prodgers, a possible bridge to Nice society at Spa. The Mon Journal t.4 index lists «Foster (M.), Anglais», «Foster (Mme)», «Foster (Mlle Florence)», «Foster I (fils)» and «Foster II (fils)».
