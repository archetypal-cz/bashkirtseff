# Comtesse de Trobillant

## Overview
An American lady with a French title, met in Paris in April 1881: the Mon Journal t.14 index reads «Trobillant (comtesse de), Américaine : 117». No first name is known.

## Relevance to Marie
On 18 April 1881 the family goes with Alexis and Bojidar, Bailleul, la Cerny and the Tchoumakoffs to a fashionable gathering of «Des Américaines ravissantes et ... beaucoup de vieilles cascadeuses du monde». After Mme Cartwright, who invites Marie to call, «la comtesse de Trobillant une Américaine vieille types-se excentrique qui nous invite chez elle» (091.0491). So she is an elderly eccentric American who invites the family. See [CARTWRIGHT](CARTWRIGHT.md).

We cannot confirm the French spelling of the title or who she was. If you can identify her, we would be glad to hear from you.
