# Stendhal

## Overview
Stendhal (Marie-Henri Beyle, 1783–1842), French novelist and essayist, author of *Le Rouge et le Noir* and *De l'amour* (1822), an essay on how love is born and on its kinds, with the concepts of *cristallisation* and *amour-passion* (Britannica, «Stendhal»; en.wikipedia «De l'amour (Stendhal)»; Project Gutenberg 60882). He is Marie's guide to her own heart in the second half of 1883. The Mon Journal t.15 index has «Stendhal (Henri Beyle dit), écrivain : 303, 305–306, 309, 324», t.16 «5–6, 11, 17, 32, 64, 90, 92, 150».

## Relevance to Marie
- **14–17 July 1883:** «Avez-vous lu "L'amour" de Stendhal ? Je le lis à présent» (100.0330). He is «plus délicat que Balzac, plus véritable, plus harmonieux et plus poétique ... Il exprime divinement ce que tout le monde a senti, même moi» (100.0332). She distinguishes *amour-goût* (which she has felt) from *amour-passion* (still to come) (100.0349), summarises his account of how love is born (100.0351), and tests her own heartbeats against his (100.0366, 100.0382). She dislikes the word *cristallisation* but accepts that it saves a long phrase (100.0359). «Je l'adore, lui Stendhal, et Balzac aussi parce qu'ils expriment mes pensées en un langage clair et admirable» (100.0354).
- **August–September 1883:** after reading him she goes to see Raphael at the Louvre and cannot like him (100.0542, 100.0544); she reads «les histoires de la peinture de Stendhal» (101.0006) and «l'histoire de Michel-Ange par Stendhal», which makes her want to kill herself for lack of genius (101.0157). She follows «les préceptes de Stendhal» in a meeting with a man (101.0018) and writes «ô cristallisations ! Stendhal tu es vrai» (101.0051); on 13 September she reads that sorrows seem less bitter when idealised (101.0333).
- **October 1883 to February 1884:** a marginal note, «ce sont tout bonnement les cristallisations de Stendhal» (102.0055); she re-reads *L'amour* on 2 December, finds the chapter on *engouement* describes her feelings, and says she has felt almost everything he calls *amour-passion* (102.0408, 102.0409). On 25 February 1884 she blushes at a name, as he says lovers do (103.0401).
