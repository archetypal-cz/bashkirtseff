## Les Ganaches

%% GLO_LES_GANACHES.0002 %%
Comedy by Victorien Sardou, first performed at the Théâtre du Vaudeville in 1862 (Wikipedia, «Victorien Sardou»). Marie sees it at the Vaudeville on 13 May 1874 and finds it «fort immorale; voilà la vraie dépravation»: «Aimer le petit-fils d'un domestique !» (019.0198). On 16 June 1874 she quotes a line from it, «Ces choses-là sont écrites dans le ciel», about her old certainty that she would marry her first love (020.0304). See SARDOU.
