# Muliner

## Overview

M. Muliner, a dark-skinned friend of the Morenos introduced to Marie at Spa on 14 June 1874 as «un homme des tropiques» (Mon Journal t.3 index: «Muliner (M.), 233–234»); his wife is [Mme Muliner](MME_MULINER.md).

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. No forename; 'tropics' as Marie's phrase. Not the Moliner of 1880 (MOLINER).

## References in Diary

Moreno introduces him: «son ami, un homme des tropiques, M. Muliner» (020.0261); Marie notes that he and his wife are friends of the Morenos, «Encore une famille de singes», and that the two dark men said the best things about her (020.0265, 020.0266).
