## Lagnadès-Effendi

%% GLO_LAGNADES_EFFENDI.0002 %%
Guest of the Bashkirtseffs' first Saturday reception on 2 April 1881 in Paris, with the Bailleul, the Tchoumakoffs, the Gavinis and Odette Randouin: «Lagnadès-Effendi (ambassade turque)» (091.0408). The title Effendi marks an Ottoman official; he belongs to the Turkish embassy staff, as Marie says. Nothing more is known; no first name.
