# Arnim

## Overview

Two different men are tagged here. (1) A **Count Arnim, a Prussian**, in the Nice winter society of 1874–1875: «un comte Arnim, Prussien, son bon ami selon les méchantes langues» of Princess Souvoroff (017.0091, 1 March 1874); «il y avait Arnim et Furstenberg» at the Italiens (029.0081, 27 January 1875); among a crowd of gamblers and marksmen with Galve, Lambertye and Furstenberg (029.0091, 28 January 1875); and Marie's mockery of his looks (029.0214, 6 February 1875: «Arnim était là, Audiffer aussi... il existait pour son teint»). The Mon Journal t.3 index has «Arnim (comte d'), Prussien, 92» and t.4 «Arnim (comte) Prussien, 216, 237, 252, 254, 261, 276, 292–293, 304, 342». (2) **Harry von Arnim**, German ambassador to France, in the political news of 30 August 1877 (074.0144): «Bechman… est rentré en faveur de Bismarck en lui livrant le comte d'Arnim». Harry Karl Kurt Eduard, Count von Arnim-Suckow (3 October 1824 – 19 May 1881), ambassador to France from 1872, fell out with Bismarck, was prosecuted in 1874–75 for removing official papers and lived abroad, mainly in Austria. (Source: Wikipedia, «Harry von Arnim».)

## Identity uncertain

We are not sure who the Prussian count of the Nice society is. The diplomat was ambassador in Paris until 1874 and was under prosecution from the autumn of 1874, which makes it unlikely, though not impossible, that he is the count who sat in the Nice theatre in January 1875 beside Furstenberg; the two may also be relatives. The entry COUNT_ARNIM (people/aristocracy) is tagged on the Nice-season paragraphs of 026–030 and gives the diplomat as the likely identity; no source consulted settles it. If you can identify the Prussian count of Nice, we would be glad to hear from you.
