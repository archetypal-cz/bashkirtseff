# Cavally (couturière, Paris)

## Overview

A Paris dressmaker (Mon Journal t.3 index: «Cavally, couturière, 218»), one of the houses Marie and her mother visit in Paris in June 1874.

## Relevance to Marie

On 5 June 1874, going out alone with her mother for the first time in several days: «nous étions chez Caroline, chez Cavally; et enfin chez Worth le coupable» (020.0132), where the vendeuse Augustine apologises after a quarrel over the Worth bills: «Il sont très aimables et je leur pardonnerai bien.»
