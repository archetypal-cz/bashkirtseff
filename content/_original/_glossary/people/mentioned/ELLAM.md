# Ellam

A London maker of whips, from whom Marie buys her riding whip: on 1874-09-08 (023.0598), in the list of purchases for her riding outfit (costume - Wolmershausen: 11 £; amazone - Paole: 10 £; waterproof - Scott: 3 £; umbrella - Saugster: 4 £; hats - Brown, 2 £, and Harman, 18 s), «cravache - Ellam: 2 £ 4 s». The Mon Journal t.4 index has «Ellam, magasin, 137». No further detail on the shop was found.
