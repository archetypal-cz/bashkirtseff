# Paul Veronese

## Overview

Paolo Veronese (Paul Véronèse, 1528–1588), Venetian painter of the Renaissance, famed for his colour and his huge banquet scenes; his *Apotheosis of Venice* (1585) is on the ceiling of the Sala del Maggior Consiglio in the Doge's Palace, Venice (RSR at 028.0180; en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paolo_Veronese).
