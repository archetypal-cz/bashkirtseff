## Histoire de Monaco

%% GLO_HISTOIRE_DE_MONACO.0002 %%
A book Marie says she is reading in April 1875 at Nice: «Je lis cinq livres à la fois, la Sainte Bible, Napoléon, l'Histoire de Monaco et un roman de Dumas» (031.0240, 15 April 1875). Author and edition are not given and no source on disk identifies them; several histories of the principality existed (Marie lived close to Monaco and went there often). Not identified further.
