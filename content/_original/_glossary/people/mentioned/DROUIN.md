# Drouin

Mlle Drouin, a member of the women's-rights society Le Droit des femmes, Hubertine Auclert's group (Mon Journal t.14 index: «Drouin (Mlle), société des droits de la femme: 189»). On 1881-08-18 (092.0422) Marie attends the meeting at which fourteen members vote «l'expulsion du commandant d'Epailly et de la demoiselle Drouin qui ont calomnié et embêté Hubertine». The dispute arose over an article by Epailly on the women's demonstration of the 14th (July) and a riposte by Lévrier in *La Citoyenne*; see [#Epailly](EPAILLY.md) and [#Hubertine Auclert](HUBERTINE_AUCLERT.md). Hubertine Auclert founded *La Citoyenne* in 1881 and the society in 1876 (en.wikipedia «Hubertine Auclert»). No first name for Mlle Drouin was found.
