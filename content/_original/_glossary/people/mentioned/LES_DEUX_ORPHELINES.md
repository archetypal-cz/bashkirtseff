## Les Deux Orphelines

%% GLO_LES_DEUX_ORPHELINES.0002 %%
Melodrama by Adolphe d'Ennery and Eugène Cormon, first given at the Porte-Saint-Martin in the winter of 1874 and a great popular success. Marie sees it on 14 May 1874: «drame fort remuant, sanglant, déchirant, saisissant» (019.0222).
