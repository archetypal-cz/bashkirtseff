# Euphrosine

«Euphrosine», one of the Three Graces: the name Marie gave to Olga Sapogenikoff in the secret society A.T.E. (Aglaé–Thalie–Euphrosine) founded at Nice on Sunday 9 May 1875. Marie writes: «Les trois fondatrices de la société que l'on sait. Moi - Thalie, Marie - Aglaé et Olga Euphrosine. Voyez-vous cela, les trois Grâces» (032/1875-05-15). On 1875-05-30 (033.0358) she goes «à la musique avec ma tante, Aglaé et Euphrosine» (the printed text reads «Agiaé» and «Eupnrosine», OCR slips). See [#Aglaé](AGLAE.md) and [#Olga](OLGA.md).

The Mon Journal indexes disagree: t.5 reads «Euphrosine: Sapogenikoff (Olga)», t.7 «Euphrosine: Sapogenikoff Marie»; the diary text (032/1875-05-15, and the regulations of 3 May 1876, 059.0690) supports Olga. The tag is a nickname, merge into OLGA (see the merge list).
