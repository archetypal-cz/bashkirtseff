## Mme Hochon

%% GLO_HOCHON.0002 %%
Parisian hostess of the winter of 1883-84, a friend of the maréchale (Canrobert's circle). Marie describes her as «fille de M. Lefurel architecte du Louvre», a salon-keeper and «puissance artistique» who «connaît tous les artistes, les grands, les savants et les puissants»; she is received at the Orléans princes', «très belle, très grande et très sage» (101.0325-101.0326, 10 Sept 1883). Through her Marie obtains a subscription to the Théâtre-Italien. Mon Journal t.16 index: «Hochon (Mme)», pp. 31, 160, 162, 188.

%% GLO_HOCHON.0003 %%
Marie attends her soirées: 4 April 1883 (099.0036, with General Fleury and wife; the maréchale introduced the Bashkirtseffs); 14 March 1884, an intimate evening with artists, the duchesse d'Uzès, the comtesse Cornet and the maréchale (103.0516); 1 May 1884, where she receives compliments on her painting and Massenet, Carolus-Duran, Madeleine Lemaire, the Fleurys and the Canrobert are present (104.0078). Mme Hochon and her mother are among the callers of 15 March 1884 (103.0528).

%% GLO_HOCHON.0004 %%
The architect Marie means is Hector Lefuel (1810-1881), architect of the New Louvre (Wikipedia, «Hector Lefuel»); the diary's spelling is «Lefurel». No source consulted confirms Mme Hochon as his daughter, so this rests on Marie's statement. See LEFUREL.
