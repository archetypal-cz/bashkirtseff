# Martinoff (the prefect of Poltava and his wife)

## Overview

The head of the provincial administration at Poltava in 1876 and his wife, whom Marie visits with her father. Mon Journal t.9 index: «Martinoff (M.), préfet : 76, 78, 81» and «Martinoff (Mme), femme d'un préfet, fille du général Ogareff». Marie's father offers to take her «chez Mme Martinoff (la préfète)» (065/1876-08-25, 065.0457); on 15 September they call: Mme Martinoff, «fille du général Ogareff», was married off by the Emperor to «un M. Martinoff auquel on donna une préfecture» after a scandal with the Emperor's valet (066.0242); she is «femme du monde, bien aimable», and "le gouverneur aussi", who interrupts a committee for «une demoiselle aussi charmante» (066.0243); M. Martinoff asks how long Marie will stay with her father (066.0254). Marie calls him both «préfet» and «gouverneur» in the same pages.

The wife's father is the [General Ogareff](OGAREFF.md). Not to be confused with Nadine Martinoff (Marie's aunt, see [Nadine Martinoff](NADINE.md)), nor with the Neapolitan Mlles Martinoff of 1881-83 ([MLLES_MARTINOFF](MLLES_MARTINOFF.md)).
