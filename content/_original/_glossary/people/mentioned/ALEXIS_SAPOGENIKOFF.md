# Alexis (at the Sapogenikoffs)

**Research Status**: Stub
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-31
**Diary Coverage**: Carnets 005 (May–June 1873, Geneva)

## Overview

A young man referred to as "Alexis" or "Aliocha" (Russian diminutive of Alexis) who lived with the Sapogenikoff family in Geneva in May 1873. Marie describes him as "the boy who lives with them" ("le garçon qui vit avec eux"). He attended the circus and went horseback riding with the Bashkirtseff party during their brief Geneva visit. He is distinct from the Sapogenikoff daughters (Marie and Olga) and may have been a boarder or relative.

## Diary References

- **May 24, 1873** (005.0036): "At eight o'clock Mr Yourkoff with Alexis and Cima, the boy who lives with them, came to go to the circus with us."
- **May 27, 1873** (005): Referred to as "Aliocha" alongside Cima on a horseback excursion.

## Research Notes

Identity uncertain; no surname confirmed. The diminutive "Aliocha" (Альоша) confirms Russian given name Alexei/Alexis.
