# Tregouboff

## Overview
A Poltava family. Mme Tregouboff is the daughter of Domenica (Georges Babanine's former wife) by her first marriage, so the half-sister of Dina and Lola: «une Mme Tregouboff, la fille du premier mariage de Domenica» (067.0632, 067/1876-11-03). Her husband, M. Tregouboff, is the «témoin oculaire» of the after-concert talk Marie hears at their house (067.0632). The Mon Journal t.9 index lists «Tregouboff (M.)» twice and «Tregouboff, (fille du premier mariage de Domenica)». A «M. Trégouboff» is among the callers at Gavronzi on 2 June 1881 (092/1881-06-02). On 11 June 1881 Marie and Maman call «chez les Trégouboff, madame est la sœur de la petite tante, la femme à Etienne», where Dina and Lola are staying (092/1881-06-11): so Machenka (uncle Etienne's wife) would be her sister, which fits the 1876 line only if Machenka too is a daughter of Domenica's first marriage (unverified).
