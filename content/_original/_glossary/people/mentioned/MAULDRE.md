# M. de Mauldre

## Overview
A gentleman of the Nice season whom Marie meets on rides, on the Promenade and at the Opéra in the winters of 1874-75 and 1875-76. Only the surname is given; the Mon Journal t.6 index lists him as "Mauldre (M. de)" without further identification.

## Relevance to Marie
- 1874-12-31 (028.0095): he joins Marie and Foster on a ride to the Var: "M. de Mauldre nous rejoint près du jardin public et nous allons au Var".
- 1875-01-02 (028.0125): at the Opéra he visits their box and "nous accompagne jusqu'à la voiture".
- 1875-01-24 (029.0022, 029.0031): on the Promenade "il n'y a que M. de Mauldre qui a sa voiture derrière la nôtre", and he comes up to talk.
- 1875-12-14 (050.0755): at the Opéra (*Il barbiere*), he sits in their box, is "fort aimable" to Marie and reports that Nice is short of men this winter: at Mme Sabatier's matinée "une foule de dames et trois messieurs et demi".

## Historical Context
Not identified. No family or title is given in the diary.
