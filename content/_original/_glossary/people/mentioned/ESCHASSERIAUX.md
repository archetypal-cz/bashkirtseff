# Eschassériaux (baron Eugène and his son)

## Overview

The tag covers two Bonapartist deputies of the same name, father and son.
- **Baron Eugène Eschassériaux** (1823–1906), deputy for Charente-Inférieure in the Corps législatif and then in the Chamber of Deputies 1876–1893 (Wikipedia, "Eugène Eschassériaux"). Mon Journal t.12 index: «Eschassériaux (Eugène, baron, comte de Bouville) : 149, 154» (the «comte de Bouville» looks like an index slip; Bouville has his own index line).
- **«René» Eschassériaux**, his son. Index: «Eschassériaux (René) fils du précédent, homme politique : 199». Marie calls him a deputy like his father (084.0029) and addresses her note «à M. R. Eschassériaux député» (084.0035). Wikipedia gives the baron's own forenames as Eugène René François, and no source was found for a son sitting in the Chamber in 1879, so this identification is open (0.5).

## Relevance to Marie

- 083.0096 (1878-11-03): the Gavinis' nephew asks «le député Eschassériaux fils» for gallery tickets.
- 083.0139 (1878-11-08): «baron Eschasseriaux» is among the Bonapartist deputies introduced by Gavini on the way back from the Chamber at Versailles.
- 084.0029–0038 (1879-01-15): the son is introduced to Marie at the Opéra. She admires his head («une des plus belles têtes qu'on puisse voir») but not his clothes, writes him a mocking note about his frock coat «à Versailles» (084.0033–0035), and admits she found him handsome because she saw something of Cassagnac in him (084.0038).
