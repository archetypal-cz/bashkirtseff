# Baron Eugène Eschassériaux

## Overview

Baron Eugène Eschassériaux (1823–1906), Bonapartist deputy for Charente-Inférieure in the Corps législatif and then in the Chamber of Deputies 1876–1893 (Wikipedia, "Eugène Eschassériaux"; Assemblée nationale, Sycomore). Mon Journal t.12 index: «Eschassériaux (Eugène, baron, comte de Bouville) : 149, 154» (the «comte de Bouville» looks like an index slip; Bouville has his own index line).

His son, the deputy «René» Eschassériaux whom Marie meets in 1878–1879, has his own entry: [RENE_ESCHASSERIAUX](../politicians/RENE_ESCHASSERIAUX.md) (split 2026-10-22).
