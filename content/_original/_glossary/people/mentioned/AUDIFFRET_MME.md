# Mme d'Audiffret

## Overview

The wife of Léon d'Audiffret of the Tour Audiffret at Nice ([LEON_D_AUDIFFRET](LEON_D_AUDIFFRET.md)) and stepmother of Emile d'Audiffret ([EMILE_D_AUDIFFRET](../recurring/EMILE_D_AUDIFFRET.md)); mother of the girls Marie ([MARIE_D_AUDIFFRET](MARIE_D_AUDIFFRET.md)) and Dolorès. Marie meets her in Paris on 11 April 1880: «Chez elle je fais connaissance de Mme d'Audiffret, la belle-mère de l'illustre Emile d'Audiffret et de ses filles Marie et Dolorès» (087.0833). Per Kernberger (2013), «Mme Audiffret» is Emile's stepmother (see EMILE_D_AUDIFFRET).

## In the diary, 1880

In May–August 1880 «les Audiffret» (the mother and her daughters) are rivals in society: they receive on Saturdays (088.0264), go to Mme Türr's balls and the comtesse de Bailleul's baccarat evenings (088.0119, 088.0230, 088.0307), and are dressed by Savarre (089.0180); Soutzo frequents them (088.0258, 088.0264, 088.0410). Marie's aunt recalls the scandal: «Madame Audiffret s'est séparée de son mari à grand scandale, son mari, un ancien marchand de drap qui était conspué à Nice» (088.0333), and Soutzo calls her «une cocotte ou à peu près» (088.0251). In the summer the girls are at Biarritz and Luchon (089.0144, 089.0450).

## Earlier carnets

The tag is also used for «Mme d'Audiffret» of the Nice winters 1873–1876 (carnets 012–053); that she is the same woman is likely (the family «à Nice voyaient le meilleur monde», 088.0319) but was not checked paragraph by paragraph here.
