# Mme Yakovleff

## Overview

Mme Yakovleff, a Russian lady of the Nice colony in 1874 and Paris/Nice in 1877; Mon Journal indexes: «Yakovleff (Mme)» t.3 p.28, t.10 pp.189, 213.

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Not the Yakovlevitch couple of t.15. Forename unknown.

## References in Diary

On 4 November 1874, at the fireworks for Prince Charles III of Monaco's name day, she takes Marie to a window (025.0109). On 27 May 1877 Marie calls her «un vieux dragon, maigre et laide, mais qui a des parents parmi les Troubetskoy, les Mouchy, et surtout qui a une parente qui va partout à Florence»; Mme Yakovleff is «bien comme il faut», her mother stopped visiting her a year or two ago, and as the fault was theirs Marie will try to renew (071.0478). In June 1877 she is among the women asking who a gentleman friend of her mother is (072.0051).
