## Alessandro Manzoni

%% GLO_MANZONI.0002 %%
Italian writer (1785-1873), author of *I promessi sposi*. Verdi composed his *Messa da Requiem* in his memory, first performed in Milan on 22 May 1874 (Wikipedia, «Messa da Requiem (Verdi)»). On 8 June 1874 Marie's mother takes a box for 120 francs for «la Messe de requiem en mémoire de Manzoni, de G. Verdi» (020.0176); see MESSA_DA_REQUIEM_VERDI.
