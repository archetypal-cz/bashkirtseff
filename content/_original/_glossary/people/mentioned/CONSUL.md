# Consul

Not a person's name but a title: «le Consul», used three times by Marie without a name. One is explicitly the Russian consul at Nice (068.0903); the other two are probably the same man.

- 1876-12-10 (067.1511), Nice: «— Mais moi aussi je les connais, s'écria le Consul, ils ont un procès depuis quatre ans, et on ne leur ose rien faire, car ce sont d'infâmes calomnies.» (a visitor in a social conversation about a family under slander).
- 1877-01-30 (068.0903): Marie's aunt tells the visiting princess that she never knew Nice society except «le consul qui était tout près à servir et par lui les Howard». An RSR note on that paragraph identifies him as the Russian consul in Nice, the man Marie calls «un misérable» on 13 December (068.0023); through him the family had known the Howards.
- 1882-02-12 (094.0243), Nice: «Puis papa est allé chez le Consul.»

The consul's name is not given in these passages and was not found in the Mon Journal indexes. Common-noun entry; see the retire list.
