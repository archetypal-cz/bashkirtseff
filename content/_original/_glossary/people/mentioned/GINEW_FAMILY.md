# Ginew Family

**Research Status**: Stub
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-31
**Diary Coverage**: Carnet 005 (June 1873, Nice)

## Overview

A family (possibly English or Russian) encountered at the Howard household in Nice, June 12, 1873. Their daughter is described as "très gentille" with a "manière de donner la main très franche" (very friendly handshake). Marie notes she apparently spoke only English.

## Diary References

- **June 12, 1873** (005.0256): "Le soir chez les Howard. Nous y trouvâmes les Ginew. La fille est très gentille, a une manière de donner la main très franche. Elle ne parle je crois que l'anglais, nous avons dit quelques mots. Elles partirent."

## Research Notes

The surname "Ginew" is unusual; it may be a transliteration of an English or other European name. Only one mention in the diary.
