# Comte de Robillard

## Overview
A young man presented to Marie at the bal champêtre in Nice on 4 July 1875: «On m'amène Désiré, le comte d'Etienne de Robillard» (035.0191). The Mon Journal indexes list him on his own, «Robillard (comte de)» (t.5 p.161; t.6 p.286), and index Godard («Désiré») separately on the same t.5 page, so the line names two men: Godard, and the count (0.85).

## References in Diary
- 1875-07-04 (035.0191): presented to Marie at the bal champêtre, with Godard.
- 1876-02-10 (053.0491): the face Marie saw in the mirror on New Year's Eve was «Laide et bête… un mélange de Godard et de Robillard».

## Identity uncertain
We are not sure who this is. Marie names him only twice and gives no first name; «d'Etienne de Robillard» may be her spelling of a family name such as «d'Estienne de Robillard», but no such count could be traced in Nice in 1875. Sources checked: Mon Journal t.5 and t.6 indexes; a web search (2026-10-22). If you can identify him, we would be glad to hear from you.
