# Willy Howard

## Overview

Willy, the son of the Howard family of Nice, English friends of the Bashkirtseffs in 1873-74 (see [Howard family](../recurring/HOWARD_FAMILY.md), [Lise Howard](LISE_HOWARD.md)). Mon Journal indices: t.1 «Howard (Willy), 63, 120, 140, 149, 181, 184, 185, 189»; t.2 «Howard (Willy), 161, 201, 216, 266-267». He is never described at length.

## In Marie's diary

- 1873-11-05 (012.0068): Marie, Dina, Paul and Hitchcock go to the station at 8.30 in the evening to meet the arriving Howards.
- 1873-12-07 (013.0239): at the English church in Nice, «M. Howard avec Lise et Willy y étaient, nous nous sommes vus»; the Bashkirtseffs are to dine with them that day.
