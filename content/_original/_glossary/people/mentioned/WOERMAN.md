# Woerman

**Research Status**: Basic
**Last Updated**: 2025-07-06
**Diary Coverage**: Up to 1874-03-08

## Identity
A person in Nice society whom Marie considers a "fou" (madman). Has unpleasant manners that Marie dislikes.

**Name and nickname** (added 2026-10-21, RSR): the Tome 3 name index of *Mon Journal* (Cercle des Amis de Marie Bashkirtseff edition) lists him as «Woerman (Jean), [Le baron]» and cross-refers «Baron (le): Woerman». In Nice in the spring of 1874, «le baron» in Marie's text is Woerman (e.g. 017.0014, where «Le baron» stands treat at a kiosk and pays a hundred francs for the champagne, then boasts of the expense).

## Diary References

### 1874
- **March 8**: Greets Marie during morning walk, but she's unsure if she returned the greeting. Dina says she didn't. Marie is glad: "les façons de ce fou ne me plaisent pas" (this madman's manners don't please me).
