# Nini (Alexandrine Patchenko)

## Basic Information

- **Full name**: Alexandrine Patchenko (married name: Bashkirtseff/Bashkirtseva)
- **Nickname**: Nini
- **Father**: [M. Patchenko](M_PATCHENKO.md)
- **Sister**: [Catherine](CATHERINE_ERISTOFF.md) (called "soeur de Nini")
- **Husband**: [Paul Bashkirtseff](PAUL.md), Marie's brother (married 1880)
- **Residence**: Gavronzi estate, near Poltava, Ukraine

## Overview

Nini was Paul Bashkirtseff's wife and Marie's sister-in-law ("belle-soeur"). From a landowning family in the Poltava region, she married Paul in 1880 after a dramatic courtship disrupted by Constantin Bashkirtseff's intervention at the church. Marie described her as "jolie comme un amour" (pretty as a picture) with "de tres beaux cheveux noirs" (very beautiful black hair).

## Courtship and Marriage

Marie described the fiancee in December 1879: "d'assez bonne famille connue, du meme pays, voisine, assez riche, jeune, jolie et d'apres ses lettres bonne nature" (carnet 087). The wedding was initially blocked by Paul's father at the church in March 1880, causing a family crisis, but eventually took place at Micha's country estate. The dramatic arrival at Gavronzi saw Nini throw herself at Maman's feet "en poussant des cris et des sanglots, les cheveux denoues" (carnet 091, February 25, 1881).

## Marie's Assessment

Marie was deeply ambivalent about Nini. She doubted Nini's love for Paul, noting she had kept a wealthy backup suitor. At Gavronzi in 1881, she found the couple's domestic life joyless and provincial. By 1883, Marie was furious at Paul and Nini's treatment of their mother, writing "Paul et sa femme se conduisant comme des cochons avec maman" (carnet 100, July 13, 1883).

Yet Nini was also protective of Marie's fragile health -- in Paris she was "all in supplications and tears" when Marie opened a carriage window (carnet 087, January 2, 1880).

## Marie's Portrait of Nini (1881)

Marie painted *Portrait of Alexandrine Patchenko, the Painter's Sister-in-Law* in 1881 (oil on canvas, 92.5 x 73 cm), showing Nini relaxed on a sofa with a book. This painting survives in the [Rijksmuseum](https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en/collection/SK-A-1988), Amsterdam.
