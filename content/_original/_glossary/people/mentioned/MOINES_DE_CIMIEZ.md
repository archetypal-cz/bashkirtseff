# Les moines de Cimiez

## Overview
Not real monks: «les (frères) moines de Cimiez» is the signature Marie used for a series of anonymous prank letters to Emile d'Audiffret («le Surprenant», «Soroka») in Nice, January–March 1876, in a disguised man's hand or in printed capitals. The joke is that Audiffret has sold his soul («la vente de l'âme», 051.0292) and that the friars of the Franciscan house at Cimiez, above Nice, are searching for it on behalf of his poor body («Cherche ton âme ô corps infortuné !», 052.0072). Variants: «les frères moineaux de Cimiez» (a pun on moines and moineaux, sparrows, 054.0012) and a letter in Niçois signed «Le padri de Cimiès» (054.0116, 054.0119). She planned to collect them into a mock-epic «comme le Lutrin» (052.0079) and still thought them «une drôlerie très amusante et très spirituelle» in 1883 (100.0264).

The place is CIMIEZ; real monks (the two friars who actually called at the Audiffret tower after her letter to the Superior, 052.0199, 052.0297, 060.0259) are not tagged here. Companion masks in the same campaign: ALFEDA_DA_FE, DOM_FABRICE, RELIGIEUSES_DU_BON_PASTEUR, CIREURS_DE_BOTTES_DE_NICE.
