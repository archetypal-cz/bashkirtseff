# Miss Smith (Florence)

## Overview

An English or American lady of Florence «qui a un des salons les plus élégants de Florence», to whom Miss Hall, Marie's American acquaintance at Schlangenbad, offers her a letter of introduction in August 1877 (073.0265). Mon Journal t.10 index «Smith (Miss), 286, 293-294». Not the Paris furniture shop [Smith](../../places/shops/SMITH.md).

## Relevance to Marie

The letter would have opened Florentine society to her: «Cela flatte ma manie» (073.0265). A week later she fears she will not get it: «Miss Smith va voyager cet hiver, mon Américaine l'a appris par la sœur de cette personne», and suspects the sister has warned Miss Hall against her (073.0351, 073.0355).
