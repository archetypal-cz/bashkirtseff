# Sandwich (dog)

## Overview
Sandwich, a dog of the Bashkirtseff household in Nice in October 1874; the Mon Journal t.4 index has «Sandwich, chien, 178». The entry is filed under people but names a pet. It has no connection with the Earl of Sandwich, whom the t.2 index lists separately.

## Relevance to Marie
On 14 October 1874, with everyone at the theatre, Marie stays with her mother, sleeping at her aunt's: «à droite dort Sandwich, à gauche sur le lit, Dori; vis-à-vis, sur le canapé, Bagatelle, devant est un plateau contenant du poulet froid et des fruits» (024.0342). So Sandwich is one of the dogs that share her room, with [DORI](DORI.md) and [Bagatelle](BAGATELLE_DOG.md). No more is known.
