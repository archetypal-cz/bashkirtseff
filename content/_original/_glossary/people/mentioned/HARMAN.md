## Harman (milliner)

%% GLO_HARMAN.0002 %%
A milliner (modiste) in London. In the list of purchases and orders Marie records on 8 September 1874 she writes «chapeau Harman: 18 s» (023.0598), a hat at 18 shillings, among a riding habit (Wolmershausen, £11), a waterproof (Scott), a parasol and a whip. Mon Journal t.4 index: «Harman, modiste, 137». Not otherwise documented.
