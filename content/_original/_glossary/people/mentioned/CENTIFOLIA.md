%% GLO_CENTIFOLIA.0002 %%
"Centifolia" (the hundred-petaled rose, *Rosa centifolia*) is Marie's poetic code name for **Mme Amélie Gioia**, the demi-mondaine who was companion of the Duke of Hamilton. The main comprehensive entry is at [places/cities/GIOIA.md](../../places/cities/GIOIA.md).

%% GLO_CENTIFOLIA.0003 %%
Marie uses this name in carnets 002-005, later abandoning it: "Non, elle n'est pas la duchesse Centifolia, ni Laïs non plus, elle est simplement la Gioia" (carnet 002).
