# Alban (Emma Albani)

## Overview

Marie's «Alban» in the cast of *Faust* at Monte Carlo is almost certainly the soprano **Emma Albani** (1847–1930), Canadian-born, one of the leading Marguerites of the day (Wikipedia, «Emma Albani»; her entry in the project is ALBANI). The Mon Journal t.14 index lists «Alban (M.), acteur : 293», which treats the name as a male actor; t.11 has «Albani (Marie Emma Mlle), chanteuse : 137» for another page. Confidence 0.8; the only independent evidence is a Journal de Monaco bulletin of 1882, as reported by a web search, naming Albani, Scalchi and Faure in the *Faust* of Saturday 25 February (the issue was not opened). The five names Marie lists (Faure, Maurel, Gayarre, Alban, Scalchi) match the roles of Méphistophélès, Valentin, Faust, Marguerite and Siebel.

## Relevance to Marie

On Saturday 25 February 1882, in Nice, «Nous allons tous à Monte-Carlo pour la représentation de "Faust" avec Faure, Maurel, Gayarre, Alban et Scalchi. Cinq artistes réunis, dont un seul peut faire le bonheur d'une soirée...» (094.0289). Nothing else is said of Alban.
