## M. Léon d'Audiffret

%% GLO_LEON_D_AUDIFFRET.0002 %%
The old M. d'Audiffret of the Tour Audiffret in Nice, to whom Marie addresses letters in 1875-76. The relation to Emile d'Audiffret ([#Emile d'Audiffret](../recurring/EMILE_D_AUDIFFRET.md)) is not stated (Marie calls him "le vieux d'Audiffret papa"); an elder relative, perhaps the father, is likely but unproved.

## 1874: «le vieux Emile», «le père Audiffret» (RSR, 2026-10-22)

The old man is also Marie's «vieux Emile» of the Nice winters of 1874–75, and her text makes him Emile d'Audiffret's father: «Le vieux Emile était dans la loge avec ses filles (le père Audiffret)» (027.0082, 29 Nov 1874); «on vit d'Audiffret, père et fils» (027.0370, 23 Dec 1874); in her verse on the Opera boxes, «Emile d'Audiffret / Rouge joli et frais / Se cache derrière son père» (027.0316–0317, 19 Dec 1874); «le vieux Emile (il se nomme Léon, mais je le nomme vieux Emile)» (045/1875-10-01). The Mon Journal t.4 index has «Audiffret (Emile), père». So «le vieux Emile» = Léon d'Audiffret = Emile's father (0.85); the 2026-10-03 note below («kinship … not established») predates these passages being read, and the owner ruling kept the two men apart as persons, which stands. Tags on «le vieux Emile» in 018.0291, 018.0326, 027.0082, 027.0183, 027.0317, 027.0370, 027.0371 point here; people/mentioned/EMILE.md («le vieux Emile») is the same man and is a merge candidate (EMILE → LEON_D_AUDIFFRET; its last use is 028.0207).
