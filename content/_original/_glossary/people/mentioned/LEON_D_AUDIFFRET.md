## M. Léon d'Audiffret

%% GLO_LEON_D_AUDIFFRET.0002 %%
The old M. d'Audiffret of the Tour Audiffret in Nice, to whom Marie addresses letters in 1875-76. The relation to Emile d'Audiffret ([#Emile d'Audiffret](../recurring/EMILE_D_AUDIFFRET.md)) is not stated (Marie calls him "le vieux d'Audiffret papa"); an elder relative, perhaps the father, is likely but unproved.
