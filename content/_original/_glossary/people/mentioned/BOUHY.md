# Bouhy

**Last Updated**: 2026-10-21

## Identity

A singer in the circle of the art dealer Gambart at Spa in July 1874; the Tome 4 name index of *Mon Journal* lists him as «Bouhy, chanteur» (pp. 36, 50). Probably (0.8) the Belgian baritone **Jacques Bouhy** (1848–1929), born at Pepinster near Spa, who had made his Paris Opéra debut as Méphistophélès in 1871 and created Escamillo in *Carmen* at the Opéra-Comique on 3 March 1875 (Wikipedia, «Jacques Bouhy»; Morgan Library catalogue, «Couplets du Toréador», credited to Bouhy, Jacques, 1848–1929). The identification rests on the name, the profession and the place; the diary gives no first name.
