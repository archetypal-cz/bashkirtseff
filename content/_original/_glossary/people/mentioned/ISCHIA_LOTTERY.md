## Ischia lottery

%% GLO_ISCHIA_LOTTERY.0002 %%
A charity lottery in Paris for the victims of the Casamicciola earthquake on Ischia (28 July 1883; Wikipedia, «1883 Casamicciola earthquake»). On 27 August 1883 Marie writes: «J'ai donné mon Pêcheur à la ligne à la loterie d'ischia; les lots sont exposés rue de Sèze chez Petit» (101.0266), that is at the gallery of Georges Petit in the rue de Sèze. She is dissatisfied with the frame and the setting.
