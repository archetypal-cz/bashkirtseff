# Nasimoff

## Overview

M. Nasimoff, Russian vice-consul in Naples: «Le vice-consul de Russie, M. Nasimoff, averti hier de notre arrivée, a été chez nous. Maman l'a connu à Paris par le diacre» (058/1876-04-15). Mon Journal t.7 index: «Nasimoff (vice-consul de Russie)»; Kernberger (2013, glossary): «Nasimoff: consul in Naples».

## Relevance to Marie

At the Naples races Maman regrets not having spent the winter there, since Nasimoff could have introduced them «chez cette princesse Ouchtomsky, chez laquelle tout le monde se rassemble» (059.0055). In Naples again in 1877 he is often in the family's company, e.g. dining with Maman and Dina at the Fabbricatores' (069/1877-03-12).
