# M. Angel

## Overview
%% 2026-10-22T22:16:12 RSR: Created in the stub-pass apply (2026-10-22) from the paragraphs listed below. %%
The husband of [Mme Angel](MME_ANGEL.md), «Ange», the Russian lady of Marie's Nice circle in 1874. He arrives in Nice in November 1874, some weeks after his wife. Mon Journal t.4 index: «Angel (M.), 202, 204, 209». Nothing more is known of him.

## In Marie's diary
- 8 November 1874: «Le mari d'Ange est arrivé et ils nous firent une visite ainsi que Patton et Mme Howard» (025.0171).
- 11 November 1874: at the small tea, «M. et Mlle Pelikan, Bihovetz, M. Angel et Lubimoff fils» (025.0201).
- 16 November 1874: «Ange avec son mari, Pelikan avec sa fille, et Bihovetz et la Daniloff déjeunent chez nous» (025.0252).
