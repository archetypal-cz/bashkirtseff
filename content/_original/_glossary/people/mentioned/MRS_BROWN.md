# Mrs Brown

## Overview

Mrs Brown, a London hatter (Mon Journal t.4 index: «Brown, chapelier, 136-137, 139-140»).

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Address not given; the 'Harman' hat on the same list is a separate maker.

## References in Diary

On 7 September 1874, in London, Marie orders a hat at «Mrs Brown», an amazone habit at Paole and a grey costume at Wolmershausen (023.0593); in her shopping list of 8 September «chapeau Brown : 2 £» (023.0598); on 10 September the family goes «chez Brown et ensuite au Crystal Palace» (023.0630).
