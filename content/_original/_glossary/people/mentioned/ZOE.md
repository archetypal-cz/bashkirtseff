# Zoé (Zoé Pelikan)

## Overview

Both tagged mentions are Zoé Pelikan, the Nice friend of Marie's and Dina's in 1874-75: see [Zoé Pelikan](ZOE_PELIKAN.md). This bare-name entry is a duplicate.

## In Marie's diary

- 1874-11-26 (027.0037, Nice): «Après le thé Zoé, moi et Dina allâmes au salon, le comte nous suivit, je lui ordonnai de jouer pour m'en débarrasser.»
- 1875-04-22 (031.0338, Nice): her mother tells «que Zoé s'embrassait avec son Cross», i.e. with the naval officer Cross, whose marriage to Zoé Pelikan her father had announced on 2 March 1875 (030.0115).

Merge proposed: ZOE → ZOE_PELIKAN (confidence 0.85; the 1874 tea fits the Pelikan visits of November 1874 and the Cross link settles the second mention). Not to be confused with the other Zoé of the late diary (Mon Journal t.16 index «Zarondny Mme Zoé»).
