## Lévrier

%% GLO_LEVRIER.0002 %%
Member of Hubertine Auclert's society *Le Droit des femmes*. At the meeting of 17 August 1881, held after Hubertine's resignation, it is «Lévrier qui expose les faits qui ont motivé la démission»; Marie guesses at old grievances between Lévrier, Epailly and Hubertine. Lévrier had answered the commandant d'Epailly's article on the women's demonstration of 14 July with «des grossièretés» in *La Citoyenne*, and writes to Chapron (092.0422). The fourteen members present vote the expulsion of Epailly and Mlle Drouin so that Hubertine will come back. Only mention; no first name.
