# Gedanoff

## Identity
A Poltava acquaintance of Marie's father, constantly at the Bashkirtseffs' in the autumn of 1876 (Mon Journal t.9 index: "Gedanoff (M.)"). Marie: "Gedanoff le plus utile, le plus aimable et le mieux élevé des parasites était là comme toujours" (067.0055). He amuses the company with Michel Eristoff's circus adventures (067.0020) and, with the Volkovitskys, offers her a seat on the train at Iskrowka (066/1876-10-06).
