## Prince Jacqua de Bikowsky

%% GLO_JACQUA_DE_BIKOWSKY.0002 %%
A Polish pretender to princely rank met in Paris on 14 May 1874: «le diacre nous présente M. le prince Jacqua de Bikowsky, le tenant par le collet. Ce misérable petit fou est devenu presque idiot à force d'être fat ... Quand on pense qu'il écrit sur ses cartes le prince Jacqua de Bikowsky !!» (019.0216). He has run through his money and his aunt has put him out of doors (019.0217). The Mon Journal t.3 index lists him as «Bikowsky (prince Jacqua de), 190, 209».

%% GLO_JACQUA_DE_BIKOWSKY.0003 %%
He is the same person as the Paris Bikowsky of 1873-74 (see BIKOWSKY) and as the «Jaxa» and «Jaxa Bikowsky» of the same spring and autumn (JAXA, JAXA_BIKOWSKY; the t.10 index spells the Nice cousin «Gryf-Jaxa-Bikowsky», see GRYF_JAXA_BIKOWSKY). The name «Jaxa» is the alternative name of the Gryf coat of arms (Wikipedia, «Gryf coat of arms»). The princely title is his own claim, as Marie shows.
