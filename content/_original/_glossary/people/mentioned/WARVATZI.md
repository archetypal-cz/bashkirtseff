# Mme Warvatzi

## Overview

«Mme Warvatzi», a lady of the Russian colony in Nice. On 6 May 1874 Marie, Dina and Bête go in the evening to church «pour le mariage de la nièce de Mme Warvatzi, assez laide, et le fiancé de quarante-cinq ans, cinq enfants»; there «la princesse me présenta à Mme Angel» (019.0134). Mon Journal t.3 index: «Warvatzi (Mme), 183». The spelling is Marie's (the index prints the same). Nothing else is said of her; her niece's name is not given.

## In Marie's diary

- 1874-05-06 (019.0134, Nice): the wedding of her niece; see [Mme Angel](MME_ANGEL.md) in the same paragraph.
