# Mme Laloff

## Overview

Mme Laloff, wife of a M. Laloff of Geneva; the Mon Journal t.4 index has «Laloff (Mme), 185, 321». Nina Sapogenikoff's children nicknamed the couple Ménélas and Hélène, hinting at a fickle wife.

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Only one tagged paragraph; the index shows a second mention (t.4 p.321). The Hélène/Ménélas nicknames are the children's.

## References in Diary

On 26 October 1874, listing who is in Nice, Marie explains that Pâris (Yourkoff) was so named by Nina's children, just as «un monsieur et Mme Laloff, à Genève» were called «Ménélas et Hélène, en dévoilant un caractère soit-disant volage. Nina raconte elle-même tout» (024.0414).
