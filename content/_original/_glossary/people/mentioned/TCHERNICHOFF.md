# Tchernichoff

## Overview
This entry mixes at least two different men named Tchernichoff, whom the Mon Journal indexes also keep apart: **M. Tchernichoff**, an elderly ladies' man of 1874, and **Dr Tchernichoff**, a physician at Nice in 1876 (index t.3 «Tchernichoff (M.), 165–166»; t.7 «Tchernichoff (médecin) : 323, 343»; t.8 «Tchernicheff (M.) médecin : 354»). Marie says it herself in 1874: «M. Tchernichoff, celui qui était dernièrement à Nice; celui qui a épousé Mme Ketly, qui dit-on, a empoisonné sa première femme, pas le docteur Tchernichoff enfin» (024.0122). Another entry, [TCHERNICHEFF](TCHERNICHEFF.md), exists under a variant spelling; the index also has a «Tchernikoff, médecin» in t.1. First names are never given.

## M. Tchernichoff (1874)
- 19 April 1874 (Nice): introduced to her mother the day before, he calls. Marie (writing in English) reports «a very bad story about him»: his first wife poisoned herself, some say was poisoned, and he then married the woman who had caused it; «they say no woman can resist him», but she finds him «an old dog with almost rude manners», a second *Lautrec*. He shares her word *poussière* for common people, takes the family to the exhibition and to London House for ices (018.0310–018.0330).
- 27–29 September 1874 (Paris): he lunches with them at the café Riche, goes to the races, leaves a basket of fruit, and goes with them to shops (024.0122, 024.0123, 024.0150, 024.0156–024.0158). On 29 November 1874 (Nice): «M. Tchernichoff qui est arrivé il y a de cela quelques jours. Véritablement je crains pour ma tante, on le dit dangereux» (027.0073).

## Dr Tchernichoff (1876)
In spring 1876 at Nice a Dr Tchernichoff is in the circle around Nina and Marie Sapogenikoff: «le docteur Tchernichoff. Ce laid fait beaucoup la cour à Marie» (059.0848); he brings bouquets with Antonoff (059.0863). Whether the Tchernichoff who goes with Marie's mother to Monte Carlo and from whom she takes a hundred francs on 10 May 1876 (060.0004, 060.0007) and the one named without title on 6 May (059.0828) is the doctor is likely (the index places the doctor on those pages) but is not stated in the text. 29 April 1876 (059.0621) concerns the aftermath of Yourkoff's suicide.

## Identity uncertain
We keep the two men together here only because the tags do. The 1874 M. Tchernichoff has no further identification; his two marriages («Mme Ketly») are Marie's report of gossip. See the list of tags: the 1876 paragraphs should be moved to a separate entry for the doctor.
