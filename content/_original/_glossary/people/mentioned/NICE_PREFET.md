# Nice Prefet

## Overview

The prefect of the Alpes-Maritimes in Nice, mentioned without name by Marie in October 1875: «Et il passe avec le préfet» (047/1875-10-18, 047.0157); the prefect escorts Gioia's visitor along the Promenade (047.0160, 047.0222) and «Bibi et Enoteas sont chez la préfète … n'allant que chez le préfet !» (047/1875-10-20, 047.0290). In October 1875 the prefect was **Albert Decrais** (in office 11 November 1874 to 21 March 1876; English Wikipedia, «Albert Decrais»), whom Marie names in 1874-76 (see [DECRAIS](DECRAIS.md)). This entry therefore duplicates DECRAIS and should be merged into it (lead's decision; not merged here).
