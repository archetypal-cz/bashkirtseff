# Michelazzi

## Overview

Michelazzi, Marie's teacher of Latin and Italian in Nice in autumn 1874 (Mon Journal t.4 index: «Michelazzi (M.), professeur de latin et d'italien, 232»).

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Role and page from the t.4 index; no forename or outside source found.

## References in Diary

On 3 December 1874 Marie dismisses him: «J'ai renvoyé Michelazzi, le professeur de latin et d'italien, parce qu'il prenait plusieurs cachets pour une seule leçon» (027.0134). She could not tell him he was stealing, and he insisted with such aplomb that he had not been paid that she gave him another cachet, so she preferred «renvoyer ce voleur».
