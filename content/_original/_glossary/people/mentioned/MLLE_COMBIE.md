## Overview

A young woman known to the Bashkirtseff family, encountered at Nice station on May 22, 1873, traveling with the Rice family (Mme Rice, her brother, and her cousin) toward Geneva and Lyon. The Bashkirtseffs joined their group for the train journey. Marie describes her as an acquaintance ("que nous connaissions") without further detail.
