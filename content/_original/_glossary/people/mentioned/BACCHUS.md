# Bacchus (not a person)

## Overview

Bacchus, the Roman god of wine, appears only in a figure of speech: on 12 October 1874 Marie says that her brother Georges, «qui commence en ce moment ses libations à Bacchus», kept the carriage until half past six and brought the solicitor Florès to dinner, with whom he «was drinking» all day (024.0295). It refers to Georges's drinking and is not a reference to the god as a subject of art or story.
