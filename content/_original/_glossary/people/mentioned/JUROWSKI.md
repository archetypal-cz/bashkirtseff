## Comte Jurowski

%% GLO_JUROWSKI.0002 %%
Guest at the Bashkirtseff dinner of 13 January 1882 in Paris, described as «beau-frère de l'innocente Isabelle»; sixteen at table with the Gavinis, the Gérys, Morgan, Saint-Amand, the princess and Bojidar (094.0160). «L'innocente Isabelle» also appears at 091.0499 (21 April 1881). Only mention; no first name.
