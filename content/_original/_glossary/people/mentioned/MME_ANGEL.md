# Mme Angel

## Overview

Mme Angel, a Russian lady of thirty-five whom Marie meets at Nice in autumn 1874 and nicknames «Ange» (Angel in French) because «Ange a la voix et le caractère plutôt d'un hussard que d'un ange» (025.0108). Mon Journal t.4 index: «Angel (Mme): [Ange], 180–185, 187–189, 192, 196…»; «Angel (M.), 202, 204, 209» for her husband; also t.3 pp.183, 199 and t.5 pp.28, 32, 82, 96.

## References in Diary

Marie describes her at 024.0362: «une dame russe âgée de trente-cinq ans, ayant des cheveux magnifiques, et belle… jolie, élégante et a de l'esprit, de plus de la sympathie pour moi»; she is musician and painter (024.0363), and says she prefers Marie to Paul, at which Papa is disappointed. Marie first meets her on 6 May 1874, when the princess presents her at a wedding in church (019.0134, 019.0136: «Mme Angel se retournant: N'écoutez pas ça, ce sera bien ainsi»). In Nice she is at the Bashkirtseffs' almost daily in October–November 1874: piano duets with Dina (024.0415), the theatre in the family box (024.0392), the Thiers crowd (025.0021), dinners at her place with the Pelikans (025.0248, 025.0252; Marie meets Pelikan's daughter Zoé at her place, 025.0123). Her husband arrives on 8 November (025.0171). In April 1875 she returns from Petersburg, where she lost her mother (031.0335, 032.0026), and is with the family in May (033.0157, 033.0375).
