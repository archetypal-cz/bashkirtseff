# Mme Kabniline

**Research Status**: Stub
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-31
**Diary Coverage**: Carnet 005 (May 1873, Geneva)

## Overview

A woman in Geneva from whom M. Yourkoff borrowed a riding habit (amazone) for Marie during the Bashkirtseffs' brief Geneva visit in May 1873. She appears to have been part of the Russian social circle in Geneva.

## Diary References

- **May 25, 1873** (005.0046): "M. York a proposé de me procurer une amazone chez Mme Kabniline, et un cheval."

## Research Notes

Only one mention. Kabniline (Кабнілина) appears to be a Russian surname. She lent Marie her riding costume for an afternoon excursion — a mark of social friendliness.
