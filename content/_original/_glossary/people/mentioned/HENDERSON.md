# Henderson (Mme and family)

## Overview
A family of the Nice winter colony in 1873 (nationality not stated; the name is British). Mme Henderson arrives for the season with the vicomtesse Vigier and Mme Stokes Boyd: «(Mmes Vigier, Henderson et Stokes Boyd arrivées)» (010.0051, 24 Sep 1873); three days later Marie sees «les Henderson à pied» (010.0088); on 18 December 1873 «En wagon nous sommes avec Mme Henderson» (014.0128). Mon Journal t.2 index: «Henderson (M.), 84», «Henderson (Mme), 81, 84, 243». Not identified further.

«Stokes Boyd» in 010.0051 is a third lady (t.2 index «Stokes Boyd (Mme), 81»), not the Mme Boyd of Berthe and Blanche.
