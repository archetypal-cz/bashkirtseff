## L'Italiana in Algeri

%% GLO_L_ITALIANA_IN_ALGERIA.0002 %%
Opera by Gioachino Rossini, first performed at Venice in 1813 (Wikipedia, «L'italiana in Algeri»). Marie attends it at the Nice Opera on 22 November 1874, wearing the dress of the other evening: «même robe de l'autre soir, ma coiffure, très bien. Je suis de bonne humeur» (026.0030); Count Arnim is there and Moreno with his family.
