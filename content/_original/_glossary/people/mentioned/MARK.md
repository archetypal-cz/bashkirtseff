# Mark

## Overview

Mark, a Paris horse dealer, whose stables Marie visits in September 1874. The Mon Journal t.4 index has «Mark, marchand de chevaux, 150-152, 154». He is distinct from the dealer [Maurice](MAURICE.md), who has his own index line (t.4, pp.157, 159, 161).

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Identity as horse dealer from the t.4 index; no forename or address given in the diary or index.

## References in Diary

Marie is in Paris looking for a saddle horse. On 22 September 1874 an intermediary says «Mark a un cheval convenable»; at two o'clock they go «chez Mark, mais on ferrait le cheval» (024.0045, 024.0046), and she is «heureuse et fière d'avoir été parmi les chevaux, dans les écuries chez Mark» (024.0061). On 24 September, having asked Mark to bring his horse to the Bois, she drives there in a landau and finds «une revue de nos Spadois, Ostendais et Niçois» (024.0089).
