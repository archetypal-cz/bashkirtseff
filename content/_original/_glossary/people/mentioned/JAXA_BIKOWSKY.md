## Jaxa Bikowsky

%% GLO_JAXA_BIKOWSKY.0002 %%
«Jaxa Bikowsky, ce misérable bouffon vient» on 28 September 1874, at the Paris dinner after which the deacon proposes a visit to Worms (024.0151). The same person as the «prince Jacqua de Bikowsky» of 14 May 1874 (JACQUA_DE_BIKOWSKY) and as «Jaxa» (JAXA); not to be confused with the cousin whom Marie meets in Nice and Naples in 1877 (GRYF_JAXA_BIKOWSKY). Mon Journal t.4 has no separate index entry for this mention; the earlier volumes list «Bikowsky (M.), 20-23, 25» (t.2) and «Bikowsky (prince Jacqua de), 190, 209» (t.3). See also BIKOWSKY.
