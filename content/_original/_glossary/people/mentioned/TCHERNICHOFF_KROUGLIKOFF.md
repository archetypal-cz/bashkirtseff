# Comte Tchernichoff-Krouglikoff

## Overview
A Russian count with a double name seen at the Orthodox Easter service in Nice in 1875; the Mon Journal t.5 index has «Tchernichoff-Krouglikoff (comte), 36». Nothing more is known; the double surname is not found in the other indexes.

## Relevance to Marie
At midnight on Easter night (24 April 1875, Nice), when the priest begins «Ta résurrection, Seigneur» in profound silence, «le comte Tchernichoff-Krouglikoff portait la croix, plusieurs généraux suivaient» (032.0103): a person of rank among the Russian colony.
