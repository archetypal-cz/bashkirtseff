# Mme Voyeikoff

## Overview

Mme Voyeikoff, a Russian lady at Nice, mother of Julie (Mon Journal indexes: «Voyeikoff (Mme) Julie» t.4 pp.104, 118, 120; t.5 pp.27–36; also t.2 pp.12–13).

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Julie's fiancé is not named in the paragraphs read. No outside source.

## References in Diary

In April 1875, around Orthodox Easter, Marie meets her at church: after a friendly greeting (031.0320) Mme Voyeikoff and daughter Julie spend an hour at the Bashkirtseffs' talking of Julie's fiancé, of the families, the splendid alliances and the dowry, «pas plus grande que la mienne, trois cent mille roubles» (031.0329). Marie visits her at the Hôtel du Luxembourg, adoring the view of the Promenade (032.0003), sees her at matins (032.0095, 032.0105) and notes that her aim was to meet Princess Belosselsky and the grand future relatives of Julie (032.0106).
