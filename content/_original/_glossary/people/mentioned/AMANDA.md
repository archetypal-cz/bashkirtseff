## Identification (RSR, 2026-10-21)
Amanda Sidwall (1844–1892), Swedish painter and illustrator, pupil of Tony Robert-Fleury at the Académie Julian from 1874, back in Paris from 1879 (Wikipedia, «Amanda Sidwall»; Svenskt kvinnobiografiskt lexikon). The Mon Journal t.13 index lists «Sidval (Amanda) élève atelier Julian : 99-100, 104, 107». Marie calls her «Amanda (la Suédoise forte)» (086.0296, 086/1879-09-17); they visit Bastien-Lepage's studio and the Luxembourg together (086.0308) and go to Saint-Marceaux (086/1879-10-19).
