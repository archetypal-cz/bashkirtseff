# Mme Basilewsky

## Overview

The mother of Mme Doubelt and, by that, of the other Basilewsky daughters (Princess Souvoroff, Mme Kondareff, Mme Smelsky), a Russian multimillionaire widow of seventy-one in 1883 (presumably the wife of the old [Basilewsky](BASILEWSKY.md), the gold-mine owner of 075.0121; the diary does not say so). Marie reports the gossip, which she presents as hearsay, that the Metropolitan [Isidore](ISIDORE.md) of St Petersburg, aged eighty-four, has for years been tied to her «par une amitié que l'on dit criminelle».

## In Marie's diary

- 18 September 1883 (101.0381–0382): the letter her mother obtained through Mme Doubelt from the Metropolitan to Prince Orloff «doit être bonne», since «le saint père est lié depuis des années à Mme Basilewsky (mère de Mme Doubelt)»; «Mme Basilewsky est archi-millionnaire, elle a soixante et onze ans et le Métropolite en a quatre-vingt-quatre, il s'appelle Isidore» (see METROPOLITAN, MME_DOUBELT).
