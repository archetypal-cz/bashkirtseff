# Albin Body (Spa)

## Overview

Albin Body is the young man «du pays» of the Spa season of 1874 who dances and walks with Marie's party. The Mon Journal indexes disagree on his nationality: t.3 «Body (Albin), Belge» (with «Body (frère du précédent)»), t.4 and t.6 «Body (Albin), Anglais». The best candidate is Albin Body (Spa, 17 October 1836 – Spa, 18 December 1916), historian and archivist of the town of Spa, author of the *Bibliographie spadoise* (1875) and *Le Théâtre et la musique à Spa* (1885) (French Wikipedia, «Albin Body», https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albin_Body). Marie's «M. Body (du pays)» (020.0420) and the dry, bespectacled wit of 020.0474 fit a scholar of the town; confidence 0.75, since Marie never names his occupation.

## Relevance to Marie

- **28 June 1874.** At Gambart's dinner at Spa: «un M. Body (du pays) et un Anglais dont j'oublie le nom... Wheelwright» (020.0420).
- **30 June 1874.** Dances with Marie, Basilévitch and Paul «une contredanse fantaisie» (020.0468) and is one of the four who gather round them (020.0470).
- **1 July 1874.** «M. Body, un homme sec, à lunettes, faisant beaucoup d'esprit et en ayant moins» (020.0474); Marie says that a Body is the intimate friend of the other brother of Princess Eristoff (020.0478) and that the princess «veut se divorcer pour épouser un Body» (020.0509, one of the Body brothers); he walks with the princess on 5 July (021.0003).
- **5 July 1874.** On the outing he speaks ill of M. de Neufarge (021.0004). On 10 July Body says that Bagatelle is a real bulldog (021.0099).
