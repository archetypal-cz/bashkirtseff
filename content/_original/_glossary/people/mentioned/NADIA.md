# Nadia

**Research Status**: Basic
**Last Updated**: 2026-10-22
**Diary Coverage**: September 1873 – May 1874 (Nice)

## Identity
Mlle Nadia Galitzine, the young daughter of the princess Nathalie Galitzine, Marie's friend «Bête» ([#Bete](BETE.md)). Mon Journal t.2 index: «Galitzine (Mlle Nadia), [Nadinka], [Bijou], fille» (pp. 51, 61, 87, 96, 144, 161, 174, 251, 259–260); t.3 index: «Galitzine (Mlle Nadia), [Bijou]» and «Bijou: Galitzine (Mlle Nadia)». She is with her mother at the Bashkirtseffs' in Nice («Je couche Nadia au salon jaune», 009.0183) and in their theatre box («moi, Dina, Nadia, Bête et Bijou», 019.0160); «Depuis deux jours la pauvre Nadia est malade et la princesse ne vient pas» (016/1874-02-04).

## Names
Marie also writes «Nadinka» (012.0075, 012.0207; tagged NADINKA, a duplicate stub for 1873; the «Nadinka» who writes about Paul in 1875, 031.0307, is someone else). The edition's indexes give her the nickname «Bijou», but «Bijou» is also the name of a dog (see [#Bijou](BIJOU.md)); in the passages checked Bijou is the dog except perhaps 010.0210.
