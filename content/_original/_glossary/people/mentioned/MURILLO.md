# Murillo

## Overview

Bartolomé Esteban Murillo (1617–1682), Spanish Baroque painter of Seville, famous for his Virgins and Immaculate Conceptions and for his street children (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartolomé_Esteban_Murillo).

## References in Diary

Marie's taste cools over the years. In September 1875 in the museum at Florence a Murillo Virgin with the Child attracts her: «c'est frais, c'est naturel» (043.0169). In October 1881 she sees «une salle unique pleine de Murillo»: «Murillo ne me dit pas grand chose je l'avoue, ces Vierges aux joues roses et aux visages ronds» (093.0275). In October 1882 a visitor praises Murillo's «magie de sa peinture» but, she says, Velázquez is the one who painted most admirably (096.0381).
