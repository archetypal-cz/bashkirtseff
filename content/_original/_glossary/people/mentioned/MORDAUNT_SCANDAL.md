# Mordaunt Scandal

## Overview

The Mordaunt divorce case in England: Sir Charles Mordaunt's suit against his wife Harriet and the co-respondents Viscount Cole and Sir Frederick Johnstone, tried in 1869–70 with the Prince of Wales called as a witness (he denied any impropriety); the divorce was refused on the ground of Lady Mordaunt's insanity and finally granted in 1875 (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harriet_Mordaunt; en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir_Charles_Mordaunt,_10th_Baronet). Not a person; see [Lady Mordaunt](../aristocracy/LADY_MORDAUNT.md) and [Johnstone](SIR_FREDERIC_JOHNSTONE.md).
