# Marguerite de Valois

## Overview

Marguerite de Valois (1553–1615), daughter of Henri II and Catherine de Médicis, first wife of Henri IV (married 1572, marriage annulled 1599), known as «la reine Margot». Her *Mémoires*, covering her life up to about 1582, were first published in 1628 (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marguerite_de_Valois; fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marguerite_de_Valois).
