# Pecoule

## Overview
M. Pecoule, the *gérant* (manager or caretaker) of the villa at Nice that the family occupies from 30 January 1882. The Mon Journal t.14 index lists him as «Pecoule (M., gérant)» (p.283); he appears nowhere else.

## Relevance to Marie
On arriving at night from Paris with Paul, Nini, Dina, Rosalie, Basile and Coco, Marie finds the house ready: «Nous trouvons tout préparé et gérant M. Pecoule, avec des bouquets» (094.0219). The villa lies «en pleine campagne», ten minutes from the Promenade des Anglais, with a terrace, gardens and a tramway passing the garden. The diary does not name the villa, and nothing more is known of M. Pecoule.
