## Overview

%% GLO_SOPHIE_CROIZETTE.0002 %%
Sophie Croizette (1847-1901) was a leading actress of the Comédie-Française in the 1870s, famous for her beauty and for her rivalry with Sarah Bernhardt, whom she is usually remembered alongside as a star of Octave Feuillet's *Le Sphinx* (1874). She left the stage about 1882 and married a banker. (Dates and career summary as given in the web sources consulted, which disagree on some details such as the year she joined the troupe; not checked against the Comédie-Française archives.)

## Relevance to Marie

%% GLO_SOPHIE_CROIZETTE.0004 %%
Marie sees her at the Théâtre-Français on 18 June 1874 in a double bill, «Le village» and «Le sphinx»: «voilà un théâtre: Croizette est admirable» (020.0360; see [#Le Sphinx](../../culture/theater/LE_SPHINX.md) and [#Théâtre-Français](../../places/theaters/THEATRE_FRANCAIS.md)). Four years later, at the Julian atelier, she describes a model or fellow student as «Une femme qui ressemble un peu à Croizette» (079.0083, 25 March 1878), taking the actress as a known beauty type.
