# Botkine

Currently covers two Botkin brothers; see Identity below.

## Identity: two brothers under one file (split pending)

This file is tagged for two different men, both called "Botkine" by Marie:

1. **Mikhail Petrovich Botkin (1839-1914)**, painter, engraver and collector (Wikipedia, "Mikhail Botkin"), brother of the physician. Marie calls him "Michel Botkine" (030.0423, 31 March 1875) and, in Rome, "Botkine, le frère du médecin de l'impératrice ... un laid qui s'occupe de peinture" (051.0354, 4 January 1876). In Rome in 1876 he introduces the family to the Russian artists (051-059). The file's folder (people/artists) fits him.
2. **Sergei Petrovich Botkin (1832-1889)**, the physician to Empress Maria Alexandrovna whom Maman consults at San Remo in the winter of 1874-75 ("Botkine le médecin de l'impératrice", 028.0077; carnets 028-030). The tags on the Nice paragraphs of 028-030 mean him. people/mentioned/BOTKIN.md already holds the doctor (053, 064).

The Mon Journal tome 4 index has "Botkine, (Michel) médecin" and "Botkine, (M.), frère du précédent", which conflates the two brothers; Marie's own text keeps them apart (030.0423 names Michel, 051.0354 calls the painter the doctor's brother), and so does Kernberger (2013, glossary: "Botkine: doctor who treats Marie's mother in Nice"; "Botkine: brother of doctor, acquaintance in Rome").
