# Henri Chapu

## Overview
Henri-Michel-Antoine Chapu (1833–1891), French sculptor, Prix de Rome 1855, member of the Académie des Beaux-Arts from 1880; he taught at the Académie Julian (Wikipedia, «Henri Chapu»).

## Relevance to Marie
- 19 November 1879: Tony Robert-Fleury, encouraging Marie to model in the evenings, promises that «un soir il m'amènera Chapu» (086.0552). The Mon Journal t.13 index reads this as «Chapu : modèle atelier Julian», p.123, which does not fit a man brought to give advice; the sculptor is meant (0.8).
- 18 April 1883: in a list of sculptors who can «pétrir une figure avec du limon» but cannot give it a soul, as Saint-Marceaux can: «Chapu, Dubois, Falguière, Barrias, Millet» (099.0156). The Mon Journal t.15 index has «Chapu (Henri), peintre : 224».
