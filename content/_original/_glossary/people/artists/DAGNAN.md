## Dagnan, a painter of Bastien-Lepage's circle (1883)

%% GLO_DAGNAN.0002 %%
A painter whom Marie weighs among the Salon names of 1883. The diary gives only the surname "Dagnan"; the identification with the painter Pascal Dagnan-Bouveret (1852-1929) is settled by the owner's ruling of 2026-10-03.
