## Dagnan, a painter of Bastien-Lepage's circle (1883)

%% GLO_DAGNAN.0002 %%
A painter whom Marie weighs among the Salon names of 1883. The diary gives only the surname; an identification with Pascal Dagnan-Bouveret is plausible but NOT stated in the text and is left for the owner to confirm.
