# Johann Strauss II

## Overview
Johann Strauss II (1825–1899), the Viennese composer and conductor, «the Waltz King» (Wikipedia, «Johann Strauss II»). Not his brother Eduard, the conductor whom Marie hears at Frankfurt in 1878 ([#Edouard_Strauss](../mentioned/EDOUARD_STRAUSS.md)).

## References in Diary
- 1873-07-04 (006.0181, Nice): a concert piece «la marche égyptienne de Strauss» — his *Egyptian March* (Ägyptischer Marsch), op. 335 (1869).
- 1873-08-09 (007.0372, Vienna, during the World Exhibition): at an Exhibition concert «d'une façon inattendue Strauss (Johann) paraît. Il donne un autre caractère à l'orchestre et à l'ensemble. Chaque musicien a une face animée et riante».
- His operetta *La Tzigane* (Paris, 1877) is footnoted at 077.0051.
