# Camille Lefèvre

## Overview

Camille Lefèvre (1853–1933), French sculptor, who gave Marie her first sculpture lessons in Paris in June 1880 on Julian's recommendation. Pupil of Pierre-Jules Cavelier and Aimé Millet at the École des beaux-arts (from 1872); second Grand Prix de sculpture in 1877 and 1878, but never the first prize (en.wikipedia «Camille Lefèvre»; INHA AGORHA notice 606).

## Identity

Marie introduces him herself: «Monsieur Camille Lefèvre, un second prix de Rome, et en train de concourir pour attraper le premier, à moi recommandé par Julian, me donne la première leçon de sculpture» (089.0022, 23 June 1880). The next day she is already modelling a figure over an iron armature, and on «les jours où vient Lefèbvre, nous travaillons de sept heures à dix» (089/1880-06-24). Her lessons with Mlle Marie Delsarte, another early teacher, followed ([MARIE_DELSARTE](../mentioned/MARIE_DELSARTE.md)).

The Mon Journal t.13 index files him as «Lefèvre (Camille), peintre : 245, 295»; the lessons show he is a sculptor. He is not the Nice doctor [LEFEVRE](../mentioned/LEFEVRE.md) and not the painter Jules Lefebvre of the Académie Julian ([LEFEBVRE](LEFEBVRE.md)), whom Marie also spells «Lefèvre».
