# Titian

## Overview

Tiziano Vecellio (c. 1488/90 – 1576), the leading painter of the Venetian Renaissance (Wikipedia, "Titian"). Marie writes «Titien» or «le Titien».

## Relevance to Marie

In the galleries of Florence and Rome in 1875–76 Titian is the painter she names most, and in the Doria gallery she calls him «mon peintre favori» (053.0378, 4 Feb 1876). What she admires in him is mostly his women: their red-gold hair, fleshy arms and wrists and warm skin, which she sets against Raphael («ce pauvre Raphaël», 043.0167). In 1876 she builds a theory of blonde and dark beauty around his figures (061.0187–0192). By 1881 she has changed her mind and dismisses «les chairs […] fiques mais bêtes du Titien» next to Rubens (093.0207).

- 053.0358 (Rome, 3 Feb 1876): «des Titien avec ces beaux cheveux roux, et ces poignets énormes».
- 053.0378 (Rome, 4 Feb 1876, Doria gallery): «Une Madeleine du Titien, mon peintre favori».
- 061.0187, 061.0189 (Nice, 26 May 1876): the blonde as «la femme matérielle par excellence … que Titien peint si bien», and her memory of *Sacred and Profane Love*, then in the Palazzo Borghese («l'amour pur et l'amour impur»).
- 093.0207 (1881): «les chairs ignobles de Rubens et des chairs [Mot noirci:magni]fiques mais bêtes du Titien».
- 093.0211 (1881): a singer is «un être superbe, un Titien».
- 093.0309 (1881): «comme François 1er avec le Titien» (the anecdote of the king and the painter's brush).
