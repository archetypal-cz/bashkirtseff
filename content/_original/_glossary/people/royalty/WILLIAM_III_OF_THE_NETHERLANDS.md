# William III of the Netherlands

## Overview
William III (Willem III, 1817-1890), King of the Netherlands and Grand Duke of Luxembourg from 1849. His first wife, Queen Sophie, died in June 1877; he then lived openly with the French soprano Émilie Ambre, whom he made "comtesse d'Ambroise" and for whom he kept a house at Meudon near Paris, and announced a morganatic marriage that his ministers made him give up. In 1879 he married Emma of Waldeck and Pyrmont. Sources: Wikipedia, "William III of the Netherlands" and "Émilie Ambre".
