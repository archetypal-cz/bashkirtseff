# Queen Louise of Prussia

%% 2026-10-22T22:16:12 RSR: Body rewritten in the stub-pass apply (2026-10-22): the entry was split; the other referent is tagged elsewhere or carries an RSR note. %%
Louise of Mecklenburg-Strelitz (1776–1810), Queen of Prussia, wife of Frederick William III. On 29 July 1876, near Berlin, Marie's party visits «le mausolée du roi Frédéric et de la reine Louise» (064.0307). The mausoleum of the king and queen stands in the park of Charlottenburg Palace, so Marie confused it with Schloss Bellevue (diary footnote 1 at 064.0307, citing Wikipedia «Mausoleum (Charlottenburg Palace Park)»).

The chimpanzee «Louise» of 12 June 1874 (020.0217) is not this person; that paragraph carries an RSR note and no tag.
