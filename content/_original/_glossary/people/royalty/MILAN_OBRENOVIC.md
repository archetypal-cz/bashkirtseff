# Milan Obrenović (Milan I of Serbia)

## Overview

Milan Obrenović (1854–1901), Prince of Serbia from 1868 and King of Serbia (as Milan I) from 1882 until his abdication in 1889. On 17 October 1875 (New Style; 5 October Old Style) he married, in Belgrade, Natalija Keško, the girl Marie calls "Kechko", who was his second cousin (Wikipedia, "Milan I of Serbia"; "Natalie of Serbia").

## Relevance to Marie

Marie never meets him. He matters to her as the prince who turns "Kechko", a girl of her own age and milieu whom she sees in Paris, into a reigning princess "comme par un coup de baguette". The engagement and wedding feed her envy and her sense that "il pleut des bonheurs" on everyone but her.
