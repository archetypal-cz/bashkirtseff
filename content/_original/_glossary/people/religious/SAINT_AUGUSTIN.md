# Saint Augustine (Augustin)

## Overview
Saint Augustine of Hippo (354–430), Father of the Church, author of the *Confessions*. In Rome on 18 February 1876, at the Capitol ball, Marie and Pietro Antonelli talk «de saint Augustin et de l'abbé Prévost» (054.0279), the Father of the Church side by side with the author of *Manon Lescaut* (footnote there).
