# Providence

## Overview

Divine Providence is God's care for and government of the world: the Christian belief that events are guided by God's purpose rather than by chance. In nineteenth-century French «la Providence» is used both for God himself in this role and, with a small letter, for any person who rescues or provides for others («être la providence de quelqu'un»). See [#God](GOD.md).

## Relevance to Marie

- **086.0526 (14 November 1879):** the tagged passage. In a bitter reflection Marie wonders whether God is «la nature même» and life is ruled by «le Hasard», which sometimes brings coincidences «qui font croire en une Providence».
