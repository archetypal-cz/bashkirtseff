# Robert Milton (Albert de Saint-Albin, 1843-1901)

## Overview
«Robert Milton» was the pen name of Albert de Saint-Albin (1843-1901), a French turf journalist who wrote race reports and sporting chronicles, later in Le Figaro, and published Les courses de chevaux en France (Hachette, 1890) as "A. de Saint-Albin (Robert Milton)" (Wikipedia, "Albert de Saint-Albin"; Gallica, BnF). Kernberger (2013, glossary): "Milton, Robert: sports reporter".

## Relevance to Marie
His report on the Nice races in Le Sport, read by Marie on 27 January 1877, described «deux petites dames appartenant au monde très aimable» in a white pony carriage, «un vrai chou à la crème» (068.0499): an insinuation that Marie and Dina were demi-mondaines. The family consulted their lawyer Malausséna and approached M. de Villemessant (068.0504-0509, 068.0515). Milton's next report (068.0552) half-retracted: the young ladies belonged «au meilleur monde russe», but the equipage remained «un parfait à la vanille». Marie suspects he was put up to it (068.0502).
