# Alphonse Karr

## Overview
Jean-Baptiste Alphonse Karr (1808-1890), French journalist and novelist, founder of the satirical monthly *Les Guêpes*, which the BnF dates 1839-1849; Wikipedia records a failed attempt to revive it in 1852-55 and his move to Nice in 1855, where he took up flower-growing (Wikipedia, «Jean-Baptiste Alphonse Karr»; data.bnf.fr, «Les Guêpes (1839)»). The Mon Journal t.9 index calls him "Karr (Alphonse), journaliste".

## Relevance to Marie
Mme Tutcheff claims that the scandal around Marie's family began when Karr told the Romanoff lawsuit "d'une façon assez singulière dans ses 'Guêpes'"; Marie answers that he wrote at the instigation of Mme Boutowsky and Mme Bezobrasoff (067.0283). In April 1877 she dismisses an argument as "emprunté à Alphonse Karr" (070/1877-04-17).
