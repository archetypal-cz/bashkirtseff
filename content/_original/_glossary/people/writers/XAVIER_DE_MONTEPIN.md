# Xavier de Montépin

## Overview
Xavier de Montépin (1823-1902), French author of popular serial novels (romans-feuilletons) full of crime and melodrama, among them *La Porteuse de pain* (1884) (Wikipedia, «Xavier de Montépin»).

## Relevance to Marie
For Marie the name stands for vulgar sensational fiction. In Russia in 1876 she "swallows" one of his books "par mégarde" and feels sick (067.0021); a Poltava suitor's declaration "sentait de dix lieues son Xavier de Montépin traduit en russe" (064/1876-08-16); uncle Alexandre warns that her father's sisters could drug her "comme dans les romans de M. Xavier de Montépin" (067.0265).
