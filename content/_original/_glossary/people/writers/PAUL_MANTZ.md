# Paul Mantz

## Overview

Paul Mantz (1821–1895), French art critic and historian, art critic of *Le Temps* and director of the Beaux-Arts in 1882 (en.wikipedia «Paul Mantz»). Marie calls him «ce grand critique».

## Relevance to Marie

At the Salon of 1884, word reaches Marie through the Abbema family that he admires her *Meeting*: «Mme Abbema qui me dit que son beau-frère M. Paul Mantz (du Temps, vous savez bien) me trouve beaucoup de talent», and his daughter is introduced to say the same (104.0186); «Abbema et la fille de Paul Mantz en me disant ce que ce grand critique pense de moi» (104.0187); Mme Gaillard is «dans l'enthousiasme de ce que M. Paul Mantz lui a dit de moi» (104.0217).
