[Mathilde Blind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathilde_Blind) (1841–1896), German-born English poet and woman of letters, produced the first English translation of Marie's diary: *The Journal of Marie Bashkirtseff* (London: Cassell, 1890), made from the censored [1887 Charpentier edition](../../culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) and abridged further still. It remained the only English version until [Katherine Kernberger](../../culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md)'s unabridged translation of 2013.

## Significance for This Project

%% GLO_MATHILDE_BLIND.0004 %%
Her Introduction to the 1890 volume is quoted in several glossary entries as an early English witness to Marie's reception: it calls the diary "a book in the nude, breathing and palpitating with life" and remarks on Marie's facility for languages and the range of her personal library (see the [French](../../culture/languages/FRENCH.md) and [English](../../culture/languages/ENGLISH.md) language entries). Because Blind worked from the 1887 text, her version inherits all of its cuts; passages absent there, such as the Georges material, are absent in Blind too.
