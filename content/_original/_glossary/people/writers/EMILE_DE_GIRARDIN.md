# Émile de Girardin

**Research Status**: Basic
**Last Updated**: 2026-10-21
**Diary Coverage**: 1878-07 to 1883-08 (died 27 April 1881)

## Overview

Émile de Girardin (1802-1881) was the founder of the modern French popular press (*La Presse*, 1836), a deputy for Paris (9th arrondissement) from 7 November 1877 until his death on 27 April 1881 (Wikipedia «Émile de Girardin»; BnF authority record, as recorded in the RSR note at 090.0397). The diary's own footnotes give 1802 for his birth (090/1880-11-13, 091/1881-02-05) and his death at 79 (091/1881-04-29). Marie meets him in December 1880 and at the Juvisy dinner of February 1881, reads his brochure *L'égale de l'homme*, and hears of his death and will from Mme de Brimont.

## In Marie's diary

- 1878-07-03: Géry, friend of Prince Jérôme, offers to introduce her to Girardin; «Le Pays» attacks «le Journal de M. de Girardin» (081.0296). 1879-10-06: «le vieux Géry … m'a offert de me présenter Emile de Girardin. Oui, mais rien n'arrive» (086.0354). 1879-10-22: she reads the account of a dinner at Girardin's for Gladstone (086.0409); 1879-11-15: «je parle d'Emile de Girardin, de politique, je m'insinue» (086.0534); 1879-12-26: she would ask to be introduced only to «un Gambetta, un Girardin, un Dumas» (087.0055).
- 1880-05-10: gossip that «la petite Juvisy est la fille d'Emile de Girardin» and of the canoness who passes for her sister; «Je n'y crois pas» (088.0145). Repeated at the dinner of 1881-02-05 (091.0082).
- 1880-11-13 and 12-02: the Juvisy ladies want Marie to dine with him (090.0218, 090.0301). 1880-11-21: reads his brochure *L'égale de l'homme*, «filandreux … d'une logique qui n'est pas logique du tout. Mais malgré tout c'est bon puisque c'est en faveur de l'égalité» (090.0260).
- 1880-12-16: Mme de Brimont introduces him at her oriental salon; Marie fears not hearing him; he advises against painting Hubertine Auclert («elle est laide») and says opinion is not yet ready for her cause (090.0396-0397).
- 1881-01-01: the scandal in which Mme Gros, widow Duvergier de Hauranne, accuses Mme de Brimont of Prussian espionage; «Girardin a obtenu pleine satisfaction à la Chambre» (090.0510-0511).
- 1881-02-05: the dinner at the Juvisys', 17 guests; «voici Emile de Girardin, âgé de Dieu sait combien d'années, il marche, cause et flirte encore» (091.0080-0084). 1881-04-06: his remark that the Cassagnac-Mitchell quarrel is «la déconsidération de l'injure» (091.0430).
- 1881-04-29: Mme de Brimont, affected, tells her «tous les détails» of his death (091.0535). 1881-05-15: «le testament de Girardin» is one of the stories the two women swap that evening, with magnetising and costume fittings (092.0010, 092/1881-05-15). Marie gives no details of the will.
- 1883-08-20: in the marriage scene with Saint-Amand, Marie lists the advantages of a husband who leaves her free: «M. Récamier, M. de Girardin etc.» (101.0195) and «Saint-Amand-Récamier-Girardin» (101.0212). Probably Girardin as the husband of the salon hostess Delphine Gay, with Récamier as the husband of Juliette Récamier (inference; confidence about 0.6).

## Not to be confused with

- The comte de Girardin, member of the Jockey Club, fiancé of the comtesse de Bailleul: «le comte de Girardin (rien d'Emile)» (087.0832, 1880-04-11; 088.0004, 1880-04-24).
- Mme E. de Girardin (presumably Delphine Gay, Émile's wife, the writer and hostess; her *Lettres parisiennes* are presumably meant by «lettre XV») Marie reads on 1880-08-20 (089.0395) (not checked further).
