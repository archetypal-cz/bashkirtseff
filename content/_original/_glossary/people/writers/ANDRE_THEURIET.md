[André Theuriet](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9_Theuriet) (1833–1907) was a French poet and novelist, later a member of the Académie française. For this project he matters twice over: as a writer Marie read and quoted, and as the man her mother enlisted after Marie's death to edit the diary for publication. The two-volume [1887 Charpentier edition](../../culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) that made Marie famous, and that cut roughly three quarters of what she wrote, appeared under his editorship, with a preface by François Coppée.
