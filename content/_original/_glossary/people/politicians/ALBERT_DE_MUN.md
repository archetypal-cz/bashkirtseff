# Albert de Mun

## Overview

Comte Albert de Mun (1841–1914), cavalry officer turned legitimist and Catholic deputy for Pontivy (Morbihan) from 1876 (English Wikipedia, «Albert de Mun»). The index of *Mon Journal* t.12 lists «Mun (Albert, comte de) : 159». Per the Encyclopædia Universalis («Mun Albert de (1841-1914)»), his Pontivy election was invalidated, he was re-elected, was invalidated a second time in 1878, and was re-elected in 1881.
