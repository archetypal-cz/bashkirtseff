# Count Harry von Arnim

## Overview
%% 2026-10-22T22:16:12 RSR: Entry narrowed to the diplomat in the stub-pass apply (2026-10-22); the four Nice paragraphs of the Prussian count of 1874–75 are now tagged COUNT_ARNIM. %%
Count Harry von Arnim (Harry Karl Kurt Eduard von Arnim-Suckow, 1824–1881), German ambassador in Paris from 1872 until his recall in 1874. Bismarck had him prosecuted for keeping embassy documents («the Arnim affair», 1874–75); he was convicted and died in exile at Nice in 1881 (en.wikipedia «Harry von Arnim»).

## In Marie's diary
- 30 August 1877: of the baron von Bechman, an embassy attaché in Paris «qui a été décoré par la France pour des trahisons et qui est rentré en faveur de Bismarck en lui livrant le comte d'Arnim» (074.0144). This is Marie's report of gossip about the Arnim affair.
