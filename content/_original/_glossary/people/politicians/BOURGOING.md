# Baron Philippe de Bourgoing

## Overview

Philippe La Beaume, baron de Bourgoing (1827–1882), Grand Squire (premier écuyer) to Napoleon III, later inspector of the Haras, and five times Bonapartist deputy for the Nièvre; he died in Paris on 20 April 1882 (English Wikipedia, «Philippe La Beaume de Bourgoing»). The index of *Mon Journal* t.12 lists «Bourgoing (Philippe, baron), député : 174, 181».

## Relevance to Marie

- 1878-12-07 (083.0365): maman and ma tante meet him and Marshal Canrobert at the theatre with the Gavinis.
- 1878-12-25 (083.0463): Marie regrets missing a performance where «il y avait Canrobert, Fleury, de Bourgoin, Duruy».

## Not the same person

The «jeune M. de Bourgoing» whom Marshal Canrobert has just taken as his officier d'ordonnance (105, 1884-07-30) and «M. de Bourgoing et sa sœur Mme Fortoul» (105, 1884-08-03) cannot be Philippe, who died in 1882; they are not linked to this entry.
