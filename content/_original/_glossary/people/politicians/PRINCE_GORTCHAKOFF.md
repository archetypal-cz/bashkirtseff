# Prince Alexander Gorchakov (Gortchakoff)

## Overview
Prince Alexander Mikhailovich Gorchakov (1798–1883), Russian foreign minister and chancellor of the Empire. The Mon Journal t.13 index lists «Gortchakoff (prince Alexndre Mikhaïlovitch) : 121, 316» [sic].

## Relevance to Marie
- 15 November 1879: «Chaudordy est resté très tard à raconter des histoires sur M. Thiers et Gortchakoff» (086.0541).
- 23 July 1880: Marie was urged to write to Prince Ouroussoff «pour lui demander une lettre de Gortchakoff pour Orloff» (089.0216), the chancellor being a means to reach the ambassador in Paris.
- 1878: a correspondent cites «le prince Gortchakoff» as an example of Russian name usage (081/1878-07-22).
- Other «Gortchakoff» mentions (052/1876-01-18, 058/1876-04-18) were not checked and may be other members of the family.
