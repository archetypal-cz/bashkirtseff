# Jules Ferry

## Overview

Jules Ferry (1832–1893), republican statesman: Minister of Public Instruction from February 1879, président du Conseil in 1880–1881 and 1883–1885, the architect of the secular school laws and of French colonial expansion (Tonkin). On 15 March 1879 he tabled the bill on higher education whose Article 7 barred members of unauthorised religious congregations, above all the Jesuits, from teaching; the Chamber passed it, the Senate struck out Article 7, and the law of 18 March 1880 was passed without it (Encyclopædia Universalis, «Jules Ferry»; English Wikipedia, «Jules Ferry»).

Not the same as [FERRY](../../places/social/FERRY.md), the bootmaker: «Je suis allée chez Ferry» (085.0229) and «Jeandron (M. Ferry)» (085.0231), Marie's informant about the Acard family, belong to the shop.

## Relevance to Marie

Marie follows the Ferry bill as a spectator in the Chamber gallery in 1879, on the Bonapartist side, and later sees Ferry as minister and prime minister.

- 1879-06-16 (085.0328): «Il s'agit comme vous le savez du projet de loi Jules Ferry», the sitting at which Cassagnac is censured and excluded; 085.0352: Cassagnac «ne pourra pas discuter les lois Ferry».
