# Charles-Alphonse Levert

## Overview

Bonapartist politician, a prefect under the Second Empire (prefect of the Bouches-du-Rhône 1866–1870, per the prefecture's list of prefects) and deputy for the Pas-de-Calais from 1872 to 1889, voting with the Bonapartist right against the republican governments (English Wikipedia, «Charles Levert», which gives his death as 1899). The index of *Mon Journal* t.13 lists «Levert (Charles Alphonse) homme politique : 36».

Not the same person as [Mlle Levert](../aristocracy/MLLE_LEVERT.md), who married the deputy Sarlande (084.0207: «Sarlande le député qui a épousé il y a un an Mlle Levert»); that she was his daughter is not established here.

## Relevance to Marie

One of the Bonapartist deputies of the Gavini circle who escort Marie to the Chamber at Versailles.

- 1878-12-07 (083.0362): in the omnibus from the Chamber to the station «M. Levert un ancien préfet badinguisard et le ministre de l'intérieur de Marcère».
- 1879-06-16 (085.0327): with Janvier de la Motte, one of the «cavaliers» of Marie and her mother at the sitting where Cassagnac is excluded.
