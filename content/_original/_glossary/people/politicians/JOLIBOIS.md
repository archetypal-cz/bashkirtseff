# Eugène Jolibois

## Overview

Bonapartist deputy for the Charente-Inférieure. The index of *Mon Journal* t.12 lists «Jolibois (Eugène), homme politique : 154». English Wikipedia («Eugène Jolibois») gives him as deputy from 1876 to 1893, first elected for the second constituency of Saintes on 20 February 1876 with the backing of Baron Eugène Eschassériaux.

## Relevance to Marie

- 1878-11-08 (083.0139): one of the Bonapartist deputies (with Abbatucci, Amigues, Eschassériaux, Bouville) on the train back from Versailles, introduced by Gavini.
- 1883-02-01 (098.0259): with Paul Leroux, Roy de Loulay and Gavini, «réquisitionné» to get Marie's party into the President's gallery of the Chamber.
