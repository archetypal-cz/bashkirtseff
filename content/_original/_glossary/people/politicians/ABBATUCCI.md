# Jean-Charles Abbatucci

## Overview

Corsican lawyer and Bonapartist politician. The index of *Mon Journal* t.12 lists him as «Abbatucci (Jean-Charles), homme politique : 154-155». English Wikipedia («Jean-Charles Abbatucci (politician)») gives him as a deputy under the Second Republic and again under the Third, representative in 1872–1876 and deputy of Corsica from 14 October 1877 to 27 October 1881, a Bonapartist throughout.

## Relevance to Marie

One of the Bonapartist deputies Gavini introduces on the train back from Versailles after Cassagnac's speech on 7 November 1878 (083.0139). In the conversation about Julia Acard's looks that Marie writes up on 9 November he cries «Quelle mauvaise langue ce Gavini !»; Marie calls him «un drôle de type» (083.0155).

Not to be confused with Mme Abbatucci, met at the theatre with Mme Gavini in December 1879 ([MME_ABBATUCCI](../mentioned/MME_ABBATUCCI.md), 086); whether she is his wife is not shown in the diary.
