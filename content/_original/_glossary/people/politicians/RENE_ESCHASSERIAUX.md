# René Eschassériaux

## Overview

René-Pierre Eschassériaux, Bonapartist deputy for the arrondissement of Jonzac (Charente-Inférieure), elected in 1877 and sitting with the Appel au peuple group; son of baron Eugène Eschassériaux (1823–1906), himself deputy for the same department ([ESCHASSERIAUX](../mentioned/ESCHASSERIAUX.md)) (fr.wikipedia, «Élections législatives de 1877 en Charente-Inférieure», Jonzac; genealogical summaries on Geneanet, «René François Eugène Eschassériaux» tree, which make the deputy René-Pierre the baron's son). Confidence 0.85. The Mon Journal t.12 index has «Eschassériaux (René) fils du précédent, homme politique : 199». His dates and later career were not found (searched 2026-10-22; one source says he withdrew from the Jonzac contest of 21 August 1881).

## Relevance to Marie

- 083.0096 (3 November 1878): the Gavinis' nephew asks «le député Eschassériaux fils» for gallery tickets to the Chamber.
- 084.0029–0038 (15 January 1879): introduced to Marie at the Opéra by the Gavinis: «Eschassériaux, fils du baron et député pareillement … Eschassériaux, René comme l'appelle Mme Gavini a une des plus belles têtes qu'on puisse voir», but badly dressed. Marie writes him a mocking note about his frock coat, addressed «à M. R. Eschassériaux député» (084.0033–0035), and admits she found him handsome because something in him recalled Cassagnac (084.0038).
