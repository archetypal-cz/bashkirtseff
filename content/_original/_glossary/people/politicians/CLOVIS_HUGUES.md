# Clovis Hugues (1851–1907) and Mme Clovis Hugues

## Overview
Clovis Hugues was a Provençal poet and journalist, a félibre, and socialist deputy for Marseille from 1881 (re-elected 1885) (Wikipedia, "Clovis Hugues"). His wife, Jeanne Royannez (b. 1855), was a sculptor; they married in 1877 (Wikipedia, "Jeanne Royannez"). In 1883-84 a private detective named Morin spread slanders about her past; in November 1884 she shot and killed him, and her trial was a sensation (Mary Evans Picture Library, captions after *L'Illustration*, 1884; *Auckland Star*, 7 February 1885, via Papers Past). Marie does not live to see the shooting; she knows only the slander.

## Relevance to Marie
Marie meets the couple in the republican and artistic circle around the deputy Gaillard in 1883:
- **21 March 1883** (098.0545-0549): at a dinner with deputies; Clovis Hugues does imitations of the orators of the Chamber; "Mme Clovis Hugues a chanté aussi et faux. Une femme énorme, beaucoup plus grande que son gringalet de mari".
- **1 and 9 May 1883** (099.0300-0301, 099.0382-0394): met at the Salon (she thinks of painting his portrait); a "dîner républicano-artistico-poétique" at the Bashkirtseffs' where he recites verse and imitates Gambetta.
- **16 May 1884** (104.0189-0191): met at the Salon; "Lui a l'air très malheureux. C'est cette affreuse affaire d'il y a quelques mois" (the slander against his wife).
- **17 May 1884** (104.0212-0216): their party for Frédéric Mistral, full of félibres; Marie dances a polka with Clovis Hugues.
- A cousin of Mme Clovis Hugues speaks to Marie on 22 June 1883 (100.0192).
