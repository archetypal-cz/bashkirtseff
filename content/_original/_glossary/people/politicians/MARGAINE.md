# Henri Camille Margaine

## Overview

A questeur of the Chamber of Deputies in 1879–1880. Identification rests on the index of *Mon Journal* t.13, «Margaine (Henri Camille) homme politique) : 37, 148», and on Marie's «M. Margaine le questeur» (087.0462). His constituency and dates were not found in the sources searched (web, 2026-10-21); check Sycomore (Assemblée nationale) or Robert & Cougny, *Dictionnaire des parlementaires*.

## Relevance to Marie

- 1879-06-16 (085.0332–0337): during the uproar at the sitting where Cassagnac is excluded, Margaine has the corridors cleared («huissiers, faites sortir le public !») and argues with Janvier de la Motte: «laissez-moi donc faire respecter la loi !».
- 1880-02-12 (087.0462): Marie brings him a note from Dr Krishaber; after «péripéties nombreuses avec Bescherelle, Margaine etc.» she is seated in the ministers' box; «ce Margaine est un homme aimable».
