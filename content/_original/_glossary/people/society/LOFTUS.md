## Loftus (young Englishman)

%% GLO_LOFTUS.0002 %%
A young Englishman, «lord Loftus» for Marie, handsome but «paraît bête» (031.0249, 16 April 1875, Nice, with Lord and Lady Folkner), whom she recalls from Baden-Baden as a child, often with Lady Folkner (050.0705). Marie also notes in January 1874 that «Loftus le frère de cette Loftus à laquelle Hamilton faisait la cour à Bade» was at the Bashkirtseff soirée (015.0028). In December 1875 she says that he lacks vigour and his profile makes an angle (051.0010). The young man is most likely a son of Lord Augustus Loftus (1817-1904), former ambassador at Berlin, her neighbour in Nice in autumn 1875; the identification is not settled (see LORD_LOFTUS, LORD_AUGUSTUS_LOFTUS and the RSR note at 050.0705). Mon Journal t.6 index: «Loftus (lord Augustus), ex-ambassadeur à Berlin».
