## Overview

%% GLO_ETIENNE_ALEXANDROVITCH.0002 %%
Marie's small cousin, the young son of her uncle [#Alexandre](../core/ALEXANDRE.md) Babanine and his wife Nadine, who live at [#Tcherniakovka](../../places/residences/TCHERNIAKOVKA.md). The Mon Journal t.9 index lists him as «Babanine (Etienne Alexandrovitch), cousin de Marie Bashkirtseff» (pp. 76, 78, 152, 281-282). The diary names him only as «le petit Etienne», which is why the bare tag ETIENNE (Georges's son, who died at sixteen) and the uncle Etienne (Stiopa; [#Tante Etienne](TANTE_ETIENNE.md)) are both wrong for him.
