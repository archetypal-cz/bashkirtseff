# Papa (Constantin Bashkirtseff)

**Research Status**: Basic
**Last Updated**: 2026-09-26
**See also**: [#Father_Bashkirtseff](FATHER_BASHKIRTSEFF.md) (fuller entry on the same man)

> **Disambiguation — who is "papa"?** In the 1873–1875 Nice diary (carnets 002–030 and after), Marie's bare "papa" is almost always her **maternal grandfather, Stepan Babanine** — see [#Grand_papa](GRAND_PAPA.md). Her father Constantin is "mon père", "mon (cher / auguste / illustre / honorable) père" or "mon pater". This entry is for Constantin only. Tag bare "papa" in 1873–1875 as #Grand_papa, not #Papa.

## Overview

Marie's father, Constantin Bashkirtseff (1833–1883), a nobleman and landowner of Gavronzi near Poltava (Ukraine, then part of the Russian Empire). He separated from Marie's mother in 1861 and lived apart from the family, who moved to Europe in 1870.

## In the diary, 1873–1875

Constantin is absent from the Nice household. Marie mentions him with marked forms:

- **Durocher affair** (1873-10-25, 011.0277–0284): "Mon très cher père a une actrice française Durocher…" — his public liaison in Russia.
- **Announced visit** (1873-11-07, 012.0096): "Mon cher père vient à Nice. Animal !"
- **Visit to Nice / Monte-Carlo, 24–27 November 1873** (013.0082–0109): "Mon auguste père est arrivé." Only during this visit does Marie call him "Papa" / "le Papa" / "mon adorable papa" (013.0085, 013.0093, 013.0109), always next to "mon père".
- **Family quarrels**: the grandfather reproaches "ma race, mon nom, mon père" (019.0313); Marie defends him (025.0054: "Je connais peu mon père et l'aime encore moins…").
- **Rank**: "mon père est maréchal de noblesse" (023.0208); "mon honorable père, maréchal de noblesse" (030.0128).

From 1876, when Marie visits him in Poltava and Gavronzi (carnets 065 onward), "Papa" in the diary does mean Constantin.

## Marriage History

A story told on 1 September 1873: Marie Ivanovna Perlik warned Marie's mother against marrying Bashkirtseff. The warning went unheeded, which foreshadows the separation.
