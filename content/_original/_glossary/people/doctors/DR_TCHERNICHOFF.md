# Dr Tchernichoff

## Overview
A Russian physician of the Nice colony in spring 1876, in the circle of Nina Sapogenikoff. He is not the M. Tchernichoff of 1874 ([Tchernichoff](../mentioned/TCHERNICHOFF.md)); Marie herself separates them: «M. Tchernichoff ... pas le docteur Tchernichoff enfin» (024.0122). Mon Journal t.7 index: «Tchernichoff (médecin) : 323, 343». His first name is not given.

## In Marie's diary
- 29 April 1876: after Yourkoff's suicide at Monaco, Marie Sapogenikoff sees the body «accompagnés du consul, du docteur Tchernichoff et du paysan Antonoff» (059.0621).
- 6 May 1876: in the street dance, «moi, Giro, Marie, Walitsky, Tchernichoff et Dina» (059.0828); on the excursion, «le docteur Tchernichoff. Ce laid fait beaucoup la cour à Marie» (059.0848).
- 7 May 1876: «Tchernichoff et Antonoff arrivèrent avec des bouquets» (059.0863).
- 10 May 1876: he is with her mother as far as Monte Carlo when Marie leaves for Rome (060.0004), and she takes a hundred francs from him to buy her mother an Italian costume (060.0007). The index places the doctor on these pages; the text names him without title.
