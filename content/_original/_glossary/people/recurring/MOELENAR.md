# Moelenar

## Overview

Édouard, chevalier de Moelenar, a man Marie first thought dashing and then despised (Mon Journal t.4 index: «Moelenar (Édouard, chevalier), 120-121, 124, 129, 225»).

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Chevalier title and forename from the t.4 index only; 'plobster' is unexplained (perhaps a misreading of 'lobster' or Marie's slang).

## References in Diary

On 28 November 1874 Marie blushes at having believed «un seul instant» that M. de Moelenar is «un plobster» (sic; Marie's word), «il n'est qu'une sale bête» (027.0068).
