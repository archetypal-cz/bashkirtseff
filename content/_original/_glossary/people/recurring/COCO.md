# Coco

**Research Status**: Basic
**Last Updated**: 2025-06-28
**Diary Coverage**: 1873-02-21

Marie's horse, which she rode during outings in Nice. Coco appears to have been temperamental and difficult to manage.

## Diary References
- **1873-02-21**: During a riding excursion with the Howards, Coco misbehaves ("faisait des bêtises"), wants to return to the stable, and stops stubbornly in front of Marie's house, forcing her to end the ride early.

## Context
Horseback riding was a fashionable activity for young ladies of Marie's social class in Nice. The fact that she had her own horse indicates her family's wealth and social standing.
