## Coco, Marie's riding horse (1873)

%% GLO_COCO.0002 %%
Marie's horse, which she rode during outings in Nice in 1873. Coco appears to have been temperamental and difficult to manage. Horseback riding was a fashionable activity for young ladies of Marie's class in Nice, and having her own horse points to her family's wealth and standing.
