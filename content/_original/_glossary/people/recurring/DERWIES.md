## The Derwies family and Valrose

Pavel Grigorievich von Derwies (1826–1881) made an enormous fortune from railway concessions (Moscow–Ryazan, Ryazan–Kozlov) and settled in Nice, where he built the Villa Valrose with its private theatre, gardens and greenhouses (Wikipedia, «Paul von Derwies»). The concert Marie longs to attend on 25 February 1874 is «le concert chez Derwies» (017.0049). The former entry VON_DERWIES (misfiled under culture/literature) is merged here.
