# Louise de La Vallière

## Overview
Louise de La Vallière (1644-1710), mistress of Louis XIV in the 1660s, made a duchess in 1667; she retired to the Carmelite convent in Paris in 1674. Source: Wikipedia, "Louise de La Vallière".

## Relevance to Marie
Marie knows her mainly through Arsène Houssaye's *Mlle de La Vallière et Mme de Montespan*, which she reads in January 1874 and identifies with ("Elle aimait le roi parce qu'il avait l'air d'un roi", 015/1874-01-19; see [Mademoiselle de La Vallière (Houssaye)](../../culture/literature/MADEMOISELLE_DE_LA_VALLIERE_HOUSSAYE.md)). Later the name serves as a type of romantic beauty: Barnola tells her she looks like La Vallière with her hair done that day (035.0188), and in 1881 she sees in her ringlets "un faux air de La Vallière" (091/1881-03-25). In 1882 she cites her, with Mary Stuart, as a worn-out painting subject that only Bastien-Lepage could bring back to life (096/1882-08-18).
