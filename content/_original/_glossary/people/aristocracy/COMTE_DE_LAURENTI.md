# Comte de Laurenti

The Nice nobleman comte Laurenti (Laurenti-Roubaudi), who courts Marie's attention in 1875–77: the full account, with 47 tagged paragraphs, is in [#Laurenti](../mentioned/LAURENTI.md). This entry holds the one paragraph tagged separately, a four-line verse portrait written at Schlangenbad on 1875-08-21 (039.0177):

> Le comte de Laurenti / Royaliste et gentilhomme / Noir, maigre, sec, petit / Et ridé comme une vieille pomme.

The description matches the other portraits of him in the diary («ce comte si noir, si petit et si grave», 037.0348; «cet être noir, sec, petit», 060.0004), and the Mon Journal t.5 index puts «Laurenti (comte), 101, 201, 203, 205, 255» together on one line. It is the same man; the two entries should be merged (see the merge list).
