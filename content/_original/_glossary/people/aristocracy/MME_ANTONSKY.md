# Mme Antonsky

%% GLO_MME_ANTONSKY.0001 %%
## Identity

%% GLO_MME_ANTONSKY.0002 %%
A Russian lady of the Nice colony whom Marie knew from 1873 to 1884. She was first married to M. Rembelinsky, by whom she had a son, and then to M. Antonsky (081/1878-07-09, 081/1878-07-13). Her daughter Sonia married M. Warrodel in late 1873 (014/1873-12-31; see [#Sophie_Warrodel](../mentioned/SOPHIE_WARRODEL.md)).

%% GLO_MME_ANTONSKY.0003 %%
## In the diary

%% GLO_MME_ANTONSKY.0004 %%
- **Nice, winter 1873–74**: meets Marie in a shop on New Year's Eve and tells her that her daughter has married M. Warrodel (014/1873-12-31). Maman returns her visit (015/1874-01-07). A rehearsal of Nagornoff's concert is held at her rooms in the hôtel de la Paix (015/1874-01-21). Further visits and meetings on the promenade (015–016, 018/1874-04-05).
- **Soden, July–August 1878**: Marie meets her again at the spa, "Mme Antonsky qui était à Nice avec sa fille que maman menait souvent aux bals", and is introduced to her son and daughter-in-law, the Rembelinskys (081/1878-07-08, 081/1878-07-09). Marie takes tea with "les Antonsky" (081/1878-07-13); their departure is noted in 082/1878-08-12.
- **Later**: writes to the family (082/1878-09-09); is in St Petersburg in 1879 (084/1879-03-05); visits in Paris (089/1880-08-31); passes through Paris in 1884, when the family takes her to see the Munkácsy painting (104/1884-05-04).
