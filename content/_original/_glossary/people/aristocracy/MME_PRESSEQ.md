## Mme Presseq (Marie Presseq, "Paul de Charry")

%% GLO_MME_PRESSEQ.0002 %%
A woman of letters of over fifty-five, art critic of the journal "Le Pays" under the pen name Paul de Charry, who lived with or worked for Paul de Cassagnac as a housekeeper ("gouvernante") according to what Mouzay told Marie. She seeks Marie's acquaintance in the spring of 1883 through the comte de la Ferrière.
