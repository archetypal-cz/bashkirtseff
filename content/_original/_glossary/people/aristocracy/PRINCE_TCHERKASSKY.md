## Schlangenbad, August 1875 (RSR, 2026-10-21)
A Russian prince staying at Schlangenbad with his wife ([Princesse Tcherkassky](PRINCESSE_TCHERKASSKY.md)) when Marie joins her mother there: «à l'entrée on me présente à la princesse Tcherkassky, puis le prince m'est présenté. Je ne les crois pas du grand monde» (039.0006, 13 August 1875); the family sups with them (039/1875-08-13). On 14 August he offers to introduce «un attaché militaire russe» and jokes that «demain il y aura un enterrement» (039.0043–0044). The Mon Journal t.5 index spells the name «Tcherkassy (prince), 243-244, 246, 254, 257, 261». Forename not given; not identified with any known Prince Cherkassky.
