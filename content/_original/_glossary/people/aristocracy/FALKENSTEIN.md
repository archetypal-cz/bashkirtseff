# La Falkenstein

**Research Status**: Basic
**Last Updated**: 2025-07-05
**Diary Coverage**: Up to 1874-02-14-15

## Identity
Member of the aristocratic society in Nice, part of Princess Souvoroff's exclusive circle.

## Diary References

### 1874
- **February 14**: Present in Princess Souvoroff's rented tribune at the Cercle de la Méditerranée concert. Part of the brilliant gathering that makes Marie feel her social isolation acutely.

## Context
- Austrian or German aristocratic name
- Part of Princess Souvoroff's intimate social circle
- Represents the exclusive society that Marie desperately wants to join
- Her presence in the tribune emphasizes Marie's exclusion from high society
- Associated with other noble figures like Galve, Rudiger, Prince Gagarine
