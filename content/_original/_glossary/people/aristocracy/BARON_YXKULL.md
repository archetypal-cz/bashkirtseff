# Baron Yxkull (the Russian envoy in Rome, 1876)

## Overview
The Russian envoy («l'ambassadeur», «le ministre») in Rome in 1876, husband of the [Baronne Yxkull](BARONNE_YXKULL.md). Letters for Maman come addressed «A son Excellence l'ambassadeur de Sa Majesté l'Empereur de toutes les Russies» (053.0292); Marie fears «l'accueil si froid de l'ambassadeur russe» (054.0006). On 2 April 1876 she tells how «Yxkull était un vieux célibataire» whose secretary Glinka surprised him with his wife and made him marry her (056.0323).

## Identification
Very probably Baron Karl Petrovich Ikskul von Hildenbandt (1817–1894), diplomat, whose wife was Varvara Ivanovna (née Lutkovskaya, formerly the wife of the diplomat Nikolai Dmitrievich Glinka-Mavrin): the age gap and the Glinka marriage fit Marie's story. About 0.8; his posting at Rome in 1876 was not found in a list of envoys.
