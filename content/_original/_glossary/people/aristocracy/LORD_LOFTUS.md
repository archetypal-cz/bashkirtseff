## Basic Information

%% GLO_LORD_LOFTUS.0002 %%
Lord Loftus was a British nobleman encountered by Marie in Nice during the social seasons. The Loftus family held the title Marquess of Ely (Irish peerage); the young man Marie calls «lord Loftus» is most likely a son of Lord Augustus Loftus (1817–1904), the diplomat (who was not a peer: «Lord Augustus» is the courtesy style of a marquess's younger son); see «Identity uncertain» below.

%% GLO_LORD_LOFTUS.0003 %%
## Connection to Marie's Diary

%% GLO_LORD_LOFTUS.0004 %%
In her Audiffret entries (carnet 047, October 1875) «Bibi» first seems to mean a Loftus (047/1875-10-16 footnote: «here apparently Lord Loftus»), which she then applied universally to various people in her circle, including Audiffret himself ("Bibi-Girofla"). This suggests Lord Loftus was a familiar figure in Marie's Nice social world before Audiffret's prominence.

## Identity uncertain
We are not sure who this is. The tag covers the handsome young «lord Loftus» of Nice: with lord and lady Folkner at the theatre and on the Promenade in April 1875 (031/1875-04-14, 04-16: «Loftus est joli mais paraît bête»), then Marie's «bel inconnu» of December 1875, «un demi-dieu» who reminds her of Hamilton and is always with Mlle Leech (050/1875-12-12 to 12-19; 059/1876-05-04: «Loftus est amoureux de Leech… depuis l'été dernier quand je les ai vus tous deux à Wiesbaden»). On 12 December she decides that «l'autre n'était pas lord Loftus, l'autre avait l'air d'un sac» — probably the father, Lord Augustus Loftus (1817–1904), the ex-ambassador at Berlin who was the family's neighbour at no. 55 in October 1875 (see LORD_AUGUSTUS_LOFTUS). The Mon Journal t.6 index files every «Loftus» page under «Loftus (lord Augustus), ex-ambassadeur à Berlin», which cannot be right for a young man. Lord Augustus's sons were Henry John (b. 1849), Augustus Pelham Brooke (b. 1851) and Montagu Egerton (b. 1860) (thepeerage.com p6301; Wikipedia, «Lord Augustus Loftus»); the elder two fit the age, while Marie's memory of him as a child «de sept [?] ans» at Baden (the editor's [?]) fits only Montagu. Sources checked: Mon Journal t.5 and t.6 indexes; Kernberger (2013) glossary («Loftus, Lord: English lord who rents a villa on the Promenade in Nice»); thepeerage.com; Wikipedia. If you can identify him, we would be glad to hear from you.
