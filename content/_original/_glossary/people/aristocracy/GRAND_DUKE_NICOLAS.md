# Grand Duke Nicolas

**Research Status**: Moderate
**Diary Coverage**: 1874-03-15 to 1879-12-08

## Identity
In Marie's 1874-1876 pages «le grand-duc Nicolas» is Grand Duke Nicholas Konstantinovich (1850-1918), «fils de Constantin», nephew of the Emperor, the scandalous grand duke of the Fanny Lear affair and the diamond theft of 1874 (Wikipedia, "Grand Duke Nicholas Konstantinovich of Russia"; the diary footnote at 064.0562 gives the same identification). The entry's earlier line that he was "marrying Mlle Skobeleff in 1874" is not established: it is only Marie's report of a rumour.

## Diary References

- **1874-03-15** (017): in a paragraph of marriage gossip («Tout le monde se marie cette année…», with the closing words of the Lewin item «Ce n'est qu'un *on dit* d'ailleurs»), Marie adds: «J'oublie un grand mariage, le grand-duc Nicolas, fils de Constantin, épouse Mlle Skobeleff sœur de la princesse Belocelsky.» This is a report of what is being said in Nice, not a fact; the English Wikipedia article on him lists other spouses (Nadezhda Dreyer, 1882) and no Skobeleff, so the rumour does not seem to have come true. See [#Skobeleff](../mentioned/SKOBELEFF.md).
- **1875-12-01, 12-05** (050.0342, 050.0516) and **1876-02-28, 03-31** (054.0575, 056.0287): the Fanny Lear scandal; «ce grand-duc Nicolas qui a fait tant de scandale», «le malheureux grand-duc Nicolas Constantinovitch», his letters published in England by Fanny Lear.
- **1876-08-09 to 08-15** (064): she is at General Rostovzeff's, «chef de la cour du grand-duc Nicolas» (064.0476); «Le grand-duc Nicolas, neveu de l'Empereur et amant… de Fanny Lear» (064.0562); she jokes about loving him: «d'un duc tu es montée à un grand-duc» (064.0611); on 08-28 (065.0572) she falls asleep «en rêvant à Cassagnac ou au grand duc Nicolas».
- **1876-10-26, 11-08, 11-09** (067.0329, 067.0921, 067.0926): «Je crois que je deviens amoureuse du grand-duc Nicolas.» The footnote and RSR note there say Nikolai Nikolaevich (1831-1891), which does not obviously fit a man «dont le profil a de l'Antonelli» whom Lola «a dansé avec lui à l'Institut»; unresolved.
- **1879-12-08** (086.0632): at the Russian church, «le Grand duc Nicolas l'a saluée»: another grand duke, not necessarily the same man.
