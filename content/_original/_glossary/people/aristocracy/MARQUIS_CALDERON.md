# Marquis Calderon

## Overview

A young Spaniard of the Nice «bataclan», «le petit Espagnol noir», whom Marie jokes about as «le marquis Calderon». The Mon Journal t.5 index has «Calderon (Claro) [Colorado Claro, marquis], Espagnol, 17, 54». The nickname is a cigar grade: the joke is that his name is «un peu autrement; Colorado Claro» (031.0171). Probably the same man as [Calderon](../mentioned/CALDERON.md) (032.0331, 7 May 1875; 039.0139, 19 August 1875: «un petit qui était souvent avec Calderon, le petit Espagnol noir»).

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Duplicate of CALDERON (same Spaniard, the Mon Journal t.5 index puts both under one line, pp.17 and 54) - listed for merge.

## References in Diary

On 9 April 1875, as Olga sighs over him, Marie quips that «le petit Espagnol se nomme le marquis Calderon» but that his real name would rather be Colorado Claro (031.0171).
