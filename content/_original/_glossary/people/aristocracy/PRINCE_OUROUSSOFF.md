# Prince Ouroussoff

**Last Updated**: 2025-11-20

## Overview


## Soden, 1878 (carnets 081–082)

Mon Journal t.12 index: «Ourousoff (Serge prince) : 63, 65, 67, 70-73, 75-76, 78, 85, 90». A Russian guest at Soden in August 1878: ma tante speaks to him about the decoration for Cassagnac (081.0593), and Marie hopes he can introduce her at the Russian embassy in Paris (081.0621). The print once spells him «Ourassoff» (082.0127). Not identified further.
