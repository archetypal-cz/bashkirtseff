# «La petite blonde Nathalie Galitzine» (died 1875)

## Overview
A young Princess Nathalie Galitzine of the Nice winter society of about 1873, who died in Russia in 1875. On 16 December 1875 Marie writes of the comte de Simiane: «Il était fort amoureux de la petite blonde Nathalie Galitzine, il l'a suivie en Russie, où elle est morte dans ses bras, il y a de cela quelques mois» (050.0824). In Rome on 11 January 1876 she recalls how the young men of Nice, led by Tournon and Audiffret, had formed «une espèce de cour comme il y a trois ans à la princesse Galitzine et à Mme Chilowsky, toutes deux mortes il y a un mois» (052.0089).

She is not [Bête](../mentioned/BETE.md), Princess Nathalie Galitzine of 1873–74, who is alive in December 1875 and sends the family «des bonbons et des choses russes» (050.0966, 19 December 1875; Mon Journal t.6 p.242). The t.6 index has a single entry, «Galitzine (Mme Nathalie, princesse), 234, 242, 316», which runs the two women together.

## Identity uncertain
We are not sure who this is: the Galitzin (Golitsyn) family was very large, and Marie gives only the first name, the colouring and the death in Russia in 1875. Marie's figures («il y a quelques mois», «il y a un mois») do not agree exactly. Sources checked: 050.0824, 052.0089, the t.6 index. If you can identify her, we would be glad to hear from you.
