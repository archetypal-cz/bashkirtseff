# Marquis de Constantin

## Overview

One of the «petits noirs de Nice», the dark young men who haunt Nice's theatre and promenade. The Mon Journal t.4 index has «Constantin (marquis de), 207, 226-227, 250-255, 266, 269…». Marie does not give a forename.

## Relevance to Marie

Stub replaced. Identity beyond the diary and t.4 index not found (not in Gotha-type sources checked); no forename.

On 14 November 1874 at the Nice theatre, as Audiffret, Galula and Saëtone appear in the second act, «le marquis de Constantin, un des petits noirs de Nice, est là depuis le commencement» (025.0226).
