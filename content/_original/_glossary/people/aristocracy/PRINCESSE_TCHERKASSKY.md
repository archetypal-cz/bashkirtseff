## Schlangenbad, August 1875 (RSR, 2026-10-21)
Wife of [Prince Tcherkassky](PRINCE_TCHERKASSKY.md), at Schlangenbad with Marie's mother in August 1875; she is the first person presented to Marie on arrival (039.0006, 13 August 1875). On 18 August she «m'admire réellement, elle ne fait que dire que je lui ressemble ou que je ressemble à son fils» (039.0106); the son is not named. The Mon Journal t.5 index spells the name «Tcherkassy (princesse), 243-244, 251, 254, 257-258, 261». All tags of this file are in carnet 039.
