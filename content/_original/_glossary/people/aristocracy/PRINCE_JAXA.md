# Prince Jaxa

## Overview
«Jaxa» is Marie's short name for the Paris-based Polish pretender to princely rank who calls himself «le prince Jacqua de Bikowsky» on his cards. Both the Mon Journal t.3 index («Jaxa, 204, 206»; «Bikowsky (prince Jacqua de), 190, 209») and t.4 («Jaxa (prince), 158») list him. The glossary already identifies the persons as one: see [JACQUA_DE_BIKOWSKY](../mentioned/JACQUA_DE_BIKOWSKY.md) and [JAXA_BIKOWSKY](../mentioned/JAXA_BIKOWSKY.md). «Jaxa» is the alternative name of the Polish Gryf coat of arms (en.wikipedia «Gryf coat of arms»).

## Relevance to Marie
- 26 September 1874: «Le prince Jaxa dîne chez nous et me fatigue par des discours bêtes et vides» (024.0120). This entry carries the only paragraph tagged here.
- Paris, May 1874 (tagged JAXA, JACQUA_DE_BIKOWSKY): he is presented on the 14th (019.0216), dines with them on the 26th, when Marie remarks «après tout, il ne fait de mal à personne» (019.0365), and on the 29th he calls and behaves «si bêtement, sottement ... insupportable et inconvenant» (019.0391).
