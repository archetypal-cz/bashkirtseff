## Lord Falkner

%% GLO_LORD_FALKNER.0002 %%
An English peer of the Nice season, husband of Lady Falkner/Folkner (LADY_FOLKNER). Marie sees him near the London House in January 1874 (015.0020), and on 26 May 1874, in the Bois, blushes at the sight of him: «Ah ! si, lord Falkner. J'ai rougi en le voyant» (019.0369). On 13 June 1874 she mentions the men's costumes he wore at Baden-Baden (020.0239), as a model for the dress she fancies. Mon Journal t.3 index: «Falkner (lord), 7, 21, 204, 232». Not identified further; no source found.
