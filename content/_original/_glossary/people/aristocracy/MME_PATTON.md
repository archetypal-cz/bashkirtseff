# Mme Patton

%% GLO_MME_PATTON.0001 %%
## Identity

%% GLO_MME_PATTON.0002 %%
Wife of Oscar Patton, the Russian consul at Nice ([M. Patton](../mentioned/M_PATTON.md)), born Savelieff: the Mon Journal indexes list her as «Patton (Mme), née Savelieff» (t.1, t.2 pp. 35, 55, 194; t.6 pp. 311, 318) beside «Patton (Oscar), consul de Russie» (t.2, t.6). She is the daughter of the Mme Savelieff who died at Nice in May 1873 and the mother of Nathalie, Catherine and «Bébé» (t.1 index). Her first name is not given. See also [the Patton family](../mentioned/PATTON_FAMILY.md) and [Nathalie Patton](../mentioned/NATHALIE_PATTON.md).

## In the diary

%% GLO_MME_PATTON.0004 %%
- **April–May 1873** (004): she comes to the Bashkirtseffs' at-home day (004/1873-04-21); she and her mother dream the same remedy, and the grand duchess sends word that her mother should be massaged (004/1873-04-28). At the funeral service «Mme Patton (sa fille) a eu le *hysterics,* après la *pannychide*» (004/1873-05-14), and faints at the burial (004/1873-05-15). On 16 May she calls with M. Savelieff and Nathalie (004/1873-05-16).
- **11 September 1873** (009): when her husband is threatened with dismissal she telegraphs to the grand duchess and to the comtesse Keller.
- **28 February 1874** (017): named with Mme Howard and Mme Teplakoff as hostesses before whom no teacher would dare raise his voice.
- **23 April 1875** (032): at Mme Angel's she crosses the room to curtsey to Marie.
- **12 February 1882** (094): at the Russian church she speaks to Marie's parents «avec une extrême amabilité».
