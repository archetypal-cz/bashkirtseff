# Prince Pierre Bonaparte

## Overview

Prince Pierre-Napoléon Bonaparte (1815–1881), son of Lucien Bonaparte and nephew of Napoleon I, notorious for shooting the journalist Victor Noir in January 1870 (footnotes at 090.0234 and 097.0349; en.wikipedia «Pierre-Napoléon Bonaparte»). He married late Éléonore-Justine Ruflin, «la princesse Pierre» ([PRINCESSE_PIERRE_BONAPARTE](PRINCESSE_PIERRE_BONAPARTE.md)); their children were Roland Bonaparte and Jeanne, marquise de Villeneuve ([PRINCESSE_JEANNE_BONAPARTE](PRINCESSE_JEANNE_BONAPARTE.md)).
