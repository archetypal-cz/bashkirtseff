# Prince Tchetvertinsky

## Overview
A Russian prince of the Tchetvertinsky (Chetvertynsky, Sviatopolk-Chetvertinsky) family. The Mon Journal t.3 index has «Tchetvertinski (prince), 84, 91, 93, 108–109, 111, 256». The glossary also has [TCHETVERTINSKI](../mentioned/TCHETVERTINSKI.md) for the prince seen at Nice in 1874, who is probably the same man.

## Relevance to Marie
On 15 August 1875, at Schlangenbad, someone says that «la fille de la princesse Souvoroff épouse le prince Tchetvertinsky qui a cent mille roubles de rente. Magnifique parti !» (039.0066). The bride is a daughter of Princess Souvoroff, the Russian lady of Nice (see [SOUVOROFF](../mentioned/SOUVOROFF.md); the t.3 index has «Souvoroff (Mlle)»). Other Tchetvertinski passages of Marie's circle: the Nice charity sale of February 1874 (see TCHETVERTINSKI), a list of Nice men on 1 July 1874 (020.0487, not tagged here), and Nice in January–February 1877 (carnet 068: 18 and 21 January, 8 February, not tagged here).

## Identity uncertain
The diary gives no first name, and we found no source for a marriage of a Tchetvertinsky and a Souvoroff daughter in 1875 (a search of the families' genealogies produced nothing). Whether the prince of 1875 is the Nice prince of 1874 is likely but not proven. If you can identify him, we would be glad to hear from you.
