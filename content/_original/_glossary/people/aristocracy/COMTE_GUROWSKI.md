# Comte Ignacy Gurowski

## Overview
Marie calls him «le comte Gurowski (beau-frère de la Reine Isabelle)» (086.0624). That fits Count Ignacy Gurowski (1812–1887), a Polish nobleman who married the Infanta Isabel Fernanda, sister of Francisco de Asís, husband of Isabella II (Wikipedia, «Ignacy Gurowski») (0.8). The Mon Journal t.13 index lists «Gurowski (Comte Ignace) : 131».

## Relevance to Marie
- 4 December 1879: a guest at Mme de Mouzay's dinner with Marie (086.0624).
- 6 December 1879: in the gallery of the Chamber with Mme Gavini and M. de Morgan while Marie is there with her aunt (086.0626).
