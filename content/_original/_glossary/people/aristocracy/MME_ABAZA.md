## Poltava, 1876
Marie's text: «J'ai été chez Mlle Dietrich qui, devenue Mme Abaza, ne m'a pas rendue ma visite» (067.0619, 067/1876-11-02); at the concert of the Poltava Assembly of the Nobility she sits beside Mme Abaza without greeting her (067.0618, 067.0623), and M. Abaza claims to have returned the call «avec sa nièce» (067.0626). The Mon Journal t.9 index has «Abaza (Mme) (née Dietrich)» and, separately, «Abaza (M.)». On 15 September Marie had called «chez la nièce de M. Abaza, l'exmillionnaire» (066.0244, 066/1876-09-15). It is not established that this Poltava Mme Abaza is the singing Mme Abaza Marie meets at Soden and on the Riviera in 1878-79 (081-084), whom this file's tags mostly mean.
