# The Antonelli family (Rome)

## Overview
«Les Antonelli»: the Roman family of Cardinal Giacomo Antonelli, Secretary of State to Pius IX, as a whole: his brother count Louis (Luigi) Antonelli, the countess Camilla Folchi and their sons Dominique, Paul and Pietro. Marie describes the family herself on 29 April 1876: «Il y a d'abord et avant tout le cardinal-diacre Jacques Antonelli… le comte Louis Antonelli marié à la comtesse Camille Folchi dont il a eu trois fils: Dominique, Paul et Pierre» (059.0601). Use this file where Marie means the family collectively, chiefly as the obstacle to her marriage with Pietro in 1876 («l'opposition ne vient pas de notre côté mais bien du côté des Antonelli», 056.0157); use the individual files where a person is meant.
