# Mme Dimitri Kanschine

%% GLO_MME_DIMITRI_KANSCHINE.0001 %%
## Identity

%% GLO_MME_DIMITRI_KANSCHINE.0002 %%
A rich Russian society lady in Paris in 1877–78, wife of a Dimitri Kanschine, whom Marie's mother knew from Geneva through the Sapogenikoffs (077/1878-01-01). Marie calls her «une femme du monde, riche et comme il faut» (076/1877-12-08) and «très élégante et grande dame» (077/1878-01-01). Her own first name is not given.

%% GLO_MME_DIMITRI_KANSCHINE.0003 %%
## In the diary

%% GLO_MME_DIMITRI_KANSCHINE.0004 %%
- **22 October 1877** (075.0379): named among the «dames du grand monde, très lancées» expected at the gaming house the baronne is to open for Mme Doubelt.
- **2 December 1877** (076/1877-12-02): the family pays calls «à Mesdames Kanschine, Doubelt, de Fayet, Boyd».
- **8 December 1877** (076/1877-12-08): «Madame Kanschine et venue» (sic).
- **1 January 1878** (077/1878-01-01): before dining at the Boyds', Marie, Dina and her mother call on «Mme Dimitri Kanschine».
