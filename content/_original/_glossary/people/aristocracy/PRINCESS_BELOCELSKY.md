# Princess Belocelsky

**Research Status**: Basic
**Last Updated**: 2025-07-06
**Diary Coverage**: Up to 1874-03-15

## Identity
Russian princess, sister of Mlle Skobeleff who is marrying Grand Duke Nicolas in 1874.

## Diary References

### 1874
- **March 15**: Mentioned as sister of bride: "Mlle Skobeleff sœur de la princesse Belocelsky."

- Sister marrying into imperial family
- Shows Skobeleff family's high connections
