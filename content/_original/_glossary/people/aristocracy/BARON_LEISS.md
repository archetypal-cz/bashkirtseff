# Baron Leiss (Ostend)

## Overview

Baron Leiss, an acquaintance of Marie and her mother at Ostend in August 1874, who speaks «comme les Belges» (028.0096), so a Belgian or Belgian-resident baron; first name not given. Mon Journal t.4 index: «Leiss (baron), 110, 113, 116, 121, 265». He is not the count «Noël de Leiser», the Pole naturalised English who appears in the same weeks (023.0128).

## Relevance to Marie

At Ostend: he leaves the Kursaal while Marie stays with Merjeewsky (23 August, 023.0265); on 24 August «Le baron Leiss me fait danser, je le présente à maman» at the Cercle des Bains (023.0311); on 26 August he is a guest at the dinner of thirteen with the Fosters, «Leiss trouve Merjeewsky et l'amène, mais c'est avec lui que nous sommes treize» (023.0348), and gives Marie a bouquet with «une centaine de compliments, comme à Mlle Foster» (023.0351, 023.0353); on 31 August they meet him on the digue with Florence (023.0416). Four months later, on 31 December 1874, she compares a young man of Nice, who has «une allure saccadée» as a rider, to him: «parle comme les Belges, comme le baron Leiss d'Ostende» (028.0096).
