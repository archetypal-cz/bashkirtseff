Ce sont des femmes qui ont trois, quatre ans d'atelier, de Louvre, d'études sérieuses. Et moi... Larderel[^10] et voilà tout.

[^10]: Count Alessandro (Alexandre) de Larderel, the Florentine with whom Marie was infatuated in Naples in 1876–77, not a teacher: the print index lists this page under «Larderei (Alexandre) [Bijou]» (Mon Journal t.11 p.17; index, tome11.docx ¶4824). Against the other students' years of serious study, Marie sets her infatuation.
