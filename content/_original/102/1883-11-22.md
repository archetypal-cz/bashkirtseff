# Jeudi 22 novembre 1883
"L'Illustration universelle[^1]'' (de Russie) publie sur la première page le dessin de mon tableau.
%% 2026-10-02T15:25:07 RSR: CORRECTION: source footnote 4 corrected (no «Melbourne Museum of Art»; the painting is in the National Gallery of Victoria, dated 1878, Salon 1879; source ngv.vic.gov.au/explore/collection/work/3768 and en.wikipedia.org/wiki/October_(painting); not verified in print). %%

[^1]: *L'Illustration universelle* (*Vsemirnaya Illyustratsiya*): major Russian illustrated weekly, published in St. Petersburg from 1869.

[^2]: Marie's aside to her imagined future reader is characteristic of her self-conscious relationship with her diary as a literary document.

[^3]: *Les Foins* (*The Haymakers*, 1877): one of Bastien-Lepage's celebrated paintings of French peasant life.

[^4]: *Saisons d'octobre* (*October*, 1879): also called *Les pommes de terre* (*The Potato Harvest*); Bastien-Lepage's most celebrated work, painted in 1878 and shown at the Salon of 1879; now in the National Gallery of Victoria, Melbourne.
