%% 2026-02-09T18:50:00 RSR: Brief note of afternoon portrait work. Paul is Marie's brother/cousin (Paul Babanine). Home studio practice complementing atelier work. %%
L'après-midi je fais le portrait de Paul.[^084.0168.1]

[^084.0168.1]: The only occasion on which the diary records Marie painting a Paul. A *Portrait de son frère Paul*, dated 1876, is held by the Musée des Beaux-Arts Jules Chéret in Nice, but nothing in the carnets corroborates that date, and it is not settled whether the sitter of this 1879 sitting is her brother or her cousin Paul Babanine.
