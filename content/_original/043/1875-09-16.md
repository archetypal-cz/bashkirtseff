Nous quittons la superbe Florence, cette lauza leggierci a pusta molto, che di [illisible] maculato era coperta[^043.0210.1] comme dit le Dante au long nez pendant.

[^043.0210.1]: A garbled recollection of Dante, *Inferno* I.32–33: *una lonza leggiera e presta molto, / che di pel macolato era coverta* («a leopard, light and very swift, covered with a spotted coat»), the first of the three beasts that bar Dante's way. The illegible word stands where Dante has *pel* (coat).
