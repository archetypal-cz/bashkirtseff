Toute cette semaine j'ai manqué plusieurs leçons, toujours à cause de ce Ravel[^017.0196.1] qu'on raccommode.

[^017.0196.1]: *Ravel*: apparently the household's name for Marie's alarm clock (*réveil*): in January 1874 the printed *Mon Journal* has "mon *Ravel* n'a pas sonné" where this transcription reads *réveil*. A clock under repair would explain the missed lessons.
