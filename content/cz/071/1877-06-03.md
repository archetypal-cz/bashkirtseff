The neighborhood comes to pay respects -- the grocer, the washerwoman, the cobbler's daughter, a "magnifique monsieur" representing the whole quarter. Marie's democratic warmth with tradespeople ("on m'appelle Mademoiselle Marie et on m'aime") contrasts with aristocratic pretensions elsewhere. The Rossignol (nightingale) is a Nicois street festival tradition with lanterns. Catherine Segurana is Nice's legendary heroine who defended the city against the Ottoman-French siege of 1543. Triphon organizes the party but steals the funds. The "boite blanche" containing old journals triggers self-disgust about a past weakness (likely Antonelli). Rosalie's servants' quarter dramas (Joseph Arozzo, Emile, Audiffret) prompt Marie's aspiration to write their stories -- "si j'avais le temps je les raconterais en histoires" -- a literary impulse she never fulfilled. %%

Triphon je velkým mistrem obřadu. K vypsání tohoto tvora by bylo třeba celý svazek. Nelze být větší podvodník, drzejší, větší šašek, větší Tartuffe...[^071.256.3]
