%% 2026-02-09T22:09:00 RSR: Holy Thursday (Maundy Thursday), the Passion service. Easter 1879 fell on April 13. Princess Ypsilanti (nee Sturdza) — member of the prominent Phanariot Greek-Romanian aristocratic Ypsilanti family, well connected in Russian diplomatic circles. Marie deliberately dresses attractively for church, hoping to be noticed by the Princess who receives Grand Dukes and ambassadors. The "bonnet de Marguerite" refers to the simple cap worn by Marguerite in Gounod's Faust (1859), the iconic operatic character. Marie's strategy is transparent: church attendance to combat the perception that she's a freethinking painter ("peintre et libre penseuse et nihiliste"). %%
