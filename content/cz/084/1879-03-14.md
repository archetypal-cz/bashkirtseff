%% 2026-02-09T19:53:00 RSR: Paul departs despite Marie physically blocking the door. His "fermete de faible" -- stubbornness about departure while being passive in serious matters -- infuriates her. She drafts a slanderous telegram to their father in Poltava but at that exact moment, Rosalie announces that Champion (a seamstress who makes Marie's dresses) has typhoid fever, her workers have fled, she's alone. Marie tears up the telegram and sends the 20 francs to Champion instead. This moral pivot -- from vindictive rage to spontaneous charity -- is one of the entry's most revealing moments. Extended philosophical reflection on charity: she won't do good because her family would mock her ("la manie des pauvres"), turn it into petty squabbling about expenses, and offend her sensibility. Final devastating self-critique: "Et par paresse!" %%
