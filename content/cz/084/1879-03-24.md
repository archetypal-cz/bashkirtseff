%% 2026-02-02T09:06:00 LAN: "fait dire" - sent word; formal message via servant or intermediary %%
%% 2026-02-09T20:02:00 RSR: A day of missed opportunities. Gavini sends word that the parliamentary session won't be interesting, so they skip Versailles. Marie goes to the atelier instead, then visits the Basilewsky family (Russian acquaintances from Nice).
