%% 083.0124 %%

%% 2026-02-09T12:54:00 RSR: Exchange between a colleague and Cassagnac praising his "talent and energy" - his gracious response calling it "the most flattering tribute I've ever received." %% 083.0126 %%
%% [#Clemenceau](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/CLEMENCEAU.md) %%
%% 2026-02-09T12:54:00 RSR: Georges Clemenceau (1841-1929) was at this time a relatively new deputy (elected 1876), leader of the Radical Republicans.
