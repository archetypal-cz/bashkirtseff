The Roi (King Victor Emmanuel II, d. Jan 9) and Saint Père (Pope Pius IX, d. Feb 7, 1878) — both deaths killed the carnival season. Melissano and Alexandre are in Florence; Alexandre has "become very wise." At Monte Carlo, M. des Perrières of Le Figaro told Marcuard that Marie is marrying P. de Cassagnac — gossip that infuriates Marie. She writes a denial letter in Blanc's presence, tries to include violettes, but her mother stops her (don't send flowers to men — Alexandre will gossip). Marie throws her letter in the fire and sulks on the sofa. "Amourette" = flirtation (mother's Russian word). %%
