%% 055.0700 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/people/writers/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% 2026-02-09T14:25:00 RSR: Longest and richest entry in the carnet. Marie drives Torlonia's horses in his "panier" (two-wheeled carriage) outside Porta del Popolo — their first extended private conversation. Torlonia admires her waist repeatedly, makes a risqué double-entendre about "cornes" (cuckoldry). At Besnard's studio, the painter confirms Torlonia is "le lion d'ici" and a drinker.
