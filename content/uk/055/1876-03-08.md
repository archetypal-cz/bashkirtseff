%% 2026-02-09T14:15:00 RSR: The most dramatic entry of the carnet — Antonelli's sustained declaration of love during a horseback ride to the Farnesina hills. They climb a trackless mountain, and at the summit among shepherds (Marie evokes Faustulus and Acca Laurentia, parents of Romulus), Antonelli declares his love repeatedly.
