%% 2026-02-09T19:30:00 RSR: Marie's name-day celebration (Sainte Marie, typically June 2-3 in the Russian/Orthodox calendar). The neighborhood comes to pay respects -- the grocer, the washerwoman, the cobbler's daughter, a "magnifique monsieur" representing the whole quarter. Marie's democratic warmth with tradespeople ("on m'appelle Mademoiselle Marie et on m'aime") contrasts with aristocratic pretensions elsewhere. The Rossignol (nightingale) is a Nicois street festival tradition with lanterns. Catherine Segurana is Nice's legendary heroine who defended the city against the Ottoman-French siege of 1543. Triphon organizes the party but steals the funds. The "boite blanche" containing old journals triggers self-disgust about a past weakness (likely Antonelli). Rosalie's servants' quarter dramas (Joseph Arozzo, Emile, Audiffret) prompt Marie's aspiration to write their stories -- "si j'avais le temps je les raconterais en histoires" -- a literary impulse she never fulfilled. %%

# Неділя, 3 червня 1877
Мене будять пані Рондель і Сір, бакалійниця й рантьє з будинку навпроти. Виряджені так, що очі вибиває, вони приносять мені два букети до свята, яке я влаштовую сьогодні ввечері.
