# Friday, 14 July 1882
Pollack comes to lunch, then we take a drive through the flag-bedecked city.[^1] As for the evening — it is sad: we walk with the Gavinis and Géry, whom I detest.

[^1]: The 14th of July, Bastille Day, celebrated since 1880 as France's national holiday.

%% 095.0271 %%
%% On aurait pourtant pu s'amuser à courir les rues illuminées avec des êtres amusants et jeunes. %%
One might, however, have enjoyed running through the illuminated streets with amusing and youthful companions.
