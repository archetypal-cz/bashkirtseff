# Monday, 1 May 1882
We go to see the Queen[^1] with Maman, and then to the Salon with Villevieille. I am charming in grey, with the look of a rebel.[^2]

[^1]: Queen Isabel II of Spain, in exile at the Palais de Castille on the Avenue Kléber, kept a Bonapartist salon.
[^2]: *Faux air de frondeuse*: the slight air of a *frondeuse* — a female rebel, evoking participants in the 17th-century Fronde civil war. Marie's self-image: elegantly defiant.
