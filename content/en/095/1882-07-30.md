# Sunday, 30 July 1882
Little Juvisy — that is to say Mme de Sonis — has just had a baby; we go to see her; she is very pretty lying there in white. The household lives with the family. They have taken the husband in as a boarder... who has done his duty by giving them a child... Ugh!
