# Wednesday, 7 June 1882
Opera with the Gavinis. I am again charming in a white Louis XV gown with a great garland of Bengal roses, without foliage. A rose comb in my hair, dressed à la Psyché as before. We are a large party: the Comte and Comtesse de Béarn, Saint-Amand, Calvet-Rognat, Kiki, a host of others.
