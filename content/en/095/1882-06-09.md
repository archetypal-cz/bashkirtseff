%% Il m'a semblé que j'étais froide avec Gabriel hier, surtout après avoir logé chez son père... Alors je le fais venir ce soir et nous allons au musée Grévin; ce n'était qu'un prétexte à flirtage. %%
# Friday, 9 June 1882
It seemed to me I was cold with Gabriel yesterday, especially after having lodged at his father's... So I summon him this evening and we go to the Musée Grévin[^1] — it was merely a pretext for flirting.

[^1]: The Musée Grévin, Paris's celebrated wax museum, had opened in 1882.

%% 095.0150 %%
%% [Dans la marge: Je le fais venir par un billet écrit sur un chiffon de papier ficelé avec du fil et un rond d'élastique.] %%
[In the margin: I summon him by a note written on a scrap of paper tied with thread and a rubber ring.]

%% 095.0151 %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: "Tortoni" - Tortoni's; fashionable cafe on Boulevard des Italiens %%
%% Et après chez Tortoni. %%
And afterwards to Tortoni's.[^2]

[^2]: Tortoni's, the celebrated café on the Boulevard des Italiens, famous for its ices.

%% 095.0152 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/people/writers/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: "souterrain du crime" - crime basement; murder exhibit at wax museum %%
%% Les Engelhardt et la Cerny sont venus avec nous jusqu'à Grévin. Du reste nous sommes sortis de la maison à dix heures. Et puis après ? Rien... On s'étouffait un peu chez Grévin dans le souterrain du crime et j'avais le bras de ce Gabriel derrière moi, preque autour de moi... Il a dû y penser... Les hommes pensent à ces choses-là, en dansant même. %%
The Engelhardts and Mlle Cerny came with us as far as Grévin's. Besides, we had left the house at ten. And then? Nothing... One was a little stifled at Grévin's in the crime cellar,[^3] and I had Gabriel's arm behind me, almost around me... He must have thought about it... Men think of such things — even while dancing.

[^3]: The Musée Grévin's *souterrain du crime*, a dramatic lower-level exhibit recreating famous crimes.

It seems that when I went to the ball without a corset they all noticed.

Well, then — I was saying that, but that is all.

It is dreadful. I tease him a great deal this evening and without much wit... Only I keep thinking... to what purpose? It hardly amuses me.
