# Friday, 21 July 1882
I want to arrange things so that there is always music playing, for in the presence of any background noise I hear conversations like a person with normal hearing. Dusautoy, Alexis's friend, plays the piano admirably — he shall be the official house musician, and in the studio I am going to have an organ.
%% 2026-03-11T10:00:00 TR: Marie is partially deaf; she hears better when there is background noise to focus through. "Personne naturelle" = a person with unimpaired hearing. %%
