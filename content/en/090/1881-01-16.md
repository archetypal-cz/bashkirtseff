Mme Mouzay dines here and we talk about my illness; I wanted to have it out once and for all so as to put an end to it. Ah! yes, indeed. She told me again to my face that I am lost, but that there is still hope. She was in a state of great exaltation.

%% 090.0603 %%
%% Il faut bien que cette femme ait de l’affection pour moi pour être importune et embêtante à ce point. C’est égal elle a une manière spéciale. Il y a deux, trois ans, lorsque maman était très malade, elle venait pleurer sur elle et lui crier: Ah ! ma chère amie votre nez est déjà tout blanc ! - Comme c’est agréable de se voir enterrer de la sorte. Enfin je lui ai répété cent fois que c’est pas charitable de me dire des horreurs pareilles ! %%
%% 2026-09-28T21:01:18 RSR: Restored from tome14.docx ¶843, Mon Journal t.14 p.66 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
This woman must indeed be fond of me to be as importunate and tiresome as that. All the same, she has a peculiar manner. Two or three years ago, when Maman was very ill, she used to come and weep over her and cry out to her: "Ah! my dear friend, your nose is already quite white!" How pleasant it is to see oneself buried like that. In short, I have told her a hundred times that it is not charitable to say such horrible things to me!
