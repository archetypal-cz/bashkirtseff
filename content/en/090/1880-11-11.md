# Thursday, 11 November 1880
Tony came, and as I expressed my discouragement to him, he told me that this proves I am not blind, and he urges me to resume my study, to continue studying.

How I need encouragement! I have made myself a brown cloak with a monk's hood for the atelier, for when I happen to take a seat near the window, from which comes a hellish draught. So I have a monk's hood — which has always brought me bad luck. I wept beneath this unlucky hood, and so much [words blacked out: that this Amélie] Zillhardt, who had come to see if it was not a joke, was quite amazed. I want to make a painting depicting an expulsion of monks.[^1] So I went to the Capuchins of the rue de la Santé, and the three fathers who remain told me about and showed me the scene of the disaster; I had come to offer hospitality to two of the fathers in Nice. I hope they will not take advantage of the offer.

[^1]: A reference to the March 1880 decrees under which religious orders were expelled from France.
