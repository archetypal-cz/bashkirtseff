# Wednesday, 17 November 1880
Today Roland Bonaparte, son of Prince Pierre, the assassin of Victor Noir, marries Mademoiselle Marie Blanc, daughter of the man who ran the roulette at Monaco.[^1]

[^1]: Prince Pierre Bonaparte shot journalist Victor Noir in 1870, causing a scandal; Francois Blanc founded the Monte Carlo Casino.

We went to the Gavinis', who wheedled an invitation out of us to see *Michel Strogoff* at the Châtelet. Old Denis has asked me to paint a picture from the photograph of an old portrait of a Monseigneur, a bishop, his uncle — well and good. If it were a matter of getting me some celebrity to paint for the Salon, that would be different.

How sickening!

Paul de Cassagnac writes in defence of the murder of Victor Noir, hails Mademoiselle Blanc as a "valiant woman," and attends the wedding in the front row. Coming from him, who married the daughter of Countess Acard, it is natural enough, but how sickening!

Maman's letter, in which she does not complain and says only a few words, makes me cry because she seems so desolate. The handwriting is the way it is when she is unhappy and crying; I can barely see to write through my tears. [Blacked-out word: Her] handwriting is beginning to resemble Grand-maman's. It is horrible to know she has gone off like that, all of a sudden, and is kept there by force.
