# Saturday, 11 December 1880
Tony told me that my head for the competition is the best thing I have done so far. Ah! What does it matter — this is no life!

The Queen of Spain is to give an evening party, and I wonder if we would be invited if Maman were here. Well! The de Lesseps, Hecht, Dieppe and heaven knows what else make a horrible chaos in my head, and all I can do is burst into tears. After three years, to have got no further than this.

I go to ask my aunt for five hundred francs to pay the harpist, and she answers with the everlasting phrase: I swear to you we haven't a sou, but tomorrow or the day after, perhaps. That brought my exasperation to a head!

%% Enfin ce n'est pas une existence cela ! Ceux qui vous mettent au monde devraient s'occuper de vous un peu plus que cela ! Vous donner un état, une carrière, une position je ne sais pas ce que je dis, mais je me comprends. Oh ! les terribles gens, ó l'atroce éducation. %%
In short, this is no existence! Those who bring you into the world ought to take a little more care of you than that! Give you a station, a career, a position — I do not know what I am saying, but I understand myself. Oh, the terrible people! Oh, the atrocious upbringing!

%% 090.0334 %%
%% [#Family](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/FAMILY.md) %%
%% Dans tout ce que j'ai fait de fou ou de bête c'est eux qui m'ont poussée. Je ne dis pas depuis deux, trois ans. Depuis trois ans je suis seule responsable ne les écoutant plus et les sottises qu'ils disent ne m'influencent plus. Quelle atroce maison, quelles mœurs ! %%
In all the foolish or stupid things I have done, it is they who pushed me. I do not mean in the last two or three years. For three years I have been solely responsible, no longer listening to them, and the foolish things they say no longer influence me. What an atrocious household, what ways!
