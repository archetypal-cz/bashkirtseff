Like this expression of Maman's in her last letter: "Dina," she says, "is quite *tapée*" (the Russian word is untranslatable and has a more wretched and extremely humiliating sense), "send her some dresses [word blacked out: that] Marie does not want, and some of Marie's boots too, the ones she does not wear."
