# Monday, 21 June 1875
%% J'ai reçu la caisse de Worth. C'est une charmante robe de mousseline de laine blanc-jaune, drapée devant comme une robe de statue; à longue traîne et, çà et là, des nœuds rose tendre d'une forme exquise. %%

Marie and Olga come to join us. Their manners are deplorable. Olga was telling I no longer know what story, with so many gestures and in such ill-chosen terms that Audiffer and I looked at each other and smiled. Saëtone is there. He leaves this evening; he keeps talking to me. If you think you amuse me, you old dog!

%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶2238 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Girofla is very pale — not sallow, he never will be, because he has a fine complexion and is in good health, but compared with the colour he used to have, he is pale. He looks unhappy, and I feel so sorry for him that I come home quite pensive inwardly. I say inwardly because I laugh and talk loudly. I do my hair and dress with some care for dinner, and in the evening everyone comes to see me on my terrace.

The superb Saëtone went past us, past the terrace, at nine in the evening, in the diligence that leaves every evening for Puget-Théniers. He will stay there a few days.
