The Sapogenikoffs came; I laughed a great deal. We went to his house twice — only once, the day of the English girls. The first time, when that coachman was bent on making us go in, we merely passed by. He seemed to be running after us — the Niçois, this was before the introduction; I thought a great many things, or rather one thing, and the idea came to me that it would be amusing if one could have a little fun.
