I should like the love and homage of the whole world. By the way, does anyone know that I am exceedingly susceptible to humility, to courtesy, to exquisite gallantry? With humble and submissive airs — provided he is not disagreeable to me, like the Pole — a man can make me the most submissive and gentle of creatures. But with airs of conquest and disdain he makes me furious, enraged and as wicked as the devil.

%% 034.0333 %%

%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶2066 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
If, after those disdainful airs, I saw the person come back submissive and imploring, I believe I should weep for joy, and far from taking advantage I should be kind and charming.
