## Summary

Volume 34 covers 18 days in June 1875, centred almost entirely on Nice. The dominant thread is Marie's flirtation with Émile Audiffret ("Girofla"), a handsome local young man whose family is engulfed in a domestic scandal. Alongside: chemistry lessons, poolside body comparisons, castle-watching through binoculars, a picnic at the Loup river, a dinner party at home, dancing, card readings, and a major financial crisis in the final entry.

Notable: the 25 June entry contains Marie's first mention of her voice returning and Barnola's professional verdict that she could become a great singer — one of the diary's pivotal moments.
