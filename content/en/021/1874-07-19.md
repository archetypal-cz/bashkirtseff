# Sunday, 19 July 1874

%% 021.0397 %%

> Dear Berthe,
> You will be surprised to receive my letter; I am surprised myself to be writing to you. We were never intimate enough to write to each other, which I have always regretted.

%% 2026-09-28T20:55:11 RSR: Restored from tome04.docx ¶536 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
> I had the pleasure of meeting the Comte de Tanlay here, who is one of your friends; we spoke of you, and the idea came to me to send you a message through him. There was a time when we detested each other -- you remember, at Baden? Since then that has changed completely, I hope.

> We met in Paris recently, but it was not kind of you never to come and see me. I am going to put you to shame: the next time we are in Paris, I shall call on you.

%% 021.0407 %%

At the band concert there was a crowd of pigs from the towns round about (white and silver dress, good). We had not been seated ten minutes when Gericke and *Mikhail Petrovich*, the Greek, came up.

%% 021.0408 %%
%% Gericke est rêveur, il reste auprès de nous sans y penser, il entame des conversations comme une machine. Depuis qu'il est ainsi, il est convenable et je ne puis placer ma belle phrase. A quatre heures et demie nous allons tous au Pouhon, où nous rencontrons M. de Jahal, un charmant homme. Basilévitch se montre a a fenêtre, Gericke court, elle disparaît, il s'arrête au milieu de la rue. %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:55:11 RSR: Restored from tome04.docx ¶547 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Gericke is dreamy; he stays near us without thinking about it and starts up conversations like a machine. Since he has been like this he is well-behaved, and I cannot deliver my fine speech. At half past four we all go to the Pouhon, where we meet M. de Jahal, a charming man. Basilevitch shows herself at the window; Gericke runs; she disappears; he stops in the middle of the street.

%% 021.0409 %%
%% Nous dînons à l'hôtel de Flandre, Gericke vient avec nous, mais aussitôt qu'elle le vit avec nous, elle vient et l'enlève. %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:55:11 RSR: Restored from tome04.docx ¶550 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
We dine at the Hotel de Flandre. Gericke comes with us, but as soon as she saw him with us, she comes and carries him off.

%% 021.0410 %%
%% Nous eûmes le temps de faire une visite aux Row et de nous placer à table, avant qu'elle l'eut lâché. C'est qu'on va le prendre pour de bon, Tanlay part, on prend Gericke. Si Gericke n'est pas, on prend le petit Polonais, sinon Body même, ce qui m'étonne c'est la patience de Gericke, il sait de Tanlay et attend tranquillement son tour. Il ne désire pas *avoir* seul, il veut *avoir* voilà tout. %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:55:11 RSR: Restored from tome04.docx ¶551 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
%% 2026-09-29T14:08:15 TR: The impersonal "on" (Basilevitch, unnamed) kept impersonal via the passive: "Gericke is taken". %%
We had time to pay a call on the Rows and take our places at table before she let him go. The fact is, he is going to be taken in earnest: Tanlay is leaving, so Gericke is taken. If there is no Gericke, the little Pole is taken, or failing him, even Body. What astonishes me is Gericke's patience: he knows about de Tanlay and quietly waits his turn. He does not want to *have* her to himself; he wants to *have*, that is all.

%% 021.0411 %%
%% A la table d'hôte à côté de nous sont M. et Mme de Bauche et le comte Bloudoff. %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:55:11 RSR: Restored from tome04.docx ¶552 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
At the table d'hôte next to us are M. and Mme de Bauche and Count Bloudoff.

%% 021.0412 %%
%% J'avais peur que Gericke ne dise quelque bêtise devant Mme de Bauche née princesse Troubetskoy et sœur de la princesse Woronzoff. Heureusement il ne dit rien de mauvais. C'est une désagréable connaissance qu'un monsieur pour lequel on a peur. Je commence à me lasser de lui, j'aime peu ce genre commun et libre. Je ne lui refuse pas un extérieur agréable, mais l'esprit et les manières sont gâtés et inconvenants. Il a dit *la *sotte et *Douchenka* mais c'est permis. Mme de Bauche s'est adressée alors à Dina, disant que sans doute ce monsieur pensait dire quelque chose d'agréable et parla pendant le dîner avec elle. %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:55:11 RSR: Restored from tome04.docx ¶553 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
%% 2026-09-29T14:08:15 TR: "*la *sotte" -- garbled emphasis in the source; read as "la sotte" (the silly goose), a name Gericke called Marie, italicised like *Douchenka*. %%
I was afraid Gericke would say something foolish in front of Mme de Bauche, née Princess Troubetskoy and sister of Princess Woronzoff. Fortunately he said nothing bad. A gentleman one has to be afraid for is an unpleasant acquaintance. I am beginning to tire of him; I have little liking for this common, free-and-easy style. I do not deny him a pleasing exterior, but his mind and manners are spoiled and unseemly. He said *silly goose* and *Douchenka*, but that is allowed. Mme de Bauche then turned to Dina, saying that no doubt this gentleman thought he was saying something agreeable, and talked with her all through dinner.

%% 021.0413 %%
%% A la musique le soir avec M. de Jahal, de Bauche, Merjeewsky qui a passé son examen très bien et qui revient peigné et en tube. Eristoff et Body nous font l'honneur de s'asseoir avec nous. Maman envoie chercher le baron qui rêve sous un arbre, il continuait de rêver avec nous mais vers la fin lia conversation avec moi, une conversation toute insignifiante, comme tout ce qu'il dit. Il a dit une chose inconvenante seulement, c'est: %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:55:11 RSR: Restored from tome04.docx ¶554 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
At the band concert in the evening, with M. de Jahal, de Bauche, and Merjeewsky, who passed his examination very well and has come back with his hair combed and in a top hat. Eristoff and Body do us the honour of sitting with us. Maman sends for the baron, who is dreaming under a tree; he went on dreaming with us, but towards the end struck up a conversation with me -- a wholly insignificant conversation, like everything he says. He said only one improper thing, namely:

%% 021.0422 %%
%% Au Casino maman se place à côté de la comtesse Merjeewsky, et moi et Dina sur un autre canapé entre Gericke, Paparigo etc. et le petit comte derrière. At the Casino Maman sits beside Countess Merjeewsky, and Dina and I on another sofa between Gericke, Paparigo etc., with the little count behind. Basilevitch was not there; I wanted her to come so that I could give my letter to de Tanlay, and I did not want it. I made eyes at the Pole a great deal, and at the others too, but very little.

%% 021.0423 %%
%% Il me dégoûte et pour ne pas laisser voir que je le déteste je le *regarde.* %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:55:11 RSR: Restored from tome04.docx ¶567 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
He disgusts me, and so as not to let it show that I detest him, I *look* at him.

%% 021.0424 %%
%% Gericke m'a demandé la permission de me faire une déclaration d'amour; après qu'il l'eut récitée le Polonais dit qu'il répétait la même chose au superlatif. Les plaisanteries étaient convenables et propres, je ne sais pas ce que Gericke a. %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:55:11 RSR: Restored from tome04.docx ¶568 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Gericke asked my permission to make me a declaration of love; after he had recited it, the Pole said he repeated the same thing in the superlative. The jokes were proper and clean -- I do not know what has come over Gericke.

%% 021.0425 %%
%% Arrive Basilévitch etc. je m'apprête à voir tous nos cavaliers s'envoler mais pas du tout, ils ne bougent pas. C'est surnaturel. %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:55:11 RSR: Restored from tome04.docx ¶569 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Enter Basilevitch etc. I brace myself to see all our cavaliers fly off, but not at all -- they do not stir. It is supernatural.

%% 021.0426 %%
%% Je remis ma lettre au milieu d'une contredanse à de Tanlay qui est le seul véritable monsieur parmi tous ces êtres qui portent des pantalons. Il part demain, il était venu chercher la lettre mais ne nous a pas trouvés. Il nous fit ses adieux ce soir de la manière la plus charmante. Gericke et le Polonais disparaissent, Papa est au théâtre. %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:55:11 RSR: Restored from tome04.docx ¶570 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
In the middle of a contredanse I handed my letter to de Tanlay, who is the only real gentleman among all these creatures who wear trousers. He leaves tomorrow; he had come to fetch the letter but had not found us in. He took leave of us this evening in the most charming manner. Gericke and the Pole disappear; Papa is at the theatre.

%% 021.0427 %%
%% Nous n'étions pas rentrées une demi heure lorsqu'on frappe à la porte, *entrez* ! c'est Papa, cela m'étonne mais enfin. Il reste trois quarts d'heure, on cause; je montre beaucoup de finesse et d'esprit, je me moque de lui. %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:55:11 RSR: Restored from tome04.docx ¶571 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
We had not been home half an hour when there is a knock at the door. *Come in!* It is Papa -- that surprises me, but still. He stays three-quarters of an hour; we chat; I show a great deal of finesse and wit; I make fun of him.

%% 021.0428 %%
%% Ce n'était pas trop ennuyeux. %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:55:11 RSR: Restored from tome04.docx ¶572 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
It was not too boring.
