# Wednesday, 14 April

%% 2026-03-01T10:00:00 TR: "le premier" = the first floor of the pavilion. "cave d'alchimiste" = an alchemist's cellar/den; rendered "a genuine alchemist's den!" %%
I go with Collignon to various places; I have it in my head to furnish the first floor of the pavilion and make it my library, and to take the kitchen of the same pavilion and make it my chemistry laboratory. A genuine alchemist's den!

%% 2026-01-30T16:10:00 LAN: "robe Archiduc" - dress named after the operetta "Madame l'Archiduc" by Offenbach; Marie names her dresses after memorable occasions %%
%% Le soir au Français (robe prince de Galles, bien) je nomme cette robe paille, prince de Galles parce que je l'avais le soir où le prince était au théâtre. Non ! Non ! Je la nomme Archiduc, Archiduc me rappellera le prince et la pièce que j'aime à cause de lui (robe Archiduc, bien). %%
%% 2026-03-01T10:00:00 TR: Kept the parenthetical outfit notes as Marie writes them — they are part of her diary character. "robe prince de Galles" / "robe Archiduc" = dresses named after occasions, like soldiers name battles. %%
In the evening at the Théâtre Français (Prince of Wales dress — good), I call this straw-coloured dress the Prince of Wales dress because I was wearing it the evening the Prince was at the theatre. No! No! I shall call it the Archiduc dress — Archiduc will remind me of the Prince and of the operetta I love because of him (Archiduc dress — good).

%% [#Health](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/HEALTH.md) %%
%% 2026-01-30T16:10:00 LAN: "le petit paysan" - Marie's dismissive nickname for Audiffret; "paysan" = peasant, used contemptuously to suggest lack of refinement %%
%% 2026-01-30T16:10:00 LAN: "dédains" - disdain/contempt; here meaning his failure to be properly introduced shows disrespect %%
%% 2026-01-30T16:10:00 LAN: "s'être présenté" - to have been introduced (proper social etiquette required formal introductions) %%
%% Presque tout le temps je me cachais sous l'éventail à cause de la rampe et puis le petit paysan ne me plaît plus, il a les joues ridées au soleil et ses *dédains* m'ont fatiguée. Comment ! jusqu'à présent ne pas s'être présenté ! Ce sont de véritables dédains. Que le diable l'emporte mais je voudrais me venger. %%
%% 2026-03-01T10:00:00 TR: "le petit paysan" = the little peasant — Marie's contemptuous nickname for Audiffret (implying lack of refinement). "dédains" = contempt/slights — the fact that he hasn't had himself introduced is read as deliberate snubbing. %%
Almost the whole time I hid behind my fan because of the footlights — and besides, the little peasant no longer pleases me; his cheeks are wrinkled by the sun, and I have wearied of his *snubs*. What! — not to have got himself introduced by now! Those are real snubs. The devil take him — but I should like to have my revenge.

We leave during the last interval. The peasant comes out at the same time as us and looks at me a great deal while we are waiting for the carriage, and I thought I heard — quite clearly:
