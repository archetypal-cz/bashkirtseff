I am mad about *Madame l'Archiduc* — I do nothing but sing it. Arriving at Cannes at seven o'clock, instead of speaking I sing l'Archiduc and behave like a madwoman. Collignon is to come and stay with us for a month; I am pleased — she is a very agreeable person, extremely useful, indispensable even.
