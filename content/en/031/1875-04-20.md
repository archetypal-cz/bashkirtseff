Tuesday, 20 April

%% 031.0292 %%
%% [#Howard_family](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/HOWARD_FAMILY.md) %%
%% 2026-01-30T16:10:00 LAN: "mal faites" - badly built/shaped (physical appearance); "mal mises" - badly dressed; double criticism %%
%% 2026-01-30T16:10:00 LAN: "j'en suis fort aise" - idiomatic: I am very pleased about it (Schadenfreude at rivals' poor appearance) %%
%% Les Howard étaient si laides, si mal faites, si mal mises hier soir que j'en suis fort aise. %%
%% 2026-03-01T10:00:00 TR: "j'en suis fort aise" = I am very pleased about it (Schadenfreude); rendered naturally. "mal faites, mal mises" = badly built, badly dressed — the double dismissal kept. %%
The Howards were so ugly last night, so badly built, so badly dressed, that I am extremely pleased about it.

%% 031.0293 %%
%% Je ne puis arriver à composer la lettre pour le portrait. Je crains de paraître ridicule. Je verrai si demain l'inspiration vient, sans l'inspiration on ne peut rien. %%
I cannot manage to compose the letter about the portrait. I am afraid of appearing ridiculous. I shall see if the inspiration comes tomorrow — without inspiration one can do nothing.

%% 2026-03-01T10:00:00 TR: "il y a encore du monde" = there is still society present; "monde" = fashionable society per TM. "je me gène de parler anglais" = I feel embarrassed to speak English in front of Collignon (who doesn't understand it). %%
We are fully into spring and there is still some society about. I dreamed of the devil. I walked with Rickard, then we get into the carriage with Collignon, in front of whom I feel embarrassed to speak English.
