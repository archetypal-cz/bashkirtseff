Well, Dumas always entertains and even [Crossed out: sometimes] often instructs. One cannot imagine how much I have thought of Sir Frédéric. From the very first moment I saw him he struck me as a rake of Carlo's sort — and I love nothing so much as Carlo's sort. I remember it so well: on the 10th of January last I was with Dina in a half-closed landau coming from the Promenade; we were passing the public garden, almost at the entrance to the Quai Masséna, when I saw a ==gentleman==[^1] walking along the boulevard towards the Promenade. He had his hands in his pockets and was opening his mouth to yawn at the very moment we passed.
[^1]: In English in the original.
