# Sunday, 20 August 1876 (8 August)

%% 065.0026 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Politics](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/POLITICS.md) %%

%% Et peu à peu, au troisième plat une discussion politique nous engloutit, moi et Hamsley. Ça a duré jusqu'à deux heures et demie. %%
Gradually, by the third course, a political discussion engulfed Hamsley and me. It lasted until half past two.

%% 065.0027 %%
%% D'ailleurs il était convenu qu'on veillerait jusqu'à trois heures, moment marqué pour mon départ. %%
In any case it had been agreed we would sit up until three, the appointed hour of my departure.

The whole "court" (one says *court* here to mean all the servants of a house) saw us off to the carriage. The neighbours departed at the same time as I, with [words blacked out: Étienne accompanied] by a calèche containing Paul, Amélie, Chocolat up on the box, two carts with the luggage, and preceded by Michel Hamsley mounted on a racing droshky (a plank set on four wheels) with a little coachman and a rustic lantern. He had taken it upon himself to clear the road ahead of us.

At Kharkoff we waited two hours. Alexandre was there. Despite my telegrams, he was practically stupefied to see me; I detected embarrassment in his manner but held myself well. He has no idea what I am or why I have come — he flatters me, he bows. He is a thorough scoundrel, and if I have trouble ahead it will be with him. [words blacked out: He told me] how very anxious my father had been, terribly worried that I would not come to him. My father had come to beg Alexandre not to accompany me, so that Paul could be sent to bring me to Gavronzi; he kept asking for the telegrams I sent my uncle, to know how far I had got on my journey. In a word, the greatest eagerness to have me, if not out of love then at least out of pride. Alexandre took a few digs at him, but my policy is to remain neutral.
