%% — Pendant l’hiver quand tout autour de nous est froid, sombre, muet, nous sommes sombres et froids, défiants. — In winter, when everything around us is cold, dark and mute, we are dark and cold and mistrustful. Then come the warm, sunny days, and there we are transformed, for the weather has an enormous influence on a man's character, his temper and even his convictions. In spring one feels happier and consequently better, and hence one cannot believe in the wickedness and baseness of men. How, when everything is so beautiful and when I am so happy, so full of enthusiasm and so disposed to good that I am quite intoxicated with it, how can there be room for evil thoughts in the hearts of others? That is what one tells oneself. Well, where we live one does not feel these intoxications, or at least far more faintly, from which I conclude that one is in a more normal state, and much the same all the time.
