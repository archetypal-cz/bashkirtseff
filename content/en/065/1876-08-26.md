%% # Samedi, 26 août 1876 (14 août) %%
# Saturday, 26 August 1876 (14 August)

%% 065.0501 %%
%% C’est crevant la campagne. %%
Country life is deadly dull.

%% 065.0502 %%
%% Quelle honte ! Quelle horreur ! Et je ne l’ai pas fait jeter à la porte après cela ! Je l’ai pardonné, je lui ai parlé, je l’ai embrassé ! Moi ! J’ai pardonné cette insulte ! La plus abominable, la plus sale ! Personne ne le sait au moins... il l’a peut-être raconté ! Fi ! Saleté ! Dégradation ! Honte, honte, honte ! %%
What shame! What horror! And I did not have him thrown out of the house after that! I forgave him, I spoke to him, I embraced him! I! I forgave that insult! The most abominable, the filthiest! At least nobody knows... perhaps he has told! Fie! Filth! Degradation! Shame, shame, shame!

With astonishing speed I sketched two portraits, my father and Paul. It took thirty-five minutes.

"How many women in this world could not say as much."

When I think of the ovation given to Cassagnac on 15 August![^065.0506.1] I read of it here in a Russian newspaper. A Bonapartist demonstration of the most pronounced kind. Cassagnac was all but smothered in the general enthusiasm. Reading it sent a shiver through my whole body and so went to my head that I am still choking with it.

They questioned me about Cassagnac; I told what I know of him, and in talking about him I worked myself up into a fanatic's enthusiasm for him. His astonishing article in *Le Pays* crowned it all and... well, there I am!

I was still in bed when my father came into my room, and after a few ordinary remarks, Paul having left the room, there was a silence during which I felt that my father had something to say; and as I wanted to speak of the same thing, I kept silent on purpose, as much so as not to be the one to begin as for the pleasure of seeing someone other than myself hesitate and be embarrassed.

Because my father has more wit than Maman, and not as much as I have. Besides, he respects me enormously, for I always beat him in argument, and my conversation is full of interest for a man who has grown rusty in Russia but has enough knowledge to appreciate it in someone else.
