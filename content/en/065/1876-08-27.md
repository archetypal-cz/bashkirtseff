%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶633, Mon Journal t.9 p.39 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
He wrote to his mother asking her permission to stay on in Russia at higher wages than those I pay him. This ingratitude pained me on his account, and, calling him in, I *exposed* his baseness before everyone and ordered him to go down on his knees. The child went down on his knees. The child began to cry and would not obey, so I was obliged to take him by the shoulders and the knees, and, more from shame than from force, he knelt, jolting a shelf laden with Sèvres. And I, standing in the middle of the drawing room, hurled the thunderbolts of my eloquence and ended by saying that I would send him back to France in fourth class with the oxen and the sheep, through the good offices of the consul of the Negroes.
