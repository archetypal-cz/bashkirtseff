This evening, Saturday, a concert and ball for the Murcia flood victims at the Hotel Continental, under the patronage of Queen Isabella, who after attending the concert came down to the ballrooms where she stayed for an hour. Maman and I had taken our places behind her; it was even tedious. We could not go down, nor could anyone come near us, until the Court had left. It was M. de Miranda who gave Maman his arm and had found us good places. He is invaluable. Soutzo had taken charge of us at the entrance, but do you know whose was the first face I saw among those lining the way at the door? Baron

My ineffable father writes me an astonishing letter in response to my business letter about Paul. He adopts a paternal tone, very tender and kind, speaking to me of our theatricals, begging me to do him the honour and the happiness of spending six months with him, after which he will be at my service for Paris. He promises me amusements at Poltava, playing comedy. He is sure I am adorable at the ball and that I act delightfully; he only regrets not having been there. "That is a fit occupation for a gentlewoman," he thinks, "as for painting (do not be angry), I do not like it. I like paintings, but it is not fitting to dirty one's own fingers," and then thirty-six kind things, not a word about Paul. Here is my reply. (See published letters)

Quite a crowd today. The Tchoumakoffs, Bizinsky, Soutzo gloomy, de Morgan, Wodzinsky, Mlle de Cerny — I must open a parenthesis: the Tchernitsky girl (de Cerny is her stage name) is from a good family, raised at the Institute for Noble Maidens; they noticed her beautiful voice and she is pensioned by the Emperor to develop this talent. Instead, she goes about, knows all the Russians, and amuses herself. An honest girl. I believe her mad; she speaks of her sciences, she claims to write on pathology, physiology, education, chemistry, etc., etc. And with all that she stirs up business deals and always announces that she is going to make 11 or 12 millions. Twenty-four or twenty-five years old, [Crossed out: looks very much like Nilsson.] Generally tedious, stupid, shrill, and irritating. There you have it. M. Dubois [words blacked out: the Count] de Fromessant (introduced yesterday), the Comte de Bouzat, Mme Gavini, Berthe, Mme de Bailleul, Mme Kent, a very rich American. Berthe says she looked at me for five minutes before recognizing me yesterday, I was so pretty. I hope Arnaud saw me, especially at the entrance, in my cloud. I have been up for more than thirty-six hours. I left my family at dinner with Tolmatcheff, Soutzo and Wodzinsky and the Cerny girl. They bore me.
