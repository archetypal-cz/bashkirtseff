%% [Mot noirci: Filippini] a posé, l'après-midi. Il est assez amusant à peindre, petit, blond, la figure ronde, la moustache jaune, l'air bonasse et martial. %%
[word blacked out: Filippini] sat, in the afternoon. He is rather amusing to paint: small, blond, a round face, a yellow moustache, a good-natured and martial air.

My archangel Gabriel is slow to reply. And my forfeit — I certainly won it fairly, and nothing. What does it mean? You know that since he left, there are days when I think of him a great deal, to the point of being sentimental... this evening, for example. He is an adorable being. It is good that he left. After our visit to the foyer, it might perhaps have turned out foolishly for me; it was a good moment, [Crossed out: almost as good as] even better than all the time spent with the pure, the immaculate one. I hope never to see him again so that there will be no shadow... And there already is one. He left us for "cousins I must accompany," and after putting the said cousins in their carriage, he ran into us on his way to fetch his own carriage just as we were leaving. It looked to me like a lie.

%% Et puis il avait l'air d'être comme j'étais, je ne sais comment... abattu, agité. And then he seemed to be as I was, somehow... downcast, restless. I take no credit for it, rest assured... besides, if he returned the regrets I gave him, that would not be so very excessive after all. Only, then one is all... I don't know how; one loves someone's presence and seems to be looking elsewhere; one has kindly thoughts and says things that are almost insolent; one would like to chat and says nothing; one remains there in a sort of torpor, cold in appearance and almost weeping within.
