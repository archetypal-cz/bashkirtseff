%% Trente six mille choses à la fois ! L'ancien Régime de Taine, puis la politique de la "Nouvelle Revue", puis les compositions classiques, puis une sur Nana, puis un roman à faire ou une pièce. Et le tout en même temps, je n'ai le temps de rien faire étant occupée jusqu'à vers cinq heures du soir. %%
Thirty-six thousand things at once! Taine's *The Ancient Regime*[^1], then the politics of the *Nouvelle Revue*, then classical compositions, then one on *Nana*, then a novel or a play to write. And all of it at the same time; I have no time for anything, being busy until five o'clock or so in the evening.

The evening before last I wrote to three people to obtain tickets, and it is Krishaber who sends me some. He is related to the Prefect of Police Andrieux, and has a very fine house of his own, and connections.

Gambetta was not presiding; we arrive late (Thursday) and the session is closed at twenty past three. Besides, I only went because Gavini had no tickets. She had told me the session would be worthless, but I did not believe her; besides, it is old Adeline's habit, she finds the Chamber tedious.
