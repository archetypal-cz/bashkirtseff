# Monday, 15 March 1880

%% 087.0610 %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% Drames inutiles. Tout est arrangé, samedi on a reçu des dépêches rassurantes. Il n'y a rien de grave. Le père Bashkirtseff propose à Paul, dès que maman sera arrivée, d'aller demander la main de l'infante officiellement pour effacer le scandale. %%
Needless dramas. Everything is settled; on Saturday reassuring telegrams arrived. There is nothing serious. Old Bashkirtseff proposes to Paul, as soon as Maman has arrived, to go and ask officially for the infanta's hand, to wipe out the scandal.

%% 2026-02-09T13:00:00 LAN: "Pyrame et Thisbé" - Pyramus and Thisbe; classical lovers from Ovid's Metamorphoses who killed themselves; Marie's tone is ironic %%
%% Quant aux jeunes gens ils sont d'accord que si le carême passé (pendant lequel on ne peut se marier) ils ne sont pas mariés ou si les parents d'un côté ou de l'autre s'opposent à leur union ils vont tout simplement se faire périr. Comme Pyrame et Thisbé. C'est beau l'Amour. %%
As for the young people, they are agreed that if they are not married once Lent is over (one cannot marry during Lent), or if the parents on either side oppose their union, they will quite simply do away with themselves. Like Pyramus and Thisbe. Love is a beautiful thing.

%% 087.0616 %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%

%% Soutzo a envoyé de superbes asperges entourées de fleurs. J'ai si peur de ce que dira Tony, il me semble que c'est si présomptueux à moi d'exposer bien que Julian ait dit que s'il n'y avait que des exposants de ma force ce serait bien heureux. %%
Soutzo sent superb asparagus surrounded by flowers. I am so afraid of what Tony will say; it seems to me so presumptuous of me to exhibit, although Julian said that if all the exhibitors were of my calibre it would be a very good thing.

%% 087.0617 %%

%% Il me semble que je serai anéantie de honte quand il viendra voir le tableau, je ne sais pas si j'oserai, pourtant je ne pourrai donc pas m'en aller s'il vient. C'est incroyable que ce soit moi qui dise cela et voyez pourtant ! Si je dirais cela tout haut on croirait que je blague. %%
I think I shall die of shame when he comes to see the picture; I do not know whether I shall dare, and yet I can hardly leave the room if he comes. It is incredible that it should be I who say this, and yet you see! If I said it aloud, people would think I was joking.

The Pure One has arrived. Our ladies met him yesterday on the Champs-Élysées. But my poetic notions are over.
