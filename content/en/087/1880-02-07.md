# Saturday, 7 February 1880

%% 2026-02-09T13:00:00 LAN: "roi Don Francois d'Assises" - King consort Francis of Assisi (1822-1902); his homosexuality was a public scandal, hence the "ineffable gesture" %%
%% C'est aujourd'hui que M. de Miranda nous présente à la Reine Isabelle, en audience particulière. Elle est très simple, très bonne femme, elle tend la main et fait asseoir maman à côté d'elle sur le canapé et moi en face. J'ai pour la première fois le plaisir de dire: votre majesté, c'est très agréable. Sa majesté est indignée d'un article paru dans "le Voltaire" contre le roi Don François d'Assises. "Car après tout, dit-elle en nous regardant et en faisant un geste ineffable, c'est mon mari". %%
Today M. de Miranda presents us to Queen Isabella in a private audience. She is very unaffected, very good-natured; she holds out her hand and has Maman sit beside her on the sofa and me opposite. For the first time I have the pleasure of saying "Your Majesty" — it is very agreeable. Her Majesty is indignant about an article in *Le Voltaire* attacking King Don François d'Assise. "For after all," she says, looking at us and making an ineffable gesture, "he is my husband."

She is also furious about the Grévy ball: "an impossible set of people, and women in day dresses with the sleeves cut off for the occasion." We stay ten minutes or a quarter of an hour, I think. The Queen is not so ugly as they say; she even has very pale blue eyes that are pretty.

On saying goodbye she held out her hand to Maman and to me; Maman kissed it, and I bowed very low over it. This Miranda wants to marry. As I shall not marry him, he may well become an enemy. But bah! Whenever some trouble befalls me, my first thought is: I wish I were a fortnight older. Everything fades or becomes bearable.
