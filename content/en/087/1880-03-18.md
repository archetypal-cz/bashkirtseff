Here is a letter from L. Enault of the *Moniteur des Arts*. No doubt Mme de Mouzay spoke to him about me. And the painting that is not coming along. What is maddening is that the arrangement is very pretty and I feel and know I could do better. What I do in studies is better. What I have done here is shriveled work; it is mangy painting! Oh dear!
