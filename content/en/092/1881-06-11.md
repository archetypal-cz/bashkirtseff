# Saturday, 11 June 1881

Yesterday we went to Poltava — my father was to go officially to see Prince Sviatopolk-Mirsky,[^1] governor-general of three or five provinces, who comes to spend twenty-four hours in Poltava. It was raining and as I could not work I wished to come. [*entry continues*]

[^1]: Prince Sviatopolk-Mirsky: a senior imperial administrator responsible for the governance of several provinces in the Ukrainian region. Official visits of this kind required local nobles such as M. Bashkirtseff to present themselves formally.
