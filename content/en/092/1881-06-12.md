# Sunday, 12 June 1881

I have begun a life-size peasant woman, standing, leaning against a woven wattle fence with dry branches like a basket. Straw and planks have been laid on the ground to protect me from the damp, and a small portable two-room pavilion has been erected there — so I am admirably set up.
