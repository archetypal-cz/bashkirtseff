# Friday, 12 August 1881

You perhaps think I have decided something! I can do nothing! I have the dreadful certainty of my own incapacity! Breslau's paintings pass before my eyes and I can undertake nothing! It has been more than a month, counting the time lost in travelling, that I have done nothing! I cannot even imagine myself working — I am horrified in advance at the talentless, dry, cold things I shall produce.

It is odious — I can do nothing!

%% 092.0410 %%
%% Et tout s’en mêle. Je renonce au tableau et me décide à peindre Oelsnitz mais elle part dans deux jours. Alors je vais chercher un modèle que je ne trouve pas. Puis je cours chez Julia, elle ne peut poser qu’à partir de lundi. Je me retourne vers la petite du concierge mais elle a encore trois jours de classes. Enfin... S’il y avait encore quelqu’un mais je suis toute seule, le milieu fait tant ! Et quand je ne suis pas seule je suis avec des êtres qui ne comprennent rien à tout cela. Ah ! que Breslau est heureuse. Je suis idiote à force de ne pas échanger trois mots par jour. Ma tante m’agace pour peu qu’elle me demande si je n’ai pas faim ou bien ce que je compte faire dans la journée. %%
%% 2026-09-28T21:01:18 RSR: Restored from tome14.docx ¶2338, Mon Journal t.14 p.186 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
And everything conspires against me. I give up the picture and decide to paint Oelsnitz, but she leaves in two days. So I go looking for a model, and find none. Then I hurry to Julia's; she cannot pose until Monday. I turn to the concierge's little girl, but she still has three days of school. Well... If only there were someone else, but I am all alone; surroundings count for so much! And when I am not alone I am with beings who understand nothing of all this. Ah! how happy Breslau is. I am growing stupid from not exchanging three words a day. My aunt irritates me if she so much as asks whether I am not hungry, or what I mean to do with the day.

%% 092.0411 %%
%% Enfin c'est que probablement je suis malade. Non mais dévoyée, étourdie, assommée en peinture. %%

Well, the reason is probably that I am ill. No — rather led astray, dazed, stunned where painting is concerned.

So I go to see Amanda (!) — who is working in the courtyard of her house at Issy! It does me good, even though she is not artist enough to really put new heart into me — no matter, it revives me, and I come home resolved to do this wretched picture.
