# Friday, 12 August 1881

You perhaps think I have decided something! I can do nothing! I have the dreadful certainty of my own incapacity! Breslau's paintings pass before my eyes and I can undertake nothing! It has been more than a month, counting the time lost in travelling, that I have done nothing! I cannot even imagine myself working — I am horrified in advance at the talentless, dry, cold things I shall produce.

%% 092.0398 %%
%% C'est odieux, je ne puis rien faire ! %%

It is odious — I can do nothing!

%% 092.0399 %%
%% Eh bien j'y travaille deux heures pas meme et puis va te promener ! La question est que mettons que j'y renonce et alors je me dis: qui sait ? Ca aurait peut-etre ete tres bien. Alors je me decide et aussitot de me dire, c'est pretentieux et ne dit rien. %%

Well then, I work at it — barely two hours — and then: give it up! The question is this: suppose I abandon it, and then I say to myself: who knows? Perhaps it would have been very good. So I decide to go on, and immediately I tell myself: it is pretentious and says nothing.

%% 092.0400 %%
%% Enfin c'est que probablement je suis malade. Non mais devoyee, etourdie, assommee en peinture. %%

In short, the truth is that I am probably ill. No — but astray, dizzy, stunned as a painter.

%% 092.0401 %%
%% [#Amanda](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/AMANDA_SIDVAL.md) %%
%% Alors je m'en vais voir Amanda (!) qui travaille dans la cour de sa maison a Issy ! Ca me fait du bien, bien qu'elle ne soit pas assez artiste pour que je sois vraiment remontee, c'est egal ca vivifie, je rentre resolue a faire ce sacre tableau. %%

So I go to see Amanda (!) — who is working in the courtyard of her house at Issy! It does me good, even though she is not artist enough to wind me up fully again — no matter, it revives me, and I come home resolved to do this confounded painting.
