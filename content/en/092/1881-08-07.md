# Sunday, 7 August 1881

Saint Agathe got us a box for the dress rehearsal of *Oedipus Rex* at the Comédie-Française.[^1]

[^1]: *Oedipus Rex*: a new production of Sophocles's tragedy at the Comédie-Française, one of the major theatrical events of the 1881 season.
