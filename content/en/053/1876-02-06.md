# Sunday, 6 February 1876

I dreamed that the Surprising One's château had such wide windows that one could see all the interior; then servants were carrying immense dishes — I asked why? I was told it was for Audiffret.

%% 053.0387 %%
— But why are they carrying them into the garden?
— Because Audiffret is giving dinner in a little pavilion.

%% 053.0388 %%
And then one of the servants said:
— Do you not know that those who approach him to serve his food risk being beaten?

%% 053.0389 %%
An absurdity that worries me, as we have had [not] a single piece of news from Nice.

%% 053.0390 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/people/writers/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
I was at church with Maman and Domenica. This woman is so vulgar that I am shocked at every moment. She is truly impossible when she speaks — I have never heard such a pronunciation in Russian. She is fifteen years older than her husband. Besides, it was quite a story, and it is only now that Papa has permitted her to come. Now — that is to say, for five or six years.

%% 053.0391 %%
Then we go to Mme de Reculât's — whom we do not find, as she is at lunch at her sister's, Princess Gianetti, daughter of the Duchess of Valmy, or so d'Épinay told me. De Daillens writes me — she was at the races and saw Galula,[^1] who spoke to her about me, "red as a cockerel."

[^1]: *Galula*: Marie's private code-word for an eligible bachelor of suitable quality.

%% 053.0392 %%
At two o'clock we at last receive a telegram worded thus:
All well, busy arranging furniture arrived from Paris.

%% 053.0393 %%
%% [#Mortality](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MORTALITY.md) %%
I breathe — no one is dead; I shall therefore not be wearing black. As for everything else, I am not reassured — I do not wish to be. I expect the most grievous news of all kinds; that way the blow will seem lighter, at least I shall be prepared.

%% 053.0394 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/people/writers/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Music_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MUSIC_THEME.md) %%
It is hailing, it is cold — I go nowhere. And this evening we go to the Opera — *Ruy Blas*.[^2] I did a card reading to find out if Soroka would be there! The cards said *yes*.

[^2]: *Ruy Blas* (1869): opera by Filippo Marchetti based on Victor Hugo's play.

%% 053.0395 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/people/writers/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Theater_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/THEATER.md) %%
O veridical prophets! Soroka was not at the theatre. I had only Lola and her mother with me. The boxes of the King's suite and those of the hunt committee were full of opera glasses that were often directed toward me — but Soroka was missing and I was bored. *Ruy Blas* is a charming opera.
%% 2026-03-05T11:00:00 TR: "lorgnettes" = opera glasses; here metonymy for the gentlemen using them to look at her. %%

%% 053.0396 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/people/writers/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
— Marie, Lola said to me suddenly, Soroka — the real Soroka — has arrived!

%% 053.0397 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/people/writers/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Music_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MUSIC_THEME.md) %%
And immediately I was animated — but it was false: Soroka was not there. After the Opera and before the ballet, Rossi comes, and I converse with him in a most charming way — all the while thinking that at this very moment the Cercle de la Méditerranée is illuminated and crowded with fine clothes, and that Vigier is singing the role of Marguerite,[^3] the most charming after Mignon.

[^3]: Marguerite: the soprano lead in Gounod's opera *Faust*. Vigier is singing Faust; the role of Marguerite would be sung by a woman, but Marie means Vigier sings in *Faust* — she has inverted subject and object. Preserved as written.

%% 053.0398 %%
And here I am mouldering in Rome — and despite all the promises I go nowhere. In Rome as everywhere: alone and miserable.
