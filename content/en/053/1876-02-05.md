# Saturday, 5 February 1876

Tomorrow, Vigier will sing *Faust* at the Cercle de la Méditerranée.

%% 053.0383 %%
We go out with Maman, just the two of us — bad weather, grey and cold; I have a slight headache and am very anxious about Nice. Neither letters nor telegrams. What has become of them all?

%% 053.0384 %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
%% [#Mortality](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MORTALITY.md) %%
In the evening we telegraph them, asking an explanation of this strange silence. And we make suppositions — we hover between someone's death, Audiffret's marriage, or something even worse.

%% 053.0385 %%
%% [#Religion](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/RELIGION.md) %%
%% [#Latin](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/LATIN.md) %%
As for me, I pray to God nonetheless and go to bed sad and bored, having copied at least two letters — one is the one in which the soul is recovered, the other is the old *Rosière* crowning ceremony[^1] that has not yet been sent. One had only to change the dates — and I have fixed the ceremony for the 22nd of May, the feast day of Saint Émile,[^2] and have signed it — *The citizens of the city of Nice* — and beneath that I have drawn in ink the arms of Nice: a crowned eagle on three hills with the inscription: ==Nicae Civitas==.[^3]

[^1]: *Rosière*: traditionally a virtuous village maiden crowned with roses for her good conduct — applied here with biting irony to Audiffret, a man.

[^2]: Saint Émile's feast day: 22 May — Audiffret's first name being Émile.

[^3]: *Nicae Civitas*: "City of Nice" — the Latin inscription on Nice's coat of arms.
