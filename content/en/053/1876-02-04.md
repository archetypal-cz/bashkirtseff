# Friday, 4 February 1876

I have no wish to go out — I am bored. We visit the gallery of the Palazzo Doria on the Corso.

%% 053.0355 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% [#Reading](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/READING.md) %%
*Sapristi*,[^1] how beautiful it is. There is a gallery with statues and great windows with red curtains that give an enchanting light. How fortunate these princes! There are Poussins, Guercinos, Van Dycks, and Titians of rare beauty. A portrait by Diego Velázquez is admirable for its naturalness. A Magdalen by Titian — my favourite painter — a true Magdalen, beautiful and with the right expression. And all these paintings are placed in magnificent rooms of richness and taste, so that the whole ensemble is truly astonishing. All visitors inscribe their names in the book — and I wrote: Mlle Marie Bashkirtseff, M. Saëtone, Miss Robenson.
%% 2026-03-05T11:00:00 TR: "Sapristi" = mild oath, surprise. "Miss Robenson" = la Pointue from carnet 049/051 — so Robenson is with them in Rome. %%

[^1]: *Sapristi*: a mild exclamation of surprise (euphemism for *sacristie*).

%% 053.0356 %%
At four o'clock, having resumed my white clothes — that is, my normal form — I go to the Pincio with Lola.

%% 053.0357 %%
No one else would come. Besides, we are very well together — she is just as mad and amusing as my Graces.

%% 053.0358 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/people/writers/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
The Ruspoli brothers we encounter ten times on the Pincio and almost as many on the Corso. The elder pays no attention to me but the younger looks. As for us, we cannot see them without laughing — and to be decorous we pretend to be saying important things to each other and look away. When it is already growing dark we go to Tua's, to speak of the domino and other matters.

%% 053.0359 %%
%% [#Russian](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/RUSSIAN.md) %%
On the way back we talk nonsense — of Ruspoli, whom Lola calls ==Sobaka==[^2] (dog), of Soroka whom we have not managed to see, and of Colonna whom we have never seen, who perhaps does not exist, but whom Dina finds handsome after visiting his gallery.
%% 2026-03-05T11:00:01 TR: "Sobaka" = Russian for dog — Lola's nickname for the younger Ruspoli. Highlighted and footnoted. %%

[^2]: *Sobaka*: Russian for "dog."

%% 053.0360 %%
It is amusing — we always give our suitors feminine-gender names: Girofla, Sobaka, Soroka... and Colonna. I was wondering what to put on the ring that will serve as the badge of recognition for the members of our society.

%% 053.0361 %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
For the president: a column on a heart, [blacked-out words: at the top] of the column a Soroka, across the column a key, and the initials T.A.E. — that is [blacked-out words: Thalie,] Aglaé, Euphrosine.[^3]

[^3]: The Three Graces (*Charites*) of Greek mythology: Thalia, Aglaea, and Euphrosyne. Marie's secret society is named after them.

%% 053.0362 %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
For the two grace-members founding members, Marie and Olga — the same, minus the initials; and for the other members, only the column, the Soroka, the heart, and instead of the key and initials, a rose.

%% 053.0363 %%
In the evening we speak of the old Audiffret's scandal with his wife, and all manner of Nice affairs.

%% 053.0364 %%
%% [#Reading](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/READING.md) %%
I have read the days of the hero's presentation. I truly believe he was very pleasing then — otherwise I would not hate him so much now.

%% 053.0365 %%
%% [#Latin](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/LATIN.md) %%
But first of all, one must write him this: ==Turpissimus omnium homo!=[^4]

[^4]: *Turpissimus omnium homo!*: "Most base of all men!"

%% 053.0366 %%
After many revisions we have it at last! I need not copy it here — here is the draft.

%% 053.0367 %%
%% [#Music_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MUSIC_THEME.md) %%
%% [#Religion](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/RELIGION.md) %%
%% [#Theater_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/THEATER.md) %%
After having passed through the cigar-smoke of M. Milon de Véraillon, your soul sojourned for a week in the office of the Théâtre Français [Crossed out: from which it was rejected with] — from which M. Piano had it rejected along with the old cobwebs — and, driven again by some diabolical breath, it fell into the Paillon near the Pont Neuf. But your patron saint, martyred in Africa in 250, was watching over it, for at that very moment the doorman of the Cercle Masséna, who was crossing the bridge on his way to the Hôtel de la Rose, recognised your soul (having often had it in pawn during your financial crises) and fished it out with his halberd (note the difference! — Brother Ardigo descended himself to the bed of the torrent). The doorman therefore fished it out and went to deposit it at the poorhouse [Crossed out: where it sojourned for another week, after which interval]. As its description had been circulated everywhere, we were informed of its whereabouts, and two brothers — the same who came to see you that other Sunday [Crossed out: and whom you received so ill] and whose advice you would not hear — went to fetch it and brought it back to us. For greater security and to avoid misfortunes that have filled us with bitterness, we have entrusted your soul to the keeping of Brother Ardigo, who leaves it neither night nor day, reading the prayers for the dying.
%% 2026-03-05T11:00:02 TR: Second elaborate mock-convent letter, continuing the narrative of the retrieved soul. "Paillon" = the river through Nice. "Brother Ardigo" = a recurring figure in Marie's religious satires. The Prodigal Son parallel continues with the "poorhouse" echo. %%

%% 053.0368 %%
Hardened sinner, come and reclaim it.

%% 053.0369 %%
%% [#Politics](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/POLITICS.md) %%
I forget to say that I saw the Greek minister and that the Comtesse de Reculât and Mme and Mlle Soukowkine were with us. No one could be received. My mother is in bed.

%% 053.0370 %%
%% [#Meta_diary](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/META_DIARY.md) %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
Scoundrel of an Audiffret! Reading my diary, I recalled his mouth and closed my eyes to reconstruct his whole face. And I confess to my shame that it made me blush... and gave me pleasure.

%% 053.0371 %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
For four days, no letters. Thinking of what might have happened, I felt a great pounding of the heart, and like the madwoman I am, I lost my breath for a few seconds — then I went in to Maman as if nothing had happened, but my heart was still leaping and I was greatly agitated. And why? One must be so stupid!

%% 053.0372 %%
Despite all his coarseness, this [blacked-out word: man] still interests me.

%% 053.0373 %%
%% [#Theater_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/THEATER.md) %%
Before his memory I seem to forget that he treated us like the lowest creatures, that he turned his back on us like — creatures! Yes, despite all my pride, I forget all that before his memory, and go on giving him the leading role in all my bedtime stories.

%% 053.0374 %%
One can easily understand how much I must hate him for his contempt, for his coarseness.

%% 053.0375 %%
You will perhaps say he acted perfectly naturally after the anonymous letters — yes, if he knows who wrote them. But how can he know? How would he dare suspect me capable of such a thing?

%% 053.0376 %%
And besides, he began to withdraw before the letters. No, no — it is appalling! What a pity!

%% 053.0377 %%
— At last! I cried on the day he was presented — not everything in my life will be a vain desire and a constant disillusion!

%% 053.0378 %%
%% [#Reading](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/READING.md) %%
Book 33 [Vol. V, open at p. 92] — and I read this phrase with the bitterness you can imagine!

%% 053.0379 %%
I reread the time when all those Nice people, and Girofla at their head, ran after me. And then I believed it could last! I wanted to go to Italy, to return for the races in Nice and triumph before the Prodgerses, Robensons, etc. etc.

%% 053.0380 %%
Yes — all that is good for others, not for a wretched disgraced creature like me!

%% 053.0381 %%
No — everything in my life is nothing but vain desire and constant disillusion!
