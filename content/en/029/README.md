## Translation Notes

### Major Decisions
- **Goethe/Mignon parody** (1875-01-21): Marie's own French verse inspired by *Mignon*; translated with footnote on source
- **Extended English passage** (1875-01-27): Marie's imperfect English preserved verbatim with all her errors (*appearence*, *not so stay*, etc.); marked ==highlight==, footnoted "*In English in the original*"
- **Italian passages in Monte Carlo** (1875-01-28): *muso*, *untranquilla* kept in Italian with footnotes explaining theatrical family slang
- **Italian practice entries** (1875-02-13): Entire entry in Italian; each paragraph ==highlighted== and translated, with notes on grammar errors ("uno vecchia" for "una vecchia")
- **Gonzales phonetic speech** (1875-02-14): Spanish-accented French ("zé souis fautif") rendered phonetically in English approximation; footnoted
- **"That is the question"** (1875-02-07): English in the original (Shakespeare); kept and marked per protocol
- **"bataclan"** (various): Marie's idiomatic slang; rendered contextually as "the usual splendid show" or "the whole gang"
- **"plobster"** (1875-01-27): Marie's phonetic spelling of British slang "lobster" (man of fashion); kept as-is with footnote
- **"époque hamiltonienne"** (1875-01-27): Marie's coinage — rendered "the Hamiltonian era"
- **Biblical inversion** (1875-01-27): Marie inverts the miracle of five loaves and seven fish; preserved as written for comic effect
- **D.D. Home** (1875-02-18): Daniel Dunglas Home, famous spiritualist medium; identified in footnote
