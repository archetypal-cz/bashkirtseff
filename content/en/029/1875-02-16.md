Tuesday, 16 February 1875.

The Englishwoman is slowly being tamed, and today she spoke. Not that her conversation strikes me as interesting — but she must speak, as a lesson.

I have begun Latin with Brunet, and, save for the execrable French pronunciation, his lessons are excellent.

I am quite abashed by the praises my family lavishes upon me — the more so as I deserve barely a quarter of them.
