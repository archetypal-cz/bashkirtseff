# Friday, 22 January 1875
%% A huit heures nous sommes à Nice, entrons droit chez maman, nous la trouvons debout et assez bien, avec Pelikan, Mme Sapogenikoff et ses deux filles, Marie seize ans, Olga quatorze. %% 2026-09-28T20:55:11 RSR: Restored from tome04.docx ¶4090 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
At eight o'clock we are in Nice; we go straight to Maman's and find her up and fairly well, with Pelikan, Mme Sapogenikoff and her two daughters, Marie, sixteen, and Olga, fourteen. Marie is almost as small as before; she is in love with Cima — the one from Geneva — and he with her, and they are to be married, but they look such children that it is ridiculous: no one would give the bride more than twelve or the groom more than fifteen.

%% 029.0016 %%
%% Olga est plus grande que sa soeur et ressemble d'une façon fort compromettante à Pâris. Pure imagination, Olga est le portrait de M. Sapogenikoff, du reste Nina n'a rencontré Pâris qu'après la naissance d'Olga. %%

Olga is taller than her sister and bears a most compromising resemblance to Pâris. Pure imagination: Olga is the image of M. Sapogenikoff; besides, Nina met Pâris only after Olga was born.
