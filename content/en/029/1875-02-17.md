# Wednesday, 17 February 1875

It is raining. I go out with my aunt (blue dress). I go to the ==club==[^4] — that is to say, to Rumpelmayer's. I forgot to say that last evening I was at the opera — *La Sonnambula*[^1] — and was bored. This evening at the Théâtre Français with Nadia, Sacha, and Julie — *Le Petit Faust*[^2] — as bored as yesterday (blue cloth dress, white *fichu*,[^3] hair done).

[^1]: Title in the original: Marie writes the French *La Somnambule*. *La Sonnambula* (The Sleepwalker): opera by Vincenzo Bellini (1831), one of the most popular works of the bel canto repertoire.
[^2]: Title in the original: *Le petit Faust* (*Little Faust*). Comic operetta by Hervé (1869), a broad parody of Gounod's *Faust*.
[^3]: *Fichu*: a triangular scarf worn folded around the shoulders and neck, fashionable in the 1870s.
[^4]: In French in the original: *cercle*.
