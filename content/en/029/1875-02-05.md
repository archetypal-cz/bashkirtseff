# Friday, 5 February 1875

%% 029.0126 %%
%% 2026-01-30T15:20:00 LAN: "à la rencontre de" - to meet (at the station) %%
%% 2026-01-30T15:20:00 LAN: "Botkine" - RUSSIAN: Russian visitor, likely doctor/intellectual %%
%% 2026-01-30T15:20:00 LAN: "Doria" - Italian acquaintance %%
%% 2026-01-30T15:20:00 LAN: "pas comme avant" - not like before (implying previous false alarm) %%
%% Ma tante étant allée à la rencontre de Botkine vit sortir de la gare Doria qui cette fois est bien arrivé pas comme avant. %%
My aunt, having gone to meet Botkine, saw Doria come out of the station — who this time has genuinely arrived, unlike before.

%% 029.0127 %%
%% 2026-01-30T15:20:00 LAN: "(gris, bien)" - parenthetical outfit note: grey, looking good %%
%% 2026-01-30T15:20:00 LAN: "trois cents francs, misère !" - three hundred francs, misery! (expensive for their situation) %%
%% A trois heures je sors (gris, bien) pour différentes choses du Carnaval, il nous coûtera trois cents francs, misère ! Ce n'est pas comme cela que je voudrais. %%
At three o'clock I go out (grey, well) for various Carnival errands; it will cost us three hundred francs — misery! That is not how I should like it.

%% 029.0128 %%
%% 2026-01-30T15:20:00 LAN: "mon érudition" - my learning/scholarship (ironic self-deprecation) %%
%% Aujourd'hui est arrivé un Russe qui donne des leçons de latin, mais le malheureux a tant entendu parler de mon érudition qu'il n'ose pas m'accepter pour élève. %%
Today a Russian has arrived who gives Latin lessons — but the poor man has heard so much of my erudition that he dares not accept me as a pupil.

%% 029.0129 %%
%% 2026-01-30T15:20:00 LAN: "notre pauvre monde" - our poor circle (social group) %%
%% Voilà comme on exagère dans notre pauvre monde ! %%
That is how they exaggerate in our poor circle!

%% 029.0130 %%
%% 2026-01-30T15:20:00 LAN: "allons donc !" - come now! (dismissive) %%
%% Moi, une savante, allons donc ! %%
Me — a scholar! Come now!

%% 029.0131 %%
%% 2026-01-30T15:20:00 LAN: "grand événement" - great event (ironic or sincere?) %%
%% 2026-01-30T15:20:00 LAN: "on se le dispute" - they fight over him (socially) %%
%% 2026-01-30T15:20:00 LAN: "comme un roi, comme un dieu" - like a king, like a god (hyperbolic) %%
%% 2026-01-30T21:00:00 RSR: Botkine - likely Sergei Botkine (1832-1889), famous Russian physician or a relative; Russian intellectual celebrities were feted in expatriate circles %%
%% Le soir Botkine vient, grand événement, on se le dispute et on l'honore comme un roi, comme un dieu. C'est à ce bon Pelikan que nous devons cela. %%
In the evening Botkine comes — great event! Everyone vies for him and he is honoured like a king, like a god. It is to good Pelikan that we owe this.

I have had two glimpses of the devil; he looks so cold, so haughtily stupid that he frightens me — yet I cannot deny him a certain Macainne-ish air, stupidly presumptuous.
