%% Il y a entre certaines lamentations de Nice et de Rome une différence radicale. A Nice c’était jeune et quoique je me répétais ce n’était pas trop fatigant et c’était naïf, véritable. %% 2026-09-28T21:06:13 RSR: Restored from tome06.docx ¶4270, Mon Journal t.6 p.268 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Between certain lamentations from Nice and those from Rome there is a radical difference. In Nice it was youthful, and although I repeated myself it was not too tiresome, and it was naive, genuine. Whereas this Antonelli whom I created in Rome... Almost everything I say about him rings false and unpleasant; I have just reread it all from a distance of five or six years. So I beg the editor to cut as many of the Rome pages as he can. Leave enough for people to see how far it all went — but I wrote the same thing every day, and without naivety; in short, this bombastic literature rings false, speaking of a love created, forced, imposed by me upon myself. If Torlonia had declared himself to me, he would have been another Antonelli.
