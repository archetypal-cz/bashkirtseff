# Wednesday, 21 March 1877
Rosalie wakes me with a letter. Read it.[^1] It gave me less pleasure than the first, because I wanted it yesterday.

[^1]: *Lisez-là* — a minor grammatical slip in the original (should be *Lisez-la*).

Larderel has written to the racing committee — he is entering horses. Then Mme Hamontoff arrives, then Bikowsky.
And then I go to see the Fabbricatore young women, who have colds.

%% 069.0462 %%
%% Et puis... Je reste là une heure pour écrire ces quelques lignes, depuis quelques jours il me faut un temps infini pour écrire une ou deux malheureuses pages. Je suis paresseuse et je ne m'intéresse plus. %%
And then... I sit there an hour to write these few lines. For some days now it has taken me an eternity to write one or two wretched pages. I am lazy and nothing interests me any more.
