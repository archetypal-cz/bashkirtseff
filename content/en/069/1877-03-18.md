%% 069.0434 %%
%% - Comment, vous si savante, si artiste, si spirituelle ! vous vous moquez de moi ! %%

%% 069.0440 %%

%% 2026-09-28T20:52:24 RSR: Restored from tome10.docx ¶521, Mon Journal t.10 pp.36–37 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
In my letter to Collignon I send my regards to la Daniloff; the poor woman will be touched by it — she was not received in our house. And then the letter to Lefèvre. He was not expecting it. It costs nothing, and it wins... friendships better than a great deal of expense... to have people speak well of me can do no harm.
