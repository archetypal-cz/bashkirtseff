# Saturday, 24 March 1877
[Crossed out: Since this morning]

%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Naples](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/NAPLES.md) [#Worth](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/WORTH.md) [#Nasimoff](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/NASIMOFF.md) [#Duc_de_Melito](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/DUC_DE_MELITO.md) [#Maman](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/MAMAN.md) %%
%% [#Health](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/HEALTH.md) %%
%% Je suis restée longtemps au lit m'efforçant de dormir pour être réveillée par une lettre. Mais non, rien n'arrive si ce n'est une caisse de Worth et puis Nasimoff et enfin le duc de Melito. On n'a reçu que Nasimoff parce que maman est malade. %%
I lay in bed a long time, trying to sleep so as to be woken by a letter. But no — nothing arrives except a box from Worth,[^1] then Nasimoff, and finally the Duc de Melito. We received only Nasimoff, because Maman is ill.

[^1]: Worth: the celebrated Paris couturier Charles Frederick Worth (1825–1895), whose house dressed the elite of Europe.

%% 069.0495 %%

%% Je vais en ville avec Dina. Le jeune Pignatelli commence à me regarder sérieusement. Mais c'est Melissano qui est le plus fervent. %%
I go into town with Dina. Young Pignatelli is beginning to look at me seriously. But it is Melissano who is the most ardent.

The letter seems impertinent to me and I am flushed with vexation.
