The letter is postmarked the 13th, that is, Tuesday; it left Florence on Monday the 12th. And Altamura only brought it on the 16th! Only now, examining the envelope, have I noticed this. I replied on the 17th; he will receive it on the 18th. That means he will have waited six days for an answer!! That is too long in such a case. *I am sorry about it.* This phrase carries enormous weight, because as a rule I pride myself on being careless.
