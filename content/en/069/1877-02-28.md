%% Pour abréger le temps je dors tard et Doenhoff est entré deux fois au salon sans y trouver personne. %%
# Wednesday, 28 February 1877
To make the time pass I sleep late, and Doenhoff came into the drawing room twice and found no one there.

Yesterday Nasimoff spoke of a Mme Hamontoff, born Countess Projex, whose acquaintance would be most agreeable, and today that lady's husband paid us a call to invite us to their home this evening at five o'clock — we went. They are ==respectable==[^3] and extremely amiable.

%% [#Veglione](../../_original/_glossary/culture/social_customs/VEGLIONE.md) %%

Then we went to the Skating; I am more or less cured of my madness — our cavaliers from the Veglione[^1] were there.

[^1]: *Veglione*: a Neapolitan masked ball.
