# Tuesday, 22 April 1884
I go to see Julian and he says: *medal* — as if it were a real possibility. I am enchanted, and feel ready to paint pictures, sculpt statues, and write books, straight away. Books above all.

%% 104.0024 %%
%% [#Religion](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/RELIGION.md) %%
%% Ce n'est pas une vie cela ! Ne voir personne, n'aller nulle part !!! A quoi bon alors !! Mon Dieu je l'ai déjà dit mille fois mais il y a des moments où il faut le répéter encore. [Rayé: tellement] %%
This is no life! Seeing no one, going nowhere!!! What is the point then!! Dear God, I have said it a thousand times already, but there are moments when one must say it again. [Crossed out: so much]
