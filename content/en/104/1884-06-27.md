# Friday, 27 June 1884
Just as we are about to leave for a drive in the Bois, the architect comes up to the carriage. They arrived this morning, and he comes to tell us that Jules is a little better, that he made the crossing well, but that unfortunately he cannot go out. That Jules would have taken such pleasure in telling me himself the success my painting had with everyone he showed it to in Algiers, etc.
