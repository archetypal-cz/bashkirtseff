# Sunday, 27 April 1884
Yesterday Rosalie brought me a letter from Guy de Maupassant from the poste restante. The fifth, and the best. So we are no longer at odds?

%% 104.0032 %%
%% Et puis il a fait dans "Le Gaulois" une chronique ravissante, je me sens radoucie... C'est très amusant. %%
And he has written a ravishing piece in *Le Gaulois* — I feel myself softening towards him... It is all very amusing.

%% 104.0033 %%
%% 2026-02-02T15:00:00 LAN: "que je ne connais pas" - whom I don't know; Marie corresponded with Maupassant anonymously as "Domino rose" %%
%% Cet homme que je ne connais pas occupe toutes mes pensées depuis vingt-quatre heures. Pense-t-il à moi ? Pourquoi m'écrit-il ? %%
This man whom I have never met has occupied all my thoughts for twenty-four hours. Does he think of me? Why does he write?
