It is an infamous conspiracy with Saetone and everyone! He believes I am in love with him — upon my honour. Ah! how I would prove to him that he is mistaken, if God were willing! But God is not willing — God does not trouble himself with such trifles! God concerns himself with everything. He ought to favour me a little — at the beginning I had no wicked thoughts. And if I have them now, it is because I am exasperated! The longer my revenge is delayed, the greater it shall be.

Well then — no. I feel myself capable of no malice.
