# Sunday, 8 August 1875
%% J'étais à l'église puis à l'exposition du Palais de l'industrie où nous avons déjeuné tant bien que mal. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3832 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
I was at church, then at the exhibition at the Palais de l'Industrie, where we lunched as best we could.

%% 038.0197 %%
%% [#Weather](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/WEATHER.md) %%
%% [#Fashion](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/FASHION.md) %%
%% Il fait beau, je suis en blanc avec un grand chapeau de paille, forme mousquetaire, relevé par un nœud blanc et deux plumes. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3833–3835 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
It is fine; I am in white, with a large straw hat, musketeer shape, turned up with a white bow and two feathers.

%% 038.0198 %%

%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3836 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
I came home, leaving Stiopa and his wife there; I am tired — we walked for two hours. They are going to look and admire some more; as for me, I would rather stay at home alone. There are handsome carriages at the exhibition, and I want one.

It will be dull in the Bois; there will be riff-raff. Writing paper with my arms on it has just been brought to me. How long the days are!

%% Je suis dame de carreau, le roi pareil sortait toujours, or ce peut aussi bien être un monsieur blond qu'un homme amoureux, puisque le roi pareil à la dame veut dire un amoureux ou un fiancé ou un homme qu'on aime. %% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3843 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
I am the queen of diamonds; the king of the same suit kept coming up. Now, that may just as well be a fair-haired gentleman as a man in love, since the king of the same suit as the queen means a lover, or a fiancé, or a man one loves. But he may also be someone indifferent. I did not understand the cards well then; Stiopa has taught me, and now I know. That fair king is Smirnoff, that good soul who leaves for Russia this evening. He has been very useful to us, and very convenient.

%% 038.0206 %%

My aunt, who told me with a certain satisfied air that I would see Audiffret in Paris, that we would be staying next door, and a thousand other little things which, now that the affair has turned out so badly, are so many vexations to me.

All that already seems far away — and so sweet, so pleasant!

Back from Paris, when I was still pale, we began looking at each other and blushing. Then the evenings at the Opera, where I went for that wretch, then at last the mad drives of the spring, and at last we become acquainted.
