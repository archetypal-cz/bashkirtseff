# Wednesday, 5 January 1876

%% 051.0383 %%
%% C'est hier qu'a eu lieu la vente de l'âme du frère Emile. %%
It was yesterday that the soul auction of Brother Émile took place.

%% 2026-09-28T09:54:44 ED: Paragraph kinds marked in this entry (letter 051.0385, …); presentation only (marker, quoting, clipping tags), wording unchanged; approval flags kept. %%
> — ==Well then==[^it13], here I am in Rome. "Oh, how astonishing! Oh, how surprising!" There is a Russian cold here — the water is freezing in the fountains — but the cold would be nothing if it were only the cold. Since this morning we have been searching for lodgings and have seen only one. I had not the courage to go up when they pointed out to me some black, gaping, filthy, frightening hole.

[^it13]: In Italian in the original: *Orsu* — well then, come now.

%% 051.0387 %%

%% > En vain je cherche quelque maison ayant quelque ressemblance avec les maisons de France, je ne trouve que des masures ou des colonnes craquées. %%
> In vain I look for some house bearing any resemblance to the houses of France — I find nothing but hovels or cracked columns.

%% 051.0388 %%
%% kind: letter source="Lettre de Marie au général, Rome, 5 janvier 1876" %%
%% > C'est sans doute beau mais convenez avec moi qu'un bon appartement confortable est infiniment plus agreable tout en etant moins artistique. Je crois que nous finirons par nous loger dans les bains de Caracalla ou dans le Colisee. %%
> It is no doubt beautiful, but you will agree with me that a good comfortable apartment is infinitely more agreeable, even if less artistic. I think we shall end up lodging in the Baths of Caracalla or in the Colosseum.

%% 051.0389 %%
%% kind: letter source="Lettre de Marie au général, Rome, 5 janvier 1876" %%
%% 2026-02-02T12:05:00 LAN: "chretienne martyre" - Christian martyr; reference to Colosseum's history %%
%% > Les étrangers me prendront pour quelque ombre de chrétienne martyre dévorée par quelque tigre féroce, devant quelque empereur carnivore ! %%
> The foreigners will take me for the shade of some Christian martyr devoured by some ferocious tiger before some carnivorous emperor!

%% 051.0390 %%
%% kind: letter source="Lettre de Marie au général, Rome, 5 janvier 1876" %%
%% > Quant aux meubles nous nous contenterons de fragments de statues ou d'ossements, restes sublimes d'un passé désormais impossible ! %%
> As for furniture, we shall make do with fragments of statues or bones — sublime remnants of a past that is henceforth impossible!

%% 051.0391 %%
%% kind: letter source="Lettre de Marie au général, Rome, 5 janvier 1876" %%
%% > Après mon installation dans le Colisée ou dans le Forum je vous donnerai de plus grands détails sur la ville Eternelle. %%
> After I have settled in the Colosseum or the Forum I shall give you more details about the Eternal City.

%% 051.0392 %%
%% kind: letter source="Lettre de Marie au général, Rome, 5 janvier 1876" %%
%% > En attendant j'attends une lettre de vous, cher général, qui je le sais d'avance sera très charmante et très aimable. %%
> In the meantime I await a letter from you, dear General, which I know in advance will be most charming and most agreeable.

%% 051.0393 %%
%% kind: letter source="Lettre de Marie au général, Rome, 5 janvier 1876" %%

> ==*Orsu*==, au revoir.
> Marie Bashkirtseff.

%% 051.0394 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Philosophy](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/PHILOSOPHY.md) %%
%% Voilà la vérité ! Pas un appartement habitable ! où sommes-nous ! Où sommes-nous ? Cette affreuse ville peut-elle se nommer une capitale ! Nous ne sommes pas en Europe ! Pas une maison convenable à louer ! Je suis découragée, ennuyée ! %%
That is the truth! Not a single habitable apartment! Where are we! Where are we? Can this dreadful city call itself a capital? We are not in Europe! Not one decent house to let! I am discouraged, exasperated!

%% 051.0395 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% Mais je ne bougerai pas avant mai. %%
But I shall not move before May.

%% 051.0396 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% O Rome ! Je crois que nous prendrons un plus grand appartement à l'hôtel et y resterons. A la place d'Espagne seulement on respire. %%
O Rome! I think we shall take a larger apartment in the hotel and stay there. On the Place d'Espagne at least one can breathe.

%% 051.0397 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% Est-ce possible que ce soit Rome ! Quel affreux mélange de belles antiquités et de nouvelles vilenies ! %%
Can this really be Rome! What a dreadful mixture of beautiful antiquities and modern vileness!

%% 051.0398 %%

%% 051.0399 %%

I am so frightened, so discouraged. At Nice I was at least at home — but here! Who knows what awaits us! Let us have faith in God — if not in Rome, where?

I have seen the façade of St Peter's. It is superb — it ravished my heart, especially the left colonnade, because no building rises above it and its columns against the sky as their background produce the most striking effect. One might think oneself in ancient Greece.

==" Arches on arches! As it were that Rome
Collecting the chief trophies of her line
Would build up all her triumphs in on dome,

As twere its natural torches, for divine
Should be the ligth which streams here to illume.
This long-explored but still exhaustless mine

[^en15]: In English in the original. From Byron, *Childe Harold's Pilgrimage*, Canto IV. Spelling errors (*moobeams*, *ligth*, *on dome*) as Marie copied them.

I have to say that last night I dreamed of Audiffret, and pleasantly. I have sent several more letters to be posted from Nice.

%% 051.0407 %%

I am ugly and tired. I shall have to arrange everything tomorrow — everything must be arranged. I am in suspense, anxious.

%% [#Religion](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/RELIGION.md) %%
%% Dieu ! tranquillisez-moi. J'ai chez moi à arranger tout cela à Rome. %%
God! Set my mind at rest. I have all of this to arrange here in Rome.

%% 051.0409 %%

The first thing to do is to take an apartment and unpack the trunks, and then make use of the letters.
