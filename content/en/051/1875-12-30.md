# Thursday, 30 December 1875

%% 051.0109 %%
%% 2026-02-02T12:05:00 LAN: "depeche" - telegram; sending from Monte-Carlo to hide origin %%
%% La lettre signee *Alfeda-da-f*e, n'a pas ete envoyee, et a sa place, maman a envoye de Monte-Carlo cette depeche: %%
The letter signed *Alfeda-da-fe* was not sent; in its place, Maman sent the following telegram from Monte-Carlo:

%% 051.0110 %%
%% Nice, Emile d'Audiffret, Tour Audiffret. %%
%% Le dehors est fleuri, %%
%% Le dedans est pourri. %%
%% Miserere ! %%
%% Alfeda-da-fé. %%
*Nice, Émile d'Audiffret, Tour Audiffret.*
*The outside is in bloom,*
*The inside is in rot.*
*Miserere!*
*Alfeda-da-fe.*

%% 051.0111 %%
%% Par télégraphe c'est beaucoup mieux. %%
By telegram it is far better.

%% 051.0113 %%
%% 2026-02-02T12:05:00 LAN: "vipere" - viper; Marie's animal metaphor for disliked people, here paternal aunt %%
%% Ça commence par le magasin Adé. Pendant que nous y sommes, entre Mme Tutcheff. Maman me dit d'aller la saluer, mais je rougis, et ne bouge pas, alors elle-même s'approche de cette vipère et lui parle. %%
It starts at the Ade shop. While we are there, Mme Tutcheff comes in. Maman tells me to go and greet her, but I blush and do not move, so she herself goes up to this viper and speaks to her.

%% 051.0114 %%
%% Chaque fois que je pleurais à la maison, maman me disait: %%
Every time I cried at home, Maman would say to me:

%% 051.0115 %%
%% - Va vivre avec la tante Tutcheff. Et dernièrement elle a parlé sérieusement d'un rapprochement. %%
— Go and live with Aunt Tutcheff. And recently she spoke in earnest about a reconciliation.

%% 051.0116 %%
%% Mais ma chere tante est aussi froide que possible. Elle n'etait pas seule, Mme Meynek, une autre soeur de mon pere. %%
But my dear aunt is as cold as possible. She was not alone — Mme Meynek, another sister of my father's, was with her.

%% 051.0117 %%
%% Celle-là a parlé plus en tante, celle-là n'avait pas l'air d'un serpent, jaune et sifflant ! %%
That one spoke more as an aunt — she did not look like a yellow, hissing serpent!

%% 051.0118 %%
%% Mais passons cet incident sec et désagréable. %%
But let us pass over this dry and disagreeable incident.

%% 051.0119 %%
%% J'étais chez les Sapogenikoff pour leur dire de venir demain veiller avec moi, et faire la bonne aventure. %%
I went to the Sapogenikoffs' to invite them to come and sit up with me tomorrow and tell fortunes.

This evening, *Sapho*. I wore a sort of Neapolitan blouse in blue crêpe de chine and old lace. The front is white, the sleeves wide — well, it defies description, but original and as charming as possible. With a white skirt and a white satin reticule.

We arrive at the end of the first act and find ourselves next to the Prodgers woman, and with her is Miss Robenson, and I hear the voice of the Surprising One. It stopped me short like a bit stopping a horse in full gallop. I stayed throughout the entire interval without moving, listening to what was being said next door.

%% 051.0122 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% 2026-02-02T12:05:00 LAN: "la Pointue" - Marie's nickname for Robenson, "the Pointed One" (thin/angular) %%
%% 2026-02-02T12:05:00 LAN: "figure" - 1870s: face, NOT body shape %%
%% 2026-02-02T12:05:00 LAN: "(faux ou vrai, qu'importe ?)" - referring to whether beauty is natural or achieved artificially %%
%% 2026-03-05T10:00:00 TR: "la Pointue" → "the Pointed One" (Marie's nickname, kept). "figure" → "face". %%
%% Ce n'est pas en vain que la Pointue est connue sous le nom de la Belle Américaine. On ne peut rien dire contre sa figure, elle est jeune, fraîche, (faux ou vrai, qu'importe ?) et adorable. On pourrait dire qu'elle est trop maigre, c'est vrai, mais une figure aussi ravissante fait passer ce défaut sous silence. Elle a les cheveux.... Ah ! ça je ne sais pas, à Spa elle était plus blonde que moi, ici elle est plus brune. N'importe, elle a de fort jolis yeux, elle est très bien coiffée. En la regardant je me suis de suite sentie les yeux trop clairs, le nez trop gros, les petits cheveux devant mal arrangés. Il n'y a rien qui enlaidit comme se sentir inférieure aux autres, les yeux deviennent ternes et l'on perd toute assurance. %%
It is not for nothing that the Pointed One is known as the Beautiful American. There is nothing to be said against her face — she is young, fresh (genuine or otherwise, what does it matter?) and adorable. One might say she is too thin, which is true, but so ravishing a face silences that objection. Her hair... Ah, as for that, I cannot say — at Spa she was blonder than I am; here she is darker. No matter: she has very pretty eyes, and her hair is beautifully dressed. Looking at her I immediately felt my eyes too pale, my nose too large, the little curls at my temples ill-arranged. There is nothing that makes one uglier than feeling inferior to others — one's eyes grow dull and one loses all assurance.

%% 051.0123 %%
%% Maman et ma tante ne faisaient que promener leurs yeux de l'Américaine à moi. %%
Maman and my aunt did nothing but shift their eyes from the American to me.

%% 051.0124 %%
%% - Marie a une jolie bouche, dit maman après un long examen. %%
— Marie has a pretty mouth, says Maman after a long scrutiny.

%% 051.0125 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% Aussitôt la toile levée, j'entends le Surprenant sortir, et je vois la Robenson lui prendre la main. Comme il a du la lui serrer, et comme elle doit être heureuse ! %%
As soon as the curtain rises I hear the Surprising One leave, and I see Miss Robenson take his hand. How he must have pressed it — and how happy she must be!

%% 051.0126 %%
%% Il s'en alla chez une dame anglaise qui, depuis peu arrivée ici, a attiré tous les hommes. Tournon, Bergerault et d'autres encore sont chez elle. %%
%% 2026-03-05T10:00:00 TR: "tous les hommes" in source — "a attire tous les men" looks like a transcription error in source ("men" for "hommes"). Translating as "attracted all the men". %%
He went off to an English lady who, having recently arrived here, has attracted all the men. Tournon, Bergerault and several others are at her house.

%% 051.0127 %%
%% Le voyant là je me remets un peu. De loin je vois mai et il avait l'air assez vilain, il restait là comme à regret et sans dire un mot. %%
Seeing him there I recover somewhat. From a distance I see poorly, and he looked rather wretched — standing there as if reluctantly, without a word.

%% 051.0128 %%
%% - N'est-ce pas qu'il a l'air bête quand il se tait ? demandai-je. %%
— He looks rather dull when he says nothing, doesn't he? I asked.

%% 051.0129 %%
%% 2026-02-02T12:05:00 LAN: "bete fauve" - wild beast/predator; Marie finds this attractive in men %%
%% - Non, répondit ma tante, mais il a l'air féroce, il a quelque chose de la bête fauve. %%
— No, replied my aunt, but he looks fierce — there is something of the wild beast about him.

%% 051.0130 %%
%% Vous savez qu'on ne pouvait pas mieux dire pour me l'embellir. %%
You will understand that nothing could have been better calculated to make him more attractive to me.

%% 051.0131 %%
%% Miss Robenson... (remarquez, comme depuis la Pointue je suis arrivée à dire Miss Robenson) Miss Robenson donc est parfaitement jolie. %%
Miss Robenson... (note how, since "the Pointed One," I have progressed to saying "Miss Robenson") — Miss Robenson, then, is perfectly pretty.

%% 051.0132 %%
%% [#Theater_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/THEATER_THEME.md) %%
%% 2026-03-05T10:00:00 TR: "brûlais" is physical burning (emotional agony), not metaphorical warmth — "I was burning up inside" to capture the intensity. %%
%% Je reconnais simplement mon infériorité et, écrasée, ne bouge pas et ne désire rien. Seulement je brûlais et il fallait à chaque instant me mordre les lèvres, tant parce qu'elles séchaient que pour ne pas pleurer comme une bête en plein théâtre. %%
I simply acknowledge my inferiority and, crushed, do not move and desire nothing. Only I was burning, and every moment I had to bite my lips — both because they were dry and to keep from weeping like a brute in the middle of the theatre.

%% 051.0133 %%
%% [#Theater_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/THEATER_THEME.md) %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% Le Surprenant reste un acte et un entracte chez cette Anglaise, puis revient dans sa loge, et pose mélancoliquement, pas comme avant et d'une façon exagérée, mais on voit bien qu'il ne le fait pas exprès. %%
The Surprising One stays one act and one interval at the English woman's box, then comes back to his own and poses melancholically — not as before, not in an exaggerated way, but one can plainly see he is not doing it on purpose.

%% 051.0134 %%
%% Prodgers et l'Américaine s'en vont avant la fin, beaucoup avant la fin et Audiffret n'a pas la patience de rester plus de dix minutes, plus qu'elles. Au moment où la Robenson s'est levée et tournait le dos au public, j'ai vu un regard du Surprenant se fixer sur son dos tout ouaté, un regard involontaire et qui en disait assez. %%
Prodgers and the American leave before the end — well before the end — and Audiffret cannot be patient enough to stay more than ten minutes longer than they do. At the moment when Miss Robenson rose and turned her back to the audience, I saw a glance from the Surprising One fix itself on her quilted back — an involuntary glance that said quite enough.

%% 051.0135 %%
%% Pour un pareil regard je donnerais un jour de ma vie, venant de n'importe qui, et venant du Surprenant, une semaine. %%
For a glance like that I would give a day of my life — if it came from anyone; and if it came from the Surprising One, a week.

%% 051.0136 %%
%% Avec ça que quelqu'un la prendrait cette semaine d'une vie aussi misérable et aussi triste que possible. %%
As if anyone would take that week of a life as miserable and wretched as possible.

%% 051.0137 %%
%% Tant que je suis animée, heureuse, je suis jolie, mais si je suis comme depuis quelque temps, adieu tout. %%
As long as I am animated, happy, I am pretty — but if I am as I have been of late, goodbye to all of it.

%% 051.0138 %%
%% Je deviens et surtout je me sens laide, confuse, misérable, à me cacher sous terre. %%
I become — and above all I feel — ugly, confused, miserable enough to bury myself underground.

%% 051.0139 %%
%% A présent, l'Américaine, rentrée chez elle dort sans doute d'un sommeil heureux et tranquille qui lui conservera son teint, et soutiendra sa peau de vingt-sept ans. Tandis que moi, je veille, et cette veille me ride, me vieillit, me dessèche. %%
By now the American, safely home, is no doubt sleeping a happy, tranquil sleep that will preserve her complexion and maintain her twenty-seven-year-old skin. While I lie awake, and this wakefulness is wrinkling me, ageing me, drying me out.

%% 051.0140 %%
%% Je n'ai pas parlé en voiture, je sentais qu'avec les paroles viendrait un déluge. Une ou deux minutes avant d'arriver seulement, je me suis sentie assez forte pour dire ceci: %%
I did not speak in the carriage — I felt that with words would come a flood. Only a minute or two before arriving did I feel strong enough to say this:

%% [#Theater_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/THEATER_THEME.md) %%
%% 2026-02-02T12:05:00 LAN: "traiter son monde en canaille" - to treat people like riffraff; strong social insult %%

— Truly, M. de Tournon and Bergerault have behaved like extremely rude people. They were invited, they did not come, they paid no call afterwards, they did not even come to the box. Oh! it is truly treating one's acquaintances like riffraff — like absolute riffraff.
