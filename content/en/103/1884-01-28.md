# Monday, 28 January 1884
I start Dina's portrait again, but it is still unspeakable toil — she does not give me what I want; finally around five o'clock I manage to sketch her in charcoal. As for Claire, she does nothing and is greatly amused watching me fret and listening to me make my observations, my digressions, etc., etc.

%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% [#Nausicaa](../../_original/_glossary/culture/art/DOULEUR_DE_NAUSICAA.md) %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:30:00 LAN: "Nausicaa" - planned painting subject, reference to Homer's Odyssey princess %%
%% A cinq heures j'essaye d'un nouveau modèle pour Nausicaa. Irma a vingt-trois et quoique très belle vingt-trois ans ne sont pas quinze. %%
At five o'clock I try a new model for Nausicaa.[^2] Irma is twenty-three, and though very beautiful, twenty-three is not fifteen.

[^2]: Nausicaa: the young princess in Homer's *Odyssey* who discovers the shipwrecked Odysseus on the shore; a subject requiring a young, innocent model.

%% 103.0154 %%
%% Il vaut mieux des formes plus... simples mais de la jeunesse et de la naïveté. La petite a seize ans mais... enfin nous verrons. %%
Better to have simpler... forms but youth and naivety. The little one is sixteen, but... well, we shall see.

%% 103.0155 %%
%% Et le soir j'écris quoi ? Mais je ne sais pas, je n'ai pas encore trouvé le moule... %%
And in the evening I write — what? But I don't know; I have not yet found the mould...

%% 103.0156 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:30:00 LAN: "croquent" - make quick sketches %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:30:00 LAN: "croquis littéraires" - literary sketches, written vignettes %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:30:00 LAN: "carton" - preparatory sketch/cartoon for a painting %%
%% Seulement ce qui est incontestable c'est qu'il m'est plus naturel d'écrire que... de peindre. Voyez les vrais peintres dessinent, croquent, composent, sans rien savoir, enfants tous seuls... Moi je dessinais aussi mais pas trop, ça a toujours été plutôt cette idée: "je suis douée pour la peinture et un beau jour j'en ferai." Tandis qu'écrire, j'ai des masses de croquis littéraires et depuis l'âge de dix ans et maintenant et toujours... Des ébauches d'impressionniste, d'ignorante, de pauvre étrangère qui ne sait même pas bien s'expliquer (ô modestie, vite détrompez-moi) enfin comme le carton d'un peintre qui ne sait rien mais qui a la vocation. %%
Only this much is undeniable: it is more natural for me to write than... to paint. Look at real painters — they draw, sketch, compose, knowing nothing, children on their own... I drew too, but not much; it has always been rather this idea: "I am gifted for painting and one day I shall do it." Whereas for writing, I have masses of literary sketches, going back to age ten, and now and always... Impressionist, ignorant, poor-foreigner drafts from someone who cannot even express herself well (oh, modesty — quickly disabuse me!) — in short, like the portfolio of a painter who knows nothing but has the vocation.

%% 103.0157 %%
%% On ne peut pas faire tant de choses... Mais si... %%
One cannot do so many things... But yes one can...

%% 103.0158 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% Peindre tant qu'il fait jour, modeler jusqu'à dîner et écrire quand ça vient ou avant de dormir. %%
Paint as long as there is daylight, model clay until dinner, and write when it comes — or before sleeping.

For after all... what is needed? Failing to have lived *everything*... But who has lived everything? So, failing to have lived everything, one must feel very keenly and live wholly in imagination...

%% 103.0165 %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:30:00 LAN: "seize et neuf ans (additionnez)" - sixteen and nine years (add them) = 25, Marie's cryptic way of giving her age %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:30:00 LAN: "manier de la terre" - handling clay, reference to sculpture work %%
%% Ça... et d'autant plus que j'ai seize et neuf ans (additionnez) à cet âge on est paraît-il... On peut même avoir des visions... Mais je n'ai pas le temps et puis après être restée plusieurs heures debout et les bras en l'air à manier de la terre on n'a qu'une aspiration, dormir et recommencer le lendemain. %%
That... and all the more so as I am sixteen and nine years old (add them up)[^3] — at that age, it seems one is... one can even have visions... But I have no time, and besides, after spending several hours on one's feet with arms raised, working clay, one has but one aspiration: to sleep and begin again the next day.

[^3]: Marie's coded reference to her age: 16 + 9 = 25.

I am very happy.
