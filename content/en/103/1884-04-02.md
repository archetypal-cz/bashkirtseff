# Wednesday, 2 April 1884
I went to Petit's[^1] (Exhibition in the rue de Sèze) and stayed there for an hour before Bastien's incomparable canvases. Cazin is good too — but!

[^1]: The Galerie Georges Petit (rue de Sèze): one of the most prestigious private galleries in Paris, which hosted the major exhibitions of Bastien-Lepage, Monet, Renoir, and other leading artists of the period.

The maréchale had Claire sit her examinations at the Hôtel de Ville, had her learn to paint, instilled a taste for serious literature, and set her up as a superior young woman. I helped with this image-making — and now. That will teach me.
