Cette femme qui était blanchisseuse à ce qu'on dit, est encore belle à cinquante deux ans; avec des mains longues, souples et blanches. Parlez-moi de la race après de pareilles mains... %%
# Friday, 1 February 1884
I work on the painting outdoors, then to the Canroberts' and to Princess Jeanne's — we find only her mother there. This woman who was a laundress, they say, is still beautiful at fifty-two, with long, supple, white hands. Talk to me about breeding[^1] after such hands...

[^1]: *Race* in the 1880s sense of inherited refinement and physical distinction, not ethnicity.

%% Ce soir vernissage aux Aquarellistes. Foule énorme, personne de connaissance et fatigue... %%
This evening, the private view at the Watercolourists' exhibition.[^2] Enormous crowd, no one of acquaintance, and fatigue...

[^2]: The *vernissage* (varnishing day): a preview evening for artists and invited guests before a public exhibition opened.
