# Friday, 21 March 1884
To see the exhibition of the painter Raffaëlli,[^1] then to the maréchale's.

[^1]: Jean-François Raffaëlli (1850–1924): painter and printmaker celebrated for his scenes of Parisian suburban and working-class life; he exhibited with the Impressionists in 1880 and 1881.
