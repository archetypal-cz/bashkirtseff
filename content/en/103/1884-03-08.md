# Saturday, 8 March 1884
Morning on the portrait, afternoon on the painting; at half past five they came upon me in the garden — I consented to receive them, and went into the dining room covered in paint-stains, with my hair loose down my back and palette in hand, where a crowd of people were taking tea... The Baron d'Alt and Zuccarini dine.
