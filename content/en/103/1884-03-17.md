# Monday, 17 March 1884
We drink to the Orthodox priest's cross — an intimate dinner: the Orthodox priest himself, Eshmann, Géry, M. and Mme Pelikan (he is old Pelikan's nephew and has just returned from Japan, where he was consul), Mme and Mlle Kanchine.
