%% # Mercredi 12 mars 1884 %%
%% Jeudi 13 mars 1884 %%
%% Vous ne vous imaginez pas cette folie, je me figure que le portrait de Claire sera prêt samedi à quatre heures. Je le fais au jardin, grandeur nature jusqu'aux genoux. C'est très gentil mais... %%
# Wednesday, 12 March 1884
# Thursday, 13 March 1884
You cannot imagine this madness — I am imagining that the portrait of Claire will be ready by Saturday at four o'clock. I am painting it in the garden, life-size to the knees. It is charming, but...

%% 103.0478 %%
%% Bastien-Lepage va mieux, il est même sorti, ma tante assure l'avoir vu monter en voiture soutenu par une dame et Emile. Elle dit soutenu pour que je ne sois pas vexée de ce qu'il ne vienne pas ici. %%
Bastien-Lepage is better — he has even been out; my aunt swears she saw him getting into a carriage, supported by a lady and Emile. She says "supported" so that I will not be put out by his not coming here.

%% 103.0479 %%
%% Mais je suis vexée... %%
But I am put out...

%% 103.0480 %%
%% [#Health](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/HEALTH.md) %%
%% Enfin que voulez-vous c'est comme ça, il a d'autres préoccupations, soucis, etc. C'est tout naturel. Est-ce que je m'occupe moi de X, Y, Z., que je puis trouver même aimables à l'occasion mais que je n'irais pas voir étant souffrante ou ennuyée. %%
Well, what would you have — it is how things are; he has other preoccupations, worries, etc. It is entirely natural. Do I myself concern myself with X, Y, Z — whom I may even find agreeable on occasion — but whom I would not go to see when I am unwell or out of sorts?

%% 103.0481 %%
%% [#Health](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/HEALTH.md) %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: "Le Gaulois" - conservative Parisian newspaper, society/arts coverage %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: "Damvillers" - Bastien-Lepage's birthplace in Meuse %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: "cascadeuse" - loose woman/adventuress, theatrical slang for woman who "falls" easily %%
%% C'est égal. Pourquoi ? Pourquoi, pourquoi ? %%
%% Ça me rend malheureuse pendant trois heures. "Le Gaulois" parle de lui et des études qu'il a rapportées de Damvillers... et annonce son départ prochain pour l'Algérie où il va achever sa convalescence... Ce vieux malade, ce petit paysan tranquille amoureux d'une cascadeuse et de quelle cascadeuse encore, l'ancienne, l'amie intime de Mme Cartwright ! %%
Never mind. But why? Why, why?
It makes me miserable for three hours. *Le Gaulois* speaks of him and of the studies he brought back from Damvillers,[^1] and announces his imminent departure for Algeria where he will complete his convalescence... That old invalid — that quiet little peasant in love with a loose woman, and what a loose woman at that — the former one, the intimate friend of Mme Cartwright!

[^1]: Damvillers: Bastien-Lepage's native village in the Meuse, to which he regularly returned to paint. Several of his most celebrated works, including *Joan of Arc* (1879) and *Les Foins* (1877), were painted there.

%% 103.0482 %%
%% [#Health](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/HEALTH.md) %%
%% Fi ! Et le voilà malade et laid et ratatiné. Et pourquoi m'en occuper. Bah. Je trouverai un autre sujet. Bonsoir. %%
Faugh! And there he is, ill and ugly and shrunken. Why occupy myself with him? Bah. I shall find another subject. Good night.

%% 103.0483 %%
%% [#Reading](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/READING.md) %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: Flaubert reference - his famous dedication to writing as sole purpose %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: "toiles à barbouiller" - canvases to daub, self-deprecating for painting %%
%% Enfin je n'ai donc aucun intérêt dans la vie ?! Et faut-il dire comme Flaubert que je n'entrevois qu'une suite de feuilles de papier à nourrir, c'est-à-dire de toiles à barbouiller ? %%
So then — have I no interest in life whatsoever?! Must I say, like Flaubert,[^2] that I see nothing before me but an endless succession of pages to fill — that is to say, canvases to daub?

[^2]: Gustave Flaubert (1821–1880) was famous for his total dedication to writing as a vocation, treating literary work as the only worthwhile end in itself. Marie wryly translates his writer's vocation into the painter's equivalent.

%% 103.0484 %%
%% Alors la vie n'est remplie que de travail ? Mais si c'est quelquefois un plaisir c'est plus souvent une souffrance. Mais alors ? %%
So life is filled with nothing but work? But if it is sometimes a pleasure, it is more often a suffering. But then?

%% 103.0485 %%
%% [#Mortality](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MORTALITY.md) %%
%% Rien ? %%
%% On verra, si je ne meure pas. %%
Nothing?
We shall see — if I do not die.
