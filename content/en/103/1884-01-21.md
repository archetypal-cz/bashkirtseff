# Monday, 21 January 1884
Mme Sapogenikoff is passing through Paris on her return from Nice, and I find the good Nina of the Nice days again when I get her to say to the architect: "My regards to M. Jules." This good brother came this evening because I had sent him a small Russian towel with Jules's head embroidered in the centre in red cotton, with these words above in blue cotton: HIS HEAD. I know that head by heart — I stitched it once on the sewing machine, and this time embroidered it on canvas. With Nina and the Orthodox priest gone, I batter him again with: "no, but admit he has a vile character — and ugly with it!" — "An ignoble disposition!" — "A cretin, for he does not appreciate me!

%% 103.0075 %%
%% 2026-02-02T13:30:00 LAN: "au point de vue auvergnat" - from an Auvergnat perspective - implies shrewd, practical assessment (stereotype of people from Auvergne) %%
%% Comprenez-vous c'est au point de vue auvergnat que je parle d'apprécier, eh bien ceux qui ne m'apprécient pas je les méprise, car ils sont idiots." %%
You understand — it is from the Auvergnat point of view[^3] that I speak of appreciating; well, those who do not appreciate me I despise, for they are idiots."

[^3]: Auvergnats were stereotyped as shrewdly practical and hard-nosed in their judgments.

%% — S'il ne vous apprécie pas assez, c'est que vous n'êtes pas naturelle quand on ne vous connaît pas ! %%
— If he does not appreciate you sufficiently, it is because you are not natural with people who do not know you!

— Why doesn't he know me then? He is a sluggish, whining creature!
— You saw him when he was ill.

%% 103.0078 %%
%% — Ça ne fait rien. %%
— No matter.

%% 103.0079 %%
%% Mais moi je vous raconte ces joutes oratoires comme si le vrai Jules devait en prendre connaissance tandis que son architecte de frère ne lui dit rien, oh ! mais il dira... %%
But I am telling you all about these verbal jousts as if the real Jules were to be informed of them — whereas his architect of a brother tells him nothing; oh! but he will...

%% 103.0080 %%
%% — Enfin voyons, voilà Madame (Nina) qui est une femme intelligente et elle m'apprécie, voilà Monsieur (le pope) qui est également intelligent, il m'apprécie; vous enfin que vous soyez ou non, intelligent vous m'avez appréciée tout de suite que j'aie été naturelle ou non. C'est donc qu'il est pire qu'un vieux tube de couleur vide. %%
— Come now — here is Madame (Nina), who is an intelligent woman and appreciates me; here is Monsieur (the Orthodox priest), who is equally intelligent, and he appreciates me; and you, whether intelligent or not, appreciated me at once, whether I was natural or not. Therefore he is worse than an old empty paint tube.

%% 103.0081 %%
%% — Mais moi j'ai été éblouie tout de suite... %%
— But I was dazzled at once...

%% 103.0082 %%
%% — Oui, tournez-le en plaisanterie, je vous le conseille, ça n'en est pas moins horrible. %%
— Yes, turn it into a joke, I advise you to — it is no less horrible for that.

%% 103.0083 %%
%% Quelle salle nature. %%
What a vile disposition.
