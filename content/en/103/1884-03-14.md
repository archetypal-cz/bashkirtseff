# Friday, 14 March 1884
It will not be finished — I shall therefore send only *Un Meeting*.

%% 103.0488 %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: "réunion intime" - intimate gathering, small salon %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: "la duchesse d'Uzès" - Anne de Rochechouart de Mortemart, first woman to obtain driver's license %%
%% Ce soir réunion intime chez Mme Hochon, beaucoup d'artistes et quelques dames comme la duchesse d'Uzès, la comtesse Cornet, la maréchale. Nous. %%
This evening an intimate gathering at Mme Hochon's — many artists, and several ladies: the Duchesse d'Uzès,[^1] the Comtesse Cornet, the maréchale. Us.

[^1]: Anne, Duchesse d'Uzès (1847–1933): one of the most prominent aristocratic women of the Belle Époque, celebrated for her sculpture, her hunting, and her political activism; later the first woman in France to receive a driver's licence (1898).

%% 103.0489 %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: "Cabanel" - Alexandre Cabanel, leading academic painter, professor at Ecole des Beaux-Arts %%
%% En fait de peintres Cabanel, Jalabert, Hébert, G. Ferrier, Boulanger etc. %%
As for painters: Cabanel,[^2] Jalabert, Hébert, G. Ferrier, Boulanger, etc.

[^2]: Alexandre Cabanel (1823–1889): leading academic painter and professor at the École des Beaux-Arts, famous for his *Birth of Venus* (1863); a dominant figure in official French art of the period.

%% 103.0490 %%
%% [#Music_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MUSIC_THEME.md) %%
%% On fait de la musique et Salvayre joue et chante des morceaux de son Henri III. %%
Music is performed, and Salvayre plays and sings pieces from his *Henri III*.[^3]

[^3]: Gaston Salvayre (1847–1916): French composer; his opera *Henri III* was premièred at the Paris Opéra in 1883.

%% 103.0491 %%
%% Tous ces gens ont été aimables pour moi; Cabanel a été charmant. %%
All these people were kind to me; Cabanel was charming.

%% 103.0492 %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: "à la Directoire" - in Directory style, late 18th-century revival fashion with high waist and crossed bodice %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: Marie's detailed outfit notes, characteristic of her diary %%
%% Je sors si peu et si rarement que cela m'étonne; ma robe a eu beaucoup de succès. Jupe drapée en mousseline de soie noire très souple, corsage croisé à la Directoire, et une large ceinture de satin noir. %%
I go out so little and so rarely that it astonishes me; my dress was very much admired. A draped skirt in very supple black silk muslin, a *Directoire*-style[^4] crossed bodice, and a wide sash of black satin.

[^4]: *Directoire* style: a revival fashion recalling the dress of the French Directory period (1795–99), characterised by high waists, crossed or draped bodices, and a deliberately neo-classical severity.
