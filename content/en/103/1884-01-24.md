# Thursday, 24 January 1884
Young Randouin sits for his portrait. We take him home to his mother, who receives on Thursdays, then to Doucet's, etc...

Randouin is a member of the Mirlitons,[^1] as is Jide, the husband of Mme Randouin's mother. If only I could make a success of this portrait.

[^1]: Les Mirlitons: a Parisian artistic and literary club.
