# Wednesday, 6 February 1884
[Crossed out: The painting again. Then sculpture by gaslight]
Gaillard to dinner.

%% 103.0274 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: "Julian" - Rodolphe Julian, founder of Academie Julian where Marie studied %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: "ce marseillais" - this Marseillais, mildly dismissive reference to Julian's southern origin %%
%% J'ai été chez Julian lui montrer le portrait Randouin; ce marseillais a l'air très content et me dit que ça va de mieux en mieux. %%
I went to Julian's to show him the Randouin portrait; this Marseillais looks very pleased and tells me it is getting better and better.

%% 103.0275 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% Ce n'est pas mon opinion pour le portrait, je le déteste mais si les autres pensent comme Julian... Eh bien je le referai tout de même, je tâcherai qu'il me plaise, encore une séance. %%
That is not my opinion of the portrait — I detest it; but if others think as Julian does... Well, I shall redo it all the same; I shall try to make it please me — one more session.

%% 103.0276 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: "Ce Juif de Julian" - antisemitic epithet, common in 1880s discourse; Julian was not actually Jewish %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: "santé en peinture" - painting health/condition, metaphor for artistic progress %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: "dégringolée" - tumbled down/declined %%
%% Ce Juif de Julian m'agace. Il s'obstine à me parler ainsi: votre santé en peinture va de mieux en mieux; il veut que j'aie été très forte, puis dégringolée, puis encore forte; c'est faux, faux, faux ! Mes études sont là, vérifiez ! %%
That Jew Julian[^1] irritates me. He persists in addressing me thus: your painting health goes from better to better; he wants me to have been very strong, then to have fallen off, then to be strong again — it is false, false, false! My studies are there — verify it!

[^1]: Marie's use of this antisemitic epithet, common in 1880s Parisian discourse, is recorded as written. Rodolphe Julian (1839–1907) was not in fact Jewish.

In any case...

I have dinner brought up to the studio. The tranquillity is ravishing.
