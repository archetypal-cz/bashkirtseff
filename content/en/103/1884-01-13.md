# Sunday, 13 January 1884
I worked a little, though it is our New Year,[^1] and then the Karageorgevitches and the priest[^2] for dinner; there were callers during the day.

[^1]: The Russian Orthodox New Year, celebrated thirteen days after the Western New Year according to the Julian calendar.
[^2]: *Pope*: a Russian Orthodox priest. Not a Catholic pope.

Bojidar and I are outraged by the horrible, flat satirical verses about Bastien — it was Irma who taught them to me, and she attributes them to Gervex, but I say that Gervex has too much talent to commit verses like those... especially the couplet about the old rat... Fie! It reeks of the tavern,[^3] it reeks of wine, it reeks, in short, of the failed art-school dauber[^4] — it is contemptible.

[^3]: *Estaminet*: a cheap bar or tavern, particularly associated with the working-class north of France and Belgium.
[^4]: *Rapin*: a derogatory term for a mediocre art student or would-be painter.
