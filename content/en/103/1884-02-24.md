# Sunday, 24 February 1884
The Claire contingent and the Villevieille contingent and the Kesslers and the Bonapartes pestered me so thoroughly with their admirations and observations about Dina's portrait that I wipe out the head — it was ruined; one had to destroy.

I went to church, then to the Gavinis', then for a moment to the Bois.

%% [#Mortality](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MORTALITY.md) %%
