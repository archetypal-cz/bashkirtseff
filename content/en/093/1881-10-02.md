If you imagine that traveling with my mothers is a pleasure, you are quite mistaken. Besides, it is entirely natural — they have neither my youth nor my curiosities… In short, since it is past, I shall speak no more of their innocent small vexations — especially since they are so fussy that I shall have a thousand occasions to return to the subject. Martyred airs, and then absurd questions, pretending to believe themselves in some wilderness, a country one never goes to. And the guide saying it is cold in Burgos — a true desolation; one should have brought the pelisses, what is to be done, what will become of us. What a country! And see what there? The cathedral — but only the English go… And the worst of it is that all this was addressed to me in the third person, or nothing was said at all, but while speaking of something else — airs! And if I protest, they say nothing was said and that I am looking for pretexts to quarrel.
They act as if they were forced into the journey that makes them miserable — and nothing is more difficult than fighting against such airs; one resigns oneself, says nothing — what do I ask for, in the end? And yet I did not insist — it was they who proposed coming to Spain; I insist on nothing anymore. In short, we are not happy together, and for as long as we are together it will be a thoroughly successful little bourgeois hell.
So — Burgos… Ah! they are insupportable; when it is not the doleful resignation and complaints in the third person, it is so complete an indifference that one is quite taken aback. Imagine someone pulling on a rope attached to a great weight — one gets out of breath, one drags this ball and chain with every difficulty in the world, and crack! One feels nothing more; the rope is left in one's hands. Perhaps it is absurd, but I am always very much influenced by my surroundings, and the most beautiful things in the world, seen in such company… God knows what one would do, what one would say, what ideas one would have in a different environment — above all for an artist, it is very painful, I assure you. Everything depends on!… I do not know what — a nothing, and the wings fall and one is left a fool. Where am I going? What does Burgos matter to me — Madrid, the museum? What is the use?

One talks well with… well, I talk well with Julian, who is not a fool — but there is always a touch of exaggeration so that enthusiasm, for instance, has a mocking side that shields it from ridicule, however slight, or simply if one is not understood… you understand. But to receive a deep impression and express it simply, seriously, as one felt it… I cannot imagine being able to do that with anyone except someone I loved completely…
And if I could do it with a stranger, it would immediately create an invisible bond — very embarrassing afterward; one seems to have committed a wrong action together. Or else it is the Parisian-style exchange of ideas — affecting a slightly detached viewpoint, or the craftsman's perspective, so as not to appear too poetic — and speaking of the artistic side with words that make of it something exquisite but also something of the boulevardier: *saveur*, *finesse*… And then: it is very strong, it is quite simply the most astonishing thing one can see [words blacked out: and there is] something there truly unprecedented.
