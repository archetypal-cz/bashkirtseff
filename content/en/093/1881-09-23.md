# Friday, 23 September 1881

The Russians of yesterday have managed to strike up a conversation. Naturally I am praised to the skies; the *others* looked at these two unfortunates together and did not fail to laugh a little.

Marie is reviewing her earlier diary pages and interpreting them to her future reader — a characteristic fourth-wall moment. %%

I cannot go to Italy as I wish to — recommence the troubles of 1876? Ah no — once, to learn, yes; but now, no. I could go there well married or celebrated; without that, no. It is to go toward endless torments. To think that I had added still more to my real troubles… that Antonelli who never pleased me for an hour and about whom they made a novel, a story, almost a scandal… It is not because it is the past — but see, that horror of Larderel: well, there at least there was something, a violent and real infatuation — whereas… And if I appeared to, if I made that indifferent man believe I loved him, it was Maman who would say: it commits you to nothing, and you'll attract him; perhaps we can make something of him, don't rush things, even if you were to tell him you love him… And hearing this — such frivolous advice… I would tell myself that a prudent woman and my mother would not give me such advice if it were not truly useful; when one hears foolishness from people one believes sensible, those foolishnesses seem to be the fruits of experience… I cannot explain it to myself… One says to oneself: since someone authorizes a liberty I can well take it. I swear to you I am not inventing excuses — and besides I have the habit of assuming responsibility for my own acts… But there! My mother is a child and a provincial… and has never — besides, if I told her this she would cry out against the abomination; she never advised anything..
