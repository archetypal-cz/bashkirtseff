# Tuesday, 27 September 1881

%% 2026-09-28T21:01:18 RSR: Re-set from tome14.docx ¶2632, Mon Journal t.14 p.213: this text sat under «Lundi 26 septembre 1881» in the old extraction but belongs to the date line «Mardi 27 septembre 1881» (¶2629) (2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Yesterday to Bayonne *en famille*, today to Fontarabie[^1], and the family too; I never go out without the Karaouleffs. I wanted to go on horseback, but the bodice of my riding habit fits so badly, and besides it would be irritating to ride along with this Karaouleff, whom I do not know and who is a bore. The wife is very beautiful but not of the best sort, and then they do not seem to look after the child enough, and then those people get on my nerves at times... Fontarabie is charming; besides, Biarritz is so common, so clumsy in its very banal beauty, that one is glad to get out of it. And straight away, there on the little harbour, beggar children who would easily make a picture. Only I want to see Spain first, and if I find nothing better there I shall come back by way of Fontarabie... I gambled, there is a roulette table, but having lost forty francs I made sketches instead. It is a little out-of-the-way spot; I hope nobody saw me gambling, anyway...

[^1]: Fontarabie: Fuenterrabía (now Hondarribia), a Spanish town on the border with France at the mouth of the Bidasoa river.

It is partly my fault — I always reproach them for not knowing anyone, and I say humiliating things to push them to do something… But it only gives them that pitiful attitude… And when the occasion arises they get on their high horses and speak loudly to be heard by the other Russians, always to boast and dwell on a heap of imaginary grandeurs — if it were done with skill I would say nothing… But it is childish… I always complain about my family… but I love them, and I am fair. And do I even love them? If they were strangers to me, such as they are, would I love them? No, probably not.

Besides the grey, the white dress must be mentioned, which had equal success: a Louis XV dress in muslin embroidered with little flowers, white on white — pointed bodice like the grey one, cut square; a high chemisette with a frill, closely circled by a black velvet ribbon. Hat lined with black velvet, with white feathers. The skirt entirely in lace flounces at the front, since the overskirt, obviously, gathered and sewn to the bodice, opens to be looped up into panniers, though only at two points very far back. All the fashionable people ask Rosalie for the dressmaker's address — so glory to Doucet.[^2] I had gone to this expense out of coquetry for Soria; we have talked twice on the beach and I shall paint his portrait. Soria is the famous singer, an amateur and a wine merchant (in Bordeaux) in France, who has himself paid abroad. He sings like an angel… an angel with a biblical head of great beauty — black beard, splendid eyes — in short, the ideal of biblical beauty. An olive complexion and no hair, nothing but a crown. He is tall; in short, he would make a character portrait that would be stunning… We shall probably meet again in Madrid…

[^2]: Doucet: the House of Doucet, one of the great Paris couturiers of the late 19th century.
