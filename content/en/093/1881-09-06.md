# Tuesday, 6 September 1881

Étienne is here; he has come from Nice where he has bought a villa and where he lives with that lady. My aunt and he go to the theatre, and I remain alone — disturbed in that solitude which I am quite compelled to find delightful — by the visit of the Tchoumakoffs. We talk all evening and I manage well enough; they were perhaps raising their voices.
