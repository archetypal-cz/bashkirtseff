# Tuesday, 13 September 1881

%% Je viens de calculer ce que ces ecritures feraient imprimees ! Plus de trois mille pages, c'est-a-dire sept volumes ordinaires 11! %%
I have just calculated what these writings would amount to if printed! More than three thousand pages — that is to say seven ordinary volumes!! 11!
