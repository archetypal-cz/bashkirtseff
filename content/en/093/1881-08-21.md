# Sunday, 21 August 1881

%% Elections. Je suis inquiete de Gambetta. Il serait malheureux qu'il fut mal ou pas elu. Cet homme-la habituera une quantite de gens a la Republique, les intransigeants effrayent. %%
Elections. I am worried about Gambetta. It would be a pity if he were elected poorly or not at all. That man will reconcile a great many people to the Republic — the *intransigeants* are frightening.[^1]

[^1]: *Les intransigeants*: the uncompromising radical republicans of the 1880s, who refused any accommodation with monarchists or moderates.
