%% # Dimanche 6 novembre 1881 %%
# Sunday, 6 November 1881

%% [Ajoute : C'est-a-dire que l'enquete a montre qu'il n'y a pas motif a proces.]
Le proces est fini, gagne. Ca parait impossible tellement il y a longtemps que ca dure et pourtant cela est. Nous venons de recevoir la depeche de maman pendant que Mme de Brimont etait la, vers dix heures du soir. Voila un jour heureux.
Nous avons vu les Gavini le matin puis Saint Amand, Bojidar etc. Demain je vais a la Chambre avec Adeline qui est ravie de me voir et qui me charge de secouer les tristesses des peres Gavini et Gery. Preparee par la conversation avec Julian et avec la nouvelle de ce soir je pense a l'air triomphant que j'aurai demain en robe Louis XV, [Mots noircis: feutre qui a une forme un peu] Henri III mais le tout bien parisien avec un bouquet de roses du Bengale tout en haut du corsage. J'aurais prefere les violettes mais elles contiennent des allusions. Grande seance. Affaires tunisiennes, chute du ministere, discours de Gambetta peut-etre. Plusieurs fois je me suis prise a sourire toute seule en pensant a demain, a la seance, a la toilette grise et au bouquet de roses et a mon air heureux. %%
[Added: That is to say, the inquiry showed that there was no grounds for a lawsuit.]
The lawsuit is over, won. It seems impossible — it has dragged on so long — and yet there it is. We have just received Maman's telegram while Mme de Brimont was here, around ten o'clock in the evening. What a happy day.
We saw the Gavinis in the morning, then Saint-Amand, Bojidar, etc. Tomorrow I go to the Chamber[^1] with Adeline, who is delighted to see me and charges me with lifting the spirits of the elder Gavini and Géry. Prepared by the conversation with Julian and by this evening's news, I think of the triumphant air I shall have tomorrow in my Louis XV dress — [blacked out: a felt hat of a somewhat] Henri III shape, but the whole thing perfectly Parisian, with a bouquet of China roses at the very top of the bodice. I would have preferred violets — but they carry symbolic implications.[^2] A great session. The Tunisian question, the fall of the cabinet, Gambetta's speech perhaps. Several times I caught myself smiling to myself, thinking of tomorrow — the session, the gray dress, the bouquet of roses, and my happy air.

[^1]: The Chamber of Deputies: the lower house of the French parliament, housed in the Palais-Bourbon on the Quai d'Orsay.
[^2]: Violets carried a well-known symbolism of hidden love in the period's flower language; Marie does not want to signal what she feels.
