# Friday, 12 October 1883
Miss Webb writes to me from Concarneau, in reply to my question about the weather, that it is impossible to paint *en plein air* there at present. And so in winter, all in all, I believe my Concarneau is washed out. That settles a good many things. We went to lunch at Jouy, and around five o'clock we find Adeline at home; we talk until six, and then nothing.
