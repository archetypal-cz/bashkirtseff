# Monday, 10 September 1883
Madame Hochon dines with her two children. Through her we shall obtain a subscription to the Italiens — and she is also a figure of artistic influence: daughter of M. Lefurel, architect of the Louvre, she knows all the artists, the great, the learned, and the powerful.
