%% 2026-03-10T12:00:00 TR: "Le sexe fort" / "faibles femmes" — ironic; preserved the sarcasm as it reads naturally in English. %%
The strong sex goes its own way, and we feeble women go to the château. I retain today only the Napoleonic battles, the splendours of this greatest of men, and his marble statue at Saint Helena. Ungrateful people, infamous people, blind people!

But that would carry me too far!
