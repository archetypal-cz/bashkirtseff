Claire, the boys, and I go to Versailles.

%% 2026-03-10T12:00:00 TR: "Le sexe fort" / "faibles femmes" — ironic; preserved the sarcasm as it reads naturally in English. %%
The stronger sex goes off to amuse itself, and we weak women go to the château. All I take away from it today is the Napoleonic battles, the splendours of this greatest of men, and his marble statue at Saint Helena. Ungrateful people, infamous people, blind people!

%% 101.0300 %%
%% Mais ça m'entrainerait trop loin ! %%
But that would carry me too far!

%% 101.0301 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%

%% On a acheté un jeu de croquet et le soir nous faisons de la musique amusante avec un doigt, n'importe comment, tous les airs, tous les opéras, tous les souvenirs. %%
We have bought a croquet set, and in the evening we make amusing music with one finger, in no particular fashion — all the tunes, all the operas, all the memories.

%% 101.0302 %%
%% Il y a des moments ou sans qu'on sache pourquoi tout paraît rose, on rirait au nez d'une mauvaise nouvelle et l'on croit que tout va réussir. %%
%% 2026-09-29T06:53:05 RSR: Restored from tome16.docx ¶353, Mon Journal t.16 p.28 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
There are moments when, without one's knowing why, everything looks rosy; one would laugh in the face of bad news, and one believes that everything is going to succeed.

%% 101.0303 %%
%% Heureuse nature va ! Faut-il qu'on te tracasse pour que tu crie ! L'absence de calamités serait le bonheur avec mon imagination. %%
%% 2026-09-29T06:53:05 RSR: Restored from tome16.docx ¶354, Mon Journal t.16 pp.28–29 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Oh, happy nature! Must you be pestered before you will cry out! With my imagination, the absence of calamities would be happiness.
