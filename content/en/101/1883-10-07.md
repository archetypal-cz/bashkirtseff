%% J'ai vu la nouvelle lune... de l'œil gauche et j'en suis attristée. %%
# Sunday, 7 October 1883
I have seen the new moon… with my left eye, and I am saddened by it.

%% 101.0522 %%
%% [#Superstition](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/SUPERSTITION.md) %%

"Really, Mademoiselle — how dare you confess to such flat superstitions!" Why flat? Napoleon and Caesar were superstitious — to cite only those two most illustrious examples.

This evening, after careful reflection, I declare flatly that he will never be in love with me. Let us set that preoccupation aside. Never. I am at peace. But what a pity, my God!

I am trying to civilise Bojidar, who is entirely untamed. He knows nothing of the real world, cheapens himself God knows where — among false princesses and dubious foreign women — lays down the law, argues, attacks the most well-known figures, and commits a thousand naïve blunders. If at least he frequented second-rate drawing rooms because one can amuse oneself there, that would not be so bad; but I suspect he is a little… Saint-Amand — he was raised like a girl; he is economical, tight-fisted.

Thanks to this quasi-marriage, the possibility of loving freely and, above all, of not falling in his eyes. Women deceive their husbands and are not fallen in the eyes of the world… I know that, but I could never deceive a real husband, never. And I cannot marry for love. What is left? Saint-Amand, or Bojidar if… But Bojidar would be tiresome and loud and wilful… But there is one great advantage: nobody knows it…
