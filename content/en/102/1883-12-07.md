# Friday, 7 December 1883
Dina is often ill — migraines. She cannot pose. I am wasting my time. I am doing nothing. For a month now. Nothing! It puts me in a rage that makes me want to scratch!

I am making sketches for Julius... Caesar. It does not grip me, but one can find interest in it, put something amusing, something of one's own into it — and in the end *one must* do it.

I reopen the diary to say something about Bastien-Lepage — anything at all.
