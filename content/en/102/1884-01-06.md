# Sunday, 6 January 1884

It is Christmas for the Russians;[^1] we go to mass and I am pretty.

%% 102.0121 %%
%% Quelques personnes dans l'après-midi. %%

A few visitors in the afternoon.

[^1]: Russian Orthodox Christmas falls on 6 January (25 December in the Julian calendar, which ran 13 days behind the Gregorian).
