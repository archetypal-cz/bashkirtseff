# Friday, 28 December 1883

We return the call to Mme Berteaux, then I come home to find Claire and Villevieille painting.

My statue drives me mad — at moments it seems to me deranged, then superb, then hideous...

In the evening, more of yesterday's sort — the Engelhardts. They disgust me after the newspaper affair.
