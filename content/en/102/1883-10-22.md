# Monday, 22 October 1883
I have four or five years left to live. It vexes me, because when I am dead there will be people who will say — especially that Cassagnac, who already believes I am not marrying because he left a deep impression on me. And Mme Presseq!... That Cassagnac about whom I care as much as about last year's gloves. If it were true, if I were "*struck* by love," and if I were dying of it, I would let him know — but since it is not so, and people may think it is, that is infuriating.
