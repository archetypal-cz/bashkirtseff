# Monday, 19 November 1883
%% 2026-10-03T09:08:46 ED: re-cut as printed (split wave 2): 102.0265…102.0275 now hold 11 print paragraphs (verified on the scan); the existing translation was redistributed over them unchanged — check the cut points. %%
Father Tchoumakoff brings us his daughter — that great Léonie who announced her marriage to me with such joy barely a year ago. The husband: a Greek-Turk, a dismissed embassy attaché, twenty-four years old, named Loghadès. He takes her to Constantinople, where instead of a palace she finds herself in an oriental hovel.

Sleeping in a loft — a kind of cupboard that served as a bedroom — no linen; and eating olives, cucumbers, and cold water... She did not write to her mother out of pride — but when her mother came for the birth of the child...

%% 2026-10-03T09:08:46 RSR: Re-cut (paragraph wave 2): print paragraph 5 of 11 of the re-cut run 102.0265…102.0275 (Mon Journal t.16; cut points verified on the scan indentation / print layout). %%
This great turkey,[^1] who has a marvellous profile when she is silent, pours out all her miseries to me with a very calm air, and platitudes about life... As though reciting. But what is charming is that she abandoned her child — a little girl only a fortnight old. Warm-hearted, I suppose. In any case... But *that does not exist* for me — and I add: unfortunately.

[^1]: *Grande dinde*: lit. "big turkey" — a term for a stupid, vain woman.
