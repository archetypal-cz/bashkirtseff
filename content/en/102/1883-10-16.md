# Tuesday, 16 October 1883
I have just re-read, by chance, a few pages of my life in 1880. And I find myself far happier now. It is quite remarkable — in comparison, and even without comparison, I have not a care in the world; I am at peace. And yet back then! I wept every day, I fretted... There was reason enough...
