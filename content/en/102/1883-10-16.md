All psychic changes are bound up with physical movements and call into play a certain number of nerve centres.

%% 102.0003 %%
%% La psychologie moderne reconnaît que nous sommes une société de cellules... Tout acte de *conscience* implique un concert de cellules qui exige un certain temps pour se produire. %%
%% 2026-10-02T18:53:40 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 3 of 8 of 102.0001, which held 8 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.16; tome16.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
Modern psychology recognises that we are a society of cells... Every act of *consciousness* implies a concert of cells which requires a certain time to take place.

%% 102.0004 %%
%% La vie *inconsciente.* %%
%% 2026-10-02T18:53:40 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 4 of 8 of 102.0001, which held 8 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.16; tome16.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
The *unconscious* life.

%% 102.0005 %%
%% Sphère du *moi* au moment ou elle semble vide de sentiments de pensées d'actes, projetés rayon d'attention et vous verrez des atomes comme dans un rayon de soleil. Les bruits montrent les perceptions sans perception . %%
%% 2026-10-02T18:53:40 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 5 of 8 of 102.0001, which held 8 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.16; tome16.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
Sphere of the *self* at the moment when it seems empty of feelings of thoughts of acts, projected ray of attention and you will see atoms as in a sunbeam. Noises show perceptions without perception.

%% 102.0006 %%
%% Hartmann attribue mille vertus à *l'inconscient,* instinct profond, pensée infaillible d'autant plus qu'elle s'ignore. %%
%% 2026-10-02T18:53:40 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 6 of 8 of 102.0001, which held 8 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.16; tome16.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
Hartmann attributes a thousand virtues to *the unconscious*, deep instinct, a thought all the more infallible for being unaware of itself.

%% 102.0007 %%
%% L'être ne fait rien plus sagement que quand il ne sait ce qu'il fait. "Sentir toujours la même chose revient à ne pas sentir." Fouillez, c'est immense. Oui pourqu'on ne perçoit pas de choc nouveau... C'est Hobbes. %%
%% 2026-10-02T18:53:40 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 7 of 8 of 102.0001, which held 8 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.16; tome16.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
A being never acts more wisely than when it does not know what it is doing. "Always to feel the same thing amounts to not feeling." Dig into it, it is immense. Yes so that one perceives no new shock... That is Hobbes.

%% 102.0008 %%
%% Tout homme comme moi *connaît* les principes philosophiques, *d'instinct,* il les trouve seul *naturellement.* %%
%% 2026-10-02T18:53:40 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 8 of 8 of 102.0001, which held 8 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.16; tome16.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
Every man like me *knows* philosophical principles *by instinct*; he finds them out alone, *naturally*.]

%% 2026-10-02T16:09:56 ED: RESOLVED (RED 2026-10-02, seam checked: reads as one paragraph, nothing lost or duplicated) — paragraphs merged at a page break (102.DROPPED-0003 joined to 102.0009; one paragraph in the print, p59->p60 indent=3 above=0). %%
I have just re-read, by chance, a few pages of my life in 1880. And I find myself far happier now. It is quite remarkable — in comparison, and even without comparison, I have not a care in the world; I am at peace. And yet back then! I wept every day, I fretted... There was reason enough... Now I see it all from higher up, from very high indeed; our situation is better; I no longer trouble myself to talk with my family — I extract from them what I can and let the little lies pass; these people know they no longer deceive me. Oh! yes, I am well now, and I give thanks to God for it.
