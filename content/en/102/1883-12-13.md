# Thursday, 13 December 1883
All day, Bojidar poses.

%% 102.0472 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% [#Bojidar](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/BOJIDAR.md) %%
%% Assis devant un paravent japonais rose, suçant sa canne et riant à peine de trois quarts. En habit et cravate blanche. Eclairé au gaz, je le place dans un coin noir éclairé au gaz et moi avec la lumière du jour, c'est très commode mais très difficile. %%
%% 2026-10-02T18:53:40 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 2 of 8 of 102.0471, which held 8 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.16; tome16.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
Seated before a pink Japanese screen, sucking his cane and barely laughing, in three-quarter view. In evening dress and white cravat. By gaslight — I place him in a dark corner lit by gas, while I have daylight on me; it is very convenient but very difficult.

%% 2026-10-02T18:53:40 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 4 of 8 of 102.0471, which held 8 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.16; tome16.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
All these heads are life-size, the shoulders visible; in Bojidar's case there is a gloved hand holding the cane. It is rather amusing, and besides I enjoy painting a head a day... it broadens one's handling...

%% 102.0475 %%

%% Mais j'ai souvent la fièvre, c'est la poitrine qui en est cause... Je sens bien que ça avance petit à petit... On ne peut pas guérir ces maladies-là... Voici le régime à suivre. Vie végétative, aucune préoccupation; manger et dormir beaucoup. Et d'autres saletés. Des choses impossibles ! %%
%% 2026-10-02T18:53:40 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 5 of 8 of 102.0471, which held 8 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.16; tome16.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
But I frequently have a fever — it is the chest that is the cause... I feel clearly that it is advancing little by little... These illnesses cannot be cured... Here is the regimen to follow: vegetative life, no cares; eat and sleep a great deal. And other such rubbish. Impossible things!

%% 102.0476 %%
%% [#Mortality](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MORTALITY.md) %%
%% Je fais tout ce que je peux et ce n'est pas assez... Pourvu que ça dure une dizaine d'années seulement... C'est bien peu dix ans. %%
%% 2026-10-02T18:53:40 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 6 of 8 of 102.0471, which held 8 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.16; tome16.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
I do everything I can and it is not enough... If only it lasts just ten years or so... Ten years is so little.

%% 102.0477 %%
%% [#Health](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/HEALTH.md) %%
%% Enfin me voilà poitrinaire... Ah ! Si ça pouvait n'être que dans mon imagination ! %%

Well then — here I am consumptive... Ah! If only it could be merely in my imagination!
