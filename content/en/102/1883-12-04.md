It did not go as I wished — the whole day; I continued by gaslight and could not get free of it...

[words blacked out: That] gives me an air as concentrated and as disagreeable as Bastien-Lepage — and I am flattered by it, just as formerly I was flattered to wear a skirt askew like Breslau. One is Up There... It does not make one popular... And although this manner of being reminds me of the great painter and makes me believe that we have the same grandiose preoccupations... I am not always like that. But there are old Tchoumakoff and his wife, the Engelhardts, Dusautoy, Saint-Amand. Tchoumakoff has given Maman a pastel — for perhaps I have not said it — he is celebrated in Russia and sells very well in France: heads of pretty women, always.

In any case — if Bastien-Lepage marries Mackay, it will be all the same to me at bottom and on the surface too. But I shall have to find another man of genius — an exceptional nature... concentrated, detached, and disagreeable. It seems to me that the amiability of concentrated people... must be more precious than that of others, for either it is natural, in which case they love you, or it is forced, in which case it is a great effort they are making. As for me, I endeavour to be benevolent... as far as my genius permits... That word amuses me — it is immense, and I am so little that I am permitted to play with it.
