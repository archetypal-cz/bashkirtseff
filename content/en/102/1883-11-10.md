I should like to attribute a slight fever — brought on by yesterday's wind on the Seine — to moral causes...

%% 2026-10-02T18:53:40 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 3 of 8 of 102.0217, which held 8 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.16; tome16.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
My poor child, everything drives you to the feet of Art; do not mistake these various signs, go to it. Glory alone gives what you desire, and they say you can attain it.

%% 102.0220 %%
%% N'éparpille pas tes esprits, ne te dépense pas en Jules chimériques que tu n'aimes pas le moins du monde. %%
%% 2026-10-02T18:53:40 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 4 of 8 of 102.0217, which held 8 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.16; tome16.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
Do not scatter your energies, do not spend yourself on fantastical Juleses whom you do not love in the slightest.

%% 102.0221 %%
%% Depuis ton malheur tu ne peux trouver grâce qu'en devenant illustre. Eh bien au lieu de... Mais est-on maître de ses pensées ? Il faut l'être... à peu près. %%
%% 2026-10-02T18:53:40 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 5 of 8 of 102.0217, which held 8 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.16; tome16.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
Since your misfortune, you can find grace only by becoming illustrious. Well then, instead of... But is one master of one's thoughts? One must be... more or less.
