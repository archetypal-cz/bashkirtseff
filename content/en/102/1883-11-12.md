# Saturday, 12 November 1883
Drumont of *La Liberté*[^1] comes to make notes on the Grand Dukes and the prominent Russians in Paris.

%% 102.0229 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% Il déteste le genre que je fais mais me fait de grands %%
%% compliments tout en me demandant avec stupeur comment il se peut que moi, entourée d'élégances et de raffinements j'aime le laid ! Il trouve mes gamins laids, "pourquoi ne les avez-vous pas choisis jolis ?." "Ce serait aussi bien." %%
%% 2026-10-02T18:53:40 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 2 of 6 of 102.0228, which held 6 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.16; tome16.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
He dislikes the type of work I do, but pays me great compliments while asking in bewilderment how it is possible that I — surrounded by elegance and refinement — could love ugliness! He finds my urchins ugly: "Why did you not choose pretty ones? It would have been just as good."

%% 102.0230 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% Je les ai choisis expressifs "si j'ose m'exprimer ainsi, du reste dans les gamins qui courent les rues on ne voit pas tant que ça d'astres de beauté, il faudrait pour cela aller aux Champs Elysées et peindre les pauvres petits bébés enrubannés et flanqués de gouvernantes." %%
%% 2026-10-02T18:53:40 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 3 of 6 of 102.0228, which held 6 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.16; tome16.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
I chose them for their expressiveness. "If I may put it that way — besides, among the urchins who run the streets one does not see so very many stars of beauty; for that, one would need to go to the Champs-Élysées and paint the poor little ribbon-bedecked babies flanked by their governesses."

%% 102.0231 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% Où est le mouvement, où est la liberté[^1] sauvage, primitive, où est l'expression vraie alors ? %%
%% 2026-10-02T18:53:40 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 4 of 6 of 102.0228, which held 6 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.16; tome16.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
Where is the movement, where is the wild, primitive freedom, where is the true expression in that?

%% 102.0232 %%
%% Dans les enfants bien élevés il y a déjà de la pose. %%
%% 2026-10-02T18:53:40 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 5 of 6 of 102.0228, which held 6 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.16; tome16.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
In well-brought-up children there is already affectation.

%% 102.0233 %%
%% Et puis... Enfin, j'ai raison. %%
%% 2026-10-02T18:53:40 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 6 of 6 of 102.0228, which held 6 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.16; tome16.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
And then... in any case, I am right.
