# Tuesday, 20 November 1883
Does he love me? One asks that of a book — a random page, the 3rd or 4th or 20th line to the right...[^1] And if the oracle gives a favourable answer, one believes it. Is that not foolish?

%% 2026-10-02T18:53:40 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 10 of 15 of 102.0275, which held 15 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.16; tome16.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
The family and Géry only. This young man tries to impress me by remaining seated behind me in the darkened theatre, at the back of the dark box, and as I lean forward often to see better he contrives to brush my back with his paws, his nose, or his shoulder. And as I *deliberately* passed my hand before his face he kissed it. So I told him I should pinch him if he did not behave. Breathing on the nape of my neck all the while, he even grazed the fabric of my bodice at the shoulder with his diplomatic lips. I pretended to feel nothing. We are good friends... And so that he does not read too much into it... I say in front of everyone in the carriage that this Géry was behind me the whole time and that I could not move without running into him, and that I even gave him a punch — which is true.

[^1]: Bibliomancy: the practice of seeking guidance by opening a book at random and reading the first passage encountered.
[^2]: *Aurea mediocritas*: Latin (Horace, *Odes* II.10) — "the golden mean"; here used mockingly for a man of pleasant, unremarkable mediocrity.
[^3]: *Les Invisibles*: a popular scientific entertainment in which audiences viewed microscopic creatures enlarged by a powerful projector.
[^4]: *La Vie Parisienne*: satirical illustrated magazine known for its risqué humour and depictions of Parisian social life.
