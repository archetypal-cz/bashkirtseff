# Tuesday, 22 July 1884
Bojidar and Maman went to see Jules, who is ill again. After the two days in the country and the Algerian luncheon. Serves him right.

But he showed me a delicate kindness. Naturally, he and Bojidar were talking about me, and Bojidar told him he found me tired and that I ought to go to the country.

%% 105.0278 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%

%% 2026-03-10T12:00:00 TR: "coup monté" = a setup, a conspiracy — everyone conspiring to push her to the country. Jules refrains from saying so himself precisely not to seem part of it. %%
— I wanted to tell her so too, said Jules, but I held back lest she think it a conspiracy — everyone advises her to go to the country and it is tiresome.

%% 105.0279 %%
%% [#Bastien_Lepage](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/BASTIEN_LEPAGE.md) %%
%% Si ce n'est pas la lettre c'est l'esprit. Du reste ça ne m'étonne pas, comment un artiste d'un tel génie n'aurait-il pas toutes les délicatesses. %%
If not the letter, at least the spirit. Besides, it does not surprise me — how could an artist of such genius not possess every refinement.
