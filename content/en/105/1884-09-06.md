# Saturday, 8 September 1884
I ought to be sorry that peasant Emile did not die. Do you know what he had left me when he set off for Algeria? For it seems he made his will after the Gambetta monument. He told me so because I was talking about my funeral, and because it would fall to him to carry out my last wishes.
