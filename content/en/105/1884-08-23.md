%% 105.0637 %%
%% # Samedi 23 août 1884 %%
%% J'en suis attristée ce matin, prête à pleurer mais tout ça dans le vague...! %%
# Saturday, 23 August 1884
I am saddened by it this morning, ready to weep — but all in a vague sort of way...!

But last night I received a letter from *Le Figaro*. Yes — from *Le Figaro*, from M. Perivier, asking to visit my studio with a view to putting together the *Figaro illustré*, which comes out at the end of the year, at Christmas.
