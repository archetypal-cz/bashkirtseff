Friday, 4 July 1884. The Sèvres painting is here, at the studio. One might call it *April.* It makes no difference to me, but this *April* strikes me as execrably bad!!! The background is an intense green and dirty at the same time. The woman is not at all what I wanted, not at all. I dashed it off like that, but it is not the feeling I was after, not at all. In sum, more than three months wasted.

%% 105.0029 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% Pour comble ! Pour comble il paraît que les lutteurs... %%
To crown it all! To crown it all, it turns out the wrestlers do not perform outdoors but inside the booths and chiefly in the evenings. That changes everything, for I have no wish to paint by artificial light, and I would lose the appeal of street types altogether.
