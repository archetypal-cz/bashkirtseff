# Monday, 28 July 1884
I am at Jouy. Here I am. Before my departure Bojidar had time to [Crossed out: come and] report that Emile came to see him this morning, returned with him to Jules's, and on the way Emile stopped to look in on a dining room he is designing — in a private mansion entirely ornamented with the initials K.M. and where there is a parrot; seeing which Emile said: "Here is the parrot that was at our house" (the cook had told Rosalie that, Mme Mackay having sent a parrot, they were going to send it back to her) — why? — and then he said, pointing above the mantelpiece: "A panel by my brother is to go there."
