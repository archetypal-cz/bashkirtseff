%% Choses sérieuses. %%
# Saturday, 30 August 1884
Serious things.

%% 105.0706 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% [#Le_Printemps](../../_original/_glossary/culture/art/LE_PRINTEMPS.md) %%
%% Je ne fais rien. Depuis que le tableau de Sèvres est fini, je n'ai rien fait. Rien, sauf deux misérables panneaux. %%
I do nothing. Since the Sèvres painting was finished, I have done nothing. Nothing, save two wretched little panels.

%% 105.0707 %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% Je dors des heures entières en plein jour. J'ai bien fait une petite étude en fiacre. Mais cela fait rire. %%
I sleep for hours on end in broad daylight. I did make a small study in a cab. But that is laughable.

%% 105.0708 %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% [#La_Rue](../../_original/_glossary/culture/art/LA_RUE.md) %%
%% Le tableau est installé, tout est là, il n'y a que moi qui manque. %%
The painting is set up, everything is there — only I am missing.

%% 105.0709 %%
%% Que Bastien et toutes mes imaginations dont je l'habille sont peu de chose ! %%
How little they amount to, Bastien and all the fancies I dress him up in!

%% 105.0710 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Death](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/DEATH.md) %%
%% Non, ce n'est pas une vie ! Voilà deux ans que je n'ai quitté Paris. Pas de voyage, pas de ville d'eaux, et cet hiver en deuil, si triste. %%
No, this is not a life! It is two years since I left Paris. No travel, no watering-place, and this winter in mourning — so sad.

%% 105.0711 %%
%% Et c'est par ma propre volonté que je m'enterre ! %%
And it is by my own will that I bury myself!

%% 105.0712 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% Ne pouvant aller, sauf à de rares exceptions, dans le grand monde je devrais me contenter des salons à *côté* où on s'amuse, où on danse, où on cause. Chaque fois que je suis sortie j'ai eu du succès excepté chez les horribles Fitz-James, où l'on m'a traînée sans me prévenir, sans robe... %%
%% 2026-03-10T12:00:00 TR: "salons à *côté*" = the lesser salons alongside the great world — *côté* italicised in original, suggesting second-tier society. "sans robe" = without a proper gown — dragged there in unsuitable dress. %%
Unable to go, save on rare occasions, into the *grand monde*, I ought to content myself with the salons *à côté* where one amuses oneself, dances, converses. Every time I have gone out I have been a success — except at the horrible Fitz-James's, where they dragged me without warning, without a proper gown...

%% 105.0713 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% Eh bien oui, même le salon Cartwright, n'importe quoi, mais s'amuser, se faire faire la cour, rire. Au lieu de rester des mois entiers enfermée dans ce 30 de la rue Ampère comme dans une bastille. Vivre enfin ! %%
%% 2026-03-10T12:00:00 TR: "bastille" = fortress-prison — the word resonates with the famous Paris fortress; 30 rue Ampère is their apartment. %%
Well yes — even the Cartwright salon, anything at all, but to amuse oneself, to be courted, to laugh. Instead of spending whole months shut up in this No. 30 rue Ampère as in a Bastille. To live, at last!

%% 105.0714 %%
%% Mais ici ! Le pope ! Les Engelhardt, l'architecte ! %%
But here! The Orthodox priest! The Engelhardts, the architect!

%% 105.0715 %%
%% N'est-ce pas comme dans un village. Je n'en peux plus, tous les raisonnements s'envolent! Et tout ce qu'il y a de jeune en moi se révolte ! %%
Is it not like being in a village! I can bear it no longer — all my reasoning flies away! And all that is young in me revolts!

%% 105.0716 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%

%% Si je disais tout ! Les craintes affreuses. Voici septembre, le mauvais temps n'est pas loin. Le moindre refroidissement peut me flanquer au lit pour deux mois puis la convalescnece... Et le tableau !! Ainsi j'aurais tout sacrifié et... %%
%% 2026-03-10T12:00:00 TR: "Le moinde refroidissement" = the slightest chill — "moinde" is a manuscript spelling variant of "moindre." "me flanquer au lit" = to throw me into bed — colloquial, emphatic. %%
%% 2026-10-01T12:31:13 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French (checked against current French; conditional «j'aurais tout sacrifié» now "should have"). %%
If I were to say everything! The dreadful fears. September is here; bad weather is not far off. The slightest chill can put me in bed for two months, then the convalescence... And the painting!! So I would have sacrificed everything, and...

%% 105.0717 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% [#Religion](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/RELIGION.md) %%

Ah! here is the moment to believe in God and pray to Him.

%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%

Comme je travaillerai quand même au tableau... et qu'il fera froid... Et si ce n'est pas en travaillant ce sera en me promenant, ceux qui ne font pas de peinture et qui meurent tout de même... Enfin. %%
%% 2026-03-10T12:00:00 TR: "je puis en finir en six semaines" = I can be finished off in six weeks — Marie knows her tuberculosis is advanced; any additional pulmonary infection could be fatal. "C'est ainsi que je partirai" = that is how I shall depart — "partir" used as a euphemism for dying. %%
Yes, it is the fear of falling ill; in the state I am in, any pleurisy at all can finish me off in six weeks. That, in any case, is how I shall go. Since I shall work on the painting regardless... and it will be cold... And if not from working, then from walking — people who do not paint die all the same... Well.

%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%

So here it is — the end of all my miseries! So many aspirations, so many desires, plans, so much of everything... to die at twenty-five, on the threshold of everything.

%% 105.0722 %%
%% [#Philosophy](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/PHILOSOPHY.md) %%
%% A qui la faute ? A ma famille ? Au destin ? Qu'ai-je fait? %%
Whose fault is it? My family's? Destiny's? What have I done?

%% 105.0723 %%

I had foreseen it. "God, being unable, without showing Himself partial, to give me what is necessary to my life, will make me die." Years ago. So many years, so few. And nothing.
