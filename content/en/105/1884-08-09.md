These ladies took a small ice-box to the rue Legendre. He had been wanting one that could be set near the bed.

%% 2026-03-10T12:00:00 TR: Marie's improvised continuation of the Jonah doggerel — kept in its mock-popular register. The French is in colloquial spelling (d'la, vlà, j'suis). %%
Said: Ah! how I'd like to get out!
One may have leisure and to spare,
But staying here just makes me low;
I've been here three days now or so
And start to find it hard to bear;
I'm parted from my loves, and so
I want to leave! Oh, mother dear!
Not to mention my anguish is vast,
For if I'm ever out at last,
It means this misshapen carcass here
Has driven me mad... or worse by far.
