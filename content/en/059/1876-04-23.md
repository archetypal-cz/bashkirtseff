Everything was going well — we left the hotel cheerfully; I was pleasant with Altamura, and Saint-Joseph's bouquet served as our topic of conversation; but that is my habit — to talk at great length about nothing and sometimes to develop a single word for two hours. Everything was going well, I say, until five minutes before the train's departure. I was standing on the platform, one foot on the step, dusting the carriage door with Saint-Joseph's bouquets, when... shudder!
