# Friday, 7 December 1877
The Bim-bam march, Nina... there was something Homeric about that woman — the disguises, the nocturnal excursions in men's dress, my graces... Heavens, yes, that was youth — first youth, true youth! Marie's braided froggings, Olga's grey overcoat... the evenings at the Opera, the Tower; I believe all the gaiety that Providence had allotted us was spent then...

%% Oui, c'était le bonheur, le Quartier Latin, la joie, nous étions des étudiants... qui n'étudient pas... mais qui ont eu leur grisette %%
Yes, that was happiness — the Latin Quarter, joy — we were students... who do not study... but who had their grisette[^1] —

[^1]: A *grisette* was a working-class young Parisian woman, traditionally the romantic companion of a student; the word evokes Bohemian youth and the legend of student life in the Latin Quarter.
