# Friday, 21 December 1877
After the atelier I went to the Swiss girls', and Breslau and I argued about the different theories of creation!!! The girl is afflicted with Darwinism — but since that system is proven false... no doubt false, very logical, very learned, but it is like a drawing whose starting point was badly placed. And it is so unfortunate when a man employs his logic, his science, his talent in the service of false things — the more talent one has, the better one succeeds in passing them off as scientific truths, which is truly terrible.
