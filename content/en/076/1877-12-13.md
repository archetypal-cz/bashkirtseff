Grand-papa is if not better at least no worse, and la Fayet came this morning because we had written to tell her that we would not be coming this evening — nonetheless Maman went, to please me. You understand: I want to do nothing either for or against, since if it comes to nothing one is humiliated at having made the effort. Maman now knows the prince. It was a very intimate tea; Baron d'Alt (who has come several times since Papa has been ill) and the prince escorted Madame my mother to her carriage, the latter opening the door bareheaded.
