A l'occasion de la prise de Kars (bataille de Kars, octobre 1877) il y eut un grand Te Deum à l'église. L'ambassadeur et tous les Russes de distinction se trouvaient là. %%
# Sunday, 25 November 1877
On the occasion of the fall of Kars,[^1] there was a grand Te Deum at the church. The ambassador and all the Russians of distinction were present.

[^1]: The fortress of Kars (in present-day Turkey) fell to Russian forces on 18 November 1877, a decisive event of the Russo-Turkish War.
