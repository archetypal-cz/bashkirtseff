# Friday, 14 December 1877
Pierre, who has been sent for from Nice to nurse Grand-papa, has arrived. This intelligent servant, having hoisted his trunk on his shoulders, made straight for the Trocadéro [word blacked out: the Exposition] under construction, as the place where he would be given the best information about Madame Romanoff's address, although he had it in his pocket.
%% 2026-03-08T10:00:00 TR: The Palais du Trocadéro was being constructed for the 1878 Universal Exhibition. "Mme Romanoff" is the family's social name in Paris, a variant of Bashkirtseff. %%

At the Trocadéro no one knew Madame Romanoff, and he decided to take a cab while cursing the stupidity of Parisians.
