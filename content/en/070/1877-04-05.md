> RACES IN NAPLES AT THE CAMPO DI MARTE
> 5 April 1877.
> Barring circumstances beyond our control.
> At 2 p.m. — Prize of the Municipality: 1,000 lire.
> For horses foaled and bred in the Southern Provinces, three years old and no older. — Distance: 1,700 metres. Three horses to start, or the race is cancelled. — Entrance: 100 lire, play-or-pay (run or pay). Half of the said entrance fees to go to the winner; the other half, less one entrance fee to be retained for the funds, to go to the second horse, not however to exceed half the prize. — Weight: 54 kg. Thoroughbreds to carry 3 kg extra. = Mares and geldings 2 kg less.
> 1. M.lle Angot. Chestnut mare, half-bred, by Heir-at-Law out of Beauty Bright, Gerace stud. 52 kg — Marquis F. Serra Gerace — Red and yellow jacket: white cap.
> 2. Star-of-Lavello. Chestnut mare, half-bred, by Sleight-of-hand out of Jessy, Ginistrelli stud. 52 kg — Signor Od. Ginistrelli — White and dark-blue jacket: white sleeves, dark-blue cap.
> 3. Light-Boy. Bay colt, thoroughbred, by Pirate-King out of Lady di Vergy, Ginistrelli stud. 57 kg — the same — White and dark-blue jacket: dark-blue sleeves and cap.
> 4. Sporting-Country. Bay colt, thoroughbred, by Heir-at-Law out of Biscia, Ginistrelli stud. 57 kg — the same — White and dark-blue jacket: dark-blue cap.
