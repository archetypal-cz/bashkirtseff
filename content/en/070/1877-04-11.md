# Wednesday, 11 April 1877
%% Depuis hier à quatre heures du matin je me suis renouvelée, c'est comme un changement de temps. Je m'habille en chantant très heureuse de tout et nous allons à l'Exposition des Beaux Arts inaugurée il y a quelques jours par le Roi. %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:52:24 RSR: Restored from tome10.docx ¶1885, Mon Journal t.10 p.109 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
Since four o'clock yesterday morning I have been made new; it is like a change in the weather. I sing as I dress, very happy with everything, and we go to the Fine Arts Exhibition, opened a few days ago by the King.

%% 2026-09-28T20:52:24 RSR: Restored from tome10.docx ¶1886–1887, Mon Journal t.10 pp.109–110 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
We run into Zunica, who takes us round. I am pale, and when a large painting reminds me of La Piccolellis, I grow nervous. I was ridiculous to make that sort of jealous scene! For after all, by what right? He had to respond to it and bring me back to my senses. Yes, that is it. So much the better! I have only myself to blame. He made me ashamed [word erased] repulsive. So much the better that it is my fault; I shall have nothing to reproach him with. But what a misfortune that he does not love me. He acted very foolishly; if he had not provoked me, I should have done nothing. Well, let us leave it; it is very well explained in these last sentences.

%% 2026-09-28T20:52:24 RSR: Restored from tome10.docx ¶1889, Mon Journal t.10 p.110 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
Marcuard dines with us, and then Doenhoff; there is talk of the Mirafiores and the Larderels. Marcuard is all too charitable: he blackens Larderel as much as he can, telling stories of monstrous loans and of sharp dealings with moneylenders. Everyone knows that Larderel has been placed under legal guardianship, that he receives ten thousand a month and that the rest goes to pay his debts. Marcuard claims otherwise — that he pays nothing at all, that he cheats the moneylenders, that he has taken his mother to court and has not a penny.
