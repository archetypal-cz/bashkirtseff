%% Après quelques visites que j'ai laissé recevoir par maman, nous allons chez le photographe. Je pose souvent pour que dans l'édition de mon Journal on puisse intercaler des photographies qui sont parfaitement d'accord et représentent exactement mon humeur et mon état du moment. %%

%% 070.0543 %%

%% 070.0544 %%

I saw him drive off in a cab; he bowed to me again, and I made him a sign to come, which he took to mean the Skating. I hardly knew what I was doing.

Two days ago in Rome he went to the King and asked his permission and protection to enlist as a volunteer in the Russian army on the day war is declared. It may be declared tomorrow. His Majesty promised to smooth away the obstacles that exist for a man who has never been a soldier, and to give him letters of recommendation. The King encourages and approves him. So it is decided: he leaves Naples directly for Vienna, from where he will make his way to Saint Petersburg and the army. Maman asked him whether it was because of *Mme la Coquine*[^coquine] that he was exiling himself like this, and he answered that it was the deplorable state of his finances.
