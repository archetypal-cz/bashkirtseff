%% Très tard dans la nuit je causais encore avec la princesse de Melissano, autrement dite Rosalie. Very late at night I was still chatting with the Princess de Melissano, otherwise known as Rosalie. Still afraid that the girl might have discovered the real secret of the affair, I made her tell me everything as though to someone else. The frequent regrets I have voiced bring her to this conclusion, reassuring and almost pleasant for me.
