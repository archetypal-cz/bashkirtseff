# Wednesday, 4 April 1877

Setting aside all gloomy thoughts, I rose at six in the morning, returned the ring to Maman, and having looked out at the sea and sky and the rising sun, I made my toilette while drumming on our neighbours' door — they were sleeping like two angels. The poor souls were furious, and every five minutes I reminded them that they must come out. *If Bijou is not a fool*, I thought, *he will dress and come out as I have.* On opening the door of my room, whom do I meet? Marcuard. Marcuard is in love with Dina. What a charming man!

It is not for that love that I praise him — it is to do honour to the truth. I was exchanging a few morning pleasantries with him when Bijou appeared. I had been eager to see him again; it had seemed yesterday as though he would remain drunk for the rest of his days. We went out onto the balcony to look!
