# Saturday, 7 April 1877

At nine o'clock I was strolling in the Villa Reale.[^villareale]

— Mademoiselle, you are losing your dog! said Larderel, coming up from the west side of the Aquarium.[^aquarium]

— Really, Monsieur? Well, let it get lost. Go away and let me go on with my walk.

— I assure you it will get lost.

— Go away.

— Would you like me to go and find it?

— Go...

[^aquarium]: The Stazione Zoologica aquarium, built 1872-74 by Anton Dohrn — one of the first public aquariums in Europe.

[^villareale]: The Villa Reale (now Villa Comunale), Naples' premier public garden along the Chiaia seafront, designed by Vanvitelli in 1778.

— Go and tell that young lady that I thank her for the act of courtesy she performed; tell her that her kindness and her generous impulse touched me deeply, that I thank her and all her family. Far from being angry, I am delighted — tell her mamma so *(sua mamma)*[^suamamma]; say that I shall always remember it. The King saw that this impulse came from your good heart, and that is what flattered him; the King knows that you need nothing, that you are a foreigner — that is precisely why he is so touched. He talked of it the whole time, and he sends me *to make his apologies* for the unpleasantness you have suffered.

[^suamamma]: In Italian in the original: the King's messenger's own words ("her mamma"), which Marie notes in brackets after her French *sa maman*.
