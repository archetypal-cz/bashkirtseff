# Friday, 20 April 1877
%% Les cartes de Camillo Caracciolo, des princes Villa présents chez les Fabbricatore. Je suis sortie de bonne heure, entre une heure et trois heures, quant à la promenade, je m'en suis abstenue toujours dans l'attente d’une visite du très-honoré comte de Larderel. Attente vaine, soit dit en passant. %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:52:24 RSR: Restored from tome10.docx ¶2140, Mon Journal t.10 p.126 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
Cards from Camillo Caracciolo and from the Princes Villa, who had been at the Fabbricatore's. I went out early, between one and three o'clock; as for the promenade, I kept away from it, still waiting for a visit from the most honourable Count Larderel. A vain wait, be it said in passing.

The Hamontoff ladies came and spent an hour. I scarcely speak and am absent-minded, as may be imagined. He came in and went out several times with disreputable-looking people.

Doenhoff too, of course, as far as was possible with regard to the prince.
