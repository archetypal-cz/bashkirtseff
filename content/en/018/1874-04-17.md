# Friday, 17 April 1874

%% 018.0288 %%
%% [#Dina](../../_original/_glossary/people/core/DINA.md) %%
%% 2026-09-29T15:43:08 RSR: OCR «I»→«!» corrected (tome03.docx has the same OCR slip; «I» for «!» is confirmed on the scans of tomes 6–16). %%
%% 2026-02-27T19:55:00 TR: "Mam'zelle" = colloquial for Mademoiselle (mock formal). "auguste mère" = august mother (mock elevated). "en voilà une affaire!" = well, what an affair! %%
%% En voilà une affaire ! Mam'zelle Dina écrit. Comprenez-vous cela elle écrit, elle écrit ! Et encore chez moi, devant moi, à côté de moi pendant que j'écris. Elle écrit à son auguste mère je suppose, d'ailleurs c'est égal, le principal c'est qu'elle écrit, en voilà une affaire ! %%
Well, what an affair! Mam'zelle Dina writes. Do you understand -- she writes, she writes! And in my room, too, in front of me, beside me, while I write. She is writing to her august mother, I suppose; besides, it does not matter -- the main thing is that she writes. What an affair!

We take Bete along while everyone is at Monaco. Barely have we entered the Promenade when something passes us so fast I hardly saw it and did not recognise it. Dina looked for a moment, then: *It is* Wittgenstein. -- *Where?* -- Where?

%% 018.0291 %%
%% [#Emile](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/EMILE.md) [#Wittgenstein](../../_original/_glossary/culture/music/WITTGENSTEIN.md) [#English](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/ENGLISH.md) %%
%% [#Rosalie_Leon](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/ROSALIE_LEON.md) %%
%% 2026-02-27T19:55:03 TR: "quick" = English in original. "drive, ride, drink and gamble" = English in original. "bourgeois" = middle-class (pejorative). "cabriolet" = light two-wheeled carriage. %%
%% Je n'aurais jamais cru que ce fut lui. Un cabriolet comme celui du vieux Emile, attelé d'un cheval noir, russe et quick. Avec Rosalie. Ils avaient l'air misérable, comme des bourgeois. Je ne peux me faire une idée de ce prince, vivre avec cette femme comme avec sa femme, tranquillement; se promener, drive, ride, drink and gamble ! %%
I would never have believed it was he. A cabriolet like old Emile's, drawn by a black horse, Russian and ==*quick.*==[^1] With Rosalie. They looked wretched, like bourgeois. I cannot form an idea of this prince -- to live with this woman as with his wife, calmly; to stroll, ==drive, ride, drink and gamble!==[^2]

Every day, exactly, he comes home at six o'clock; madame receives the doctor's visit. All this is so calm, so ordinary, so patriarchal.

A young man, rich and noble, who lives like that! I do not understand it. What a difference from the Duke of Hamilton -- the other was all nobility, noise, gaiety, youth, madness, and at the same time wisdom and poetry. This one is a dull, besotted creature, and I cannot say anything more; I do not know him well enough, neither him nor his life. Only, I do not understand him.

C'est si bête vraiment. %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:55:11 RSR: Restored from tome03.docx ¶2150 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Really, Bete often bores me with her common banalities; today, if you please, she says: *I wish she would leave and you were in her place*, Princess of Wittgenstein, *a millionairess*. Last year this did not sicken me, because it was the first time, but now that I know she repeats endless nonsense like a parrot, like a parakeet, I am thoroughly sick of it. It is really so stupid.

%% 018.0295 %%
%% [#Marriage](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MARRIAGE.md) %%
%% [#Wittgenstein](../../_original/_glossary/culture/music/WITTGENSTEIN.md) [#Georges](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/GEORGES.md) %%
%% L'été commence et elle commence aussi. Pourvu que ça ne dure pas, le mariage de Wittgenstein ou, pour dire mieux, pourvu que ce ne soit pas le présage de son mariage. Je crois avoir dit que je n'aimais pas quand les gens comme j'aime se mariaient. D'ailleurs il n'est pas comme j'aime, il se conduit si misérablement, si petitement, si tranquillement, qu'il a l'air d'un ==propriétaire==[^018.0295.r1] niçois, il se conduit comme Georges. Et c'est misère ! %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:55:11 RSR: Restored from tome03.docx ¶2151 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Summer is beginning, and she is beginning too. Let us hope it does not last -- Wittgenstein's marriage, or, to put it better, let us hope it is not the omen of his marriage. I believe I have said that I did not like it when people of the kind I love got married. Besides, he is not of the kind I love; he behaves so wretchedly, so pettily, so tamely, that he looks like a Nice ==householder==[^018.0295.r1]; he behaves like Georges. And it is pitiful!

%% 018.0296 %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
%% [#Wittgenstein](../../_original/_glossary/culture/music/WITTGENSTEIN.md) [#Duke_of_Hamilton](../../_original/_glossary/people/core/DUKE_OF_HAMILTON.md) %%
%% Quel homme étrange il doit être. Souvent et aujourd'hui il est répugnant et poussiéreux et avec cela ==effronté==[^018.0296.r1]. Mais Hamilton était un *effronté* adorable et divin, tandis que celui-ci est un *effronté* commun et indigne d'attention. Il est dépourvu de toute originalité et je ne remarque son existence que par pitié qu'un homme jeune, riche, noble et même beau s'encanaille et se poussiérise [sic] ainsi. On peut s'encanailler originalement, mais lui s'encanaille comme le ferait Gros ou Saëtone. %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:55:11 RSR: Restored from tome03.docx ¶2152 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
%% 2026-09-29T13:59:46 TR: "se poussiérise [sic]" — Marie's coinage (the [sic] is the edition's); rendered by the rare but real "bedust". "s'encanailler" = to keep low company. %%
What a strange man he must be. Often, and today, he is repellent and dusty, and ==brazen==[^018.0296.r1] into the bargain. But Hamilton was an adorable and divine *brazen fellow*, whereas this one is a common *brazen fellow*, unworthy of attention. He is devoid of all originality, and I notice his existence only out of pity that a young, rich, noble and even handsome man should so keep low company and bedust himself [sic]. One can keep low company with originality, but he does it as Gros or Saëtone would.
