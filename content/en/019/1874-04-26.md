%% 2026-02-28T10:00:00 TR: Star marks uncertain reading of "Norov" in manuscript. Parenthetical outfit note is Marie's habit. %%
Papa has found his dear and famous ==Norov==[^019.0014.r1]. They have not seen each other for forty years! I have a headache. In the evening I go to the theatre. "The Barber of Seville"[^1] (walking dress, not bad).

%% 019.0015 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Paris](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/PARIS.md) %%
%% Je voulais qu'on arrangeât un pique-nique avant Paris, mais il y a toujours des empêchements, des difficultés, on y renoncera, voilà tout. Je dois renoncer à tant de choses ! (voir à la fin). %%
I wanted them to arrange a picnic before Paris, but there are always obstacles, difficulties -- we shall give it up, that is all. I must give up so many things! (see at the end).

[^019.0014.r1]: Written in Russian in the manuscript; French translation by the editor.
[^1]: In French in the original: *Le Barbier de Séville*.
