# Sunday, 26 April 1874

%% 019.0014 %%
%% [#Papa](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/PAPA.md) [#Norov](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/NOROV.md) [#Barber_of_Seville](../../_original/_glossary/culture/theater/BARBER_OF_SEVILLE.md) %%
%% Papa a trouvé son cher et fameux ★Norov*. Ils ne se sont pas vus depuis quarante ans ! J'ai mal à la tête. Le soir je vais au théâtre. "Le Barbier de Séville" (robe de promenade, pas mal). %%
%% 2026-02-28T10:00:00 TR: Star marks uncertain reading of "Norov" in manuscript. Parenthetical outfit note is Marie's habit. %%
Papa has found his dear and famous Norov. They have not seen each other for forty years! I have a headache. In the evening I go to the theater. "The Barber of Seville" (walking dress, not bad).

I wanted them to arrange a picnic before Paris, but there are always obstacles, difficulties -- we shall give it up, that is all. I must give up so many things! (see at the end).
