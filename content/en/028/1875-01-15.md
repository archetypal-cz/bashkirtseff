# Friday, 15 January 1875
The same round of errands, and in the evening we go to see *Around the World in Eighty Days* — the great success at the Porte Saint-Martin.[^1]

[^1]: Jules Verne's *Around the World in Eighty Days*, adapted for the stage and premiered at the Théâtre de la Porte Saint-Martin in 1874; a spectacular production with elaborate scenic effects.
