At seven o'clock we are in Paris, at the Grand Hôtel — room 132, second floor.[^1]

[^1]: Le Grand Hôtel, on the Boulevard des Capucines — one of Paris's grandest hotels, opened 1862. "Second floor" in the European numbering system corresponds to the third floor in American usage.
