In the evening to the Opera — "Romeo and Juliet" by Bellini,[^1] for Dory's benefit, lighting ==as bright as day==[^2] (white dress, my own hairstyle, with half up and half — there it is!). I find myself looking very old and plain — but no matter, it is only the hairstyle. In a word, I was well coiffed. This dreadful little sketch represents a white muslin dress, with the sleeves and the upper part of the bodice in Valenciennes insertion lace. I am pleased with myself this evening. Entering at the same moment as us, Mme de Galve and her daughter-in-law, both in white.
