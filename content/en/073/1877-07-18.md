# Wednesday, 18 July 1877
The elder and younger Gonzalès, whom we have already met several times, leapt from their coupé towards our landau, so that we had to stop in the very middle of the Champs-Élysées, and these two Argentines poured out to us their most exaggerated and perhaps most sincere sentiments, exclaiming that they had looked for us in all the hotels where we usually stayed.
