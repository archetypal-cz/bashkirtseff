# Sunday, 15 July 1877
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% Hier j'ai recommencé à dessiner, mon atelier de voyage est dressé. %%
Yesterday I began drawing again; my travelling studio is set up.

%% Nous parlions hier avec ma tante de Marcuard et de Dina. Je ne sais si je vous ai tenu au courant de cette cour bizarre. Marcuard n'a pas cessé de venir chez nous en devenant tout à fait froid, tout juste assez poli, avec Dina. Cela tient comme nous pensons, maman et moi, à ce qu'elle a tenu vis-à-vis de lui une conduite très bête. Tantôt elle se laissait baiser la main dans chaque coin commode et tantôt elle était coquette avec tous ces petits jeunes gens et ne lui adressait pas la parole. Ces farces-là réussissent quelquefois, mais très rarement avec des hommes sérieux, positifs, suisses et qui ont des idées de mariage. %%
%% TR: "positifs" = practical/matter-of-fact, not the English "positive"; "suisses" as adjective = characteristically Swiss: level-headed, solid, reliable %%
Yesterday my aunt and I were talking of Marcuard and Dina. I do not know whether I have kept you informed of this strange courtship. Marcuard has gone on calling on us, while growing quite cold towards Dina, only just polite enough. This comes, Maman and I believe, from her having behaved very foolishly towards him. At times she would let him kiss her hand in every convenient corner, and at times she would flirt with all those callow young men and not say a word to him. Such antics sometimes succeed, but very rarely with men who are serious, practical, Swiss, and have marriage in mind.

I suppose he did have them; otherwise his conduct would be altogether too impertinent. He paid no sugary compliments, but conducted himself like an honourable man who loves a girl and does not conceal his intentions. Dina's manners were not what they should have been; you can easily imagine what it is like when a naturally heavy person tries to be light and airy. In any case it is a very great disappointment for us all, for which Maman has greatly reproached Dina.

We were walking together in the evening, on our way back from the Hamontoffs'. Alexandre looked bored and lost, and I trembled for fear of making him impatient and boring him. Dina and Marcuard overtook us.
