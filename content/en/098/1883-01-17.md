# Wednesday, 17 January 1883

%% 098.0082 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% "Le Sport" de ce matin parle encore de l'atelier decore par Bastien-Lepage. %%
This morning's *Le Sport* speaks again of the studio decorated by Bastien-Lepage.

%% 098.0083 %%
%% [#Mortality](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MORTALITY.md) %%
%% 2026-02-02T13:30:00 LAN: "plaisir de vanite" - pleasure of vanity, satisfaction of pride %%
%% J'aimerais mieux recevoir un coup de pied. Tout le plaisir de vanite de cette soiree est empoisonne. %%
I would rather receive a kick. Every vain pleasure of that evening is poisoned.

%% 098.0084 %%
%% Le prince Jerome est arrete pour un manifeste placarde hier. %%
Prince Jérôme has been arrested for a manifesto posted yesterday.

%% 098.0085 %%
%% [#Politics](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/POLITICS.md) %%
%% Le manifeste n'est ni republicain, ni bonapartiste et pourtant ce qu'il demande est juste, un plebiscite. %%
The manifesto is neither republican nor Bonapartist, and yet what it calls for is just — a plebiscite.

%% 098.0086 %%
%% [#Politics](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/POLITICS.md) %%
%% 2026-02-02T13:30:00 LAN: "plebiscite" - referendum, direct popular vote; Bonapartist political concept %%
%% 2025-01-22T23:01:00 RSR: Entry from Book 15, raw carnet lines 3435-3445. Note: "Le Sport" again attributes soiree decoration to Bastien-Lepage; Prince Jerome arrested for manifesto; Marie wishes Gambetta still lived %%
%% *Il est* d'un imperialisme loyal et liberal qui plait assez... %%
*It is* a loyal and liberal imperialism that has a certain appeal — in the end it is the Republic with a hereditary empire... One hardly knows what to make of it. These idiots in the chamber voted to expel all the princes en bloc. Ah, if only Gambetta were alive.
