%% 098.0280 %%
# Sunday, 4 February 1883

%% 098.0281 %%
%% [#Religion](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/RELIGION.md) %%
%% C'est un peu passe aujourd'hui, nous allons a l'eglise. %%
It has subsided a little today. We go to church.

%% 098.0282 %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: "le Bois" - Bois de Boulogne; fashionable park for carriage rides %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: "rosse" - nag/old horse; here: nasty old woman, sharp-tongued %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: "perruque" - wig; sign of age/vanity %%
%% Mme Kanckine vient vers quatre heures et nous l'emmenons au Bois... %%
Mme Kanchine comes around four o'clock and we take her to the Bois and then to the Jides', the Sonis'... Mme Kanchine is an old sharp-tongued woman with a blonde wig whose eldest daughter has just married and who has another daughter, young and insignificant.

%% 098.0283 %%
%% 2025-01-22T23:53:00 RSR: Entry from Book 15, raw carnet lines 3891-3897. Note: Scandal subsiding; church; Mme Kanchine visit %%
%% C'est une femme extravagante un peu et surtout intelligente et recherchant a vivre dans un milieu intelligent. %%
She is a somewhat eccentric woman, and above all intelligent, seeking to live in an intelligent circle.
