%% J'avais ecrit a la Presidente de l'association des femmes peintres et sculpteurs... %%
I had written to the President of the Union of Women Painters and Sculptors; she replied that she would be at home today, and we go. It is the sculptor Mme Bertaux — she has medals... But for this year's exhibition it is too late... In any case she is a very agreeable woman; one must pay five hundred francs.
