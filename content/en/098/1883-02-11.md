%% 098.0317 %%
# Sunday, 11 February 1883

%% 098.0318 %%
%% [#Religion](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/RELIGION.md) %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% Je change de toile et recommence. Je vais a l'eglise, ca me prend deux heures au tableau ! %%
I change canvases and start over. I go to church — it takes me two hours away from the painting! The started canvas is abandoned; I began again yesterday evening and it seems to be coming.

%% 098.0319 %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: "asile" - here: asylum/shelter for poor children, NOT mental institution %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: "bien enveloppee" - well wrapped up; against cold while painting outdoors %%
%% Ce sont comme j'ai dit deux gamins qui vont a l'asile ou a l'ecole... %%
They are, as I said, two young children going to the shelter or to school — they hold each other by the hand, one is not yet six and the other has just turned four. I work in the shelter courtyard, outdoors, well wrapped up.

%% 098.0320 %%
%% [#Health](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/HEALTH.md) %%
%% [#Mortality](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MORTALITY.md) %%
%% Comme mon pere est tres malade et maman me dit que je pourrais regrettrer plus tard etc. etc. %%
Since my father is very ill and Maman tells me I may regret it later, etc. etc. What if he were to die without our being reconciled, etc. etc.

%% 098.0321 %%
%% En somme, il ne pourrait vraiment pas me faire des excuses. Alors je vais dans sa chambre... %%
In any case, he really could not make me an apology. So I go to his room and say good day to him, and that is all.

%% 098.0322 %%
%% 2025-01-23T00:05:00 RSR: Entry from Book 15, raw carnet lines 3981-3991. Note: Changed canvas, started over; painting "two children going to school/asylum"; father very ill - reconciliation visit; dinner at Kanchines' %%
%% Diner chez Mme Kanchine, peu de monde, les Molinari, de la Ferriere, et monsieur Garanay... %%
Dinner at Mme Kanchine's — few people: the Molinaris, de la Ferrière, and M. Garanay, a dean of I know not what faculty, and others of less importance.
