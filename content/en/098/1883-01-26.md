%% 098.0156 %%
# Friday, 26 January 1883

%% 098.0157 %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: "nefastes" - ill-omened, inauspicious; stronger than modern "bad dreams" %%
%% Des reves nefastes... %%
Ill-omened dreams...

%% 098.0158 %%
%% [#Religion](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/RELIGION.md) %%
%% [#Emotions](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/EMOTIONS.md) %%
%% [#Health](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/HEALTH.md) %%
%% Mon Dieu je ne veux pas me plaindre et j'ai meme honte de vivre comme je vis lorsque tant d'etres souffrent mille fois plus. %%
My God, I do not wish to complain, and I am even ashamed to live as I live when so many beings suffer a thousand times more. Those who are disabled and poor and obliged to work.

%% 098.0159 %%
%% Oui, c'est vrai au point de vue pratique, raisonnable, reflechi, ils sont bien plus a plaindre... %%
Yes, it is true from a practical, reasonable, considered point of view — they are far more to be pitied... But there is perhaps in my case something more cruel, something that seems more terrible... But it is impossible — it is not me!!! This does not happen to anyone.

%% 098.0160 %%
%% [#Health](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/HEALTH.md) %%
%% Voir un etre jeune, brillant... Ce medecin n'a jamais ose dire le mot... %%
To see a young, brilliant being... This doctor never dared to say the word... He said "not hearing very well, a man who did not hear well." "When one does not hear," etc.

%% 098.0161 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% [#Mortality](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MORTALITY.md) %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: "gatee" - spoiled/pampered, NOT damaged %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: "difficile et capricieuse a table" - fussy/picky about food; class-conscious self-criticism %%
%% Oh ! oui cela doit influer sur la peinture aussi ! %%
Oh! yes, it must affect the painting too! But why! But what have I done? No doubt I am not just — I enjoy a great fortune, I am spoiled, I do not give enough to the poor. While so many people die of hunger, I am fussy and capricious at table... In short, I commit this injustice of spending on a dress what would feed a family for six months...

%% 098.0162 %%
%% [#Theater_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/THEATER_THEME.md) %%
%% 2025-01-22T23:14:00 RSR: Entry from Book 15, raw carnet lines 3599-3612. Note: Nightmares; guilt about privilege; doctor won't say the word "deaf"; guilt about spending %%
%% Mais tout le monde fait comme moi ! %%
But everyone does as I do! It is no less wrong for that. Yes, I know — but there is not even anyone who reproaches himself merely for living according to the milieu into which the accident of his birth cast him. I could have... And yet what could I have done... My family feeds me, dresses me, houses me — but I do not think it would have permitted me to strip it bare... There would have been... In any case...

%% 098.0163 %%
%% Ah ! misere ! %%
Ah! Wretchedness!
