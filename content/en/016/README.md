## Key Themes

- Hamilton obsession: "I look upon others as men, upon him as a god"
- Lambertye as persistent nuisance -- involuntary blushing, "regardations [sic]"
- Carnival season in Nice: confetti battles, dominos, veglione (masked ball)
- Family "martyrizations" -- every pleasure poisoned by scenes and tears
- Social isolation crisis at Cercle de la Mediterranee concert (Feb 14-15)
- Filimonoff wedding -- Orthodox ceremony, "adoucir" wordplay
- Princess Souvoroff as new ideal: "pulchra," divine figure at charity sale
- Marie at 16: marriage anxiety, family meeting about her future
- Class shame: "nobodies who murder me" by claiming acquaintance
- Dress and appearance as armor: Worth gowns, corsets, elaborate coiffures
- Self-awareness: "I think one day I shall go mad"
