# Thursday, 11 January 1883

%% 097.0414 %%
%% Michka vient d'arriver. La maison est en l'air pour demain, demenagement de meubles, pose de lustres, tentures, etc. etc. On a demande des quantites d'invitations. %%

Michka has just arrived. The house is in upheaval for tomorrow — furniture moved, chandeliers hung, drapes put up, etc. etc. Enormous quantities of invitations have been sent.
