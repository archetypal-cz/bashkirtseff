# Sunday, 10 December 1882

%% 097.0217 %%
%% Saint Amand a dejeuner et puis Soutzo arrive. On lui fait poser les assiettes sur les murs de la salle a manger. %%

Saint-Amand for lunch, and then Soutzo[^1] arrives. We have him hang plates on the walls of the dining room.

[^1]: Soutzo: a Greek-Romanian aristocratic family with branches in both Constantinople and Paris. The Soutzos were part of the same social circle as the Bashkirtseffs; this individual was evidently useful with a hammer.
