# Sunday, 10 December 1882

%% 097.0223 %%
%% [#Saint_Amand](../../_original/_glossary/people/writers/SAINT_AMAND.md) [#Soutzo](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/SOUTZO.md) %%
%% Saint Amand à déjeuner et puis Soutzo[^1] arrive. On lui fait poser les assiettes sur les murs de la salle à manger. %%

Saint-Amand for lunch, and then Soutzo[^1] arrives. We have him hang plates on the walls of the dining room.

[^1]: Soutzo: a Romanian prince of the Soutzo family, a friend of the Karageorgevitch circle (glossary SOUTZO).
