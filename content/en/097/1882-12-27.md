# Wednesday, 27 December 1882

Well — this evening as I am going to bed I have sketched the painting I should like to do: a man in profile in the shadow playing the piano, two others listening... One lets himself go, his head resting against the top of a statue; the other sits astride a chair, face-on, a cigarette in hand. Flowers, crockery, a palette, give the amusing note — not to mention the heads! I should want Émile Bastien face-on, Dusautoy at the piano, and Bojidar. Dusautoy has an enormous nose; he is very tall, thin, with arms and hands — in short, he resembles an immense grasshopper. Émile Bastien is short, fair, with a bare forehead, blue eyes, a full face but a great deal of character — a dreamy eye if one wishes. As for Bojidar, he is a dandy with a Byzantine head — the kind one sees in holy images.
