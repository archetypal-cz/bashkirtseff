%% 097.0264 %%
# Monday, 18 December 1882

%% 097.0265 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% C'est un découragement morne, la fin de tout, j'en ai mal dans le dos, dans les jambes, mes cheveux tombent plats et droits. %%

It is a dreary discouragement — the end of everything; my back aches, my legs ache, my hair falls flat and straight.

%% 097.0266 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Saint_Marceaux](../../_original/_glossary/people/artists/SAINT_MARCEAUX.md) [#Bastien_Lepage](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/BASTIEN_LEPAGE.md) %%
%% Saint Marceaux envoie deux invitations pour l'exposition de la rue de Sèze[^1] et Bastien en a envoyé cinq. %%

Saint-Marceaux sends two invitations for the exhibition in the rue de Sèze,[^1] and Bastien has sent five.

[^1]: The exhibition in the rue de Sèze, where the dealer Georges Petit had opened his gallery in 1882.
