%% 097.0258 %%
# Monday, 18 December 1882

%% 097.0259 %%
%% C'est un decouragement morne, la fin de tout, j'en ai mal dans le dos, dans les jambes, mes cheveux tombent plats et droits. %%

It is a dreary discouragement — the end of everything; my back aches, my legs ache, my hair falls flat and straight.

%% 097.0260 %%
%% Saint Marceaux envoie deux invitations pour l'exposition de la rue de Seze et Bastien en a envoye cinq. %%

Saint-Marceaux sends two invitations for the exhibition in the rue de Sèze,[^1] and Bastien has sent five.

[^1]: The rue de Sèze exhibition: the annual winter exhibition of the Cercle des arts libéraux, held at their premises on the rue de Sèze near the Madeleine. A prestigious small exhibition of invited artists, quite distinct from the official Salon — it was at this exhibition that Marie would encounter Saint-Marceaux's sculpture the following day.
