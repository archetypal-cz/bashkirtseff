To Toto's château — to the château of Emile Audiffret, rosy, pretty, and fresh. I had not mentioned before that Terffidua[^1] possessed a château; but now that I have seen it close, I shall describe it. Everyone sees it from afar, for it dominates the whole city — and I set my watch by the *great clock of the château.*

[^1]: *Terffidua*: an anagram of "Audiffret," one of Marie's code names for him.

Having seen the Sapogenikoffs home, I was returning with Collignon when the carriage full of Maman, Nina, and Walitsky goes by; Nina stays at her house, we get in, and it was in the carriage that I had warned Maman.
