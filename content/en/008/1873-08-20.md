# Wednesday, 20 August 1873

%% 008.0094 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Paris](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/PARIS.md) [#House_of_Worth](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/HOUSE_OF_WORTH.md) [#Paul](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/PAUL.md) [#Dina](../../_original/_glossary/people/core/DINA.md) [#Loge](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/LOGE.md) [#Porchefontaine](../../_original/_glossary/places/hotels/PORCHEFONTAINE.md) %%
%% 2026-02-16T14:45:00 TR: "ombrelle" — parasol. "recouvrir l'ombrelle" — re-cover the parasol. %%
%% Je me réveille à onze heures et commence à compter mon argent. Je vois que deux mille cinq cents francs ne sont rien quand il faut les dépenser. Je ne puis m'arranger au-dessous de trois mille francs, et encore, c'est bien modeste. J'ai acheté du papier avec mes initiales, fait recouvrir l'ombrelle, commandé une paire de bottines grises (j'en ai cinq paires) des gants, des robes; il me faut des chapeaux, maintenant. [Rayé: toujours] %%
I wake at eleven and begin counting my money. I see that two thousand five hundred francs are nothing when one must spend them. I cannot manage on less than three thousand, and even that is quite modest. I bought stationery with my initials, had my parasol re-covered, ordered a pair of grey boots (I have five pairs), gloves, dresses; I need hats now. [Crossed out: always]

%% J ai commandé encore une robe chez Worth, drap gros bleu, très jolie sans doute, sept cents francs. Je n'ai pas de quoi la payer, il me manque trois cent cinquante francs. On me l'enverra à Nice. I ordered yet another dress at Worth's — coarse blue woolen, very pretty no doubt — seven hundred francs. I have not the means to pay for it; I am short three hundred and fifty francs. They will send it to me in Nice. We lost Paul; dinner at the Russian restaurant, opposite the Opéra-Comique. There were only men. I played the fool and said a thousand silly things; everyone who heard me could barely keep from laughing. Sometimes I am seized by

%% 008.0096 %%
%% accès de bavardage, alors rien ne peut me retenir. Je suis comme une machine. %%
fits of chattering, and then nothing can stop me. I am like a machine.

%% 008.0097 %%
%% 2026-02-16T14:45:02 TR: "coupe" — enclosed two-seat carriage. "ascenseur" — lift/elevator (still a novelty in 1873). %%
%% Il pleut, on amène un coupé, nous rentrons. Dans l'ascenseur je vois Paul, il avait rencontré un ami. Il a faim et est allé manger en bas. %%
It is raining; they bring round a coupé and we go back. In the lift I see Paul; he had met a friend. He is hungry and went to eat downstairs.

%% 008.0098 %%
%% Cette journée est courte, parce que j'ai écrit celle d'hier aujourd'hui. %%
Today's entry is short, because I wrote yesterday's today.

%% 008.0099 %%
%% 2026-02-16T14:45:03 TR: "sacrilege" — Marie uses this hyperbolically about missing the races. %%
%% Nous avons rencontré Logé sur le boulevard. Dimanche courses à Porchefontaine, ce serait un *sacrilège* de ne pas y aller. Aussi je prierai Dieu ce soir de me pardonner mes folies et de ne pas me punir ! %%
We met Logé on the boulevard. On Sunday there are races at Porchefontaine — it would be a *sacrilege* not to go. So I shall pray to God tonight to forgive my follies and not to punish me!
