Dina et Saint Amand ravis du spectacle de ce bonheur conjugal. Nous sortons ensemble M. Mme et bébé, dans des magasins pour Monsieur puis au Bois et un instant chez les Karageorgevitch. %%

A surprise! M. Bashkirsseff has arrived.[^2] I am fetched from the atelier and find him in the dining room with Maman, who is lavishing every affection on him. Dina and Saint-Amand delighted by the spectacle of this conjugal happiness. We go out together — M., Mme, and the baby — to shops for Monsieur, then to the Bois, and briefly to the Karageorgevitches'.

He has no doubt come to fetch Maman — but I know nothing yet; we are still too unsettled.

[^2]: M. Bashkirtseff: Marie's father, from whom the family had been estranged. His appearance in Paris is unexpected and clearly fraught with complicated emotions — Maman's excess of tenderness hints at the complexity.
