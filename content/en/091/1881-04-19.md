# Tuesday, 19 April 1881

I lunched at the Gavinis'. Bojidar dines here. He is a very charming boy. Alexis is a little too conscious of being a man, and a handsome one — but Bojidar is kind, intelligent, well-bred, and thinks me far more than I really am. He is also a gossip, but that does not bother me.
