# Tuesday, 8 March 1881

%% 091.0277 %%
%% [#Money](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MONEY.md) %%
%% [#Money_family](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MONEY_FAMILY.md) %%
%% [#Villevieille](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/VILLEVIEILLE.md) %%
%% Maintenant je me reproche de ne pas avoir donné mes trois mille francs. %%

Now I reproach myself for not having given my three thousand francs.

%% 091.0278 %%
%% Mais enfin pourquoi, à des étrangers, des inconnus presque ! %%

But why, after all — to strangers, to people I scarcely know!

%% 091.0279 %%
%% [#Money](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MONEY.md) %%
%% [#Money_family](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MONEY_FAMILY.md) %%
%% Dans ce cas il aurait mieux valu les donner aux pauvres selon ma première idée. %%

In that case it would have been better to give them to the poor, as I had first intended.
