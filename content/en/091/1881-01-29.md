%% 2026-09-28T21:01:18 RSR: Restored from tome14.docx ¶950–953, Mon Journal t.14 pp.73–74 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). printed «séprarée» (Mon Journal t.14 p.74) corrected to «séparée», a typesetting typo. %%
> *The worst of all is the loss of time, and my anxiety about you, which cannot even be put into words. Had I been able to imagine the suffering I am now obliged to live through, separated from you, I should of course never have gone to Russia.*

> Marie Bashkirtseff
