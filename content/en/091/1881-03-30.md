# Wednesday, 30 March 1881

%% 091.0395 %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% [#Paris](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/PARIS.md) [#JULIAN](../../_original/_glossary/places/schools/JULIAN.md) %%
%% J'ai fait semblant de dormir jusqu'à dix heures pour ne pas aller à l'atelier. Et suis très misérable. %%

I pretended to sleep until ten o'clock so as not to go to the atelier. I am very miserable.

%% 091.0396 %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% [#Julian](../../_original/_glossary/places/schools/JULIAN.md) %%
%% Voici la réponse de Julian. Cela me calme un peu... Songez donc. Non, vous ne pouvez pas vous imaginer ce que serait pour moi le refus du tableau ! Ce ne serait plus... enfin *je n'aurais à crier que contre moi* ! Et je ne sais pas ce qui est plus affreux, être soi-même coupable de son infortune ou souffrir à cause des autres. Ah ! ce serait un de ces coups, en pleine poitrine, je ne m'imagine pas ce que je ferais... Enfin il faut espérer... %%

Here is Julian's reply. It calms me a little... Just think. No, you cannot imagine what a rejection of the canvas would mean to me! It would no longer be... well, *I should have no one to rail at but myself!* And I do not know which is more dreadful — to be oneself the cause of one's misfortune, or to suffer on account of others. Ah, it would be one of those blows, full in the chest — I cannot imagine what I would do... One must hope, in any case...

%% 091.0397 %%
%% [#Saint_Amand](../../_original/_glossary/people/religious/SAINT_AMAND.md) [#Detaille](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/DETAILLE.md) [#Dalmas](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/DALMAS.md) %%
%% Saint Amand est venu aujourd'hui, très inquiet. Il a passé la soirée hier avec Détaille (le peintre) qui croit vaguement avoir vu un atelier refusé. Il s'occupe de moi, de notre fête. Enfin sa manie est bien innocente et peut devenir un vrai bienfait. Voici dans le "Sport" la description du bal de Dalmas. %%

Saint-Amand came today, very anxious. He spent yesterday evening with Détaille[^1] (the painter), who has a vague notion he saw a picture of an atelier rejected. He is busying himself with me, with our party. His mania is entirely innocent and may become a real benefit. Here in *Le Sport* is the description of the Dalmas ball.
%% 2026-10-01T08:42:47 RED: MEDIUM Para 091.0397 — «un atelier refusé» = a picture of an atelier (Marie's own Salon subject), not «a studio canvas»; re-rendered. %%

[^1]: Édouard Détaille (1848–1912): celebrated French military painter, extremely popular in this period, best known for his panoramas of the Franco-Prussian War. He was active on the social scene and knew the Salon jury proceedings well.
