# Friday, 1 April 1881

%% 091.0411 %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% [#Paris](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/PARIS.md) %%
%% [#Julian](../../_original/_glossary/places/schools/JULIAN.md) %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% [#Atelier_Julian](../../_original/_glossary/culture/art/L_ATELIER_JULIAN.md) %%
%% Poisson d'avril à part, je suis reçue. Julian est venu le dire lui-même hier après minuit en sortant de chez Lefèbvre. C'est Oelsnitz qu'il a vue. %%

April Fool's aside — I am accepted.[^1] Julian came to say so himself last night after midnight, on leaving Lefebvre's. He saw Oelsnitz there.

[^1]: *Poisson d'avril*: April Fool's Day — 1 April. The joke is that the news of acceptance (which came the previous night) might have seemed too good to be true on this particular day.

We drank punch at the studio. Bojidar, without being asked, inquired of Fidière (a young man from downstairs) and assures me I have *number two.*[^2] This seems to me excessive.

[^2]: Catalogue number two: with a surname beginning "A" (Andrey), Marie would be near the front of the alphabetical catalogue. Number 2 would place her as the second work listed — a prominent position.
