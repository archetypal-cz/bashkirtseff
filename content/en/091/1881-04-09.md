%% 091.0417 %%
# Saturday, 9 April 1881

%% 091.0418 %%
%% [#Paris](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/PARIS.md) [#Chambre_des_Deputes](../../_original/_glossary/places/social/CHAMBRE_DES_DEPUTES.md) [#Tony](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/TONY.md) [#Adelina_Patti](../../_original/_glossary/culture/theater/ADELINA_PATTI.md) [#Theatre_des_Italiens](../../_original/_glossary/places/theaters/THEATRE_DES_ITALIENS.md) %%
%% Je vais à la Chambre avec mon père puis nous allons chez Tony et le soir à Patti dans "Semiramis". Que ce chef d'œuvre est donc ennuyeux ! %%

I go to the Chamber[^1] with my father, then we visit Tony, and in the evening to Patti in *Semiramide.*[^2] How tedious this masterpiece is!

%% 091.0419 %%
%% [#Reille](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/REILLE.md) [#Gavini](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/GAVINI.md) [#Wodzinsky](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/WODZINSKY.md) [#Mme_Engelhardt](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/MME_ENGELHARDT.md) [#Kiki](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/KIKI.md) %%
%% Comme monde il y a eu Mmes Reille, Gavini, Odette. Wodzinsky, Engelhardt, Kiki. %%

Among the company: Mmes Reille, Gavini, Odette; Wodzinsky, Engelhardt, Kiki.

%% 091.0420 %%
%% Je suis très fatiguée. %%

I am very tired.

[^1]: The Chambre des Députés: the lower house of the French parliament, then housed in the Palais Bourbon. Marie went with her father, who was apparently taking an interest in French politics during his Paris visit.
[^2]: *Semiramide* (1823): grand opera by Rossini, based on Voltaire's play. An extremely demanding work for soprano, it was one of Patti's showpieces. Marie finds its grandeur soporific rather than thrilling.
