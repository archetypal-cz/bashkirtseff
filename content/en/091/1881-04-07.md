# Thursday, 7 April 1881

%% 091.0441 %%
%% [#Theater_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/THEATER_THEME.md) %%
%% Nous sommes allés voir "Divorçons", c'est ravissant. %%

We went to see *Divorçons*[^4] — it is delightful.

[^4]: *Divorçons* ("Let us divorce"; 1880): a hugely successful comedy by Victorien Sardou and Émile de Najac, running at the Palais-Royal. It satirised the new divorce debates of the Third Republic.
