# Thursday, 7 April 1881

%% 091.0441 %%
%% Nous sommes allés voir "Divorçons", c'est ravissant. %%

We went to see *Divorçons*[^4] — it is delightful.

[^4]: *Divorçons* (1880): a hugely successful comedy by Victorien Sardou and Émile de Najac, running at the Palais-Royal. It satirised the new divorce debates of the Third Republic.
