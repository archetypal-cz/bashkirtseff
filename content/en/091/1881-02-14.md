# Monday, 14 February 1881

%% 091.0129 %%
%% [#Louise_Michel](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/LOUISE_MICHEL.md) [#RODOLPHE_JULIAN](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/RODOLPHE_JULIAN.md) %%
%% Ce matin Julian a reçu la lettre dont voici le brouillon, [manque] accompagnée d'une vieille tulipe rouge. Comme nous avons souvent ri ensemble de Louise Michel, cela fait beaucoup d'effet. %%

This morning Julian received the letter whose draft is here [missing], accompanied by an old red tulip. As we have often laughed together about Louise Michel,[^1] it makes a great impression.

[^1]: Louise Michel (1830–1905): French anarchist and feminist revolutionary, "the Red Virgin of Montmartre," prominent in the Paris Commune (1871). By 1881 she was a celebrity of the radical left. Marie and Julian apparently joked about her dramatic persona — hence the red tulip sent with the letter as a knowing reference.

%% La tête de Brisbane (Alice) est peinte en deux heures de temps, et Julian me dit de la laisser. Et d'autres fois on reste huit jours à faire une saleté... Une partie du tablier et le corsage sont aussi peints. %%

Brisbane's head (Alice) is painted in two hours, and Julian tells me to leave it. And other times one spends eight days producing something wretched... Part of the apron and the bodice are also painted.

The *perspecteur* [sic] comes and preaches at me for twenty-five minutes the necessity of submitting to his infallible rules. Tony then?... and I? This man cannot be wrong, since they are fixed rules — but Tony, but I?

%% 091.0132 %%

I know nothing about it. Let us not go deeper, so as not to tire ourselves at the moment when we need great freedom of mind to produce the masterpiece in peace. But I opt for the mathematician.
