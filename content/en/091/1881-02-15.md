%% 091.0133 %%
# Tuesday, 15 February 1881

%% 091.0134 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% [#Salon](../../_original/_glossary/culture/art/SALON.md) %%
%% Trente-deux jours jusqu'à l'Exposition. %%

Thirty-two days until the Exhibition.[^2]

%% 091.0135 %%
%% [#Bojidar](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/BOJIDAR.md) %%
%% [#Alexis](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/ALEXIS.md) [#Princess_Karageorgevitch](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/PRINCESS_KARAGEORGEVITCH.md) [#Nice](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/NICE.md) %%
%% Cette folle de princesse est revenue de Nice et est chez nous avec ses deux enfants, elle est venue hier aussi avec Bojidar; ils s'en vont de bonne heure et Alexis reste jusqu'à ce que nous le flanquons à la porte... en vue de l'atelier. %%

That mad princess is back from Nice and staying at our house with her two children; she came yesterday too with Bojidar. They leave early and Alexis stays until we throw him out... for the atelier's sake.

%% 091.0136 %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
%% [#Appearance](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/APPEARANCE.md) %%
%% [#Alexis](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/ALEXIS.md) %%
%% Je l'aime bien parce qu'il me trouve "épatante" et tellement "galbeuse"[sic] vraiment trop galbeuse, le bon Dieu m'en a trop donné aussi je suis unique. Enfin il me trouve aussi jolie, je sais cela par voies indirectes et puis dans sa conversation. %%

I am fond of him because he finds me "stunning" and so "well-curved"[^3] — truly too well-curved; God gave me too much of it, so I am unique. He also finds me pretty — I know this through indirect channels and from his conversation.

%% 091.0137 %%
%% Cela ajoute du piquant à nos entretiens. %%

It adds spice to our conversations.

[^2]: The Salon of 1881 opened in May; Marie is counting down the 32 days remaining to submit her entry.
[^3]: Marie's coinage: *galbeuse* [sic] — her invented or half-heard word from *galbe* (curve, contour); used admiringly of a shapely figure. She treats it with ironic pride.
