%% Julian et Tony, Julian surtout puisqu’il l’a vu plus souvent sont contents du tableau et me l’ont dit, et Julian me l’a dit plusieurs fois et j’en étais contente moi-même et très montée. %% 2026-09-28T21:01:18 RSR: Restored from tome14.docx ¶962, Mon Journal t.14 p.74 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Julian and Tony — Julian especially, since he has seen it more often — are pleased with the picture and have told me so, and Julian told me so several times, and I was pleased with it myself and greatly fired up. Now it is falling flat; I am no longer satisfied with my composition, and it is no use telling myself over and over that Tony has seen it twice and told me that it is very well arranged, amusing, and that nothing must be changed. Julian tells me to change nothing. I no longer have confidence. Julian too tells me to change nothing. In short, everyone thinks it good, one group in the middle ground particularly pretty, but I am not pleased. I see it differently; there must be no more thought of alterations, it is too late... besides...

%% 091.0039 %%
%% C’est tout de même curieux que tant de choses me choquent dans ce tableau et ne choquent ni Julian ni Tony... C’est qu’ils pensent que je ne puis pas faire mieux et ne veulent pas me laisser enfoncer en cherchant midi à quatorze heures... %%
%% 2026-09-28T21:01:18 RSR: Restored from tome14.docx ¶963, Mon Journal t.14 p.74 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
All the same, it is curious that so many things in this picture shock me and shock neither Julian nor Tony... The truth is that they think I cannot do better and do not want to let me bog myself down by making things harder than they are...
