# Wednesday, 4 May 1881

%% 091.0559 %%
%% [#Salon](../../_original/_glossary/culture/art/SALON.md) %%
%% [#Bailleul](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/BAILLEUL.md) %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% La Bailleul pose ! Je fais son portrait. Elle l'a tellement demandé qu'il l'a fallu. Mais c'est bien embêtant. J'ai été au Salon avant dix heures, et à dix heures elle est arrivée poser. %%

La Bailleul is posing! I am painting her portrait. She asked so insistently that it had to be done. But it is quite tiresome. I went to the Salon before ten o'clock, and at ten she arrived to sit.

%% 091.0560 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% [#RODOLPHE_JULIAN](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/RODOLPHE_JULIAN.md) %%
%% [Mots noircis: J'ai été payer Julian et lui ai montrer] mes photographies en costume. Ce juif paye la démolition de la cloison et ne me réclame rien pour le tableau crevé. Je ne sais que faire. %%

[words blacked out: I went to pay Julian and showed him] my photographs in costume. This Jew[^1] pays for the removal of the partition and claims nothing from me for the punctured canvas. I do not know what to do.

%% 091.0561 %%
%% [#Bois_de_Boulogne](../../_original/_glossary/places/social/BOIS_DE_BOULOGNE.md) %%
%% [#Alexis](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/ALEXIS.md) %%
%% [#RODOLPHE_JULIAN](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/RODOLPHE_JULIAN.md) [#Princess_Karageorgevitch](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/PRINCESS_KARAGEORGEVITCH.md) %%
%% Après un tour au Bois nous amenons la princesse dîner à la maison, Alexis est arrivé le soir. Vous savez Julian l'ayant vu me suivre au Salon s'imagine qu'il y a quelque chose. Ça me fait rire. %%

After a drive in the Bois we bring the Princess home to dinner; Alexis arrived in the evening. You know that Julian, having seen him following me at the Salon, imagines there is something between us. It makes me laugh.

[^1]: Marie uses an antisemitic epithet here in a characteristically contradictory way — the "Jewish" quality she means is supposed to be a shrewd, calculating businessness, yet the very next clause praises Julian's unexpected generosity. The casual antisemitism is a feature of her period and milieu, not endorsed.
