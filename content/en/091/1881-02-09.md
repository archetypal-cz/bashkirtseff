%% Voici un article de Popaul qui résume un évènement du jour, l'affaire Dugué, et qui en outre donne des explications sur la politique du sieur Popaul. Très montée après la conversation avec Julian, je suis très refroidie et n'ai plus hâte de relire mes impressions pour me rendre compte... %%

Here is an article by Popaul[^1] summarising a current affair — the Dugué case — and which moreover offers explanations of the said Popaul's politics. Very worked up after my conversation with Julian, I am now much cooled down and no longer in a hurry to re-read my impressions to take stock...

[^1]: "Popaul": Marie's mocking nickname for Paul de Cassagnac (from "Paul" — the diminutive "Popaul" was a common comic name). He was editor of *Le Pays* and later *L'Autorité*, Bonapartist newspapers. The "Dugué affair" has not been identified precisely.

Besides, I am absolutely absorbed by the picture. I am wearing out my youth in work — and why?
