%% 2026-09-29T01:37:07 RSR: Restored from tome08.docx ¶3150, Mon Journal t.8 p.283 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-29 rebuild). %%
There are only two mansions on the Champs-Élysées that I should like to have: Baron Roger's on the right, and the former Italian embassy on the left. And even those I should want only *while I wait*. While I wait for what? I do not know, but *while I wait*, for I am waiting.

%% 063.0340 %%
%% Nous devions aller à Versailles mais la chaleur était accablante, nous nous sommes contentées d'une promenade au Bois. Je pris quelque plaisir de savoir que Chocolat voyait pour la première fois Paris. %%

We were to go to Versailles, but the heat was overwhelming, so we contented ourselves with a drive in the Bois. I took some pleasure in knowing that Chocolat was seeing Paris for the first time.

%% 063.0341 %%
%% Ce petit être vient d'avoir treize ans je lui en croyais dix. Je me forme un domestique pour l'avenir. Je déteste les domestiques qui ont servi ailleurs que chez moi. — « Rien avant, rien après ». %% 2026-09-29T01:37:07 RSR: Restored from tome08.docx ¶3152, Mon Journal t.8 p.283 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-29 rebuild) The call of the edition's footnote (¶3155) after «Rien avant, rien après» removed (p.283).. %%
This little creature has just turned thirteen; I thought he was ten. I am training a servant for the future. I detest servants who have served anywhere but with me. — "Nothing before, nothing after." Only at half past nine did we rise from table. On the steps we meet Baron de Mertens and his wife, who are there with a Mme Square, and a gentleman I have seen everywhere. He is introduced to us: M. Marx. This creature wanted to persuade me that I would keep up neither singing nor painting, that I would end up like every other society girl. And I answered that in ten years I meant to be crowned at the Capitol.

%% Quand je pense au bonheur de Mlle Kechko, princesse de Serbie, tous les mauvais instincts se réveillent en moi, c'est-à-dire l'envie ! Cette fille si misérable, si commune avec ses joues rouges et son gros nez moldave ! Elle est belle, mais c'est une beauté que je voudrais avoir pour femme suivante, habillée d'un costume bizarre, une femme pour me chausser et pour me rafraîchir avec un grand éventail. Et la voilà reine, et reine dans un moment de trouble, c'est-à-dire dans un moment inappréciable pour les ambitieux. Certes sa place est marquée dans l'histoire. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:37:07 RSR: Restored from tome08.docx ¶3154 + ¶3156, Mon Journal t.8 pp.283–284 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-29 rebuild). One paragraph cut by the page break and the edition's footnote (¶3155), rejoined. %%
When I think of the good fortune of Mlle Kechko, Princess of Serbia, all my bad instincts awaken — that is to say, envy! That girl, so wretched, so common, with her red cheeks and her big Moldavian nose! She is beautiful, but it is a beauty I should like to have for a lady's maid, dressed in some outlandish costume, a woman to put on my shoes and to cool me with a great fan. And there she is, a queen, and a queen at a time of turmoil — that is to say, at a priceless moment for the ambitious. Her place in history is certainly assured.
