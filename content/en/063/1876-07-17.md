%% Je suis contente, excitée plutôt. *J'accepte* l'absence de Pietro, j'explique ainsi son silence. Je lui pardonne de ne pas m'aimer, je consens à une alliance intéressée. Pietro me servira de marchepied. Je crois à la prédiction, on croit ce qu'on désire. Elle m'a terriblement monté la tête et, avec une femme comme moi, les choses arrivent parce qu'on a prédit plutôt qu'elles sont prédites parce qu'elles doivent arriver. %%
I am happy, excited rather. *I accept* Pietro's absence; that is how I explain his silence. I forgive him for not loving me; I consent to a match of interest. Pietro will serve me as a stepping-stone. I believe the prediction; one believes what one desires. She has worked me up dreadfully, and, with a woman like me, things happen because they have been predicted rather than being predicted because they must happen.

God, what good feelings wasted! If I were given the means to act I would move the world! Courage, energy, ambition, will! What use are you to me? When shall I employ you worthily? When will my ardent aspirations be anything but a dream, the delirium of an ambition too vast for the narrow space in which it is confined?

[^1]: Cardinal Giacomo Antonelli, Secretary of State to Pope Pius IX since 1848, died in Rome on 6 November 1876, less than four months after this séance; he never became pope (Wikipedia, "Giacomo Antonelli"). Marie learns of his death from a telegram in Russia and gives up the prophecy: «Comme rien ne s'est accompli de la prédiction de la Mongruel je n'en parlerai plus jamais» (15 November 1876).
[^2]: Italian troops had taken Rome on 20 September 1870, ending the Papal States; Rome became the capital of the Kingdom of Italy under King Victor Emmanuel II, while Pope Pius IX refused to recognise the loss and stayed within the Vatican (Wikipedia, "Capture of Rome"). Marie's dream of driving «le roi» out of Rome is the cause of the papal party, to which the Antonelli family belonged.
