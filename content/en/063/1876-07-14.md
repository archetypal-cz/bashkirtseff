%% 2026-09-29T01:37:07 RSR: Restored from tome08.docx ¶3044, Mon Journal t.8 p.276 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-29 rebuild). %%

Only at one o'clock do I take a cup of coffee and eat an egg so salty that it is more salt with an egg than an egg with salt. I have an idea that salt is good for the throat. Georges is with us. I put on a dress of grey batiste, quite plain, a black lace fichu and a brown hat. But once dressed I think I look so well that I should like to be like this always!
