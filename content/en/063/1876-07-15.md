%% Ne nous consumons pas en élans et en regrets inutiles. Je déteste faire des choses inutiles. Pour moi chaque chose a un but, quand elle n'en a pas, je m'en invente un pour tranquilliser ma conscience. Je me demandais l'autre jour à quoi me servirait mon amitié pour Mme de Mouzay. C'est révoltant ! Bah ? Elle me sert à me donner l'amitié de cette dame. Mais son amitié à quoi me sert-elle ? Sans compter que souvent ce qu'on dit en bas est entendu en haut. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:37:07 RSR: Restored from tome08.docx ¶3140, Mon Journal t.8 p.282 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-29 rebuild) Line-break hyphen «sert- elle» joined (p.282).. %%
Let us not wear ourselves out in useless yearnings and regrets. I hate doing useless things. For me everything has a purpose; when it has none, I invent one to quiet my conscience. The other day I was asking myself what use my friendship with Mme de Mouzay would be to me. It is revolting! Bah? It serves to win me this lady's friendship. But what use is her friendship to me? This: that Mme de Mouzay speaks very highly of me; she judges me as I am, as I show myself; and the good that is said of you is not a commodity to be scorned. Not to mention that what is said below is often heard above.
