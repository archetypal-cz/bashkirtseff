# Thursday, 26 October 1876 (14 October)
%% Bénis soient les chemins de fer, nous sommes à Harkoff, chez le fameux aubergiste Andrieux; partis à une heure de la vieille Tchernakowska : sur des chevaux âgés de trente ans, des chevaux de grand-papa. Et le départ et le voyage ont été un vrai feu d'artifice de gaieté simple et honnête. On respire autrement avec des gens qui ne vous veulent que du bien et qui ne sont ni tordus ni bossus. %%
%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶2026, Mon Journal t.9 p.144 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
Blessed be the railways! We are in Kharkoff, at the famous innkeeper Andrieux's. We left old Tchernakowska at one o'clock, behind thirty-year-old horses — grandpapa's horses. Both the departure and the journey were a real firework display of simple, honest gaiety. One breathes differently among people who wish you nothing but good and who are neither crooked nor hunchbacked.

So I was not mistaken when I described the portrait of Grand Duke Nicholas. Lola, who has seen him and danced with him at the Institute, says that his profile has something of Antonelli, and Antonelli... is he thinking of me? We parted on 22 May... I believe... five months ago. That is a long time... bah! I remember him, and he remembers me too. As for the telegram, he pretends not to have received it. That is wise. But I curse him. I swore, and I do not regret it. What I say about it I say *just like that*; the pen writes well, and the holder I bought today amuses me. It is of ivory and contains a pencil, two penknife blades and a pen; what is more, it is marked off in centimetres and can measure anything you like.
