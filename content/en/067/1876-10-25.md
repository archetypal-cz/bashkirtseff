# Wednesday, 25 October 1876 (13 October)
%% Je reçus une impression si odieuse avant-hier, qu'il me répugne de retourner à Gavronzi. %%
%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶2013, Mon Journal t.9 p.143 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
The day before yesterday left me with so odious an impression that I am loath to go back to Gavronzi.

%% 067.0304 %%
%% Je suis agacée par les retards, et il me semble qu'on les crée. %%
%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶2014, Mon Journal t.9 p.143 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%

%% Je regarde Nadine et je m'émerveille: orpheline à quatre ans, recueillie par Wassilissa (la folle d'à présent) chez laquelle elle fut comme un chien; et ce n'a été guère parce que la dame fût méchante ou avare, (Nadine avait une fortune) mais la vieille fille tout en procurant des professeurs à la petite, était incapable de s'en occuper et la laissait traîner avec les domestiques. Lorsqu'elle eut treize ans il fallut venir ici, car la riche Wassilissa était ruinée. A quinze ans et demi Nadine épousa mon oncle Alexandre dont elle se trouvait la cousine. %%
%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶2015, Mon Journal t.9 p.143 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
I look at Nadine and marvel: orphaned at four, taken in by Wassilissa (now the madwoman), in whose house she was treated like a dog; and that was hardly because the lady was wicked or miserly (Nadine had a fortune), but the old maid, while providing teachers for the little girl, was incapable of looking after her and left her to trail about with the servants. When she was thirteen she had to come here, for the rich Wassilissa was ruined. At fifteen and a half Nadine married my uncle Alexandre, whose cousin she happened to be.

She has educated herself; she has civilised and polished her husband, whom she keeps in check through respect; she knows a great deal, and without talking of Homer or Roman history she will everywhere seem, and will truly be, a cultivated woman. I bow and admire.

I had something like a scene with Alexandre, and the money arrived in the evening. The man took two days to get here. I am unjust, and who knows? He could not have come any sooner; Vidnewka is a hundred leagues from here, and there are almost no railways. Come, it is over, and I regret the scene, after which poor Alexandre took to his bed.

I saw Marfa, Trifon's wife; that scoundrel is married — after eight months of marriage he left his wife in the lurch to follow Monsieur. Marfa has a charming little girl of six named Pauline, who looks like her wretch of a father. She has other children too... but it is not her fault; a sturdy chambermaid is not abandoned by her husband with impunity.
