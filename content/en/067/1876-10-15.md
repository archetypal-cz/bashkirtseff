# Sunday, 15 October 1876 (3 October)
%% Et d'abord je suis indépendante ! A seize [ans] on a le droit de se choisir un tuteur qu’on est libre de changer tant qu’on veut. Avec la signature de ce tuteur je puis faire tout ce que je veux. Pour agir plus librement je demandai et obtins de suite comme tuteur Alexandre. De cette façon je n’ai pas besoin d’être majeure. %%
%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶1762, Mon Journal t.9 p.125 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
And first of all, I am independent! At sixteen one has the right to choose a guardian, whom one is free to change as often as one likes. With this guardian's signature I can do whatever I want. To act more freely I asked for Alexandre as guardian, and obtained him at once. In this way I have no need to be of age.

After seeing Alexandre we left. Absorbed in daydreams the whole way, I did not say a word. Summer seems to have come back, and were it not for the trees, yellow and ready to shed their leaves, one would think oneself in some lovely country.
