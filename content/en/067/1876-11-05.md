# Sunday, 5 November 1876 (24 October)

When I was told of it I answered: it is not true. For really, you had been entrusted with a low office there, and the position of watchman seems to me rather degrading for a young man of your age, and then... well, without being a crime, it is ugly. And do not think that I am being unjust, or that I say this because... !!

You are like those children who, whatever one tells them, will never understand that they must study until the time comes to understand; now you neglect it, later you will weep! Strained relations lead to nothing good; I want no more of them. Let us be sincere, let us be simple, let us be kind. Our sourness, our mutual discontents, our quarrels are natural and necessary things at the beginning of our friendship, and because of the existing circumstances. We have told each other everything, explained everything; enough now.
