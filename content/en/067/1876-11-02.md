%% Il fallut un “tas d'histoires” pour l'amener à Poltava vers ce soir. A l'assemblée de la noblesse un piano-quatuoriste donne un concert. Je voulus y aller pour me faire voir, et ce sont des petits obstacles sans fin. Ce caractère pointilleux, mesquin et méchant, assassine par les petitesses. %%

It took "a heap of fuss" to get him to Poltava by this evening. At the Assembly of the Nobility a piano-quartettist is giving a concert. I wanted to go so as to be seen, and there were endless petty obstacles. That fussy, mean and spiteful character murders one with pettiness.

%% 067.0609 %%
%% Ce n'est pas assez de m’avoir tenue enfermée pendant trois mois, n'invitant que le troupeau de tricheurs aux cartes et donnant par cette étrange manie de me cacher et séparer du reste de l'univers (Poltava ?) pleine pâture aux chiens affamés de cancans. %%
%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶2330, Mon Journal t.9 pp.161–162 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
It is not enough to have kept me shut up for three months, inviting no one but the herd of card-sharps, and, through this strange mania for hiding me and cutting me off from the rest of the universe (Poltava?), handing the gossip-hungry dogs a free feast.

%% 067.0610 %%
%% L'intérêt faute de satisfaction, a passé à l'état d'un léger mécontentement et, faute de vérité, on a commencé à supposer et à inventer. Ce n'est pas assez de ne m'avoir pas procuré le moindre plaisir, d'avoir chassé ceux qui pouvaient m'être des égaux, d'avoir fait la sourde oreille à toutes mes insinuations et même à mes demandes concernant un fichu spectacle d'amateurs. Ce n'est pas assez. Voilà qu'au bout de trois mois de câlineries, de gentillesses, de frais d'esprit, d'amabilité, j'obtiens... une forte opposition à ce que j'aille à ce misérable concert ! Ce n'est pas tout, et de ceci je vins à bout, mais alors il fallut faire une histoire sur le choix de la toilette. Il fallut m'imposer une robe de laine, un costume de promenade. %%
%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶2331, Mon Journal t.9 p.162 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
Curiosity, for want of satisfaction, has passed into a state of mild discontent, and, for want of the truth, people have begun to suppose and to invent. It is not enough to have procured me not the slightest pleasure, to have driven away those who could have been my equals, to have turned a deaf ear to all my hints and even to my requests concerning a wretched amateur performance. It is not enough. And now, after three months of coaxing, of sweetness, of wit spent on him, of amiability, I obtain... strong opposition to my going to this miserable concert! That is not all; I got the better of this, but then there had to be a fuss over the choice of dress. He had to force a woollen gown on me, a walking costume.

%% 067.0611 %%
%% Que c'est petit tout cela, que c'est indigne d'êtres intelligents ! %%
%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶2332, Mon Journal t.9 p.162 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
How petty all this is, how unworthy of intelligent beings!

%% 067.0612 %%

I did not absolutely need this gentleman; I had Nadine, Alexandre, Paul and Pacha; but I took him along out of caprice and to my great displeasure.

%% 067.0613 %%
%% Une robe en étoffe orientale de laine, soie et or, rayée en travers, et en forme de chemise biblique, carrée autour du cou à l'orientale, et serrée à la taille par une ceinture anguleuse afin qu'elle puisse descendre très bas; des souliers de satin rouge et un collier de trois rangées de perles... fausses mais personne ne le sait. Racontée, la toilette paraît ébouriffante, mais sur moi elle n'était que légèrement piquante, mais majestueusement simple et gracieuse. Nadine mit une superbe robe noire avec des dentelles brodées d'acier. %%
%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶2334, Mon Journal t.9 p.162 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
A gown of Oriental stuff, wool, silk and gold, striped crosswise, shaped like a biblical shift, cut square at the neck in the Oriental manner and gathered at the waist by an angular belt so that it could hang very low; red satin shoes and a necklace of three rows of pearls... false, but nobody knows it. Described, the outfit sounds outlandish, but on me it was only slightly piquant, and majestically simple and graceful. Nadine put on a superb black gown with lace embroidered in steel.

%% 067.0614 %%
%% M. Bashkirtseff me trouva trop belle et ce fut une autre histoire, il eut peur que je parusse trop différente des dames de Poltava, et il me *supplia* cette fois de me mettre autrement, lui qui m'avait priée de m'habiller ainsi à Harkoff. %%

M. Bashkirtseff found me too beautiful, and that made another fuss: he was afraid I would look too different from the ladies of Poltava, and this time he *begged* me to dress otherwise, he who had asked me to dress like this in Kharkoff.

We arrived halfway through the concert. I went in on the arm of that horrid man, head high and with the air of a woman certain of being admired. Nadine, Paul and Pacha followed. I walked past Mme Abaza without greeting her, and we took our places in the front row beside her.

One can tell a success of extravagance from a real one. I was rather proud, and I looked at the curs, my elbow propped on the arm of my chair, playing with my pearl necklace, which I nibbled so that people would believe my teeth did not suffer by the comparison.
