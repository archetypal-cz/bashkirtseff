We were hardly back at the house when it was the turn of my aunts — that is to say, of the Princess — still on the subject of Maman's letter, to talk, and to give my father advice which, without being exactly insulting, was very disagreeable to hear from the third room; they were talking loudly. I might perhaps have liked to walk in among them and say impertinent things, but I contented myself with smiling; they were playing straight into my hands for the evening when I would talk with my father alone. Before, I had only Mme Tutcheff's despotism to reproach him with. So she is not the only one who rules you! And you dared to claim that all those things which I maintained came from other minds than yours were your own! And you dare deny that you are under the most despotic tutelage! You dare claim that you are your own master! When you are a miserable nought, a cipher — not even that! And I who talked to you, who wore myself out with persuasion! And I who took a dummy for a man! And I who thought I was acting, making headway! And I who built castles… on sand! Truly, it grieves me to see that I took an interest in all these things in vain, that I raised an edifice on a floating island, on a volcanic whim! They thought I was gaining influence over you, and they are beginning to turn you away from me. They are too clever to do it point-blank; they go about it with art; they say to you: “What a charming daughter you have there, Constantin! She has turned the whole house upside down, she is a real demon of grace and wit! *You yourself acknowledge her sway.*” And since weak men have a horror of being caught in the act, you will do everything in the world to prove the contrary of what they take for submission. Oh! they have chosen the true, the right method.

Then I declared that I could not go upstairs unless I was carried. The two Pauls carried me, and I was hardly in my room when I called Amalia and, throwing a fur cloak over my shoulders, rushed off to the red house, where I busied myself finishing the decoration of Paul's room until one in the morning. While hammering in nails I amused myself dumbfounding Pacha, who held the candle for me. He saw me back to my rooms, and on the way I wrung from him phrases such as this: “I came only for you,” and others less strong.
