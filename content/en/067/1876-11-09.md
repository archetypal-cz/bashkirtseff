%% Mon séjour ici m'aura du moins servi à connaître la littérature splendide de mon pays. Mais de quoi parlent ces poètes et ces écrivains? De *là-bas.* %%
# Thursday, 9 November 1876 (28 October)
At the very least, my stay here will have served to acquaint me with the splendid literature of my country. But what do these poets and writers speak of? Of *over there.*

%% Et d'abord citons Gogol notre étoile humoristique. %%
And first, let us cite Gogol, our star of humour.[^1]

[^1]: Nikolai Gogol (1809–1852), the great Russian writer; his novella *Rome* (1842) contains an ecstatic description of the city that moved Marie to tears.
