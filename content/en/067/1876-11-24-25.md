Until the evening things went tolerably enough, but suddenly a very serious, very measured, very honest conversation was entered upon concerning my future. %% 2eme - Nous l'avons trouvée. %%
%% 1er - Je te l'ai donnée ? %%

%% 1er - Et tu l'as prise ? %%
%% 2eme. je l'ai prise. %%
%% 1er. où est-elle donc ? %%
%% 2eme. Quoi ? %%
%% 1er - La pelisse ! %%
%% 2eme- Quelle pelisse ? %%
Two peasants:
1st — We were walking together on the high road.
2nd — Yes, we were walking.
1st — We found a fur pelisse?
2nd — We found one.
1st — I gave it to you?
2nd — You gave it to me?
1st — You took it?
2nd — I took it.
1st — Where is it?
2nd — What?
1st — The pelisse!
2nd — What pelisse?
1st — But we were walking on the high road?
2nd — Yes.
1st — We found a pelisse.
2nd — We found one.
1st — I gave it to you?
2nd — You gave it to me.
1st — And you took it?
2nd — I took it.
1st — Where is it, then?
2nd — What?
1st — The pelisse!
2nd — What pelisse?
