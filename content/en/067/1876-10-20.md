# Friday, 20 October 1876 (8 October)
%% A huit heures du matin, par un temps gris, et la terre noire légèrement poudrée de neige comme la figure de Mme Boutowsky, nous sommes déjà en chasse. %%
%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶1885, Mon Journal t.9 p.135 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
At eight in the morning, in grey weather, with the black earth lightly powdered with snow like Mme Boutowsky's face, we are already out hunting.

Michel has brought his pack of greyhounds and some *rossrin*. We were barely in the fields when I got on horseback, without taking off my pelisse, which I tied round my waist with my strap. I was given three dogs on a leash, and I stuck a long dagger in my belt for greater effect.
