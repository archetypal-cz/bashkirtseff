# Friday, 10 November 1876 (29 October)
There is a piece of my father's trickery to recount: he offered to pay for my journey, but in so incomprehensible a form that I could not even understand what he meant, and as I refused — not knowing what I was refusing — he cried out that since I did not want it: "Tant pis."[^1]

He is a clever rogue — though not clever, since I call him a rogue. I played again — what else was I to do! I spent a good hour translating Gogol, but this living photograph of Rome excites me too much for me to apply myself to it. Mme de Staël's Rome seems affected and almost feeble.[^2]
