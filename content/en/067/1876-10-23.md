%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶1919, Mon Journal t.9 p.137 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
The journey, which began with the tears of the two sisters at the sight of the family vault and with utter dejection on my part — the journey was merry; the weeping at the hour of leaving the paternal roof brought on something like a general outpouring, and Pacha exclaimed that he was madly in love, with all the exaltation such a case entails. “I swear it is true,” he cried, “but I will not say with whom.”
