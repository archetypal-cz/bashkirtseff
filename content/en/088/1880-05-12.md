# Wednesday, 12 May 1880

%% Hier enfin à huit heures du soir maman et Dina sont parties. J'aurais pleuré mais nous sommes arrivés dix minutes avant le départ, on était si pressé que je ne me suis pas attendrie. %%
Yesterday, finally, at eight in the evening, Maman and Dina left. I would have cried, but we arrived ten minutes before departure — we were so rushed that I did not grow sentimental.

Anitchkoff, Pelikan, and Soutzo, naturally. The poor devil sympathises with the grief we may feel and looks at me tenderly... Yesterday evening at the Salon, he spent his time gazing at me like a soul in torment. If he is not in love, he affects a love that everyone can see.

From the station, we go to join Rosita and her governess at *Giroflé-Girofla*. Soutzo escorts us there, places himself at our disposal, begs us to make use of him — a man of the family... and all this with a very discreet simplicity.

"Shall I come to fetch you?"

%% 088.0155 %%
%% Non, merci. %%
"No, thank you."

Well, as M. de Miranda had not come, I was wondering awkwardly how we would get away at the end, when, the curtain down, the door of the box opens and Soutzo appears.

%% 088.0157 %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
%% Eh bien, cela m'a fait quelque chose. J'en suis restée un instant clouée au sol, surprise et presque touchée. %%
Well, it did something to me. I stood rooted to the spot for an instant, surprised and almost moved.

%% 088.0158 %%
%% J'espère bien, lui dis-je à notre porte, que vous n'oserez pas entrer à cette heure. %%
"I certainly hope," I said to him at our door, "that you will not dare to come in at this hour."

%% 088.0159 %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
%% [#Soutzo](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/SOUTZO.md) %%
%% Je ne voulais pas entrer mais puisque... j'entre mais après avoir pris quelque chose je quitte brusquement la salle à manger pour éviter le baise-main, du reste je n'étais peut-être pas sans penser qu'il viendrait me dire bonsoir à la bibliothèque mais il était trop tard et je suis allée me coucher. Voici sept heures du soir et le voilà qui arrive. %%
"I did not mean to come in, but since..." In he comes; but after we have taken some refreshment I leave the dining room abruptly to avoid the hand-kissing. Then again, perhaps I was not without the thought that he would come and say goodnight to me in the library — but it was too late, and I went to bed. Now it is seven in the evening, and here he comes.

%% 088.0160 %%
%% Sur quel pied est-il reçu ? %%
On what footing is he received?

%% [#Alexis](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/ALEXIS.md) [#Bojidar](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/BOJIDAR.md) [#Soutzo](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/SOUTZO.md) [#Princess_Karageorgevitch](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/PRINCESS_KARAGEORGEVITCH.md) %%

Why, like Alexis or Bojidar, but with perhaps a little more ceremony; and besides, there is no mother Karageorgevitch, who is a dangerous madwoman.

%% Ce soir j'ai voulu rire un peu, je voulais lui tendre la main, très sérieuse et quand il voudrait la porter à ses lèvres, ne pas la retirer. Mais l'idée de la tête qu'il ferait m'a fait en imagination un si drôle d'effet que la cérémonie s'est accomplie en riant et que je lui ai avoué la cause de ma gaieté folle. De cette façon il verra la valeur que j'accorde à cette faveur. Tchoumakoff est arrivé pendant que je riais aux éclats alors le petit est allé jouer avec ma tante et je suis restée jusqu'à onze heures à causer peinture. %%
This evening I wanted to laugh a little. I wanted to extend my hand to him, very seriously, and when he would raise it to his lips, not withdraw it. But the thought of the face he would make had, in imagination, such a comical effect on me that the ceremony was performed laughing, and I confessed to him the cause of my wild gaiety. In this way he will see the value I place on this favour. Tchoumakoff arrived while I was laughing uproariously, so the little one went off to play a game with my aunt, and I stayed until eleven talking about painting.
