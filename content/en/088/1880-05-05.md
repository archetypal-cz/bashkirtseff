%% 2026-03-09T11:00:00 TR: "mievrerie" — insipidity, pretty weakness; painting term for over-delicate work. %%
Morot's *The Good Samaritan* is a stunning piece of painting. Bonnat's *Job*, which is broad, well-drawn, and well-painted, looks like mere prettiness after the Morot. We run into Gavini and Kiki and the family, and it all ends with a visit to Caillas's atelier. I am pretty. As for my painting, people look at it a fair amount, for a picture placed in the gallery.
