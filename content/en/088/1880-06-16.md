# Wednesday, 16 June 1880

%% 088.0773 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Art_world](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_WORLD.md) %%
%% [#Saint_Marceaux](../../_original/_glossary/people/religious/SAINT_MARCEAUX.md) %%
%% Nous devions aller chez Saint Marceaux avec Mlle Graham, mais je ne l'avais pas prévenue, elle était absente, j'ai prévenu le grand homme qui nous attendait. %%
We were to have gone to Saint-Marceaux's with Mlle Graham, but I had not let her know beforehand, she was out, and I sent word to the great man, who was expecting us.
%% 2026-03-09T16:00:00 TR: "le grand homme" — the great man; ironic yet admiring; Saint-Marceaux is a celebrated sculptor whom Marie genuinely reveres. %%

This evening Soutzo was to have accompanied Graham and me to a lecture by Hubertine Auclert (the apostle of women's rights), but it did not take place, and we go to the Salon at eight o'clock where I run into Saint-Marceaux.
%% 2026-03-09T16:00:00 TR: "l'apôtre des droits de la femme" — Marie's own gloss on Auclert; Hubertine Auclert (1848-1914) was France's leading suffragist. Kept "apostle" as it preserves the slightly mock-solemn tone Marie uses. %%

%% Echange de banalités et je lui dis bêtement: vous ne venez jamais nous voir. - Je suis si occupé ! - Voyez-vous faire de ces reproches c'est stupide maintenant il va me semblé que Saint Marceaux sera ennuyé de me rencontrer. Non, voyez-vous il faut que j'arrive à quelque chose. Il faut que des hommes comme Saint Marceaux voient quelqu'un en moi. Attention, il ne me reste que quelques mois avant de faire mon Salon. Et moi qui pensais aller à Pétersbourg pour me marier ! Non, je reste ici, je travaille et ce n'est que l'hiver 1881-1882 que j'irai. Je n'aurai que vingt-trois, et pour le monde que vingt-deux. Que diable, cela pourra encore aller. %%
We exchange pleasantries, and I say foolishly: you never come to see us. — I am so busy! — You see, making reproaches like that is stupid; now I shall feel that Saint-Marceaux is annoyed whenever he meets me. No, you see, I must amount to something. Men like Saint-Marceaux must see someone in me. Careful — I have only a few months left before exhibiting at the Salon. And to think I meant to go to Petersburg to get married! No, I am staying here, I shall work, and I will not go until the winter of 1881–1882. I will be only twenty-three, and in the world's eyes only twenty-two. What the deuce, that will still do.
%% 2026-03-09T16:00:00 TR: "faire mon Salon" — to exhibit at the Salon; rendered as "exhibiting at the Salon." "pour le monde que vingt-deux" — for society only twenty-two; Marie habitually understated her age by one year. %%
