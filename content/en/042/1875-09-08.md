# Wednesday, 8 September 1875

My aunt breaks my mirror — a sign of death or great misfortune. A few months before Romanoff's death, the mirror in his travelling case shattered in the railway carriage.

%% 042.0003 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Dina](../../_original/_glossary/people/core/DINA.md) [#Etienne](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/ETIENNE.md) [#Oncle_Emile](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/ONCLE_EMILE.md) %%

%% A la villa Baquis il y eut deux glaces cassées dans ma chambre, et deux morts dans la famille, le frère de Dina Etienne, et Emile mon oncle. %%
At the Villa Baquis there were two broken mirrors in my room, and two deaths in the family — Dina's brother Étienne, and Émile, my uncle.

%% 042.0004 %%
%% Quatrième présage ce matin, présage double, car je me souviens de mon rêve à Schlangenbad. %%
A fourth omen this morning — a double omen, for I remember my dream at Schlangenbad.

And then there is the mad dash to Florence: two women alone, and I shall suffer a thousand torments if I miss any of the parties.

%% 042.0012 %%

I am furious and wretched at leaving without my dresses, without anything at all.

%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
%% 2026-01-30T17:30:00 LAN: "mise en gros bleu" - dressed in coarse/dark blue %%

%% J'ai passé mon temps misérablement, j'étais laide presque tout le temps. J'étais mise en gros bleu et chapeau noir, ce qui me va mal. Ma figure n'aime que les teintes claires. Je me suis montrée à mon vilain avantage. C'est abominable. Ce sont surtout mes robes qui me tourmentent. %%
%% 2026-03-05T10:00:00 TR: "a mon vilain avantage" — Marie's characteristic oxymoron; rendered as "to my worst possible advantage" to preserve the self-ironizing quirk %%
I spent my time there miserably — I was plain almost the whole time. I wore dark blue wool and a black hat, which does not suit me at all. My face demands pale, clear tones. I showed myself to my worst possible advantage. It is abominable. It is above all my dresses that torment me.

At last we leave Paris of my heart, my beloved city — at eleven o'clock I am weeping and my aunt is suffering. Ah! how I do not want to die!

%% 042.0015 %%
%% [#Mortality](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MORTALITY.md) %%
%% Mais non, je ne mourrai pas. Puisque c'est à moi que viennent les présages. %%
%% 2026-03-05T10:00:00 TR: "Puisque c'est a moi que viennent les presages" — Marie's superstitious logic: if the omens come to me, I am the one who sees them, not the one who dies. Kept the ambiguity. %%
But no, I shall not die. It is to me that the omens come.

%% 042.0016 %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
%% [#Mortality](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MORTALITY.md) %%
%% 2026-01-30T17:30:00 LAN: "perdre une dent" - folk superstition: dreaming of losing a tooth means death of relative %%
%% J'ai rêvé que je perdais une dent, ce qui veut dire mort d'un parent. Oh ! comme cela me console. Car j'aime mieux voir mourir n'importe qui que de mourir moi-même. Dans ces choses là, on est égoïste. %%
%% 2026-03-05T10:00:00 TR: Tooth-loss dream superstition — well-known in European folklore, no footnote needed %%
I dreamed that I was losing a tooth, which means the death of a relative. Oh! how that consoles me. For I would far rather see anyone else die than die myself. In such matters, one is selfish.

Good God, how I detest railways!

My aunt, seeing me smile, seizes the moment to make a few jokes about the aforesaid personage, and I laugh. It is not I who laugh — it is my face.

%% 042.0020 %%

I am wretched. These dresses are driving me mad.
