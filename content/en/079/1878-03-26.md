# Tuesday, 26 March 1878
%% [#Etienne](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/ETIENNE.md) [#Alexandre](../../_original/_glossary/people/core/ALEXANDRE.md) %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% 2026-02-02T13:00:00 LAN: "toilette" - 1870s: the act of dressing/grooming, NOT bathroom %%
%% A sept heures et demie j'ai bu une demi-tasse de lait, puis j'ai travaillé et comme il n'était que temps d'aller à la gare lorsque ma toilette (après l'atelier) était faite, je suis partie sans déjeuner avec Etienne, sa femme et Alexandre. %%
At half past seven I drank half a cup of milk, then worked, and as it was only just time to go to the station when I had finished dressing (after the atelier), I set off with Etienne, his wife and Alexandre without having lunch.

%% 2026-10-01T18:00:00 RED: RESOLVED seam checked (079.DROPPED-0106 joined to 079.0098) against _original; English reads as one continuous paragraph, nothing duplicated or lost at the join. %%
We did not return until eight o'clock; I had insisted on staying to see the end of M. d'Egremont's speech, which he read from a million disconnected pages — and the more he read, the more of these pages there appeared to be. I wager no one heard a word.

> Barbarian! May these daily letters cause you a tenth of the fatigue that today's session has caused me! What have I done to you, cruel man, to make you choose such a day to send me tickets?

%% 079.0101 %%
%% kind: letter source="Lettre de Marie à Paul de Cassagnac, 26 mars 1878" %%
%% > Ma petite tante qui était avec moi était si exaspérée de voir un député pendant près de trois heures sans bouger de la tribune qu'elle a failli être prise de folie furieuse et se faire en wagon une mauvaise affaire avec deux vieux sénateurs. %%
> My little aunt, who was with me, was so exasperated at watching a deputy hold the tribune for nearly three hours without budging that she came close to being seized with a raving fury and getting into an unpleasant altercation in the railway carriage with two old senators.
