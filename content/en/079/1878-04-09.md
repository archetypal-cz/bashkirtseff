# Tuesday, 9 April 1878
I am rarely unwell during the uncleanness natural to women — two or three times at most — and usually things pass as though nothing were happening. But today I fortunately worked in the morning; as for the afternoon I stayed in bed, and so suffered a great deal. It lasted two hours, after which I got up almost glad to have suffered — it is so good afterwards; one is so pleased to laugh at the pain. How good it is to be young! In twenty years' time it would lay me up for a whole day.
