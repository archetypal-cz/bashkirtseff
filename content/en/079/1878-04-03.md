# Wednesday, 3 April 1878
The fête was very amusing, but I did not enjoy myself, despite a sensational costume. It was a piece of dark oriental fabric that I had admirably draped so as to conceal me completely. A very long black train trimmed with old-gold braid in harmony with the upper part; pointed black shoes embroidered with gold; black hair and a black-and-gold gauze veil; then ==the half-mask==[^1].

To my misfortune I spoke to the Boyds, and they could do nothing better than betray us to everyone. I am quite annoyed, for once that I was truly unrecognisable. Everyone told me so, besides, and but for those idiots whom I detest, all would have been charming. When one goes as a family, it is never any good. I was not in good form, but I was courted; in any case, whether I am witty or not, there are few things I love as much as being masked. This freedom to speak enchants me — as for the liberties anyone might like to take with me, I put a stop to them so promptly that it is a delight.
