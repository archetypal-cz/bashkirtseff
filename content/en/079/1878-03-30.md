# Saturday, 30 March 1878
By way of contrast with the Opera, I put together a *toilette* and a bearing that are purity itself; besides, Count Multedo is too well bred to let anything show. Apart from him there are Philippini, de Beaurepaire, Goldsmid. Mmes de Mouzay, de Fayet, and Berthe. Fine company!
