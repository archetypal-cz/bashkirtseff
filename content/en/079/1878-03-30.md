# Saturday, 30 March 1878
By way of contrast to the Opera, I compose a dress and manner that are purity itself; besides, the Comte Multedo is too well bred to betray anything. Apart from him there are Philippini, de Beaurepaire, Goldsmid. Mmes de Mouzay, de Fayet, and Berthe. Fine company!
