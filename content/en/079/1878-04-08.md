# Monday, 8 April 1878
Multedo has something of the brute in his nose. During "meals" at the atelier I am reading *The Lily of the Valley*[^1]. I am halfway through the book and I do not like it. When Mme de Mortsauf speaks of her aunt I want to laugh.

The by-elections are a complete debacle! Republicans have been elected all along the line. *M. Gent is elected.* That gives you a good picture of the country's disintegration. And in the Gers at Auch they have elected some David or other instead of M. Peyrusse, for whose candidature that idiot Cassagnac — who had gone expressly to the Gers — squandered a great many speeches, all in vain!
