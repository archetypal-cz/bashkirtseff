%% Maman a dit qu'elle est dégoûtée de me voir rougir pour Audiffret. Moi aussi cela me dégoûte mais est-ce ma faute ? Je ne sais pour quel péché je rougis pour cet homme. %%
Tuesday, 20 October 1874

%% 024.0387 %%
Maman said she is disgusted to see me blush for Audiffret. I too am disgusted by it, but is it my fault? I do not know for what sin I blush for this man.

%% 024.0388 %%
It rains all day. I go out for one hour in a closed carriage with Dina and do not open my mouth.

%% 024.0389 %%
Georges continues his drinking and I am indignant. Ah! The lessons worry me; they are not yet arranged and my conscience is not at ease. This instant I go up to my study to work on the arrangement of the hours.
