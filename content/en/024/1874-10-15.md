Thursday, 15 October 1874
Biasini was at our house and we went to see the new house of Comte Starzinsky; it is not quite finished. Biasini has talent — that is what I say after having seen this house. He himself was proud of his work. It is grand, it is bright, it is spacious. All the reception rooms communicate. In short, this house is beautiful enough to make me wish to have it instead of ours, and to make me desire with ardour the building of our own drawing rooms.
%% Il pleut, c'est une preuve que la saison avance. A la musique rien excepté les Durand, Lucie Durand est devenue élégante après le mariage de l'autre. Je crois que Saëtone joue le rôle de médiateur en ce moment et amènera une réconciliation. %%
It is raining — proof that the season is advancing. At the music, nothing except the Durands. Lucie Durand has become elegant since her sister's marriage. They would dearly like Emile d'Audiffret, but some quarrel between old Emile, his wife, and old Mme Durand has separated them. I believe Saetone is playing the role of mediator at present and will bring about a reconciliation.
