%% 039.0166 %%
%% [#Saetone](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/SAETONE.md) %%
%% Gras, rouge, un peu boiteux, %%
%% Protecteur des débutants %%
%% Toujours content, toujours joyeux, %%
%% Toujours partout papillonnant. %%
Fat, ruddy, slightly lame,
Patron of beginners,
Always content, always gay,
Fluttering everywhere at all times.

%% 039.0167 %%
%% [#Saetone](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/SAETONE.md) %%

%% Et fait le tour des loges %%
%% Exposant sa main d'albâtre %%

When he enters a theatre
And makes the rounds of the boxes
Displaying his alabaster hand
He deserves the highest praise.

%% 039.0170 %%
%% [#Danis](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/DANIS.md) %%
%% [#Saetone](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/SAETONE.md) %%
%% Plus timide, plus maigre et petit %%
%% Apparaît Arthur Danis %%
%% Aux faux cols irréprochables. %%
%% Tous deux, ils sont adorables. %%
More timid, more lean and small,
Arthur Danis appears
With irreproachable detachable collars.[^39.170.1]
Both of them are adorable.

[^39.170.1]: *Faux cols* — detachable collars, a fashionable men's accessory in the 1870s.

%% 039.0173 %%
%% [#Comte_dAspremont](../../_original/_glossary/people/aristocracy/COMTE_DASPREMONT.md) %%
%% Le comte d'Aspremont %%
%% Haut comme un mât de cocagne En guise de nez il a un mont Non, une chaîne de montagne. %%
The Comte d'Aspremont,
Tall as a greasy pole[^39.173.1] —
For a nose he has a mountain,
No, a whole mountain range.

[^39.173.1]: *Mât de cocagne* — a tall greased pole from the top of which prizes are suspended; a fairground contest.

%% 039.0178 %%

%% Timide et sincère, %%
%% Qui comme un âne brait; %%
%% Fils du préfet, on le tolère %%
Maurice Godard Decrais,
Timid and sincere,
Who brays like a donkey —
Son of the prefect, he is tolerated.
%% 2026-03-05T13:05:00 TR: "brait" — brays (donkey); "brays like a donkey" for his voice. "prefet" — prefect (regional government administrator); his social tolerance comes from his father's position. %%

%% 039.0179 %%
%% [#Godard](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/GODARD.md) %%
%% Il est candide et vertueux, %%
%% Son derrière imite %%
%% Les replis tortueux De l'hydre d'Hippolyte. %%
He is candid and virtuous —
His posterior imitates
The tortuous coils
Of Hippolytus's hydra.[^39.179.1]

[^39.179.1]: In Greek myth, Poseidon sent a sea-monster (*hydra* or sea-serpent) at the prayer of Theseus; it terrified Hippolytus's horses and caused his death. Marie deploys the classical reference for comic effect.
