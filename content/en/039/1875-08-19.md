# Thursday, 19 August 1875

%% 039.0126 %%
%% [#Maman](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/MAMAN.md) %%
%% [#Dreams](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/DREAMS.md) %%
%% [#Superstition](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/SUPERSTITION.md) %%
%% [#Mme_Howard](../../_original/_glossary/people/aristocracy/MME_HOWARD.md) %%
%% J'ai fait un rêve affreux, je perdais une dent. Signe de mort dans la famille, et hier j'ai rêvé la même chose qu'avant la lettre de Mme Howard. Mais le rêve d'aujourd'hui me bouleverse tellement qu'à six heures et demie je vais chez maman et reste dans son lit à causer. %%
I had a dreadful dream — I was losing a tooth. The sign of a death in the family;[^39.126.1] and yesterday I dreamed the same thing as I did before Madame Howard's letter. But today's dream upsets me so much that at half past six I go to Maman and stay in her bed to chat.

[^39.126.1]: In 19th-century folk belief widespread in Marie's circle, dreaming of losing a tooth was an omen of death in the family.

Who then is going to die?

I dreamed the same thing before Uncle Emile's death. This time it is one of the front teeth.

I am anxious about our winter. How will all of it be arranged?

Truly I am anxious and tormented.

%% 039.0134 %%
%% [#Emotions](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/EMOTIONS.md) %%
%% [#Nice](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/NICE.md) %%
%% Que je serais furieuse si on pensait autre chose. J'étais surtout enragée à Nice, je m'attendais à une victoire éclatante et je n'eus qu'une petite bêtise. %%
How furious I should be if anyone thought otherwise. I was above all enraged at Nice — I was expecting a dazzling victory, and I had nothing but a trifling silliness.

%% 039.0135 %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
%% [#Emile_d_Audiffret](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/EMILE_D_AUDIFFRET.md) %%
%% Oh ! mais j'étais furieuse. Je m'étonne comme cela se fait, je le trouve beau et je ne l'aime pas, mais pas du tout. Surtout de loin il me semble vilain. %%
Oh! but I was furious. I marvel at how this comes about — I find him handsome, yet I do not love him, not at all. From a distance especially he seems ugly to me.

%% 039.0136 %%
%% [#Health](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/HEALTH.md) %%
%% A dîner je me trouve presque mal, je ne sais ce que c'est, la chaleur sans doute. %%
At dinner I feel almost faint — I do not know what it is, the heat no doubt.

At three o'clock we go to Schwalbach — Good Lord, what a difference!

At Schwalbach one sees living creatures, one promenades, one dresses up, one goes to the spring, there are several bands, charming walks, a lake, a gallery, a Kurhaus — in a word it is a charming spa town. I dare not say what I think of it: after Schlangenbad, it seems to me a paradise and its inhabitants angels.
