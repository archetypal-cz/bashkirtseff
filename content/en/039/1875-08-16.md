%% # Lundi 16 août 1875 %%
# Monday, 16 August 1875

%% 039.0028 %%
%% [#Schlangenbad](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/SCHLANGENBAD.md) [#Wiesbaden](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/WIESBADEN.md) [#Maman](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/MAMAN.md) %%
%% Hier il y avait des courses à Wiesbaden, aujourd'hui de même. Nous n'allons pas (maman a ses affaires). Je n'y tiens pas, je crois qu'il n'y aura personne. %%
Yesterday there were races at Wiesbaden, today again. We do not go (Maman has her own concerns). I do not care to go — I believe there will be nobody there.
%% 2026-03-05T12:10:00 TR: "courses" — horse races. "maman a ses affaires" — LAN: euphemistic for menstrual period; "has her own concerns" preserves the ellipsis without spelling it out. "Je n'y tiens pas" — LAN: "I don't care to go"; natural English. "personne" = nobody of social consequence, not literally empty. %%

%% 039.0029 %%
%% [#Carlo_Hamilton](../../_original/_glossary/people/aristocracy/CARLO_HAMILTON.md) %%
%% Pauvre Carlo, gentil Carlo ! C'est abominable, et si j'étais heureuse moi-même je pleurerais. %%
Poor Carlo, dear Carlo! It is abominable — and if I were happy myself, I would weep.
%% 2026-03-05T12:11:00 TR: "gentil" — LAN: here means kind/nice (affectionate warmth), not just handsome. "Dear Carlo" captures the personal warmth better than "sweet" or "nice". The conditional clause "if I were happy myself" is a striking admission preserved intact. %%

%% 039.0030 %%
%% En robe de chambre, la tête enveloppée d'un voile blanc, je prends un bain à huit heures. %%
In my dressing gown, my head wrapped in a white veil, I take a bath at eight o'clock.
%% 2026-03-05T12:12:00 TR: "robe de chambre" — dressing gown/morning wrapper. "la tete enveloppee d'un voile blanc" — head wrapped in a white veil, part of the bathing costume to protect the hair from mineral water at Schlangenbad. %%

%% 039.0031 %%
%% [#Fourth_wall](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/FOURTH_WALL.md) %%
%% On dîne à une heure, détestable habitude pour ceux qui mangent sérieusement. On se sent lourd et propre à rien. %%
Dinner is at one o'clock — a detestable custom for those who eat in earnest. One feels heavy and good for nothing.
%% 2026-03-05T12:13:00 TR: "diner a une heure" — the main midday meal at 1 p.m. was the spa timetable. "propre a rien" — LAN: "good for nothing, useless"; "good for nothing" is the idiom. %%

%% 039.0032 %%
%% [#Machenka](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/MACHENKA.md) [#Dina](../../_original/_glossary/people/core/DINA.md) [#Wiesbaden](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/WIESBADEN.md) [#German](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/GERMAN.md) [#French](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/FRENCH.md) %%
%% A quatre heures, moi, Machenka et Dina allons à Wiesbaden à la recherche d'une voiture et de deux chevaux convenables pour conduire moi-méme. Mais chez ces maudits Allemands on ne trouve rien. Nous avons attendu une heure devant une écurie avec une foule de gamins et gamines Allemands, sales et laids qui nous regardaient et quand nous partions se mirent à crier hourra ! Et Dina et Machenka de rire disant que c'est en l'honneur de Ma Majesté. %%
At four o'clock, Machenka, Dina and I go to Wiesbaden in search of a carriage and two horses suitable for me to drive myself. But among these cursed Germans one finds nothing. We waited an hour in front of a stable surrounded by a crowd of German boys and girls, dirty and ugly, who stared at us, and when we left broke into cries of *==hourra!==*[^39.32.1] And Dina and Machenka burst out laughing, saying it was in honour of Her Majesty.
%% 2026-03-05T12:14:00 TR: "ces maudits Allemands" — "these cursed Germans"; casual national prejudice, preserved. "gamins et gamines" — "boys and girls" (street urchins); slight pejorative implied. "Ma Majeste" — Marie's recurring self-ironic title; "Her Majesty" in English captures the mock-regal register. "hourra" — German children's cry in French phonetics; marked as foreign and footnoted. %%

[^39.32.1]: *In German in the original* (hurrah).

%% 039.0033 %%
%% [#Spa](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/SPA.md) %%
%% Nous retournons fort tard, par un beau clair de lune qui me rappelle Spa et nous parlons de Spa, puis des hommes, puis des mariages et enfin des enlèvements. Plusieurs fois les tarots m'ont prédit que je serai enlevée. %%
We return very late, by beautiful moonlight that reminds me of Spa, and we talk of Spa, then of men, then of marriages, and finally of abductions. Several times the tarot cards have predicted that I shall be carried off.
%% 2026-03-05T12:15:00 TR: "enlevements" — LAN: abductions/elopements; "abductions" captures the dramatic framing Marie prefers. "les tarots m'ont predit que je serai enlevee" — the tarot prediction is taken seriously enough to be reported without irony. %%

%% 039.0034 %%
%% [#Maman](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/MAMAN.md) %%
%% [#Religion](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/RELIGION.md) %%
%% Et tous les jours je prie maman d'être sur ses gardes et de ne plus se plaindre après puisque la voilà prévenue. %%
And every day I warn Maman to be on her guard and not to complain afterwards, since she has been forewarned.
%% 2026-03-05T12:16:00 TR: "je prie maman d'etre sur ses gardes" — "I warn Maman to be on her guard"; the verb "prie" (beg/urge) combined with the humorous context reads better as "warn" than "beg." %%

%% 039.0035 %%
%% On ne pourra m'enlever que par force, je ne consentirai jamais. %%
I can only be carried off by force — I shall never consent.

%% 039.0036 %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
%% [#Marriage](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MARRIAGE.md) %%
%% Pourquoi faire ? Je ne voudrais jamais d'un homme avec lequel le mariage ne serait pas honorable, brillant même. Or si l'homme est bien pourquoi fuir, se cacher ? Qui m'empêche de l'épouser. Et puis on ne se marie qu'une fois pour la première fois, il faut donc faire autant de parade et d'éclat que possible et non simplifier. %%
What would be the point? I would never want a man with whom marriage would not be honourable — brilliant, even. Well, if the man is good, why flee, why hide? Who is there to prevent me from marrying him? Besides, one only marries for the first time once — one must therefore make as much parade and splendour as possible, and not simplify.
%% 2026-03-05T12:17:00 TR: "brillant" — "brilliant" in the sense of socially splendid, glittering; preserved as period term. "on ne se marie qu'une fois pour la premiere fois" — the tautology is the joke; "one only marries for the first time once" preserves the wit. "parade et eclat" — "parade and splendour" captures the desire for pomp. %%

%% 039.0037 %%
%% On dira que l'enlèvement est un plus grand éclat et plus romanesque. %%
One will say that an abduction is even more splendid and more *romanesque*.[^39.37.1]

[^39.37.1]: *Romanesque* in the literary sense — novelistic, dramatic, in the manner of a romance or adventure novel — rather than merely "romantic" in the sentimental sense.

%% 039.0038 %%
%% [#Mortality](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MORTALITY.md) %%
%% D'accord, mais on peut se faire enlever après aussi bien qu'avant et autant de fois qu'on veut, tandis que la cérémonie des fleurs d'oranger est unique comme la mort. %%
Granted, but one can be carried off after marriage just as well as before, and as many times as one likes — whereas the ceremony of the orange blossoms is unique, like death.
%% 2026-03-05T12:18:00 TR: "fleurs d'oranger" — orange blossoms, traditional bridal flowers; kept as evocative image rather than translating as "wedding ceremony." "unique comme la mort" — the dark wit of comparing wedding and death as once-in-a-lifetime events; preserved. %%

%% 039.0039 %%
%% [#Ma_tante](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/MA_TANTE.md) [#Russian](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/RUSSIAN.md) [#French](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/FRENCH.md) %%
%% A dix heures nous rentrons et on me lit la lettre de ma tante. Elle écrit en russe mais je parlerai français et ne dirai que ce qui m'intéresse particulièrement. %%
At ten o'clock we come in, and my aunt's letter is read aloud to me. She writes in Russian, but I shall speak in French and say only what particularly interests me.
%% 2026-03-05T12:19:00 TR: "on me lit" — the letter is read aloud to her (confirming her limited Russian reading). Marie's editorial note about what she will and will not include is characteristic self-awareness. %%

Can you imagine? But I shall believe only my own eyes — this naughty boy is a prankster and capricious.
