# Saturday, 10 December 1881

Sunday, 11 December 1881

I am better — almost no more fever.

Julian came this evening. He is truly good, and very amusing besides. We have the terrible plan, as you know... But I don't much believe in all that, though it would be good for Dina, who with her hundred thousand or a hundred and fifty thousand francs dowry cannot hope to marry in France. But the horrible Amélie — if the matter depended on me, I hesitated; on the one hand to make one woman wretched, but on the other to saddle Julian with that bone structure, those seven-league legs... Dark hair grows on her bosom like a man's.
