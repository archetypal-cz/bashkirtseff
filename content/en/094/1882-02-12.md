# Sunday, 12 February 1882
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Mme_Patton](../../_original/_glossary/people/aristocracy/MME_PATTON.md) %%
%% [#Religion](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/RELIGION.md) %%
%% Maman et papa sont alles a l'eglise ou Mme Patton est venue leur parler avec une extreme amabilite. %%
Maman and Papa went to church, where Mme Patton came to speak to them with extreme amiability.

%% 094.0183 %%
%% [#Consul](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/CONSUL.md) %%
%% Puis papa est allé chez le Consul. %%
Then Papa went to the Consul's.

%% 094.0184 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Health](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/HEALTH.md) %%
%% 2026-03-11T10:00:00 TR: "une ordonnance" → "a ruling." "il n'y avait pas meme lieu a proces" → "there was not even grounds for a trial." %%
%% Le procès victorieusement terminé, c'est-à-dire que l'on a rendu une ordonnance disant que l'enquête a démontré qu'il n'y avait pas même lieu à procès. %%
The lawsuit victoriously concluded — that is to say, a ruling has been handed down stating that the inquiry has established that there were not even grounds for a trial.

%% 094.0185 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% 2026-03-11T10:00:00 TR: "triompher un peu" → "triumph a little." "un effet formidable" → "a tremendous effect." %%
%% Et naturellement on va chez le Consul pour triompher un peu, bref ce changement heureux et les articles du "Figaro" et autres ont produit ici un effet formidable. %%
And naturally one goes to the Consul's to triumph a little — in short, this happy turn of events and the articles in the *Figaro* and elsewhere have produced a tremendous effect here.
