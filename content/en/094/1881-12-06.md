# Tuesday, 6 December 1881

Villevieille came yesterday — how very kind that is... I am always astonished when anyone shows me any interest... Perhaps this deep-down sense that I deserve nothing.

And this evening Julian came; Tchernitsky and Brisbane had also dined. Julian is very amusing; he stayed after everyone else. I should like to put him off the horrible Amélie and marry him to Dina... Why not? He has his decoration and earns a great deal of money.

But it is a chimera — firstly because the implacable Amélie is inescapable, and secondly because she would be too unhappy; and however odious she may be, I have not the heart... "to pierce hers."
