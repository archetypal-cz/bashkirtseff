%% Le comte de Bari, frere du roi de Naples qui a epouse une-coureuse a ce qu'on dit... %%
The Count de Bari, brother of the King of Naples, who married a woman of loose morals by all accounts — she was there the day before yesterday and will come tomorrow, Saturday being her day... She was met at the Bailleul's. Brisbane went to Tony's and to Breslau's, who asked news of her *beautiful* enemy, who appears to interest her greatly. And Tony said to Webb that I am gifted as no one else.
