# Wednesday, 15 February 1882

It is little by little that one's eyes open. Before, I saw only the drawing and the subject of pictures; now... Ah, now, if I could paint as I see, I should have talent. I see the landscape, I see and I love the landscape, the water, the air, the colour, the colour, the colour.
