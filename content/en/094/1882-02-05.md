[Words blacked out: We all go to the promenade] together — Papa, Maman, [words blacked out: the Princesse Eristoff,] Mme Gorpintchenko! Dina and the young couple. I present the miracle of a long, slim, supple figure, filled out where needed, *without a corset.* Everyone will say my corsetière is remarkable and that they have never seen a corset moulding the figure so well. Mme Tutcheff sees us and greets Papa very warmly; he barely responds. I turn away.
