# Tuesday, 29 November 1881

Well, it has been a fortnight now, and I have at least another fortnight ahead of me.

On n'a jamais eu une plus ingénieuse façon d'envelopper un homme de tous côtés. %%

Little Brisbane came to see me, then Villevieille — who has herself been ill. And Amélie! Julian is forever anxious for news of me, and the other evening she came running [two words blacked out:] to give him her report, that very same evening at the class. That girl will no doubt achieve her ends. No one has ever found a more ingenious way to wind herself around a man from every side.

%% 094.0020 %%
%% Madame Nachet m'apporte un bouquet de violettes aujourd'hui, je la reçois comme tout le monde car malgré la fièvre qui ne me quitte pas depuis quinze jours et une congestion pulmonaire du côté gauche, alias pleurésie, et deux vésicatoires, je ne capitule pas, je suis levée et me comporte comme une personne naturelle. Seulement la quinine me rend sourde, l'autre nuit j'ai pensé mourir de terreur n'entendant plus ma montre. Et il faudra en prendre encore et toujours. %%
%% 2026-10-02T19:11:03 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 3 of 9 of 094.0018, which held 9 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.14; tome14.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
Madame Nachet brings me a bouquet of violets today; I receive her as I do everyone, for despite the fever which has not left me for a fortnight and a pulmonary congestion on the left side — alias pleurisy — and two blister plasters, I do not capitulate. I am up and about and conduct myself as a person in ordinary health. Only the quinine is making me deaf; the other night I thought I would die of terror, hearing my watch no longer. And it will have to go on yet and always.

[word blacked out: Besides] I feel almost strong, and were it not that for a fortnight I have been unable to swallow anything, I would not feel my illness at all.

%% 094.0022 %%
%% C'est égal, mon travail, mon tableau, mon pauvre tableau. Nous sommes au 29 novembre. Je ne pourrai pas commencer avant fin décembre, en deux mois et demi jamais je n'aurai le temps. Quelle malchance et comme quand on est né malheureux il n'y a pas à lutter, voyez moi... La peinture semblait un refuge et voilà que je deviens sourde, d'où gêne affreuse avec les modèles, angoisses perpétuelles et impossibilité de faire des portraits à moins d'avouer et je n'ai pas encore ce courage. Puis cette maladie, impossible de travailler et obligée de rester enfermée un mois... Mais c'est par trop triste. %%
%% 2026-10-02T19:11:03 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 5 of 9 of 094.0018, which held 9 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.14; tome14.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
Even so — my work, my painting, my poor painting. We are the 29th of November. I shall not be able to begin before the end of December; in two and a half months I shall never have the time. What ill luck, and when one is born unhappy there is no fighting it — look at me. Painting seemed a refuge, and now I am going deaf, which means terrible embarrassment with the models, perpetual anxieties, and the impossibility of doing portraits unless I confess — and I have not yet the courage for that. Then this illness, unable to work, forced to stay shut in for a month... But it is too sad for words.

%% 094.0023 %%
%% Dina ne me quitte pas, elle est si gentille. %%
%% 2026-10-02T19:11:03 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 6 of 9 of 094.0018, which held 9 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.14; tome14.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
Dina does not leave my side; she is so good to me.

%% 094.0024 %%
%% Paul et sa femme sont arrivés hier. Les Gavini et Géry sont venus. Bojidar, Alexis. Et moi toujours entrain et me tirant des mauvais pas à force de courage et des blagues. %%
%% 2026-10-02T19:11:03 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 7 of 9 of 094.0018, which held 9 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.14; tome14.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
Paul and his wife arrived yesterday. The Gavinis and Géry came. Bojidar, Alexis. And I, always keeping my high spirits, extricating myself from difficult moments by sheer courage and jokes.

%% 094.0025 %%
%% Les médecins sont le sujet des plaisanteries à présent. Potain ne pouvant toujours être là m'envoie un docteur qui viendra tous les jours. Charcot [Mots noircis: au travail déjà a] envoyé un remplaçant mais tellement bête que je n'ai pu le souffrir et profitant de ma maladie je lui ai dit que je le déteste, qu'il m'ennuie, que c'est un vrai supplice de le voir etc. Naturellement je raconte tout cela à Potain et il me promet que le sien sera moins bête. Néanmoins ce brave homme avant d'entrer s'est adressé à ma tante en disant que M. Potain lui avait dit que je suis excessivement capricieuse et fantasque et qu'il voudrait bien savoir comment il faut m'aborder. %%
%% 2026-10-02T19:11:03 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 8 of 9 of 094.0018, which held 9 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.14; tome14.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
The doctors are the source of amusement now. Potain, being unable to come every day, sends me a doctor who will come daily. Charcot [words blacked out: at work already has] sent a replacement, but so stupid that I could not stand him, and taking advantage of my illness I told him I detested him, that he bored me, that it was a positive torment to see him, etc. Naturally I relate all of this to Potain, who promises me that his man will be less stupid. Nevertheless this worthy fellow, before entering, addressed himself to my aunt to say that M. Potain had warned him I was excessively capricious and wayward, and that he would very much like to know how one ought to approach me.

%% 094.0026 %%
%% Et ça m'amuse ! Car je joue la folle et profite de cet état pour débiter des insanités. %%
%% 2026-10-02T19:11:03 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 9 of 9 of 094.0018, which held 9 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.14; tome14.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
And it amuses me! For I play the madwoman and profit from this state to deliver absurdities.
