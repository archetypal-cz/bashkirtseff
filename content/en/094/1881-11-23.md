# Wednesday, 23 November 1881

And amid all of that, something like a guilty pleasure at the idea of seeing Nice again. [Blacked out: Wretched nature II and time wasted! More amateur walks, and I can find neither excuse nor consolation in myself. I who have prayed so much to God! Well, I can defy them as I did last winter — but if I often have weeks like this one... I did not have them last year. In the end I asked Charcot: shall I die if I stay? That is hardly likely.
