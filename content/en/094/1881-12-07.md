# Wednesday, 7 December 1881

Mme Gavini, her cousin and her niece have just left. A [Blacked out: evening?] spent deep in Corsica. Imagine — Berthe is coming back. After all her shipwrecks. No, I will not see her; she has never done me anything but harm; she is a liar, wicked, and not even amusing with all that.

To die then, to die here, in the midst of life, in good health — my life here is a tomb, but ill as I am I do not want to go away, and I know it is almost essential, but the thought tears me, despairs me! Hotels again, railway carriages again; here I do nothing, but that lasts twenty days, a month — whereas there one does not know where — exile, far from everything. No, No, No! I cry out No like someone who fears being forced to yield, who knows they will yield.
