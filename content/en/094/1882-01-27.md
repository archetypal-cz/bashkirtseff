# Friday, 27 January 1882

Gambetta has fallen — that is to say, he is no longer a minister — but that is nothing, in my view. Only observe what is happening here: the cowardice and bad faith of men. Those devouring Gambetta, the same intransigents, do not believe a word of all these idiotic accusations — dictatorship and so on.
