We encountered Cassagnac in a friend's carriage, driving a pair of fine horses. You will recall that Maman caught sight of him this way some time before his marriage, and that I was once indignant about it for some reason I cannot explain — Cassagnac in a high-wheeled young man's carriage seemed to me a kind of comedown. We told the coachman to drive faster: we want to catch someone up — and the coachman manoeuvred so as to bring us across him three times; the man had guessed it, I'll wager: three young women and Cassagnac going by... Neither a coachman nor a concierge would have any doubt about it.

In that class, he is a legend.
