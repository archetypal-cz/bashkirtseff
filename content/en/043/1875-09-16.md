%% 2026-10-03T17:29:49 TR: Reading-conventions wave (KRR 2026-10-03): garbled Dante quotation translated in text (gap kept), Marie's Italian in the footnote. %%

[^043-222]: In Italian in the original: *lauza leggierci a pusta molto, che di* [illisible] *maculato era coperta*. Evidently a garbled memory of Dante, *Inferno* I.32–33: *una lonza leggiera e presta molto, / che di pel macolato era coverta* ("a leopard, light and very swift, covered with a spotted coat"). The translation follows Dante's sense; the illegible word stands where Dante has *pel* (coat).
