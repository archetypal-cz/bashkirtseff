# Saturday, 6 January 1877
I met Princess Souvoroff at the Skating, and she... repeated to me the same thing about Tutcheff as the other day at Mortier's. It came up apropos of what I said about Nice.

%% 068.0287 %%
%% C'est une ville de cancans et de calomnie, en revenant de Rome, j'ai appris. %%
%% [#Rome](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/ROME.md) %%
It is a town of gossip and slander, as I learned on my return from Rome.

%% 068.0288 %%
%% Et je lui racontai tout cela. %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
And I told her all of this.

%% 068.0289 %%
%% - Ne faites pas attention à ces cancans, dit-elle, et prenez vos ennemis par la bonté. %%
%% 2026-09-30T05:40:00 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
— Pay no attention to this gossip, she said, and win your enemies over with kindness.

Bihovetz, seeing me chatting with the princess at length and on friendly terms, dissolved into smiles and nods of approval. He never leaves the princess's side.

As I glided on, I heard two voices: "Good day, Mademoiselle, how are you?" It was M. and Mme de Lewin. I gave them a slight nod — wrongly, perhaps, but I am curt with everyone so as not to regret it afterwards.

%% 0 quelle terrible chose que d'avoir honte de soi-même ! %%

%% 068.0300 %%
%% Béni soit le jour où j'ai eu l'idée d'écrire. Je lis et de cette façon j'embrasse en une heure tout un hiver, tout une *époque.* C'est un enseignement souverain. Dans ce cas donc, mieux a valu ne pas écrire. Je n'avais à qui me plaindre et je n'ai pas éclaté. %%
%% [#Meta_diary](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/META_DIARY.md) [#Rome](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/ROME.md) %%
%% 2026-09-27T21:49:56 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶3840, Mon Journal t.9 p.252 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
%% 2026-09-27T22:40:54 LAN: «tout une *époque*» (for «toute») = a whole era; keep the italic. «si j'avais où développer les moindres choses» = if I had somewhere to pour out the least thing. «je n'ai pas éclaté» = I did not explode (emotionally). %%
Blessed be the day I had the idea of writing. I read, and in this way I take in a whole winter, a whole *era*, in a single hour. It is a sovereign lesson. And yet, had I had my journal with me this time in Rome, I... would perhaps have been quite different; I would not have been so indifferent if I had had somewhere to pour out the smallest things. In this case, then, it was better not to write. I had no one to complain to, and I did not explode.

%% 068.0301 %%
%% A propos... j'avais soif de revoir Rome, je l'ai revue, et je commence à avoir soif de nouveau. %%
%% [#Rome](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/ROME.md) %%

By the way... I was thirsting to see Rome again; I have seen it again, and I am beginning to thirst for it once more.

%% 068.0305 %%
%% Je lis les mémoires de Garibaldi[^068.0305.1] et encore un autre livre où il en est question et je suis électrisée. %%

I am reading Garibaldi's memoirs[^068.0305.1] and yet another book that deals with him, and I am electrified.

%% 068.0306 %%
%% Et moi je ne suis rien. J'écris mais j'écris des choses dont personne ne s'inquiète ! Quand donc aurai-je à raconter... %%
%% [#Meta_diary](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/META_DIARY.md) %%

And I am nothing. I write, but I write things nobody cares about! When shall I ever have something to tell...

[^068.0305.1]: Giuseppe Garibaldi (1807-1882), hero of Italian unification. His memoirs, edited by Alexandre Dumas, had been published in French since 1860.
