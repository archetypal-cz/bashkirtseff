Mme Harris, in the prefecture box, went so far as to point us out with her fan several times while speaking low to the prefect, M. Darcy. My aunt performed the same pantomime and levelled her lorgnette at the lady until she flushed and paled. Pelikan was with us. Galula came to see us; I told him about Pierret, putting Maman's name in place of mine, and touched in a few words on *our ruin*, on our excursion to Rome and the gloom that reigns there because of the court mourning and, in the black salons, over the death of the Cardinal; we saw someone of his family who was most displeased, *being disinherited*. All this very much to the point, and not stupidly.

[^18]: *Poliuto* — opera by Gaetano Donizetti (composed 1838, premiered 1848); a work about a Christian martyr, its themes of sacrifice and isolation would have resonated darkly with Marie's mood.
