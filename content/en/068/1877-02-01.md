# Thursday, 1 February 1877
These ladies were preparing to go and pleasantly lose a few paltry hundred francs at Monaco. I brought them back to reason with a most bitter speech, and we went, Maman and I (in the *panier*), to show ourselves in broad daylight, then to Mme de Ballore's; she is so kind, and we neglect her like ill-bred people.

I have no luck: here are ladies who, if you remember, were to get us into the Prefecture; these ladies, I say, can no longer go out, seeing that Jeanne is suing for a separation from her dreadful husband. If I had the time I would tell a few fragments of the horrors told about this man in town.

I am inviting him too, since he has paid us a call. Only I said a heap of nonsense to him, because he talks exactly like someone I know (I have no idea who), and I thought I was seeing a friend.
