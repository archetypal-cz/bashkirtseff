At one o'clock we set off for "the Var racecourse". Maman, my aunt, Mme Anitchkoff, Walitsky and Meyssner in the landau. As for me, I drive two little white horses in tan leather harness, put to my white *panier*, which has come from Binder's and is truly the prettiest little *panier* imaginable. Dina, in white, is beside me; the carriage rug is of cream cloth with a silver monogram plate, [two words blacked out] so one cannot see the hems of our dresses, which would give a ==fast==[^068.0802.1] look [two words blacked out: in this kind of rug]. My mothers fume at the sight of the Howards in a carriage next to ours with the Comtesse Koutousoff and a great many young men, throwing flowers at one another and laughing as befits young and happy girls. I fumed only at hearing their spiteful remarks, which did nothing but lower them, and so lower me, casting me in the role of a *young lady envious of other people's success.* It was ugly, and in front of Mme Anitchkoff too.
