I wandered candle in hand through the empty rooms of the first floor, as I had done the other evening... I stopped in the great drawing room to sing *Aïda* when a gentleman was announced... It was only Simonetti, who spent the whole evening — during which I could not recover from the agitation caused by: a gentleman.
