# Thursday, 18 January 1877
The secretary of the Cercle made me quite a speech as I came in, in which he said that the weather was superb, the company select and numerous, but that there had been a void, that I had been awaited, and that, now that I was here, everything was perfect.

Soroka bows from a distance; Belle-de-jour showers me with attentions because I am with Princess Souvoroff, who is, as always, beautiful and charming, with her large, hard, fixed eyes. When she said that she had trembled for me along with my aunt, old Count Blondoff hastened to express I no longer know what sentiment regarding me.
