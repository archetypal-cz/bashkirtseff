## Wednesday, 21 February 1877

%% 068.1244 %%
%% Il pleut, le soleil se montre pour éclairer l'arrivée du prince de Prusse; comme nous nous étonnions Dina et moi de ne pas voir le comte de Doenhoff, il nous salua de son balcon qui est au-dessus du nôtre. Je lui fis signe de venir et le reçus presque en sautant de joie. Il me dit les choses les plus aimables et d'un air convaincu; c'est vrai j'ai embelli et c'est tout naturel à mon âge. %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) [#Doenhoff](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/DOENHOFF.md) [#Prusse](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/PRUSSE.md) [#Dina](../../_original/_glossary/people/core/DINA.md) %%
It is raining; the sun comes out to light up the arrival of the Prince of Prussia. As Dina and I were wondering why we had not seen Count Doenhoff, he greeted us from his balcony, which is above ours. I beckoned him to come down and received him almost jumping for joy. He said the most amiable things, and with an air of conviction — it is true I have grown more beautiful, and it is quite natural at my age.

%% 068.1245 %%
%% Un peu plus tard est venu Altamura et comme d'habitude je ne lui ai parlé que de mon amour pour Larderei. %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) [#Larderei](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/LARDEREI.md) [#Altamura](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/ALTAMURA.md) %%
A little later Altamura came, and as usual I spoke to him only of my love for Larderel.

%% 068.1246 %%
%% Le soir nous avons encore eu Doenhoff, que maman a reçu dans son lit, tandis qu'Altamura entrait au salon et restait avec Dina; naturellement je préférai Doenhoff. On a causé un peu de tout, beaucoup de moi qu'on trouve très à son goût. %%
%% [#Doenhoff](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/DOENHOFF.md) [#Altamura](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/ALTAMURA.md) [#Dina](../../_original/_glossary/people/core/DINA.md) %%
In the evening we had Doenhoff again — Maman received him in her bed, while Altamura entered the drawing room and stayed with Dina; naturally I preferred Doenhoff. We chatted of this and that, much about me, whom he finds very much to his taste.

%% 068.1247 %%
%% Le paisible et gras Prussien se retire à dix heures; le saccadé Altamura reste jusqu'à onze. Une heure presque entièrement passée en enfantillages sur Larderei. %%
%% [#Larderei](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/LARDEREI.md) [#Altamura](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/ALTAMURA.md) %%
The placid, portly Prussian withdraws at ten o'clock; the twitchy Altamura stays until eleven. An hour spent almost entirely in childishness about Larderel.

How happy I am at what has happened to Clemente Torlonia! The Princess Mechtchersky, whom he pursued so ardently, is marrying Fabrizio Spinosa of the princes Ruffo. This is his second fiasco; the first was his cousin, daughter of Prince Alexandre Torlonia.

Besides, they all disgust me at the moment, except Doria who has never ceased to be congenial to me, and... naturally Larderel — or, to speak like Rosalie, "Ladreréel, brother-in-law of Mirafiol."
