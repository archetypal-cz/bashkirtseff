# Monday, 5 February 1877
Pelikan came to lunch and to reach a final understanding about his departure and what is to become of his passion for Mme Zurmüllen and of the intrigues of Broussais, who wants to turn him away from it: Pelikan wishes to cool off by travelling.

At the Skating-rink, the prettiest sight imaginable. Obstacle races, ring races. When the little girls came forward after the grown-ups, everyone sat up to admire them better. Lise Kondareff won amid loud applause
At that moment Rangoni stopped near us with the Countess del Verme on his arm. He too is going to Naples or to Sicily, so he says. A rope had been stretched all round to keep a space in the middle of the rink for the skaters who were not racing. That is also where the judges stood.

Consequently Audiffret too. Twenty times I turned away from his looks, and then, no doubt wishing to boast of a familiarity that made no sense at all, he made signs asking whether I wished to compete. I answered with another sign (between the public and the judges there is the racecourse) that I did not understand and did not care to understand. Then my aunt, highly delighted, made sign after sign and called him Soroka, to which the Surprising One replied with smiles, holding out his cane as if to unhook a ring, and then on that cane he performed a sort of silent serenade.

Well, then... An eventful evening, if you like. For besides Pelikan, Zurmüllen, Bikowsky, Meyssner, we have Galula, Gautier and.... Michka Gautier, his brother. Now you must know that I have lazily and fantastically fallen in love (?) with this Michka, following an evening at the Opera when I saw the tip of his powdered nose in the box of his sister, next to ours. Michka is shorter than Pépino; he has bulging eyes, and the rest of his face is very handsome and above all very dissolute, which gives him polite, respectful, well-behaved manners.

I was not expecting so many people, and so I had on a white cloth jacket, open at the neck over a Schiller shirt, whose sleeves, trimmed with lace as well, showed beneath the cloth sleeves.
