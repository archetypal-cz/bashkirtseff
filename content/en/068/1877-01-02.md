# Tuesday, 2 January 1877
I have carried away from Rome the same pleasant and insipid impression as from Naples, and a sketch of an old woman done tolerably well.

%% 068.0377 %%
%% [#Travel](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/TRAVEL.md) %%
%% [#Genoa](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/GENOA.md) %%
%% Je suis à Gênes. %%
I am in Genoa.

%% 068.0378 %%
%% [#Society](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/SOCIETY.md) %%
%% [#Antonelli](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/ANTONELLI.md) %%
%% Je suis tranquille. On nous a parlé de Pietro fort naturellement comme si rien n'était, personne n'avait l'air de s'occuper de lui ou de croire toute sorte de choses. Nous avons voyagé avec une espèce de fou. %%
I am calm. People spoke to us of Pietro quite naturally, as though nothing had occurred; no one seemed to take any interest in him or to believe all manner of things. We travelled with a sort of madman.

%% 068.0379 %%
%% J'ai commencé une rêverie en huit tableaux. %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
I have begun a reverie in eight pictures.

%% [#Politics](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/POLITICS.md) %%

%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) [#Italian](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/ITALIAN.md) %%

Last winter and now again I have passed almost every day through the Piazza Barberini, and on the corner of a building I read an inscription in red letters which struck me particularly yesterday: ==*Vogliamo l'accessione al regno costituzionale di Vittorio Emanuele.*==[^riso]

%% 068.0381 %%
%% [#Politics](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/POLITICS.md) %%
%% Cette volonté exprimée en cette langue harmonieuse et douce produit un charmant contraste où je trouve comme une coquetterie involontaire. Je suis naïvement ravie de voir demander un roi. %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
This wish expressed in that harmonious, gentle language produces a charming contrast in which I find something of an involuntary coquetry. I am naïvely delighted to see people asking for a king.

%% [#Italian](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/ITALIAN.md) [#Victor_Emmanuel](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/VICTOR_EMMANUEL.md) [#Rome](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/ROME.md) %%
%% 2026-02-02T12:20:00 LAN: CODE-SWITCH ITALIAN: "Re e liberatore" - King and Liberator; Victor Emmanuel II's title after Italian unification %%
Behind this sentence, in my imagination, rises the monstrous figure of Victor Emmanuel,[^ve] stamped with a strange majesty, marching on Rome, having himself proclaimed on the Capitol by a mere 4,000 as ==*Re e liberatore*==[^relibe] — and thereby accomplishing the union of the most beautiful country in the world.

%% 068.0383 %%
%% [#Victor-Emmanuel](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/VICTOR_EMMANUEL.md) %%
%% Cela vous fait passer dans les veines un frisson d'enthousiasme et je vous assure que la laide tête du roi vaut bien des têtes dans l'antiquité. %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
It sends a shiver of enthusiasm through one's veins, and I assure you that the ugly head of the king is worth many a head from antiquity.

[^relibe]: ==*Re e liberatore*== *In Italian in the original.* "King and Liberator" — Victor Emmanuel's proclamatory title after the unification of Italy.
[^riso]: ==*Vogliamo l'accessione al regno costituzionale di Vittorio Emanuele.*== *In Italian in the original.* "We want accession to the constitutional kingdom of Victor Emmanuel." A Risorgimento slogan inscribed on a building in Rome.
[^ve]: *Victor Emmanuel*: Victor Emmanuel II (1820–1878), the first King of unified Italy; known for his impressive if ungainly appearance.
[^youngitaly]: *Young Italy* (*La Giovine Italia*): the Risorgimento movement founded by Mazzini in 1831, calling for Italian unification and republican government.
