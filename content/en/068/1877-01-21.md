As a result of yesterday's fall my left ankle is swollen, which allows me to appear at the Skating in all the majesty of a long gown. My escorts were the General and the Baron de Bauche, who saw me as a little girl at Spa. He introduced to Maman his brother-in-law, M. Blancho, who is married to the sister of Princess Vorousoff; a few people passing through, Tchetwertinsky and Mlle Marie, Princess Souvoroff's ward.
