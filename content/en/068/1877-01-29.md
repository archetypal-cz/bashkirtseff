I went to the Anitchkoffs' with Dina, in my *panier*. There I soothed M. Anitchkoff's nerves with my singing, and took his wife to the Skating, where I met M. de Bauche, and all the others from a distance. Soroka too. What a hideous man. He has grown ugly, and besides, he is disagreeable.
