# Wednesday, 3 January 1877
I am back from Rome — see rather the supplement.

Today was spent at San Remo; the princess went to Maman's to invite her to luncheon. So we all were together. Maman scolded — that is, reprimanded her husband — otherwise all went well. The young couple are congenial[^sympathique] and Hélène is a great philosophical rascal, more good than bad. They were doubtless all at Maman's feet, but I do not know what I am writing — I am tired.
