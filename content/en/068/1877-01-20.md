# Saturday, 20 January 1877
Merjeewsky came this morning to say goodbye; Maman dined yesterday with his mother at Monaco.

Vous voyez que la position des acteurs est bien définie. Alors le susdit Surprenant d'un air grave et sévère nous daigna dire quelques paroles qui à la rigueur pourraient passer pour, un peu aimables. Par contraste je fus d'une légèreté rare et demandai comment il se faisait que le patineur le plus intrépide était à pied. %%

After an unbearable sitting, I went to the Skating, where I was the victim of a double accident. The first was that the Surprising One, without skates on his feet, while making the round of the gallery, stopped near us — behind Maman, my aunt, M. and Mme Anitchkoff, and facing me, since I was facing them. You see that the position of the actors is clearly defined. Then the aforesaid Surprising One, with a grave and severe air, deigned to address to us a few words which, at a pinch, might pass for somewhat amiable. By contrast I was uncommonly light-hearted, and asked how it came about that the most intrepid skater was on foot.
