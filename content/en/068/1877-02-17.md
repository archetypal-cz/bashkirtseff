## Saturday, 17 February 1877

%% 068.1531 %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
%% [#Pausilippe](../../_original/_glossary/places/neighborhoods/PAUSILIPPE.md) %%
%% Je me suis levée de bonne heure et nous sommes allées voir la grotte du Pausilippe[^37], voilà qui me plaît, puis le tombeau de Virgile. Arrivons au fait ! Je suis chagrinée, le cocher de Larderel part demain, donc il ne reviendra pas avant quinze jours comme il l'a dit, s'il revient toutefois. Le retard que mettait ce satané Charles... me semblait un indice de prompt retour... Ici j'ai été interrompue par Altamura, cet excellent garçon a été blessé de notre air le jour du balcon mais vraiment c'est un gentil chien... fi ! comme je m'exprime. %%
%% [#Larderel](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/LARDEREL.md) [#Altamura](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/ALTAMURA.md) [#Virgile](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/VIRGILE.md) %%
I rose early and we went to see the Grotto of Pausilippe[^37] — now that I like — and then Virgil's tomb. To the point! I am upset: Larderel's coachman leaves tomorrow, so he will not return for a fortnight as he said — if he returns at all. The delay of that wretched Charles... seemed to me a sign of a speedy return... Here I was interrupted by Altamura; this excellent fellow was hurt by our manner on the balcony day, but really he is a nice dog... fie! the way I express myself!

%% [#Larderel](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/LARDEREL.md) [#Merisano](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/MERISANO.md) [#Ruffo](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/RUFFO.md) %%
I skated and amused myself — there was the one we take for Prince Ruffo, and the officer who saw Larderel off, a superb young man, and Merisano the monkey of last year, and the suave young man, and many others; I was in white with two braids down my back like Marguerite, and a black velvet cap. What do you expect — it is only natural that people should take a little interest in us; we are brand-new here.

%% 068.1534 %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
%% Le départ du cocher me cause comme des secousses de désappointement, là j'entreprends une enquête avec Rosalie, je lui fais répéter comment Larderel rentrait de bonne heure et le matin sautait à bas de son lit pour regarder dans la serrure, puis la pantomime avec Charles qui lui faisait signe de venir quand il apercevait quelqu'un; comment Larderel basculait le cocher et puis se prenait la tête dans les mains et bondissait dans la chambre s'écriait: Ah ! Bon Dieu, bon Dieu ! Ce geste et ces paroles lui sont particulières, en approchant de Cancello il s'est pris la tête dans les mains en criant, Bon Dieu, bon Dieu voici Cancello ! %%
%% [#Larderel](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/LARDEREL.md) [#Rosalie](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/ROSALIE.md) [#Cancello](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/CANCELLO.md) %%
The coachman's departure sends shocks of disappointment through me; I undertake an enquiry with Rosalie, having her repeat how Larderel would come home early and in the morning leap from his bed to look through the keyhole; then the pantomime with Charles who would signal him to come when he caught sight of someone; how Larderel would knock the coachman aside, then take his head in his hands and bound across the room crying: Ah! Good Lord, good Lord! This gesture and these words are peculiar to him — approaching Cancello he took his head in his hands crying, "Good Lord, good Lord — here is Cancello!"

%% [#Mirafiore](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/MIRAFIORE.md) %%
%% Le cocher prend toute sorte d'informations sur mon compte, si j'aime les chevaux, si j'ai bon caractère et encore et encore et enfin satisfait il déclare que je suis la femme qu'il faut à son maître. Alors le cocher et la femme de chambre arrangent le mariage. Mais ce n'est pas de cela qu'il est question, il s'agit du départ ou de l'arrivée. Charles ne part plus, il a reçu des ordres du Palais où l'appelle le comte de Mirafiori pour des chevaux à Larderel, d'ailleurs c'est chaque instant autre chose... Ah ! et ne puis-je donc pas me tenir à ce qu'a dit ce Bijou-bouzouk[^38] lui-même ! %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) [#Marriage](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MARRIAGE.md) [#Larderel](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/LARDEREL.md) %%
The coachman makes all sorts of enquiries about me — whether I like horses, whether I have a good temper, and so on and so on — and at last, satisfied, he declares that I am the woman his master needs. Then the coachman and the lady's maid arrange the marriage. But that is not the question at issue; the question is departure or arrival. Charles is no longer leaving — he has received orders from the Palace where the Count of Mirafiori summons him, about horses for Larderel; besides, every moment it is something else... Ah! and can I not hold to what this *Bijou-bouzouk*[^38] himself said!

If I followed my inclination I should spend hours writing down the smallest details about the coachman, the lady's maid, the horses, the Countess of Mirafiore who left today for Florence and whom Bijou-bouzouk will come to receive in Rome and escort back to Florence at last.

True — I feel something for him... Audiffret came with dreadful slowness; Antonelli came as a result of the communicative influence of Love upon me; as for this family man — well, he came like a thunderbolt. He is the most *real* of the three; but from the portrait I draw of him you will perceive well enough, will you not, that he is not yet the ideal — or what I shall one day judge to be such.
