M. Alexis de Meyssner has come from Petersburg with a letter from Nina. It is all I can do to drag myself through until eight o'clock. He had been kept to dinner because he is Nina's nephew, and the whole time I had to talk and ask questions so as not to look like a dullard. The thing is, I am a dullard as well; when M. and Mme Zoubaloff were announced, I slipped away. I have nothing to do with such people.
