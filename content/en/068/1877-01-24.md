# Wednesday, 24 January 1877
My birthday. Poor Walitsky gives me ten metres of charming lace, and the Anitchkoffs bring three superb bouquets; after which demonstrations I go off to drive two little white horses and see how they go, for I have had my *panier*[^panier] sent from Paris. Dina gets tired, so I take Collignon; we meet my family a hundred times, we go to the station three times, bring back the *panier* and unpack it in the garden, everyone at once.
