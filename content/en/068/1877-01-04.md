# Thursday, 4 January 1877
The Anitchkoffs — grandfather who is blind, Pelikan who is deaf, Broussais who shouts, everyone shouting! The brown walls,[^murs] the cuckoo clock,[^coucou] grandfather's disputes — his howling and moaning for nothing...

%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) [#Reading](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/READING.md) [#Mme_Tutcheff](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/MME_TUTCHEFF.md) [#Binder](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/BINDER.md) [#Tutcheff](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/TUTCHEFF.md) %%
Oh, that! The gossip — for Mme Tutcheff is telling everyone that we have nothing left, that everything is to be sold, that Binder's carriages were ordered but not delivered for want of payment, that... that...

The old men, the brown walls! Oh nightmare, oh despair, oh madness!

[^coucou]: *Pendule à coucou* ("cuckoo clock"): a symbol of middle-class taste and provincial time-keeping, deeply alien to Marie's aesthetic sensibility.
[^murs]: *Murs marrons* ("brown walls"): for Marie, the epitome of bourgeois provincial drabness.
