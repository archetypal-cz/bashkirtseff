# Thursday, 4 January 1877
The Anitchkoffs, Grand-papa who is blind, Pelikan who is deaf, Broussais who shouts, everyone shouting! The brown walls,[^murs] the cuckoo clock,[^coucou] the arguments of Grand-papa, who howls and moans over nothing...

[^coucou]: *Pendule à coucou* ("cuckoo clock"): a symbol of middle-class taste and provincial time-keeping, deeply alien to Marie's aesthetic sensibility.
[^murs]: *Murs marrons* ("brown walls"): for Marie, the epitome of bourgeois provincial drabness.
