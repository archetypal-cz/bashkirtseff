# Friday, 12 January 1877
I was born on the 12th of November, but I was only meant to *be born* on the 12th of January; accordingly my age is reckoned from the 12th of January, old style.[^19] So in twelve days I shall be eighteen... Merjeewsky, confusing the Russian style with the other, comes today with his mother to congratulate me and bring me flowers. Then Galula turns up.
