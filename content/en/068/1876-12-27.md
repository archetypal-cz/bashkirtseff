My father has at least paid a call on Baron Visconti and shown himself with us at the skating rink. Paolo Antonelli is the finest skater there — all smiles over the thirty thousand francs a year left him by the Cardinal. This rather pretty and very refined miniature of a man flutters about with the Somaglia and the other Roman princesses who were there. Since these ladies skated quite badly, I ventured out and did not look too ungainly.

— No one thought of him for you, said Rossi — he is a poor young man with neither fortune nor position, who runs with a set of young men who lead him astray, into... well, that life of... of suppers with the... *danseuses*[^11]... At first people thought..., in any case you gave him a jolt, and perhaps now he will mend his ways — that is, form himself. He would need either a journey or five or six months of ordered life in society. That would give him some standing.

[^11]: *danseuses* — ballet dancers; in 1870s society a well-known euphemism for kept women or courtesans.
