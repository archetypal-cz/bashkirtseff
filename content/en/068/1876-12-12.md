— A mere formality, said the cashier, one simply has to be recommended by a member of the Cercle; this afternoon being reserved for members, you cannot attend. I am dreadfully sorry, Mademoiselle, it is only for today — tomorrow you will name me a member, one of your acquaintances. A mere formality.
I came home and instead of making a scene I sat down to paint.
What is odious is that the cashier knows me, and since I shall not go back, he will think ==che== [sic] it is the truth![^1]

I had a moment of such despair... that I would have married Audiffret; I could give no better account of my dejection.
I did not exchange a single word with my own family, as though I were looking at strangers. They asked me for money — I refused, as I refused everything without distinction.
Mmes Zurmüllen, Pelikan, Broussais, Antonoff were not favoured with my company.[^2]
What use are they to me? Neither pleasure nor profit. To the devil with them! Nor am I of any use to them.
I cannot understand how I managed to go to the ==Skating-rink==.[^3]
It is simply that I always forget.

But I am pleased with myself — I did not howl, and my eyes are dry. I have a slight fever, but bah!
Mme Howard's letter, the refusal of the invitation to the Cercle, Audiffret, and all the rest passed before my eyes like so many bleeding reproaches.
I am exhausted! And I want to ask them all, by what right they insult me‖
If it were not for this portrait which I promised (to myself) to finish, I would leave tomorrow.
Ah! how I suffer, my God, how I suffer — and I no longer weep.
