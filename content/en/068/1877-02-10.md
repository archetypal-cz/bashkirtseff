# Saturday, 10 February 1877
I stayed in bed with Maman until three o'clock, because *we found me* excessively unhappy — or rather, pursued by ill luck.

Je déteste ma figure, mon air, mes habits, ma voix, tout ! Je me déteste comme je détestais Bruschetti. %%
%% [#Emotions](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/EMOTIONS.md) [#Antonelli](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/ANTONELLI.md) [#Bruschetti](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/BRUSCHETTI.md) %%
One proof of it is that wretched Antonelli. Indeed nothing goes right for me — neither what is worth the trouble, nor what is worth nothing. Whereas so many others who are uglier, stupider, poorer, more miserable... No matter. Instead of compassion for my misfortune, all these reflections make me loathe myself. I detest my face, my bearing, my clothes, my voice — everything! I detest myself as I once detested Bruschetti.

[^20]: *veglione* — the grand masked carnival ball; an Italian tradition at major opera houses.
[^21]: *domino* — the classic masquerade costume: a long hooded cloak worn with a half-mask.
[^22]: *fini* (finished, complete) — Marie's idiosyncratic concept of character: a person either possesses an innate wholeness — in manners, presence, and social ease — or does not, regardless of intelligence.
[^23]: The Countess of Mirafiore, born Bianca de Larderel, was the wife of Emanuele Alberto Guerrieri, Count of Mirafiore, son of King Vittorio Emanuele II and Rosa Vercellana, the king's morganatic wife — hence «bru du Roi» (the King's daughter-in-law). (Source: it.wikipedia, «Emanuele Alberto Guerrieri di Mirafiori»: he married Bianca Enrichetta de Larderel, daughter of Enrico de Larderel, in 1872.)
[^24]: *traite* — a bill of exchange or promissory note: a debt instrument falling due at a specified date.
