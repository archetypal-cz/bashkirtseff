%% 2026-02-02T13:00:00 LAN: "Larderei" - Marie's previous drawing teacher in Nice, considered amateur compared to Parisian training %%
%% Ce sont des femmes qui ont trois, quatre ans d'atelier, de Louvre, d'études sérieuses. Et moi... Larderei et voilà tout. %%
These are women with three, four years of studio work, of copying at the Louvre, of serious study. And I... Larderei[^10] and that is all.

[^10]: Larderei was Marie's previous drawing teacher in Nice, evidently far less rigorous than the Parisian professional training.
