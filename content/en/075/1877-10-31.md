%% Misérable ! Il y a vingt-quatre jours que je travaille ! Misérable ! Tu aurais déjà pu être connue ! Suis-je si confiante parce que je ne vaux rien ou bien, parce que je sens mon mérite ? On le saura plus tard. %%
# Wednesday, 31 October 1877

Wretch! I have been working for twenty-four days! Wretch! You could already have been known! Am I so confident because I am worth nothing — or because I feel my own merit? We shall find out later.

Dina says he said something rather bold — here it is. I was saying in jest that he was a poor friend to take the Florentine's place beside the young harpist. It was rather amusing, because everyone except the baroness knew that it was she who was meant. Then Marcuard answered me:

%% 075.0587 %%
%% "- N'est-ce pas lui qui avait pris la mienne -." %%
— Was it not he who had taken mine?
