# Wednesday, 8 December 1875

%% 050.0562 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Fashion](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/FASHION.md) %%

%% [#Promenade_des_Anglais](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/PROMENADE_DES_ANGLAIS.md) %%
%% J'ai dormi jusqu'a deux heures, a peine ai-je le temps de dejeuner avant la Promenade. Il fait tres froid, je suis obligee de porter ma fourrure qui d'ailleurs avec ma grande robe blanche garnie de haut en bas de galons poses en travers me donne l'air d'une princesse de l'ancienne Pologne, ou d'une femme de boyard de la vieille Russie. %%
I slept until two o'clock; I barely have time to lunch before the Promenade. It is very cold and I am obliged to wear my fur — which in any case, with my great white dress trimmed from top to bottom with braids placed crosswise, gives me the look of a princess of old Poland, or of a boyar's wife from old Russia.
%% 2026-03-05T11:00:00 TR: "galons posés en travers" — decorative braids placed crosswise. "boyard" — Russian nobleman (boyar); Marie emphasises her Slavic heritage. %%

%% 050.0563 %%
%% Rien n'est beau que le blanc. %%
Nothing is beautiful but white.

%% 050.0564 %%
%% Le blanc seul est aimable. %%
White alone is lovable.

%% 050.0565 %%
%% Il doit régner partout. %%
It should reign everywhere.

%% 050.0566 %%
%% Et même sur la table. %%
And even on the table.
%% 2026-03-05T11:00:00 TR: Marie parodies Boileau's celebrated maxim "Rien n'est beau que le vrai" ("Nothing is beautiful but truth") with her obsession with white. A four-line mock-verse. %%

%% 050.0567 %%
%% [#Bueno](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/BUENO.md) [#Emile_d_Audiffret](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/EMILE_D_AUDIFFRET.md) %%
%% Qu'est-ce que je disais ! Mlle Bueno est arrivée. Pauvre Emile, quand il reviendra il ne saura de quel côté courir. %%
What was I saying! Mlle Bueno has arrived. Poor Émile — when he returns he will not know which way to run.

%% 050.0568 %%
%% [#Maman](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/MAMAN.md) [#Emile_d_Audiffret](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/EMILE_D_AUDIFFRET.md) [#Paris](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/PARIS.md) %%
%% Voilà que maman dans l'absence de ce garnement veut voir le pendant de mon voyage à Paris. %%
Now Maman, in the boy's absence, wants to see the parallel to my Paris journey.
%% 2026-03-05T11:00:00 TR: "garnement" — rascal, scamp; affectionate. "le pendant" — the counterpart/parallel: Maman suggests Audiffret followed Marie to Paris, so now he is presumably following her rivals to Nice. %%

%% 050.0569 %%
%% [#Emile_d_Audiffret](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/EMILE_D_AUDIFFRET.md) %%
%% — Vous croyez qu'il s'occupe de moi, dis-je, détrompez-vous de grâce, il me crache dessus. %%
"You think he occupies himself with me?" I say. "Disabuse yourself, please — he spits on me."
%% 2026-03-05T11:00:00 TR: "détrompez-vous de grâce" — please disabuse yourself; slightly formal, ironical register. %%

%% 050.0570 %%
%% — Qu'il s'occupe de vous, ou qu'il ne s'occupe pas, me répond-on, mais les faits sont là, c'est peut-être une coïncidence, mais c'est bien drôle. %%
"Whether he occupies himself with you or not," I am told, "the facts are there — it may be coincidence, but it is very strange."

I did not see my beautiful unknown — I looked for him everywhere, in vain.

%% 050.0574 %%
%% [#Reading](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/READING.md) %%
%% Ce soir j'ai lu de bons livres, il y a si longtemps que je ne lis plus rien. C'est très mal et j'en souffre beaucoup. %%
This evening I read some good books — it has been so long since I have read anything. It is very wrong of me and I suffer greatly from it.

%% 050.0575 %%

One cannot imagine my impatience to go to Rome and return to work. To study, to study, to study! That is my desire!

I become quite joyful at the sight of my dear books, my beloved classics, my charming Plutarch. I shall take several books with me to read in Rome — for I do not suppose we shall see much of the world there; we know no one in Rome. ==Alas!==[^ohime]
