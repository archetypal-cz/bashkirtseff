%% 050.0554 %%
# Thursday, 9 December 1875

%% 050.0555 %%
%% Grande musique et grand monde, et grand chagrin toujours pour la meme raison. %%
Grand concert and grand company — and great grief, always for the same reason.

%% 050.0556 %%
%% Robenson, la Pointue mais jolie Americaine se promene en voiture fermee. Je sens quelque chose de particulier en la voyant. %%
Robenson — the Pointed but pretty American — drives about in a closed carriage. I feel something particular on seeing her.

%% 050.0557 %%
%% Pas plus d'inconnu que sur la main. Olga se promene avec nous. A la Promenade on ne voit plus les Nicois de l'ete, ils se cachent on ne sait ou, meme Saetone ne se montre pas. %%
Not a trace of the unknown man — not so much as his shadow. Olga walks with us. On the Promenade one no longer sees the Nice people of the summer — they are hiding goodness knows where; even Saetone does not show himself.

%% 050.0558 %%
%% [#Music_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MUSIC_THEME.md) %%
%% [#Theater_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/THEATER.md) %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% Les Sapogenikoff ont une loge pour ce soir, personne d'eux ne va, excepte Giro et nous allons a l'Opera. "Mignon", mais j'ai peu ecoute, Fiouloulou est venu, ensuite Pepino qui etait assez amusant car il copiait Audiffret. Pauvre modele ! J'etais gaie. %%
The Sapogenikoffs have a box for this evening; none of them goes except Giro, and we go to the Opera. *Mignon* — but I listened little; Fiouloulou came, then Pepino, who was quite amusing because he was mimicking Audiffret. Poor original! I was gay.

%% 050.0559 %%
%% — J'ai envie de me poser ainsi, dis-je en avancant la jambe droite et en appuyant la main sur le genou, comme le Surprenant. %%
"I want to pose like this," I say, advancing my right leg and resting my hand on my knee — like the Surprising One.

%% 050.0560 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% — Oh ! Mademoiselle, dit Gautier, ne faites pas cela, c'est la pose de Napoleon %%
"Oh, Mademoiselle," says Gautier, "do not do that — it is Napoleon's pose."

%% 050.0561 %%
%% — Vraiment ? %%
"Really?"

%% 050.0562 %%
%% — Oui, tenez, voici Napoleon a Trocadero, voici Napoleon pensif, voila Napoleon soucieux, voila Napoleon content, et voila Napoleon ennuye. %%
"Yes, look — here is Napoleon at Trocadero;[^1] here is Napoleon pensive; here is Napoleon careworn; here is Napoleon content; and here is Napoleon bored."

[^1]: *Napoleon at Trocadero*: a reference to the famous images of Napoleon in various characteristic poses; Trocadero was the site of a Spanish battle in 1823 associated with French heroism.

%% 050.0563 %%
%% Et ce disant il faisait l'une apres l'autre et d'une facon remarquable les poses du Surprenant. La derniere surtout etait frappante, et nous avons eclate de rire. %%
And as he spoke he struck one after another — and in a remarkable manner — the poses of the Surprising One. The last in particular was striking, and we burst out laughing.

%% 050.0564 %%
%% Bueno est non loin de nous, elle a une figure fort originale. %%
Bueno is not far from us — she has a very original face.

%% 050.0565 %%
%% [#Theater_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/THEATER.md) %%
%% [#Emotions](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/EMOTIONS.md) %%
%% Les loges de Leon et du Surprenant sont vides, nous echangeons un coup d'oeil avec Olga, et ma tante se leve pour partir, au lieu de la suivre nous montons en courant l'escalier, appelons l'ouvreuse tout etourdie de notre fureur, lui ordonnons d'ouvrir la loge n 1 et de la toutes tremblantes et animees regardons le monde evacuer la salle. %%
The boxes of Léon and the Surprising One are empty. We exchange a glance with Olga, and my aunt rises to leave — instead of following her we run up the stairs, call the usherette, quite giddy with our own boldness, order her to open box No. 1, and from there — all trembling and agitated — we watch the audience pour out of the hall.

%% 050.0566 %%
%% Giro est en extase devant le petit canape sur lequel s'assied son bien-aime. %%
Giro is in ecstasy before the little sofa on which her beloved sits.

%% 050.0567 %%
%% - Tiens, dit-elle, quand nous eumes rejoint ma tante et Dina, tiens, j'ai perdu mon bouquet ! %%
"Look," she says, when we have rejoined my aunt and Dina. "Look — I have lost my bouquet!"

%% 050.0568 %%
%% [#Theater_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/THEATER.md) %%
%% Elle l'a laisse dans la loge de son Emile. Pauvre bete ! %%
She left it in her Émile's box. Poor creature!

%% 050.0569 %%
%% [#Music_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MUSIC_THEME.md) %%
%% Je soupe avec Dina de dinde froide. C'est comme l'hiver dernier. Pour moi, l'opera, la dinde froide et Audiffret sont trois choses inseparables. %%
I sup with Dina on cold turkey. It is like last winter. For me, the opera, cold turkey, and Audiffret are three inseparable things.
