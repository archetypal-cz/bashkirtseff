%% 050.0360 %%
# Thursday, 2 December 1875

%% 050.0361 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/people/writers/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% ## Prediction de Cassandre a M. Leon d'Audiffret. %%
## Cassandra's Prediction to M. Léon d'Audiffret.[^1]

[^1]: *Cassandra's Prediction*: Marie's satirical poem in 38 stanzas, addressed to Audiffret père (Léon), prophesying the moral ruin of his son Émile. The Cassandra allusion adds irony — the true prophet whom no one believes.

%% 050.0362 %%
%% 1 Ton fils d'ici partira
Et tres grand bruit menera
Chantera criera rira
mais pourrira mais pourrira.
A Paris il s'en ira,
Chez Bignon dejeunera
Au Bois se promenera
et pourrira et pourrira. %%
1 Your son will depart from here
And make a very great noise,
Will sing, will shout, will laugh —
but will rot, but will rot.
To Paris he will go,
At Bignon's[^2] he will breakfast,
In the Bois[^3] he will promenade —
and will rot, and will rot.

[^2]: *Bignon*: fashionable Parisian restaurant on the Boulevard des Italiens.
[^3]: *Le Bois*: the Bois de Boulogne, Paris's fashionable park, favoured for afternoon drives and promenades.

%% 050.0363 %%
%% Tous les sales lieux frequentera
Des cocottes entretiendra
Un nabab s'estimera
et pourrira et pourrira ! %%
All the vile places he will frequent,
*Cocottes* he will keep,
A nabab[^4] he will consider himself —
and will rot, and will rot!

[^4]: *nabab*: a man of ostentatious wealth; from the Indian nawab, the term was fashionable in 1870s France for the parvenu rich.

%% 050.0364 %%
%% Au Moulin Rouge soupera
Beaucoup de champagne boira
Des assiettes des verres brisera
et pourrira et pourrira. %%
At the Moulin Rouge[^5] he will sup,
Much champagne he will drink,
Plates and glasses he will smash —
and will rot, and will rot.

[^5]: *Moulin Rouge*: famous Parisian cabaret in Montmartre, associated with risqué entertainment and the demi-monde.

%% 050.0365 %%
%% A Mabille dansera
Tant que son habit otera
Ses gants jaunes salira
et pourrira et pourrira. %%
At Mabille[^6] he will dance
Until he removes his coat,
His yellow gloves he will soil —
and will rot, and will rot.

[^6]: *Mabille*: the Bal Mabille, a famous outdoor dance garden on the Champs-Élysées, known for the cancan and the company of courtesans.

%% 050.0366 %%
%% Son chapeau tube bosselera
La chemise dechirera
Puis par terre se roulera
et pourrira et pourrira. %%
His top hat he will batter,
His shirt he will tear,
Then on the ground he will roll —
and will rot, and will rot.

%% 050.0367 %%
%% Grand scandale produira
Honteusement se conduira
Et enfin se grisera
Il pourrira, il pourrira ! %%
Great scandal he will produce,
Shamefully he will conduct himself,
And at last he will get drunk —
he will rot, he will rot!

%% 050.0368 %%
%% La police le pincera
Au violon le fourrera
Ou la nuit il passera
et pourrira et pourrira. %%
The police will nab him,
Into the lock-up[^7] they will throw him,
Where he will spend the night —
and will rot, and will rot.

[^7]: *le violon*: 1870s slang for a police holding cell (literally "the violin," from the instrument's shape).

%% 050.0369 %%
%% Quand de la il sortira
Encore pire recommencera
Jouera au cercle et perdra
et pourrira et pourrira. %%
When he gets out of there
He will begin again, worse than before,
Will gamble at the club[^8] and lose —
and will rot, and will rot.

[^8]: *cercle*: a private gentlemen's club, often a venue for high-stakes gambling.

%% 050.0370 %%
%% Ayant tout perdu pleurera
Les cheveux s'arrachera
Andriot regrettera
et pourrira et pourrira. %%
Having lost everything he will weep,
Will tear his hair,
Will regret Andriot[^9] —
and will rot, and will rot.

[^9]: Andriot: an unidentified creditor or former associate of the Audiffret family.

%% 050.0371 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/people/writers/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
%% Puis le bonnet grec mettra
Chez Gioia se presentera
De l'amour demandera
et pourrira et pourrira. %%
Then he will put on his Greek cap,[^10]
Will present himself at Gioia's,
Will beg for love —
and will rot, and will rot.

[^10]: *bonnet grec*: a Greek-style soft cap, worn informally; in this context it signals reduced circumstances or bohemian decline.

%% 050.0372 %%
%% "Je suis gueux" il lui dira
Et la belle le chassera
Car payer il ne pourra
Il pourrira il pourrira. %%
"I am a pauper," he will tell her,
And the beauty will turn him out,
For he will not be able to pay —
he will rot, he will rot.

%% 050.0373 %%
%% 13 Vers Laura se tournera
A l'hotel l'amenera
Plusieurs jours la retiendra
et pourrira et pourrira. %%
13 To Laura[^11] he will then turn,
He will bring her to a hotel,
Will keep her there for several days —
and will rot, and will rot.

[^11]: *Laura*: Madame Laura, the high-class courtesan revealed by the Paris chambermaid (see entry of 27 November).

%% 050.0374 %%
%% 14 Quand s'habiller elle devra
Comment s'y prendre ne saura
Et Emile l'habillera
Il pourrira il pourrira ! %%
14 When she must dress herself
She will not know how to set about it,
And Émile will dress her —
he will rot, he will rot!

%% 050.0375 %%
%% 15 Tant de joie exprimera
Que l'hotel revoltera
Et chasse, chasse sera
Il pourrira, il pourrira. %%
15 So much joy will he express
That he will scandalise the hotel,
And turned out, turned out he will be —
he will rot, he will rot.

%% 050.0376 %%
%% 16 Enfin a Nice reviendra
Dans la mer se plongera
Toutes ses saletes y laissera
Mais pourrira, mais pourrira. %%
16 At last he will return to Nice,
Will plunge into the sea,
Will leave all his filth behind him there —
but will rot, but will rot.

%% 050.0377 %%
%% 17 Au chateau remontera
Son fichu pere embrassera
L'habit blanc endossera
Et pourrira et pourrira. %%
17 He will go back up to the château,[^12]
Will embrace his blasted father,
Will put on his white coat —
and will rot, and will rot.

[^12]: *le château*: the Tour Audiffret, the Audiffret family's château-tower near Nice.

%% 050.0378 %%
%% 18 Qu'il est sage t'assurera
Mille balivernes te contera
Comme une vieille bete te dupera
Et pourrira et pourrira. %%
18 How reformed he is he will assure you,
A thousand foolish tales he will spin you,
Like an old fool he will dupe you —
and will rot, and will rot.

%% 050.0379 %%
%% 19 Avec Saetone dinera
Au Francais se montrera
Des regards de feu lancera
Mais pourrira mais pourrira ! %%
19 With Saetone he will dine,
At the Théâtre-Français he will show himself,
Flashing glances of fire —
but will rot, but will rot!

%% 050.0380 %%
%% 20 Des lecons de chant prendra
Mais sa voix ne retrouvera
Toussera et hurlera
Et pourrira et pourrira. %%
20 Singing lessons he will take
But will never recover his voice,
Will cough and will howl —
and will rot, and will rot.

%% 050.0381 %%
%% [#Music_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MUSIC_THEME.md) %%
%% 21 A speculer songera
L'Opera dirigera
Mille depenses supprimera
Mais pourrira, mais pourrira. %%
21 He will dream of speculation,
Will direct the Opera,
Will suppress a thousand expenses —
but will rot, but will rot.

%% 050.0382 %%
%% [#Theater_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/THEATER.md) %%
%% 22 Les decors barbouillera
Les actrices fardera
Aux entrees assistera
Mais pourrira, mais pourrira. %%
22 The scenery he will daub,
The actresses he will paint,
At rehearsals he will attend —
but will rot, but will rot.

%% 050.0383 %%
%% [#Theater_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/THEATER.md) %%
%% 23 Son ouvrage admirera
Dans sa loge se pavanera
Sur son siege s'endormira
Et pourrira et pourrira. %%
23 His own work he will admire,
In his box he will strut,
In his seat he will fall asleep —
and will rot, and will rot.

%% 050.0384 %%
%% [#Theater_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/THEATER.md) %%
%% 24 En sursaut s'eveillera
Au cri aigu que poussera
L'actrice dont la voix craquera
Et pourrira et pourrira. %%
24 He will wake with a start
At the shrill cry emitted
By the actress whose voice will crack —
and will rot, and will rot.

%% 050.0385 %%
%% 25 Le public se recriera
Chez Emile en foule montera
Et d'en haut le lancera
Et pourrira et pourrira. %%
25 The audience will cry out,
Will surge up to Émile's box in a mob,
And will hurl him from on high —
and will rot, and will rot.

%% 050.0386 %%
%% 26 Brise il se relevera
Au chateau boiteux courra
Comme toi fourbu demeurera
Et pourrira et pourrira. %%
26 Broken he will pick himself up,
Will limp back to the château,
Like you, worn out, will remain —
and will rot, and will rot.

%% 050.0387 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/people/writers/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Theater_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/THEATER.md) %%
%% 27 Le theatre on fermera
Des effets on disposera
Emile une perruque aura
Et pourrira et pourrira. %%
27 The theatre will be closed,
The effects will be disposed of,
Émile will have a wig —
and will rot, and will rot.

%% 050.0388 %%
%% 28 Pour battu ne s'avouera
L'arrosage entreprendra
Mais la aussi echouera
Et pourrira et pourrira. %%
28 He will not admit defeat,
Will undertake market-gardening,[^13]
But there too will fail —
and will rot, and will rot.

[^13]: *l'arrosage*: literally "watering/irrigation," here indicating a venture in market-gardening or horticulture — a humiliating fall for a gentleman.

%% 050.0389 %%
%% 29 Tous ses biens dissipera
Son joli chateau vendra
Toi-meme il t'hypothequera
Et pourrira et pourrira. %%
29 All his goods he will dissipate,
His pretty château he will sell,
You yourself he will mortgage —
and will rot, and will rot.

%% 050.0390 %%
%% 30 Ces epaves ramassera
De nouveau tout depensera
Un dernier jour brillera
Et pourrira et pourrira. %%
30 These remnants he will gather up,
Everything he will spend again,
One last day he will shine —
and will rot, and will rot.

%% 050.0391 %%
%% 31 Aux amis empruntera
Jamais rien ne leur rendra
Tous les derniers sous gobera
Et pourrira et pourrira. %%
31 From friends he will borrow,
Never a thing will he return,
Every last sou he will swallow —
and will rot, and will rot.

%% 050.0392 %%
%% 32 Enfin se repentira
Le metier il reprendra
Et le drap mesurera
Mais pourrira mais pourrira. %%
32 At last he will repent,
Will return to his trade,[^14]
And will measure out cloth —
but will rot, but will rot.

[^14]: *le drap mesurera*: measuring cloth — returning to trade as a draper. For a man of Audiffret's social pretensions, this is the ultimate humiliation.

%% 050.0393 %%
%% 33 Jamais ne se corrigera
La boutique bouleversera
Les commis ameutera
Et pourrira et pourrira. %%
33 Never will he reform,
Will throw the shop into chaos,
Will incite the shop-boys to mutiny —
and will rot, and will rot.

%% 050.0394 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/people/writers/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% 34 Du metier s'indignera
Du bon temps se souviendra
Et enfin s'echappera
Et pourrira et pourrira. %%
34 At the trade he will be indignant,
Will remember the good times,
And at last will escape —
and will rot, and will rot.

%% 050.0395 %%
%% 35 Son oncle sur lui crachera
Scelerat le nommera
Et ensuite le maudira.
Il pourrira, il pourrira. %%
35 His uncle will spit upon him,
Will call him a scoundrel,
And then will curse him —
he will rot, he will rot.

%% 050.0396 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/people/writers/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% 36 Alors il s'arretera
Son bonnet grec enfoncera
Autour de lui regardera
Et pourrira et pourrira. %%
36 Then he will stop,
Will pull his Greek cap down,
Will look all around him —
and will rot, and will rot.

%% 050.0397 %%
%% 37 Un beau jour se mariera
Sur sa femme comme toi tirera
Et par elle chasse sera
Il pourrira, il pourrira. %%
37 One fine day he will marry,
Will draw upon his wife as he does upon you,
And will be turned out by her —
he will rot, he will rot.

%% 050.0398 %%
%% 38 Comme une pale ombre errera
Chavirera et tournera
Mais pourrir toujours devra
Et pourrira et pourrira. %%
38 Like a pale shadow he will wander,
Will reel and spin —
But rot he always must,
And will rot, and will rot.

%% 050.0399 %%
%% J'ai comme on a pu le voir augmente et revu ce poeme de trente-huit couplets. %%
As one may have observed, I have expanded and revised this poem to thirty-eight stanzas.

%% 050.0400 %%
%% Nous avons fait cela avec Collignon qui est une femme serieuse. %%
We did this with Collignon, who is a serious woman.

%% 050.0401 %%
%% J'ai deja commence a copier et l'on recevra cette beaute sous peu de temps. %%
I have already begun to copy it out, and the recipient will receive this masterpiece before long.

%% 050.0402 %%
%% Suis-je assez miserable quand il pleut ! Je ne suis pas sortie, je n'ai vu personne. Je ne sais rien de ce qui ce fait ! Sommes-nous assez seuls, assez abandonnes ! %%
Am I not wretched enough when it rains! I did not go out, I saw no one. I know nothing of what is happening! Are we not sufficiently alone, sufficiently abandoned!

%% 050.0403 %%
%% Varpahovsky a dine ici. %%
Varpahovsky dined here.

%% 050.0404 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/people/writers/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% Que la vanite des hommes est grande ! pensai-je en regardant le chateau, si Audiffret n'avait pas cette grande masse de pierres je n'en parlerais meme pas. %%
How great is the vanity of men! I thought, looking at the château. If Audiffret did not have that great mass of stone, I should not speak of him at all.

%% 050.0405 %%
%% Chaque fois que je suis prete a l'abandonner je n'ai qu'a regarder son chateau pour me dire: Non, il a un trop beau chateau. %%
Every time I am ready to give him up I have only to look at his château to tell myself: No — he has too fine a château.

%% 050.0406 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/people/writers/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% Et pourtant que me fait ce chateau, puisque je ne desire meme pas l'avoir. Tout seul oui, mais avec Audiffret dedans non. %%
And yet what does the château matter to me, since I do not even desire to have it? Alone, yes — but with Audiffret inside it, no.

%% 050.0407 %%
%% Georges, cet homme cheri, est ici dans un etat impossible a imaginer. Heureusement il ne fait pas de tapage, et on le cache de moi. %%
Georges, that cherished man, is here in a state impossible to imagine. Fortunately he makes no racket, and they keep him hidden from me.

%% 050.0408 %%
%% Ah ! que c'est embetant, la vie ! %%
Ah! How tiresome life is!
