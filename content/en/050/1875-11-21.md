Do you know the land where the orange tree blooms,
The land of golden fruit and crimson roses,
Where the breeze is softer and the bird more light,
Where in every season the bees gather honey,
Where, like a blessing from God, there shines and smiles
An eternal spring beneath a sky forever blue?
Alas, that I cannot follow you
To that far land from which fate exiled me!
It is there that I would live,
Love, love, and die!
