%% 050.0443 %%
# Saturday, 4 December 1875

%% 050.0444 %%
%% Hier, a la gare, Audiffret s'est approche de maman, qui allait a Monaco. Il avait l'air le plus canaille du monde avec son long paletot jaune degrafe et flottant, en societe de Gros et de Mayer l'ex-banquier. %%
Yesterday at the station, Audiffret approached Maman, who was going to Monaco. He had the most scoundrel-like air in the world — his long yellow overcoat unfastened and flapping — in the company of Gros and Mayer the former banker.

%% 050.0445 %%
%% [#Music_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MUSIC_THEME.md) %%
%% [#Theater_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/THEATER.md) %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) [#Italian](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/ITALIAN.md) [#French](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/FRENCH.md) %%
%% Il faut etre moi pour avoir pu m'humilier jusqu'a m'occuper d'un Audiffret. Et je suis bien honteuse, mais la veritablement honteuse et confuse. Un paysan nicois qui s'encanaille avec des Gros et Cie fi ! Tant qu'il se conduisait bien, ca allait encore. Mais a present, le theatre, les chanteuses, les cocottes et Gros. Il en est tout sale. Vous ne croirez peut-etre pas, mais je le dirai tout de meme. Non seulement je ne l'aime pas, mais je ne m'en occupe meme plus, je le trouve paysan, canaille, insignifiant. J'allais dire degradant, mais c'est trop fort, cela ferait croire qu'il m'est quelque chose. Je ne veux plus le cribler d'injures, il ne le merite pas. Un petit creve nicois. Ecco. %%
It would take someone like me to have been capable of humiliating herself so far as to occupy herself with an Audiffret. And I am well and truly ashamed — genuinely ashamed and mortified. A Nice peasant who degrades himself with the likes of Gros & Co. — fie! So long as he conducted himself well, it still passed. But now — the theatre, the singers, the *cocottes*, and Gros. He is all tainted by it. You may not believe me, but I shall say it all the same: not only do I not love him — I no longer even occupy myself with him. I find him a peasant, a scoundrel, insignificant. I was about to say *degrading*, but that is too strong — it would suggest he is something to me. I no longer wish to shower him with insults: he does not merit them. A little Nice fop.[^1] *Ecco.*[^2]

[^1]: *un petit crève nicois*: a Nice dandy or fop — an affected young man of the pleasure-seeking class, the term implying something hollow and pretentious.
[^2]: *Ecco* (Italian): there you have it; Marie's code-switching for dismissive finality.

%% 050.0446 %%
%% A propos d'Audiffret, maman en a reve hier. Elle voyait une grande procession religieuse, et derriere tout le monde, sur une voiture a deux roues, tres haute et toute noire, Audiffret en soutane noire avec une croix blanche sur la poitrine. %%
On the subject of Audiffret — Maman dreamed of him yesterday. She saw a great religious procession, and at the very rear, on a two-wheeled vehicle, very high and entirely black, Audiffret in a black soutane with a white cross on his chest.

%% 050.0447 %%
%% - "Maman, regarde, lui dis-je en designant Audiffret, voila un bon et vrai catholique". %%
"Maman, look," I said to her in the dream, pointing at Audiffret. "There is a good and true Catholic."

%% 050.0448 %%
%% A ces mots elle se reveille. Ce reve ne lui presage, a Audiffret rien de bon. %%
At those words she woke. This dream augurs nothing good for Audiffret.

%% 050.0449 %%
%% S'il pouvait crever, il me ferait plaisir. Je souhaite du mal a tout le monde. %%
If he could croak, he would do me a favour. I wish ill to everyone.

%% 050.0450 %%
%% [#Religion](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/RELIGION.md) %%
%% [#Theater_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/THEATER.md) %%
%% [#Emotions](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/EMOTIONS.md) %%
%% J'ai pleure chez maman et a dejeuner j'ai annonce que j'allais etudier le chant et me faire actrice. Et je le ferai si Dieu veut bien me conserver ma voix je le ferai. C'est le seul moyen pour moi d'acquerir cette celebrite dont j'ai soif. Pour laquelle je donnerai dix annees de ma vie sans hesiter. Je ne peux pas moisir ! %%
I wept in Maman's room, and at luncheon I announced that I am going to study singing and become an actress. And I shall do it — if God is willing to preserve my voice, I shall do it. It is the only means for me to acquire the celebrity for which I thirst. For which I would give ten years of my life without hesitation. I will not rot![^3]

[^3]: *moisir*: to mould, to rot — Marie's recurring metaphor for wasting away in social isolation and obscurity.

%% 050.0451 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/people/writers/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Philosophy](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/PHILOSOPHY.md) [#Latin](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/LATIN.md) [#French](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/FRENCH.md) %%
%% Il me faut du bruit, de l'eclat, de la gloire ! Et j'en aurai Deo juvante %%
I need noise, brilliance, glory! And I shall have it — *Deo juvante.*[^4]

[^4]: *Deo juvante* (Latin): God willing; with God's help.

%% 050.0452 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/people/writers/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% Il n'est jamais arrive qu'on le veuille et qu'on ne l'aie pas. J'ai les idees les plus vastes du monde. Je me desole a cause du manque de societe. Foin de tout cela ! Est-ce cela que je veux ! %%
It has never happened that one truly desires something and does not obtain it. I have the grandest ideas in the world. I grieve over the lack of society. A fig for all that! Is that what I want!

%% 050.0453 %%
%% Cent fois non ! Mille fois non ! %%
A hundred times no! A thousand times no!

%% 050.0454 %%
%% Je suis nee pour etre une femme remarquable peu importe par quoi et comment. %%
I was born to be a remarkable woman — no matter in what way or by what means.

%% 050.0455 %%
%% Toutes mes tendances vont vers les grands de ce monde, vers les tetes couronnees, elles seules sont mes egales, le reste est sous mes pieds, si bas, si bas que c'est extraordinaire. %%
All my inclinations tend towards the great of this world, towards crowned heads. They alone are my equals; the rest is beneath my feet — so far beneath that it is extraordinary.

%% 050.0456 %%
%% [#Religion](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/RELIGION.md) %%
%% [#Emotions](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/EMOTIONS.md) %%
%% [#Mortality](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MORTALITY.md) [#Latin](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/LATIN.md) [#French](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/FRENCH.md) %%
%% Je serai celebre, je serai grande ou je mourrai. Il est impossible que Dieu m'ait donne cette *gioriae cupiditate* comme a Severe, pour rien, sans but. Mon temps viendra et je le crois si fermement que je crache sur tout et sur tous. Je suis heureuse quand je pense comme aujourd'hui. %%
I shall be celebrated, I shall be great — or I shall die. It is impossible that God should have given me this *gloriae cupiditate*[^5] as He gave it to Severus, for nothing, without purpose. My time will come, and I believe it so firmly that I spit on everything and everyone. I am happy when I think as I think today.

[^5]: *gloriae cupiditate* (Latin): desire for glory; from Tacitus on the Emperor Severus. Marie, who knew her Latin, invokes this classical tag as self-identification with greatness.

%% 050.0457 %%
%% O ma voix ! %%
O my voice!

%% 050.0458 %%
%% [#Music_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MUSIC_THEME.md) %%
%% Je suis allee a la musique, il y avait beaucoup de monde, mais rien de plus curieux que la Vigier. %%
I went to the concert; there was a great crowd, but nothing more remarkable than Mme Vigier.

%% 050.0459 %%
%% J'ai pousse un petit cri en la voyant. Jamais on ne s'imaginera rien de plus absurdement extravagant et ridicule. %%
I let out a small cry on seeing her. One could never imagine anything more absurdly extravagant and ridiculous.

%% 050.0460 %%
%% [#Music_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MUSIC_THEME.md) %%
%% [#Theater_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/THEATER.md) %%
%% Nous sommes allees a l'Opera prendre une loge pour ce soir, on donne "Le Barbier", mon petit opera favori. Je sais que le Surprenant n'y sera pas, il est a Menton pour quelques jours, il l'a dit a maman. Mais que m'importe. Je ne me pardonnerai jamais de m'etre occupee d'un pareil saligaud. Je suis dans des nuages dores. J'aspire a quelque chose d'inoui, de fabuleux, je veux me reveler au monde. Je veux etre celebre. Je chanterai. %%
We went to the Opera to take a box for this evening — they are performing *Il Barbiere*,[^6] my favourite little opera. I know the Surprising One will not be there — he is in Menton for a few days; he told Maman as much. But what do I care. I shall never forgive myself for having occupied myself with such a wretch. I am in golden clouds. I aspire to something unheard of, fabulous — I want to reveal myself to the world. I want to be celebrated. I shall sing.

[^6]: *Il Barbiere di Siviglia* (*The Barber of Seville*): opera by Rossini (1816).

%% 050.0461 %%
%% C'est drole, toute la troupe italienne me salue. %%
It is amusing — the entire Italian company bows to me.

%% 050.0462 %%
%% Nous sommes ce soir au n 2, ou au n 2 en face se trouve le vieux chien fourbu. %%
This evening we are in box No. 2, and directly opposite in No. 2 is the old worn-out dog.

%% 050.0463 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/people/writers/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) [#English](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/ENGLISH.md) [#French](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/FRENCH.md) %%
%% J'ai ma robe Empire, avec laquelle je m'aime le mieux, une coiffure de deesse de l'Olympe et des cheveux tombant plus bas que la ceinture et boucles naturellement au bout. Une figure radieuse et fraiche sans etre rouge comme un roastbeef ou comme Helene Howard. Je suis comme d'habitude fort remarquee, mais Bihovetz et le cardinal Antonelli seuls viennent nous voir. %%
I have my Empire gown,[^7] in which I like myself best; my hair dressed like an Olympian goddess, falling below the waist and naturally curled at the ends. A face radiant and fresh — without being red as a ==roastbeef==[^8] or like Hélène Howard. As usual I am much remarked upon — but only Bihovetz and Cardinal Antonelli come to see us.

[^7]: *robe Empire*: a high-waisted neoclassical style dress; a fashionable revival in the 1870s.
[^8]: *roastbeef*: in English in the original. A Franco-English term of mockery for a ruddy complexion, here applied as an insult.

%% 050.0464 %%
%% [Dans la marge: C'est Antonoff qu'on a surnomme cardinal Antonelli ] %%
[In the margin: Antonoff is the one who has been nicknamed Cardinal Antonelli]

%% 050.0465 %%
%% Quand Bihovetz reste seul je suis plus a l'aise. Il est dit que si ce n'est le fils c'est le pere, si ce n'est le pere c'est le chien, si ce n'est le chien c'est le cheval mais seulement le pere. Il m'a lorgne comme un fou ou comme un ami. Je laissais faire, et ne regardais pas de son cote afin qu'il put m'examiner a loisir, etant fort satisfaite de moi ce soir. Le general est comme toujours charmant. %%
When Bihovetz is alone I am more at ease. It is decreed that if not the son, then the father; if not the father, then the dog; if not the dog, then the horse — but in this case, the father only. He studied me through his glass like a madman, or like a friend. I let him, and did not look his way, so that he might examine me at leisure — being very well satisfied with myself this evening. The general is, as always, charming.

%% 050.0466 %%
%% — Tenez, lui dis-je, savez-vous ce que je vais faire ? %%
"Listen," I said to him. "Do you know what I am going to do?"

%% 050.0467 %%
%% — Qu'allez-vous faire Mademoiselle ? %%
"What are you going to do, Mademoiselle?"

%% 050.0468 %%
%% — Je vais faire le miroir. %%
"I am going to play the mirror."

%% 050.0469 %%
%% — Comment ? %%
"What do you mean?"

%% 050.0470 %%
%% — Regardez. %%
"Watch."

%% 050.0471 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% Et je prends la pose de papa, il met sa main sur la rampe, j'en fais autant, il s'appuie sur sa main, je m'appuie sur la mienne, il se renverse dans son fauteuil, je me renverse dans le mien, il joue avec sa chaine, je joue avec mon ruban, il s'assied cavaliererement et appuie la main sur ses genoux, je l'imite, il met le poing sur la hanche, j'en fais autant, il se tire l'oreille, je me tire l'oreille aussi, enfin il se tord la moustache, je fais semblant de me tordre ma moustache imaginaire. %%
And I take papa's pose — he puts his hand on the rail of the box, I do the same; he leans on his hand, I lean on mine; he leans back in his armchair, I lean back in mine; he plays with his watch-chain, I play with my ribbon; he sits carelessly and rests his hand on his knee, I imitate him; he puts his fist on his hip, I do the same; he pulls his ear, I pull mine; and finally he twists his moustache — and I pretend to twist my imaginary moustache.

%% 050.0472 %%
%% Je ris et lui sourit doucement. %%
I laugh; he smiles gently.

%% 050.0473 %%
%% [#Theater_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/THEATER.md) %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% Le general rit, Dina rit, ma tante rit. Chaque minute papa change la pose de sa main je l'imite comme le plus fidele miroir. C'est le dernier acte, le theatre est a moitie vide et je continue mon jeu en liberte jusqu'au dernier moment. Il met son paletot, je mets ma pelisse, il s'approche de la rampe, moi aussi. La toile se baisse, je m'arrete un instant avant de sortir, papa en fait autant, me regarde et ote son chapeau. %%
The general laughs, Dina laughs, my aunt laughs. Every minute papa changes the position of his hand; I imitate him like the most faithful mirror. It is the last act — the theatre is half empty — and I continue my game freely until the final moment. He puts on his overcoat; I put on my pelisse. He approaches the front of the box; so do I. The curtain falls; I pause for a moment before leaving — papa does likewise, looks at me, and takes off his hat.

%% 050.0474 %%
%% A droite il n'y avait personne, a gauche quatre loges etaient vides, c'est bien moi qu'il a saluee. A moins que ce ne soit quelqu'un en bas, mais non, puisqu'il me regardait. %%
To the right there was no one; to the left four boxes were empty. It is certainly me he bowed to. Unless it was someone downstairs — but no, for he was looking at me.

%% 050.0475 %%
%% Je sors en sautant de joie. Pendant que nous attendons notre voiture l'immense Pepino vient me saluer. %%
I go out leaping with joy. While we wait for our carriage, the immense Pepino comes to greet me.

%% 050.0476 %%
%% [#Health](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/HEALTH.md) %%
%% - Est-ce que vous etes malade, qu'on ne vous voit plus ? lui dis-je. %%
"Are you ill, that one no longer sees you?" I ask him.

%% 050.0477 %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
%% Je declare a Antonelli et au general que je viens de tomber subitement amoureuse du vieil Audiffret, et ils nous mettent en voiture. %%
I declare to Antonelli and the general that I have just fallen suddenly in love with old Audiffret — and they hand us into the carriage.

%% 050.0478 %%
%% Je rentre riante et gaie et bavarde de papa. %%
I return home laughing and gay, chattering about papa.

%% 050.0479 %%
%% [#Religion](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/RELIGION.md) %%
%% [#Emotions](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/EMOTIONS.md) [#Italian](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/ITALIAN.md) [#French](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/FRENCH.md) %%
%% Mais une fois devant la glace: Ah ! ma tante, je dirai comme Rosine, a che serve lo spirito, [a que ?] este giova la bellezza fra quattro mura, mi hare d'esser proprio in sepoltura ! et je fonds en larmes en me voyant si jolie, en voyant mes bras et mon cou divins. %%
But once before the mirror: "Ah, Auntie, I shall say with Rosina — ==*a che serve lo spirito, a che giova la bellezza fra quattro mura, mi pare d'esser proprio in sepoltura!*==[^9]" — and I dissolve in tears at seeing myself so pretty, at seeing my divine arms and neck.

[^9]: Rosina's aria from *Il Barbiere di Siviglia*, Act I (Italian): "What good is wit, what good is beauty within four walls — I might as well be in a tomb!" Marie quotes from memory with slight variations.

%% 050.0480 %%
%% [#Emotions](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/EMOTIONS.md) %%
%% — Il vaut mieux etouffer les enfants au moment de leur naissance, dis-je a travers les larmes, que de les martyriser comme vous me martyrisez ! De quoi etes-vous faites ! De bois, de marbre ! Cela ne vous fait donc rien de voir que je me dechire ! %%
"It would be better to smother children at the moment of their birth," I say through tears, "than to martyrise them as you martyrise me! What are you made of — wood, marble? Does it mean nothing to you to see me tearing myself apart!"

%% 050.0481 %%
%% — Mais que faire ! %%
"But what is to be done!"

%% 050.0482 %%
%% [#Emotions](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/EMOTIONS.md) %%
%% [#Health](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/HEALTH.md) %%
%% — Que faire ! Que font les autres ! Ah ! tenez vous etes des miserables ! Chacun pense a ses enfants, et vous ! Peu vous importe que je pleure, que je souffre, que je moisis ! Je ne veux pas moisir, je ne veux pas, je ne veux pas, je ne veux pas ! Entendez-vous ! %%
"What is to be done! What do other people do! Ah — you are miserable wretches! Everyone thinks of their children — and you! It matters little to you that I weep, that I suffer, that I rot! I will not rot — I will not, I will not, I will not! Do you hear me!"

%% 050.0483 %%
%% — Ou prendre de l'argent ! %%
"Where are we to find the money!"

%% 050.0484 %%
%% [#Religion](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/RELIGION.md) %%
%% — Pourquoi de l'argent, etre dans la societe ne coute rien, est-ce que Bihovetz vous coute cher, Pepino vous coute cher ? Les autres couteront donc plus ! Mon Dieu, mon Dieu ! %%
"Why money? Being in society costs nothing — does Bihovetz cost you dear? Does Pepino cost you dear? Will the others cost more then? My God, my God!"

%% Je vous prie ne riez pas, vous qui ne lirez jamais ce journal, si vous etiez a ma place vous me comprendriez et vous pleureriez comme je pleure. J'ai dix-sept ans ! Depuis quatre ans on me dit: Attends, quand tu seras grande tout changera ! J'ai attendu, je suis grande ! J'ai dix-sept ans ! Je moisis, moi qui veux vivre en courant, moi qui ai la fievre de la vie, moi qui voudrais tout absorber, tout envahir, etre partout, moi je suis enchainee, cachee, seule, miserable ! Dieu, Dieu, Dieu, Dieu, Dieu ! Je ne trouve pas d'autres paroles que celle-la. Dieu ! Dieu ! %%
I beg you — do not laugh, you who will never read this journal. If you were in my place you would understand me, and you would weep as I weep. I am seventeen! For four years I have been told: *Wait — when you are grown everything will change!* I have waited; I am grown. I am seventeen! I rot — I, who want to live at a run; I, who have the fever of life; I, who would absorb everything, invade everything, be everywhere — I am enchained, hidden, alone, wretched! God, God, God, God, God! I find no other word but that. God! God!
%% 2026-03-05T11:00:00 TR: "vous qui ne lirez jamais ce journal" — Marie's direct address to imagined future readers; she simultaneously denies and invites audience, one of the diary's recurring self-conscious gestures. %%

God! Hear me then. God — have pity on me!

%% 050.0487 %%

%% Dieu tourne-toi vers la plus miserable de tes creatures, Dieu sois bon. Dieu sois misericordieux ! Dieu ne sois pas toujours le Dieu de la vengeance et du chatiment. 0 Sainte Vierge Marie et Jesus, priez Dieu pour moi ! %%
God — turn towards the most wretched of Your creatures. God, be good. God, be merciful! God — be not always the God of vengeance and punishment. O Holy Virgin Mary and Jesus — pray God for me!
