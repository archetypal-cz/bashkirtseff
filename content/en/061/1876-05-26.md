# Friday, 26 May 1876

%% 061.0155 %%
%% Je suis sortie ce matin et j'ai mis ma lettre ou plutôt ma fleur à la poste. %%
I went out this morning and posted my letter — or rather my flower.

"Antonelli is nothing but a…" — I omit the word my aunt used; she is fond of words like that. "For," she continued, "he is nothing but a child."

"Oh! That is true," said Maman; "they get everything from him. They make him fast for three days, give him not a penny, and dictate the law to him."

%% 061.0158 %%
%% Bon ! Voilà quelque chose pour me faire rager, sans le vouloir, je sais. %%
Well! There is something to make me furious — unintentionally, I know.

Those words, perfectly true, make it abundantly clear to me that I have dirtied myself for nothing. For I have dirtied myself — without love and without advantage! It is vexing. No person in the world is more sensitive[^1] than I am. Since my face and my lips have been defiled I feel soiled — as though after twenty-four hours of railway travel.

Everyone will say that we loved each other but that the young man's family would not have me — no one will say that the refusal came from me. We are neither loved enough nor great enough for that.
