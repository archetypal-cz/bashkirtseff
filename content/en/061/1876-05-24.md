"Mme de Camprien has just left," continued Maman in great agitation. "She came in all haste to warn me. An Italian count has arrived from Rome — she would not say his name — who has applied to them because Madame is Russian, married to a Frenchman acquainted with Monseigneur Zamboni of Rome, and who can better than anyone supply every possible piece of information. She also told me he mentioned some great official scandal involving Paul and begged me to say whether it was true, so as to smooth things over if there are rough edges,[^2] and prevent this count from going to seek information at the central police commissioner's. I told her it was not true. As for Georges — he is a man who lives with a kept woman and has done disgraceful things, but he is no concern of ours."
