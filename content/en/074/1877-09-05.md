# Wednesday, 5 September 1877

%% 074.0229 %%
%% [#Health](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/HEALTH.md) %%
%% [#Schwalbach](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/SCHWALBACH.md) %%
%% Nous avons répété la promenade à Schwalbach, mais en voiture, et vu le même monde qu'hier. J'étais indisposée et nous sommes rentrés de bonne heure. %%
We repeated the excursion to Schwalbach, this time by carriage, and saw the same people as yesterday. I was indisposed and we came home early.

%% 074.0230 %%
%% [#Maman](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/MAMAN.md) %%
%% [#Wiesbaden](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/WIESBADEN.md) %%
%% [#Health](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/HEALTH.md) %%
%% [#Oncle_Lautrec](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/ONCLE_LAUTREC.md) %%
%% On devait aller à Wiesbaden où Lautrec nous attend ayant fait des dépenses (il est très avare) pour nos billets, au bal des artistes, savants etc. qui a lieu ce soir. Maman est malade et je suis contente de ne pas voyager. %%
We were to go to Wiesbaden, where Lautrec is expecting us, having gone to some expense (he is very miserly) over our tickets to the ball of artists, scholars, etc., which takes place this evening. Maman is ill and I am glad not to travel.

And I, who in the Underworld had assigned you a place among ==the suspended ones==[^1], where Virgil is, and all those who cannot go to paradise despite their virtues but cannot be sent to Hell either, and who hang suspended between the two! You deserve to be beside Lucifer himself, at the very bottom. Could you be cross about the trinity? No, surely not.
