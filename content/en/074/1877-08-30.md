# Thursday, 30 August 1877

%% 074.0143 %%
%% [#Wiesbaden](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/WIESBADEN.md) %%
%% 2026-02-09T16:00:00 RSR: Marie's vow of silence (from previous entry) worked — she learns that the Shipka Pass remains in Russian hands. %%
%% Je n'ai pas parlé et ce soir à Wiesbaden nous avons appris que Chibka est aux Russes, que les Turcs sont battus (du moins dans le moment) et que de grands renforts nous arrivent. %%
I did not speak, and this evening at Wiesbaden we learned that Shipka is in Russian hands, that the Turks have been beaten (at least for the moment), and that great reinforcements are coming to us.

%% TR: "En travers" = written across the page; editorial note preserved. %%
This news is conveyed to us by [written across the page: the Baron von Bechman], an embassy attaché in Paris who has been decorated by France for acts of treachery, and who regained Bismarck's favour by delivering up the Count d'Arnim to him. Mme Bechman, I am told, has made many advances which were crowned this evening by her sharing this despatch — which they have through the embassy — announcing good news for us. Lautrec will be, I believe, our go-between.

%% [#Baden_Baden](../../_original/_glossary/places/travel/BADEN_BADEN.md) %%
%% 2026-02-09T12:00:00 LAN: "l'affaire de toujours" - the perpetual matter; Marie's ongoing battle with family %%
%% 2026-02-09T12:00:00 LAN: "faire des scènes" - to make scenes; create emotional outbursts %%
%% 2026-02-09T12:00:00 LAN: "un parti pris" - a foregone conclusion/prejudice %%
%% Au retour j'ai eu une nouvelle conversation... ou plutôt non, j'ai parlé de l'affaire de toujours et après m'avoir exaspérée par un silence honteux, on m'a rendue folle en criant que je ne savais ce que je voulais, que je faisais des scènes pour tout, que toujours, dans tout et pour tout je ne faisais que des querelles etc. etc. Toujours, dans tout et pour tout, ces trois phrases répétées avec des variations infinies, entremêlées de souvenirs de ce que j'avais fait en Russie ou à Spa, ou à Baden-Baden. On ne peut rien contre un parti pris. Et il est impossible de raconter ces réponses infâmes, lâches, imbéciles, entêtées, sur un seul point quoi qu'on dise, supplie, maudisse, pleure ! %%
On my return I had a new conversation... or rather, no: I spoke of the perpetual matter, and after they had exasperated me with a shameful silence, they drove me mad by shouting that I did not know what I wanted, that I made scenes about everything, that always, in everything and for everything, I did nothing but quarrel, etc., etc. Always, in everything and for everything — these three phrases repeated with infinite variations, interspersed with memories of what I had done in Russia, or at Spa, or at Baden-Baden. One can do nothing against a foregone conclusion. And it is impossible to recount those infamous, cowardly, imbecile, stubborn answers — on one single point no matter what one says, begs, curses, weeps!

%% 074.0147 %%
%% [#Emotions](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/EMOTIONS.md) %%
%% Rien de plus navrant et risible que de parler de recommandations à l'ambassade avec des larmes, des cris. Je criais parce qu'on m'empêchait de parler en criant des stupidités. %%
Nothing is more heartbreaking and ridiculous than to speak of embassy introductions through tears and shouts. I shouted because they prevented me from speaking by shouting stupidities.

%% 074.0148 %%
%% Et c'est aussi impatientant en diable. Il y a des choses qu'on est gêné de préciser, d'exiger, et [on] m'y forçait pour m'embarrasser. %%
And it is also infernally maddening. There are things one is reluctant to specify, to demand — and they forced me to, in order to embarrass me.

%% 074.0149 %%
%% Peut-on être aussi... mauvais, mauvais, mauvais ! %%
Can one be so... wicked, wicked, wicked!

%% 074.0150 %%
%% [#Emotions](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/EMOTIONS.md) %%
%% Je me pleure comme j'ai pleuré le pavillon dont on m'avait dégoûtée. %%
I weep for myself as I once wept for the villa they had sickened me of.

%% 074.0151 %%
%% Qu'est-ce que ça coûterait de me répondre amicalement, de tâcher avec moi de trouver quelque moyen, au lieu de me blaguer impitoyablement, de m'exaspérer ! ! %%
What would it cost to answer me in a friendly way, to try with me to find some means, instead of mocking me pitilessly, exasperating me!!

%% 074.0152 %%
%% Quoi de plus horrible qu'un drame à propos d'une invitation. On est tout honteux d'insister sur ces choses-là... %%
What is more horrible than a drama over an invitation? One is quite ashamed to press on such things...

%% 074.0153 %%
%% - Tu ne sais pas ce que tu veux, tu fais cela dans tout, en tout, et pour tout. %%
"You don't know what you want; you do this about everything, in everything, and for everything."

%% 074.0154 %%
%% Et puis, voilà. %%
And there you have it.

%% 074.0155 %%
%% 2026-02-09T12:00:00 LAN: "acquit de conscience" - clearing of conscience; doing something only so one cannot be reproached later %%
%% A présent je ne veux plus rien, ce que je dis c'est par un dernier acquit de conscience, mais je ne veux plus rien. Comment puis-je m'amuser, ou sortir, ou danser ? %%
Now I want nothing more; what I say is for a last easing of conscience — but I want nothing more. How can I enjoy myself, or go out, or dance?

%% 074.0156 %%
%% J'ai perdu la confiance, en moi-même et en tout. J'ai peur de tout. Et tous mes plaisirs futurs sont tellement arrosés de larmes et couverts de souffrances, d'exaspérations et d'humiliations qu'on ne les voie plus à travers et que je serais honteuse d'en jouir. %%
I have lost confidence — in myself and in everything. I am afraid of everything. And all my future pleasures are so drenched in tears and covered with suffering, exasperation, and humiliations that one cannot see them through all that, and I would be ashamed to enjoy them.

%% 074.0158 %%

When I was small, every one of my pleasures was preceded by a three-hour scene, which did not at all prevent my being "adored" — and yet I enjoyed nothing; I had wept away all the pleasure I might have had. So it is now.

And I am even sadder when I begin to grieve that it is so, and to weep because I cannot [Crossed out: be able to] enjoy what I obtain — disgusted, anxious, on edge, afraid of everything, not daring to take a step [Bottom of page removed] or say a word.

%% 074.0160 %%
%% 2026-02-09T12:00:00 LAN: "j'ai beau écrire" - no matter how much I write %%
%% J'ai beau écrire, on ne comprendra jamais quelle importance peuvent prendre les plus misérables choses, et comme on peut en être malheureux. %%
No matter how much I write, one will never understand what importance the most wretched things can assume, and how unhappy they can make one.

%% 074.0161 %%
%% Que pourrais-je sacrifier pour vivre ? Tout. Sans exceptions et restrictions, tout, tout. Et comme ce serait mal, j'en serais punie par des remords que j'aurais mérités. Et soyez tranquilles, je ne m'en plaindrai pas ! %%
What could I sacrifice to live? Everything. Without exceptions or restrictions — everything, everything. And since it would be wrong, I should be punished for it by the remorse I would have deserved. And rest assured, I shall not complain!

%% 074.0162 %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
%% Dans des moments comme celui-là je me demande très sérieusement: à quoi bon tout ce que je désire ? Et je ne comprends ni le monde, ni la gloire, ni l'amour. %%
In moments like this I ask myself very seriously: what is the use of everything I desire? And I understand neither the world, nor glory, nor love.

%% 074.0163 %%
%% Je suis morte. Comment ne pas être au désespoir ? %%
I am dead. How can one not be in despair?
