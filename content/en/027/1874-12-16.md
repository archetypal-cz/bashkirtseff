Wednesday, 16 December 1874.

Lies! A day less happy than the others — quite the contrary — for during my ride with Foster and Dina I was constantly troubled by either my black riding habit, or the hat, or the horse. The weather was magnificent — cold and almost clear. Morgan dined with us: an Englishman of eighteen, well-dressed and presentable, but ==""of no consequence."==[^1] My new dog is a ==pur dog==[^2] — a purebred.

[^1]: *In English in the original.*
[^2]: *In English in the original* — "a purebred dog." Marie uses the English phrase "pur dog" (from *pure-bred dog*) in a French sentence, reflecting Nice's cosmopolitan expatriate community.
