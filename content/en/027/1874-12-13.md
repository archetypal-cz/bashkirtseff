# Sunday, 13 December 1874

I sleep until twelve; at two I set to work again drafting and composing the *answers*, until five — without once leaving Maman's room. Yesterday I drafted Maman's answers; today, my aunt's.

%% 2026-01-30T14:40:00 LAN: QUIRK: "Je me suis née" - I was born myself (reflexive error); Walitsky's grammatical mistake, should be "Je suis née" %%
%% Markevitch a dîné ici. Walitsky a fait ses réponses lui-même et le pauvre commence ainsi: *Je me suis née* etc. %%
Markevitch dined here. Walitsky has composed his own answers, and the poor man begins thus: *Je me suis née*[^1] — etc.

[^1]: *Je me suis née* — a grammatical blunder: the reflexive "me" is superfluous; correct French would be simply *Je suis née* ("I was born"). Marie quotes it with evident amusement at Walitsky's ineptitude.
