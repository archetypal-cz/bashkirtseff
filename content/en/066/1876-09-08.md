%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶963, Mon Journal t.9 p.64 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
Once again I held court on the staircase... I love staircases because one goes up... Pacha was to leave tomorrow, but I carried on so this evening that perhaps he will stay — or else he will go, which would be more sensible, for it is dangerous for a country fellow, a dreamer, a brooding young man of twenty-two, to love me like a sister. With him and Michel I am on the best possible terms, which is why they are so fond of me. But when I am with stupid men I become stupid myself; I do not know what to say that they would understand, and I am afraid every moment that they will suspect me of being in love with them. Like poor Gritz: he thinks all the young ladies want him, and in the slightest smile he sees ambushes and plots against his celibacy. Do you so much as know the etymology of that word?
