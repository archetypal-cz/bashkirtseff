# Friday, 15 September 1876 (3 September)
%% Ayant passé la nuit dans le lit et dans la maison du Prince, je me levai un peu fatiguée et lorsque Paul m'amena le petit Etienne, le fils d'Alexandre, je ne le reconnus pas le premier moment. %%

Having spent the night in the Prince's bed and in the Prince's house, I got up a little tired, and when Paul brought me little Étienne, Alexandre's son, I did not recognise him at first.

%% Je ne fis aucune attention au plus ou moins de plaisir que causait à mon père la vue d’un Babanine et je m’occupai du gentil petit lorsque il entra tenant Michel par la main et le présenta à Etienne comme mon fils ? Je crois que le petit le crut, car il joua et causa avec le principino comme avec un égal, au grand amusement de nous tous. %%
%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶1109, Mon Journal t.9 p.76 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%

I paid no attention to how much or how little pleasure the sight of a Babanine gave my father, and I was busying myself with the dear little boy when he came in holding Michel by the hand and introduced him to Étienne as my son? I believe the little one believed it, for he played and chatted with the *principino*[^1] as with an equal, to the great amusement of us all.

The Emperor, to make amends for his valet's misdeed, married her to a M. Martinoff, who was given a governorship.

Mme Martinoff saw us out as far as the anteroom, and we set off again in search of "suitable people."
