The horse I have been spoiling with lumps of sugar, as you know, having had two rides without a saddle, now refuses to be harnessed at all. From lively he has become ungovernable — he gallops, he leaps; a true *cheval soumis*,[^1] as they call the trace-horse in Russia that is harnessed to the side and which bounds and caracoles with its head bent outward. I mean to bring him to reason, and today I went for a drive of fifty metres in the tilbury
%% 2026-03-06T12:30:00 RED: Original says "50 mètres" — no unit conversion needed; "yards" was an error. %%, with my blasted father holding the reins whenever I could no longer manage. I now see that the Russian coachman's posture is not affectation, but a necessity for controlling frantic beasts. My hands are swollen from it — but it flatters my vanity.

[^1]: The *cheval soumis* (literally "trace-horse") is the side-horse in a Russian troika, which runs in a controlled sideways canter with its head turned outward while the centre horse trots straight.
