%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶919, Mon Journal t.9  (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
Michel arrived for dinner, and would you believe it? I was almost glad of it, I am so bored; besides, he is very much in love with me, and that amuses me. You know, he had nothing more pressing to do than announce it to all Poltava, adding that Miloradovitch *was standing in his way*. In the way of what? Oh! the poor creature. I grew lively in the evening, thanks to the delightful hotbed of intrigue that was revealed to me. Pacha detests Mme Tutcheff for her haughty airs, and above all for her way of slandering people and spreading gossip. I am very glad to hear it! So it is not an invention of Maman's. Michel detests her because of his brother, and the Princess does not like her for the same reason. All this was said in front of me, out loud; I joined in gaily, but prudently.

I am like our ancient Prince Oleg, who, at a time when war was waged in the most treacherous way imaginable without anyone thinking ill of it (for Oleg was regent for Igor, son of Rurik, the first Russian prince, in the ninth century), in that savage age in Russia, Oleg never attacked a nation without first sending it his eternal: "I am marching against you."
