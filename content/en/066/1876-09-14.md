[^1]: Russian *durak* (дурак): "fool." Marie's father puns on the cheer, shouting "fool" in place of "hurrah."
[^2]: "Connais-tu le pays où fleurit l'oranger?" — Mignon's aria from Act I of Ambroise Thomas's opera *Mignon* (1866), after Goethe's *Wilhelm Meister*.
[^3]: "Tu vois ce beau garçon-là, c'est l'amant d'A, c'est l'amant d'Amanda" — the refrain of "L'Amant d'Amanda," a café-concert song then all the rage; Marie's "he is the one who is afraid of Michel" at the station is a parody of it.
