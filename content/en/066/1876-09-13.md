# Wednesday, 13 September 1876 (1 September)
%% Hier nous fîmes au théâtre une de ces apparitions comme j'en ai rêvées à Nice du temps que la comtesse de Galve me semblait une déesse descendue du ciel. D'abord, moi, élégante comme toujours, et mes quatre cavaliers tous les quatre d'une parfaite distinction et cravatés de blanc. C'est bête mais ça fait de l'effet. %%
%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶1061, Mon Journal t.9 p.72 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
Yesterday at the theatre we made one of those appearances I used to dream of in Nice, in the days when the Comtesse de Galve seemed to me a goddess come down from heaven. First of all I myself, elegant as always, and my four escorts, all four of them perfectly distinguished and in white ties. It is silly, but it does make an impression.

An impression... on swine! An exit... before nobody! Chocolat holding the opera glasses, the Prince carrying my train — it was all in vain.

%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶1066, Mon Journal t.9 p.73 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
I went walking with my father, and in the evening we went to the theatre and sat in the left-hand stage box, the same as yesterday. This box is exactly like the one at Poltava, and I had the happiness of seeing Michel in the wings; but Michel, not daring to speak to the actresses, fraternised with the actors. Opposite there was indeed a sort of ==club box==[^1], and Paul, who had gone there to see an acquaintance, came back and repeated to me what he had heard said about me. But those people do not interest me; they are ugly, and I do not even know their names.
