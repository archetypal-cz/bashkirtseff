# Wednesday, 11 October 1876 (29 September)
%% Ces trois jours se résument en un mot : souffrance, souffrance et souffrance ! %%
%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶1630, Mon Journal t.9 p.115 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
These three days can be summed up in one word: suffering, suffering and suffering!

%% 066.0751 %%
%% [#Alexandre](../../_original/_glossary/people/core/ALEXANDRE.md) %%
%% Je n'avais pas assez des difficultés d'argent, de fermiers, d'Alexandre, de ces milles agacements navrants ! %%
%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶1631, Mon Journal t.9 p.115 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%

%% 066.0752 %%
%% [#Rome](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/ROME.md) %%
%% Il me faut une autre folie. Rome ! Rome dont j'adore chaque rue, chaque maison, chaque boutique de chapelets, chaque ruine, chaque église ! %%
%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶1632, Mon Journal t.9 p.115 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
I need another madness. Rome! Rome, whose every street, every house, every rosary shop, every ruin, every church I adore!

%% 066.0753 %%
%% C'est une fièvre, un délire et je n'en dirai que quelques mots qui suffisent, quelque peu nombreux qu'ils soient à me mettre dans un de ces états d'exaspération et de désespoir dont personne au monde ne peut se faire une idée. %%
%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶1633, Mon Journal t.9 p.115 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
It is a fever, a delirium, and I shall say only a few words about it, and those words, few as they are, are enough to throw me into one of those states of exasperation and despair that no one in the world can imagine.

%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶1634, Mon Journal t.9 p.115 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
All these nights I have slept in my father's room, and we sat up very late, taken up with tragicomic discussions. My father had barely begun a conjugal complaint before I would cry out that I had not married him off, that I was not his mother-in-law and that I knew nothing about it.

%% Mais j'en finissais toujours par discuter le voyage. Mon père est pour Paris, moi aussi mais la nuit dernière j'eus comme un accès de folie romaine. Non contente, comme les nuits précédentes, de jouer du violon je m'emparai du tam-tam japonais. J'arpentais la chambre et, moitié désespérée, moitié rieuse, je criais que je voulais aller à Rome et que si mon père ne m'y conduisait dans le monde cet hiver je m'empoisonnerais en écrivant dans mon journal et partout que c'est lui qui m'a tuée ! %%
%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶1636, Mon Journal t.9 p.115 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
But I always ended up discussing the journey. My father is in favour of Paris, and so am I, but last night I had a sort of fit of Roman madness. Not content, as on the previous nights, with playing the violin, I seized the Japanese gong. I paced up and down the room and, half in despair, half laughing, I cried that I wanted to go to Rome, and that if my father did not take me there, into society, this winter, I would poison myself, writing in my journal and everywhere else that it was he who had killed me!

> Amanda.
