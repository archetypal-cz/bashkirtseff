Et d'abord rassurez-vous, ces cosaques ne sont point de ces monstres sauvages qu'on aime à se figurer à l'étranger; ce sont de bons paysans bien niais, bien rusés et bien... tout au monde excepté féroces et sauvages. %%
%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶1602, Mon Journal t.9 p.113 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
A day of judgements *par excellence*. In the morning my father, who is chief justice of the peace, had to judge a dispute between two neighbouring Cossack villages. And first of all, set your minds at rest: these Cossacks are not at all the savage monsters people abroad like to imagine; they are good peasants, very simple, very cunning and very... everything in the world except ferocious and savage.

%% Le nom *Cosaque* provient dit-on de cosà (chèvre) et fut donné aux habitants de l'Ukraine (Petite Russie) à cause de leur agilité à la course !! %% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶1603, Mon Journal t.9 p.113 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
The name *Cossack* derives, they say, from *cosà* (goat) and was given to the inhabitants of the Ukraine (Little Russia) because of their swiftness in running!! But since it is humiliating for so glorious a people to take its name from such an animal, says a historian of the country, another etymology must be sought.

%% 066.0725 %%
%% Je ne la chercherai point et je me contenterai de dire qu'à notre époque on désigne sous le nom de cosaques ceux des paysans qui n'ont jamais été serfs de propriétaires et qui lors de l'esclavage en Russie étaient inscrits comme appartenant à la Couronne. %%
%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶1604, Mon Journal t.9 p.113 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
I shall not seek it, and shall content myself with saying that in our day the name of Cossacks is given to those peasants who were never serfs of landowners and who, in the time of slavery in Russia, were registered as belonging to the Crown.
