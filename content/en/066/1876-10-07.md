And a Cross of St George on my breast.

Le grand-duc détacha la croix de sa boutonnière et la mit à celle de mon oncle. The Grand Duke unpinned the cross from his own buttonhole and put it in my uncle's. On the return from the Crimean campaign Maman had put the precious cross away, and I found it again in "the excavations" at Tchernakowka.

%% Je ne le deviendrai pas, mon état naturel est de mêler le sérieux et la folie ensemble, le grand et le grotesque, le mélancolique et le ridicule. %%

I find that I am wasting away to nothing in the country; I am growing so dull-witted that I no longer know what I want. I feel that my journal is stupid: for want of things to tell, I ought to have reasoned — that would be amusing. I am reduced to amusing myself with the astonishment of the Crocodiles: yesterday, for instance, I appeared at dinner in costume, as this evening, before two officials from Poltava; since nobody said anything to me, they contented themselves with staring. It was only at the second course that my father burst out laughing and put them at their ease by explaining that I was indulging in fancies.

%% 066.0708 %%
%% Chers et fichus-lecteurs, ne vous lassez pas, sûrement je vous dirai quelque bonne histoire dans la suite. Je suis d'avance persuadée que tous vous êtes des pédants, ou des ignorants, des ennuyeux ou des ennuyés, en un mot un troupeau de canailles qui en masse peut flatter ma vanité, mais, chacun à part, ne sont bons à rien. %%
%% 2026-09-28T12:25:39 RSR: Restored from tome09.docx ¶1587, Mon Journal t.9 p.112 (missing from the original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
Dear and blasted readers, do not grow weary; I shall surely tell you some good story later on. Live my life, be bored, wait with me; I promise you to do everything I can to amuse myself. Well! after all, if you would rather read some grubby novel, read it! I am convinced in advance that you are all pedants or ignoramuses, bores or the bored — in a word, a herd of rabble who, taken in the mass, may flatter my vanity, but who, taken one by one, are good for nothing.
