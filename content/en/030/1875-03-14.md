# Sunday, 14 March 1875
%% J'ai dormi douze heures; depuis le retour de maman la vie est dans la maison car toute malade qu'elle est elle en est l'âme. %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:55:11 RSR: Restored from tome04.docx ¶4770 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
I slept twelve hours. Since Maman came back there is life in the house, for ill as she is, she is its soul.

In the evening at the opera, *Il Trovatore* (blue dress, pretty coiffure, blue bow in the middle — good). Nadinka in her white woollen dress from Laferrière, a medieval-style dress; I have sent for one like it with a slight change, and I have also ordered from Reboux a Watteau-shaped hat of yellow straw with white feathers and straw trimmings.
