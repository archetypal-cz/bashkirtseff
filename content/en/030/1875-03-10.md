%% 2026-09-28T20:55:11 RSR: Restored from tome04.docx ¶4733 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Once into my brown dress, I loop up the train, put on Dina's hat, my fur-trimmed mantle and a pair of quite charming otter-fur mules, and set off alone on foot, followed by Fortuné Saïd in livery, to the Sapogenikoffs'. Nothing could be more elegant than my figure in this attire. Off I go, proud as can be of my turnout, imagining myself a duchess of the good old days.
