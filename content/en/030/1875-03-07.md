Enfin nous arrivons saines et sauves, les autres nous rejoignent, on dîne à la vapeur et j'emmène tout le monde au cirque, après quoi je soupe et *je me* couche, fatiguée, ennuyée, étourdie, en pensant à "l'animal que je n'ose nommer..." %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:55:11 RSR: Restored from tome04.docx ¶4705–4708 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Several times my eyes were flayed by the sight of my detestation. Scarcely back in the house and into my brown dress, Nadinka proposes going out; we take a cab and drive about until half past seven, and before going home we call at the Sapogenikoffs' and find Pâris just off the boat. The house is overjoyed! They were about to come and share it with us; I take them all to dinner. Marie, Olga, Nadinka and I take each other by the hand and run like the madcaps we are down the rue de France — and on a Sunday evening, too; it makes me shudder. At last we arrive safe and sound, the others join us, we dine at full steam and I take everyone to the circus, after which I have supper and go to bed, tired, bored, dazed, thinking of "the animal I dare not name..."
