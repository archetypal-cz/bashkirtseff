At that instant he took up his opera glass and levelled it at me for the first time in a hundred years. I looked straight into the glass with an air of astonishment, for a moment only, then pretended not to see him. Only, as the lamps dazzled me, I kept hidden behind my immense Japanese fan, through which I saw Terffidua looking at me from time to time. Galula leaves the stalls; I say to myself, he is coming to us and is going to present Audiffret. And indeed he comes in and stops in the middle of the (continued in the next book).
