This evening: the Passion, and we go to church. I do my hair with a braid and a tiny plain black straw hat — quite bare — that looks like a Marguerite bonnet. I was being coquettish on account of old Princess Ypsilanti, who has her place near ours and at whose house all the Grand Dukes and ambassadors etc. are received.
