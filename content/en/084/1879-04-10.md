%% Ce soir La Passion et nous allons a l'eglise, je me coiffe avec une natte et un petit chapeau de paille noir tout nu qui ressemble a un bonnet de Marguerite. This evening: the Passion, and we go to church. I do my hair with a braid and a tiny plain black straw hat — quite bare — that looks like a Marguerite bonnet. I was being coquettish on account of old Princess Ypsilanti, who has her place near ours and at whose house all the Grand Dukes and ambassadors etc. are received.
