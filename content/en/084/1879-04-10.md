This evening the Passion, and we go to church. I do my hair in a plait, with a little black straw hat, quite unadorned, which looks like a Marguerite bonnet. I was being coquettish for old Princess Ypsilanti, who has her place near ours and to whose house all the Grand Dukes and ambassadors etc. go.
