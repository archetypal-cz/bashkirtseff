# Sunday, 9 March 1879

%% 2026-10-01T07:51:39 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
Do you know too that I cannot recall ever having obtained a hundred francs without drama? Every time I have to pay at the atelier I must endure a more or less stormy dialogue. Bills get paid, because there is nothing to say to tradespeople — but when I ask for ready money... These ladies are amusing: they often urge me to buy a completely useless hat, or encourage me to produce a masterpiece like my last trip — and when I ask for 300 francs to pay what I owe Julian for three months at the atelier, they raise a heap of objections, as though this payment could be avoided.

%% 084.0438 %%
%% Savez-vous aussi que c'est une existence diaboliquement assommante ! %%
Do you know too that it is a devilishly tedious existence!

Goldsmid and his wife came and spent an hour here. As for the others, we have let no one know that we are back and receiving. I have not gone to see Berthe, because she is at her mother's, and her mother has never come since that fine marriage.

%% 2026-10-01T07:51:39 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
I am pleased to find that a man like Dumas cares about the quality of paper, ink and pens. For every time some accessory prevents me from working I tell myself it is laziness and that the great painters had no fads... Wait — I understand that Raphael, suddenly inspired, should draw his Madonna della Sedia on the bottom of a barrel; but I do believe, all the same, that this same Raphael, to paint and finish that same picture, resorted to all his favourite tools — and that if he were forced to take a place against his will he would be as irritated as I, a mere mortal, am at the atelier Julian.

Now then... I should very much like to write a book, because this one is so long that no one will be able to read it.

Do you not understand that, with three hundred francs owing, I dislike going to the atelier without paying them?

%% 084.0443 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% Je les paye demain par bonheur. Vous ne comprenez pas non plus qu'ayant mes albums mélangés avec des livres et des chiffons et des planches que pose le menuisier je sois peu disposée à faire des croquis ? %%

Fortunately I pay them tomorrow. Do you not understand either that, with my sketchbooks mixed up with books and rags and the planks the carpenter is putting up, I am little inclined to sketch?

%% 084.0444 %%
%% Enfin que m'importe ! Allez-vous en au diable. %%
Well — what does it matter to me! Go to the devil.
