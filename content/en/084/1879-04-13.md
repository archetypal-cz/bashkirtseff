# Sunday, 13 April 1879 — Easter

I finished sitting at eleven in the morning. The portrait is ugly and not a likeness. Afterwards we lunch at Karageorgevitch's. There are Mademoiselle Mavrocordato and her brother, Prince Soutzo, and Monsieur Koechlin, the brother-in-law of Andrieux, the Prefect of Police. They talked of Gambetta, and I declared myself on the side of those in power, because it is more convenient, alas. I am on no side at all!

%% 2026-10-01T07:55:05 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
The princess brought us home, and as it is Easter we sit down to table. According to Russian custom the table must be kept open for three days; we content ourselves with one day. Madame Gavini arrived with her husband, Filippini and Multedo, who had already come yesterday. We sit down to table again; the charming Adeline wanted to bring the Vicomtesse Pernety and the Randouin woman, but they did not dare: "You will tell them on Tuesday, Marie, at the horse show, that you would have been delighted to see them, will you not? You will scold them?"

— But of course — above all Vicomtesse Pernety; I shall be pleased to see her at our house.
