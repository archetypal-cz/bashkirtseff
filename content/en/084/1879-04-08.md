%% 084.0631 %%

These things of Dumas's enchant me — the noblemen, the duels, the ambushes, the love affairs; all of it as improbable as it is ravishing? I shall go and see it again.

%% 084.0633 %%
%% [#Multedo](../../_original/_glossary/people/aristocracy/COMTE_MULTEDO.md) %%
%% Multedo a enlaidi au surplus, il a avec moi un air confus qu'il déguise mal sous des amabilités et des taquineries qu'il croît spirituelles, il se fait humble pour rire et moi je suis toute simple avec lui et cela très sincèrement, il m'est tout à fait indifférent et toutes ses lettres etc. auxquelles il attache de l'importance et auxquelles il croît que je pense... Je n'y pense nullement. Je me serais peut-être trouvée dans sa position... mais c'est la pièce qui est adorable. %%
%% 2026-10-01T07:54:44 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
Multedo has grown uglier, moreover; with me he has a sheepish air which he disguises badly under civilities and teasing he believes to be witty; he plays at being humble, and I am quite simple with him, and very sincerely so: he is entirely indifferent to me, and all his letters etc., to which he attaches importance and which he believes I think about... I do not think of them at all. I might perhaps have found myself in his position... but it is the play that is adorable.
