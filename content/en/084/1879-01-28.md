But my love for Cassagnac, I fear, is nothing but a flash of the moment. This evening we went to the theatre with the Gavinis. An actor reminded me of Larderel and I *understood* having adored him — I give myself the excuse that this Larderel is original precisely through being perverted, degraded, depraved.
