And even in painting! Breslau knows artists, visits the great painters, sees their ateliers; whilst I! At the atelier everyone assumes I move in society and have every connection imaginable — this, combined with my fortune, separates me from the others and prevents me from asking them for anything, the way they ask each other about visiting a painter or an atelier... Judge the depth of my wretchedness.

C'est une nature estropiée et à côté de choses bonnes on y trouve de la méchanceté, de la bassesse et même de la lâcheté. %%
Amélie cordially hates me — the devil knows why — but she is polite and even agreeable. With the others I am on good terms. I would like to make it up with Breslau, who would be precious to me, but I have no desire to — she is a liar and sometimes false, all while enjoying a reputation for frankness and bluntness; that is what displeases me most. She is a crippled nature, and alongside good qualities one finds in her malice, baseness, and even cowardice.

%% 2026-09-29T01:01:26 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶2189, Mon Journal t.12 p.196 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
It is true that whenever she happens to twist my words, I at once make a speech before everyone, without addressing her, which flattens her; and ten minutes later she answers it with a sally that nobody understands, since most of them are busy drawing and are foreigners — she is satisfied with it, but for the most part nobody has heard it. And if it is heard, and if it is something sensible, I answer it by addressing the whole company, and the last word is mine. ==Hence the anger==[^084.0006.1].

%% 084.0007 %%
%% [#Religion](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/RELIGION.md) %%
%% J'ai honnêtement travaillé toute la semaine jusqu'à dix heures du soir du samedi. Puis je suis rentrée et me suis mise à pleurer. Jusqu'à présent je me suis toujours adressée à Dieu mais comme il ne m'entend pas du tout je n'y crois... presque plus. Ceux-là seuls qui ont éprouvé ce sentiment en comprennent toute l'horreur. Ce n'est pas que je veuille prêcher la religion par vertu, mais Dieu est une chose bien commode. Quand on n'a à qui s'adresser, quand on est à bout de moyens on a Dieu. Cela n'engage à rien et n'inquiète personne et l'on a une suprême consolation. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:26 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶2190, Mon Journal t.12 p.196 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
I worked honestly all week, until ten o'clock on Saturday evening. Then I came home and began to cry. Until now I have always turned to God, but since He does not hear me at all, I hardly... believe in Him any more. Only those who have felt this know all its horror. Not that I wish to preach religion out of virtue, but God is a very convenient thing. When one has no one to turn to, when one is at the end of one's resources, one has God. It commits one to nothing, troubles nobody, and one has a supreme consolation.

%% 084.0008 %%
%% [#Religion](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/RELIGION.md) %%
%% Qu'il existe ou non, *il faut y croire absolument* ou bien être très heureux, alors on s'en passe. Mais dans le chagrin, dans le malheur, dans toutes les choses désagréables enfin, il vaut mieux mourir que ne n'y pas croire. Dieu est une invention qui nous sauve d'un désespoir définitif. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:26 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶2191, Mon Journal t.12 p.196 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Whether He exists or not, *one must believe in Him absolutely* — or else be very happy, and then one can do without Him. But in grief, in misfortune, in every disagreeable thing, in short, it is better to die than not to believe in Him. God is an invention that saves us from final despair.

%% 084.0009 %%
%% [#Religion](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/RELIGION.md) %%

Just think what it means to call upon Him as one's last or only resource and not to believe in Him.
