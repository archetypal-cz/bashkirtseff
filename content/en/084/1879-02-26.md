# Wednesday, 26 February 1879

%% [#Nice](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/NICE.md) [#Paul_de_Cassagnac](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/PAUL_DE_CASSAGNAC.md) [#Eristoff](../../_original/_glossary/people/aristocracy/ERISTOFF.md) [#Paul_frere](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/PAUL_FRERE.md) %%
%% Il paraît que l'élection de Condom est nulle. On a omis je ne sais quel enregistrement... si on l'annule vraiment il ne passera plus. Mais je n'y pense et que peu; nous attendons les témoins d'Eristoff. %%
It appears that the Condom election is null. Some registration or other was omitted... if it is truly annulled he will not win again. But I think very little about it; we are awaiting Eristoff's seconds.
