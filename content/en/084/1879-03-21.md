# Friday, 21 March 1879

%% 084.0410 %%
%% [#Paris](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/PARIS.md) [#Cassagnac](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/CASSAGNAC.md) %%

I amuse myself telling the studio that I have seen a man — truly beautiful — a "bronze"!!

%% 084.0411 %%

It is Cassagnac who is beautiful under that overhead light — and when he lowers his head, his face takes on warm, dark tones — superb.
