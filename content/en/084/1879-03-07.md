# Friday, 7 March 1879

%% 084.0429 %%
%% [#Paris](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/PARIS.md) [#Collignon](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/COLLIGNON.md) [#Howard](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/HOWARD.md) [#Tante](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/TANTE.md) %%
%% Collignon de retour chez ses parents est venue nous voir, je l'ai gardée toute la journée et j'ai parlé tout le temps; il faut bien se dédommager. Seulement il y avait ma tante qui voulait aussi parler et qui fait des fautes à chaque instant disant le pour la et puis des s'où il n'en faut pas, et puis se montrant hargneuse en parlant des Howard dont une se marie.. Cela m'a agacée un peu. %%
%% 2026-10-01T07:51:06 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
Collignon, back at her parents' home, came to see us; I kept her the whole day and talked the whole time — one must make up for things somehow. Only there was my aunt, who also wanted to talk, and who makes mistakes at every moment, saying *le* for *la* and then putting in s's where there should be none, and then showing herself ill-tempered when speaking of the Howards, one of whom is getting married... That irritated me a little.

Paul says he could barely recognise Cassagnac, he has aged so much — and Rosalie says she likes him better now. And why? "Because he no longer has that insolent gaze, that look from on high which gave him such a hateful air."
