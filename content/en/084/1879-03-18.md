Amélie improvised a hoax that kept us from working. Madame Worms was assured that Mademoiselle Jeanne had been shut up in the Maison Dubois, while the said Jeanne was hiding behind an easel; it went on for an hour, and on discovering the trick the victim cried her eyes out. Charming creatures, women.
