Long ago I promised to sit for Anna Nordgren; this week I keep my word. I sat, and made the acquaintance of the famous (at Julian's) Amanda Sival, who won a medal at the atelier for a painting, is fairly well known in Sweden, and has exhibited at the Salon. As for Anna, as you know, she had a picture at the Universal Exhibition, in the Swedish Fine Arts section.
