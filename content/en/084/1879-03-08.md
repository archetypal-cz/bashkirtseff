%% 2026-10-01T07:51:06 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
The jardinières and vases are filled with violets. I shall have them for a long while — they are planted in earth. The blue satin, these violets and the light that comes from above, the harp... not a sound, no one... I do not know why I am so afraid of the country; I am not afraid of it, but I do not seek it out, just as... well, all this is delightful for resting, but I am not tired. I am bored.
