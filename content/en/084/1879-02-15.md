# Saturday, 15 February 1879

%% 084.0221 %%
%% [#Alexandre_Dumas](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/ALEXANDRE_DUMAS.md) %%

%% Il n'y a dans tout le monde entier qu'un seul homme, c'est Alexandre Dumas père et fils. Tout le reste c'est du fatras. %%
In the whole world there is only one man: Alexandre Dumas père and fils. Everything else is rubbish.

%% 084.0222 %%
%% [#La_Tour_de_Londres](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/LA_TOUR_DE_LONDRES.md) [#Filippini](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/FILIPPINI.md) [#Baron_Rognat](../../_original/_glossary/people/aristocracy/BARON_ROGNAT.md) %%

We went to see *La Tour de Londres*[^084.0222.1]. I love melodrama. Filippini came to our box and on leaving introduced his friend Baron Rognat, whom we have often encountered on our travels.
