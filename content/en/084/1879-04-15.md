# Tuesday, 15 April 1879

%% Julian est entré en annonçant la mort de notre Empereur et j'en fus tellement saisie que je ne n'y comprends rien. Tout le monde s'est levé pour me voir: je devins blanche, des larmes dans les yeux, des lèvres tremblantes. Me voyant toujours me moquer de tout, l'aimable Julian a voulu rire, la vérité est qu'un individu a tiré quatre coups à bout portant et que l'Empereur n'a pas été atteint. %%
%% 2026-10-01T07:55:05 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
Julian came in announcing the death of our Emperor, and I was so struck by it that I cannot understand it at all. Everyone stood up to look at me: I went white, tears in my eyes, lips trembling. Seeing me always making fun of everything, the amiable Julian meant it as a joke; the truth is that some individual fired four shots at point-blank range and the Emperor was not hit.

%% Et Julian se tapait la cuisse et s'écriait qu'il ne m'aurait jamais de sa vie cru capable d'une telle émotion. Mais, ni moi non plus. Je remercie le ciel qu'il ne m'a pas annoncé le mariage ou la mort de quelqu'un d'autre car je crois que cela était à cause du moment j'aurais tremblé à n'importe quelle nouvelle. Enfin, l'Empereur ne compromet pas au contraire je ferai courir le bruit que je serai sa fille. %%
%% 2026-10-01T07:55:05 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
And Julian slapped his thigh and cried that never in his life would he have believed me capable of such emotion. Nor would I. I thank heaven he did not announce the marriage or the death of someone else, for I think it was because of the moment; I would have trembled at any news whatever. Well, the Emperor is no compromising attachment; on the contrary, I shall spread the rumour that I am his daughter.

%% 2026-10-01T07:55:05 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
Bojidar came to dinner and to arrange my books for me; he is a good-natured little poseur who believes himself a spoilt child, a character, an original who has seen everything, read everything, and to whom, as to Berthe, extraordinary things are always happening.

%% 2026-10-01T07:55:05 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
It is curious how, when I am alone, I am inclined to elegy. I recite Nadaud's *La Maison blanche*[^084.0671.1] to myself and weep in the right places; as for music, Amneris's underlined monologue in the last act of *Aida* gives me as much emotion as I want. It is an extraordinary and admirable piece of music.
