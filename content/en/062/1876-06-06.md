%% # Mardi 6 juin 1876 %%
# Tuesday, 6 June 1876

%% J'ai lu la journée d'hier, la dernière page. On ne me comprendra pas, c'est que j'écrivais à fur et à mesure que je souffrais et entre chaque mot il y a trois mots de douleur et de larmes. %%
I read back over yesterday's entry, the last page. People will not understand it — the thing is that I was writing as I suffered, and between each word there are three words of pain and tears.

%% 062.0261 %%
%% Il fait une chaleur insupportable. %%
The heat is unbearable.

%% 062.0262 %%
%% [#Mortality](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MORTALITY.md) %%
%% Le sultan Abdul Aziz s'est tué ou a été tué. Je sais bien, on le craignait, Mourad V commence mal, d'ailleurs non, puisque cette mort du souverain déposé peut être utile au repos de l'État, Mourad a raison. %%
Sultan Abdul Aziz has killed himself, or been killed.[^1] I know well enough — they feared it. Mourad V is starting badly — or rather, no: since the death of the deposed sovereign can serve the peace of the State, Mourad is right.

[^1]: Sultan Abdul Aziz of the Ottoman Empire was deposed on 30 May 1876 and found dead on 4 June — officially a suicide, though murder was widely suspected. He was succeeded by Murad V (r. 1876). Marie's awareness of this current event is characteristic of her habits of political self-education.

%% 062.0263 %%
%% Mais au diable ! %%
But to the devil with it all!
