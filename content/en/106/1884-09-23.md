> Mademoiselle,

%% 106.0101 %%
%% kind: letter source="Lettre du Figaro illustré rêvée par Marie, 23 septembre 1884" %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%

%% 2026-10-01T12:32:00 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
> I have the pleasure of informing you that your drawing has been accepted by the *Figaro Illustré* and has even been particularly noted by our contributor M. Albert Wolff.

%% 106.0102 %%
%% kind: letter source="Lettre du Figaro illustré rêvée par Marie, 23 septembre 1884" %%
%% > Veuillez agréer etc. %%
%% 2026-10-01T12:32:00 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
> Yours, etc.

%% 106.0103 %%
%% 2026-02-03T10:20:00 LAN: "fin de non recevoir" - rejection, refusal to consider; legal term used colloquially %%
%% 2026-02-03T10:20:00 LAN: "leger froid" - slight chill; emotional coldness, disappointment %%
%% Ça c'est ce que j'ai rêvé. Mais en réalité. Une fin de non recevoir. Comme je n'y comptais pas trop, ça me fait un léger froid puis c'est tout. %%
%% 2026-10-01T12:32:00 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
That is what I dreamed. But in reality: a flat refusal. As I had not counted on it too much, it gives me a slight chill — and then that is all.

%% 106.0104 %%
%% Je suis sans forces. Les yeux même ont faibli en proportion. %%
I am without strength. Even my eyes have weakened proportionately.

%% 106.0105 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% Nous avons été prendre Jules au Bois et quand il a été couché je l'ai endormi en lui passant la main dans les cheveux. %%
We went to collect Jules from the Bois and when he had been put to bed I lulled him to sleep by running my hand through his hair.

%% 106.0106 %%
%% Ça c'est ce que j'ai rêvé. %%
That is what I dreamed.

%% 106.0107 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% 2026-02-03T10:20:00 LAN: "tripote la tete" - stroked/fiddled with his head; informal, slightly crude %%
%% 2026-02-03T10:20:00 LAN: "bien portante" - in good health; contrast with Marie's frailty %%
%% 2025-01-23T03:45:00 RSR: Entry from Book 16, lines 9414-9430. Acceptance of drawing by Figaro Illustré praised by Albert Wolff, but Marie's joy tempered by weakness and illness. Dina, not Marie, comforts Bastien. %%
%% Mais en réalité c'est Dina qui lui a tripoté la tête pour l'endormir. Elle est si bien portante, si grasse, si forte que c'est à elle qu'il l'a demandé. Il va du reste affreusement mal. C'est abominable. %%
%% 2026-10-01T12:32:00 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
But in reality it is Dina who stroked his head to put him to sleep. She is so robust, so plump, so strong that it was her he asked. Besides, he is dreadfully ill. It is abominable.
