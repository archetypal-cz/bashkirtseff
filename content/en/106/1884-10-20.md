# Monday, 20 October 1884
Despite magnificent weather Bastien comes here instead of going to the Bois. He can scarcely walk any more; his brother props him up under both arms — practically carries him. And once in the armchair the poor child is utterly spent.

Woe to us. And how many concierges are in perfect health![^9]

[^9]: Marie's bitter irony: the most humble and inconsequential people flourish, while a genius like Bastien-Lepage dies at 36.

%% Depuis deux jours mon lit est au salon, mais comme il est très grand et divisé par des paravents et des poufs et le piano, on ne s'en aperçoit pas. Il m'est trop difficile de monter les escaliers.[^10] %%
%% 2026-10-01T12:32:45 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
For two days now my bed has been in the drawing room; but as the room is very large, and divided by folding screens and ottomans and the piano, one does not notice it. It is too difficult for me to climb the stairs.[^10]

[^10]: This is Marie Bashkirtseff's last diary entry. She died on 31 October 1884, eleven days after writing these words. She was twenty-five years old. Jules Bastien-Lepage died on 10 December 1884.
