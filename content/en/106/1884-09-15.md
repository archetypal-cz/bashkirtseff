# Monday, 15 September 1884
You do not know that ten days ago I entrusted the background of my picture to M. Geraldy, the perspective man, who is putting it into perspective — knowing Emile to be very busy. Well, that creature Geraldy, who is occupied with putting Benjamin-Constant's picture in perspective, has been keeping me waiting for ten days.
