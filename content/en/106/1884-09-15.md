# Monday, 15 September 1884
You do not know that for ten days now I have entrusted the background of my picture to M. Geraldy the perspective artist, who is to put it in perspective — knowing that Emile is very busy. Well, that creature Geraldy, who is occupied with putting Benjamin-Constant's picture in perspective, has been keeping me waiting for ten days.
