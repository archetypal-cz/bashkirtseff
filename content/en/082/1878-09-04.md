Multedo writes. Mme Sarasin de Bock and several others have sent their cards; the Gavinis as well. When all our encounters with the Deceased run through my head, I believe I was truly stupid.

%% 082.0222 %%
%% Il me semble incroyable que l'ayant approché de si près j'ai été si folle. %%
It seems incredible to me that, having approached him so closely, I was so mad.

%% 082.0223 %%
%% Comment ! me dis-je, il est venu et il ne vient plus; il m'a... %%
How is it, I say to myself — he came, and he no longer comes; he...
