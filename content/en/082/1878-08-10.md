# Saturday, 10 August 1878

%% 082.0021 %%
%% [#Soden](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/SODEN.md) [#Princesse_Thyra](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/PRINCESSE_THYRA.md) [#Napoleon_IV](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/NAPOLEON_IV.md) [#Dourassoff](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/DOURASSOFF.md) [#Ma_tante](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/MA_TANTE.md) [#Schwarz](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/SCHWARZ.md) [#Gans](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/GANS.md) [#Muhle](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/MUHLE.md) [#Mme_Doubelt](../../_original/_glossary/people/aristocracy/MME_DOUBELT.md) [#Paul_de_Cassagnac](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/PAUL_DE_CASSAGNAC.md) [#Imperatrice_Eugenie](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/IMPERATRICE_EUGENIE.md) [#Prince_Ouroussoff](../../_original/_glossary/people/aristocracy/PRINCE_OUROUSSOFF.md) %%
%% 2026-02-02T14:00:00 LAN: "un coup" - a blow/shock, idiomatic expression for emotional impact %%
%% 2026-02-09T10:05:00 RSR: Princess Thyra of Denmark (1853-1933) was rumored as a potential bride for the Prince Imperial (Napoleon Eugene, sometimes called Napoleon IV). In reality, she married Ernest Augustus, Crown Prince of Hanover, in December 1878. Marie's political alarm about this "alliance dangerous for the Republic" reflects her Bonapartist sympathies - she sees a royal Danish match legitimizing Bonapartist claims to the French throne. The "Napoleon IV" is the Prince Imperial, son of Napoleon III and Empress Eugenie. %%
%% La princesse Thyra épouse Napoléon IV... C'est un coup pour moi. %%
Princess Thyra is to marry Napoleon IV...[^82.541.1] It is a blow to me.

[^82.541.1]: Princess Thyra of Denmark (1853–1933) was rumoured in 1878 as a potential bride for the Prince Imperial (Napoleon Eugene Louis Jean Joseph Bonaparte, 1856–1879), commonly called Napoleon IV by Bonapartists. The match never materialized; Thyra married Ernest Augustus, Crown Prince of Hanover, in December 1878. The Prince Imperial died the following year in the Anglo-Zulu War.

%% [#Politics](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/POLITICS.md) %%
%% Madame Doubelt m'écrit que l'on sollicite et que probablement mon ami sera décoré. Et Napoléon qui se marie avec Thyra. Cette alliance est dangereuse pour la République. Notre héritière, sœur de la princesse Thyra, va à Paris. En voyant cette ravissante petite future Impératrice, les Français songeront à sa sœur qui sera la femme de leur empereur probable,... ils se monteront la tête, l'envie leur prendra d'avoir une impératrice, non pas comme le fut Eugénie, mais une vraie... %%
Madame Doubelt writes to tell me there is lobbying under way and that my friend will probably be decorated. And Napoleon going to marry Thyra. This alliance is dangerous for the Republic. Our heiress, Princess Thyra's sister, is going to Paris. Seeing this ravishing little future Empress, the French will think of her sister — who will be the wife of their probable emperor — they will let their imaginations run away with them, they will grow hungry for an empress, not the sort that Eugénie was, but a real one...

His moustache droops over the side, long as a Chinese queue; the pince-nez on his nose, the cord of the pince-nez falling lower than the moustache, and a white cat [word blacked out: pulling] the cord while regarding the public with a vexed air. %% S'il pouvait me poser au moins trois fois... Il est onze heures et j'ai une envie folle de dessiner, de composer ce que je n'ai jamais compris malgré les instances de Julian[^82.549.1]. Je viens de faire à la plume notre table et c'est très vraisemblable, avant je n'avais jamais pu mettre deux personnes ensemble. %%
If only he would sit for me at least three times... It is eleven o'clock and I am seized with a mad urge to draw, to compose — something I have never grasped despite Julian's urgings.[^82.549.1] I have just sketched our table in pen and it is very lifelike; before, I had never been able to put two people together.

[^82.549.1]: The Académie Julian was a private art school in Paris founded in 1868 by painter Rodolphe Julian. It was one of the few schools that accepted women and allowed them to draw from the nude male model, making it crucial for aspiring female artists like Marie who were barred from the official École des Beaux-Arts.
