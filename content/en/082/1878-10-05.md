# Saturday, 5 October 1878

%% 082.0535 %%
%% [#Doenhoff](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/DOENHOFF.md) [#Mme_Coube](../../_original/_glossary/people/aristocracy/MME_COUBE.md) %%

%% Doenhoff a déjeuné avec nous, essayé ma pelisse, fait le gentil. En rentrant d'atelier je l'ai trouvé au salon, Mme Coubé aussi. %%
Doenhoff lunched with us, tried on my pelisse,[^82.535.1] made himself agreeable. On returning from the atelier I found him in the salon — Mme Coubé as well.

%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%

But today is the day Robert-Fleury comes to give corrections at the atelier. Well, I was horribly frightened.

%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%

He said "ah! ha" and "oh! ho" in several different tones, and then:

"Not quite, *Monsieur* — I shall only paint once a month..."

%% 082.0540 %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%

"No, you are right to begin — you can paint. There is good in it, there is good..."

"I was afraid I was not sufficiently advanced to paint..."

"Not at all — you are sufficiently advanced; continue; it is not bad..." etc. etc.

%% 082.0543 %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% [#LOUISE_BRESLAU](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/LOUISE_BRESLAU.md) %%

%% Et là-dessus une longue leçon qui prouve que ce n'est pas *sans espoir* comme on dit à l'atelier. On ne m'aime pas à l'atelier et à chaque malheureux succès Breslau a des regards furieux tout à fait risibles. %%
And thereupon a long lesson that proves it is not ==without hope==[^82.543.1], as they say at the atelier. I am not liked at the atelier, and at every wretched success Breslau gives me furious glances that are quite laughable.

%% 082.0544 %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% [#Carolus](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/CAROLUS.md) %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% [#Robert_Fleury](../../_original/_glossary/people/artists/ROBERT_FLEURY.md) %%
%% Mais Robert-Fleury ne croit pas que je n'aie jamais appris à peindre. Il est resté longtemps corrigeant, causant et fumant comme s'il était Carolus. J'aurai reçu plusieurs conseils *extra* et puis cet imbécile m'a demandé comment j'ai été placée au dernier concours de l'année passée. Et quand je dis deuxième. %%
But Robert-Fleury does not believe I have never learned to paint. He stayed a long time — correcting, chatting, and smoking as if he were Carolus.[^82.544.1] I received several pieces of *extra* advice, and then this imbecile asked how I had placed in the last competition of last year. And when I said second —

%% 082.0545 %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% - Et cette année, dit-il, il faudra... hum ? %%
"And this year," he said, "you must... hm?"

%% 082.0546 %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% [#Robert_Fleury](../../_original/_glossary/people/artists/ROBERT_FLEURY.md) [#RODOLPHE_JULIAN](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/RODOLPHE_JULIAN.md) %%
%% C'est si bête, il a déjà dit à Julian qu'il pensait que j'aurais une médaille. %%
It is so absurd — he has already told Julian he thinks I shall have a medal.

%% 082.0547 %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% Enfin me voilà sans aucun tirage, autorisée à peindre la nature sans avoir peint des natures mortes; je les passe comme j'ai passé les plâtres. %%
And so here I am, without any difficulty, authorised to paint from life without having painted still lifes; I skip them as I skipped plaster casts.

%% 082.0548 %%
%% [#Doenhoff](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/DOENHOFF.md) [#Exposition_universelle](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/EXPOSITION_UNIVERSELLE.md) [#Mme_Randouin](../../_original/_glossary/people/aristocracy/MME_RANDOUIN.md) [#Gavini](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/GAVINI.md) [#Nice](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/NICE.md) %%

Doenhoff had accompanied the ladies to the Exposition; at the entrance, they see Mme Randouin — who was a sub-prefect's wife in the days when the Gavinis entertained all the crowned heads who passed through Nice so magnificently. Doenhoff knew her from that time too.

%% 082.0549 %%

"Here is Mme Randouin," Maman said to him.

%% 082.0550 %%
%% [#Society](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/SOCIETY.md) %%
%% [#Mme_Randouin](../../_original/_glossary/people/aristocracy/MME_RANDOUIN.md) [#Gavini](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/GAVINI.md) [#Nice](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/NICE.md) %%
%% Elle l'a entendue et aussitôt s'approcha de la voiture et commença mille choses aimables, qu'elle est bien heureuse d'avoir *été appelée* par Madame, que Mme Gavini lui a tant parlé, qu'elle nous connaît si bien de Nice, qu'elle est enchantée de faire connaissance etc. %%
She heard her and at once came up to the carriage and launched into a thousand amiable remarks — how happy she is to have *been called* by Madame, how much Mme Gavini has spoken to her of us, how well she knows us from Nice, how delighted she is to make our acquaintance, etc.
