> *Dear brother,*
> *I have heard it said that you intend to contract a lawful union with a Mademoiselle Patchenko. I do not know her personally, but I assure you that she is nothing very distinguished.*
> *The name does not suit me and the alliance hardly flatters me. I would even be grateful to you, and very seriously so, if you would spare me this kinship.*
> *I do not see what can drive you to such an act of desperation at your age. I understood the plans concerning Nadine (Eristoff): the young lady's mother was a friend of Grand-maman's and of all of us, although the father was an imbecile, and besides, the girl is charming, though she has fallen into ignoble hands. That marriage, though senseless considering your age, seemed possible; but this other flower of Poltava — I beg you, do not think of it. Do not let yourself be worked up by citizens of that great capital of knights, who will get you drunk (at your expense) in a public garden and stuff into your brain the more than preposterous idea of sacrificing yourself at eighteen. For that they will receive twenty-five or fifty roubles as a tip from the family of your quasi-intended. I wrote to you not very long ago, and I am characteristically astonished that my letter should be lost. Be so extremely obliging as to enlighten me regarding your matrimonial intentions; I hope to have the satisfaction of learning that it is not serious. Alas, I cannot think of any career for you other than that of a gentleman-farmer, thanks to your absolutely incomplete education, but I oppose seriously, officially and formally this mad idea of plunging headfirst into that bourgeoisie. I shall not look for an archduchess for you — she would not have you — but I want for a sister-in-law someone of good family — distinguished, nice and well brought up. A woman who does us credit and whom I can show without embarrassment.*
> *I have the right to be particular, since I hold myself extremely high and set you the example of a dignified pride, without exaggeration, and absolutely necessary in this world.*
> *Write to me quickly, and... No foolishness!*
> *Marie*
