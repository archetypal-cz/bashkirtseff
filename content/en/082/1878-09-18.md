# Wednesday, 18 September 1878

%% 082.0421 %%
%% [#Dreams](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/DREAMS.md) %%

%% J'ai rêvé de chats et de serpents et j'ai renversé une salière à déjeuner. Ce qui me rend toute peureuse. Que va-t-il donc m'arriver ? Et mon oreille gauche qui brûle. %%
I dreamt of cats and serpents, and I upset a salt cellar at luncheon. It makes me quite apprehensive. What is going to happen to me? And my left ear is burning!

Not a word about the famous bouquet presented to Popaul[^82.422.1] in any newspaper — *Le Pays* alone has glorified its patron. Now Gambetta is on tour and is welcomed as a conquering hero; Popaul commits the blunder of devoting entire articles to mockery that is amusing though worn out; [Three lines crossed out: gives them a rather piquant turn] and resembles impotent jealousy — and is therefore ridiculous. He does not proceed according to — not my desires, but my apprehensions.

%% 082.0423 %%
%% [#Superstition](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/SUPERSTITION.md) %%
%% Le nez ne cesse de me démanger, encore une mort que je vais apprendre. %%
My nose keeps itching — another death I am about to hear of.

[^82.422.1]: "Popaul": Marie's affectionate-mocking diminutive for Paul de Cassagnac.
