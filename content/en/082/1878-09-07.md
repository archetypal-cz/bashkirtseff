I saw Robert-Fleury,[^82.321.1] but had only a single drawing to show him — on which I had worked only one day (this week there was illness and a change of model) — Thursday — and an hour this morning.

Triphon left this evening. And Papa leaves him two thousand roubles; in addition he was given clothing and seven hundred francs. The wretch received a hundred francs a month and was perpetually in debt. He was very eager to stay — crossed himself, kissed our hands, spoke of his faithfulness, and so on. We fortunately overcame all sentimentality.
