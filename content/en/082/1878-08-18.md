%% Il n'y a rien eu de remarquable au 15 août. Une simple messe voilà tout. En dehors de Soden Schwarz et Gans ne sont plus rien du tout. Tout est remis en place dans mon salon, à côté du squelette on voit le bas commencé et les *tableaux* de Soden traînent de tous côtés. %%
Nothing of note happened on the 15th of August — a simple mass, nothing more. Outside of Soden, Schwarz and Gans are nothing at all. Everything is back in place in my sitting room; beside the skeleton lies the stocking I had begun, and the Soden *tableaux* are scattered everywhere.

%% 2026-03-08T10:00:00 TR: "home" — Marie writes in English; kept as-is per TM instructions (English passages in English original kept as-is with highlight and footnote). %%
This apartment is disagreeable to me. Having left it and then returning makes it seem like a detested ==home==.[^82.142.1] Walitsky died here, Pincio was lost here, and Cassagnac will return no more. It is childish of me to pretend to lump all these misfortunes together. I denied God as the men I do not love deny my heart.

[^82.142.1]: In English in the original.
