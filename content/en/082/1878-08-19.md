%% Il y a quelque chose d'insupportable dans notre intérieur pour moi ! Les tracas à propos de rien, l'incroyable insouciance quant aux choses graves, voilà le fond de tous. Et puis ce qui me révolte c'est cet abrutissement moral qui produit une espèce de cruauté affreuse qui consiste à abandonner Dina et moi. Tellement on nous abandonne que j'ai comme honte de la moindre revendication. Il faudrait jouer des drames pour obtenir les choses les plus ordinaires et on vous regarderait comme une capricieuse exorbitante qui ne sait pas ce quelle veut. %% 2026-10-01T07:27:29 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
There is something in our household I cannot bear! The petty vexations about nothing, the incredible carelessness in matters of consequence — that is what lies at the bottom of them all. And then what revolts me is this moral torpor, which produces a kind of dreadful cruelty that consists in abandoning Dina and me. We are so abandoned that I am almost ashamed of making the slightest claim. One would have to stage dramas to obtain the most ordinary things, and one would be regarded as an exorbitantly capricious girl who does not know what she wants. They are so utterly ignorant of the more or less tolerable conditions in which young girls must live that I do not even dare ask for anything. I do not even accuse them; they would not understand.

They think it natural that a girl of my age should spend her life within four walls — literally. It is a truly dreadful life, for one cannot do without the ordinary relations of civilised society unless one is an exception... and... but I am not speaking of exceptions, nor of disenchanted, morose, old people, of monks or of madmen.

My notebooks, my notes, the big books I always need. Then a vase to toss my brushes into, and other things I need to make myself a modest little comfort.

%% Mademoiselle Oelsnitz, celle qui était gouvernante chez Mme Anitchkoff, est maintenant chez nous... Les Anitchkoff sont en Russie. Je crois vous avoir raconté que Mlle Oelsnitz est fille d'un baron Oelsnitz, (un de ces barons allemands, dont la Russie regorge) et qu'elle a quitté la maison par suite d'intrigues de sa belle-mère et qu'elle était amoureuse de Walitsky... Tout cela et son séjour chez les Anitchkoff et surtout les soins qu'elle a prodigués à grand-papa pendant l'opération, l'ont en quelque sorte fait une des nôtres, elle me sera d'ailleurs utile; maman ne s'est pas encore aperçue qu'il ne m'était pas convenable de sortir avec une femme de chambre ou seule. Ce sera une sorte de gouvernante, je l'habillerai et lui montrerai un grand respect dans les magasins pour en imposer; car elle n'est pas imposante, petite, rousse, jeune, triste. %% 2026-10-01T07:27:29 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
Mademoiselle Oelsnitz, the one who was governess at Mme Anitchkoff's, is now with us... The Anitchkoffs are in Russia. I believe I told you that Mlle Oelsnitz is the daughter of a Baron Oelsnitz (one of those German barons with whom Russia teems), that she left home as a result of her stepmother's intrigues, and that she was in love with Walitsky... All that, and her stay with the Anitchkoffs, and above all the care she lavished on Grand-papa during the operation, have made her one of us, so to speak; besides, she will be useful to me: Maman has not yet noticed that it is not proper for me to go out with a maid or alone. She will be a sort of governess; I shall dress her and show her great deference in the shops to give her authority, for she is not imposing: small, red-haired, young, sad. A round face that looks like a caricature of the moon when the moon is sad; that expression makes one laugh. Eyes of a comic dreaminess — but with a hat of my choosing she will do, and I shall go to the atelier with her.

There is nothing to be proud of, except this superiority.
