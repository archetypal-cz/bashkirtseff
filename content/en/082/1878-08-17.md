%% 2026-10-01T07:27:29 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
This morning we were still at Soden. I had promised five hundred prostrations if I found Grand-papa alive. I kept my promise. He is not dead, but he is scarcely better. All the same... here is my Ems cure *ruined* for nothing. I hate Paris! One can be happy and content and satisfied there better than anywhere else; in Paris life can be complete, intelligent, glorious — I am far from denying it. But for the existence I lead, one needs to love the city itself; cities, like people, are congenial or antipathetic to me whether or not they are beautiful and agreeable, and I cannot make myself like Paris.
