I have sent them a caricature by post; they are already so furious with me that one more trifle will make no difference. They spent the whole day on the terrace, passing and repassing in front of us — in short, a heap of nonsense. Mme Abaza came to tell me in secret what Dourassoff had told her, which I already knew. I promised that distinguished woman to tell her, at the moment of departure, something which has supposedly set my self-esteem at rest, and I exacted the most absolute secrecy... adding that she seemed to me a woman who could keep it. I shall tell her that Schwarz is desperately in love with me, that he has written to tell me so, and that, seeing my disdain, he has sworn to flee from me. I shall tell it convincingly.

Once they had gone I put on a blue pince-nez, a hat like theirs, turned up my coat collar, took a ribbon in my mouth to simulate a moustache and, taking a cane, gave a performance for the Coubes! There was no one else.
