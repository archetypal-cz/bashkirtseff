# Wednesday, 16 August (4 August 1876)
%% [huit lignes cancellées] Une foule de voisins et voisines, la crème de ces nobles lieux. Une dame qui a été à Rome, aime l'Antiquité et possède une fille qui ne parle pas. D'une manière subite ainsi qu'inattendue, il nous arriva trois anges. Le juge d'instruction, le notaire et le secrétaire. %% 2026-09-29T01:37:11 RSR: Restored from tome08.docx ¶4237, Mon Journal t.8 p.373 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-29 rebuild). Line-break hyphen «An- tiquité» removed. %%
[eight lines crossed out] A crowd of neighbours, ladies and gentlemen, the cream of these noble parts. A lady who has been to Rome, loves Antiquity and possesses a daughter who does not speak. In a manner as sudden as it was unexpected, three angels descended upon us: the examining magistrate, the notary and the secretary. My uncle, who has been a justice of the peace for seven years, always has dealings with these officials.
