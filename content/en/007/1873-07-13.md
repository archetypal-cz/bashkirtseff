# Sunday, 13 July 1873

%% 007.0002 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Meta_diary](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/META_DIARY.md) %%
%% J'ai dormi jusqu'à onze heures, quelle honte ! Hier j'ai revu tous mes journaux du commencement. Je n'ai pas tout lu et cela m'a pris deux heures. J'y ai trouvé des choses absurdes, dont je suis honteuse maintenant. %%
I slept until eleven, how shameful!
%% 2026-02-16T12:00:00 GEM: "what a disgrace!" → "how shameful!" — "disgrace" is too strong for waking up late in English (sev B) %%
Yesterday I reread all my journals from the beginning. I did not read everything, and it took me two hours. I found absurd things in them, of which I am now ashamed.

%% 2026-02-16T10:00:00 TR: "bourgeois" — kept in English as it conveys the class contempt Marie intends. "a la musique" — "at the concert" per TranslationMemory. "commis, employes" — clerks, employees; social inferiors in Marie's view. %%
%% Maman etc. à Monaco; moi et Dina avec la princesse Galitzine à la musique. Je suis à mon aise avec elle, je puis dire ce que je pense, avec les autres je me gêne. Devant elle je puis dire que je n'aime pas les bourgeois, que j'aime la noblesse, que la société de Nice est trop mêlée, qu'on y rencontre des commis, des *employés* etc., que j'aime aller dans les meilleures maisons pour mes robes, je puis critiquer à volonté. Tandis que devant bien d'autres je ne puis dire mes idées et opinions, car chacun à Nice a plus ou moins ces défauts. On a même touché la question du mariage. %%
Maman and the others went to Monaco; Dina and I with Princess Galitzine at the concert. I am at ease with her; I can say what I think — with others I feel constrained.
%% 2026-02-16T12:00:00 GEM: "Maman and the others to Monaco" → "Maman and the others went to Monaco" — added missing verb for clarity (sev B) %%
%% 2026-02-16T12:00:00 GEM: "I hold back" → "I feel constrained" — more accurate translation of "se gêner" (sev B) %%
Before her I can say that I do not like the bourgeois, that I love the nobility, that the society of Nice is too mixed, that one meets clerks, *employees*, and so forth, that I like to go to the best houses for my dresses — I can criticise at will. Whereas before many others I cannot express my ideas and opinions, for everyone in Nice has more or less these faults.
%% 2026-02-16T12:00:00 GEM: NOTE: "more or less" — could also be expressed as "to some degree" or "in one way or another" for a less literal translation of "plus ou moins" (sev C) %%
We even touched upon the question of marriage.

— Marry a baker, so long as he is rich; do not look for titles — God preserve us from that.

She probably looked for the title and found that scoundrel of a husband. My opinion is different: I detest the ==peasants==[^2]. I proposed going for ice cream at La Victoire, by carriage ==of course==, and then we dropped the lady at her house and went home. Paul at the theatre.[^1]

[^1]: "Of course" in English in the original.
[^2]: In French in the original: *moujiks* (the Russian word for peasants).
