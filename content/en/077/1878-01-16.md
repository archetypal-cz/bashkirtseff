# Wednesday, 16 January 1878
Berthe came to the atelier with me; she wants me to go out into society with them, and her mother is asking Maman to allow it.

%% 2026-09-30T06:23:18 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
> A very great misfortune has befallen us: our dear Dr. Wolitski [Walitsky], whom you saw at our house, died last Friday at two in the morning. He was the best friend of our entire family, Grand-papa's godson; he watched us all grow up — you can well imagine what an irreparable loss. Friends such as he are so rare, not to say impossible to find. Grand-papa, ill himself as you know, wept the whole day and is still very sad even now. But I do not wish to speak to you of such sombre things.

%% 077.0227 %%
%% kind: letter source="Lettre de Marie à F. de Marcuard, 16 janvier 1878" %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
%% > Vous me demandez si je n'hésite pas entre l'amour de l'art et l'amour de la belle nature; je n'hésite pas : je les aime également, mais la belle nature ne donne des jouisances à peu près complètes que lorsque l'on sait que l'on est soi-même quelque chose, lorsqu'on possède la force de l'art qui est un très grande et très grande force. %%
> You ask whether I do not hesitate between love of art and love of beautiful nature; I do not hesitate: I love them equally — but beautiful nature yields pleasures that are nearly complete only when one knows that one is oneself something, when one possesses the power of art, which is a very great, a very great power.

> There is someone who wishes to know all the ill that is said of a certain M. L[ancaster]. Do you not know him?

%% kind: letter source="Lettre de Marie à F. de Marcuard, 16 janvier 1878" %%

> How lucky, how very lucky you are to be going to Rome! I envy you — and I confess it, though envy is a meanness.

%% 077.0231 %%
%% kind: letter source="Lettre de Marie à F. de Marcuard, 16 janvier 1878" %%
%% [#Death](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/DEATH.md) %%
%% > Racontez-moi ce que vous avez vu aux funérailles du roi et tout le reste. Soyez bien aimable et donnez-moi toutes les nouvelles et vieilleries imaginables... Je lirai cela à table, puisque c'est là seulement que je suis libre. %%
> Tell me everything you saw at the King's funeral and all the rest. Be so kind as to send me all the news — and all the olds — imaginable... I shall read it at table, since it is only there that I am free.

%% 077.0232 %%
%% kind: letter source="Lettre de Marie à F. de Marcuard, 16 janvier 1878" %%
%% > On vous fait dire mille choses aimables. Est-ce qu'il y aura un carnaval ? %%
> Everyone sends you a thousand kind regards. Will there be a carnival?

%% 077.0233 %%
%% La maison est si vide. Il n'y a que quatre femmes et grand-papa dans sa chambre. %%
The house is so empty. There are only four women and Grand-papa in his room.

%% 077.0234 %%

It is impossible for me to believe in the death of Walitsky. It would be such a great, such a profound grief.
