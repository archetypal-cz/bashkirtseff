%% > Votre Idée est drôle, mais vous aviez raison de supposer qu'il me serait difficile de vous répondre dans les conditions que vous prescriviez. %% 2026-09-30T06:23:31 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
> Your idea is amusing — but you were right to suppose that it would be difficult for me to reply under the conditions you prescribed. I am not the enemy of original things — indeed they are the only true things in our ordinarily banal world. Yet you must admit that the signature you indicated verged a little on the music-hall. Why not sign "Canada", while we were about it?

I am delighted with it. Berthe and I do nothing but cook up nonsense at the atelier during the rests. Berthe informs me that the *arm* she had counted on will not hear of anything once there is another man in the business. So, with a very innocent air, I tell her it makes no difference whether we are together at the ball or not, so long as we meet up again in the box.

%% 077.0322 %%
%% — Je n'y ai seulement pas pensé ! s'écrie la jeune fille avec un accent tel que je sais bien ce qu'elle pense. %%
— I had not even thought of that! cries the young lady with an inflection that tells me exactly what she is thinking.

%% 077.0323 %%
%% Mais cela m'est bien égal, chacun pour soi, mentons pour tous et soyons contents. %%
But it is all the same to me: each for herself, lies for all, and let us be content.
