# Friday, 5 December 1873

%% 013.0205 %%
%% *Dans cinq jours.* %%
*In five days.*

%% 013.0206 %%

%% [#Hitchcock](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/HITCHCOCK.md) %%
%% Je me lève à huit heures. Hitchcock est le comble de dégoûtation, et ==arrogante==[^013.0206.r1]. Paysanne, bête, ignorante ! Fi ! Je sors d'abord en voiture (robe brune, pas mal) puis nous marchons avec Hitchcock, il y a beaucoup de monde. Il commence à faire froid, c'est un grand plaisir pour moi. J'aime à sentir mes joues fraîches et rosies par le froid. %%
I rise at eight. Hitchcock is the height of repulsiveness, and ==arrogant==[^013.0206.r1]. A peasant, stupid, ignorant! Fie! I go out first by carriage (brown dress, not bad), then we walk with Hitchcock; there are many people. It is beginning to turn cold -- a great pleasure for me. I love to feel my cheeks cool and rosy from the cold.

%% 013.0207 %%
%% [#Maman](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/MAMAN.md) %%
%% [#Monaco](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/MONACO.md) [#Howard_family](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/HOWARD_FAMILY.md) [#Edy](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/EDY.md) %%
%% Il y a quelques jours de cela que Howard S.T. a présenté à maman Edy à Monaco. Aujourd'hui je les ai rencontrés tous deux à pied. %%
Some days ago Howard S.T. introduced Edy to Maman at Monaco. Today I met them both on foot.

%% 013.0208 %%
%% [#Duke_of_Hamilton](../../_original/_glossary/people/core/DUKE_OF_HAMILTON.md) [#Gioia](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/GIOIA.md) %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
%% La Delaroche fait du chic, ça marche, mais jamais elle ne me plaira comme l'autre. Comme le duc de Hamilton est pour moi l'idéal, la perfection de l'homme, ainsi *Gioia* est la perfection, l'idéal, la divinité de la femme. Tout en lui me plaît jusqu'au nom, comme elle aussi. Toutes les fois que je rencontre le mot Gioia je fais semblant de ne pas comprendre pour le lire plusieurs fois, toujours trois fois et l'Italien m'explique le passage que je comprends parfaitement. %%
%% 2026-02-19T15:40:00 TR: "fait du chic" = puts on airs/shows style. "l'autre" = the other one -- refers to Gioia, Hamilton's mistress. "Gioia" means "joy" in Italian; Marie pretends not to understand Italian passages containing this word so she can read it three times. %%
La Delaroche puts on airs; it works, but she will never please me as the other one does. As the Duke of Hamilton is for me the ideal, the perfection of man, so *Gioia* is the perfection, the ideal, the divinity of woman. Everything about him pleases me, down to his name, as does everything about her. Every time I come across the word *Gioia* I pretend not to understand, so as to read it several times -- always three times -- and the Italian teacher explains the passage, which I understand perfectly.

%% 013.0209 %%
%% [#Appearance](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/APPEARANCE.md) %%
%% Je suis si indifférente à tout maintenant. Je n'étais pas mal en marchant car on me regardait pas comme avant, mais comme je veux bien. %%
I am so indifferent to everything now. I did not look bad while walking, for they looked at me not as before, but just as I like.

%% 013.0210 %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
%% [#Duke_of_Hamilton](../../_original/_glossary/people/core/DUKE_OF_HAMILTON.md) %%
%% Je vais très rarement à la promenade et tant mieux. Presque pas une fois par semaine ou deux. Mais lui, le duc ne me quitte pas. %%
I go very rarely to the promenade -- so much the better. Hardly once in a week or two. But he, the duke, never leaves me.

Alcibiades is a name that has become dear to me. I call Gioia *Laïs* -- it is pretty, and I can speak of her before everyone.

%% 013.0212 %%
%% [#Reading](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/READING.md) %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
%% [#Duke_of_Hamilton](../../_original/_glossary/people/core/DUKE_OF_HAMILTON.md) [#Bouillet](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/BOUILLET.md) [#Alcibiades](../../_original/_glossary/culture/history/ALCIBIADES.md) %%
%% Je lis tout ce que je puis trouver où l'on parle de Hamilton, même d'un duc et même d'Alcibiade. Toutes les fois que je lis dans Bouillet des Hamilton, des Arran que j'ai trouvé par accident ce soir, mon coeur palpite, j'ai froid, je m'évanouis presque. Je cherchais Aspasie et j'ai rencontré Arran, c'était inattendu et fort; on y parle des Hamilton ce nom divin. Non, vraiment, le nom est aussi très beau, idéal, superbe, ravissant, adorable. %%
%% 2026-02-19T15:42:00 TR: "Bouillet" = Dictionnaire universel d'histoire et de geographie, a standard reference work. "Arran" = Isle of Arran, Hamilton family seat in Scotland. "Aspasie" = Aspasia, Pericles' companion -- Marie is looking up Greek courtesans and stumbles on the Hamiltons. %%
I read everything I can find that speaks of Hamilton -- even of a duke, even of Alcibiades. Every time I read in Bouillet of the Hamiltons, of Arran -- which I found by accident this evening -- my heart pounds; I feel cold; I nearly faint. I was looking up Aspasia and I came upon Arran; it was unexpected and powerful -- they speak there of the Hamiltons, that divine name. No, truly, the name too is very beautiful -- ideal, superb, ravishing, adorable.

%% 013.0213 %%

I am mad; I adore him like a god. But truly he unites all qualities; one may without scruple worship him on one's knees.

I wish he would marry sooner; I am always like that. When something unpleasant is bound to happen -- at the departure, everyone, for I knew that pill had to be swallowed; the same with arriving in Nice -- I was burning to get there sooner so as not to wait for this catastrophe any longer, for the waiting is more terrible than the event itself.
