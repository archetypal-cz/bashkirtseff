# Sunday, 30 November 1873

%% 013.0137 %%
%% [#Fashion](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/FASHION.md) %%
%% [#Appearance](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/APPEARANCE.md) %%
%% [#Church](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/CHURCH.md) %%
%% Je voulais me lever de bonne heure et faire beaucoup de besogne, mais comme cela arrive toujours lorsque je fais des plans pour l'avenir, je n'ai rien fait. A peine ai-je pu être prête pour aller à l'église (robe bleue, chapeau noir, bien) tous les dimanches je suis laide. J'ai une figure si fragile, dormir une heure de plus ou de moins rend ma face fatiguée. %%
%% 2026-02-19T15:05:00 TR: "besogne" = work/tasks. "figure" = face/complexion per LAN, NOT body shape. %%
I meant to rise early and do a great deal of work, but as always happens when I make plans for the future, I did nothing. I could barely be ready in time for church (blue dress, black hat, fine). Every Sunday I am ugly. I have such a fragile face -- an hour more or less of sleep makes it look tired.

%% 013.0138 %%
%% [#Anitchkoff](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/ANITCHKOFF.md) [#Fedoroff](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/FEDOROFF.md) [#Tormosoff](../../_original/_glossary/culture/theater/TORMOSOFF.md) [#Diadia](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/DIADIA.md) %%
%% Il nous vient plusieurs chiens; les Anitchkoff, Mme Fedoroff, Tormosoff, diadia, étaient le matin. Peut-on le croire qu'Arnoldi lui a fait une visite ! Il l'a reçu comme il faut car il connaissait un Arnoldi, un grand coquin et son ennemi. %%
%% 2026-02-19T15:06:00 TR: "chiens" = dogs, Marie's contemptuous term for undesirable visitors. "coquin" = rogue/scoundrel. %%
Several dogs come to call: the Anitchkoffs, Mme Fedoroff, Tormosoff and *diadia* were here in the morning. Can one believe that Arnoldi paid him a visit! He received him properly, for he knew an Arnoldi, a great rogue and his enemy.

Mais elle entre, une tête énorme comme un paquet de foin surmonté d’un chapeau noir avec plusieurs plumes blanches, une longue robe de velours noir mal faite, le corsage garni de dentelles, des perles, des diamants, une espèce de chiffon jaune brodé sur les épaules. Sa face un peu blanchie. Affectée, maniérée, mal faite, corps allemand ou italien, tête italienne, elle me fait l’effet d’une petite détestation. Une visite très courte, le singe l’effraye, elle *screams,* puis on la mena je ne sais pourquoi dans mon étude, la porte s’est ouverte, le vent a soufflé, encore un *scream* et un saut en arrière. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:07:18 RSR: Restored from tome02.docx ¶7722–7751 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
At last comes ==the long ago spoken of== Seraphine. I expected to see a graceful, elegant, shapely woman with ravishing manners. But in she comes: an enormous head like a bundle of hay, topped by a black hat with several white feathers; a long, badly made black velvet dress, the bodice trimmed with lace; pearls, diamonds; a sort of embroidered yellow rag over her shoulders. Her face a little whitened. Affected, mannered, ill-shaped -- a German or Italian body, an Italian head -- she strikes me as a little horror. A very short visit; the monkey frightens her, she ==*screams,*== then they took her -- I do not know why -- into my study; the door flew open, the wind blew, another ==*scream*== and a jump backwards.

%% Mais le vent est terrible, on ferme le landau, la musique finie à quatre heures et il n’y a plus un chat. Vraiment je hais Nice. Ensuite nous allons à l’hôtel de la Paix chercher diadia, il va à Monaco avec les nôtres. Je ne monte pas et nous restons en voiture avec Dina, mais on vient nous chercher. Séraphine me paraît mieux cette fois, je m’habituerai à ses singeries. Elle regarde maman comme je regarde Hélène; comme maman est belle, simple, *unie, ronde, régulière à* côté de ce petit singe. C’est my mother's friend, - friends of girlhood. Ils partent. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:07:18 RSR: Restored from tome02.docx ¶7753 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
But the wind is terrible; the landau is closed, the concert ended at four, and there is not a soul left. Truly I hate Nice. Then we go to the Hôtel de la Paix to fetch *diadia;* he is going to Monaco with our party. I do not go up, and we stay in the carriage with Dina, but they come to fetch us. Seraphine seems better to me this time; I shall get used to her monkey tricks. She looks at Maman as I look at Helene. How beautiful Maman is -- simple, *even, rounded, regular* -- beside this little monkey. She is ==my mother's friend -- friends of girlhood.== They leave.

^[In English in the original.]

All three of us are bored and try to invent something extraordinary to dispel this boredom, but nothing comes to my dismal imagination; we exchange silly, affected remarks, and at last we play kings. After the first game I go off to sleep, and Bete wishes me to dream of the ==duke==[^013.0144.1] -- which I would gladly do.

[^013.0144.1]: In Spanish in the original: *duque*.
