# Wednesday, 3 June 1874

%% 020.0106 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% Je résume ces deux jours en peu de mots. Hier nous eûmes un désagrément à l'hôtel. %%
I will sum up these two days in a few words. Yesterday we had a disagreeable incident at the hotel.

There have been complaints that we go to bed too late, that I sing late, and finally -- oh horror! -- that we are seen undressed in the evening. Everyone's indignation and fury are beyond description.

I am ill, or rather very weak, I scarcely have the courage to write, but I was absolutely determined to record that as we were going along the rue de Rivoli, already almost at the place de la Concorde (grey dress, it is raining), a gentleman went by in a fast cab, like the Duke of Hamilton. He struck me as very handsome. He seemed to me a man of good birth, though of course one can be mistaken.
