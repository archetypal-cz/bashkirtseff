# Tuesday, 30 June 1874

We dine at the Marquise Viviani's. Mme Basilevitch comes with us, on foot. We were received by a servant in livery, then by the marquis and the marquise. I was in white, with my hair down my back; these past few days ==I fall in love with my hair.==[^1] I find it golden, curly, silky.
[^1]: In English in the original.

I reconcile myself to the marquis's manners: he is Italian! And on that account one may forgive him much. This Kirch bores me; I believe he dares to admire me. I have never looked him in the face; he is not worth the trouble. If at least he kept his distance; but he wants to be an equal.

I find that men [Crossed out: blasé] worn out and whatever else you like can never say as much as two women together. Maman and Basilevitch chatted and said so much that we were sent off; then Kirch came to say that, old and blasé as he is, he cannot bear to hear what those two ladies were saying. At the marquis's we laughed the whole time; it was about our ages, mine and my brother's. He wanted to prove that he is the elder; the marquis could not understand how for two months every year we could be the same age. I explained it to him and we laughed. I find him charming, and as comme il faut as an Italian can be. The marquise had me show my foot; her husband took it in his hand and saw, to my great satisfaction, that my shoes could not have been roomier.

Italian air; youth is poetry, but at thirty it is prose, and what prose!

I never turn my head towards him; he comes up and talks, I very little. Let us leave him to that poor woman who is looking for someone. He is handsome, but ordinary. But near the Pouhon we stop; we wake the woman, who appears at the window quite frightened, in a nightcap, holding a candle. The spring is opened and we take some water.

On leaving she shook hands with the Englishman, but he did not let go at once -- only little by little, sliding, and ended by pressing her fingers; it was dark, but I saw. Throughout dinner there had been talk of nothing but him. Puns and witticisms hardly spared him.
