I would depart for Paris on my way to Russia; he would follow me as far as Paris and speak to me of his love; I would let him speak. I even went so far as to have him set off for Russia. He — a submissive dog; I — a benevolent queen.

Moreover, his father looks like a duke — anyone seeing him would ask who he is. He (Girofla) is amusing, gay, agreeable.
