# Wednesday, 28 July 1875
%% Nous faisons nos photographies chez Bienmüller. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3514 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%

%% 037.0472 %%
%% [#Sapogenikoff](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/SAPOGENIKOFF.md) %%

%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3515 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%

%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3517 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Four poses:

%% 037.0474 %%
%% [#Olga](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/OLGA.md) [#Marie_Sapogenikoff](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/MARIE_SAPOGENIKOFF.md) %%

1. Just the three Graces.

2. The three Graces sewing; then the three Graces in medallion profile; and finally the three Graces in ringlets. Machenka stood in for Nina, since her face cannot be seen — nothing but a thick black veil.

%% 037.0476 %%
%% Il fallait immortaliser l'expédition. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3520 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
The expedition had to be immortalised.

%% 037.0477 %%
%% Jamais, je crois, depuis que cette photographie existe on n'y a vu pareil vacarme. Nous y restons de deux heures à cinq heures et demie. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3521 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Never, I think, since this photographer's studio has existed, has such an uproar been seen there. We stay there from two o'clock until half past five.

%% 037.0478 %%
%% Le photographe perdait haleine et ne faisait que courir comme un fou. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3522 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
The photographer was out of breath and did nothing but run about like a madman.

%% 037.0479 %%
%% [#Fashion](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/FASHION.md) %%
%% La sortie surtout était remarquable, nous étions toutes décoiffées, vêtues de chiffons et portant dans les bras nos toilettes jolies avec lesquelles nous étions venues. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3523 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Our departure above all was remarkable: we were all dishevelled, dressed in rags, and carrying in our arms the pretty outfits we had come in.

%% 037.0480 %%
%% [#Galula](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/GALULA.md) [#Saetone](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/SAETONE.md) [#Danis](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/DANIS.md) [#Nina](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/NINA.md) [#Ma_tante](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/MA_TANTE.md) %%
%% Nous étions très tranquillement assises chez moi le soir quand arrivent, Fiouloulou, Mercure et Danis. Comme je pars demain, je les reçois malgré l'absence de ma tante, d'ailleurs nous avons Nina. Mais ils ne restent pas longtemps. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3524 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
We were sitting very quietly at home in the evening when Fiouloulou, Mercury and Danis arrive. As I am leaving tomorrow, I receive them in spite of my aunt's absence; besides, we have Nina. But they do not stay long.

%% 037.0481 %%
%% [#Ma_tante](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/MA_TANTE.md) [#Tante_Etienne](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/TANTE_ETIENNE.md) [#Nina](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/NINA.md) [#Monaco](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/MONACO.md) %%
%% Cela ne nous empêche pas de veiller jusqu'à l'arrivée de ma tante et de Stiopa, de Monaco. Nina nous racontait ses aventures galantes. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3525 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
That does not stop us sitting up until my aunt and Stiopa come back from Monaco. Nina was telling us about her amorous adventures.

%% 037.0482 %%
%% [#Emile_d_Audiffret](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/EMILE_D_AUDIFFRET.md) [#Paris](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/PARIS.md) %%
%% On parle d'ajourner le voyage jusqu'à samedi. Cela me contrarie, je n'aime pas remettre. C'est si désagréable, quand on croit partir tel jour, quand on a tout préparé, quand on s'est habitué à cette idée, de ne pas partir. Et puis je me dépêche volontiers, Girofla étant à Paris. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3526 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
There is talk of putting off the journey until Saturday. That annoys me; I do not like postponing. It is so disagreeable, when one expects to leave on a certain day, when one has prepared everything, when one has grown used to the idea, not to leave. And besides, I am glad to hurry, Girofla being in Paris.

%% 037.0483 %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
%% [#Emile_d_Audiffret](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/EMILE_D_AUDIFFRET.md) %%
%% Depuis trop longtemps je suis dans l'inaction, [Rayé: il nous faut] je suis engourdie. Ce ne serait pas mal d'attendre jusqu'à samedi, cela le ferait languir, on ne languit parce qu'on aime, mais simplement pour rien. Souvent on m'annonce telle chose pour tel jour, la chose n'arrive pas et je suis contrariée quelque indifférente qu'elle me soit. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3527 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
I have been idle too long, [Crossed out: we must] I am torpid. It would not be a bad thing to wait until Saturday; it would make him pine — one does not pine because one loves, but simply for nothing. I am often told that something will happen on a certain day; it does not happen, and I am vexed, however indifferent it may be to me.

%% 037.0484 %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
%% [#Emile_d_Audiffret](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/EMILE_D_AUDIFFRET.md) [#Nice](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/NICE.md) %%
%% Restons jusqu'à samedi à Nice, oui, mais il me rendrait ce petit désagrément avec les intérêts. A quoi bon faire la guerre, d'autant plus que je ne sais si je suis de force pour lutter contre un homme aussi rusé. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3528 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Let us stay in Nice until Saturday, yes — but he would pay me back for this little annoyance with interest. What is the good of making war, especially as I do not know whether I am strong enough to fight a man so cunning?
