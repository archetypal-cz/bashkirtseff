Never by my will, never. Besides, there are plenty of dark men. Miloradovitch is dark, Blackprince too. Ah! if only it were Blackprince!

%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3387 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
In the dream Girofla was giving us dinner at his house, and in his garden he had a house built of strawberries, ravishingly large and fresh. Saëtone comes up, I look, and his waistcoat too is made of strawberries. Girofla was offering me gherkins: "Gherkins — you will marry a fool," says my dream book. That is reassuring; so I shall not marry the Niçois.

%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3390 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
At the concert we see Saëtone, Galula and Danis. The last says that neither he nor d'Aspremont can forget my entrance at the Carnival committee — that it was done with too much grace and charm. I send him on my behalf to challenge Saëtone to a duel, and, as Mercury refuses, I throw down my glove to him. Meanwhile Galula steals the other one. They will not give them back, and so I am robbed of a brand-new pair of gloves. Truly, they are not worth a pair of gloves.
