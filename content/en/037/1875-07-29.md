# Thursday, 29 July 1875
%% Je subis tous les ennuis qui accompagnent un départ. On se dépêche, on se fâche, on court, on oublie, on se rappelle, on crie. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3531–3533 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
I am suffering all the annoyances that go with a departure. People hurry, lose their tempers, run about, forget things, remember them, shout.

%% 037.0488 %%
%% Je suis toute déferrée. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3534 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
I am completely thrown off.
%% 2026-09-29T15:29:02 TR: "toute déferrée" → "completely thrown off" per TM (déferré). %%

%% 037.0489 %%
%% Et voilà qu'on parle de rester jusqu'à samedi. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3535 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
And now there is talk of staying until Saturday.

%% 037.0490 %%
%% [#Family](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/FAMILY.md) %%
%% [#Tante_Etienne](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/TANTE_ETIENNE.md) [#Russia](../../_original/_glossary/places/countries/RUSSIA.md) %%
%% Ce Stiopa voudrait remettre de jour en jour. C'est son caractère, il n'a le courage de rien. Il devait quitter la Russie au commencement d'avril et il n'est parti qu'au commencement de juillet. C'est impatientant ! %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3536 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
That Stiopa would like to put it off from day to day. It is his character: he has no courage for anything. He was to leave Russia at the beginning of April and only left at the beginning of July. It is exasperating!

%% 037.0491 %%
%% [#Family](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/FAMILY.md) %%
%% Nous restons. On me supplie de partir voyant que je suis contrariée mais je déclare qu'on m'a impatientée par ces stupides discours et que je ne veux pas partir et ne partirai pas. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3537 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
We are staying. They beg me to leave, seeing that I am vexed, but I declare that they have exasperated me with their stupid talk and that I do not want to leave and shall not leave.

%% 037.0492 %%
%% [#Health](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/HEALTH.md) %%
%% A la suite de ça, chacun plie devant moi et je fais la capricieuse. J'ai mal à la tête. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3538 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
As a result everyone gives way to me, and I indulge my whims. I have a headache.

%% 037.0493 %%
%% [#Promenade_des_Anglais](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/PROMENADE_DES_ANGLAIS.md) [#Villefranche](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/VILLEFRANCHE.md) %%
%% Nous allons à Villefranche. Pour m'amuser je menace du doigt trois curés en pleine Promenade qui restent ébahis et un quatrième gros et grave sur le pont et, enchantée je rentre de la meilleure humeur du monde. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3539 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
We go to Villefranche. For fun I wag my finger at three priests in the middle of the Promenade, who stand there dumbfounded, and at a fourth, fat and solemn, on the bridge; and, delighted, I come home in the best humour in the world.

%% 037.0494 %%
%% [#Family](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/FAMILY.md) %%
%% [#Grand_papa](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/GRAND_PAPA.md) [#Georges](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/GEORGES.md) %%
%% A dîner moitié riant, moitié sérieusement, je cherche querelle à mon grand-père. Mon cher oncle Georges est ici, je dois subir cette face d'ivrogne ! %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3540 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
At dinner, half laughing, half in earnest, I pick a quarrel with my grandfather. My dear uncle Georges is here — I must endure that drunkard's face!

%% 037.0495 %%
%% Après tout ce n'est pas un mal de rester encore deux jours. J'ai le temps. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3541 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
After all, it is no bad thing to stay two more days. I have time.
