# Monday, 26 July 1875
%% Je me sens mieux ayant plus dormi, mais je me suis réveillée dix fois la nuit. Cela ne m'arrive jamais, c'est étrange. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3451 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
I feel better, having slept more, but I woke ten times in the night. That never happens to me; it is strange.

%% 037.0419 %%
%% [#Olga](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/OLGA.md) [#Marie_Sapogenikoff](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/MARIE_SAPOGENIKOFF.md) %%
%% Hier j'étais d'une bonté divine, je trouvais tout le monde charmant et quand mes Grâces voulaient se moquer de quelqu'un je les arrêtais et disais tant de bien de ce quelqu'un que je riais moi-même. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3452 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Yesterday I was divinely kind: I found everyone charming, and when my Graces tried to make fun of someone I stopped them and said so many good things about that someone that I laughed myself.

%% 037.0420 %%

%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3453 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
We have this Russian cook, as I said. He was formerly with the consul, who, for some offence I no longer remember, had him imprisoned for six months. He came out when his term expired, and by law, within a week he must either leave France or find someone to vouch for his future conduct.

I shall make a very brilliant marriage, and very soon; several people will try to prevent it out of jealousy and will try to tarnish my reputation, but I shall come out victorious.
