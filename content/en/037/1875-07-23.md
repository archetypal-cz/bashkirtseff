# Friday, 23 July 1875
%% Ma tante qui était hier à Monaco avec Nina me raconte que cette dame, étant allée dîner, se mit selon sa charmante habitude à causer avec les domestiques. Et que le domestique lui a dit de Girofla bien des choses. Il a dit ce que *je pense de* lui et ce que j'ai dit hier dans ce même journal. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3400 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
My aunt, who was at Monaco yesterday with Nina, tells me that this lady, having gone there to dine, began, as is her charming habit, to chat with the servants. And that the manservant told her a great many things about Girofla. He said what *I think of* him and what I wrote yesterday in this very journal.

That Girofla was a demon of perfidy. That is no news to me; I had guessed it long before I knew him, this hero of so much gossip.

%% Ne suis-pas au-dessus des Audiffret et de ses semblables. Heureusement ou malheureusement je m'estime un tel trésor que personne à mes yeux n'est digne de ce trésor, et ceux qui osent lever les yeux sur lui sont regardés par moi comme de misérables vermisseaux à peine dignes de pitié. %% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3405 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Am I not above the Audiffrets and his like? Fortunately or unfortunately, I think myself such a treasure that no one in my eyes is worthy of it, and those who dare to raise their eyes to it I regard as wretched little worms, scarcely worthy of pity. I think myself a divinity, and cannot conceive that a man like Girofla could seriously dare to have designs.

%% A peine pourrai-je traiter d'égal un roi. Je crois que c'est très bien. Je regarde les hommes d'une telle hauteur que je suis charmante pour eux car il ne sied pas de mépriser ceux qui sont si bas. Je les regarde avec bienveillance tellement ils me semblent chétifs, je les regarde comme un lion regarderait une souris. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3406 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
I could scarcely treat a king as my equal. I believe that is quite right. I look down on men from such a height that I am charming to them, for it is not seemly to despise those who are so low. I look on them with benevolence, so puny do they seem to me — I look on them as a lion would look on a mouse.

Instead of going out I paint — a history painting. On an azure-blue background, with clouds: Godard dressed in blue (as is his custom), collar and cuffs of dazzling white, red cravat, red-and-white stockings, holding a rose in one hand and a bow and arrow in the other; he stands on one foot. He has a quiver and two little pink wings.

%% Il est si ressemblant que je ris tout en le fabriquant. Tout à fait cet air effrayé et cette croupe proéminente qu'il fait mouvoir comme se meuvent les queues de monstres qui entourent Neptune. Dans le coin à gauche est la tête de Saëtone, rouge, roux, avec des ailes roses et rousses et son monocle. Celui-là est reconnaissable à cause de la couleur et des attributs mais la face n'est pas réussie. A gauche est Fiouloulou ressemblant à faire crier, avec des ailes de chauve-souris, un monocle pendant dans l'air et une plume derrière l'oreille. Celui-là est si parfait qu'en le voyant les Sapogenikoff s'écrièrent que ce n'est pas une caricature mais c'est du talent, que c'est superbe. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3408 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
He is such a good likeness that I laugh as I put him together. Exactly that frightened air and that prominent rump, which he wags the way the tails of the monsters around Neptune wag. In the left-hand corner is the head of Saëtone, red, ginger-haired, with pink and russet wings and his monocle. That one is recognisable by the colour and the attributes, but the face has not come off. On the left is Fiouloulou, a likeness to make one shriek, with bat's wings, a monocle dangling in the air and a pen behind his ear. That one is so perfect that on seeing it the Sapogenikoffs cried out that it is not a caricature but real talent, that it is superb.

%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶3409–3411 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
At the bottom on the right is a tower, from whose battlements emerges a butterfly of a thousand colours with a man's head seen from behind, topped by four snail's horns. On the tower there is a large white heart with this inscription: ==Tower, beware!==[^1] Out from under the tower come two feet and a black tail. At the bottom on the left there is a prickly pear, like the one we stole from Gioia, from behind which a profile emerges — I meant it to be Danis's, but there is almost no likeness.
