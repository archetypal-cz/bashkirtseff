## Content Summary

Marie's summer in Nice, dominated by her flirtation with Audiffret (nicknamed "Girofla" and "le Surprenant"), his departure, and her enduring obsession with the Duke of Hamilton. Highlights:
- Moon divination ritual (Russian folk tradition)
- Audiffret's coded declaration of love written on biscuits (J.V.A.)
- Extended reflection on the constraints placed on unmarried women in courtship
- Visit to Gioia's villa (Hamilton's former lover), an intense emotional pilgrimage
- Commentary on Russian socialist/nihilist movements
- Latin quotations from Phaedrus, Caesar, and the *Dies Irae*
