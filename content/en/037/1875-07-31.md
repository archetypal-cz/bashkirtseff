%% 037.0529 %%

%% [Fin de page et deux pages suivantes blanches] Quid evenit ? %%
[End of page and the two following pages blank] ==What has happened?==[^1]
%% 2026-10-03T17:35:00 TR: Reading-conventions wave (KRR 2026-10-03): Latin lines translated in text and highlighted, Marie's Latin (with the transcription's slips) in the footnotes. %%

[^1]: In Latin in the original: *Quid evenit ?*

%% 037.0530 %%
%% [#Latin](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/LATIN.md) %%
%% Amor et spes qui sunt contraria semper nos agitant. Sumus juvenes et audaces. %%
==Love and hope, which are opposites, always agitate us. We are young and bold.==[^2]

[^2]: In Latin in the original: *Amor et spes qui sunt contraria semper nos agitant. Sumus juvenes et audaces.*

%% 037.0531 %%
%% [#Latin](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/LATIN.md) %%
%% [Un dessin de profil, des rangées de chevrons] lacrimosa dies illa. Qua resùrget ex favilla. %%
[A profile sketch; rows of chevrons] ==That day of weeping, when from the ashes shall rise==[^3]

[^3]: In Latin in the original: *lacrimosa dies illa. Qua resùrget ex favilla.* From the *Dies Irae*, the medieval Catholic requiem hymn.

%% 037.0532 %%
%% [#Latin](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/LATIN.md) %%
%% Judicàndus homo reus: . mie ergo parce, Deus. %%
==The guilty man, to be judged: . therefore spare, O God.==[^4]

[^4]: In Latin in the original: *Judicàndus homo reus: . mie ergo parce, Deus.* The *Dies Irae* continues: *Judicandus homo reus: huic ergo parce, Deus* ("the guilty man, to be judged: spare him therefore, O God"); the gap and *mie* are as in the transcription.
