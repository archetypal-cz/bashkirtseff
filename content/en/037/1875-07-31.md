# Saturday, 31 July 1875

%% 037.0333 %%
%% [#Latin](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/LATIN.md) [#French](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/FRENCH.md) %%
%% [Fin de page et deux pages suivantes blanches] Quid evenit ? %%
[End of page and the two following pages blank] *Quid evenit?*[^1]

[^1]: *Quid evenit?* — "What happened?" / "What has come to pass?" Latin.

%% 037.0334 %%
%% [#Latin](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/LATIN.md) [#French](../../_original/_glossary/culture/languages/FRENCH.md) %%
%% Amor et spes qui sunt contraria semper nos agitant. Sumus juvenes et audaces. %%
*Amor et spes qui sunt contraria semper nos agitant. Sumus juvenes et audaces.*[^2]

[^2]: "Love and hope, which are opposites, always agitate us. We are young and bold." Latin.

[A profile sketch; rows of chevrons] *lacrimosa dies illa. Qua resurget ex favilla.*[^3]

[^3]: From the *Dies Irae* (medieval Catholic requiem hymn): "Tearful is that day / when from the ashes / shall rise..." (*lacrimosa dies illa, / qua resurget ex favilla*).
