%% Je ne suis dégoûtée de la routine de cette vie niçoise et entièrement intérieur, qu'au point de ne pas le crier dans mon journal. C'est assez heureux. %%
# Tuesday, 15 May 1877
My disgust with the routine of this Nice life, lived entirely indoors, stops just short of screaming about it in my journal. That is lucky enough.

I saw Barnola only to receive his farewell — he came to deliver the customary cargo of innocent and not-so-innocent gossip, news, and all those things which have once again invaded me like the more or less agreeable perfume of this provincial life: its petty interests, its trivialities, its platitudes.

Audiffret has an air of confusion and sadness; one almost pities him. I wrapped him in a cascade of lively, mocking, slightly disdainful phrases — I who in the old days never knew what to say. His jokes seemed feeble to me, but his neck is very white.
