%% 2026-09-28T20:52:24 RSR: Restored from tome10.docx ¶2853, Mon Journal t.10 p.181 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
— Ah! Mademoiselle, said Rosalie, how sad it is when you leave a house because the masters, they cannot keep you on any longer! Just think of poor Monsieur Audiffret, in furnished rooms after so much wealth, after the tower, the palace! Nègre had tears in his eyes, and Monsieur Emile went straight to his room and shut himself in, very quickly, without saying a word. Ah! Justine is very sad, and it gave me a pang too. Wretched man! he... will rot.

I have undertaken a great work: a chronological and historical catalogue of all the writers in the world, starting from Moses. It will take a very long time; I want to read everything in order. I am whimsical and yet mad about order, symmetry, mathematical exactness. I should like everything to be labelled and numbered.
