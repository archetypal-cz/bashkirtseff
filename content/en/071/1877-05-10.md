# Thursday, 10 May 1877
%% Voici le programme des courses. Il faisait beau, nous étions placées tout près du pesage mais atrocement loin de tout le monde. %%

> Horse races
> in Florence
> 10 May 1877, at 3½ p.m.
> I. — The Elena Race
> Prize of 1,000 lire given by Princess Elena Demidoff
> for two-year-old horses foaled and bred in Italy.
> Entrance: 50 lire, run or pay. — The said entrance fees to go to the horse placed second, less one to be retained for the race fund.
> (Criterium)
> Distance: 1,700 metres. — Weight: colts 54 kg, fillies 52½ kg.
> 1. Palma. Chestnut filly, 2 years, foaled and bred in the Province of Pisa, by Yellow Oak out of Golden Dream, 52½ kg. — Count Gaston De Larderel. — White, green sleeves and cap.
> 2. Don Pasquale. Chestnut colt, 2 years, foaled and bred as above, by Yellow Oak out of Orpheline, 54 kg. — The same. — White, sash, green sleeves and cap.
> 3. Vasco. Chestnut-bay colt, 2 years, foaled and bred as above, by Yellow Oak out of Refraction, 54 kg. — Signor Ranieri Galletti. — Red, black sleeves and cap.
> 4. Bambagella. Chestnut filly, 2 years, foaled and bred as above, by Yellow Oak out of Royaline, 52½ kg. — The same. — Red, black sleeves and red cap.

> II. — The Cascine Race
> 1st prize 1,500 lire — 2nd prize 300 lire, given by the Society
> for horses of any age, foaled and bred in Italy.
> Entrance: 150 lire, half forfeit. — Of the said entrance fees, one to be retained for the race fund; the others, up to 300 lire, to go to the horse placed third, and any remainder to be added to the first prize.
> Distance: 1,937 metres, each horse carrying: 3 years, 51 kg; 4 years, 62 kg; 5 years, 65 kg; 6 years and upwards, 66½ kg. — Mares and geldings 1½ kg less. — Mares and horses out of an Italian dam that is not thoroughbred to carry 3 [?] kg less. Winners of a prize of 1,500 lire or more during the year to carry 2 kg extra.
> 1. Lucciola. Dark bay mare, 5 years, by Good By out of Refraction, 63½ kg. — Signor Federigo Tani. — Yellow, black cap.
> 2. Lady Hope. Bay mare, 5 years, by Heir-at-Law out of Verbena, 63½ kg. — Signor Od. Ginistrelli. — White and blue, blue cap.
> 3. Sporting Country. Bay colt, 3 years, by Heir-at-Law out of Biscia, 51 kg. — The same. — White and blue, blue sleeves and cap.
> 4. Vespa. Bay mare, 4 years, by Typhon out of Candia, 60½ [?] kg. — Signor Ranieri Galletti. — Red, black sleeves and cap.
> 5. Falconiere. Bay colt, 3 years, by Yellow Oak out of Royaline, 51 kg. — The same. — Red, black sleeves and red cap.
> 6. La Gorgona. Bay mare, 3 years, by Satyr out of Orpheline, 49½ [?] kg. — Count Gaston De Larderel. — White, green sleeves and cap.
> 7. Don Giovanni. Bay colt, 4 years, by Dusk out of Orpheline, 64 [?] kg. — The same. — White, sash, green sleeves and cap.

> V. — The Consolation Race
> Prize of 1,000 lire given by the Society
> for all horses and mares that have run in the races of the Florence season and have not won a first prize.
> Entrance: 100 lire, run or pay. — Of the said entrance fees one to be retained for the race fund, the others, up to 300 lire, to go to the horse placed second, and any remainder to be added to the prize.

> Entries to be made on the racecourse, and closed a quarter of an hour after the preceding race has been run.

But quickly, the story — good heavens, it is quite simple: it concerns Alexandre, who, to hear Marcuard, is a young man gone to the bad. Very fortunately he has fought a duel and gone off travelling, and in the meantime they will try to put his affairs in order. Gaston was a different matter; Gaston was the most elegant, the most sought-after man in Italy, whereas dear Alexandre has spent his life among moneylenders and *cocottes*, has been thrown out of the clubs, etc., etc. I know all that; that is not how I should like to see him. I should like him to be what his brother was... and more.
