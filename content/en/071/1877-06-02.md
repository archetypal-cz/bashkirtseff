%% # Samedi 2 juin 1877 %%
%% Toute la nuit je suis poursuivie par trois serpents qui m'enlacent, me mordent et que je finis avec des peines inouïes à couper en morceaux et à écraser. %%
# Saturday, 2 June 1877
All night I am pursued by three serpents that coil round me, bite me, and which I finally manage, with unimaginable difficulty, to cut into pieces and crush.

%% 2026-03-08T10:00:00 TR: "Les touas camaouades Laoudéel" = phonetic transcription of Lise's speech impediment: *r* → *oa* or *l*, so "Les trois camarades Larderei" becomes this garble. "Tarentelle" = tarantella, southern Italian folk dance. "Sérail... ennuque" = harem... eunuch. %%

The three comrades Larderei, Bernardaki and Grottaminarda discourse on the Théâtre Français while drinking thirty-three glasses of Roederer. And she begins by saying: *Les touas camaouades Laoudéel*[^29] etc. etc. Walitsky swoons with laughter, and we fall to talking of Melissano; then, warming up gradually, Dina rises to her feet and it all ends in a tarantella. I felt like drinking something extraordinary; Lise and I went to make chocolate in the gas kitchen where Maman makes her jams; then we drank the chocolate while Madame Kondareff lamented this convent life, a regular seraglio with Walitsky for its eunuch. Then we made the dogs howl, then I went to see Grand-papa, and, having at last exhausted all these occupations, I went to read and play Neapolitan songs, and finally, feeling very tired, I set about chasing Rosalie with... live fleas taken off Prater's back.

[^29]: Phonetic transcription of Lise's speech, who cannot pronounce *r*: "Les trois camarades Larderei" becomes "Les touas camaouades Laoudéel."
