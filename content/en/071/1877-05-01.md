# Tuesday, 1 May 1877
%% Je me suis endormie de si bonne heure hier, que je n'ai pas écrit, d'ailleurs il n'y a presque rien à dire. Il pleuvait, nous nous sommes promenées jusqu'à six heures en voiture fermée. Je ne pouvais me lasser d'admirer Florence, l'harmonie qui règne entre les anciennes merveilles et les maisons neuves me plaît infiniment. %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:52:24 RSR: Restored from tome10.docx ¶2450, Mon Journal t.10 p.150 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
I fell asleep so early yesterday that I did not write; besides, there is almost nothing to say. It was raining; we drove about in a closed carriage until six. I could not tire of admiring Florence; the harmony that reigns between the old marvels and the new houses pleases me immensely.

We find Marcuard's card. And that is all.

%% Aujourd'hui il y a plu, il y a une promenade aux Cascine, où nous avons vu Silène Righi, ex-coquine de Monsieur le comte. Je ne l'ai pas bien remarquée, d'ailleurs il faut que je la vois nue, absolument. Notre cocher s'est trouvé comme exprès, l'avoir servie lorsqu'elle était à Larderel; entre autres choses remarquables il nous l'a indiquée, promettant de montrer aussi Niccolini. %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:52:24 RSR: Restored from tome10.docx ¶2452, Mon Journal t.10 p.150 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
Today it rained; there was a promenade at the Cascine, where we saw Silène Righi, the former mistress of Monsieur the Count. I did not get a good look at her; besides, I absolutely must see her naked. Our coachman, as if on purpose, turned out to have served her when she belonged to Larderel; among other remarkable sights he pointed her out to us, promising to show us Niccolini as well.

%% 071.0081 %%
%% [#Marcuard](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/MARCUARD.md) %%
%% Il y avait peu de monde, et Marcuard. C'est l'homme qui me fait le plus bavarder. Son air paternel, serré et sournois me fait dire beaucoup de bêtises. %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:52:24 RSR: Restored from tome10.docx ¶2453, Mon Journal t.10 p.150 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
There were few people — and Marcuard. He is the man who makes me chatter most. His fatherly, close, sly air makes me say a great many foolish things.

%% 071.0082 %%
%% [#Ruffo](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/RUFFO.md) [#Veglione](../../_original/_glossary/culture/social_customs/VEGLIONE.md) [#Larderei](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/LARDEREI.md) %%
%% Enfin en repassant (avant de finir la promenade) devant la maison Larderel, nous avons rencontré le Persévérant du Veglione, le soi-disant prince Ruffo, mais comme il était dans sa voiture je suppose qu'il est Florentin. %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:52:24 RSR: Restored from tome10.docx ¶2454, Mon Journal t.10 p.150 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
At last, passing the Larderel house again (before the end of the drive), we met the Persevering One from the Veglione, the so-called Prince Ruffo; but as he was in his own carriage, I suppose he is a Florentine.

The wind has turned. Naples was a lovely dream. There I did not feel shut out of everything; we had acquaintances, and without going to the houses of the Neapolitan aristocracy we saw them at the Club, a distinguished gathering and rather hard to gain access to. And everyone expected to see us again next winter, receiving everyone and going everywhere; since people had that idea, it came to us as well — and besides, it is quite possible.

How lovely it would be to travel, to go to paradises like Naples, Florence, Rome, if one went there as the Mechterskys do, the Soukowkines, the... what do I know — all those people, in short.

%% Pendant trois mois j'avais oublié tout cela, et voilà que tout d'un coup, et dans une ville plus belle que Rome et que j'aime presque autant que Rome... Vous ne comprenez pas l'amour des villes ? Moi, j'ai des frissons aux seuls noms de Naples, (par convention, tout le monde l'aime) Florence et Rome. Quelle vie d'enchantement ce serait !! Car, s'il y a quelqu'un qui peut être parfaitement heureux, c'est moi. En effet je n'ai qu'un seul chagrin, tout ce que les autres pleurent tout cela me plaît. Ai-je crié pour Larderel ? Oui, mais seulement pour raconter la chose; c'était une grande émotion mais la vie ne serait pas belle s'il n'y en avait pas. %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:52:24 RSR: Restored from tome10.docx ¶2461, Mon Journal t.10 p.151 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). OCR corrected: «serait II Car»→«serait !! Car». %%
For three months I had forgotten all that, and now, all of a sudden, and in a city more beautiful than Rome, which I love almost as much as Rome... You do not understand the love of cities? I shiver at the mere names of Naples (by convention, everyone loves it), Florence and Rome. What an enchanted life it would be!! For if there is anyone who can be perfectly happy, it is I. Indeed, I have only one sorrow; everything that others weep over pleases me. Did I make an outcry over Larderel? Yes, but only in telling the story; it was a great emotion, but life would not be beautiful without such things.

In my distress I cling to the thought that the *Persevering One* may be some *suitable* prince, all the more since the cards promise me a fair man.

Ah! *mon empereur*.[^1] All the same, I cannot calmly tell myself that if you went into society in Florence you would meet the family of *Bijou*,[^2] and that it would make a very good impression — not that he would fall in love with you on that account, or that you would marry him, no, but simply that it would be a very fine thing.

%% 071.0094 %%

But do understand... No, you will never understand this torture! Imagine being given a splendid dress and then having a hole made in the back of it! That is the position. Laughable and heartbreaking.
