Ainsi aujourd'hui j'ai vu le palazzo Vecchio, ce témoin des grands actes politiques de la Toscane... et à présent infesté par un tas de plumitifs qui n'ont rien d'artistique même dans le sens du patriotisme. %%
%% 2026-09-28T20:52:24 RSR: Restored from tome10.docx ¶2596, Mon Journal t.10 p.163 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-27 rebuild). %%
I like visiting monuments and sights when the fancy takes me, as the urge to eat or sleep does. Sightseeing in the English manner... hm. So today I saw the Palazzo Vecchio, that witness of the great political deeds of Tuscany... and now infested with a pack of pen-pushers who have nothing artistic about them, not even in the sense of patriotism.

Since I have been studying Dante more, I find him truly miraculous, and it is not because everyone says so; people always said so, and I am only saying it now, which proves that I really admire him and do not simply follow received opinion. Then we went round the remarkable squares; I got out to see the statues and fountains close up.

Gaston de Larderel and his wife were at the Cascine in a ==*four-in-hand*==[^1] and looked at me a great deal. I am, besides, an object of attention for my whole family, which, be it said in passing, I find exceedingly likeable. I do not know whether it is because of... the family connection.

I skated for them, I smiled, I talked for them...
