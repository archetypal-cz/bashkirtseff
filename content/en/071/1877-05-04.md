%% # Vendredi 4 mai 1877 %%
%% Marcuard m'a trouvée barbouillant mon propre portrait. Si je réussis à faire une esquisse ce sera déjà bien, les ombres et les couleurs par cœur, ce n'est pas une plaisanterie. %%
# Friday, 4 May 1877
Marcuard found me daubing at my own portrait. If I manage to produce a sketch it will already be something — rendering shadows and colours from memory is no small matter.

I think and speak about my family as much as about Larderei himself. The day I went to the Naples Skating rink with Pelikan, I saw Mme de Mirafiore and she struck me as so disagreeable that I returned to the hotel declaring there was nothing there but a dreadful little woman and a sort of adventurer (her husband). But now I have changed my mind — not on account of the family connection, but because it seems to me they all look at me with such kindness.

%% 071.0143 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Righi](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/RIGHI.md) %%
%% J'ai poursuivi la Righi autant que possible en voiture, elle était avec l'enfant qui jouait et qu'elle embrassait de sa vilaine bouche peinte ! %%
I pursued la Righi as far as I possibly could in the carriage — she was with the child, who was playing, and whom she kissed with her ugly painted mouth!

%% 071.0144 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% J'ai réussi à voir l'enfant qui ressemble beaucoup... le même nez élégant et les mêmes yeux très-noirs et sans éclat, et déjà le même air, insoucieux, hautain et insensé. %%
I succeeded in seeing the child, who is very like... the same elegant nose and the same very dark, lustreless eyes, and already the same manner — nonchalant, haughty, and wild.
%% 2026-03-08T14:00:00 CON: "insensé" → "wild" (not "unreasonable"); the word means reckless/senseless/mad — describing a child's air alongside "insoucieux" and "hautain," "wild" captures the untamed quality Marie admires. %%

%% 071.0145 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% Je déteste les enfants, mais je prendrais bien celui-là, je voudrais tant l'embrasser, le tenir sur mes genoux... l'avoir toujours à moi. Et puis... ce serait si original. %%
I loathe children, but I should be glad enough to take that one — I should so like to kiss her, to hold her on my knee... to have her always for my own. And besides — it would be so original.

I want to see la Righi completely naked, and for that one would need to see her... we shall think of something... we have found a way.

%% 071.0147 %%
%% [#Dina](../../_original/_glossary/people/core/DINA.md) %%

In the evening Dina imitates the woman for me and we laugh until one in the morning.

%% 071.0148 %%
%% [#Aquarium](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/AQUARIUM.md) %%
%% Oh ! la folle ! L'Aquarium m'apparaît de jour en jour plus stupide. %%
Oh! the madwoman! The Aquarium[^3] strikes me as more stupid by the day.

[^3]: "L'Aquarium": Marie's nickname for a person (likely a rival or social figure from Naples). The exact referent is unclear from context alone.

%% 071.0149 %%
%% [#Pergola](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/PERGOLA.md) %%
%% La Pergola étant fermée je n'ai aucun moyen de me montrer; de la sorte on ne me présentera personne et je m'ennuierai... non, il faut espérer que non. %%
Since the Pergola[^4] is closed I have no means of showing myself; so no one will be introduced to me and I shall be bored — no, one must hope not.

[^4]: The Teatro della Pergola, Florence's principal opera house and theatre; closed for the season.

%% 2026-03-08T10:00:00 TR: [SUPERSEDED — see RED note below; Silène is La Righi] "Silène" is Marie's code name for Larderei, derived from the Sorrento hotel "Hôtel de la Sirène." "La fille de Silène" = Larderei's illegitimate daughter Alexandrine. %%
God, how I want Silène's daughter.[^5]
%% 2026-09-30T06:15:05 RED: HIGH 071.0150 — footnote [^5] and the TR note above called Silène Larderei's code name; per TM ("La Righi / Silène"), 070.0001 [^silene] and 071.0120 (Silène's sister la Michetta, Silène a former mime), Silène is La Righi. [^5]: "Silène" (Silenus) is the code name for La Righi, Larderei's mistress, coined by Melissano and Marcuard at Sorrento by swapping the *r* of the Hôtel de la Sirène for an *l*. Her daughter by Larderei, Alexandrine, is the child Marie has been obsessing over.
