Who? I asked calmly. I thought she meant Fiouloulou in my dream.

What nonsense, my dear girl, ==and we let me drop the subject==[^1].

%% 035.0132 %%

Nina dresses and takes us to Rumpelmayer's. She is a woman I like: she makes no fuss and is as mad as we are.

%% 035.0133 %%

In the evening she tells us about her love affairs, her fine adventures. I cannot help doubting.

She was never beautiful. True, one does not need beauty for that.

I have a new torment.

%% 035.0136 %%

For the benefit of those poor flood victims of Toulouse and elsewhere,[^035.0136.1] they are going to organise fêtes, country balls, collections, etc., etc.

%% 035.0137 %%
%% [#Society](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/SOCIETY.md) %%
%% [#Religion](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/RELIGION.md) %%
%% [#Mouzay](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/MOUZAY.md) %%
%% De Mouzay est venue mercredi nous dire qu'on veut nous offrir d'être dames patronesses, (elle est partie hier). Elle nous a dit que l'on viendrait chez nous pour cela. On ne vient pas, je n'entends plus parler de rien. Mon Dieu est-ce que vous me ferez subir la vexation de me faire assister à ces fêtes en étrangère, en vilaine tandis que tout le monde y prendra part. Même en été, rien, rien ! %%
De Mouzay came on Wednesday to tell us that they want to invite us to be lady patronesses (she left yesterday). She told us someone would call on us about it. Nobody calls, and I hear nothing more of it. My God, will you make me suffer the vexation of attending these fêtes as an outsider, a nobody, while everyone else takes part? Even in summer — nothing, nothing!

%% 035.0138 %%
%% C'est terrible. %%
It is terrible.

%% 035.0139 %%
%% [#Nice](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/NICE.md) %%
%% Vrai Dieu ! Nice ne me donne qu'ennuis et chagrins. Je veux quitter cette ville maudite qui me maltraite tellement. %%
By heaven! Nice gives me nothing but vexations and sorrows. I want to leave this accursed town that treats me so badly.

%% 035.0140 %%
%% [#Society](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/SOCIETY.md) %%

This winter I so longed to sell at the stalls in the Square Masséna.

%% 035.0141 %%
%% [#Society](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/SOCIETY.md) %%
%% Je voyais toutes ces heureuses taquines, taquines pas autre chose. Et moi, misérable, de côté, dans l'ombre, à me promener par ce vil jardin. Quel intérêt pour avoir une telle fête si l'on n'en est point ? %%
I watched all those happy teases — teases, nothing more. And I, wretched, off to one side, in the shadows, walking about that vile garden. What is the good of having such a fête if one is not part of it?

%% 035.0142 %%
%% [#Emotions](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/EMOTIONS.md) %%

I lament in advance because I am full of doubts and uncertainties, from being so used to seeing every pleasure pass under my nose.

%% Je puis espérer mieux ! %%
I may hope for better!

And what gives me that assurance? Are we not in the most execrable of positions? Scorned even in Nice, even by the vile women here. And why?

[^035.0136.1]: On 23 June 1875 the Garonne flooded Toulouse, rising to 8.32 m at the Pont-Neuf, the highest level on record; about 208 people died and some 1,200 houses were destroyed, and the Saint-Cyprien suburb was the worst hit (herodote.net, «France: des crues dévastatrices»; Université Toulouse Jean Jaurès, programme of the 1875 flood colloquium, citing La Petite Gironde). The Café Américain benefit and the bal champêtre in Nice (035.0151, 035.0167) were held for these victims.
