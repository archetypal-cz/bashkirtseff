# Monday, 28 June 1875
%% Je vais au bain et j'y vois Saëtone, mon oncle Saëtone. Bihovetz cause avec ma tante: %%
I go bathing and there I see Saëtone, my uncle Saëtone. Bihovetz is chatting with my aunt.

%% 035.0028 %%
%% [#Saetone](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/SAETONE.md) [#Emile_d_Audiffret](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/EMILE_D_AUDIFFRET.md) %%
%% — Avez-vous vu Audiffret avant son départ ? me demande mon oncle. %%
Did you see Audiffret before he left? my uncle asks me.

%% 035.0029 %%
%% — Non Monsieur. %%
No, Monsieur.

%% 035.0030 %%
%% — Comment ! s'écrie-t-il comme si je disais une chose incroyable. %%
What! he exclaims, as though I had said something incredible.

%% 035.0031 %%
%% [#Emile_d_Audiffret](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/EMILE_D_AUDIFFRET.md) %%
%% — Il est venu mais nous étions sorties, de sorte qu'il a laissé ses cartes seulement. %%
He called, but we were out, so he only left his cards.

%% 035.0032 %%
%% [#Saetone](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/SAETONE.md) %%
%% — Ah ! fit mon oncle, comme pour dire, ah ! la bonne heure, ceci est croyable, puis: %%
Ah! said my uncle, as if to say: ah, that's more like it, that I can believe. Then:

%% 035.0033 %%
%% [#Emile_d_Audiffret](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/EMILE_D_AUDIFFRET.md) %%
%% — D'ailleurs il sera ici après-demain, j'ai reçu une lettre de lui aujourd'hui, il me dit que demain ou après-demain il sera ici. %%
Besides, he will be here the day after tomorrow. I had a letter from him today; he tells me he will be here tomorrow or the day after.

%% 035.0034 %%
%% [#Ma_tante](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/MA_TANTE.md) %%
%% A ce moment ma tante se mêle à la conversation. %%
At this point my aunt joins in the conversation.

%% 035.0035 %%
%% [#Ma_tante](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/MA_TANTE.md) [#Saetone](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/SAETONE.md) [#Emile_d_Audiffret](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/EMILE_D_AUDIFFRET.md) %%
%% A la maison elle raconte que Saëtone m'a dit cela à demi-voix, que l'autre a écrit cette lettre pour que mon oncle Saëtone me le dise etc. %%
At home she tells everyone that Saëtone said it to me in an undertone, that the other one wrote that letter so that my uncle Saëtone would tell me, etc.

%% Alors l'homme revient dans deux jours, et moi qui le croyait arrivé ce matin, ses volets étaient ouverts. J'étais même à cause de cela, impatiente de sortir et j'ai manqué la traduction d'un chapitre de César et l'étude d'une tarentelle de Chopin. %%
So the man is coming back in two days — and I thought he had arrived this morning, his shutters were open. Because of it I was even impatient to go out, and I missed translating a chapter of Caesar and practising a Chopin tarantella.

%% 035.0037 %%
%% [#Bihovetz](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/BIHOVETZ.md) [#Saetone](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/SAETONE.md) [#Galula](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/GALULA.md) %%
%% Le soir on se rassemble chez nous. Bihovetz, Saëtone et Galula, Galula avec mon sou en breloque. %%
In the evening everyone gathers at our house: Bihovetz, Saëtone and Galula, Galula wearing my sou as a charm on his chain.

%% 035.0038 %%
%% [#Society](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/SOCIETY.md) %%
%% On danse Sir Roger de Coverley, on joue à beaucoup de jeux innocents et on rit comme des bienheureux. %%
We dance Sir Roger de Coverley[^035.0038.1], play a great many parlour games, and laugh as happily as the blessed.

%% 035.0039 %%
%% [#Society](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/SOCIETY.md) %%
%% [#Olga](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/OLGA.md) [#Marie_Sapogenikoff](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/MARIE_SAPOGENIKOFF.md) %%
%% Ces faquins restent jusqu'à deux heures du matin et seraient demeurés plus longtemps si Marie et Olga n'eussent donné le signal, et encore ils n'avaient pas l'air d'avoir envie de se retirer. %%
These rascals stay until two in the morning and would have stayed longer still if Marie and Olga had not given the signal, and even then they did not look as if they wanted to leave.

%% 035.0040 %%

Maman was in her room, ill and in bed.
