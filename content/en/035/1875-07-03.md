# Saturday, 3 July 1875
%% Au bain de mer nous sommes avec les Sapogenikoff et le frère d'Olga. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶2554 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
At the sea baths we are with the Sapogenikoffs and Olga's brother.

%% 035.0150 %%
%% [#Saetone](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/SAETONE.md) %%
%% Enotëas ne fait que saluer et s'en va causer avec d'affreux singes. J'ai dormi cinq heures, je me sens molle. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶2555 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Enoteas merely bows and goes off to chat with some hideous monkeys. I slept five hours; I feel limp.

%% 035.0151 %%
%% [#Society](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/SOCIETY.md) %%
%% Nous allons prendre des billets pour le Café Américain. C'est un jardin et on y joue des petites pièces et on chante. Ce soir c'est au bénéfice des inondés et tout le monde y va. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶2556 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
We are going to get tickets for the Café Américain. It is a garden where they put on little plays and there is singing. This evening it is for the benefit of the flood victims, and everyone is going.

%% [#Mme_de_Ballore](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/MME_DE_BALLORE.md) [#Wykerslooth](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/BARONNE_DE_WYKERSLOOTH.md) [#Maman](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/MAMAN.md) %%

%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶2557 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Mmes de Ballore and Wykerslooth asked Maman to go.

%% 035.0153 %%

%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶2558 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
We have two benches to ourselves. On the left, next to me, is Saëtone, and almost opposite, a little to one side, Girofla, in a straw hat and with the most Niçois air imaginable. On the way past I say good evening to my friend de Bauche and to M. de Ballore.

%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶2562 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
This darling boy, with a behind as prominent as a woman's, is paying court to my friend Fiouloulou. As ==he is (he, Godard) of no consequence==[^1], I look at him as much as I please. When Fiouloulou came over to me, he, my darling Godard, stood by a tree, where he stayed all evening and where from time to time I sought him out with my eyes.

%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶2563 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Girofla was as melancholy as a lover, hardly spoke, watched the actors, then lowered his head and hid his face in his hands, or covered it with his dreadful hat, and from under the hat his beautiful black eyes fixed themselves on me so intently that I turned towards him involuntarily, as if spellbound, and the instant our eyes met I felt something like an electric shock and looked away — but not for long.

S. Olga Sapogenikoff, cette enfant-là est toute rouge des regards que le beau jeune homme lui lance. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:28:41 RSR: Restored from tome05.docx ¶2565 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
I am in the most unearthly high spirits, renewed at every moment by the sweet nothings Saëtone murmurs to me, and by *Désiré* (I desire Godard out loud, so I have named him *Désiré*), who with his imbecile air pays attention to me from afar, and by Girofla, who, head bowed, traces my initials M.B. in the sand in every possible way, then his own, *E.A.* — but to throw me off the scent he traces O.S., Olga Sapogenikoff. That child is scarlet from the looks the handsome young man throws her.
