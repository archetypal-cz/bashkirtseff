## Key Translation Decisions

### The Veauradieux Motif
The comedy *Le Procès Veauradieux* (Hennequin & Delacour, 1875) — a Paris sensation with its catchphrase *bonnet grec et robe de chambre* — runs through the entire carnet as the lens through which Marie processes Audiffret's visits to Gioia. All three anonymous letters use the formula. The play is footnoted on first occurrence (1875-09-28) with plot summary.
