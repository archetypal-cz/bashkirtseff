I go out alone — I am pretty. Sabatini joins me at ten o'clock; we resume our Italian conversations. Gone out to observe — I do not forget it — and I seat myself on a bench halfway between Gioia's house and ours. Sabatini reads me *Niccolò de' Lapi*.[^45.379.1] I listen little. At half past ten a cab with a white horse passes, followed by a calèche. And ten minutes later the calèche passes again, containing Gioia, her old woman, and her two children. As for the two-seat cab — it goes off by the Rue de France, no doubt, since I do not see it pass.
