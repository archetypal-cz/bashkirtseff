We do not do this individual the honour of taking offence at his absences. Everything goes on as it did a year ago, except that the Comtesse Couvelet has replaced the Hungarian woman. Blanc tells me several things I knew and others I did not. [words blacked out: The chivalrous] Popaul gives his mistress's name to his wife. He calls her Ninette so as not to get them mixed up. Ninette "chatters all day long", enough to tire out a regiment. She claims to dictate his newspaper articles to the chivalrous Popaul, who has grown thin, while Ninette, on the other hand, has grown fat, not at the waist, [words blacked out: adds Blanc. Her face is covered in] pimples. I am disgusted merely writing it. Do you remember the superb Popaul saying that if he saw the slightest little blemish on a woman's face he was disgusted by it? I asked nothing; Dina asked very few questions, and in jest, and the captain needed no urging to tell.
