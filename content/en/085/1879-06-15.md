%% Cela m'amuse comme si je... et même quand on parle de l'hôtel Mirabeau je souris comme si je venais de faire la connaissance d'un Mirabeau qui serait quelque chose de moderne et *d'intéressant.* %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:30 RSR: Restored from tome13.docx ¶492–495, Mon Journal t.13 pp.34–35 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
It amuses me as if I... and even when they speak of the Hôtel Mirabeau I smile as though I had just made the acquaintance of a Mirabeau who would be someone modern and *interesting.*

%% 085.0312 %%

%% 085.0321 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
