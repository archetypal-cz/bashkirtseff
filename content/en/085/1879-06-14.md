%% 2026-09-29T01:01:30 RSR: Restored from tome13.docx ¶488, Mon Journal t.13 p.34 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
A fair number of people: Monsieur Randouin — Madame is unwell — and Monsieur Obidine, a Russian whose acquaintance we made at Nice. A gentleman who says he would have done anything in the world to be introduced to me had he not been already, after what the Greek Despotopoulo told him about me — you know, the old fellow I talked with on the way to Monaco this winter, who finds my knowledge of politics, of reading, of everything, prodigious. I must say he is rather stupid.
