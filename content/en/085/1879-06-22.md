I read the *Figaro* on waking; it made me weep again, so much that my pillow was damp and my hair [words blacked out: wet. It is funny] if you like, but I am sad and furious with the English, who murdered him or handed him over. We are still at table; Bichinsky and his wife have been invited, to thank them for Thursday's tickets and to encourage them to render other small services.
