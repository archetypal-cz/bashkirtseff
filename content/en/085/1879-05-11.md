%% 2026-10-01T07:55:00 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French (date heading moved to 085.0061; paragraph text unchanged). %%
I wake in a wild good humour and go to church with Maman to set a good example to the common people. For the first time in quite a while, I am very pretty today. We go to the Gavinis', where there is a crowd — three former ambassadors and I do not know how many others. Yesterday Mother Gavini dined at the Rothschilds' with our ambassador, which my aunt has the bad taste to seem surprised by, adding that Prince Orloff went nowhere, was invisible. "Not at all, not at all," came the reply.
