# Wednesday, 26 August 1874
No beautiful dream. Maman and Dina go out without me and I go back to bed at seven.

Maman told my aunt last evening, ending thus: "*Moussia* must be delighted; yesterday they chased after her and stared at her truly to the point of importunity."

%% 2026-10-02T19:50:16 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 3 of 15 of 023.0341, which held 15 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.4; tome04.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
Merjeewsky comes; I am not in the salon, I am not receiving. All at once everyone rushes forward crying, "*Look, look who is passing!*" I lift the blinds and see Doria, but as he inspires little enthusiasm in me, I withdraw while they all throw themselves at the windows and cry out like village dogs when a carriage passes.

On the pier, with Foster I -- but there we are set upon by that Jewish monster, cad, Rosen. A man one cannot shake off. Before the pier I took a boat ride in the harbour with my aunt and Dina.
