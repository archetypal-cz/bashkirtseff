# Monday, 24 August 1874
I bathe with Lise and Katia; the second bath is more pleasant. But the people bathing jump like devils and make me laugh. I lunch at the Plage; Basilewsky is at a table not far from us. Doenhoff comes, then Basilewsky joins the conversation. If Basilevitch only knew!

I have my hair down my back; brown dress and hat. We go to the pier -- nothing of note. Little Labanoff is there, and as we return she was walking behind and I had the advantage of hearing her small, broken voice carrying on a leaning sort of conversation.

Doenhoff comes home with us and stays rather long.

A great annoyance befalls Maman. At six o'clock, as agreed, we, Basilevitch and Basilewsky were to dine at the Cercle des Bains. Barkowsky was to come and play. They took out my hairpins and I spent a long time doing my hair. Basilevitch went ahead with Dina. When I arrive with Maman, there is no one.

Basilevitch has led Basilewsky off to the Hotel de l'Ocean to dine with the Gardeffs, and left Dina all alone -- who fortunately found the Tamancheffs.

Maman is in despair at having missed this dinner with Basilewsky. She has all sorts of ideas.

At last, after several tugs of war which I endure with all due dignity, we sit down to table at the Kursaal cafe while the music plays. Maman in her despair eats only an omelette and a cup of chocolate. The Tamancheffs arrive with Dina. They are kind and I shall miss them. They leave the day after tomorrow.

To be with them I go to the Cercle des Bains. Baron Leiss asks me to dance; I introduce him to Maman. "Well, well, *si' plait*?" [sic] There is his conversation.

We stay until the end.
