# Saturday, 12 September 1874
We left London at twenty past four, I with all imaginable regrets. And at six o'clock we arrived at Canterbury. In the street we meet Mrs Howard; she recognises us. [Crossed out: In great haste] We jump from the carriage before the house already; Mrs Howard, who was on foot, comes running, and then all the children. Lise has become pretty -- prettier than her sister, who loses a great deal in her presence.
