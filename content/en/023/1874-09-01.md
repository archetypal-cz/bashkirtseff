# Tuesday, 1 September 1874
I took my bath; I remember nothing because I did not have the key to my diary, and it is only on Thursday that I find it and write.

%% 023.0436 %%
%% Basilewsky au moment où nous nous levions de table à déjeuner, vient avec nous jusqu'à chez nous. Basilévitch le voyant chez nous accourt, parée de toutes ses parures. Comme elle traversait la rue Basilewsky dit qu'elle avait peu de goût et mettait beaucoup trop de choses. Aussitôt qu'elle entra il la complimenta sur son goût disant qu'il venait de le dire à ces dames. Il pense que nous et Basilévitch sommes des rivales et que lui est la cause des rivalités. Avec lui on rit toujours et c'est quelque chose. %%
%% 2026-10-02T19:50:16 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 2 of 16 of 023.0435, which held 16 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.4; tome04.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
Basilewsky, at the moment we were rising from the lunch table, comes with us to our rooms. Basilevitch, seeing him at ours, comes running, decked out in all her finery. As she was crossing the street, Basilewsky said she had little taste and wore far too many things. The moment she entered he complimented her on her taste, saying he had just been saying so to these ladies. He thinks we and Basilevitch are rivals and that he is the cause of the rivalry. With him one always laughs, and that counts for something.

%% 023.0437 %%
%% Je vais au bal malgré moi. Basilévitch vient et nous enlève disant qu'on lui a promis, qu'elle n'a engagé personne d'autre etc. Robe rose, comme les autres fois. %%
%% 2026-10-02T19:50:16 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 3 of 16 of 023.0435, which held 16 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.4; tome04.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
I go to the ball despite myself. Basilevitch comes and carries us off, saying she was promised, that she has engaged no one else, etc. Pink dress, as on the other occasions.

%% 023.0438 %%
%% Je ne demandais pas mieux que rester tranquille mais on m'invita chaque fois. Je crois que je plaîs à Foster 2 qui est tellement laid. J'ai dansé avec ce ministre du Portugal qui nous fût présenté au bal dernier, il danse comme un singe et j'avais beaucoup de peine à aller en mesure. %%
%% 2026-10-02T19:50:16 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 4 of 16 of 023.0435, which held 16 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.4; tome04.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
I asked nothing better than to stay quiet, but they invited me every time. I believe I please Foster 2, who is terribly ugly. I danced with that Portuguese minister who was introduced to us at the last ball; he dances like a monkey, and I had great difficulty keeping time.

%% 023.0439 %%
%% Le petit Chtcherbinisa m'a été présenté. Mais nous nous reconnûmes, en effet nous nous connaissons depuis Krement-strang. Il a peu changé, toujours les mêmes yeux baissés et effarés. %%
%% 2026-10-02T19:50:16 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 5 of 16 of 023.0435, which held 16 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.4; tome04.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
Young Chtcherbinisa was introduced to me. But we recognised each other; indeed we have known each other since Krementstrang. He has changed little -- always the same lowered, startled eyes.

%% 023.0440 %%
%% Plobster a épuisé toute son intelligence en poses; depuis que nous nous connaissons ce n'est plus intéressant. Basilévitch le veut, mais il est bête. %%
%% 2026-10-02T19:50:16 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 6 of 16 of 023.0435, which held 16 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.4; tome04.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
Plobster has exhausted all his intelligence in poses; now that we know each other, it is no longer interesting. Basilevitch wants him, but he is stupid.

%% 023.0441 %%
%% Le comte m'invite, je consens, puis en dansant: %%
%% 2026-10-02T19:50:16 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 7 of 16 of 023.0435, which held 16 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.4; tome04.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
The count invites me to dance; I consent, then while dancing:

%% 023.0442 %%
%% - Vous avez oublié que je ne vous parle plus. %%
%% 2026-10-02T19:50:16 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 8 of 16 of 023.0435, which held 16 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.4; tome04.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
"You have forgotten that I no longer speak to you."

%% 023.0443 %%
%% - Non, Mademoiselle, et c'est pour éviter le désagrément de m'adresser la parole que je me retirais; mais vous partez: %%

"No, Mademoiselle, and it was to spare you the unpleasantness of addressing me that I was keeping away; but you are leaving."

%% 023.0444 %%
%% - Oui je pars et je vous pardonne et je vous bénis. %%
%% 2026-10-02T19:50:16 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 10 of 16 of 023.0435, which held 16 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.4; tome04.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
"Yes, I am leaving and I forgive you, and I bless you."

%% 023.0445 %%
%% Puis je lui ai dit que ses façons ne me plaisent pas, il veut savoir ce qui ne me plaît pas, mais je ne le lui dis pas ne le sachant pas moi-même. En un mot, je le pardonne; on m'ennuyait trop chez nous; je serai désormais plus tranquille. Il est plus qu'heureux. Ma tante, Basilévitch, Moelenar vont à la confiserie et moi je rentre avec Paul et Merjeewsky qui est tellement enchanté qu'il prend des airs papillons et que je le réprimande. %%
%% 2026-10-02T19:50:16 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 11 of 16 of 023.0435, which held 16 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.4; tome04.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
Then I told him his manner does not please me; he wants to know what displeases me, but I do not tell him, not knowing myself. In a word, I forgive him; they were tormenting me too much at home; I shall henceforth be more at peace. He is more than happy. My aunt, Basilevitch and Moelenar go to the confectionery, and I go home with Paul and Merjeewsky, who is so delighted he takes on butterfly airs and I must reprimand him.

%% 023.0446 %%
%% Pour compléter son bonheur je lui dis d'aller chercher des gâteaux pour maman. %%
%% 2026-10-02T19:50:16 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 12 of 16 of 023.0435, which held 16 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.4; tome04.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
To complete his happiness I send him to fetch cakes for Maman.

%% 023.0447 %%
%% [Rayé: Mademoiselle, pourriez-vous demander à Walitsky s'il veut venir.] Walitsky vous fait dire que vous pouvez chercher du phosphore vous-même. Mais ce doit être ennuyeux. Je comprends lorsqu'on est en société. %%

[Crossed out: Mademoiselle, could you ask Walitsky if he wishes to come.] Walitsky sends word that you may fetch your own phosphorus. But that must be tedious. I understand, when one is in company.

%% 023.0448 %%
%% - Oui, mais on peut penser à la société. %%
%% 2026-10-02T19:50:16 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 14 of 16 of 023.0435, which held 16 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.4; tome04.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
"Yes, but one can think about the company."

%% 023.0449 %%
%% Et voilà le fou pour toute la nuit à rôder au bord de la mer et à composer des vers. %%
%% 2026-10-02T19:50:16 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 15 of 16 of 023.0435, which held 16 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.4; tome04.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
