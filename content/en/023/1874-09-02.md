# Wednesday, 2 September 1874
I am homesick; I want to return to Nice. I am weary of this existence. I am so well in Nice (I did not say "we"). I want to see the Promenade again, my dear Promenade, our villa, the October rains, the Place Massena, the quays, the public garden, the London House -- and everything. I feel extreme pleasure in picturing myself there.
