# Saturday, 22 August 1874
(Brown Worth dress, brown Virot hat, hanging golden curled hair -- not bad.) I take my first bath at two o'clock; there is no one. My bathing costume is badly made; coming out I could find no towels; the dye ran. I am very displeased.
