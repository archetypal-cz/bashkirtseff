# Thursday, 10 September 1874
Mine is a happy nature. The day before yesterday my aunt told us very bad news: that Emile, my poor uncle, died four months ago. Half an hour later I was no longer thinking of it, and only today do I remember to write it down.

I had difficulty tearing myself from those statues and busts representing ancient Rome. I love, to the point of madness, ancient and Roman history; once I am in a museum I should like to stay, because there everything represents either mythological or historical subjects. I love ancient monuments -- those temples, those amphitheatres -- everything that is old. The modern tempts me little. But among antiquities I live happily.
