# Sunday, 30 August 1874
Again it rains, and I happily miss my sea bath. Towards four o'clock we go out; I stay with my aunt at the Kursaal. Plobster comes near us. This man had a shirt so dirty I was scandalised. Basilevitch passes and stares at the sight of Moelenar.
