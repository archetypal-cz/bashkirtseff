# Thursday, 27 August 1874
Florence comes at ten and we go to the market, then to bathe. She is with us in the cabin.

The ocean is comparatively calm; ==I enjoy my bath==.[^1] Before entering the water, Florence tells me things about the English royal family, and then about Hamilton. His wife is very jealous. I asked the younger Foster what people think of him: do they call him handsome or ugly?
