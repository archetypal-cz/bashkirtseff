# Tuesday, 25 August 1874
At half past two Miss Foster and two other Englishwomen come, and we go together to the pier with the Tamancheffs and everyone. I was talking with an Englishwoman, ugly and red-haired; having her pronounce Russian words, I found she pronounces the *kh* sound. I asked whether she was not Scottish; she is.
