# Thursday, 20 August 1874
We move into the apartment we have taken for a month, at 104 rue Longue, on the first floor. Halfway between the Kursaal and the Casino, with a balcony from which one can see the ocean; a pretty little salon. Charming entrance. I have a small room to myself. A *doll's apartment*.

By two o'clock everything is done and I go with the Tamancheffs to the pier (I do not know how to spell this word); there I see no one. We go on to the Kursaal where I exchange a few words with the kind Foster girl.

Basilevitch comes to our place. [Crossed out: my aunt] And we go with her to dine at the Grand Cafe hotel where the Tamancheffs are staying; one dines badly there. This dear family bores me.

Afterwards we go to the lace shops. My aunt, who used to call Basilevitch a *worn-out woman*, etc., now fraternizes a little with her, and Basilevitch, for want of an escort, willingly discusses bobbin lace and Valenciennes.

I forgot: as I was coming home before dinner, I see Doria -- from behind, as is my custom. He was walking behind us, then passed us, before I had time to see his face.

I told the Tamancheffs that the true and first of the Plobster dynasty was before them. Each of the girls wanted to shout it to Maman, who was on the balcony, but I reserved for myself the glory of announcing it.

The Tam-tam-tams drag us to the children's ball at six o'clock. This ball reminds me of Baden; there was even a Remy de Gonzales.

From there I go home, leaving them to wander. My aunt and Basilevitch come at half past nine; they had been lace-hunting. On leaving, Basilevitch took my arm affectionately and placed her hand on my head, which my aunt observed with pleasure. It is because Basilevitch claims to feel sympathy for me.

I have just opened a letter from Kirsh in which he says there was a cotillion organized by Haristoff, de Biesme, etc., etc., which lasted until nine in the morning.

I could not make it all out, but I believe I read that they are going to organize something more and that Kirsh invites us. I have already pictured in my head the journey I should like to make -- but I should also like to go with only a few Plobsters.

I await Maman and everyone to discuss it with them and decipher the letter.
