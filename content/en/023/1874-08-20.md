# Thursday, 20 August 1874
We move into the apartment we have taken for a month, at 104 rue Longue, on the first floor. Halfway between the Kursaal and the Casino, with a balcony from which one can see the ocean; a pretty little salon. Charming entrance. I have a small room to myself. A *doll's apartment*.

Afterwards we go to the lace shops. My aunt, who used to call Basilevitch a *worn-out woman*, etc., now fraternises a little with her, and Basilevitch, for want of an escort, willingly discusses bobbin lace and Valenciennes. I forgot: as I was coming home before dinner, I see Doria -- from behind, as is my custom. He was walking behind us, then passed us, before I had time to see his face.
