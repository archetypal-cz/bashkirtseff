# Saturday, 9 March 1878
Robert-Fleury did not come. Instead of waiting for callers I went to the atelier, and on my return found Mme de Fayet — whom I make fun of for all I am worth, and who reproached me for having put off Cassagnac: *Give him to us on a Saturday — for everyone!*

I try to turn her head with the love Cassagnac has for her. She amuses me. There was no one but her and M. d'Alt.
