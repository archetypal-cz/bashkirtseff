# Friday, 8 February 1878
I made a watercolour — a dreadful daub[^1] — showing Mme de Fayet, with her opulent figure, her yellow hair and her blue dress, singing *Vivre seule ici-bas*[^3], etc., as she stretches out her arms towards a cupid[^2] with Popaul's head, who is running away as fast as his legs can carry him, looking thoroughly terrified. In the sky one can make out two birds chasing each other.
[^1]: *Pochade*: a quick rough sketch or painting, typically done in a single sitting.
[^2]: In art, *un amour* = a winged cupid figure, cherub of love.
[^3]: Literally, *To live alone here below*; the romance Marie describes on 5 February.
