%% # Vendredi 15 février 1878 %%
%% Quels ravissants pieds il a, mais je ne puis m'empécher de rire en songeant à hier. %%
# Friday, 15 February 1878
What ravishing feet he has — but I cannot help laughing when I think of yesterday.

%% 078.0179 %%
%% Cette flanelle bleue et ce liseret rouge. %%
That blue flannel and that red piping.

%% 078.0180 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% [#Opera](../../_original/_glossary/places/theaters/OPERA.md) %%
%% [#Music_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MUSIC_THEME.md) %%
%% Je n'irai pas à l'Opéra demain. Cela rachètera hier. %%
I shall not go to the Opéra tomorrow. That will redeem yesterday.

%% 078.0181 %%
%% [#Love](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/LOVE.md) %%
%% Je comprends sa passion pour les pieds en regardant les siens. Vous savez qu'il est très grand et assez fort, c'est donc d'autant plus remarquable qu'il ait des pieds et des mains comme il les a. %%
I understand his passion for feet, looking at his own. You know he is very tall and rather heavily built, so it is all the more remarkable that he should have feet and hands like his.

%% 078.0182 %%
%% [#Cassagnac](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/CASSAGNAC.md) %%
%% [#Theater_theme](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/THEATER_THEME.md) %%
%% [#Politics](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/POLITICS.md) %%
%% Ma tante dit lorsque nous lui racontons la mise en scène d'hier, elle dit donc de Cassagnac que c'est une canaille dépravée. %%
When we tell my aunt how yesterday's scene was staged, she says — of Cassagnac, that is — that he is a depraved scoundrel.

%% 078.0183 %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%

I draw as usual, which does not prevent me from being very displeased with myself. I said as much to Robert-Fleury some time ago, and last Saturday, as he was correcting our life studies:

— Not at all.

%% 078.0191 %%

— Well, in your place I should be very pleased.

%% 078.0193 %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% C'était dit avec une gaieté très bienveillante et valait bien des compliments. %%
It was said with very kindly good humour, and was worth a good many compliments.

%% 078.0194 %%

Yes! But when shall I be able to... paint portraits... In a year... at least I hope so.
