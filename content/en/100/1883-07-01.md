# Sunday, 1 July 1883

So then... This morning I go to the Luxembourg museum and carry away only two impressions: Cazin's *Hagar and Ishmael* and Saint-Marceaux's *Genius Guarding the Secret of the Tomb*.

Villevieille during the day.
I am very puffed up with my own merits — I sing my own praises, adore myself, admire myself (only in front of Dina and Bojidar) and am happy.
You cannot imagine — it is an intoxication... I feel my own strength... I worked a little on the *Holy Women*... It will be beautiful, beautiful...
