# Monday, 23 July 1883
Wholly given over to decoration, I see great machines, avalanches of human bodies, façades of monuments with figures and decorative fancies.

%% 100.0229 %%
%% Des bandes de damnés et des chœurs d’anges. Qui a fait cela ? Personne. %%
%% 2026-09-28T21:01:28 RSR: Moved out of 100.0100, which ran several manuscript days together: this day's text from tome15.docx ¶4000, Mon Journal t.15 p.313 (2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Bands of the damned and choirs of angels. Who has done that? No one.

%% 100.0230 %%
%% Notre sculpture est pauvre et si tout a été fait en peinture il y a une belle place à prendre dans la taille des pierres. C’est inapplicable car ou voulez-vous le mettre ? Au point de vue pratique c’est fou, on n’a plus de monuments à décorer. Mais comme c’est beau ! Non, c’est vraiment beau. %%
%% 2026-09-28T21:01:28 RSR: Moved out of 100.0100, which ran several manuscript days together: this day's text from tome15.docx ¶4001, Mon Journal t.15 p.313 (2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Our sculpture is poor, and if everything has been done in painting, there is a fine place to be won in the carving of stone. It is impracticable, for where would you put it? From a practical point of view it is mad; there are no monuments left to decorate. But how beautiful it is! No, it is truly beautiful.
