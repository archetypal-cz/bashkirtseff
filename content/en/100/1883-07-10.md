# Tuesday, 10 July 1883
Modelling from eight till noon, painting from one till seven, and drawing in the evening. That is the life. For I must make a drawing for this M. de Bellina's publication. The article in the *Novoye Vremya* has been reprinted in the *South* (the Russian one). That will set Poltava talking, no doubt, since they give my age as nineteen; they need only put the figure right; but had I said twenty-four, they would say twenty-nine.
