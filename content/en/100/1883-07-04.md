# Wednesday, 4 July 1883
And it appears he does not know Bastien; the architect who came today does not know him. Jules is at Chantilly for eight hours; they are both just back from the seaside.
%% 2026-10-01T12:11:28 TR: «pour huit heures» rendered as written (eight hours), not silently corrected to «huit jours»; the Rollinat margin note formerly at the end of this entry now sits under 3 July (100.0169). %%
I went to the Consulate to have inheritance papers legalised.
I scraped out two heads in the picture to redo them entirely, and I feel better for having destroyed those proofs of incapacity...
Ah!... I am trying to find what wish was to go with that sigh. The most pressing would be to have finished this picture; then I should very much like...! a heap of things...
