Cards arrive in great numbers. Mme Gavini has sent a telegram from Agen; Odette came, and M. Eschmann, a Russian, and old Géry, who arrives with a face from the other world, in tears... I found it hard to strike the right note...

Si j’avais été vraiment affligée je ne le raconterais pas, car il y a là une pudeur qu’il me semblerait blesser; c’est égal, je suis trop calme... J’ai eu modèle ce matin, Edwige qui pose chez Suchetet et Suchetet apprenant hier qu’elle poserait chez moi ce matin m’envoie ses plus sincères compliments sur mon Salon. %%
%% 2026-09-28T21:01:28 RSR: Moved out of 100.0063, which ran several manuscript days together: this day's text from tome15.docx ¶3607, Mon Journal t.15 p.282 (2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
One ought to have wept... And I did not weep, even during the service at the church, which took place at five o'clock. No one was invited, and there were only Saint-Amand, Bojidar, the Englehardts and Tchernitsky. If I had been truly grief-stricken I should not be recounting it, for there is a modesty in that which it seems to me I would be wounding; all the same, I am too calm... I had a model this morning, Edwige, who poses at Suchetet's, and Suchetet, learning yesterday that she would be posing for me this morning, sends me his sincerest compliments on my Salon picture.

Among other cards we receive that of Jules Bastien-Lepage, one of the first, and a letter from the architect, who says: "Dear Mademoiselle, if I were not afraid of being opportune etc. etc. which strikes so violently your whole amiable family, whom I have the pleasure of knowing..."; then he begs me to believe in the sincerity etc., in his respect and his admiration. Perhaps one ought not to mock the form when the substance comes from the heart, but all the same it is almost a concierge's letter. Does the elder brother not write better?... I should like to wring a letter out of him.

%% 2026-09-28T21:01:28 RSR: Moved out of 100.0063, which ran several manuscript days together: this day's text from tome15.docx ¶3616, Mon Journal t.15 p.283 (2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
He made it a point of pride. "Ah! you grovel before her — well, you shall see!!" Here in Paris he was violent in the extreme; one day he very nearly threatened to beat me; my manner had struck him as insolent, and he cried: "You'll see what I'll do to you, you'll see, you'll remember it all your life." Maman took him away, afraid he would throw something at my head. And what had I done to deserve it? Nothing but say to him: "I beg you not to shout at me; I am not accustomed to being spoken to like this!" That calm manner of mine exasperated him. When I think now of that abominable tone he took to get the better of "my bravado", the blood rushes to my head.
