# Wednesday, 6 June 1883

My family will not understand that neither Étincelle, nor *Le Sport*, nor Saint-Amand, nor anyone can go on singing my praises; I do not go out, and besides, people mock those who constantly write about someone who has no established position in society. Were it a question of launching me as a little actress, it would be another matter, but as a young lady of society it is impossible to be in fashion without being reasonably well known in the salons.

And my family, who understand none of this, is furious because Mademoiselle Martinoff is mentioned everywhere.
