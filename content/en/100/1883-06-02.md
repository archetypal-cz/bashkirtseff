%% 2026-10-02T19:05:57 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 2 of 10 of 100.0016, which held 10 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.15; tome15.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
And in the morning we called at Julian's to find out whether he is coming this evening; it is a matter of a ticket for the circus. He tells us that Mme Cartwrigth wants to have her portrait painted, and since that means fame for an unknown, he is thinking of this man or that woman — none of whom is me. And when I ask him why, he says I have as many opportunities as I could wish and have no need of anyone looking after me...

I really do not know why I persist in regarding Julian as a friend. Or why I was so indignant with Tony, who is worth ten of him.

%% Alice a écrit à la petite Canrobert avant de m'écrire. Tout ça s'enchaîne. Je l'ai traitée jusqu'ici par le dédain mais ce n'est pas toujours compris. Cette petite pour qui j'ai été absolument parfaite... Est un joli échantillon de l'humanité. %%

Alice wrote to little Canrobert before writing to me. It all hangs together. Until now I have treated her with disdain, but that is not always understood. This little thing, to whom I have been absolutely perfect... is a fine specimen of humanity.

%% 100.0020 %%
%% J'ai honte de le dire mais j'ai été jusqu'à lui donner une robe. Que le monde est méprisable et comme l'Arlequin de Saint Marceaux a raison. Saint Marceaux lui-même assure que le monde est une vaste maison d'aliénés, qu'il faut autant que possible se rendre compte de la manie de ceux qu'on rencontre et s'ils sont dangereux se garer en circulant. %%
%% 2026-10-02T19:05:57 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 5 of 10 of 100.0016, which held 10 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.15; tome15.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
I am ashamed to say it, but I went so far as to give her a dress. How contemptible the world is, and how right Saint-Marceaux's *Arlequin* is! Saint-Marceaux himself maintains that the world is a vast madhouse, that one must as far as possible make out the mania of those one meets and, if they are dangerous, keep out of their way as one goes about.

%% 100.0021 %%
%% Ma tante et Dina sont au cirque, la veille du grand Prix. Moi je reste ici à composer une lettre à la petite Américaine et une à Tony. %%
%% 2026-10-02T19:05:57 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 6 of 10 of 100.0016, which held 10 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.15; tome15.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
My aunt and Dina are at the circus, on the eve of the Grand Prix. I stay here composing a letter to the little American and one to Tony.

%% 100.0022 %%
%% Les lettres causeront ma perte et c'est une manie. %%
%% 2026-10-02T19:05:57 RSR: Split (paragraph wave): print paragraph 7 of 10 of 100.0016, which held 10 printed paragraphs under one ID (Mon Journal t.15; tome15.docx; split points checked by the survey on the docx and the scan indentation). %%
Letters will be my ruin, and it is a mania.

%% 100.0023 %%
%% Dina assure que si l'on me laissait seule avec mon propre arrêt de mort qu'il me suffirait de signer pour être exécutée, je le signerai pour le plaisir d'écrire quelque chose. %%

Dina maintains that if I were left alone with my own death warrant, which I had only to sign to be executed, I would sign it for the pleasure of writing something.

%% 100.0024 %%

What a deplorable fortnight. I have quarrelled with everyone and everyone is abandoning me.
