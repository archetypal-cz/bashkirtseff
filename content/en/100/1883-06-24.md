# Sunday, 24 June 1883

We go to the École to see the *envois de Rome*, but since the halls were open I begin by wandering through them and revisiting the copies of masterpieces glimpsed in Italy. The splendid naturalism of Michelangelo the sculptor enchants me, and we have only a moment to see the paintings — in short I see only two: Schommer's canvas and Doucet's.
