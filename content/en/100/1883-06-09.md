# Saturday, 9 June 1883
%% Cassagnac a passé hier devant la maison le long du jardin, avec sa femme, il avait passé pareillement avant-hier, ma tante et Dina qui n’ont rien à faire l’ont vu avant-hier et me l’ont montré hier, j’ai vu son profil à travers la fente de la porte. %%
%% 2026-09-28T21:01:28 RSR: Moved out of 100.0053, which ran several manuscript days together: this day's text from tome15.docx ¶3555, Mon Journal t.15 p.279 (2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Cassagnac passed by the house yesterday, along the garden, with his wife; he had passed the same way the day before yesterday. My aunt and Dina, who have nothing to do, saw him the day before yesterday and pointed him out to me yesterday; I saw his profile through the crack of the door.

%% 100.0102 %%
%% Et ça ne me fait rien. %%
%% 2026-09-28T21:01:28 RSR: Moved out of 100.0053, which ran several manuscript days together: this day's text from tome15.docx ¶3556, Mon Journal t.15 p.279 (2026-09-28 rebuild). %%

%% 100.0103 %%
%% [#Women](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/WOMEN.md) %%
%% [#LOUISE_BRESLAU](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/LOUISE_BRESLAU.md) [#Maman](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/MAMAN.md) %%
%% Et aujourd’hui j’ai eu la visite de Mme Bertaux, présidente de l’association des femmes peintres et sculpteurs. Vous vous rappelez qu’au mois de mars j’ai été chez cette dame avec maman pour lui demander d’exposer à l’exposition des femmes... pensant que Breslau en était. %%
%% 2026-09-28T21:01:28 RSR: Moved out of 100.0053, which ran several manuscript days together: this day's text from tome15.docx ¶3557, Mon Journal t.15 p.279 (2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
And today I had a visit from Mme Bertaux, president of the association of women painters and sculptors. You will remember that in March I went to this lady's house with Maman to ask her leave to exhibit at the women's exhibition... thinking that Breslau belonged to it.

%% 100.0104 %%
%% Elle a répondu alors qu’il était trop tard, il ne restait que cinq jours et nous ne l’avons plus revue. %%
%% 2026-09-28T21:01:28 RSR: Moved out of 100.0053, which ran several manuscript days together: this day's text from tome15.docx ¶3558, Mon Journal t.15 p.279 (2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
She answered then that it was too late, there were only five days left, and we saw no more of her.

%% 100.0105 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% Et maintenant elle vient très aimable et reste une heure et me comble de compliments, elle n’a pas pensé, elle ne pouvait pas supposer que je puisse avoir tant de talent *déjà,* mon Salon l’enchante, c’est un grand succès etc. etc. etc. %%
%% 2026-09-28T21:01:28 RSR: Moved out of 100.0053, which ran several manuscript days together: this day's text from tome15.docx ¶3559, Mon Journal t.15 p.279 (2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
And now she comes, most amiable, stays an hour and showers me with compliments: she had not thought, she could not have supposed that I could have so much talent *already*; my Salon picture enchants her, it is a great success etc. etc. etc.

%% 100.0106 %%
%% [#Women](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/WOMEN.md) %%
%% Enfin les talents sont si rares, les femmes ont tant besoin de s’unir pour défendre leurs intérêts, un nom de femme de talent est un apport si considérable qu’elle vient me prier de permettre d’inscrire le mien parmi ses sociétaires. %%
%% 2026-09-28T21:01:28 RSR: Moved out of 100.0053, which ran several manuscript days together: this day's text from tome15.docx ¶3560, Mon Journal t.15 p.279 (2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
In short, talents are so rare, women have such need to unite in defence of their interests, the name of a woman of talent is so considerable a contribution, that she has come to beg me to allow her to enter mine among her members.

%% 100.0107 %%
%% Enfin c’est une amende honorable complète et j’accorde l’autorisation. %%
%% 2026-09-28T21:01:28 RSR: Moved out of 100.0053, which ran several manuscript days together: this day's text from tome15.docx ¶3561–3564, Mon Journal t.15 pp.279–280 (2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
In short, it is a complete ==public apology==[^f1], and I grant the permission.

[^f1]: In French in the original: *amende honorable*.

%% 100.0108 %%
%% Il faudrait et *il faut* que *tous* reviennent comme cela. %%
%% 2026-09-28T21:01:28 RSR: Moved out of 100.0053, which ran several manuscript days together: this day's text from tome15.docx ¶3565, Mon Journal t.15 p.280 (2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
They ought to — and they *must* — *all* come back like that.

My aunt, Dina, Bojidar, Engelhardt at the circus.

%% 100.0110 %%
%% [#Cassagnac](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/CASSAGNAC.md) %%
%% Si Cassagnac avait été seul je l’aurais arrosé avec la pompe du jardin... C’est ça qui aurait été drôle. Il ne me dit plus rien, ni personne. %%
%% 2026-09-28T21:01:28 RSR: Moved out of 100.0053, which ran several manuscript days together: this day's text from tome15.docx ¶3567, Mon Journal t.15 p.280 (2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
If Cassagnac had been alone I would have sprayed him with the garden pump... That would have been funny. He means nothing to me any more, nor does anyone.

%% Ça m’ennuie, il faut toujours avoir un être à qui on puisse penser avec plaisir, plus ou moins... Quelquefois des gens à peine entrevus,... Saint Marceaux, mais il ne me dit rien... Et Jules-Jules, je sens de l’inimitié et puis Mme Massalsky, Mme Mackay, tous ces infâmes potins... Et puis Jules... au fait, oui, on peut y penser... y penser seulement, et encore... %%
%% 2026-09-28T21:01:28 RSR: Moved out of 100.0053, which ran several manuscript days together: this day's text from tome15.docx ¶3568, Mon Journal t.15 p.280 (2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
It bores me; one must always have someone one can think of with pleasure, more or less... Sometimes people barely glimpsed... Saint-Marceaux, but he means nothing to me... And Jules — Jules, I feel hostility, and then Mme Massalsky, Mme Mackay, all that vile gossip... And then Jules... after all, yes, one can think of him... only think of him, and even then...

%% 100.0112 %%
%% Si... Non... Faute de mieux... %%
%% 2026-09-28T21:01:28 RSR: Moved out of 100.0053, which ran several manuscript days together: this day's text from tome15.docx ¶3569, Mon Journal t.15 p.280 (2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Yes... No... For want of anything better...

%% [#Emile_Bastien_Lepage](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/EMILE_BASTIEN_LEPAGE.md) [#Bastien_Lepage](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/BASTIEN_LEPAGE.md) %%
%% Je me suis bien imaginée que son frère, le faux Bastien, faisait un héritage de quarante milions et venait les mettre à mes pieds. Cela m’a servi à m’endormir un soir. Et maintenant plus rien depuis que le vrai Jules m’attriste car il m’attriste, on lui dit des horreurs et son frère aussi. %%
%% 2026-09-28T21:01:28 RSR: Moved out of 100.0053, which ran several manuscript days together: this day's text from tome15.docx ¶3570, Mon Journal t.15 p.280 (2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
I did imagine that his brother, the false Bastien, came into an inheritance of forty millions and laid them at my feet. That served to put me to sleep one evening. Every evening I make up stories for myself; sometimes the stories last several evenings. And now nothing, since the real Jules saddens me — for he does sadden me; people tell him horrible things, and his brother too.

%% 100.0114 %%
%% [#Society](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/SOCIETY.md) %%
