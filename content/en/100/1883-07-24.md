# Tuesday, 24 July 1883
Perhaps it is not beautiful. Perhaps it cannot be done. I have not seen anything like it at the Salon. Nor in the museums! Then? Is it insanity? It has been raining for several days, and so as not to waste time I am beginning a portrait of Dina.

%% 100.0232 %%
%% L’architecte vient vers six heures, il apporte le dessin de l’hôtel où il m’a proposé de faire quelque petite sculpture de décoration. J’accepterais si c’était plus important. Je continue à ne faire aucune plaisanterie sur Jules, je fais de grands éloges d’autres artistes et je dis même sans affectation que je sens entre Jules et moi de *l’inaffection,* naturellement Emile proteste mais je dis c’est égal, il m’est désagréable. %%
%% 2026-09-28T21:01:28 RSR: Moved out of 100.0100, which ran several manuscript days together: this day's text from tome15.docx ¶4007, Mon Journal t.15 p.314 (2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
The architect comes at about six; he brings the drawing of the town house where he has suggested I do some small piece of decorative sculpture. I would accept if it were more important. I go on making no jokes about Jules; I praise other artists highly and even say, without affectation, that I feel a *want of affection* between Jules and me; naturally Émile protests, but I say: all the same, I find him disagreeable.

%% 100.0233 %%
%% La comtesse Malewska est venue et aussi la vicomtesse de Janzé, Gavini, Linsingen. %%
%% 2026-09-28T21:01:28 RSR: Moved out of 100.0100, which ran several manuscript days together: this day's text from tome15.docx ¶4008, Mon Journal t.15 p.314 (2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Countess Malewska came, and also the Vicomtesse de Janzé, Gavini, Linsingen.
