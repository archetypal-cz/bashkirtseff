%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%

I adore him! Ah! I am mad about him! Ah! I admire him!

Ah! men are fortunate. Ah! to be celebrated, illustrious, great!!
