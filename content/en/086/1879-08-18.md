%% 2026-10-02T15:32:51 RSR: OCR spacing «1 5 août» corrected to «15 août» (Mon Journal t.13 p.78 scan). %%
Besides the princes' flowers, yesterday we received two superb bouquets; Blanc had intended to send me flowers for the 15th of August but I do not think it was he since Labrousse charges at least 60 to 75 francs apiece for bouquets like those. We went over everyone likely to indulge in such an extravagance and can think of no one. Young Zurlo (son of Prince Zurlo who is said to be ==a bringer of ill luck==[^iettatore]) arrived here and introduced us this evening to the gentleman with the boots, the Brazilian, who was very eager to meet us. [M. da Costa Motta.]
