# Monday, 27 October 1879

%% 086.0440 %%
%% [#JULIAN](../../_original/_glossary/places/schools/JULIAN.md) %%

%% 2026-02-02T09:03:14 LAN: "vilain modèle" - unattractive model, common complaint at ateliers %%
%% 2026-02-02T09:03:15 LAN: "mal posé" - art term: poorly positioned/posed for drawing %%
%% Concours. Et comme d'habitude dans les concours un vilain modèle et mal posé. %%
Competition. And, as usual at competitions, an ugly model, and badly posed.

%% 086.0441 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% 2026-02-02T09:03:16 LAN: "chiffonnières" - insult: rag-pickers, contemptuous term for fellow female students %%
%% 2026-02-02T09:03:17 LAN: "Les sales femmes !" - exclamation: "The dirty/awful women!" - Marie's frustration %%
%% 2026-02-02T09:03:18 LAN: "Peut-on être aussi peu artiste" - rhetorical: Can one be so un-artistic %%
%% J'attendais qu'il en vint un autre plus beau et ne me suis pas occupée comment ces chiffonnières l'avaient placé. Les sales femmes ! Peut-on être aussi peu artiste, aussi bête, aussi idiot ! Enfin ! Il faut que je loue cet atelier. %%
I was waiting for a better-looking one to come and did not bother about how these rag-pickers had posed him. Horrid women! Can anyone be so unartistic, so stupid, so idiotic! Well! I must rent that studio.
