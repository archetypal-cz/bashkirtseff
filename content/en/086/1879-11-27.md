%% Une suite de déceptions. Ouverture de la Chambre à Paris et je n'ai pas eu de billets puis le soir au Français, par indisposition de Mlle Samary on ne donne pas "l'Etincelle" que je voulais voir. Cela se passe sous la Terreur, en Vendée. %% 2026-09-29T01:01:34 RSR: Restored from tome13.docx ¶1724, Mon Journal t.13 p.127 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
A series of disappointments. The opening of the Chamber in Paris, and I did not get tickets; then in the evening at the Comédie-Française, owing to Mlle Samary's indisposition, they do not give *L'Étincelle*[^1], which I wanted to see. We have a pretty box and are entirely en famille, with Alexis and Bojidar as escorts. It is a minor first night: there is a new one-act drama. It takes place during the Terror, in the Vendée. There were passages that called for underlining, and we exchange glances with Mme Adam, whom of course I do not know, but I am sure she half reads me, and so we looked at each other several times, smiling.

Bojidar goes to the atelier every day; he wants to do as I do — no... I forget to say that they introduced to us Eugène Richtenberger, secretary to the Prefect of Police, a friend of Joseph's and the son of Mme Richtenberger, a sort of Jewish political woman.
