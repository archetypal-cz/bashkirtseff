# Tuesday, 23 September 1879
%% Doenhoff est ici, il est déjà venu plusieurs fois et ce soir il a dîné avec nous. Il trouve que j'ai maigri de figure et que cela me va à ravir. %% 2026-09-29T01:01:34 RSR: Restored from tome13.docx ¶1379–1382, Mon Journal t.13 pp.100–101 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Doenhoff is here; he has already come several times, and this evening he dined with us. He finds that my face has grown thinner and that it suits me delightfully. [words blacked out: If it did not mean living in Prussia I would marry] this good man. He finds me very attractive, and the position he holds is absolutely brilliant. He is a very passable man, and in his manners he is a courtier, a German. And what a contrast with Multedo, who came in the evening.

%% 086.0311 %%

%% 2026-09-29T01:01:34 RSR: Restored from tome13.docx ¶1383, Mon Journal t.13 p.101 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
I was in high spirits, and Doenhoff told Maman that I have too much wit to have a heart. Multedo is either epigrammatic or adoring, whereas the German allows himself only one or two compliments and not too much chatter with young girls. But I like that, because he means what he says...
