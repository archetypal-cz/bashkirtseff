Here are some newspapers, and I have just read the two hundred pages that make up the first issue of Mme Adam's review — Juliette Lamber's. All this has unsettled me, and I left the atelier at four o'clock to go for a drive in the Bois in a new hat in which I cause a sensation, but just now I do not care. How fortunate I think her, Mme Adam.

%% 2026-03-09T13:30:00 TR: "j'aime toujours les violettes" — violets were the Bonapartist symbol (Napoleon's flower); Marie now loves them only as flowers %%
I believe that you, at least, know me well enough to understand the effect all these burning questions have on my poor head. There is nothing to be done about old loyalties... I still love violets, but only as flowers. My love for Paul de Cassagnac vanished along with my respect, and if I still have some weakness for him as a memory... it is for his head, his speech and the ready-made chivalry he put at the disposal of his customers... Banalities that people take as gospel. I do not disown my knight; I explain him to myself. I am less than nothing, I do not exist for others, but all this exists for me, alive, alluring and enough to make one despair. I weep and I laugh at myself. I am going over to the Republic, to all the new men and the new ideas.

Today here I am gripped by the *Revue nouvelle*; who knows whether at some given moment I might not go and enthuse over Prince Napoleon, whom for that matter I prefer to Napoleon III and who really is somebody. No, you understand perfectly well that I am joking at my own expense and that I am as *advanced* as it is possible to be.
