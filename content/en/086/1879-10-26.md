I went to look at a studio to let opposite our house, and then I worked. Multedo came and I was rude to him; then Maman, my aunt, Dina and I go for a drive in the Bois.

C'est un très joli garçon brun, conducteur de cotillons, bon danseur, excellente famille; très gentil, très lancé. Enfin c'est un des assez bons produits de ce qui se fait dans ce genre. %%
%% 2026-03-09T14:45:00 TR: "conducteur de cotillons" — cotillion leader, a prestigious social role at balls %%
%% 2026-03-09T14:45:00 TR: "très lancé" — well-launched in society, fashionable and well-connected %%
Berthe having invited herself yesterday to dine today, we invited whoever happened to be there, MM. de Tarente and Turquan. Then Berthe's husband, Bojidar, Alexis and the old Prince. So we look as though we are enjoying ourselves, and I do believe everyone did enjoy themselves, especially the excellent Turquan. Have I told you what the Marquis de Tarente is like? He is a very handsome dark young man, a leader of cotillions, a good dancer, of excellent family; very nice, very much in the swim. In short, he is one of the fairly good specimens of what is turned out in that line.
