%% 2026-03-09T12:35:00 TR: "déportés" — the Communards exiled to New Caledonia after the 1871 Paris Commune, returning under the 1879 amnesty %%
The arrival of the deportees displaying red caps and sashes is an ugly thing. One should never have brought these people back. They had grown accustomed over there and will be strangers here, God knows what complications may arise from these returns of husbands and wives after ten years of absence.

%% 086.0209 %%
