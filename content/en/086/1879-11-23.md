%% 2026-09-29T01:01:34 RSR: Restored from tome13.docx ¶1700, Mon Journal t.13 p.125 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
I am in a white dress — Empire, Directoire, Récamier, whatever you like. But I am very pretty: blood-red lips and an admirable complexion. I am never like this when it would be worth the trouble... We withdraw to my room, Dina, de Daillens and I, to *make fun of Alexis's chastity*; he is dumbfounded and flattered. It is all the same to me; there are days when I must amuse myself. It was mad but funny.
