# Friday, 31 October 1879

%% 086.0458 %%
%% [#Opera](../../_original/_glossary/places/theaters/OPERA.md) [#Berthe](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/BERTHE.md) [#Dina](../../_original/_glossary/people/core/DINA.md) [#Johnstone](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/JOHNSTONE.md) [#Gavini](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/GAVINI.md) [#Multedo](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/MULTEDO.md) [#Gery](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/GERY.md) [#Marquis_de_Casa_Riera](../../_original/_glossary/people/aristocracy/MARQUIS_DE_CASA_RIERA.md) %%

%% [#Nervo](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/NERVO.md) [#Gabriel_Gery](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/GABRIEL_GERY.md) %%
%% Nous revenons de l'Opéra, je suis de bonne humeur, nous nous sommes amusés, nous étions très chic. Mme Gavini, Berthe, Dina, moi, Johnstone, Gavini, baron de Nervo, Multedo, Géry père et fils, le marquis de Casa-Riera et son secrétaire. %%
We have just come back from the Opera; I am in good spirits, we enjoyed ourselves, we looked very smart. Mme Gavini, Berthe, Dina, myself, Johnstone, Gavini, Baron de Nervo, Multedo, Géry father and son, the Marquis de Casa-Riera and his secretary.

%% 086.0459 %%
%% [#Marquis_de_Casa_Riera](../../_original/_glossary/people/aristocracy/MARQUIS_DE_CASA_RIERA.md) %%
%% Le marquis est aveugle mais voilà la seconde fois que je lui parle, il reconnaît ma voix, je me dévoue et lui tiens compagnie pendant vingt minutes. Nous n'avons pas entendu de musique, on a causé tout le temps et comme on était très entrain il n'y a rien de spécial à raconter. %%
The Marquis is blind, but this is the second time I have spoken to him and he recognises my voice; I sacrifice myself and keep him company for twenty minutes. We heard no music at all; we talked the whole time, and as everyone was in high spirits there is nothing special to tell.

%% 086.0460 %%
%% [#Gabriel_Gery](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/GABRIEL_GERY.md) [#Arnaud_de_l_Ariege](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/ARNAUD_DE_L_ARIEGE.md) %%
%% Le fils Géry est un charmant garçon, attaché à Bruxelles, pas bête du tout et sympathique, grand brun dans le genre Joseph qu'il connaît beaucoup. %%
%% 2026-03-09T15:00:00 TR: "dans le genre Joseph" — in the manner/style of Joseph; similar type of young man %%
Young Géry is a charming fellow, an attaché in Brussels, not at all stupid, and likeable; tall and dark, of the Joseph type — and he knows Joseph well.

%% Nous avons fait un effet bœuf, je dis nous parce que Berthe n'est arrivée qu'après. Mme Gavini ne se sentait pas de joie de voir qu'on me lorgnait. Moi, je suis arrivée la tête haute comme si j'étais la reine de Paris, cet air-là avec une pointe d'enfantillage m'embellit beaucoup. %%
%% 2026-03-09T15:05:00 TR: "un effet boeuf" — 1870s slang: a tremendous effect, great impression (literally: ox effect) %%
We made a tremendous impression — I say we because Berthe arrived only later. Mme Gavini was beside herself with joy to see the opera glasses turned on me. As for me, I came in with my head held high as if I were the queen of Paris; that air, with a touch of childishness, makes me far prettier.

%% 2026-03-09T15:05:00 TR: "rosse" — nag, shrew; very harsh insult for his wife %%
%% 2026-03-09T15:05:00 TR: "le Petit Prince" — nickname for her suitor Bojidar Karageorgevitch, Serbian prince %%
%% 2026-03-09T15:05:00 TR: "Jérôme" — Prince Napoleon Jerome Bonaparte, Napoleonic pretender %%
Cassagnac is handsome and good, and he has a shrew for a wife. Do not be shocked! She is a real shrew. Well... I am almost glad it is so; what would have become of me without the Little Prince? No, actually — I would push him towards Jérôme and it could all have been arranged beautifully; his wife is so stupid! Blanc said so again this evening.
