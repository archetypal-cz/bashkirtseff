%% 2026-03-09T13:00:00 TR: "la Muette" — La Muette de Portici, opera by Auber (1828) about the Neapolitan revolution %%
%% 2026-03-09T13:00:00 TR: "mes avantages" — my assets (euphemism for breasts) %%
This evening Hecht gave us a stage box at the Opera. The first night of *La Muette*, and a very elegant house for September. I am dressed with a *stunning* simplicity; I go in for simplicity and close-fitting lines, especially the bodice, because my assets are exquisitely shaped and very dainty... I am talking nonsense... it is half past two in the morning... Hecht had sent his stall seat to Joseph and Joseph did not come to the theatre. It was as if there were no one there, because I had been thinking of this little fool while dressing. Hecht wants to give us a dinner at his house with radicals. But Gambetta is not in Paris.

%% Ma tante et maman sont couchées depuis une heure, je voudrais rester toute la nuit à fumer et à dire des bêtises car vous savez que j'ai une langue de tous les diables... il paraît que c'est le cas de toutes les femmes honnêtes... Joseph... ce nom me revient à toute minute... J'ai envie de devenir amoureuse... à condition qu'on soit amoureux de moi. J'ai envie d'une petite machine vulgaire, un homme très jeune et bébête. J'ai rêvé de grandes passions mais je n'en veux plus... ce soir. Ce serait drôle avec Joseph. Il voulait épouser une cocotte et sa mère la magnifique Mme Arnaud a eu toutes les peines du monde pour empêcher cette douce union. C'est un enfant du reste, de vingt et un à vingt-deux ans, pas même... pauvre diable... je n'ai bu que de la limonade et je suis très gaie... Dites-donc, il n'y a avec qui causer... Vous très adorés lecteurs mais un espace de cinquante années nous sépare. Ce journal c'est ma vie. Ce sera drôle ce dîner chez Hecht... très cocasse. Si au moins il y avait A...rnaud. %% 2026-03-09T13:05:00 TR: "cocotte" — courtesan/demimondaine %%
%% 2026-03-09T13:05:00 TR: "scie" — running joke (literally "saw"); a repetitive humorous phrase %%
My aunt and Maman have been in bed for an hour, I would like to stay up all night smoking and talking nonsense for you know that I have a devil of a tongue... it seems that is the case with all virtuous women... Joseph... this name comes back to me every minute... I want to fall in love... provided someone is in love with me. I want some vulgar little affair, a very young and silly man. I have dreamed of grand passions but I no longer want them... this evening. It would be fun with Joseph. He wanted to marry a courtesan and his mother the magnificent Mme Arnaud had all the trouble in the world preventing this sweet union. He is a child besides, of twenty-one to twenty-two, not even... poor devil... I have drunk only lemonade and I am in very high spirits... I say, there is no one to chat with... You, most adored readers, but fifty years lie between us. This journal is my life. It will be funny, this dinner at Hecht's... very comical. If only there were A...rnaud. Do you remember our running joke at Nice with Bihovetz, called General Bibi, and my graces?
