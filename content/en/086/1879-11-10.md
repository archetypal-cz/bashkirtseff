# Monday, 10 November 1879

%% 086.0505 %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%

I went to church yesterday; I go from time to time so that people will not say I am a nihilist.

It is a punishment for me to see all these Russians and to be so alone. I often say, as a joke, that life is only a passage. I should like to believe it in earnest, to console myself for all these miseries, all these brutal sorrows, these ignoble slights.

And the whole world is so *gossipy*; it is by feeling this disgust and this astonishment at the gossip of every day that I discover myself to be untouched by all this nauseating pettiness.

Perhaps Amélie or her friend the maid [has] put [her] paws on it... that seems impossible and monstrous to you, and to me too... But the world is so vile... How curious to be shocked by all this — am I honest, then?

I am beginning to believe it.

%% [#Amelie](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/AMELIE.md) [#Madeleine_Delsarte](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/MADELEINE_DELSARTE.md) [#Wiik](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/WIIK.md) %%
%% Ce dessin qui n'est même pas d'ensemble, mais c'est plus raisonnable que ce qu'elle fait toujours alors on a profité de l'occasion. C'est tout de même dégoûtant. Moi qui croyais aux concours. Comment diable m'ont-ils donnée la médaille avec Amélie qui leur monte la tête contre moi ?! %%
%% 2026-03-09T15:40:00 TR: "dessin d'ensemble" — compositional drawing; complete figure study vs partial %%

That drawing, which is not even a whole figure — but it is more sensible than what she usually does, so they seized the opportunity. It is disgusting all the same. And I believed in competitions! How the devil did they ever give me the medal, with Amélie setting them all against me?!

%% 086.0512 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%

J'ai toujours peur qu'on ne prenne mes réflexions pour de la jalousie, non, ça n'en est pas vous le savez, seulement toutes les injustices me révoltent; je ne dis rien aux médailles de Madeleine Delsarte ou de Wiik, mais celle d'Amélie est une mauvaise farce. %%
%% 2026-03-09T15:40:00 TR: "sous un faux nom" — under a false name; to test work without studio politics %%
%% 2026-03-09T15:40:00 TR: "soufflet" — slap in the face; metaphor for public rebuke/vindication %%
%% 2026-10-01T07:53:46 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
I shall take my drawing, repair what has been rubbed out and send it to the Salon under a false name; if it is accepted, it will be a fine slap in the face for the atelier's verdicts. I am always afraid my remarks will be taken for jealousy — no, it is not that, you know; only, every injustice revolts me. I say nothing about Madeleine Delsarte's or Wiik's medals, but Amélie's is a bad joke.

%% 086.0513 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%

Oh well.
