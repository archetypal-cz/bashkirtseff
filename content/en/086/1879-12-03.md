# Wednesday, 3 December 1879

I forgot to keep the newspaper with Gambetta's remark. M. de Baudry d'Asson, the son of giants, the Vendéen, the mighty hunter before the Lord, mounts the tribune and begins: "In the present circumstances, and not wishing to play into the hands of a ministry at bay... Monsieur le Président." M. Baudry d'Asson, I urge you to adopt a less cynegetic style.

This caused a merriment that lasted nearly five minutes; the remark could not have come more aptly. It pleased me all the more because Gambetta is reproached with lacking wit.

%% 2026-03-09T16:10:00 TR: "diner d'artistes" — artists' dinner; social gathering of artistic circle %%
This evening, an artists' dinner: Robert-Fleury, Julian, Bojidar, Alexis, Berthe and Tchoumakoff. Maman finds Tony very handsome. It is true, though; I thought him short — he is tall, and he is forty-one and does not look more than thirty-two to thirty-four.

%% Ils sont très drôles avec Julian. Je m'amuse beaucoup. Julian magnétise Dina et Berthe, nous assistons à une séance pleine d'intérêt. On ne se sépare qu'à une heure et demie et Alexis me dit que Joseph est un beau mâle. Cela aussi. Je l'ai devant les yeux depuis lundi. %% 2026-03-09T16:10:00 TR: "magnetise" — mesmerizes/hypnotizes; refers to popular 19th-century practice of animal magnetism %%
%% 2026-03-09T16:10:00 TR: "un beau male" — a handsome male; deliberately naturalistic/animalistic term influenced by Zola %%
They are very funny with Julian. I am greatly amused. Julian mesmerises Dina and Berthe; we attend a most interesting séance. We do not part until half past one, and Alexis tells me that Joseph is a handsome male. That too. I have had him before my eyes since Monday. Yes, he is a handsome male; perhaps that is why he troubled me. One can see that I am reading Zola.
