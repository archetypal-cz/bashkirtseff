# Wednesday, 17 September 1879
%% Cassagnac est l'amant de la reine Isabelle... C'est un moyen pour lui d'en sortir sans cela le "Pays" claquerait et les ressources sont minces. Quel sale moral il a ce monsieur ! Je ne devrais pas croire à tant d'infamie [Mots noircis: et je n'y] croirais pas si on me le disait mais j'y crois parce que je l'ai deviné moi-même. Oui, j'ai supposé cela en m'appuyant sur la réputation du chevalier. Mme Paul est à Vichy (foie et estomac) et M. Paul au château de la Reine. Si je juge d'après moi je n'y vois pas de mal car je ne peux jamais, *moi,* supposer une saleté; ce n'est qu'en me mettant au point de vue des autres que je comprends et soupçonne. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:34 RSR: Restored from tome13.docx ¶1350, Mon Journal t.13 p.98 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Cassagnac is Queen Isabella's lover... It is a way out for him; otherwise *Le Pays* would go under, and resources are slender. What filthy morals this gentleman has! I ought not to believe in such infamy [words blacked out: and I would not] believe it if I were told it, but I believe it because I guessed it myself. Yes, I supposed it, going by the knight's reputation. Mme Paul is at Vichy (liver and stomach) and M. Paul at the Queen's château. If I judge by myself I see no harm in it, for *I* can never suppose anything dirty; it is only by taking other people's point of view that I understand and suspect.

%% 086.0292 %%
%% L'extérieur est adorable, la mise en scène [Mot noirci: superbe] mais l'intérieur est un vomitif. Je ne demande pas mieux que de convenir que mes sentiments intimes me trompent, mais je crois bien que j'ai raison et je ne me suis imaginé tout cela que hier en retournant de théâtre. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:34 RSR: Restored from tome13.docx ¶1351, Mon Journal t.13 p.98 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
The exterior is adorable, the staging [word blacked out: superb], but the interior is an emetic. I should like nothing better than to admit that my inner feelings deceive me, but I do believe I am right, and I only imagined all this yesterday on the way back from the theatre.

%% Voilà un article du "Gaulois" qui va ébranler notre bonapartisme encore plus... je parle de ma famille qui crie encore que tous les républicains sont des paysans. Du reste les dames ne peuvent pas discuter et surtout grâce à l'éducation qu'on ne leur donne pas elles sont d'une ignorance crasse, pas seulement ma famille, mais encore les 9/10 des dames en général. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:34 RSR: Restored from tome13.docx ¶1352, Mon Journal t.13 p.98 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Here is an article in *Le Gaulois* that will shake our Bonapartism even more... I mean my family, who still cry that all republicans are peasants. When you say Republic! to them they answer: dirty linen, black fingernails, eating without a tablecloth, blowing one's nose with one's fingers! Besides, ladies cannot argue, and above all, thanks to the education they are not given, they are crassly ignorant — not only my family, but nine-tenths of ladies in general.

Today, a Wednesday, a favourable day, and the 17th, a rather good date, I begin preparing to sculpt. I went to make enquiries about ateliers. Tony came to the atelier yesterday; there was not much to correct, so he gave me some of his good advice, urging me to work on the painterly side, which I have lacked so far despite my qualities of construction, drawing, character, likeness, etc.

%% 2026-09-29T01:01:34 RSR: Restored from tome13.docx ¶1354, Mon Journal t.13 p.98 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Oh yes! And so I am going to sculpt by gaslight instead of drawing. You understand, I am not giving up colour, since I paint as long as it is light, and as soon as the daylight is gone I sculpt: is that agreed? Yes, good.

As for Breslau, he says she has not made *enough* progress, and Julian adds that her talent is mere diligence. Anna is very gifted but does no work and has mad ideas. And me: extremely gifted and at the same time a diligent, rigorous worker, astonishingly rapid progress, very good drawing — in a word, "a chorus of praise". So it is true, since they say it to outsiders. That was four months ago, and here I was believing I had been going downhill for eight or ten months... Well, it gives me courage; I shall work better and even more.
