In the evening, a performance for the benefit of Alsatians burnt out of their homes. A heap of things, among others an act of *Mary Stuart* played by the great, the admirable, the incomparable Ristori. She is given an ovation. I am delightfully dressed, so of course there is nobody amusing in the house.
