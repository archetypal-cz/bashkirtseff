%% 2026-03-09T15:15:00 TR: "apologie" — defense/justification, NOT apology in modern English sense %%
%% 2026-03-09T15:15:00 TR: "l'autre" — refers to Mirabeau, the subject of the play, compared to Cassagnac %%
I was not in the mood but I brought back from this evening a defense of Cassagnac and a greater love of liberty. Mme Gavini says she would have liked to live before the Revolution, in the time of privileges, at court... myself, if I had been born before the Revolution I would have made it, to the extent of my powers. Yes — but Cassagnac does not have the other's talent, he does not have the other's role either, he does not even have a cause to defend... so he is not excusable... But what the devil is he reproached for? What has he done? Improprieties... which I would not even concern myself with, were they of the utmost gravity, if the hero were greater.
