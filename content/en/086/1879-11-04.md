%% 2026-03-09T15:15:00 TR: "apologie" — defense/justification, NOT apology in modern English sense %%
%% 2026-03-09T15:15:00 TR: "l'autre" — refers to Mirabeau, the subject of the play, compared to Cassagnac %%
I was not in high spirits, but I brought back from this evening a vindication of Cassagnac and a greater love of liberty. Mme Gavini says she would have liked to live before the Revolution, in the days of privilege, at court... as for me, had I been born before the Revolution I would have made it, as far as my strength allowed. Yes — but Cassagnac has neither the other's talent nor the other's role; he has not even a cause to defend... so he is not excusable... But what the devil do they reproach him with? What has he done? Indelicacies... which I would not even trouble about, however grave they were, if the hero were greater.
