# Wednesday, 10 December 1879
%% Nous sommes allées voir le Père Didon au couvent des Dominicains. Ai-je besoin de vous dire que le Père Didon est le prédicateur dont la gloire grandit à vue d'œil depuis deux ans et dont en ce moment tout Paris s'occupe. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:34 RSR: Restored from tome13.docx ¶1790, Mon Journal t.13 p.133 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
We went to see Father Didon at the Dominican monastery. Need I tell you that Father Didon is the preacher whose fame has been growing before our eyes these two years and whom all Paris is talking about at the moment.

J'avais déjà vu son portrait hier et je savais qu'il a des yeux splendides, (beauté qui manque à Joseph). %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:34 RSR: Restored from tome13.docx ¶1791, Mon Journal t.13 p.133 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
He had been told to expect us; as soon as we arrive someone goes to call him, and we wait for him in one of those reception cubicles, a kind of stall or cell, all glassed in, with a table, three chairs and a good little stove. I had already seen his portrait yesterday and knew that he has splendid eyes (a beauty Joseph lacks).

He comes in, very amiable, very much a man of the world, very handsome in his fine white woollen habit, which reminds me of the gowns I used to wear. Without [Crossed out: the infamy of] the tonsure it would be a head rather like Cassagnac's, but more enlightened, the eyes franker, the bearing more natural though very lofty; a face that is beginning to thicken, with the same something disagreeably crooked about the mouth as Cassagnac's.

%% 086.0638 %%
%% [#Appearance](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/APPEARANCE.md) %%
%% Mais une grande distinction, pas de charme outré de créole, un teint mat, un beau front, la tête haute, des mains adorablement blanches et belles, un air gai et même autant que possible bon garçon. On voudrait lui voir une moustache. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:34 RSR: Restored from tome13.docx ¶1793, Mon Journal t.13 p.133 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
But great distinction, none of the overdone Creole charm; a matt complexion, a fine brow, the head held high, adorably white and beautiful hands, a cheerful air and even, as far as possible, a good-natured one. One would like to see him with a moustache.

%% Beaucoup d'esprit malgré un grand aplomb. On voit tellement qu'il mesure toute l'étendue de sa vogue, qu'il est habitué aux adorations et qu'il est sincèrement heureux du bruit qui se fait autour de lui. La mère Mouzay l'a naturellement prévenu par lettre de la merveille qu'il allait voir et nous lui parlons de faire son portrait. Il n'a pas refusé tout en disant que ce serait difficile, presque impossible, une jeune fille faisant le portrait du Père Didon, il est si en vu , on s'en occupe tant... %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:34 RSR: Restored from tome13.docx ¶1794, Mon Journal t.13 p.133 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
A great deal of wit, despite great self-assurance. One sees so clearly that he gauges the full extent of his vogue, that he is used to adoration and that he is sincerely pleased by the stir being made around him. Old Mme Mouzay has naturally forewarned him by letter of the marvel he was about to see, and we speak to him of painting his portrait. He did not refuse, though he said it would be difficult, almost impossible — a young girl painting the portrait of Father Didon; he is so much in the public eye, people talk about him so much...
