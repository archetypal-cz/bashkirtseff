# Sunday, 20 May 1883

%% 099.0502 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% 2026-03-10T10:00:00 TR: "dans la nuit de jeudi à vendredi" — during the night from Thursday to Friday. "dépêche" — telegram. "déplorable" — wretched, in a deplorable state (medical). "valet de chambre" — manservant, personal valet. "état désespéré" — hopeless condition (dying). %%
%% Maman est arrivée dans la nuit de jeudi à vendredi, nous avons une dépêche samedi ou elle dit que la santé de mon père est déplorable. Aujourd'hui son valet de chambre écrit que son état est désespéré. %%
Maman arrived during the night from Thursday to Friday; we received a telegram on Saturday in which she says my father's health is wretched. Today his valet writes that his condition is hopeless.

%% Je ne m'affligerai que tout juste ce qu'il faudra. On désarme devant la mort; Paix et recueillement. On dit qu'il souffre beaucoup. Je suis contente que maman soit arrivée à temps. %%
I shall grieve only as much as will be necessary. One lays down one's arms before death: peace and contemplation. They say he is suffering greatly. I am glad Maman arrived in time.

I admire myself: the little Swedish woman married to Chadwick asks me for my impressions of the Salon. So I write to her, and I am obliged to speak of her picture, which shows the everlasting fishermen at the seaside. The colour is pleasant, but the subject repels me. I cannot tell her so. [Blacked out: I am quite obliged to say that her picture] is better than last year's, which is true. That the background is of a truly charming colour — that is true too, and I add: *"and the rest as well, I am quite sincere."* And since I do not think so, I am troubled as I write it and turn red, red as a guilty woman. Will this so very absolute probity last?
