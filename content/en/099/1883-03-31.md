Ce Miranda attaché à l'ambassade d'Espagne il y a trois ans, a eu des revers, des misères, je ne sais quoi il y a eu un moment où il avait sombré tout à fait, et il n'est pas encore renfloué, seulement maman croit bien de se montrer gentille envers des gens attaqués. The Engelhardts, Tchernitsky — ==commonly known as==[^3] Cerny — to dinner, then Mademoiselle de Miranda with her father. This Miranda, attached to the Spanish embassy three years ago, has had reverses, hardships, something or other — there was a moment when he had gone completely under, and he is not yet back on his feet, only Maman thinks it proper to show kindness to people under attack. ==Favour for favour==[^1], Lord.
%% 2026-10-03T18:12:00 TR: Reading-conventions wave (KRR 2026-10-03): Latin "vulgo", the tic "A charge de revanche" and Bouguereau's "pas mal, pas mal" translated in the text, originals to the footnotes. %%

[^1]: In French in the original: *A charge de revanche*. The sense: hoping that the kindness will be returned in kind — a polite formula carrying the suggestion of a debt.
[^3]: In Latin in the original: *vulgo*.

%% Mais j'ai été chez Julian ce matin, me faire répéter les belles choses. Il paraît que Bouguereau lui a dit: vous avez une Russe qui a envoyé quelque chose qui n'est pas mal, pas mal[^2]. But I went to Julian's this morning to have the good things repeated to me. It seems Bouguereau said to him: you have a Russian who sent something that is ==not bad, not bad==[^2]. And you know, Julian added, from Bouguereau's mouth that is enormous, when it is not a question of his own students. In short, it seems I shall have something like a mention.

[^2]: In French in the original: *pas mal, pas mal*. High praise from the notoriously sparing Bouguereau.
