Maman left this evening, called away by a telegram from Paul. My father is at death's door. The Engelhardts, the Princess, and Dusautoy to dinner. I am tired from working… And then… My aunt is delighted — she thinks this will spare her the trip. She is wrong. Julian is very fond of Bastien — even physically…
