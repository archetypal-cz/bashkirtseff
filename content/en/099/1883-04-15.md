# Sunday, 15 April 1883

%% 099.0128 %%

%% 2026-03-10T10:00:00 TR: "prostration" — 1880s medical term for extreme physical/mental exhaustion. "insensible" — numb, unfeeling (false cognate). "[Mots noircis: me plonge dans une]" — words blacked out, partially legible. %%

My illness [words blacked out: plunges me into a] prostration that leaves me numb.

%% 099.0129 %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% 2026-03-10T10:00:00 TR: "accroché" — hung (on exhibition wall). "cimaise" — the picture rail, coveted eye-level hanging position at the Salon. "récompense" — award or prize at the Salon. "englobant peinture et pastel" — encompassing both painting and pastel; kept as a phrase. %%
%% Julian écrit que le tableau n'est pas encore accroché. Que Tony ne peut pas promettre la cimaise mais comme je ne suis pas encore accrochée... ce qui pourra être fait sera fait. Que Tony espère fortement une petite récompense *englobant peinture* et pastel. %%
Julian writes that the painting is not yet hung. Tony cannot promise the *cimaise*,[^1] but since I am not yet hung… what can be done will be done. Tony has strong hopes of a small award covering painting *and* pastel.

[^1]: *Cimaise*: the picture rail at the Salon — the most coveted hanging position, at eye level.

%% 099.0130 %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% 2026-03-10T10:00:00 TR: "mention" — honourable mention, the lowest Salon award but still prestigious. "évanouir" — to faint. The emotional contrast (expected ecstasy vs. actual numbness) is the point. %%
%% Je n'espérais rien de semblable il y a seulement deux mois et je reste insensible comme s'il n'était pas question de moi. Cette mention qui devait me faire évanouir... %%
Only two months ago I hoped for nothing of the kind — and now I remain numb, as though it were not a matter that concerned me at all. This *mention* that was to have made me faint…
