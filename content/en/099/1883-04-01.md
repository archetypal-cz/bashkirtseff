# Sunday, 1 April 1883

%% 2026-03-10T10:00:00 TR: "bonne enfant" — good-natured, easy-going. "peintures vivantes entourees d'air" — living paintings surrounded by air, Bastien's luminous naturalism. "je ne la donne pas pour definitive" — acknowledging her opinion may change. "vieilles toiles enfumees" — old smoky canvases, the darkened Old Masters of the Louvre. %%
%% Je vais au Louvre ce matin avec Brisbane (Alice). Ce n'est pas qu'elle soit très intéressante comme eut été Breslau par exemple, il n'y a pas échange d'idées; mais elle est bonne enfant, assez intelligente, elle m'écoute et je pense tout haut. C'est un exercice. Je parle de ce qui me préoccupe et de ce que je désirerais. De Bastien naturellement qui a pris une place énorme dans mes conversations avec Julian et Alice... J'aime sa peinture extraordinairement et je vous paraîtrai bien aveugle si je vous dis que ces vieilles toiles enfumées du Louvre me font penser avec plaisir aux peintures vivantes entourées d'air, aux yeux parlants, aux bouches qui vont s'ouvrir. Enfin c'est l'impression de ce matin, je ne la donne pas pour définitive. %%
%% 2026-10-02T15:31:52 RSR: Accents restored (OCR-stripped in the extraction; 13 words) per tome15.docx ¶2572–2575, Mon Journal t.15. %%
I go to the Louvre this morning with Brisbane — Alice. Not that she is very interesting, as Breslau would have been, for instance; there is no exchange of ideas; but she is good-natured, reasonably clever, she listens to me and I think aloud. It is a kind of exercise. I speak of what preoccupies me and of what I desire. Of Bastien naturally, who has taken up an enormous place in my conversations with Julian and Alice… I love his painting extraordinarily, and you will think me quite blind if I tell you that these old smoky canvases of the Louvre make me think with pleasure of the living paintings surrounded by air, with speaking eyes, with mouths about to open. In short, that is this morning's impression — I do not offer it as definitive.

I cough, and without seeing myself grow thin it seems to me that I am ill. Only I do not want to think about it. But why then do I have such a flourishing appearance? Not merely in colour but in — dimensions?

I am not sad; I simply have a fever and difficulty breathing. It is the right lung that… progresses.

%% 099.0015 %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% 2026-03-10T10:00:00 TR: "folle que vous etes" — fool that you are; second-person self-address preserved. "Des vesicatoires" — vesicants: caustic plasters used as medical treatment. "Des taches jaunes pendant un an ou deux" — yellow stains/marks from the treatment. %%
%% Et folle que vous êtes, vous vous voyez pour ainsi dire brûler et vous ne faites rien ! Des vésicatoires[^3] ? Des taches jaunes pendant un an ou deux ?! Mais qu'est-ce que deux ans auprès de la vie, de la santé, du travail ? %%
And fool that you are, you see yourself so to speak burning and you do nothing! Vesicants?[^3] Yellow stains for a year or two?! But what are two years weighed against life, health, work?

[^3]: *Vesicants*: caustic plasters applied to the skin as a counter-irritant treatment for lung ailments, leaving discoloured marks for months.

%% 099.0016 %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% 2026-03-10T10:00:00 TR: "on croit toujours que ca passera" — one always believes it will pass; the denial of serious illness rendered naturally. %%
%% Ah ! voilà. Je n'en ai même pas grand besoin de cette épaule[^4] et on peut si bien s'arranger... Eh bien alors ? Eh bien alors on croit toujours que ça passera comme ça... %%
Ah, there it is. I have barely any great need of that shoulder[^4] and one can manage so well… Well, then? Well, then one always believes it will pass just like that…

[^4]: She refers to the shoulder as the site where vesicant plasters would be applied.

%% 099.0017 %%
%% 2026-03-10T10:00:00 TR: "j'ai l'air d'une folle" — I look like a madwoman. "Ophelie ?" — Ophelia: reference to Shakespeare's drowned maiden. %%
%% Nous avons envoyé les portraits à l'architecte[^5]. Ma photographie est jolie mais j'ai l'air d'une folle. Ophélie ? %%
We sent the portraits to the architect.[^5] My photograph is pretty but I look like a madwoman. Ophelia?

[^5]: "The architect" refers to Émile Bastien-Lepage, brother of Jules Bastien-Lepage the painter. Émile was an architect who gave perspective lessons and acted as a liaison between Marie and his famous brother.
