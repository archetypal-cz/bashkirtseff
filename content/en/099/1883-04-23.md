# Monday, 23 April 1883

%% 099.0185 %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% [#Emotions](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/EMOTIONS.md) %%
%% 2026-03-10T10:00:00 TR: SPELLING: "scultpeur" — Marie's error for "sculpteur"; corrected silently. "planter là" — to abandon, leave in the lurch. %%
%% Je suis furieuse. Maman s'est arrangée avec la princesse Jeanne et son mari pour aller chez je ne sais quel scultpeur, après un déjeuner chez nous. Donc je suis obligée de causer pendant quatre heures de suite et de planter là mon tableau. %%
I am furious. Maman has arranged with Princess Jeanne and her husband to go to visit some sculptor or other after luncheon at our house. So I am obliged to talk for four hours on end and abandon my painting.

%% 099.0186 %%
%% [#Emotions](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/EMOTIONS.md) %%
%% 2026-03-10T10:00:00 TR: "tristesse noire" — black melancholy (stronger than ordinary sadness). "gaietés violentes" — oxymoron: violent gaieties (Marie's characteristic manic swings). %%
%% Du reste quoiqu'il arrive je suis plongée dans une tristesse noire dont je ne sors que par instants et alors avec des gaietés violentes. %%
In any case, whatever happens, I am plunged in a black melancholy from which I emerge only in moments — and then with violent gaiety.

%% 099.0187 %%
%% 2026-03-10T10:00:00 TR: "grand malheur" — euphemism for her deafness (consistent throughout). "l'appui de l'ambassade" — support from the Russian embassy (crucial social standing for Russian expatriates in Paris). "ça s'arrangera" — it will sort itself out (Maman's optimistic passivity, italicised in original). %%
%% Car à mon grand malheur viennent se joindre les éternels petits ennuis avec maman à propos de cette chose nécessaire d'avoir l'appui de l'ambassade, elle ne veut pas le comprendre et croit toujours que *ça s'arrangera* tout seul. %%
For to my great misfortune are added the eternal petty irritations with Maman over this necessary matter of securing embassy support — she will not understand it and always believes *it will sort itself out* on its own.

%% 099.0188 %%
%% [#Health](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/HEALTH.md) %%
%% 2026-03-10T10:00:00 TR: "*tout* ne serait rien" — all of that would be nothing (italics in original). %%
%% Mais *tout* ne serait rien si j'étais comme avant. Je vais encore retourner chez un médecin. %%
%% Krishaber qui m'a estropiée vient de mourir lui et sa femme. On dit qu'ils ont attrapé la peste des singes sur lesquels cet homme de science pratiquait des expériences. Il aurait mieux valu mon Dieu que ces gens-là vivent et que je ne sois pas si malheureuse. %%
%% 2026-09-26T01:24:09 TR: Second line translated from the French RSR restored from tome15 on 2026-09-26 (it was missing from the source before). "estropiée" — crippled, maimed: Marie blames Krishaber's treatment for her ruined hearing/throat. "la peste des singes" — kept literal ("the plague from the monkeys"); Marie repeats hearsay. %%
But *all of that* would be nothing if I were as I was before. I am going to see a doctor again.
Krishaber, who crippled me, has just died, he and his wife. They say they caught the plague from the monkeys on which this man of science was performing his experiments. It would have been better, my God, for those people to live and for me not to be so unhappy.
