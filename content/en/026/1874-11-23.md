Je m'attends à quelque éclat; un de ces beaux matins on connaîtra la somme qu'il doit, car on ne fréquente pas les actrices, les mauvaises femmes, et l'on n'est pas entouré de chenapans, des espèces de pickpockets, pour quarante francs par mois. Mon Dieu que de honte avec ce garçon ! %%
As for Paul, he is prospering along the most dreadful of paths. It would take too long to tell it all — and even if I went on as long as I pleased I could never say enough about his misdeeds. I expect some scandal; one fine morning the sum he owes will come to light, for one does not frequent actresses and loose women, nor surround oneself with scoundrels — a sort of ==pickpockets==,[^1] on forty francs a month. My God, what shame that boy brings on us!

[^1]: In English in the original.
