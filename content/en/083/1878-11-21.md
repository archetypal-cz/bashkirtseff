# Thursday, 21 November 1878

%% 083.0224 %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% [#Paris](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/PARIS.md) [#LOUISE_BRESLAU](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/LOUISE_BRESLAU.md) %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% Breslau a peint une joue si nature et si vraie que moi femme, artiste, rivale, j'ai eu envie d'embrasser cette joue de femme... il doit en être souvent ainsi pour les choses de la vie, il ne faut pas trop s'en approcher, on se salirait les lèvres et on abîmerait l'objet. %%
%% 2026-10-01T07:34:10 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
Breslau painted a cheek so lifelike and true that I — woman, artist, rival — wanted to kiss that woman's cheek... it must often be so with the things of life: one must not come too close to them; one would soil one's lips and spoil the object.

%% 083.0225 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Paul](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/PAUL.md) [#Nadine](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/NADINE.md) [#Alexandre](../../_original/_glossary/people/core/ALEXANDRE.md) %%
%% Paul, mon désormais unique frère et colosse vient d'arriver accompagnant Nadine, la femme d'Alexandre. Elle est petite et blonde, et elle a vingt-huit ans, lui n'en a que dix-neuf, mais on les prendrait pour l'homme et la femme. %%
Paul — henceforth my one and only brother, and a colossus — has just arrived accompanying Nadine, Alexandre's wife. She is small and blonde and twenty-eight years old; he is only nineteen, yet one would take them for husband and wife.
%% 2026-10-01T07:40:40 RED: HIGH Para 083.0225 - "but enormous" was an addition not in the French («lui n'en a que dix-neuf, mais on les prendrait pour l'homme et la femme»); removed. %%

%% 083.0226 %%
%% Ils ont rencontré en voyage un Russe qui les a pris pour des amoureux lorsque Nadine eût dit qu'elle voyageait avec son neveu, il alla le redemander à Paul espérant sans doute qu'ils se contrediraient. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:22 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶1813, Mon Journal t.12 p.162 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
On their journey they met a Russian who took them for lovers; when Nadine had said that she was travelling with her nephew, he went and asked Paul again, hoping no doubt that they would contradict each other.

%% 083.0227 %%
%% C'est très drôle ce couple innocent. %%
This innocent couple is very amusing.

%% 083.0228 %%
%% Madame Gavini est venue nous voir. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:22 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶1815, Mon Journal t.12 p.162 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Madame Gavini came to see us.

%% 083.0229 %%
%% Il faut commencer à rester chez soi le dimanche, Turquan et Filippini sont venus et nous étions sorties. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:22 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶1816, Mon Journal t.12 p.162 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
We must begin staying at home on Sundays; Turquan and Filippini came, and we had gone out.

Robert-Fleury came to the atelier this evening. Everything is still going very well. I have made "astonishing progress."

%% 083.0231 %%
%% Cette semaine grâce à nos Russes je ne travaillerai que le matin et le soir. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:22 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶1818, Mon Journal t.12 p.162 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
