# Wednesday, 20 November 1878

%% 083.0226 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%

%% Ce soir après mon bain je suis devenue subitement si jolie que j'ai passé vingt minutes à me regarder. Je suis sûre que si je m'étais montrée quelque part j'aurais [eu] du succès avec la figure d'aujourd'hui. %%
%% 2026-10-01T07:34:10 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
This evening after my bath I suddenly became so pretty that I spent twenty minutes looking at myself. I am sure that if I had shown myself anywhere I would have [had] a success with the face I had today.

%% 083.0227 %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% [#Appearance](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/APPEARANCE.md) %%
%% Une couleur absolument éblouissante et si fine, si douce, les joues à peine rosées; en fait de vigueur il n'y a que les lèvres et les sourcils avec les yeux. %%
An absolutely dazzling complexion — so delicate, so gentle; cheeks barely touched with rose. For vigour, only the lips and eyebrows, with the eyes.

%% 083.0228 %%

%% 2026-03-08T11:26:00 TR: "se parcheminer" = to become parchment-like, to dry and crinkle with age. %%
And to think it serves no purpose! It will serve... but what is outrageous is that all of this must fade, turn to parchment, and die!

%% 083.0229 %%
%% [#Appearance](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/APPEARANCE.md) %%
%% Je vous prie ne me croyez pas aveugle, quand je suis mal je le vois bien allez; et c'est la première fois que je suis jolie depuis bien du temps déjà. %%

Please do not think me blind; when I look bad I see it well enough, I assure you; and this is the first time I have been pretty for quite a while now.
