# Friday, 27 December 1878

My mother is at that Hottentot's again. Paul asked her to go, because the night before last, after the theatre, Maman and my aunt had promised to spend the night at his hotel — but while he went to give orders, they came back with Paul.

The father was furious at having been played for a fool, when he had been counting on amusing himself — and Paul rightly reckoned that this annoyance would be taken out on them unless some kindness were shown the following day.

My week is lost for the atelier. I had wanted to take advantage of it to go and see a drama with an actor in it whose accent, I am told, recalls Cassagnac. But de Daillens could not go with me, and I stayed at home. I do not care to go to the theatre with my family, with whom there is always some friction.

%% Depuis trois jours j'ai envie d'écrire je ne sais plus au juste quelles réflexions mais traquée par le chant de la demoiselle du 2ème je me mis à feuilleter mon passage en Italie et puis on est venu me déranger et j'ai perdu le fil de mes idées et cette disposition mélancolique qui est assez agréable. Ce qui me surprend c'est la facilité avec laquelle, dans ce temps-là, j'employais les plus grands mots pour peindre des aventures simples. Mais j'avais envie de grands sentiments, j'étais vexée de n'avoir pas à raconter des sensations étonnantes, foudroyantes, romanesque et *j'interprêtais* mes sentiments. les peintres me comprennent. Tout cela est très bien mais comment une fille qui se prétend intelligente n'a-t-elle pas mieux compris la valeur des hommes et des évènements. Je dis cela parce que j'allais dire que mes parents auraient dû m'éclairer et me dire, par exemple d'Antonelli, que ce n'est pas un homme sérieux, ni un homme pour lequel on doive se donner le moindre mal. C'est vrai on m'en a parlé tout de travers, ma mère étant plus jeune que moi, mais enfin tout cela à part et puisque j'ai si haute opinion de mon intelligence j'aurais dû mieux me rendre compte et le traiter comme tous les autres au lieu de lui donner un développement si considérable dans ce journal et ailleurs. Mais je brûlais d'impatience d'enregistrer des romans et bête que je suis ! Ç'aurait peut-être été plus romanesque autrement... bref j'ai été jeune et inexpérimentée malgré des fanfaronnades de rouerie, voilà ce dont il faut enfin convenir quoiqu'il m'en coûte. Voilà, j'ai voulu aider les évènements.. Bien d'autres ont été amoureux après... le petit Eristoff en Russie que l'on aurait pu prendre au sérieux s'il était plus éloigné, pas trop de la famille et si mon père et Paul en parlaient moins familièrement. %%
%% 2026-03-08T12:08:00 TR: "*j'interprêtais*" = I was interpreting — Marie's italics signal her retrospective self-awareness about inflating feelings into narrative. %%
%% 2026-10-01T07:27:29 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
For three days I have been wanting to write down I no longer know exactly what reflections, but, hounded by the singing of the young lady on the second floor, I began leafing through the pages of my stay in Italy, and then someone came to disturb me and I lost the thread of my ideas and that melancholy mood which is rather pleasant. What surprises me is the ease with which, in those days, I used the grandest words to paint simple adventures. But I longed for grand sentiments; I was vexed at having no astonishing, thunderous, romantic sensations to relate, and *I interpreted* my feelings. Painters will understand me. All that is very well, but how is it that a girl who claims to be intelligent did not better understand the worth of men and events? I say this because I was about to say that my parents ought to have enlightened me and told me, of Antonelli for instance, that he is not a serious man, nor a man for whom one should take the slightest trouble. It is true that I was told about him all wrong, my mother being younger than I am; but, all that aside, since I have so high an opinion of my intelligence, I ought to have seen things more clearly and treated him like all the others instead of giving him so considerable a place in this journal and elsewhere. But I was burning with impatience to record romances, fool that I am! Perhaps it would have been more romantic otherwise... in short, I was young and inexperienced despite my boasts of cunning; that is what I must at last admit, whatever it costs me. There it is: I wanted to help events along... Many others were in love afterwards... little Eristoff in Russia, whom one might have taken seriously if he had been further removed, not so much one of the family, and if my father and Paul spoke of him less familiarly.
