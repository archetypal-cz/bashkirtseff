%% Nadine a soif d'en entendre parler et en parle comme si elle en était entichée. Tu es folle, dit-elle, de ne pas l'avoir épousé. Ma foi, on n'a besoin de rien me dire et pour vous dire mon sentiment d'aujourd'hui eh ! bien... je resterai à Paris parce qu'il est à Paris et je l'aimerai comme une bête que je suis. %%
%% 2026-03-08T11:36:00 TR: "comme une bête que je suis" = like the beast that I am — raw self-comparison to an animal driven by instinct. %%
%% 2026-10-01T07:27:19 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
Nadine is dying to hear about him and talks of him as if she were infatuated with him. "You are mad," she says, "not to have married him." Upon my word, no one need say a word to me, and to tell you my feeling today — well... I shall stay in Paris because he is in Paris, and I shall love him like the beast that I am.

%% 083.0286 %%
%% J'étais seule lorsque le plus Marcuard de tous les Marcuard vint chez nous. Naturellement je le reçois et nous causons un bon moment avant la rentrée des miens. On le garde à dîner et ma foi cela me ranime. Je suis coquette, je me lance, j'ai de... l'allure enfin ! Il me donne la réplique et parle de telle façon et raisonne de telle manière que ma parole d'honneur, rien ne m'étonnerait. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:22 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶1864–1865, Mon Journal t.12 pp.166–167 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
I was alone when the most Marcuard of all the Marcuards came to see us. Naturally I receive him, and we talk for a good while before my family come in. They keep him to dinner, and upon my word it revives me. I am coquettish, I let myself go, I have... style, in short! He gives me my cues, and talks in such a way and reasons in such a manner that, on my word of honour, nothing would surprise me.

%% 2026-10-01T07:27:19 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
Already the last time he had asked me whether I could commit to loving him as he loves me... I answered with a jest. This evening I laughed at everything, and he said I had nothing in my head but my paintbrushes. Ah! everything gets on my nerves! That Marcuard! I talked furiously! I made eyes!

%% 083.0288 %%
%% Je m'ennuie ! Je suis lasse, lasse, lasse ! Ce sont des pensées horribles. %%
I am bored! I am weary, weary, weary! These are horrible thoughts.
