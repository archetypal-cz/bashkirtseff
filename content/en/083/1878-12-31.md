# Tuesday, 31 December 1878

%% 083.0510 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Paris](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/PARIS.md) [#Bashkirtseff_pere](../../_original/_glossary/people/core/BASHKIRTSEFF_PERE.md) [#Paul](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/PAUL.md) %%
%% Les manèges qu'emploie mon père pour emmener Paul que je désire garder jusqu'à la fin de janvier, sont inqualifiables. %%
%% 2026-03-08T13:55:00 TR: "manèges" = maneuvers/machinations — the word carries a connotation of circus tricks or equine circles, both appropriate. "inqualifiables" = beyond description/unspeakable. %%
The manoeuvres my father employs to take Paul away — whom I wish to keep until the end of January — are beyond description.

%% 083.0511 %%
%% Il le tourmente et lui fait entrevoir une année de pointes d'épingles en cas de désobéissance... désobéissance ! il ne peut y en avoir, car il ne dit pas: je veux, mais: comme tu veux. Et puis des méchancetés et des perfidies sans fin. J'ai écouté à la porte de la salle à manger pendant que le père et le fils se concertaient. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:22 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶2095, Mon Journal t.12 p.187 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
He torments him and holds out the prospect of a year of pinpricks should he disobey... disobey! There can be no disobedience, for he does not say "I want" but "as you wish". And then endless spite and treachery. I listened at the dining-room door while father and son were conferring.

%% 083.0512 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#De_Daillens](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/DE_DAILLENS.md) %%

%% En rentrant d'atelier j'ai pris de Daillens pour rencontrer la nouvelle année ensemble. Ma famille était au théâtre. Nous avons fait des bonnes aventures puis du champagne et du thé. %%
%% 2026-03-08T13:55:00 TR: "bonnes aventures" = fortune-telling — traditional New Year's Eve divination. %%
Coming home from the atelier I fetched de Daillens to see the new year in together. My family was at the theatre. We told fortunes, then had champagne and tea.

%% 083.0513 %%
%% [#Mme_Paul_de_Cassagnac](../../_original/_glossary/people/aristocracy/MME_PAUL_DE_CASSAGNAC.md) %%
%% J'ai acheté une paire de bretelles et un cigare de deux sous pour les envoyer à Mme P. de Cassagnac. Cette petite infamie ne fera qu'envenimer les choses. %%
%% 2026-03-08T13:55:00 TR: "bretelles" = suspenders; "cigare de deux sous" = two-penny cigar — cheap, deliberately masculine gifts intended to insult the rival's femininity and status. "Cette petite infamie" = this little infamy — Marie is clear-eyed about her own pettiness. %%
I have bought a pair of braces and a two-penny cigar to send to Mme P. de Cassagnac. This little infamy will only poison things further.

%% 2026-10-01T07:27:29 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
%% 2026-10-01T17:26:29 RSR: Missing opening quotation mark restored: the print reads «"Le Patriote Niçois" met opposition…» (Mon Journal t.12 p.188, scan Tome12.pdf; tome12.docx ¶2099). %%
The defamation suit has been decided — naturally the five or six newspapers that spread and commented on the story were condemned, but what a scandal! *Le Patriote Niçois* is lodging an objection to the judgement and declares, with useless moderation, that it hopes to prove it acted in good *faith*. No, you see, no, you see, no: the more one does, the more noise it makes. Perhaps it would have been better not to prosecute? The chief offence is that a lawsuit was announced, therefore false news. Anyway, here is the report.

> *Le Figaro* of Saturday 28 December 1878, in its "Gazette des tribunaux" column, gives a copy of the judgement handed down by the 8th Correctional Chamber: The Court./ Whereas in the articles complained of allusion is made to an alleged civil action brought by the plaintiff against his wife; whereas no such action exists, and consequently the offence of publishing false news is established../ Whereas, moreover, in making known the circumstances of which they speak, the defendants have indulged in the most outrageous and odious comments, of a nature to disturb the peace of a household, so worthy of respect./ On these grounds./ Sentences each of them to fifteen days' imprisonment and a fine of one thousand francs.

%% 083.0516 %%
%% Voilà un bien triste procès vraiment. Il l'a voulu. Il n'y a pas de fumée sans feu... pourquoi aurait-on inventé cela plutôt qu'autre chose ? Elle prête terriblement à des suppositions de ce genre savez-vous ?Dans tous les cas il est impossible qu'il l'aime. C'est une compagne, une femme de ménage, un pot-au-feu. Il arrive un moment où l'homme est fatigué de sa liberté qui devient de l'isolement et où il lui faut cette fin bourgeoise. D'ailleurs qui me dit que le Défunt Monsieur Julia ait jamais été un être poétique ? Enfin Il ne l'aime pas; c'est déjà quelque chose. Il n'a jamais été vraiment amoureux, c'est encore certain, il l'a annoncé et Blanc ne lui a jamais connu que des maîtresses de chaque jour qui duraient deux, trois et quatre ans; ou bien alors des dames du monde par ci, par là. Voilà un bel idéal. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:22 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶2101, Mon Journal t.12 p.188 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
A very sad lawsuit indeed. He would have it so. There is no smoke without fire... why would anyone have invented that rather than something else? She lends herself terribly to suppositions of that kind, you know. In any case it is impossible that he should love her. She is a companion, a housekeeper, a stockpot. There comes a moment when a man is tired of his freedom, which becomes isolation, and when he needs this bourgeois ending. Besides, who is to say that the late Monsieur Julia was ever a poetic being? Anyway, he does not love her; that is something already. He has never really been in love, that too is certain; he has said so, and Blanc never knew him to have anything but everyday mistresses who lasted two, three and four years, or else ladies of society here and there. A fine ideal, that.

%% 083.0517 %%
%% [#Paul_de_Cassagnac](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/CASSAGNAC.md) %%

He is not my ideal — I only thought I could, that I was going to love him: that is all.

%% 083.0518 %%
%% J'y pense encore... pas à lui précisément... mais je me fais une seconde existence dans laquelle il vient tel que je l'imagine et je vous avouerai franchement que je m'ennuierais fort si je ne m'arrangeais ainsi une autre vie à côté de la vraie. Là je suis toujours heureuse et rien ne peut détruire mes rêves. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:22 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶2103, Mon Journal t.12 p.188 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
I still think of it... not of him exactly... but I make myself a second existence in which he comes just as I imagine him, and I will frankly confess to you that I would be dreadfully bored if I did not arrange myself another life like this, alongside the real one. There I am always happy, and nothing can destroy my dreams.
