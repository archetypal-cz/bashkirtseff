# Sunday, 5 January 1879

%% 083.0555 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%

We have given up our apartment and are lodging in two rooms on the third floor, above the *entresol* we occupied before the Avenue de l'Alma apartment — that is to say, at 71 Champs-Élysées. It will be fifteen or twenty days before our apartment in the Avenue Montaigne is ready.

Tomorrow is Russian Christmas; I am going to open the Gospels at random to see what will happen to me: *.. la luce della vita (3 Giovanni VIII)..* I shall hold to that.[^1] I shall put a comb under my pillow. I cried at the [See p. 155] in the carriage. No, you see, I am not capable of making my way? with nothing but luck, as I hold to that. I would put a comb under my pillow.

[^1]: "La luce della vita" — the light of life (John 8:12) — from an Italian Gospel. Marie performs bibliomancy, an old Russian Christmas Eve tradition.

Tout de même, en être réduite là, c'est raide vous en conviendrez. Oh ! quoi qu'on dise cela ne s'est jamais vu qu'on rêve sans voir personne ! C'est la mort. Est-ce ma faute... %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:22 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶2136, Mon Journal t.12 p.191 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
I was about to complain, but I remembered my arrangement [As a last resort, to marry a poor but accommodating Italian prince (see 29.12 1878)]. All the same, to be reduced to that is a bit steep, you will admit. Oh, whatever people say, no one ever heard of dreaming without seeing anybody! It is death. Is it my fault...

%% 083.0558 %%
%% De cette manière il se passe des mois sans que je reçoive un seul hommage. Ce n'est pas leur faute à ces pauvres hommes. Je vis comme une Russe du Moyen Age. Oh ! écoutez par moments toute ma philosophie s'en va, tous mes arrangements, l'article 140 même, pâlissent et je suis révoltée, découragée, morte. %%
%% 2026-03-08T14:15:00 TR: "une Russe du Moyen Age" = a medieval Russian woman — confined to the terem (women's quarters) of pre-Petrine Russia. The self-comparison is precise: technically in Paris, but as cloistered as a Muscovite noblewoman of centuries past. %%
%% 2026-10-01T07:27:29 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
Thus months pass without my receiving a single tribute. It is not their fault, poor men. I live like a Russian woman of the Middle Ages. Oh, listen: at moments all my philosophy deserts me, all my arrangements, even article 140, grow pale, and I am rebellious, discouraged, dead.

%% 083.0559 %%
%% Etre tout près de tout, au centre de l'élégance, des arts, de l'esprit, du monde, et... n'être qu'à côté !! %%
To be so close to everything — at the centre of elegance, the arts, wit, society — and... to be merely beside it!!
