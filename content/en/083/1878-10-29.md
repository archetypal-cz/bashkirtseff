%% 2026-03-08T11:00:00 TR: "adorations créoles" = Marie's ironic term for her feelings about Cassagnac, referencing his Gascon origin; "pour mémoire" = for the record; "la mort de cet homme" = metaphorical death (his marriage). %%
Coming home from the Champ de Mars, fatigue combined with this election in prospect set me thinking that this man's death has broken something in me, and that a life without that something — without that purpose, without that light — is no life, whatever one does. I would like to find a distraction to change me, and to see whether it is true. For a moment, the passion described in books appeared to me: the passion one theorizes about, which makes everything else repugnant... What if I were to fall victim to it! I thought. It would be detestable, I answered myself. I am happy enough to speak of it for amusement, but I will not let it prevent me from thinking about living. One can forget everything without regret for a happy passion — but a great fantasy from which one derives neither pleasure nor profit: no, upon my word, I want none of that. It would be too serious, too all-consuming, too singular to be enjoyable. I do not even believe myself capable of it, in any case. I am quite willing to continue these Creole adorations — but quietly, in the meantime; let them not encroach on the rest. Have no anxiety — I mention it only for the record, because the thought crossed my mind this morning.
