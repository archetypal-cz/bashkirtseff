# Saturday, 16 November 1878

%% 083.0187 %%
%% Hier nous sommes allées voir Mme Gavini qui m'avait envoyé des bonbons la veille. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:22 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶1775, Mon Journal t.12 p.158 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Yesterday we went to see Mme Gavini, who had sent me sweets the day before.

And today Robert-Fleury was very pleased with Breslau and urged her to do something for the Salon, adding that she would be accepted — he would answer for it. As for me, this week I had old Granvoinnet beside me, the scourge of the atelier, a good soul but mad and maddening!

%% 083.0189 %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% [#LOUISE_BRESLAU](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/LOUISE_BRESLAU.md) %%

%% J'ai égalé Breslau en dessin, ce qu'elle a en plus c'est l'habitude; maintenant il faut que je me donne tant de mois pour peindre comme elle, car si je ne peux pas faire cela je ne suis rien d'extraordinaire. Pourtant pendant les sept ou dix mois que je me donne elle ne s'arrêtera pas... je serai donc forcée de courir de façon à rattraper les dix-huit mois qui sont passés dans les sept ou dix mois pendant lesquels nous courrons ensemble. Cela me paraît bien improbable, bien extraordinaire... Enfin, à la grâce de Dieu. %%
%% 2026-10-01T07:34:10 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
I have equalled Breslau in drawing; what she has beyond me is practice. Now I must give myself so many months to paint as well as she does — for if I cannot manage that, I am nothing extraordinary. Yet during the seven or ten months I am giving myself she will not stand still... so I shall be forced to run hard enough to make up the eighteen months already gone within the seven or ten months in which we shall be running together. That seems to me most improbable, most extraordinary... Well, God's will be done.

%% 2026-03-08T11:20:00 TR: "jusqu'au cou" = up to my neck; "réactionnaires" = reactionaries/monarchists. %%
%% 2026-10-01T07:34:10 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
You see, it has to be. I have just come back from Versailles, where I was with Maman and Dina and Gavini; I am up to my neck in politics, which is foolish, but that is how it is. I read the Republicans, who calm me by denigrating Cassagnac; I also read the reactionaries, who irritate me by glorifying him. Here is *La Jeune Garde*, a nasty rag in which the superb Captain Blanc crosses swords; read this nonsense, for it gives a very exact account of the session.

%% 083.0191 %%
%% Et puis voici un numéro du "Patriote Niçois" que j'ai fait venir et dans lequel il y a une correspondance de Versailles pour laquelle Cassagnac a dit-on intenté un procès. C'est une horreur mais une horreur possible vu le physique de l'étrange Juliette. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:22 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶1778, Mon Journal t.12 p.159 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
And then here is a number of the *Patriote Niçois*, which I sent for, containing a letter from Versailles over which Cassagnac, they say, has brought a lawsuit. It is a horror — but a possible horror, given the looks of the strange Juliet.

%% 083.0192 %%
%% C'est l'élection de Pontivy qui a passé aujourd'hui. Le comte de Mun est un orateur de salons, gentilhomme-soldat, Dieu et le Roy. Bel homme du reste, mais il parle un peu vite ! et puis ses cheveux ressemblent à une perruque. Le front énorme et une langue [de ] cheveux coupés ras descend au milieu. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:22 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶1779, Mon Journal t.12 p.159 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
It was the Pontivy election that came up today. The Comte de Mun is a drawing-room orator — gentleman-soldier, God and the King. A handsome man, moreover, but he speaks a little fast! And then his hair looks like a wig. An enormous forehead, and a tongue [of] close-cropped hair runs down the middle of it.

%% 083.0193 %%
%% D'abord j'ai rencontré le furieux Bescherelle assez calme (nous étions seules ayant manqué le premier train) qui n'a pas voulu donner un troisième billet, %%
%% - Laissez aller ces dames et attendez un peu, je vous placerai. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:22 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶1780–1781, Mon Journal t.12 p.159 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
First I met the furious Bescherelle, fairly calm (we were on our own, having missed the first train), who would not give me a third ticket:
— Let these ladies go in and wait a little; I will find you a seat.

%% 083.0194 %%
%% Quoi que traitée en habituée, je n'accepte pas et passe avec deux billets disant que j'en ai trois. Nous montons dans une horrible tribune, Gavini arrive et nous place dans la loge du Président de la Chambre juste à la place de la femme de Cassagnac. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:22 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶1782, Mon Journal t.12 p.159 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Though treated as a regular, I do not accept, and go through with two tickets, saying I have three. We go up to a horrible gallery; Gavini arrives and puts us in the box of the President of the Chamber, in the very seat of Cassagnac's wife.

%% 083.0195 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Paul_de_Cassagnac](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/CASSAGNAC.md) %%
%% Quelle affreuse dérision du sort ! Pourquoi suis-je femme ! déplacée et inutile et absurde. Paul ferait une si charmante fille et moi ferais un homme célèbre. %%
What dreadful mockery of fate! Why am I a woman! Displaced, useless, absurd. Paul would make such a charming girl — and I would make a celebrated man.

%% 083.0196 %%
%% Et la buvette ! J'y ai été à cette fameuse buvette des députés, et on nous regardait entrer là avec des yeux envieux; combien de fois n'ai-je pas été comme ceux qui me regardaient ! %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:22 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶1784, Mon Journal t.12 p.159 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
And the refreshment bar! I have been to that famous deputies' bar, and people watched us go in with envious eyes; how many times have I not been like those who were watching me!
