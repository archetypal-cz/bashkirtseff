%% 2026-10-01T07:45:04 CON: Para 083.0424 - Decided for the military sense. "Le pauvre garçon" is pity for the one sent in, not condescension towards a hothead. cz (vzplanul) and uk (спалахнув) follow the LAN anger reading, so the trees now diverge; flagged to the lead. %%
