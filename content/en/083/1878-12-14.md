# Saturday, 14 December 1878

%% 083.0389 %%
%% [#Family](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/FAMILY.md) %%
%% J'ai rarement été plus triste que ces deux jours... Je travaille peu ce dernier temps et la vie domestique est insupportable ici. %%
I have rarely been sadder than in these two days... I have been working little of late, and domestic life here is insupportable.

%% [#Maman](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/MAMAN.md) %%

%% 2026-03-08T11:52:00 TR: "mes mères" = my mothers (plural) — mother and aunt acting as dual maternal figures; "oiseuses" = idle/purposeless; "hargneuses" = shrewish/quarrelsome. %%
%% 2026-10-01T07:27:19 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
The cruellest enemy would not succeed in blackening my name more than my mothers do. I answer them indignantly and appear to be entirely in the wrong. They are inconsistent, idle, shrewish! I am on edge, angry, aged by these little miseries at every moment.

%% 083.0391 %%
%% Je suis née au milieu de tout cela et plus le temps va plus j'en souffre. J'aimerais mieux une bonne fois, une belle tourmente que toutes ces petites inconséquences, manque de tact, niaiseries qui me font bondir, pleurer, mourir. %%
%% 2026-10-01T07:27:19 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
I was born in the midst of all this, and the more time goes on, the more I suffer from it. I would far rather have, once and for all, one fine tempest than all these little inconsistencies, lack of tact, follies that make me bristle, weep, die.

This evening I went to see *La Princesse Borowska* at the Ambigu with the Gavinis. That good Mme Gavini — what an empty, amusing, and always cheerful woman. I shall make you understand her in one word. She never listens to music and never makes any. But she is so good to me; this evening I caught her admiring my ear and my cheek with a mother's delight.

%% 083.0393 %%
%% [#Dreams](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/DREAMS.md) %%
%% [#Julia_Acard](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/JULIA_ACARD.md) [#Cassagnac](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/CASSAGNAC.md) %%
%% J'ai rêvé que la femme de Cassagnac avait les bras couverts de poils noirs et qu'elle s'ajustait (toute nue) une tournure en crin devant et une derrière, cela lui faisait une sorte de jupe. Puis elle mit un turban de velours noir et parut extrêmement belle. %%
%% 2026-03-08T11:52:00 TR: "tournure en crin" = horsehair bustle; "toute nue" = quite naked. Surreal dream imagery degrading and glorifying the rival simultaneously. %%
I dreamed that Cassagnac's wife had arms covered in black hair and was adjusting (quite naked) a horsehair bustle — one in front and one behind — which formed a kind of skirt. Then she put on a black velvet turban and appeared extremely beautiful.

%% 083.0394 %%
%% [#Julia_Acard](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/JULIA_ACARD.md) %%
%% Cette canaille, comme elle a dû battre des paupières pensant que l'on faisait ce discours que vous venez de lire. De pareils mots, pour cette bécasse. %%
%% 2026-03-08T11:54:00 TR: "cette canaille" = this scoundrel/rascal; "bécasse" = woodcock/silly woman (pejorative). %%
The wretch — how she must have fluttered her eyelids, thinking that speech you have just read was addressed to her! Such words, for that goose.

%% 083.0395 %%

Speaking of dreams — Robert Mitchell has been very ill.

%% 2026-03-08T11:54:00 TR: "nullité" = nullity/nobody; the triple formulation — nullity/Acard/another — progressively dismantles the rival. %%
It is very cold; the snow has been falling for three days; all Paris is white; I am very cold, sleepy, sad — and I revolt against the stupidity of fate. The only being who was made for me has been given to a nullity, to a Mademoiselle Acard, to another, when all is said!

%% 083.0397 %%
%% Cette infamie-là c'est le couronnement de mes misères. Maintenant il est temps que l'ère de prospérité surgisse... Je n'y crois pas mais malgré cela je ne veux pas mourir. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:22 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶1976, Mon Journal t.12 p.176 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
That infamy is the crowning of my miseries. Now it is time for the era of prosperity to dawn... I do not believe in it, but in spite of that I do not want to die.
