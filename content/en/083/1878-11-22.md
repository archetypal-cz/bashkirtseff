# Friday, 22 November 1878

%% 083.0233 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Censored_1887](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/CENSORED_1887.md) %%
%% [#Paris](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/PARIS.md) [#LOUISE_BRESLAU](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/LOUISE_BRESLAU.md) %%
%% Je suis effrayée de l'avenir de Breslau. J'en suis irritée, assombrie, triste. Est-ce assez mauvais ! Il y a sept semaines que je peins, il y a dix-huit mois qu'elle peint; c'est-à-dire soixante douze semaines. %%
%% 2026-10-01T07:34:10 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
I am frightened by Breslau's future. It irritates me, casts a shadow over me, saddens me. Is that not wretched! I have been painting for seven weeks; she has been painting for eighteen months — that is to say, seventy-two weeks.

Je suis vraiment folle de l'envier, je suis une enfant dans l'art et elle une femme. Pour combler mon ennui %%

She has more than ten times as much as I have. But that is not the whole of it. She composes, and there is nothing feminine, banal or misshapen in what she does. She is already an artist, and she is only twenty-two. She will be noticed at the Salon, for besides the execution she will bring to it, she will not choose an ordinary subject. I am truly mad to envy her: I am a child in art and she is a woman. To crown my vexation

%% 083.0235 %%
%% [#Paul_de_Cassagnac](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/CASSAGNAC.md) [#Mme_Paul_de_Cassagnac](../../_original/_glossary/people/aristocracy/MME_PAUL_DE_CASSAGNAC.md) %%
%% Il paraît que le riche négociant qui avait offert 50.000 francs pour l'élection de Condom, existe et que le mari d'Acard est institué l'héritier de sa fortune qui s'élève à près de 2 millions. On le nomme, c'est un M. Julien et il avait d'abord fait un testament au profit du Prince Impérial, à présent c'est pour Cassagnac. On rit beaucoup de ce riche bienfaiteur mais les détails que donne "la Gazette" de la France sont vraiment très précis. Après tout c'est sans doute un mensonge, Acard est une femme et il l'aime. %%
%% 2026-10-01T07:34:10 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
It appears that the wealthy merchant who had offered 50,000 francs for the Condom election does exist, and that Acard's husband has been made heir to his fortune, which amounts to nearly 2 million. He is named: a M. Julien, who had at first made a will in favour of the Prince Imperial; now it is for Cassagnac. People laugh a great deal at this rich benefactor, but the details given by the *Gazette de France* are really very precise. After all, it is no doubt a lie; Acard is a woman and he loves her.

%% 083.0236 %%

No — my painting comes before the Acards, for the moment. I am having a bad day; everything seems dark to me.

%% 083.0237 %%
%% J'ai été voir Gavini, elle faisait un bésigue avec la chanoinesse qui a assisté à mes bêtises à propos de Cassagnac. %%

I went to see Gavini; she was playing bezique with the canoness who witnessed my follies over Cassagnac.

%% 083.0238 %%
%% Je suis fatiguée. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:22 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶1824, Mon Journal t.12 p.163 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
I am tired.
