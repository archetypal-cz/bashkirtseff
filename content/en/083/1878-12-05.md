# Thursday, 5 December 1878

%% J'ai mené à la Chambre Nadine et Paul. Le retour se fait avec Gavini, le baron Larrey, le prince d'Arenberg et le baron d'Ariste un des témoins des derniers duels de Cassagnac, un viveur, un Alexandre aux doigts chargés de vilaines bagues, un être qui choque. %%
%% 2026-03-08T11:44:00 TR: "viveur" = bon vivant/libertine; "un Alexandre" = referring to a type (like a conqueror), fingers loaded with vulgar rings. %%
I took Nadine and Paul to the Chamber. The return is made with Gavini, Baron Larrey, Prince d'Arenberg, and Baron d'Ariste — one of the seconds in Cassagnac's recent duels, a *viveur*, an Alexandre with fingers loaded with ugly rings, a being who shocks.

Formerly I had mistaken that feeling for amorous terror.
