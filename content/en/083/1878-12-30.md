# Monday, 30 December 1878

%% 083.0513 %%
%% [#Paris](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/PARIS.md) %%
%% J'ai repris mon travail. %%
I have resumed my work.

%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%

%% Antoine Gavini a dîné chez nous, ils cascadent avec Paul dans le quartier latin. C'est un petit brun de vingt-deux ans, intelligent et bien élevé mais il ressemble à un hibou maigre. %%
%% 2026-03-08T13:50:00 TR: "cascadent" = carouse/have escapades — youthful slang. "plaider pour rien" = plead for nothing, i.e., take pro bono cases to gain experience before running for office. %%
%% 2026-10-01T07:27:29 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
Antoine Gavini dined with us — he and Paul are carousing in the Latin Quarter. Young Antoine has just qualified as a barrister; he will now plead without fees until he is of age, and then stand for election. He is a small dark young man of twenty-two, intelligent and well brought up, but he looks like a skinny owl.

%% 083.0515 %%
%% [#Antoine_Gavini](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/ANTOINE_GAVINI.md) %%
%% On parlait du voyage que nous devions faire en Corse il y a deux ans pour m'acheter deux petits chevaux; et le jeune homme m'en offre de sa propriété. Voilà un gentil cadeau que j'accepte tout naturellement, ça vient un peu de sa tante je crois. %%
%% 2026-10-01T07:27:29 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
We were speaking of the trip to Corsica we were to make two years ago to buy me two small horses; and the young man offers me some from his own estate. That is a nice present, which I accept quite naturally; it comes partly from his aunt, I think.

%% 083.0516 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Mme_Gavini](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/MME_GAVINI.md) [#Antoine_Gavini](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/ANTOINE_GAVINI.md) %%
%% — Antoine vous reconduira, me dit-elle un jour, vous comprenez il faut s'y habituer puisque ça va arriver souvent. Antoine est comme mon fils, et si j'avais un fils vous pourriez parfaitement sortir avec lui, n'est-ce pas ? %%
— Antoine will see you home, she said to me one day; you understand, you must get used to it, since it will happen often. Antoine is like my son, and if I had a son you could perfectly well go out with him, could you not?

%% 083.0517 %%
%% Elle va, elle va; elle n'arrête jamais, plaint un mort, félicite un marié, salue à droite, à gauche, vite revient et c'est un vrai chemin de fer. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:22 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶2092, Mon Journal t.12 p.187 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
She goes and goes; she never stops: condoles with a mourner, congratulates a bridegroom, bows to the right, to the left, quickly comes back — she is a real railway train.
