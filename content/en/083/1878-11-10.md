# Sunday, 10 November 1878

%% 083.0162 %%
%% Je me suis amusée à clouer sur les murs des morceaux d'étoffe que j'avais, c'est arrangé en trophées avec des palettes. %%
I amused myself nailing scraps of fabric I had to the walls, arranged as trophies with palettes.

%% 083.0163 %%
%% [#baron_d_Alt](../../_original/_glossary/people/aristocracy/BARON_D_ALT.md) [#Lacon](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/LACON.md) [#Fio](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/FIO.md) %%

%% 083.0164 %%
%% [#Mme_Tarnowsky](../../_original/_glossary/people/aristocracy/MME_TARNOWSKY.md) %%

%% 2026-03-08T11:16:00 TR: "l'ex-député de Condom" = Cassagnac's constituency; Marie uses it both as political identification and likely for its comic double meaning. %%
%% 2026-10-01T07:33:18 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
%% 2026-10-01T17:27:43 RSR: Stray comma after the two periods removed: the print reads «de telle sorte.. c'est-à-dire» (Mon Journal t.12 p.156, scan Tome12.pdf). %%
Could it really be possible? If the ex-deputy for Condom were to separate from his wife, he would become again... No — nothing. I should like to love him in such a way — that is to say, that nothing is possible any more. Either it is a wretched banality that passes, and then it is a pity. Or else it is something respectable, in which case there is nothing more to be done, because there will always be a Mme Paul de Cassagnac, and whatever he does and whatever I do, I could never share his life completely.

%% [#Rouher](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/ROUHER.md) [#Cassagnac](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/CASSAGNAC.md) [#Julia_Acard](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/JULIA_ACARD.md) %%
%% C'est un misérable saltimbanque sans consistance ! Un salarié ! Ça se peut. M. Rouher n'est pas populaire et le jeune époux de la belle Julia, surtout dernièrement, a pris un ton d'autorité qui lui en donnera; ça paraît bête mais c'est souvent ainsi pourtant. Quel qu'il soit, il est le chef, en attendant in partibus, du parti qui seul compte en face de la République, et quoi que nous disions nous autres étrangers, petites gens, gens du monde, nous ne sommes rien et il est quelque chose. Je connais des gens qui me répondraient par ces mots souverainement bêtes, les brigands et autres sont aussi plus que nous. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:22 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶1750, Mon Journal t.12 p.156 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
He is a wretched mountebank without substance! A hireling! That may be. M. Rouher is not popular, and the young husband of the fair Julia has, especially of late, taken on a tone of authority that will give him some; it sounds stupid, but that is often how it goes. Whatever he may be, he is the leader — ==in name only==[^083.0169.1] for the time being — of the only party that counts against the Republic, and whatever we may say, we others, foreigners, small people, people of society, we are nothing and he is something. I know people who would answer me with these supremely stupid words: brigands and the like are also more than we are.
