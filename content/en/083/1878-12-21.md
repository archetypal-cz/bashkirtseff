# Saturday, 21 December 1878

%% 083.0404 %%
%% Ah ! par exemple ! ce serait drôle si ce n'était si ignoble et dégoûtant. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:22 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶1989, Mon Journal t.12 p.177 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
Ah! well, really! it would be funny if it were not so vile and disgusting.

%% 083.0405 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Paris](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/PARIS.md) [#Georges](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/GEORGES.md) %%
%% De nouveau on tolère M. Georges et on me le cache. Avant-hier il est venu la nuit crier et effrayer tout le monde. %%
Once again M. Georges is being tolerated and they hide it from me. The night before last he came and shouted in the night and frightened everyone.

Nadine is stunned and revolted. I was shocked and furious on learning from Paul that this monster was there and that my family was tolerating him and hiding it from me. I burst into tears, praying God to make me die. This happened yesterday.

%% 083.0410 %%
%% Elle sera sûrement une femme extraordinaire. Pensez donc, elle est absolument vicieuse quoique ayant quelques bons élans; méchante et menteuse enfin... Un mélange, je dirais bizarre si la bizarrerie n'était si commune par le temps qui court. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:22 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶1996, Mon Journal t.12 p.177 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
She will certainly be an extraordinary woman. Just think, she is thoroughly depraved, though she has a few good impulses; spiteful and a liar, in short... A mixture I would call bizarre, if the bizarre were not so common nowadays.
%% 2026-10-01T07:45:04 CON: Para 083.0410 - "vicious" changed to "depraved": "vicieuse" means given to vice, while English "vicious" means savage (a false friend). %%

%% 083.0413 %%
