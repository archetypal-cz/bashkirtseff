# Monday, 21 October 1878

%% 083.0018 %%
%% [#Paris](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/PARIS.md) [#Ecole_des_Beaux_Arts](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/ECOLE_DES_BEAUX_ARTS.md) %%
%% Paris est pavoisé comme au 30 juin. On distribue les Récompenses au Palais de l'industrie. Il est impossible.... Oh ! cette Ecole ! J'ai envie de pleurer quand je pense comme on peut y étudier bien ! %%
%% 2026-03-08T10:50:00 TR: "pavoisé comme au 30 juin" = decorated with flags as for the June 30th civic celebrations. %%
Paris is decked with flags, as on the 30th of June. The prizes are being awarded at the Palais de l'Industrie. It is impossible.... Oh! That School! I want to weep when I think how well one could study there!
