# Thursday, 31 October 1878

%% 2026-10-01T07:32:31 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
De Daillens was in the carriage with us when, near Doucet's shop, we met Mme Gavini, who called out to me, running after me. She knew de Daillens in Nice long ago, when Mme de Mouzay, barely arrived there, was going into society. Since then, moreover, the Mouzays have not been of her world, but I think it will do me no harm.

%% 083.0082 %%
%% [#Theatre_des_Italiens](../../_original/_glossary/places/theaters/THEATRE_DES_ITALIENS.md) [#Gavini](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/GAVINI.md) %%
%% Nous devions aller aux Italiens mais il y a relâche.[^1] Mme Gavini m'envoie un mot pour m'en prévenir. Cette diablesse de femme est partout, avant-hier elle dînait au petit cercle. %%
%% 2026-10-01T07:32:31 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
We were to go to the Italiens, but the theatre is dark tonight.[^1] Mme Gavini sends me a note to tell me so. That she-devil of a woman is everywhere; the day before yesterday she was dining at the Petit Cercle.

[^1]: The Théâtre des Italiens (Théâtre-Italien), Paris's premier venue for Italian opera.

%% 083.0083 %%

But after all — I need no one. I have my painting. Perhaps that is madness, but I do not think so.
