# Thursday, 9 January 1879

%% 083.0566 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% Nous sommes en concours depuis lundi mais le modèle est affreux, je leur ai dit que j'aimerais mieux faire un potage que cette tête-là. %%
We have been in competition since Monday, but the model is frightful — I told them I would rather make a soup than paint that head.

%% 083.0567 %%
%% [#Mme_Gavini](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/MME_GAVINI.md) %%
%% Ce soir second grand festival de musique. %% 2026-03-08T14:25:00 TR: "éclairé à l'éléctricité" = lit by electricity — a genuine marvel in January 1879; electric lighting was only beginning to appear in public venues. "Tout Paris" = le tout-Paris, fashionable society. %%

This evening a second grand music festival. It is organized at the Hippodrome, which holds more than fifteen thousand spectators and is lit by electricity. All Paris was there. I was with the Gavinis — but dressed in a manner that was, to say the least, insignificant: neither pretty nor distinguished.

Massenet a dû passer un bon moment quand on lui a fait une ovation après son troisième acte du "Roi de Latore" (fort peu apprécié et mal compris à l'Opéra). %%
