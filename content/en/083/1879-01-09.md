# Thursday, 9 January 1879

%% 083.0574 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Art_practice](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/ART_PRACTICE.md) %%
%% Nous sommes en concours depuis lundi mais le modèle est affreux, je leur ai dit que j'aimerais mieux faire un potage que cette tête-là. %%
The competition has been on since Monday, but the model is frightful — I told them I would rather make a soup than paint that head.

%% 2026-10-01T07:27:29 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
This evening a second grand music festival. It is held at the Hippodrome, which holds more than fifteen thousand spectators and is lit by electricity. It is a sort of musical Salon, like the Salon of painting. All Paris was there. I was with the Gavinis — but dressed in a manner that was, to say the least, insignificant: neither pretty nor distinguished. The effect fell flat.

Massenet a dû passer un bon moment quand on lui a fait une ovation après son troisième acte du "Roi de Latore" (fort peu apprécié et mal compris à l'Opéra). %%

I say nothing of the music, since I was with Mme Gavini and had to chatter and listen to nonsense. I should like to treat myself to a good evening at the Opéra, but alone or with de Daillens, so as to listen. Massenet must have had a fine moment when they gave him an ovation after his third act of *The King of Lahore*[^083.0576.1] (very little appreciated and poorly understood at the Opéra).
