# Monday, 6 January 1879

%% 083.0552 %%
%% [#Paris](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/PARIS.md) [#Paul_de_Cassagnac](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/CASSAGNAC.md) [#LOUISE_BRESLAU](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/LOUISE_BRESLAU.md) [#Amelie](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/AMELIE.md) %%

There is a little riot at the atelier. I did a Cassagnac, as usual on these occasions — and as those women are unaccustomed to speeches, they were left speechless. Ah! Breslau is very sorry now that she neglected me. Amélie the ferocious, Amélie the greedy, and the other one holds her tongue.

%% 083.0553 %%
%% [#Mme_Gavini](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/MME_GAVINI.md) [#Princesse_Mathilde](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/PRINCESSE_MATHILDE.md) %%
%% Je me suis promenée avec Mme Gavini. Cette lucarne ouverte sur le monde m'énerve plus qu'on peut dire. En rentrant seule, vers cinq heures il faisait noir, j'ai pleuré dans la voiture. Non voyez-vous, je ne suis pas capable de me faire pour rien qu'avec de la chance comme celles... qui l'ont pû. Je ne sais ni quoi ni comment faire ! Je n'ai pas cet esprit-là !... et tous les autres vont s'en aller faute de celui-là. Mme Gavini était hier chez la princesse Mathilde qui reçoit tout ce qui est élégant et intelligent et artiste. C'est une petite cour. J'avais une belle voix qui pouvait m'ouvrir bien des portes, une chanteuse de salons avec une voix pareille serait un trésor... Mais je n'ai plus cette voix, je n'ai rien qu'une souffrance basse, humiliante, à nulle autre pareille. %%
%% 2026-03-08T14:20:00 TR: "lucarne ouverte sur le monde" = skylight onto the world — a narrow aperture offering a glimpse of what Marie cannot fully access. Princesse Mathilde Bonaparte (1820–1904), Napoleon I's niece, held one of the most prestigious artistic salons in Paris. %%
%% 2026-10-01T07:27:29 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
I walked with Mme Gavini. This skylight onto the world exasperates me more than I can say. Coming home alone towards five o'clock, when it was dark, I cried in the carriage. No, you see, I am not capable of making my way with nothing but luck, like those women... who have managed it. I know neither what to do nor how to do it! I have not that turn of mind!... and all the others will go for want of that one. Yesterday Mme Gavini was at Princess Mathilde's; she receives all that is elegant, intelligent and artistic. It is a small court. I had a beautiful voice that could have opened many doors for me; a drawing-room singer with such a voice would be a treasure... But I no longer have that voice; I have nothing but a low, humiliating suffering like no other.

%% 083.0554 %%
%% Je vais faire une complainte comme celle d'Audiffret. La Robinson... elle n'est pas mariée, elle a trente ans, à ce qu'on dit, mais elle s'est amusée elle, elle a dansé, elle a vu le monde. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:22 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶2141, Mon Journal t.12 p.191 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
I am going to write a lament like Audiffret's. La Robinson... she is not married, she is thirty, so they say, but she at least has had her fun; she has danced, she has seen the world.

%% 083.0555 %%
%% Mais est-il vraiment possible que Dieu soit sourd ! Dieu de la clémence, de la bonté, de la miséricorde... pour moi il faut dire misère et corde comme pour le Surprenant. Je suis lasse, lasse, j'en ai assez ! Je ne sais ce qui me retient d'aller me jeter du haut de l'Arc de Triomphe ! C'est que je veux laisser ma statue et si je prends ce délai je n'aurai peut-être plus le courage. %%
%% 2026-03-08T14:20:00 TR: "laisser ma statue" = leave my statue — create a lasting work of art as sole reason to delay death. The reasoning is circular: she lives to create, but may lose the will to die if she waits. %%
%% 2026-10-01T07:27:29 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
%% 2026-10-01T07:27:29 TR: «miséricorde… misère et corde» — the pun (mercy split into misery and rope, the hangman's rope) cannot be carried into English; the French words are kept in italics with a gloss. %%
But is it really possible that God is deaf! God of clemency, of goodness, of mercy... for me one must say *misère et corde*, misery and the rope, as for the Surprenant. I am weary, weary, I have had enough! I do not know what keeps me from throwing myself from the top of the Arc de Triomphe! It is that I wish to leave my statue behind — and if I allow myself that delay, I may no longer have the courage.

%% 083.0556 %%
%% Comment sont arrivées tant de femmes ?! Parbleu c'est clair; ce sont celles qui n'avaient rien à perdre, alors c'est aisé ! Mais moi j'ai juste assez de bagage pour qu'il me cloue à ma place. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:22 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶2144, Mon Journal t.12 p.192 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
How have so many women succeeded?! Why, it is obvious: they are the ones who had nothing to lose, so it is easy! But I have just enough baggage to keep me nailed in my place.

%% 083.0557 %%

%% 2026-10-01T07:27:29 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
The senatorial elections went well — Republican. The Gers alone misbehaved, which makes me fear a success for Cassagnac... By the way, no: if it goes on, I shall tell you afterwards.
