# Wednesday, 12 June 1878
%% Madame Gavini a fait une visite à maman, j'étais sortie chez mes habilleurs avec ma tante et Dina et Marie. %%
Mme Gavini paid a call on Maman; I had gone out to my dressmakers with my aunt and Dina and Marie.

%% Monsieur Rouher m'a étonnée en plusieurs choses. %% 2026-10-01T17:52:03 RED: RESOLVED seam checked (080.DROPPED-0609 joined to 080.0579) — English reads as one continuous paragraph against _original; nothing duplicated or lost at the join. %%
M. Rouher astonished me in several respects. First by his vigour: he whom I had imagined grave, slow, decrepit — he was leaping from the carriage, offering his arm, paying the cab, running up the steps. And then by his ideas. Half-education, he says, produces a sense of one's rights and the absolute negation of all authority; it is impossible for everyone to be fully educated unless they were born that way, which no one has yet invented. He thus proclaims the benefits of ignorance (while saying it is very difficult to decide) and declares that newspapers are poison loose in the public street.

%% 2026-10-01T07:10:00 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
You can imagine how curiously I studied him and listened to him — the Vice-Emperor. But I need not give you my assessment here: first because I did not see enough of him, and secondly because I am not in the mood for it this evening. He told us several curious things that he is perfectly placed to know — about the attempt of '67 against our Emperor, and then things about his imperial family — and asked if we knew the Prince Imperial. No, of course not, but... this very day. How foolish to tell everything in advance — and besides, it is not little Badinguet we are talking about. You may well imagine I was orthodox with the master of the Bonapartists; I am even astonished at my delicate flatteries and my tact. Gavini and the Baron Larrey seemed to approve of me entirely, and M. Rouher himself was pleased — had he not been, he would simply have walked off and left us there. But — what a damp squib!!!
