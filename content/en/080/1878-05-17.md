To see nothing but this wretched harem — Marie, Dina, Maman, my aunt — in the midst of whom strolls, drags herself, the favourite sultana with her guzlas, her trombones, her devil-knows-whats!

%% 080.0171 %%
%% [#Politics](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/POLITICS.md) %%
%% Si j'étais une autre je le mènerais et nous aurions un tas de chiens bonapartistes, et si cette autre avait *mon esprit*... (si vous saviez comme mon esprit m'assomme et m'énerve !) elle deviendrait un centre, une sorte de célébrité... Mais c'est moi... Je suis charmante, bien faite, spirituelle, instruite, mais... If I were someone else I would lead him, and we would have a pack of Bonapartist dogs; and if that other had *my wit*... (if you knew how my wit wearies and exasperates me!) she would become a centre, a sort of celebrity... But it is I. I am charming, well-made, witty, educated — but... It is I, and all of that counts for nothing.
