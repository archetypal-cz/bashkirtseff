# Wednesday, 5 June 1878
%% Filippini a reçu ma lettre trop tard, il me l'écrit. Je cours chez de Daillens puis mille courses, puis Fio arrive, je l'embrasse, lui donne des photographies et je [ne] vais pas à l'Exposition avec elle. %%
Filippini received my letter too late — he writes to tell me. I dash round to de Daillens's, then a thousand errands, then Fio arrives; I embrace her, give her photographs, and do not go to the Exhibition with her.

%% 080.0419 %%
%% [#Salon](../../_original/_glossary/culture/art/SALON.md) %%
%% [#DE_DAILLENS](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/DE_DAILLENS.md) %%
%% Mais je vais au Salon avec de Daillens, elle fait de l'esprit à outrance, autrement est assez amusante. %%
But I go to the Salon with de Daillens — she overdoes her witticisms, but is otherwise quite amusing.

%% 080.0420 %%
%% [#Laferriere](../../_original/_glossary/culture/fashion/LAFERRIERE.md) [#Mme_de_Lesseps](../../_original/_glossary/people/aristocracy/MME_DE_LESSEPS.md) [#Versailles](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/VERSAILLES.md) [#Docteur_Michel](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/DOCTEUR_MICHEL.md) [#Multedo](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/MULTEDO.md) [#Yorke](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/YORKE.md) [#de_Tanlay](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/DE_TANLAY.md) [#Lindemann](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/LINDEMANN.md) %%
%% [#Fio](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/FIO.md) %%
%% Puis chez Laferrière, puis c'est-à-dire avant, Fio va me faire avoir des billets par Mme de Lesseps pour samedi à Versailles. [Le] Docteur Michel est chargé de la même commission. En outre j'en demanderai à Multedo et à Yorke qui en demandera à de Tanlay, à Lindemann, au diable. %%
Then to Laferrière's — or rather, before that: Fio is going to get me tickets through Mme de Lesseps for Saturday at Versailles. Dr Michel has been charged with the same errand. Moreover I shall ask Multedo, and Yorke, who will ask de Tanlay, Lindemann, the devil himself.

%% 080.0421 %%
%% [#Cassagnac](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/CASSAGNAC.md) [#Le_Figaro](../../_original/_glossary/culture/newspapers/LE_FIGARO.md) %%

%% Samedi on discute l'élection de Cassagnac et il prend la parole. Voici la petite note du "Figaro". %%
On Saturday Cassagnac's election is to be debated, and he is to speak. Here is the brief notice from *Le Figaro*:

> M. Paul de Cassagnac takes the floor and requests the Chamber to be so good as to[^080.0422.1]

[^080.0422.1]: *Le Figaro*, no. 156, Wednesday 5 June 1878, p. 2 — [read the issue on Gallica (BnF)](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k276749p/f2.item). The note is part of Albert Millaud's «Gazette de la Chambre», reporting the sitting of 4 June 1878.

> fix Saturday for the debate on the report concerning his election. The young deputy has barely three days to prepare his defence, for he knows he is condemned, and he does not believe the Chamber will deny him — him! — the modest delay granted to ordinary men condemned to death

> With genuine good grace, the Chamber accedes to this supreme wish and fixes the debate for Saturday.

I want to go to Versailles just as I wanted to go to the Foreign Ministry ball.

Tomorrow!.. But this evening I am going to the ball — tomorrow, we shall see tomorrow.
