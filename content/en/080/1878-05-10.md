# Friday, 10 May 1878
%% Les Suédoises m'ont raconté que Mme Boje s'est liée avec son professeur de chant et va aborder l'opéra. Son mari et ses enfants retournent en Suède et sa mère Mme Indebetten vient habiter avec elle. %%
The Swedish ladies told me that Mme Boje has taken up with her singing teacher and is going to try opera. Her husband and children are returning to Sweden, and her mother Mme Indebetten is coming to live with her.

Julian, who is a Bonapartist, came to talk with me about the pit in which Paul de Cassagnac finds himself. Defections everywhere. I have followed the appearances of the pamphlets, the letters, the articles. As for him, he has burned his bridges too often to be able to turn back.

%% Il restera tout seul si cela continue. %%
He will be left all alone if this goes on.
