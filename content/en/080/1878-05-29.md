# Wednesday, 29 May 1878
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Mme_Lacon](../../_original/_glossary/people/aristocracy/MME_LACON.md) [#cabaret_Ledoyen](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/CABARET_LEDOYEN.md) [#Lady_Lacon](../../_original/_glossary/people/aristocracy/LADY_LACON.md) [#M_Foster](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/M_FOSTER.md) %%
%% A six heures la petite Mme Lacon (Laiconn) arrive et m'enlève dîner au cabaret Ledoyen, puis au cirque qui m'a amusée. %%
At six o'clock the little Mme Lacon (pronounced Laiconn) arrives and carries me off to dinner at the Ledoyen restaurant, then to the circus, which amused me.

%% 080.0322 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% Nous étions sept : Lady Lacon, ses deux fils, sa fille, sa belle-fille et M. Foster. La conversation était très anglaise entre les messieurs et les dames, mais entre femmes on s'est dit des grivoiseries comme des Françaises. D'ailleurs ce sont des Anglaises apprivoisées. %%
%% 2026-10-01T17:52:03 RED: RESOLVED seam checked (080.DROPPED-0342 joined to 080.0322) — English reads as one continuous paragraph against _original; nothing duplicated or lost at the join. %%
We were seven: Lady Lacon, her two sons, her daughter, her daughter-in-law, and M. Foster. The conversation was very English between the gentlemen and the ladies — but among the women we said risqué things to each other like Frenchwomen. Besides, they are tamed Englishwomen.

%% Je ne crois pas que Fio ait fait un mariage d'amour, et ma foi j'aime encore mieux ces mariages-là en France ou à défaut de sentiments il y a une sorte d'élégance tandis qu'en Angleterre il n'y a que de l'honnêteté. %%
I do not think Fio made a love match, and upon my word I prefer such marriages in France, where in lieu of feeling there is a sort of elegance — whereas in England there is only honesty.

You know better than anyone whether I am deceiving myself about the passion I inspire in Cassagnac. But no matter — he resembles me; that is to say, he is possessive, touchy, and like the dog lying on the hay, who will not eat it himself and will not let the goats eat it either.
