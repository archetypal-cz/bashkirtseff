I have a migraine... And during the sitting old Robert-Fleury, the father, came by as if by chance — though I rather think it was to give advice on the portrait; I am not sorry, for the old man is far abler than his old bachelor of a son. My head aches so badly that I lie down for twenty minutes, which cures me completely, and as the Gavinis came in the evening with Géry to take us to the *café chantant*, we go. Agathe drops out on the way. For a moment I thought I was going to be ill — not a bit of it.
