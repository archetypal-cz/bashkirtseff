# Sunday, 21 July 1878

%% 081.0506 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%

%% Certes je ne pense pas à Multedo, eh bien j'en ai rêvé cette nuit et ce matin je reçois les vers que voici: la Rose m'envoie un parfum, le troubadour sa chanson, Multedo, sa composition.[^2] %%
%% 2026-10-01T07:33:53 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
I certainly do not think of Multedo — and yet I dreamt of him last night, and this morning I receive the following verses: the Rose sends me a perfume, the troubadour his song, Multedo his composition.[^2]

[^2]: Multedo's comic poem casts himself as a troubadour tormented by a cruel lady (the "Duchess of Ferrara"), alluding to the Italian tradition of the courtly lover.

%% 2026-10-01T07:33:53 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
The gallant singer did not please me — his candidacy is tainted by financial considerations. The two princes are the object of every jest at table and often elsewhere. Little Mitta (Dimitri) came to report that they are dirty because they put their finger in their mouth and then hold it up in the air, like sailors. Madame Abaza thinks the elder, the dark one, very handsome; so do I, but he looks very ill. Madame Rembelinsky and I have already divided the two deaf-mutes between us.

%% 2026-10-01T07:33:53 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
I take Auguste, the dark one; she takes Hans, the fair one. We have settled our plan of campaign... So far the princes have not failed to notice that their reserve piques us a little, and pique is such a stupid thing, all the more since they are imperturbable. It is not pride, for last year they made conversation at table with a Jewish woman. One of them is trying out *On dit que l'on te marie*[^3] on the piano, and as he cannot find the ending it gets on my nerves. So, as I was saying, the plan of campaign of Madame Rembelinsky and me consists in pretending to avoid them, not even to notice them. We have begun, and despite this we have met eleven times, whether by chance or because they obligingly contrived it.
%% 2026-10-03T18:00:59 TR: Reading-conventions wave (KRR 2026-10-03): song titles kept in original italics with a gloss footnote (titles rule). %%

[^3]: *On dit que l'on te marie*: "They say you are getting married", a popular French song.

%% Mais demain ou après-demain, aussitôt qu'elle pourra s'arranger ils auront une sérénade qui jouera aussi bien pour nous que pour eux puisqu'ils sont au-dessous de nous, mais j'espère qu'ils comprendront à qui elle s'adresse grâce au choix des morceaux : 1 Marche funèbre de Chopin. 2 Mein lieber Augustchen (valse). 3 Marche funèbre de Beethoven. 4 Marche funèbre de Mendelssohn. 5 Marche funèbre x x x 6 Galop infernal. %%
But tomorrow or the day after, as soon as she can arrange it, they shall have a serenade — which will play as much for us as for them since they are below us — but I hope they will understand to whom it is addressed by the choice of pieces: 1. Funeral March by Chopin. 2. *Mein lieber Augustchen*[^4] (waltz). 3. Funeral March by Beethoven. 4. Funeral March by Mendelssohn. 5. Funeral March x x x. 6. Infernal Galop.
%% 2026-10-03T18:10:00 CON: Para 081.0510 - gloss footnote expanded (the title is the joke: Prince Auguste). %%

[^4]: *Mein lieber Augustchen*: German, "My dear little Augustus", so in the text; a play on the popular Viennese song *O du lieber Augustin*, aimed at Prince Auguste.

We mock them and they mock us in return. But one must do something at Soden... Madame Antonsky takes an interest in the affair, as does everyone else — I am merely following the others' example; you understand that personally I do not trouble myself over these simpletons.
