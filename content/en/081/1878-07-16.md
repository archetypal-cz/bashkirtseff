# Tuesday, 16 July 1878

%% 081.0465 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Multedo](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/MULTEDO.md) %%
%% [#Napoleon](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/NAPOLEON.md) %%
%% Multedo ne sait pas faire ses affaires. Il m'a envoyé ma guitare raccommodée. Vous voyez d'ici la différence qu'aurait causée une guitare neuve dans un joli étui. Rien de tout cela. Non, vrai, pauvre garçon tu n'es pas magnifique et tu as beau ressembler à Napoléon Ier, tu n'en es pas moins un homme qui ne sait pas faire ses affaires, sans parler de politesse ou de galanterie. %%
Multedo does not know how to manage his affairs. He has sent me my guitar — mended. You can well imagine the difference a new guitar in a pretty case would have made. Nothing of the sort. No, truly, poor boy — you are not generous, and however much you may resemble Napoleon the First, you are nonetheless a man who does not know how to manage his affairs, to say nothing of courtesy or gallantry.

%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Villemessant](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/VILLEMESSANT.md) [#La_Petite_Correspondance](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/LA_PETITE_CORRESPONDANCE.md) [#Multedo](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/MULTEDO.md) [#Soden](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/SODEN.md) %%
%% Sans compter que le son est devenu mauvais après la réparation. Ce cher Multedo. Et comme exprès Villemessant supprime "La Petite Correspondance". Sans cela je ferais imprimer immédiatement que le monsieur qui a cassé une guitare avec... en s'asseyant dessus et qui l'a renvoyée collée est prié de se présenter à Soden pour qu'on la lui casse sur la tête. %%
Not to mention that the tone has gone bad since the repair. Dear Multedo. And as if on purpose, Villemessant has abolished *La Petite Correspondance*.[^1] Otherwise I should have had it printed immediately that the gentleman who broke a guitar by... sitting on it, and who has returned it glued together, is requested to present himself at Soden so that it may be broken over his head.

[^1]: The small ads / personal column of *Le Figaro*, under the editorship of its founder Hippolyte de Villemessant.

The Hohenlohes are not the ones from the Embassy — in any case I trouble myself no more about it, although there is a young man... but all men are indifferent to me at present; I prefer only those who prefer me — and the Deceased. It is only when thinking of others that I find him still within me.

There are things so... frail; they are light shadows that turn into stains once written down... and then I should not like to commit myself too far... suppose the Deceased grows fatter still and becomes a peaceable and reasonable deputy... I doubt it, but still, in that case I should be very sorry to have taken him for the Man.

I still place him in my fantasies — not because he pleases me, but rather as though he did. As for thinking of him in any other way, you will understand that there is nothing of the sort. For me, he is dead — and well dead.

%% S'il grandit je gémirai sur sa mort et me reprocherai de ne l'avoir pas empêché. C'est la vérité je vous en donne ma parole d'honneur. Je regrette de m'en détacher... et je me console en me disant que nous nous reverrons car vraiment je crois que c'est écrit et quoi qu'il arrive j'y reviendrai toujours. Cela m'a semblé pour le Surprenant mais d'une autre manière... Il me semblait que je reviendrai quelque jour à me faire faire la cour par lui à l'enrager. Eh bien, cela n'est rien d'incroyable. C'était si bête quand je pense... je n'ai cessé de le battre froid et le dernier temps quand il venait dans la loge je ne lui parlais pas *pour prévenir ses mépris* que je n'avais pas besoin de craindre, et puis je m'offensais de sa froideur. Le pauvre garçon me faisait une cour des plus banales et se voyant traité si cavalièrement il n'avait rien d'autre à faire qu'à s'en aller... Mais ici il y a trop d'affinités pour que je m'y sois réellement attachée pour que nous puissions ne pas nous rencontrer un jour; Il ne s'agit ni de circonférence ni de couleur, ce sont des attaches, des ressemblances purement morales et toutes puissantes. %%
%% 2026-09-29T01:01:15 RSR: Restored from tome12.docx ¶581, Mon Journal t.12 p.50 (missing from original extraction, 2026-09-28 rebuild). %%
If he grows great, I shall lament his death and reproach myself for not having prevented it. That is the truth, I give you my word of honour. I am sorry to be growing detached from him... and I console myself by saying that we shall meet again, for I truly believe it is written, and whatever happens I shall always come back to it. I felt the same about the Surprising One, but in another way... It seemed to me that one day I should come round to letting him court me till he was frantic. Well, there is nothing incredible in that. It was so stupid, when I think of it... I never stopped giving him the cold shoulder, and towards the end, when he came into the box, I would not speak to him *so as to forestall his contempt*, which I had no need to fear, and then I took offence at his coldness. The poor fellow paid me the most commonplace court, and seeing himself treated so cavalierly he had nothing left to do but go away... But here there are too many affinities, too real an attachment on my part, for us not to meet one day; it is not a matter of girth or of colouring; these are bonds, likenesses that are purely moral and all-powerful.
