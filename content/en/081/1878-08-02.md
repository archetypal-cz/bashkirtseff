> [Wednesday, 31 July]
> *Dona Juana*
> Your age is pitiless, and that is what excuses you, But it is a cruel game, the one that amuses you... Child, you know nothing of love and pain, Of the hurt a flower's thorn can cause, Of the irresistible allure of those charming lips From which you make intoxicating notes spring forth. Fountain of coral with jets of diamonds!
> *And you cannot guess the torments That it pleases you to inflict on a sudden whim To make me pay for one moment of delight, When, ceasing to sing, with a mocking glance And your shrill laughter you pierce my heart.*
> *Faust's counsellor mingled song with laughter, And you too would mock, faithless husband of Elvira, When you sighed out your song so tenderly While your fingers made the instrument snigger. Do not, like them, make this horrible mixture. They were the demons, and you are an angel... No doubt you suffer when you see others suffer, Above all when your hand can bring no help. Do not mock the tears your charms make flow. See my tears pouring out at your feet. Dispel my sorrows. With one word you can.*
> But if nothing touches you... Very well! Then laugh!
