%% 2026-10-01T07:35:44 TR: RESOLVED SOURCE CHANGED (2026-09-28 rebuild) — English re-aligned to the rebuilt French. %%
Madame Antonsky is a woman to be cultivated. She, Madame Doubelt, and Princess Galitzine will be very valuable to me if I ever go to seek my fortune in Petersburg — which is not impossible. It is a good reserve for the future. Let us not forget Madame Voïeykoff either. With that I have more than I need. This evening we talked of all those Petersburg people, Madame Antonsky relating the extravagances of the Basilewsky young ladies, who later became Princess Souvoroff, Mesdames Doubelt, Galve, etc. etc.
