I, however, take the trouble to inform you that you have been, at the very least, wanting in intelligence — not knowing how to judge the people around you, whom you no doubt assess by your own standard, you who are a bad friend and a false brother, which grieves us deeply, above all I, who having taken you seriously did you the honour of considering myself your sister.*
