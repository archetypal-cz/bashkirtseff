Dr. Tomachewsky, who is the physician of the Petersburg Opera, must know something — moreover his opinions agree with those of Fauvel and others; and I myself know that the Soden waters, by their chemical composition, have almost no bearing on my malady. Yesterday at six in the morning my aunt and I, accompanied by Dr. Tomachewsky, went to Ems to consult the doctors there.

%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% L'Impératrice Eugénie est à Ems, pauvre femme. Je déteste Soden parce qu'ennuyeux il m'est inutile, nuisible même, puisque c'est une perte de temps. %%
The Empress Eugénie is at Ems — poor woman.[^1] I detest Soden: being tedious it is useless to me, harmful even, since it is a waste of time.

[^1]: Empress Eugénie (1826-1920), widow of Napoleon III, lived in exile after the fall of the Second Empire in 1870.

%% [Lettre de Multedo du 22 juillet] Mademoiselle, Vos rigueurs à mon endroit me rendent fou. Veuillez donc excuser l'aigreur de ma dernière lettre ; en l'écrivant j'étais furieux des coups cruels d'aiguille que vous m'aviez porté dans la vôtre, et j'ai agi comme un sanglier blessé qui lance aveuglément un coup de boutoir. Pardon ! Je ne crierai plus. Désormais faites-moi souffrir, torturez-moi, repoussez-moi du pied et, si vous m'accordez cette faveur, je me prosternerai devant vous. A. MULTEDO %%
[Letter from Multedo, 22 July.] Mademoiselle, Your severity toward me is driving me mad. Please excuse the acrimony of my last letter — in writing it I was furious at the cruel needle-thrusts you had dealt me in yours, and I acted like a wounded boar launching a blind tusk-blow. Forgive me! I shall cry out no more. Henceforth make me suffer, torture me, push me aside with your foot — and if you grant me this favour, I shall prostrate myself before you. A. MULTEDO.
