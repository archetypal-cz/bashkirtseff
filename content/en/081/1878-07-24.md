Dr Tomachewsky, who is the physician of the Petersburg Opera, must know something — moreover his opinions agree with those of Fauvel and others; and I myself know that the Soden waters, by their chemical composition, have almost no bearing on my malady. If you are not ignorant, you must know that it is convalescents and consumptives who are sent to Soden. Yesterday at six in the morning my aunt and I, accompanied by Dr Tomachewsky, went to Ems to consult the doctors there. We have been back since eight o'clock this morning.

The Empress Eugénie is at Ems — poor woman.[^1] I detest Soden: being tedious it is useless to me, harmful even, since it is a waste of time.

[^1]: Empress Eugénie (1826-1920), widow of Napoleon III, lived in exile after the fall of the Second Empire in 1870.

> Mont-de-Marsan
> 22 July 1878
> Mademoiselle,
> Your severity towards me is driving me mad. You must judge from this how wholly you fill my thoughts, and consequently pity me for my moral sufferings instead of holding my fits of anger against me.
> Please, then, excuse the acrimony of my last letter; in writing it I was furious at the cruel needle-thrusts you had dealt me in yours, and I acted like a wounded boar lashing out blindly with its tusks. You had debased me: I suffered, I howled.
> Forgive me! I shall cry out no more. Henceforth make me suffer, torture me, spurn me with your foot, and, if you grant me that favour, I shall prostrate myself before you.
> I beg you, forget.
> Besides, I foresee it: you are so proud and so haughty that you did not feel wounded, and my retort reached only your supreme disdain. Once again, forgive me.
> I kneel before you with my lips to the ground.
> Respectful homage of my most devoted sentiments.
> A. MULTEDO

You remember this Julie — this madwoman — married to an embassy secretary, living at a breakneck pace for eighteen months, then the sale of all the furniture, bailiffs, the husband's departure for the army, the wife's for Paris where she wished to go on the stage, scandals, miseries, and finally a pistol shot at Boulogne.
