# Friday, 12 July 1878

%% 081.0418 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Hohenlohe](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/HOHENLOHE.md) [#General_Loris_Melikoff](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/GENERAL_LORIS_MELIKOFF.md) [#Lisander](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/LISANDER.md) %%
%% [#Wiesbaden](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/WIESBADEN.md) %%
%% Les Hohenlohe, ambassade d'Allemagne à Paris vont arriver ici. Aussi la famille du général Loris-Mélikoff créée comte après la dernière guerre. Ces derniers ne m'effraient pas. Ce sont des amis des Lisander à Wiesbaden. Je voudrais bien faire connaissance avec les deux et surtout avec les Hohenlohe... En attendant mon calme est troublé. %%
The Hohenlohes — from the German Embassy in Paris — are coming here. Also the family of General Loris-Mélikoff, ennobled as count after the last war.[^1] These latter do not intimidate me — they are friends of the Lisanders at Wiesbaden. I should dearly like to make the acquaintance of both, and above all of the Hohenlohes... Meanwhile my calm is disturbed.
%% 2026-03-08T12:00:00 TR: "la dernière guerre" — the Russo-Turkish War of 1877-78; rendered with footnote. "créée comte" — given hereditary title; rendered "ennobled as count." %%

[^1]: The Russo-Turkish War of 1877-78, in which Russian forces defeated the Ottoman Empire. General Loris-Mélikoff was a prominent commander in that campaign.

Madame Abaza, a small, dark, elegant woman, a little stout, sang today. She claims to have a bad throat and in reality has only swollen glands, in spite of which her voice is very strong and clear. She is unhappy with her husband and, on the pretext of illness, has obtained his leave to stay abroad most of the time. She is common and given to grimacing. I detest those two qualities together.
