At eight o'clock we set off for Wiesbaden — my aunt, Madame Abaza, and I. We go to church and thence to dinner, causing a sensation among the promenaders. We were literally the only women properly dressed. Uncle Lautrec is in Italy, the Batourines are in Petersburg; but we saw the Lisanders. Madame Lisander related a whole year's gossip in half an hour — oh! only a summary... but thrilling.

> *10 Zakariefskaya. Dear and charming Mademoiselle Marie!*
> *This time you will not accuse me of not replying, for I received your letter scarcely two hours ago.*
> *At the moment of reading it I had a visit from His Highness Grand Duke Constantin, and I took advantage of his presence on behalf of your protégé — but the Grand Duke replied that above all one must address oneself to Prince Orloff; I am therefore sending my request to Paris, and as soon as I receive a reply I shall let you know.
