# Monday, 21 June 1880

%% 089.0007 %%
%% [#Paris](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/PARIS.md) [#Cassagnac](../../_original/_glossary/people/recurring/CASSAGNAC.md) [#Le_Figaro](../../_original/_glossary/culture/newspapers/LE_FIGARO.md) %%
%% 2026-03-09T12:00:00 TR: "sale mariage" = dirty/low marriage; Marie's contempt for Cassagnac's match with the bourgeois Acard family. %%
%% Ce qu'il y a de joli c'est le "Figaro" qui dit que la famille Cassagnac s'etait reunie pour le bapteme au chateau de Garges (Seine-et-Oise) chez M. Couvelet le beau-pere de M. de Cassagnac. Non, mais ces Couvelet et ces Acard et ce sale mariage... pouah ! Voila qui demolit singulierement l'idole. %%
The amusing thing is *Le Figaro* reporting that the Cassagnac family gathered for the baptism at the Chateau de Garges (Seine-et-Oise) at the home of M. Couvelet, the father-in-law of M. de Cassagnac. Really, these Couvelets and these Acards and that sordid marriage... ugh! This demolishes the idol rather thoroughly.

%% 089.0008 %%
%% [#Gavini](../../_original/_glossary/people/aristocracy/MADAME_GAVINI.md) [#Soutzo](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/SOUTZO.md) %%
%% 2026-03-09T12:00:00 TR: "couru les magasins" = went from shop to shop; "recluse en plein Paris" = recluse in the middle of Paris (paradox). %%
%% Nous avons couru les magasins de trois a sept heures avec Mme Gavini. Maintenant qu'il n'y a plus Soutzo, recommencent de nouveau ces incroyables soirees de recluse en plein Paris ! %%
We went from shop to shop from three until seven o'clock with Mme Gavini. Now that Soutzo is gone, those incredible evenings of a recluse in the middle of Paris begin anew!

%% 089.0009 %%
%% 2026-03-09T12:00:00 TR: "la belle Odette" = ironic; "faire fortune" = to make one's fortune through marriage or liaisons. %%
%% Madame Randouin a un petit garcon depuis huit jours, nous sommes allees la voir. Le mari est tres content. On dit que l'enfant est de M. Delattre. La belle Odette a fait tout ce qu'elle a pu pour faire fortune et n'a pas reussie. %%
Madame Randouin has had a baby boy for a week now; we went to see her. The husband is very pleased. They say the child is M. Delattre's. The lovely Odette did everything she could to make her fortune and did not succeed.

%% 089.0010 %%
%% 2026-03-09T12:00:00 TR: "genes" = in financial difficulty, embarrassed financially. %%
%% Ils sont tres genes les Randouin quoiqu'entoures de luxe et d'elegance. %%
The Randouins are in financial straits despite being surrounded by luxury and elegance.

%% 089.0011 %%
%% Eh bien, je trouve qu'il est malheureux de n'arriver a rien quand on a fait tous les sacrifices. %%
Well, I find it unfortunate to achieve nothing when one has made every sacrifice.

%% 089.0012 %%
%% 2026-03-09T12:00:00 TR: "je suis morte" = I am dead (figuratively: lifeless, stagnating). Marie contrasts the vibrant life of newspapers with her cloistered existence. %%
%% Quel calme. On devore sept ou huit journaux et puis... mais ce n'est pas les journaux qu'il me faudrait, il faudrait ceux qui ecrivent, ceux dont on ecrit, tout cela enfin, la vie... et je suis morte ici a la maison. %%
What calm. One devours seven or eight newspapers and then... but it is not the newspapers I need; I need those who write them, those about whom they write, all of that, life... and here I am dead at home.
