# Tuesday, 22 June 1880

%% 2026-03-09T12:00:00 TR: "republicaine par conviction" = republican by conviction. Gambetta's amnesty speech of 21 June 1880 at the Chamber of Deputies was a pivotal political moment. Marie declares herself republican and distances herself from Bonapartism. %%
%% Comment n'ai-je rien su de la formidable seance d'hier, ca c'est un tour que m'a jouee ma peinture. Du reste, je savais qu'on discuterait l'amnistie mais qui pouvait prevoir que Gambetta parlerait; aucun journal ne le laissait prevoir. Je courais les boutiques pendant que cet homme extraordinaire parlait. Eh bien ! apres lui il n'y a plus personne. Paul de Cassagnac apparait faible, chetif, un gamin. Si j'aimais Paul de Cassagnac, il ne rentrerait si betement sous terre a mes yeux. Si je n'etais republicaine par conviction, cet homme prodigieux me le ferait devenir. Vous rappelez-vous, il y a plus de deux ans lorsque j'acclamais Cassagnac et que je disais dans mon journal que lui ou un autre pouvaient me rendre bonapartiste ou n'importe quoi par sentiment mais que j'etais republicaine dans l'ame. Cela me fait plaisir, cela prouve que j'ai quelque conviction et cela m'etonne. Et ne me suis-je pas rencontree c'est-a-dire n'ai-je pas devance de deux ans le discours d'hier de Gambetta lorsque je disais un soir que je me mis a songer a cela, n'ai-je pas dit: la Republique de maintenant est la vraie, la longtemps attendue, la definitive, benediction du ciel, enfin venue. %%
How could I have known nothing of yesterday's tremendous session? That is a trick my painting played on me. I knew, of course, that they would be debating the amnesty, but who could have foreseen that Gambetta would speak? No newspaper gave any indication of it. I was running from shop to shop while that extraordinary man was speaking. Well! after him there is no one else. Paul de Cassagnac appears feeble, puny, a boy. If I loved Paul de Cassagnac, he would not so pitifully sink into the earth in my eyes. If I were not already a republican by conviction, that prodigious man would make me one. Do you remember, more than two years ago when I was cheering Cassagnac and said in my diary that he or another could make me a Bonapartist or anything else through sentiment, but that I was a republican in my soul? This pleases me; it proves I have some conviction, and it astonishes me. And did I not anticipate --- that is, did I not precede by two years Gambetta's speech of yesterday when I said one evening, having begun to reflect on these matters, did I not say: the Republic of today is the true one, the long-awaited one, the definitive one, a blessing from heaven, finally arrived.

%% 089.0015 %%
%% 2026-03-09T12:00:00 TR: "[Mots cancelles]" = manuscript note indicating deleted words. %%
%% Qu'importe les exagerations que l'on emploie pour arriver a la mesure juste ? [Mots cancelles]. On reproche le sang, la boue etc., a la Republique. Regardez les commencements de tout, surtout lorsqu'il y a une moitie qui empeche, lutte, gate. Le pays n'est pas un salon. %%
What does it matter the exaggerations one employs to arrive at the just measure? [Words cancelled]. They reproach the Republic for blood, for mud, and so on. Look at the beginnings of everything, especially when half the country hinders, struggles, spoils. The nation is not a salon.

%% 089.0016 %%
%% Que les gens de partis choisissent leurs invites, mais la Republique n'est pas un parti, elle est le Pays. Plus on viendra a elle, plus elle ouvrira ses bras et quand tout le monde sera venu, il n'y aura ni proscrit, ni delaisse, ni choisi, il n'y aura plus de partis, il y aura la France. %%
Let the partisans choose their guests, but the Republic is not a party; it is the Nation. The more who come to it, the more it will open its arms, and when everyone has come, there will be neither the proscribed, nor the abandoned, nor the chosen; there will be no more parties, there will be France.

%% 089.0017 %%
%% Et alors les fautifs en seront punis mais non point renies. Comment cela se pourrait-il ? %%
And then the guilty will be punished but not disowned. How could it be otherwise?

%% 089.0018 %%
%% On fait un crime a la Republique d'avoir des miserables dans ses rangs et quelle est la nation qui n'en a pas ? %%
They make it a crime that the Republic has wretches in its ranks --- and what nation does not?

%% 089.0019 %%

"The Republic is neither a salon nor a coterie. If France became legitimist or imperialist, would they all therefore be pure?"
