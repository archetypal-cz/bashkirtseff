# Sunday, 15 August 1880

%% 089.0342 %%
%% Potin m'a envoyé un bouquet. Nous sommes allées à la messe, puis à cheval; Mlle Moreau, son frère, Potin, Mlle Devick (?) et moi. %%
Potin sent me a bouquet. We went to mass, then riding: Mlle Moreau, her brother, Potin, Mlle Devick (?), and I.

%% 089.0343 %%
%% 2026-03-09T12:00:00 TR: "amazone" = riding habit (women's riding outfit); "édifié sur ma taille les populations" = idiomatic, meaning the locals were impressed/enlightened about her figure. %%
%% Mon amazone va mal mais à la messe j'avais une robe qui a édifié sur ma taille les populations. Je me suis photographiée en paysanne et en costume du traitement. %%
My riding habit fits badly, but at mass I had a dress that enlightened the locals about my figure. I had myself photographed as a peasant and in treatment costume.
