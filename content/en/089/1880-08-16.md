# Monday, 16 August 1880

%% 089.0346 %%
%% A cheval, moi, Devick et Potin. Les promenades sont charmantes ici. %%
Riding: Devick, Potin, and I. The rides are charming here.

%% 089.0347 %%
%% [#Marriage](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/MARRIAGE.md) %%
%% Le prince Napoléon qui voyage avec ses deux fils va venir passer deux ou trois jours ici. Mon traitement est fini mais je reste pour voir ce candidat. Si je n'étais pas moi, Potin me l'aurait fait connaître. %%
Prince Napoleon, who is travelling with his two sons, is coming to spend two or three days here. My treatment is finished, but I am staying to see this candidate. If I were not myself, Potin would have introduced me to him.
