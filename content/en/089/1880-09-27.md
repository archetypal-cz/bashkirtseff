%% 089.0610 %%
# Monday, 27 September 1880

%% 089.0611 %%
%% Ce soir nous allons au Palais-Royal avec les deux vieillards. J'espérais qu'ils amèneraient Gabriel mais ces canailles n'y ont pas songé . %%
This evening we go to the Palais-Royal with the two old men. I had hoped they would bring Gabriel — but the scoundrels never thought of it.
%% 2026-03-09T16:30:00 TR: "canailles" — scoundrels/rascals; affectionately insulting, directed at Saint-Amand and Géry. "Palais-Royal" — the Palais-Royal theatre, known for comedies and vaudeville. %%

%% 089.0612 %%
%% Je vais mieux, mes deux anciens préfets de la pourriture impériale me font du bien. %%
I am better; my two former prefects of the imperial rot do me good.
%% 2026-03-09T16:30:00 TR: "préfets de la pourriture impériale" — prefects of the imperial rottenness; Marie's affectionate insult for Géry and Saint-Amand who served as prefects under Napoleon III; combines fondness with Republican-era disdain for the Second Empire. %%
