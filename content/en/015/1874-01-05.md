# Monday, 5 January 1874

%% 015.0042 %%
%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% [#Monaco](../../_original/_glossary/places/cities/MONACO.md) %%
%% [#Society](../../_original/_glossary/culture/themes/SOCIETY.md) %%
%% Je me décide à ne pas aller à Monaco, ce n'est pas bien qu'on me voit toutes les fois à ce Tir. %%
I decide not to go to Monaco; it is not good for me to be seen every time at the pigeon shooting.

%% 2026-02-27T10:03:00 TR: "Stiopa" = Russian diminutive of Stepan. "Machenka" = diminutive of Maria (Stiopa's wife). "engraissée" = gained weight (1870s: often complimentary for girls). %%
%% Je prenais ma leçon d'anglais lorsque Walitsky passe sa tête à travers la porte: L'oncle Stiopa. Je saute et à la porte rencontre l'oncle Stiopa; après nous avoir embrassés nous allons chez maman où était déjà Machenka. On me trouve engraissée. Stiopa me trouve belle, car il m'aime. %%
I was taking my English lesson when Walitsky puts his head through the door: Uncle Stiopa. I leap up and at the door meet Uncle Stiopa; after embracing us we go to Maman's room where Machenka was already. They find I have put on weight. Stiopa finds me beautiful, for he loves me.

%% 015.0044 %%
%% [#Hitchcock](../../_original/_glossary/people/mentioned/HITCHCOCK.md) %%

%% Ils ont apporté toutes sortes de choses salées, russes, et des confitures. Ça va sans dire que je fiche mon anglais et Hitchcock avec. %%
They have brought all sorts of salted Russian delicacies, and preserves. It goes without saying that I abandon my English lesson and Hitchcock along with it.

%% Cependant à quatre heures je sors avec Bête que j'ai injustement nommée ainsi; mais Bête est plus court que princesse et je le préfère, (robe brune et chapeau *bien*). Il n'y a plus personne, il fait froid et bientôt sombre. Stiopa et Machenka logeront chez Walitsky. A dîner Stiopa disait que je devais absolument me marier avec un grand-duc. %%
However at four o'clock I go out with Bete, whom I have unjustly named thus; but Bete is shorter than princess and I prefer it (brown dress and hat, *good*). There is no one left, it is cold and soon dark. Stiopa and Machenka will lodge with Walitsky. At dinner Stiopa said I absolutely must marry a Grand Duke.

I: It is a pity the Prince of Monaco is married.

%% 015.0051 %%
%% [#Grand_papa](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/GRAND_PAPA.md) [#Russia](../../_original/_glossary/places/countries/RUSSIA.md) %%
%% [#Tante_Etienne](../../_original/_glossary/people/family/TANTE_ETIENNE.md) %%
%% 2026-02-27T10:03:03 TR: "Gamenton, Gamentoff, Khamoutoff" = stages of Russification of "Hamilton." "troika" kept in Russian. "Pierre Ier" = Peter the Great. %%
%% Ensuite on parla des Hamilton russes, comment leur glorieux nom est devenu Gamenton, puis Gamentoff, ensuite Khamoutoff. Papa raconta comme toujours l'histoire de cette lady Hamilton qui fut décapitée sous Pierre Ier. Je me suis empressée de raconter mes goûts; que je n'aime pas les Russes, que je resterai à l'étranger, les courses et tout ce que j'aime enfin. Stiopa sans doute, me persuadait que la Russie est mieux, que les traîneaux avec ==la troïka==[^015.0051.r1] etc. etc. %%
Then we spoke of the Russian Hamiltons, how their glorious name became Gamenton, then Gamentoff, then Khamoutoff. Papa told as always the story of that Lady Hamilton who was beheaded under Peter the Great. I hastened to declare my tastes: that I do not like the Russians, that I shall remain abroad, the races and everything I love, in short. Stiopa of course was trying to persuade me that Russia is better, that the sleighs with ==the troika==[^015.0051.r1] etc. etc.

I played *Le Ruisseau*[^1] which was not understood. In short, since their arrival I come, I go, I play, I talk, and I do nothing. Tomorrow is our Christmas; they say one must ==tell the future==[^015.0052.r1]. One of the ==divinations==[^015.0052.r2] is to place a plate full of water under the bed, and one will dream [Crossed out: of the one who will be the fut] of one's future husband.

I want to try it.
