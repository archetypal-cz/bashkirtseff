%% 2026-02-09T22:00:00 RSR: The sprain is confirmed as a real "entorse." Marie spends the day drawing, playing mandolin and piano "par boutades" (in fits and starts), and reflects beautifully on art: "On oublie tout pour ne penser qu'a ce qu'on fait, on regarde ces contours, ces ombres avec respect, avec attendrissement; on cree, on se sent presque grand." Paul Poire, physics professor at Lycee Fontanes (later Lycee Condorcet), is Fauvel's cousin -- Marie has recruited him to create a "catalogue chronologique des auteurs connus a partir du commencement du monde," an ambitious intellectual project. Remy stays two hours discussing geology. Marie's list of "interesting and clean" diseases is characteristic: migraine, sprains, fever (even typhoid), but "jamais de poitrine!" -- a tragically ironic prohibition given her eventual death from tuberculosis. "Quatorze Florentins gisants sur la cheminee" refers to photographs from Florence. %%
