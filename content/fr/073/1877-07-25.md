%% 2026-02-09T22:00:00 RSR: Two events frame this entry: Marie sprains her ankle jumping from the train at Enghien (limping "comme Gaston l'annee passee") and Georges's creditors pursue her aunt. The centerpiece is Marie's brilliantly comic letter to Marcuard, reproduced in full. She has drawn Alexandre, Marcuard and Melissano as Swiss heroes ("les Horaces, le Serment de Grutli") in Guillaume Tell costumes. The letter is mock-formal, allusive, playful: she complains of the Enghien cure, her mandolin's "sons plaintifs," misquotes Dante's Inferno ("Intra calor che son sospesi" -- should be "Tra color"), and invents a narrative about "Euterpe" the harpist fainting at the sight of Larderei. The "Oeil-de-boeuf" and "Talon-rouge" references mock aristocratic affectation. She returns Alexandre's photograph in a grand envelope sealed with five red wax seals, addressed "Suisse, Berne. Valeur trois mille francs." Cost: seven francs. "Je m'amuse bien a l'idee de l'effet que produiront ces valeurs." %%
