%% [#Kernberger](../../_original/_glossary/culture/literature/KATHERINE_KERNBERGER.md) %%
%% 2026-02-09T23:00:00 RSR: Cassagnac cancels dinner because his sister is giving birth; he accompanies his mother to Coulommiers (Seine-et-Marne). Marie, Berthe, and Blanc make the best of the disappointment — the "femme hongroise" joke sung with variations until midnight. Marie plays mandoline, harps, guitar, piano — a one-woman concert out of frustrated energy. She sings "Badinguet" (derogatory song about Napoleon III) and "Ah! qu'il est bien mon superbe Nubien" (song about a handsome dark man — implying Cassagnac). They draft a mock congratulatory letter to "Coulommiers" from "les familles Bashkirtseff, Romanoff, Babanine, Boyd, Blanc" — but Blanc doesn't send it. %%
