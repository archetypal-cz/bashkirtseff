%% 2026-02-09T14:15:00 RSR: The most dramatic entry of the carnet — Antonelli's sustained declaration of love during a horseback ride to the Farnesina hills. They climb a trackless mountain, and at the summit among shepherds (Marie evokes Faustulus and Acca Laurentia, parents of Romulus), Antonelli declares his love repeatedly. Marie threatens him with her riding whip but is deeply moved. Then her horse bolts — Antonelli saves her in a terrifying gallop. Afterwards, the "verb to love is declined on every tone." Antonelli reveals he was first struck at the opera (La Vestale), describes thinking of her at 3 AM at his club. The scene is one of the most cinematically vivid in Marie's diary. %%
