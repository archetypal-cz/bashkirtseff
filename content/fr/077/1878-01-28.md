He gives each woman a character reading: the "colonelle" (Dina) is serene and indifferent, "Georges" (Berthe) is satisfied and sceptical, the "novice" (Yorke) is either perfectly innocent or perfectly perverse, and Marie is "the only one with whom something could happen." The Boyds had been groped at the Opéra ball and danced the cancan. %%
