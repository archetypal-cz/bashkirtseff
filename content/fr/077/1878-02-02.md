%% 2026-02-09T23:00:00 RSR: A dense entry mixing emotional turmoil with marriage calculations. Marie opens feeling something is missing, then the "femme hongroise" gives her nerves. The plan: go to Italy and petition King Humbert and Queen Margherita to restore Bourbon de Naples properties (confiscated at Italian unification). Marie's remarkable philosophical passage on "mariage sans amour" invokes her split self — the "first me" who acts and the "second me" who watches, remembers Epictetus, and would forbid taking a lover. Mme Yorke and Berthe spread rumors that Cassagnac is in love with Marie. Soirée with many visitors: Fayet, the prince, Beaurepaire, Comte d'Abzac (cousin of the marquis d'Abzac at the Élysée). Edmond = a fortune-teller or clairvoyant whose predictions Marie takes seriously. Mme Boyd may fulfill Edmond's prediction of a woman starting with "B" who would introduce Marie to society. %%
