# Works glossary — stage 2: research and write entries (owner 2026-10-10)

The owner wants a glossary page for every work Marie mentions — paintings, sculptures, plays, operas, books, poems, songs — with **full images** (paintings/sculptures) and **links to full texts** (books, plays, libretti, poems, scores) where they are public domain. Read first: `.claude/skills/glossary/SKILL.md` (frontmatter, `images:` and `full_text:` sections, citation rules), `.claude/skills/researcher/SKILL.md` (research standards), one good existing entry, e.g. `content/_original/_glossary/culture/art/UN_MEETING.md` (Marie's work, with images) and `content/_original/_glossary/culture/theater/FAUST.md`.

Your batch: a JSON list (path in your task) of works from the inventory: title, creator, type, Marie's spellings (`marie_forms`), the paragraphs that mention it (`mentions`), an existing glossary file if any (`existing_glossary`), and notes (doubtful identifications, wrong links).

## For each work

1. **Identify it properly** — read the mentioning paragraphs in `content/_original/CCC/*.md` (grep the IDs) and the RSR/LAN notes there; confirm the work, creator, date, first performance / Salon / publication, and what Marie says about it (when, where she saw/read it). Resolve or flag the doubts in `notes`. If the inventory merged two different works, split them.
2. **Write the entry** in `content/_original/_glossary/`:
   - Existing **proper** entry for the work (culture/…): enrich it in place.
   - Existing **stub filed under people/ or places/**, or an entry that is really the author (DANTE, PLUTARQUE…): do NOT move or delete files and do not rewrite an author entry into a work. Create the work entry under the right `culture/` subfolder (art for paintings/sculpture/drawing, literature for books/novels/poems, theater for plays/opera/operetta/ballet, music for songs/concert music), and record in your report which old stub it supersedes (the lead moves/merges and re-links later with the glossary tools).
   - New entry: `culture/<sub>/<ID>.md`, ID in CAPITAL_ASCII from the usual title (e.g. `JEANNE_D_ARC_BASTIEN_LEPAGE`, `LA_TRAVIATA`); `grep -ril` first so you never create a duplicate of an existing file.
   - Frontmatter: `id`, `name` (usual title), `aliases` (include every form in `marie_forms` and common variants — the tagger uses them), `type: Culture`, `category`, `research_status`, `last_updated: 2026-10-10`, plus `images:` / `full_text:` below.
   - Body (English, like the rest of the glossary): what the work is (creator, date, genre/medium, where it is now / first performed), why it mattered in Marie's time, **Marie and this work** (what she says, with the paragraph IDs and dates), and sources. **Every fact from a secondary source is cited** (Wikipedia, BnF, museum catalogue, Kernberger 2013…). No invented facts; if unsure, say so.
3. **Images (paintings, sculptures, drawings, also illustrations for stage works if a PD poster/photo exists):** only public-domain or CC0/CC-BY files, best from Wikimedia Commons or the museum's open-access image. Download the original-size file with curl, then resize to ≤1600 px on the long side, JPEG quality 82, target < 500 KB (`uv run --quiet --with pillow python -c …`). Save to `src/frontend/public/images/works/<kebab-id>.jpg` (Marie's own works: `src/frontend/public/images/marie/works/`). Add `images:` with `src`, `caption` (title, artist, year, medium, holder), `credit` (holder + licence, e.g. «Musée d'Orsay; public domain, via Wikimedia Commons»), `link` (Commons or museum page), and a real `alt`. No image if the work is lost or not PD — say so in the text.
4. **Full texts (books, novels, poems, plays, libretti, scores):** `full_text:` links to free copies — Wikisource (fr/en/…), Gallica (BnF), Project Gutenberg, Internet Archive, HathiTrust full view, IMSLP (scores, often libretti). Prefer the original language and an edition of Marie's time; give `label`, `source`, `language`. Open each URL (`curl -sIL`) to check it resolves; never guess a URL.
5. **Marie's own works** (batches «own»): follow the existing system for her works — `marie_work: true` and the `work:` block (year, medium, status public/attributed/private/contested/unknown, location) as in `content/_original/_glossary/culture/art/*.md`; check `MARIE_BASHKIRTSEFF_WORKS.md` and the 26 existing entries so you do not duplicate (a project or an unfinished picture is still worth an entry if Marie writes about it repeatedly; say «project» / «unfinished» / «lost»). Known traps: the Kharkiv losses were looted/burned in 1943 (not «bombed at Gavrontsi»); Salon 1880 signature «Marie Constantin Russ», 1881 «Andrey»; mention honorable 1883.
