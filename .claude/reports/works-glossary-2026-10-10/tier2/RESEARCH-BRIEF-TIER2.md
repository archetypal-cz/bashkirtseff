## What differs from tier 1

1. **Check the glossary state today, not the inventory's.** Since 2026-10-10 the glossary went through merges, moves, ~510 stub write-outs and a note cleanup. Before creating a file: `grep -ril "<title words>" content/_original/_glossary/`, look at every `glossary_hits` path, and read the two mention paragraphs (`grep -n "CCC.NNNN" content/_original/CCC/*.md`, then the lines after the ID) with their RSR/LAN notes and footnotes.
   - A proper entry for the work exists under `culture/` → enrich it in place (keep its id, add aliases, images/full_text, a «Marie and …» section with the paragraph IDs).
   - The hit is an **author or artist entry** (BONNAT, GREUZE, MICHEL_ANGE, PAILLERON, ROCHEGROSSE, GEORGES_BERTRAND, JOHN_SINGER_SARGENT, AIVAZOWSKY, AULU_GELLE…) → do not turn it into the work; create the work entry under `culture/<sub>/`, link the person entry from the body with a relative link.
   - The hit is an **empty auto-stub filed under people/ or places/** (e.g. `people/mentioned/ZAMPA.md`, `places/theaters/LE_REVEILLON.md`) → do not move, edit or delete it; create the work entry under `culture/<sub>/`; if the natural id collides, suffix the creator (`LUCREZIA_BORGIA_DONIZETTI`, as tier 1 did with `AIDA_VERDI`); record the stub in your report's `supersedes` (the lead merges and re-links with the glossary tools).
   - A paragraph already tagged with a different, wrong entry (a character, a person of the same name) → note it in `doubts`; do not edit diary files.
2. **Result notes only (owner ruling 2026-10-22).** No process notes anywhere in the entry: no «Created during tier 2», «batch», «inventory», «stub», «supersedes», wave names or timestamps-as-prose. Put doubts in a short «Notes and doubts» section only when they are about the work or the identification (genuinely uncertain → say so plainly). Facts from secondary sources carry their citation inline (Wikipedia «Title»; museum catalogue; Kernberger 2013; Mon Journal index…).
3. **Dates and paths.** `last_updated: 2026-11-10`. Reports go to `.claude/reports/works-glossary-2026-10-10/tier2/report-NN.json` (NN = your batch number), scratch to `~/.cache/works/scratch-t2-NN/`. Images: `src/frontend/public/images/works/<kebab-id>.jpg`, ≤1600 px long side, JPEG q82, < 500 KB, public domain / CC0 / CC-BY only, `credit` names holder + licence, `link` to the Commons/museum page, real `alt`. Resize with `uv run --quiet --with pillow python -c …` (Pillow works here).
4. **Links.** Wikimedia Commons, Wikisource, Internet Archive, Project Gutenberg, HathiTrust and IMSLP resolve from this machine. **Gallica answers 403 to curl** here — try `curl -sIL -A "Mozilla/5.0" <url>`; if it still does not answer 200, do not include that Gallica link (never guess an ark). Open every `full_text` url and every image source before you write it down.
5. **Body shape.** English, like the glossary. Use paragraph clusters (`%% GLO_<ID>.NNNN %%` before every heading and paragraph) for every new or substantially rewritten entry. Sections: Overview (what it is, creator, date, genre/medium, where it is / first performed, why it mattered then) · Marie and <work> (what she says, with dates and paragraph IDs, short French quotes) · Sources / Notes and doubts. Aliases: every `marie_forms` spelling plus common variants (the tagger uses them) — but not bare common words that would mis-tag (an alias like «Valentine» or «Algeria» is a trap; prefer the full title).
6. **Mentions may be incomplete** (the inventory was keyword-based). If you find further clear mentions of the work while reading, list them in your report as `extra_mentions` — do not tag anything yourself.
