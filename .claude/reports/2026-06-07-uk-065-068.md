## Proposed Changes

1. **Promote proper-noun consistency to a mechanical check.** Three name-spelling divergences this wave were all TM-pinned forms that drifted in concurrent carnets and were caught only by an alert CON. Consider a `verify-carnet`/repo lint that flags known TM-locked proper nouns appearing in a non-canonical but valid-Cyrillic spelling (e.g. Башкирцев/Башкірцев, Олександр/Александр-not-Dumas, Ерістов/Еристов). This is the UK analogue of the link-health gate: invisible to reading review, catchable by tooling.
2. **TM-append ownership in concurrent waves.** Codify in the translator skill: when other translators are active, append your new terms to a per-carnet TM section AT FILE END (tr-b did this cleanly) rather than skipping the append and delegating — reduces reliance on a downstream agent remembering to fold terms.
3. **The stale-re-delivery + re-ping pattern** for review agents is now well-understood; no skill change needed, but worth keeping the "re-ping with explicit task id if a review agent idles after a stale re-delivery" note in the ED playbook.
