## Issues Encountered

1. **Terminology conflict — translator invented a form, blamed TranslationMemory** (`terminology` / `agent-false-claim`). tr-078 rendered Breslau as «Брауль» (×3) and reported it had used "the TranslationMemory.md entry." Ground-truth check: **TM had no Breslau entry at all** — the form was invented. Repo canonical is Брезло (54×). Resolved: RED normalized Брауль→Брезло, and a Breslau→Брезло line was **added to TranslationMemory.md** (plus a Валіцький guard) so it can't recur. *Reinforces the standing "verify mechanical agent claims against disk before acting" lesson.*

2. **Single-writer overlap — RED edited a carnet after CON started** (`single-writer`). RED applied a late Валицький→Валіцький normalization to 3 files of 078 *after* marking its RED task complete and *after* CON had begun 078. Team-lead closed it: RED permanently off 078, CON told to re-read the 3 files. CON confirmed **no clobber** — RED touched body name-spelling, CON touched frontmatter + verdict comments (orthogonal). Zero data loss, but the hazard was real; the "treat a carnet as closed once its RED task is marked complete" rule was restated to RED.

3. **Gate blind spot — pure-Latin words in Cyrillic text** (`gate-gap`). `verify-carnet`'s `latin-in-cyr` check only flags *mixed-script* tokens (e.g. «смієтьcя»); it misses **pure-Latin words** sitting in Cyrillic lines (syn, prince, Marie-in-a-footnote). RED ran its own pure-Latin scan as a safety net and fixed several in 079. Team-lead confirmed the gap on sealed 078 too (residual `chaperon`×2, `bis`×2, `Fasquelle` — all judged legit foreign-term/address/publisher cases, not errors). **Proposed: add a pure-Latin-word scan to verify-carnet** (with an allowlist for highlighted quotes, footnote source terms, and proper nouns).

4. **CON missed first ping on the final carnet** (`coordination`, minor). The 080-ready ping didn't move task #9 out of pending; a disk-state check (0/48 conductor_approved) caught it and a direct nudge started CON. *Confirms the standing "watch disk state, not just task state" lesson — also applies to review agents, not just translators.*

## Observations

- **Consistency band is tightening**: 075–077 averaged 0.933; this wave is a flat 0.94 across all three. The up-front terminology lock + CON's pre-read trap-lists are the likely cause — fewer in-draft drifts reaching review.
- **The embedded-document carry-over is the headline win**: the same Talandier incest scandal appears as a legal transcript (078) and a newspaper article (079). Because CON pre-read 079 and flagged the «beau-frère»→«шурин» relation before RED reached it, both renderings match on the load-bearing term. This is exactly what the pipeline overlap is for.
- **RED's self-initiated pure-Latin scan** is a genuinely valuable habit — it found a defect class the mechanical gate is blind to. Worth promoting into the editor skill explicitly until the gate covers it.
- **Translator terminology adherence was mixed mid-flight**: the lock broadcast landed while translators were ~50% done, so early paragraphs kept drift forms (tr-080's Бресло×7, tr-078's Брауль). RED's per-carnet sweep caught all of them, but broadcasting the lock *in the spawn prompt* (as done for the "Alexandre" lock in 075–077) would have avoided the rework.

## Proposed Changes

1. **verify-carnet: add a pure-Latin-word scan** (Issue 3) — flag standalone Latin-script words ≥2 letters on rendered (non-comment, non-highlight, non-footnote-source) lines, with a proper-noun/foreign-term allowlist. This closes the gate's last reading-review-blind defect class for Cyrillic targets. *(architect / gate-backlog)*
2. **Translator spawn prompts: bake the wave's terminology/collision lock into the prompt** (Observation 4), not as a mid-flight broadcast — mirrors the proven 075–077 "Alexandre lock" practice. CON's idle pre-read can produce the list before translators spawn if the conductor is spawned first.
3. **Editor skill: add "run a pure-Latin-word scan" to the RED checklist** (Issue 3 / Observation) as an interim measure until the gate covers it.
4. **Restate review-agent ping robustness** (Issue 4): after pinging a review agent, verify task status flips to in_progress (or disk shows progress) within a few minutes; re-nudge directly if not. The "disk state, not task state" rule already covers translators — extend it explicitly to RED/CON.
