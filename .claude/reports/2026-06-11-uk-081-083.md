## Observations

- **The proactive-pre-read model is the headline.** con2's idle pre-read converted three would-be cleanup problems into in-draft/at-gate catches. The goose-pun in particular — a load-bearing bilingual joke explicitly explained in Marie's text — survived intact because the translators got the Ганс/Ґанс distinction *before* drafting. This is the single highest-leverage practice observed across both waves.
- **In-prompt terminology lock beats mid-flight broadcast** (Round-1 proposal, now validated): 083 (Breslau 35×) came through with zero drift, vs Round-1 where the mid-flight broadcast left early-paragraph drift for RED to sweep. Recommend making this standard.
- **The 85-entry carnet is feasible single-agent** — tr-083 did the project's largest carnet in one pass with no degradation. The earlier worry about context exhaustion didn't materialize for the translator (review agents handled it sequentially fine too).
- **Quality is stable at 0.94** across 6 carnets / 331 entries over two waves — the pipeline has found a consistent literary band for the Académie Julian / 1878 material.
- **Pure-Latin scan continues to earn its place** — caught «oko», «chic»×3, «homme du monde», «universum», and a Russian «ы»/«И» across the wave, none of which the gate's mixed-token check sees.

## Proposed Changes

1. **Standardize the conductor idle-pre-read trap-list** as an explicit early step when a CON is spawned for a multi-carnet wave (not improvised). It was the difference-maker in both waves. *(executive-director skill)*
2. **Standardize the in-prompt terminology/collision lock** for translator spawns (validated this wave). The lead should spawn the conductor first, let it pre-read, then bake its trap-list into the translator prompts. *(executive-director skill)*
3. **Editor skill: fix the mid-paragraph RED-note placement artifact** (Issue 1 root cause) — when inserting a `%% RED: %%` note via prefix-match Edit, ensure it lands on its own line at paragraph end, never mid-sentence. This artifact caused the sealed-carnet re-touch. *(editor skill)*
4. **Carry forward the still-open Round-1 proposal**: add a pure-Latin-word scan to `verify-carnet` (with a foreign-term/proper-noun allowlist). Two waves of RED catches confirm the gate gap is real and recurring. *(architect / gate-backlog)*
5. **Restate sealed-carnet discipline for editors**: any touch to a CON-sealed carnet — even to repair one's own note artifacts — must be flagged to the lead BEFORE editing. *(editor skill)*
