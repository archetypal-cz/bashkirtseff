## ⚠️ Flagged for KRR — not changed

2. **«Сто чортів!» for *sapristi*** (105.0405, 105.0634, 105.0699 — consistent): notably stronger than the mild French oath per the LAN notes; deliberate-looking, so left, but worth a register decision.

4. **«вишукані туалети»** for *toilettes de haut raffinement* (105.0577): period-correct but a modern reader may misparse; swap to «вбрання» only if you want maximum accessibility.
5. Minor judgment calls left alone: «уявіть собі»-class renderings all fine; «є те, що є» (*il y a ce qu'il y a*); «вона» for the *moi-spectateur…il* self-personification (aligns with Marie's real gender); *vélléités* → «порив» (stronger than French but consistent ×2); «сиділа доброчесно» register; *remise depuis trois ans* ambiguity (ambiguous in the French itself).
