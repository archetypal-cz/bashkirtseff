## Quality baseline — recurring defect categories (OPS yield order)

1. **Tense/voice**: passé composé + narrative present flattened to one tense; periphrastic *ser*-passive where Spanish wants active or *pasiva refleja*. Marie's tense shifts are deliberate (present for vanity/prophecy, preterite for chronicle) — never unify.
2. **Deixis + null-subject ambiguity**: "cette nuit" = the past night; explicit "él" needed for the Duke where a dropped pronoun left two male referents.
3. **Ser vs estar on appearance**: silent meaning errors ("je serais belle" → *estaría hermosa*, not *sería*).
4. **Register drift** into notarial Spanish; dubbing-flavoured formulas ("si tan solo" → "si al menos").
5. **Adverb/adjective placement** calqued from French ("dudar en" + adverb).
6. **Footnote fidelity**: clauses dropped when translating inherited notes.
7. (RED) garment preposition "con" not "de"; static location "en" not "a"; **asserted-but-unapplied fix** — an OPS comment claimed the 001.0018 exclamation arc was rejoined but the text was unchanged (RED caught it).
8. (CON) reflexive verbs of social display (*se montrer avec*) → "dejarse ver con", not pasiva refleja; italics for the Spanish gloss inside footnotes; telegraphese/verbless fragments are a register, don't supply verbs.
