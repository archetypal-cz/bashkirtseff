## Observations

- **The early-wave carnets carry a consistent, previously-uncaught defect signature** —
  these were conductor-approved but never editor-reviewed, and a French-vs-translation
  editor pass surfaced exactly the class CON-only review misses:
  - **2 content-completeness defects** an editor is uniquely placed to catch: a fully
    omitted source sentence (009-14) and a dropped `[Rayé:]` crossed-out word (011),
    plus a HIGH place-name mistranslation (016 «Détroit»). None are visible without
    line-by-line comparison to the French.
  - **OPS-comment splices**: these carnets were `opus_reviewed` (OPS), and stale OPS notes
    documenting already-applied fixes were frequently spliced mid-paragraph (renderer-drop
    risk). ~70 stale OPS notes dropped / ~30 mid-paragraph splices rejoined across the wave.
  - **Russianism residue**: adverbial «абсолютно» for très/tout-à-fait recurred (006-013);
    «тоска»; participle calques.
  - **Transliteration drift across carnets**: "Lambertye" was split «Ламберті» vs
    canonical «Ламбертьє» — 015/016/017 each normalized carnet-wide (~108 occurrences
    total) to match TM + carnet 014. The non-canonical niçois demonym and "bataclan"
    spelling also drifted and were normalized.
- **CON caught what RED left**: the editor↔conductor two-stage structure earned its keep —
  CON-011 found one «абсолютно» RED missed, CON-013 resolved a russianism RED flagged but
  left, CON-016 resolved a second «détroit» occurrence RED flagged. The full re-review
  (vs editor-only) was the right call.

## Proposed Changes

- **The OPS-splice + stale-OPS-note pattern is now a known, named signature of the
  early-wave (CON-but-no-RED) carnets.** Any future RED pass over an `opus_reviewed`
  early carnet should be told up front to expect it (this was baked into the spawn prompts
  from carnet 010 onward and materially improved hit rate). Worth a one-line note in the
  editor skill's "Known Issues to Watch For".
- **Completeness (French-vs-translation sentence coverage) should be an explicit RED step
  for any carnet that never had an editor pass** — it found the only two content omissions
  of the wave, which no naturalness/structural check would have surfaced.
