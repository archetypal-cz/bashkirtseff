## Observations

- **The strengthened anti-hallucination self-review pass worked.** Translators
  reported *self-catching and fixing* fabricated words during the new mandatory
  non-word scan **before handoff** — 102 fixed 6 (rukavičnatá, Canrobertyovými ×2,
  skvosté, krásnné, obrůstajícími), 099 fixed 4, 100 fixed 3, 103 fixed 1, 101
  caught a Cyrillic slip. This is exactly the behavior the cz-093-098 skill change
  intended; the class shifted from "RED/CON catches it" toward "translator catches
  its own." Net fabricated-word density reaching RED was lower than last wave.
  **Recommend keeping the change and continuing to monitor** (one wave of evidence).
- **The new TM surname rule held** — translators applied "vzor pán, no -u- infix",
  and where one slipped (Canrobertyovými) the translator self-corrected it in the
  non-word scan. Good early signal.
- **CON did the cross-entry consistency work** — 099 unified a stray "Julien"→
  "Julian" (8×) and "Julián"; 102 unified Bastien-Lepage declension and the
  poitrinaire rendering.
- **103 (Marie's final months) scored highest (0.94)** — the exalted art-mystical
  register and the late-life mortality entries (the mother's posthumous marginalia,
  "plivala jsem krev") landed with restraint; a fitting close to the near-complete
  Czech corpus.

   and recommends a project-wide TM ruling reserving **velkovévoda** for
   non-Russian grand-ducal titles. Worth a TM entry + a one-time sweep.
