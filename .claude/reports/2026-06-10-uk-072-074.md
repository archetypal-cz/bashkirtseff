## Issues Encountered

- **The "Alexandre" name-collision (dominant issue, wave-wide).** "Alexandre" in 072–074 is borne by ≥5 distinct entities: Count **Larderei** (Marie's romantic obsession, locked «Алессандро» indeclinable per 070/071), her uncle **Alexandre Babanine** («Олександр» declinable), **Tsar Alexandre II** («Олександр II»/name-day «святого Олександра»), novelist **Alexandre Dumas** («Александр Дюма», Russified-correct), and the bare-mention **Batourine** (a Russian friend, «Олександр»). All three translators rendered the romantic Larderei as a declinable «Александр»/«Олександр», colliding it with the uncle. Resolution required: (1) a team-lead policy decision (re-assert the 070/071 lock); (2) per-occurrence RSR-driven disambiguation (the glossary `[#Alexandre]` tag is **unreliable** — the bare alias auto-tags to the uncle entity regardless of true referent, so the **per-paragraph RSR comment is the authoritative signal**); (3) a TM-documented lock (new "Carnet 072–074 Additions" section); (4) edge-case rulings — the **Dumas namesake pun** (07-20/07-24: keep «Александр» so the *тезко/homonyme* wordplay matches the novelist, with TR notes; precedent = 070 saint-Alexandre pun), the **Batourine echo** (footnoted), and a caps **teasing form** (09-02 «АЛЕССАНДРО»). Team-lead made **two of its own errors** here, both caught against ground truth: an initial backwards glossary map (core `[#Alexandre]` = uncle, not Larderei), and a wrong "protected Russified «Александра II»" instruction for the Tsar (corrected to Ukrainian «Олександра II» per TM 850/2208).

- **CON's proactive pre-read flag list** (surfaced before review) moved consistency upstream, as in 069–071. This behavior is now codified into the conductor skill so all languages get it.

## Proposed Changes

1. **TM-locked-name lint at the verify-carnet gate (re-proposed, now the clear top priority — 3+ waves running).** The dominant defect class across 065–068, 069–071, and now 072–074 is valid-Cyrillic proper-noun drift, and 072–074 shows its worst form: a single French name ("Alexandre") mapping to 5 referents with 5 different correct UK forms. A lint that (a) flags a TM-locked name appearing in a non-canonical spelling, and (b) for high-collision names, cross-checks the per-paragraph RSR referent, would move this entire class to the mechanical gate. This is the single highest-leverage tooling investment for the project.
2. **Mojibake guard in verify-carnet.** Add a check for double-UTF-8 byte runs (`Ð`/`Ã`/`Ñ` sequences) — CON caught one that the existing latin-in-cyr/foreign-script checks missed. Cheap to add, invisible-to-review class.
3. **Remove "self-audit after finishing" from the translator skill once an editor owns the carnet.** The translator skill's self-grep step caused a concurrent-edit race when a persistent translator woke and re-edited a carnet the editor was working. Either (a) move the self-audit *before* handoff, or (b) drop it for persistent-teammate runs, or (c) have the team-lead shut translators down at handoff (what we did this wave — worked).
4. **Document the unreliable `[#Alexandre]`-style auto-tag in the researcher/editor skills.** The bare-alias auto-tag points at one canonical entity regardless of referent; the per-paragraph RSR comment is authoritative. Editors should be told this explicitly for high-collision names.
5. **(Shipped this wave) Conductor proactive collision-flagging is now language-agnostic** in `conductor/SKILL.md` (teammate-workflow step 2 + idle-behavior block): CON hunts proper-noun/transliteration collisions during its pre-read and delivers a structured locked-names + collision-traps flag list for the lead to broadcast to in-flight translators. Previously this lived only in ED spawn prompts + ED-skill comments, so cz/en/fr didn't reliably get it.
