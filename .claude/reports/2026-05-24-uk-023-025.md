## Issues Encountered

- tr-023: "Тумanchoфи" contained Latin characters a,n,c,h,o embedded in Cyrillic. Appeared in 2 files (08-14 and 08-16). All other occurrences in the carnet used pure Cyrillic.
- con-023: Found "oko" in Latin script (should be Cyrillic "око"), Russian "ё" in "королём" (should be "е"), and Russian "ы" in "высокий" (should be Ukrainian "високий").

### Meaning Reversals (WATCHLIST: semantic-error)
- tr-024: "Nous partîmes avant la fin" (we left before the end) → "Ми поїхали до кінця" (we went to the end). Same pattern as batch 1 — "avant" misinterpreted.
- tr-023: "en hurlant" (howling/wailing) → "вовтузилися" (fidgeting/bustling). Wrong verb entirely.

### Code-Switch Visibility Loss (NEW pattern)
- RED-024 identified that Marie's deliberate Cyrillic "мене" inside French sentences becomes invisible in Ukrainian translation since "мене" is an ordinary Ukrainian word. CON resolved this by adding ==highlight== and a footnote for para 024.0207 explaining the phenomenon. CON also correctly identified that 024.0274 was a false positive (French "moi" not Cyrillic "мене").

### Meaning Shift
- tr-025: "Grande misère" (great wretchedness/suffering) → "Велика образа" (great insult/offence). Shifted from internal suffering to external insult.
