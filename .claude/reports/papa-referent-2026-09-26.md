# Who is "papa" in carnets 002–030 (1873–1875)?

*Report 2026-09-26 · research for team-lead · no content files changed*

## Verdict

**In Marie's 1873–1875 Nice diary, bare "papa" is her maternal grandfather, Stepan Babanine (1807–1878). It is not her father Constantin.** Confidence: **very high (~97%)**. Marie refers to her father Constantin with *marked* forms: "mon père", "mon (très/cher/auguste/illustre) père", "mon pater", or, only during his Nice visit of 24–27 Nov 1873 (013.0085, 013.0093, 013.0109), "Papa"/"le Papa"/"mon adorable papa", always sitting right beside "mon père".

For carnets 009–020 the tally is: **128 "papa"/"pater" → grandfather** (1 of them only probable), **3 "papa" → Constantin** (all in the 25–27 Nov 1873 visit), and 20 further "père/mon père/pater" → Constantin. There are also 27 hits for other people's fathers, the Pope, God, and prayers, plus 1 dance.

## Key evidence (strongest first)

1. **The first editors resolved it the same way.** Manuscript 011.0168 (1873-10-21) reads "Paul est grondé par maman, **papa** empêche maman … maman court après lui, **papa** s'élance … je vais dans le corridor pour prier **papa**…". The 1887/1903 Fasquelle edition (`raw_books/Journal_censored_vol1.txt`) prints this as "**grand-papa** empêche maman … prier **grand-papa** … Grand-papa s'est mis à crier". Mathilde Blind (1890) follows it with "grandpapa interferes where he has no business to". Theuriet's editors knew the family, and they substituted *grand-papa* for Marie's *papa*.
2. **Same servant, same man.** 011.0453 has "Trifon (valet de chambre de **papa**)". Blind 1890 and the 1887 edition both give "Triphon (**grandpapa's** servant) / le domestique de **grand-papa**". In 019.0033 Trifon's father "nous servit quarante ans", so Trifon is a Babanine retainer.
3. **Name day.** 015.0071 (Thu 8 Jan 1874, which is 27 Dec O.S., St Stephen's day) reads "Nous rencontrons papa, **c'est son jour de nom**". Kernberger (2013) translates this "Today was **Grandpapa's** name day". The day can only belong to Stepan. Constantin's name day falls in May.
4. **Age.** 007.0015 (1873-07-14) reads "papa est encore fort et jeune pour **soixante-quatre ans**". That fits Stepan (b. 1807/09). Constantin, per Kernberger's glossary (b. 1833), was 40.
5. **Blindness, regiments, estate, old friends.** 010.0008–0009 reads "c'est un **vieillard aveugle** … Papa voulait qu'on enfermât Pitou". 018.0228 has "histoires de sa jeunesse … **les régiments**". 018.0231 has "grandeurs passées de **Tcherniakovka**" (the Babanine estate). 019.0014 says he has not seen Norov "depuis **quarante ans**". 024.0219 reads "on y a logé **papa, presque aveugle**, Trifon qui lui est nécessaire", and 024.0239 calls the same man "grand-papa".
6. **Marie says so herself.** 025.0185 reads "sous la protection de papa, **il me répugne de le nommer papa**". Here "papa" is the grandfather who shelters Makaroff (cf. 009.0085, 024.0228, 027.0013). In 025.0036–0054 this "Papa" insults "mon père", and Marie writes that she is "en aucun point l'obligée de **mon grand-père**".
7. **Same outing, two names.** 016.0080 has "il faut prendre **papa**, alors il commence avec Georges à crier". The next paragraph, 016.0081, lists those same men as "**mon grand-père**, mon frère et mon oncle".
8. **Babanine household matters.** 004.0387: gendarmes "demandent M. **Babanine** … supposent que c'est **papa**". 006.0171–0172: "grand-papa me parle de son testament … papa va donner des traites à maman". 006.0208/025.0252: Uncle Alexandre (Sacha) "empoche" papa's money. 008.0021: Sacha exhibits "le tabac de papa" at Vienna, which contradicts the PAPA.md claim that this was Constantin's tobacco.
9. **Kernberger's own glossary** (`content/_raw/reports/kernberger_appendix_1_glossary.md:225`) says "**Babanine, Stepan: Marie's grandfather (1807-1878), addressed as "Papa"**". The same glossary has "Golezowsky: Papa's ophthalmologist". In the body text Kernberger keeps Marie's "Papa" as it stands (e.g. 010.0009), switches to "Grandpapa" at 015.0071, and relies on the glossary to tell the two men apart.
10. **Where Constantin was.** He lived in Poltava/Gavronzi. The Durocher affair (011.0277–0284) happens "en Russie"; in 010.0145 maman offers "de retourner chez mon père". His one appearance in this period is the Nice/Monte-Carlo visit: announced at 012.0096 ("Mon cher père vient à Nice. Animal !", 7 Nov), arrival at 013.0082 (25 Nov), departure at 013.0109 (26/27 Nov). Kernberger's chronology line "1873 … Her father visits Nice" refers to this visit.

## Traps for translators

- **021.0200–022.0289: "Papa" (capital P) is *not* family.** It is Marie's nickname "Papa rigolo" for M. Paparigopoulos at Spa (021.0143 "un papa, rigo, poulos"). No #Papa tags there today (verified); keep it that way.
- **012.0189 "le prêtre représente le Christ, le papa de même"** means the Pope. It is an Italianism (*il papa*) in Bête's Catholic argument.
- **017.0253 "on a dansé le grand-papa"** is the *Großvatertanz* (a dance), not the grandfather. en TR already notes this.
- **017.0406 / 018.0024 "papa Furstenberg", "papa Galve"** mean other girls' fathers, used ironically.
- **013.0085 / 013.0093 / 013.0109** are the only "papa" = Constantin in 009–020. cz currently renders them correctly (táta/otec).
- **021.0035** (Spa, Jul 1874) "lettre … remerciante à papa … ma tante retient les mille francs que papa me donne": the aunt and grandfather stayed in Nice (019.0159 "je regrette ma tante et papa"), so this is probably the grandfather.
- Source-side error: the LAN note on 010.0008 ("vieillard aveugle … harsh judgment of **father**") misidentifies the referent.

## Recommendations

**(a) Czech.** The RED/fablelous ruling "papa → dědeček" is **correct**; keep it. Extend it to carnets 002–008 and 019–030, which still say *tatínek/táta*: cz 004 has tatínek×4, 005 ×4, 007 ×4, 008 ×5, 002 táta×3. cz 019 still leaves "Papa" untranslated (019.0014). The RED note's claim that "005–009 established dědeček" is only partly true. Keep *táta/otec* for 013.0085/0093/0109 and for every "mon père/pater". The alternative is to keep Marie's ambiguity with „tatínek" plus a note, but no Czech reader would take „tatínek" to mean a grandfather, so dědeček is better. The ingredients for a one-time translator's note at the first occurrence (005 or 009) are in the entry below.

**(b) English and Ukrainian.** Do not silently replace "Papa" with "Grandpapa" in en. Kernberger shows the literal "Papa" is a defensible scholarly choice, and it keeps a real family trait. Add **one footnote at the first occurrence** in 002 (002.0225/0276) and a reminder at 010.0002 and 013.0085. Suggested wording: "Marie's 'papa' in these years is her maternal grandfather Stepan Babanine, whom the household (including her mother) called Papa; her father Constantin lived in Poltava. The 1887 edition and Blind (1890) print 'grand-papa'; Kernberger (2013) glosses Stepan as 'addressed as "Papa"'." In uk, „тато" actively misleads: Ukrainian readers read it as the father, and 019.0313/025.0054 then become incoherent. Either switch to „дідусь" (the Czech approach), or keep „тато" with the same footnote. **Recommend дідусь** plus a single footnote, because UK is otherwise complete and corrections-only. That makes it a bounded find-and-fix pass using the table below.

- `GRAND_PAPA.md`: add aliases "Papa" and "Stepan Babanine", add the name-day, age, blindness and "addressed as Papa" facts with a Kernberger citation, and fix the stale "Diary Coverage".
- Retag. Paragraphs 002–030 now tagged `#Papa` whose "papa" is the grandfather should become `#Grand_papa`. Current #Papa counts: 015×11, 016×9, 017×15, 018×6, 019×5, 024×10, plus a few in 002–013. Keep #Papa only on the Constantin paragraphs listed above (010.0145, 011.0030, 011.0277–0284, 012.0096, 013.0069–0109, 019.0313, 023.0208, 025.0054, 030.0128). Also check that no #Papa tag sits on the "Papa rigolo" paragraphs in 021–022.
- Fix the LAN note at 010.0008.
- The glossary-tagger alias "Papa" → PAPA is presumably the source of the mis-tagging. Remove that alias so a re-run does not bring the wrong tags back.
