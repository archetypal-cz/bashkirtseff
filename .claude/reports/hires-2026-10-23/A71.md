# A71: byline of «Le Sénat : Henry [illisible]» (067.1602)

Date: 2026-10-24. Researcher: RSR (Sonnet 5.5).

## Answer

Not identified from an independent source. The best reading of the print is **«Henry Deynisse»**. The final letter may carry an accent («Deynissé»), but the 3x enlargement shows only a plain *e*. No known Figaro journalist of that name was found.

Confidence: reading of the letters, medium-high. Identification of the person, none.

## What was checked

1. **Page image.** I cropped the sommaire line from `content/_raw/scans/hires/Tome9/p225.png` and enlarged it 300%. The italic surname reads D-e-y-n-i-s-s-e. The *n* and *i* are narrow and run together. The line is «Le Sénat : Henry Deynisse.». The forename is clearly «Henry», with the *H* slightly smudged.
2. **Candidates ruled out.** Henry Fouquier, Henri des Houx (the pseudonym of Henri Hériot) and Henry de Pène do not fit the surname letters. None begins «Dey-» or ends «-isse».
3. **Gallica.** Not reachable from this machine. Every request to gallica.bnf.fr (`/ark:/12148/bpt6k...texteBrut`, `.texte.langFR`, the `services/Issues` API) returned HTTP 403, a bot block (about 5.5 KB of HTML). I could not open Le Figaro of 1876-11-07 or any neighbouring day.
4. **RetroNews.** The search page returns 200, but it is a JavaScript front end, so it gave no results from the command line.
5. **Web search.** Five queries for the spellings Deynisse, Deyniss, Deynis, Dessenné and Dessinné found no biography of such a journalist. One search summary claimed that Gallica scans of "Figaro : journal non politique" (arks `bpt6k2759923`, `bpt6k2762023`) show a «Henry Deynisse» byline near Senate coverage, dated 1876-06-08 and 1876-12-30. I could not open those pages, and the summary showed no quoted text. I therefore do **not** treat this as a source. It does agree with the reading above, and it is a lead worth following.
