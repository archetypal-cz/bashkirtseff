## Findings that need no edit, or that I could not edit

- **050/051 flyleaf (t.6 p.265).** This text is **not in `_original`**: the rebuild (6b3ffd5c35) deliberately left it out with the covers. The print reads `[Sur la page de garde:]` / `[Deux lignes illisibles:` / `[Rayé : Emile d'Audiffret` / `Marie Bashkirseff` / `Felix Galula Dechiar` / `Marie Bachkirseff]`. This matches the docx ¶4253–4258 and the rebuild notes exactly. The «Deux lignes illisibles» is the edition's own mark (it has no closing bracket in print), so a sharper scan cannot settle it. If the owner wants it carried, it belongs after 050.1345 (kind `cover`/`rayé`), and that is a rebuild decision.
- **Monks' letter facsimile (t.6 p.267).** The facsimile covers «Ô fils du péché…» through «…et y ayant trouvé» only. Besides «impiété», Marie's hand differs from the printed text (051.0435) in five places. I kept all five as printed, because the edition may have transcribed the diary copy (051.0433: pp.116 bis–quater) rather than this sheet:
  - «Ô» where the print has «O»
  - «Tu le voulais» where the print has «Et tu le voulais»
  - «7 F. 50 c.» where the print has «7 Fr. 50 c.»
  - «London-House» where the print has «London House»
  - **«Gogery» (twice, with a clear G) where the print has «Cogery»**
- **067.1619** (t.9 p.228) was not rescanned, so the placeholder stays.
- **068.1010.** The brief describes this as Marie's hand, but p.298 is ordinary typeset text in the edition's serif face for foreign words. The garbled Italian is the edition's reading and the image cannot improve on it. The phrase is not a known verse or libretto (web search); it looks like Marie's own improvised «phrase arabe».
- **A-36 «clippings one paragraph per article?»** I did not restructure anything. At present the Gazette is split into one paragraph per newspaper paragraph (1569–1601), the sommaire is one paragraph, the Ignotus article is split by its paragraphs (1603–1611), and the death notice is split by its sentences/paragraphs (1612–1618). The print's `***` section breaks are kept inside 1593, 1601 and 1609.
- **Date side note.** The sommaire belongs to the Antonelli-obituary issue (Antonelli died in the night of 5–6 Nov 1876, and the notice says «hier»), so that issue is probably 7 Nov 1876. The Gazette is dated «4 décembre», which is the Marcère/Laussedat incident of December 1876. So 067.1569–1601 and 1602–1618 come from **two different issues**. The existing source attributions already say this, so I changed nothing.
