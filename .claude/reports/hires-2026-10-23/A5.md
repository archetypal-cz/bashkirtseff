# A5: sharp-scan verification of carnets 038–044 (Mon Journal t.5), 2026-10-24

Package A5 of BRIEF-A. Hi-res images `hires/Tome5`. **Every page from Tome5.pdf p.220 to p.359 (carnets 038–044) was read on the image**, so this pass also covers runs whose stars were both lost in the text layer. The tsv rows and the old RSR/B-02 notes were checked against those images. Only `content/_original/038–044` was edited. No glossary file was touched.

**Page numbers.** In tome 5 the printed folio is **PDF page − 1**. For example, Tome5.pdf p.343 carries folio 342. The brief says "PDF page = printed page", which is not true for this tome. Every new note therefore gives both numbers: «Mon Journal t.5 p.342 (Tome5.pdf p.343)». The older notes in these carnets (2026-10-21/22, B-02) cite PDF pages as «t.5 p.N» and were not changed.

**Star glyph.** Tome 5 prints both stars as a plain asterisk: «*…*», not ★. So every `*…*` that came through as "italics" in these carnets had to be decided on the image. Real italics in this tome are set in italic type and never carry asterisks.

**Gates:** `just splicescan _original CCC` prints nothing, and `just verify-carnet _original CCC` PASSes, for 038, 039, 040, 041, 042, 043 and 044. `just check-comments _original` is OK. During the work I made two splices (039.0064 and 044.0032, by anchoring a comment mid-line). Both were repaired and re-scanned.

2. **044.0161.** The edition replaced Marie's Ukrainian proverb with an editorial placeholder between stars, «[texte du proverbe ukrainien, dont la traduction est ci-après]», and then gave its translation in italics («Pour son propre argent il s'est acheté un malheur.»). I removed the star asterisks and left both parts unmarked. Option A: leave as is (current). Option B: mark the italic translation `==…==` with a Ukrainian-run footnote, and keep the bracket roman.
