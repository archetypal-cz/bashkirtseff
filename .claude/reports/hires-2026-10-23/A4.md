**Page numbers.** In tome 5 the PDF page is **not** the printed page: printed = PDF − 1. For example, Livre 32 starts on printed p.31, which is PDF p.32, and PDF p.11 carries the folio «10». Every new note therefore cites both, e.g. «Mon Journal t.5 p.10 (PDF p.11)». The older notes in these carnets cite «Tome5.pdf p.N», which is the PDF number. The E-13 notes at 032.0225–0234 cite printed numbers.

**Method.**
- Star runs: tome 5 prints the stars as plain asterisks. Every page from PDF 5 to 218 was template-matched for the asterisk glyph on the hi-res image (`hires/Tome5`). That gave 38 pages with stars, all of which were read. They match the tsv candidates and the docx `*` lines.
- Two tsv candidates are not stars. On p.134 «sur*» is a tilde-like mark and on p.155 «plaindre. *» is a dash.
