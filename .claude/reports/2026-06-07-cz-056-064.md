## Issues Encountered

### Quality patterns (translator-side, all caught by RED/CON)
1. **Conditional 1st/3rd-person reversal** (`kdyby` vs `kdybych`) — the wave's signature
   error; several meaning-reversing (e.g. 063.0492 "S'il retournait à moi" inverted).
   **Mitigation that worked**: mid-run broadcast to active translators + a RED grep gate
   (`grep kdybych(om)? + object pronouns`, verify person vs French subject). After the
   broadcast, tr-056 and tr-063 self-caught instances; 060 and 062 came through with
   **0 reversals**. Strong evidence broadcasting RED findings mid-wave works.
2. **Dropped-"l" "miluji→miuji" typo** — 37 occurrences in carnet 056 alone (the most
   love-saturated carnet). Recurred scattered elsewhere. Needs a pre-RED grep.
3. **Cyrillic contamination incl. lookalike-letter swaps** — beyond whole Cyrillic words
   (известно), a Cyrillic "ета" had replaced Latin "eta" inside "Audiff**ета**" ×4 —
   invisible to the eye, caught only by a Cyrillic scan. Mechanical scan is essential.
4. **Mid-word truncations / jammed finite verbs** — "nemi lovala", "doumírala skončila",
   sentence cut mid-word (062.0324 restored). OCR-adjacent.
5. **Czech leaking into the French `%%` reference comments** — copy-paste artifact that
   damages the source-of-truth (5 in 056, 3 in 062). Restore the French.
6. **Number errors** — "quarante-huit heures" → "čtyřiadvacet" (24h) in two 059 entries.

### Process / tooling
7. **Mid-paragraph RED comment placement** — editing a fix into a long single-line
   paragraph can land the `%% RED %%` comment mid-paragraph, splitting readable text.
   Both editors hit it; reliable end-scan: `grep 'RED:.*%% [^%]'` then rejoin. Fold into
   the editor skill's end-of-review pass.
8. **`just verify-carnet` gate existed but was NOT run** — red-a reported the
   `just verify-carnet` recipe "does not exist" and fell back to manual greps. **That
   claim was false** (a ground-truth-vs-agent-claim error): the recipe IS in the justfile
   (line 454, `src/scripts/verify-carnet.ts`, built+validated 2026-06-06) and runs
   cleanly — `just verify-carnet cz 057` → PASS, with checks for frontmatter, links,
   glossary-depth, footnotes, %%-balance, **latin-in-cyr, and foreign-script**. The real
   issue: the gate was not run this wave — neither the ED pre-RED (used ad-hoc greps
   instead) nor translators pre-finalize (their skill says to). Had it been run, its
   `latin-in-cyr` check would have caught the Audiffr**ета** Cyrillic swap and the other
   Cyrillic hits mechanically, and `glossary-depth`/`frontmatter` would have caught those
   classes too. **Lesson: run `just verify-carnet {lang} {carnet}` pre-RED (ED) and
   pre-finalize (translator) — it already exists and covers most of this wave's mechanical
   defects.** The skills already reference it; adoption is the gap.
9. **Missing `conductor_approved` frontmatter field** — carnets 063, 064, 062 had no
   `conductor_approved` field on any entry (056 did). con added it per entry. Systematic
   tr-scaffold gap across 3 carnets — worth a one-time upstream fix, not per-carnet.
10. **~24-min conductor idle-after-assignment gap** — a queued CON assignment (059) did
    not activate until a follow-up status ping. Lesson: after assigning a carnet to a
    review agent, verify engagement (disk count climbing) within a few minutes; a status
    ping reliably kicks it into gear. Related to the "session-pause ping loss" pattern.
11. **Stale self-addressed `task_assignment` echoes** — completed tasks generated stale
    `task_assignment` echoes (assignedBy = the agent itself) for con (#19/#20/#21) and
    translators (#7/#8). Harmless; agents correctly ignored them after the first. Harness
    quirk worth noting so future leads/agents don't chase them.

## Observations

- **The larger team paid off**: parallel review surfaced systemic patterns within the
  first two carnets, enabling a mid-wave broadcast that measurably reduced the signature
  error downstream. All 8 slots stayed productive; total wall-clock ~2h45m for 130
  entries (~1.3 entries/min aggregate).
- **Error profile was overwhelmingly mechanical**, not interpretive: the hard literary
  passages (the 062 Sieyès finale, the 060 proposal, the 063 clairvoyant séances, the
  058 Pompeii/Hamilton set-pieces, dense Latin/Italian/Russian code-switching) came
  through clean; the fixes were typos, Cyrillic, case/gender agreement, and OCR artifacts.
- **The conditional-grep gate is the single most valuable mechanical check** this wave —
  it converts a meaning-reversing error into a reliable catch at both TR self-review and
  RED.
