## Issues Encountered

1. **"Girofla" nickname spelling (systemic, recurring)** — Marie's nickname for Audiffret. Established convention (carnets 030-041, 115+ instances, TM 916-917/1616) is **«Джирофла» (Дж-)** with adjective «джирофлівський»; ONLY the operetta title «Жирофле-Жирофла» keeps Ж- (TM 950). Early translators defaulted to phonetic «Жирофля»/«Жірофла» (Ж-/Жі-). Root cause compounded by ED briefly adding a wrong TM line. Fixed in 042 (RED), 044 (TR self-fix), 045 (TR, 3-case fix incl. operetta), 043 (TR). From 046 onward, baking the convention into translator **spawn prompts** eliminated the error (046/047/048/049 correct first-pass). The Ж-/Дж- distinction also carries deliberate puns (046/047 pirate-abduction: operetta character Ж- vs Audiffret Дж-) — RED reconciled these per-instance.
2. **"le Niçois" demonym (recurring)** — three different coinages appeared across carnets («ніжець» in 045, «ніццеєць» in 043, «ніццяни» pl. in 049/048) against the established **«ніццянець»** (TM 1227, ~20 instances in 030-044). Normalized in each by RED. Preempting via messages to active translators + a findable TM Key-Terms entry curbed later recurrence.
3. **CON `conductor_approved` flag-miss** — CON marked review tasks complete but failed to write the frontmatter flag on the final entry of 043/046/049 — precisely the entries where it had just applied a text edit (the flag-write got skipped after the edit on the last file). ## Observations

- **Spawn-prompt terminology injection is the highest-leverage control.** Once the Girofla + Niçois conventions were in the spawn prompt (carnet 046+), divergence stopped entirely. Mid-run correction messages frequently arrived *after* the translator had already committed the text, forcing fix cycles — the prompt is the right place, not a follow-up ping.
- **Disk-state monitoring caught two things the task board hid**: translators idle-before-finalize, and CON's flag-misses. Continue checking `grep -l` counts against entry counts, not just task status.
- Quality was consistently high and rising with the section's literary demands (anagrams, neologism verbs, 31-stanza verse, the "Бібі" self-distancing voice) — translators rose to it; RED/CON verified rather than rewrote.
- Verse handling: 043's complainte was inconsistently rendered (verse + prose) on first pass; versifying all blocks for consistency (TR) was the right literary call. Worth making "render Marie's rhymed songs as verse" a standing translator instruction.
