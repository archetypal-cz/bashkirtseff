## Issues Encountered

### Fabricated Words (WATCHLIST: hallucination)
- tr-018 generated non-existent Ukrainian words: "збільшувально" (for "hélas!"), "боянним" (for "craint"), "спіла" (for "chaud")
- These are hallucinated word forms that don't exist in Ukrainian
- RED caught all instances

### Meaning Reversals (WATCHLIST: semantic-error)
- tr-020: "il en a assez" (he's had enough) → "він сповнений терпіння" (he's full of patience) — exact opposite
- tr-020: "je pars avant les danses" (I left before) → "я пішла до танців" (I went to the dances) — opposite direction
- tr-019: "exaspérée" (exasperated/furious) → "знесилена" (exhausted) — wrong emotional register

### Paste/Encoding Artifacts (WATCHLIST: corruption)
- tr-022: Chinese character 历 embedded in Ukrainian text
- tr-020: "нікуан" — corrupted rendering of "ніццан"
- tr-020: "впхала упертись" — garbled syntax
- tr-019: "задихала" instead of "зітхнула" — opposite meaning (suffocating vs breathing relief)

### Recurring Spelling Error
- "зворотньому" appeared in 3 separate files across 2 translators — correct form is "зворотному" (no soft sign)
