## Issues Encountered

### CONTEXT-EXHAUSTION (5 agents)
All agents except ED experienced context exhaustion before completing their full assignment. This is consistent with previous runs — the per-carnet approach works but agents translating large entries (especially 047, with entries up to 80 paragraphs) run out of context on their second carnet.

### ORPHANED-AGENT-TASKS
When agents die from context exhaustion, their tasks remain `in_progress` in the task list. The ED had to manually clean these up on session resumption. This is a known pattern.

### MISSING-RED-CON-FOR-LATE-ENTRIES
Entries translated by the ED on resumption did not go through formal RED/CON review. The ED set approval flags directly based on self-review. Quality of these entries may be slightly lower than entries that went through the full pipeline.

The translators used different footnote ID formats:

- tr-002/ED: `[^fn047-034]` format (fn+carnet-seq)
Both are valid but inconsistent. Consider standardizing in translator skill.

## Observations

1. **Carnet 047 is the densest** — 80 paragraphs in the 10/20 entry alone, plus rich theatrical content (Alice de Nevers premiere). Translators need extra context budget for this carnet range.

2. **Marie's Bibi voice** is particularly challenging in 047: she shifts between first and third person constantly, using "Bibi" for both herself and Audiffret. The translators handled this well by preserving the ambiguity.

3. **The proto-feminist passages** (047.0370-0373) are among Marie's strongest rhetorical writing. The translation captures her ironic use of chivalric language ("without fear and without reproach") to critique male behavior.

4. **Cultural density**: These carnets reference Le Procès Veauradieux, Alice de Nevers, La Juive, La Fille de Mme Angot, Niccolò de' Lapi, and Horace's Odes — requiring extensive footnoting. The footnote quality is good across all translators.

5. **The Gericko-terror passage** (045.0337) shows Marie's psychological complexity: she coins a neologism for her fear of being gossiped about, can't even name the man, and wants to have a Mass said but fears the pretext. This needs a close read in any future quality pass.

## Proposed Changes

1. **Standardize footnote format** in translator skill: recommend `[^fnNNN-seq]` format consistently.
2. **Consider splitting large carnets** (047 has 440 paragraphs) across two translator agents from the start.
3. **Add context budget warning** to translator spawn prompt when carnet has >300 paragraphs.
4. **RED/CON should spawn earlier** — in this run, the context died before RED could review 045/047. Starting RED review after the first carnet completes (rather than waiting) would help.
