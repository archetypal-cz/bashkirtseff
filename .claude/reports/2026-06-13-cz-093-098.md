## Issues Encountered

1. **Elevated translator defect density vs. the 083–092 wave.** RED's first-pass
   estimates (0.82–0.90) sat below the usual ~0.92. The dominant, recurring
   classes:
   - **Hallucinated non-existent Czech words** under context pressure
     (e.g. *odhodení, rozezírala, vyhřesuji, zaslouho, nezdárštěla, zpustenost,
     nezmarniť*). Appeared in **every** carnet. RED + CON caught and fixed all.
   - **Meaning reversals / polarity flips** ("nejvýznamnější" for *la plus
     insignifiante*; dropped negations; antonym swaps). A real correctness risk;
     all caught at RED or CON's comparative pass, none survived.
   - **1st-person feminine & conditional-person agreement** (Marie rendered
     masculine/3pl, or "Rád bych" for "Ráda bych", přechodník *-je* for *-jíc*).
     Still recurring despite the locked CLAUDE.md check — caught every time.
   - **Gallicism calques**: *vzít si ženu* (épouser), *vzdává spravedlnost*
     (rendre justice ×3 in 097), *dělat dvůr*, *od X let* for *depuis X ans*.
2. **Orphan footnote slipped TR self-review (093/1881-10-15).** Reference
   `[^93.96.4]` (Coro) had no definition; caught by the pre-RED `verify-carnet`
   footnotes check, fixed by the lead before RED. Gate vindicated again.
3. **Embedded-French source contamination (094/1881-12-18).** The French `%%`
   original read "que ces dessins **jsou** extraordinaires" — a Czech word
   ("jsou") had leaked into the French source line (pre-existing in `_original`?
   or a prior-session artifact). Flagged by the translator, corrected to "sont"
   by RED. Worth a one-off check of the `_original/094` source line.
4. **Linter comment-splicing.** The repo's on-save reflow repeatedly merged newly
   added RED/CON comments into the single-line body paragraphs of edited entries
   (096, 097, 098 most affected). Every review agent caught this with an
   end-of-pass `RED:`/`CON:` splice grep and rejoined them; final state clean.
   This remains a standing friction for any agent editing these files.

## Observations

- **The mechanical gate is the backbone.** With no live team channel, the
  `verify-carnet` + `check-links-repo` gates between every stage were the entire
  safety net, and they held: caught the orphan footnote, confirmed every
  RED/CON edit kept structure intact, and proved glossary path-depth correct on
  all 376 files (the recurring `../_glossary/` vs `../../_original/_glossary/`

  Kotchoubey spelling unification.

  uncensored mandate: preserved-and-framed with a contextualizing footnote, NOT
  softened. Consistent with house policy; flagged here for the record given the
  087.0408 softening-vs-framing consistency question still open from 083–092.
- **Quality recovered to plateau at CON** (0.82–0.90 → 0.92–0.93), confirming the
  three-stage pipeline absorbs an above-average raw-translation defect load.

## Proposed Changes

1. **Translator skill — anti-hallucination guard.** Add an explicit
   self-check: "After translating, re-read for any Czech word you are not certain
   is a real, standard word — invented forms under context pressure are a known
   failure mode; verify or replace." This wave produced ≥10 distinct coined
   non-words across all 5 carnets — the single biggest defect class.
2. **TM entry — French masculine consonant-final surnames decline as vzor *pán*
   with NO inserted -u-** (Doucet→Doucetovi, not Doucet**u**ovi; same for Ballote,
   Lepage). The spurious -u- infix appeared independently in two 094 batches.
3. **Architect / gate:** the `_original/094/1881-12-18` French source line with the
   leaked Czech "jsou" suggests the long-pending `verify-carnet` enhancement for
   *foreign-token contamination of French `%%` source lines* would have value;
   add to the accumulating gate-enhancement set.
4. **Standing note:** the linter comment-splice behavior on single-line body
   paragraphs is now a reliable cost on every edit pass — worth either a
   post-edit auto-rejoin in the gate or a linter config fix (architect call).
